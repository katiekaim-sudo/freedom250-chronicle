Stablecoin Constitutions — Issuer × Reserve × GENIUS Gate

Nine dollar tokens, nine present-day promises, five statutory doorways

GENIUS does not make a blockchain or wallet lawful. It makes a particular issuer-and-reserve constitution eligible for U.S. issuance and later intermediary distribution. Existing payment adapters can survive the transition, but the token placed inside them must pass the issuer, reserve, redemption and lawful-order gates.

The two-stage statutory switch

18 Jul 2025GENIUS enacted. Present charters and licenses continue to be the current authority.
General effective dateEarlier of 18 Jan 2027 or 120 days after final implementing regulations.
At effectivenessU.S. issuance becomes a permitted-issuer activity; non-permitted coins lose specified institutional money treatment.
18 Jul 2028U.S. service providers may carry only permitted coins or coins satisfying the foreign exception.

USD₮ Foreign issuer Live + attested Tether live global scale · quarterly BDO attestation

USD₮ is the scale test—and the reserve-fit problem GENIUS makes visible

Who owes the dollar?Tether International, S.A. de C.V. is the CNAD-authorized USD₮ issuer, DASP and customer redemption counterparty. Tether Operations S.A. de C.V. is a separate owner/brand/site entity.
What backs it?Treasury-heavy but broader than the GENIUS list: Q1 2026 disclosure includes about $141 billion of direct and indirect Treasury-bill exposure, about $20 billion of physical gold and about $7 billion of Bitcoin, alongside other assets.
GENIUS gateForeign route: home-regime comparability, OCC registration, U.S. customer liquidity reserves and lawful-order capability. Reserve composition remains an additional substantive hurdle.
open the stablecoin constitution
Issuer obligation

Tether reports token-related liabilities and reserves exceeding them; direct redemption is governed through eligible Tether customer relationships.

Reserve fit

Gold and Bitcoin are not GENIUS permitted reserve categories. The current portfolio therefore cannot be assumed to satisfy the Act without restructuring, segregation or a different product.

Custody / segregation

The issuer's regulatory document describes reserve assets held with multiple third-party banks and licensed financial institutions rather than one public custodian; it does not establish the U.S.-located liquidity reserve required by the foreign exception.

Primary redemption

Direct primary redemption is permissioned; most holders use exchanges, market makers and wallets.

Distribution surface

The world's deepest stablecoin distribution network is the asset's strength—and becomes its exposure to the 2028 intermediary gate.

Required structural move

Either qualify USD₮ through the foreign route with a GENIUS-compliant reserve constitution or shift U.S. distribution toward USA₮ while USD₮ remains global.

18 July 2028

U.S. wallets and exchanges cannot offer or sell the coin to U.S. persons after July 18, 2028 unless the foreign exception is satisfied.

Treasury channel

USD₮ is already a major offshore conduit into U.S. Treasury demand; GENIUS makes continued U.S. distribution conditional on bringing the issuer and reserve constitution inside a recognized supervisory perimeter.

Do not overclaim

Reserve assets exceeding liabilities is not the same test as every reserve asset falling within GENIUS's permitted list.

Issuer obligation

Eligible Circle Mint customers may mint and redeem 1:1. A holder's direct claim is conditional on becoming an eligible Mint customer; individuals generally reach USDC through wallets, exchanges and neobanks.

Reserve fit

Strong asset-category alignment with the GENIUS schedule is visible, but no public PPSI reserve determination exists. Circle's own disclosure remains the evidence.

Custody / segregation

The Circle Reserve Fund is managed by BlackRock and BNY is primary reserve custodian. Circle National Trust is designed for future reserve management, collateral trusteeship and digital-asset custody; it expressly does not issue USDC.

Primary redemption

Direct 1:1 redemption is institutional through Circle Mint; everyday holders normally sell or redeem through an intermediary.

Distribution surface

Broad wallet, exchange, bank, card-network and processor distribution. The adapter layer is already mature.

Required structural move

Obtain NYDFS charter/stablecoin approval for Circle Internet Trust; obtain the required GENIUS state certification and issuer approval; execute assignment/assumption or new terms; align token administration, reserve-control agreements and attestations to the new issuer.

18 July 2028

If the issuer becomes permitted, U.S. digital-asset service providers can continue carrying the coin. Existing integrations do not themselves satisfy the issuer gate.

Treasury channel

USDC already converts circulation into deposits, Treasury bills and Treasury repo; GENIUS would standardize that private-dollar-to-public-debt channel.

Do not overclaim

Circle National Trust is not the planned issuer. Circle Internet Trust Company, LLC is a separate proposed NY entity, and no operative novation or NY approval was located.
