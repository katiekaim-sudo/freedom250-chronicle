Mercury–Uranus

The planets

Mercury — Communication, intellect, commerce, transport. MUNDANE: the media; the transportation and communications infrastructure; trade and commerce; education and intellectual life; the postal system and telecommunications. Mercury's condition shows how information flows through the nation — freely or obstructed, truthfully or deceptively.

Uranus — Disruption, revolution, the lightning bolt. MUNDANE: radical movements; revolutionary politics; sudden upheavals; technology and innovation; strikes and civil unrest; the urge to overthrow the established order. Uranus shows where the status quo will be broken.

The combination — mundane

Under helpful aspects (conjunction, sextile, trine), Mercury-Uranus "encourages reform; legislation proposed while in effect is more likely successful and popular; scientific discovery and technological innovation benefit," as Greer describes. The national conversation shifts toward the future. New ideas break through media gatekeepers and reach the public. Reform legislation finds unexpected support. Technology leaps forward — not incrementally but disruptively. Whistleblowers release information that serves the public interest. The education system embraces innovation. This is the aspect of the breakthrough idea that actually lands.

Under hostile aspects (square, opposition), Greer warns of "political turbulence; unexpected troubles; strange events and unsolved crimes become subjects of obsessive public interest." The information landscape is disrupted chaotically. Viral moments seize the national conversation but distort rather than inform. Technology fails at the worst moment — cyberattacks, system outages, infrastructure collapses. Political discourse becomes erratic and unpredictable. The public fixates on bizarre crimes or strange events that resist explanation, consuming media bandwidth that could go to substantive issues. Leaks happen, but they create confusion rather than clarity.

As trigger: Mercury-Uranus aspects are fast-moving (days to about two weeks) and mark the specific moments when information breakthroughs, technological disruption, and unexpected revelations activate whatever broader outer-planet storylines are in play.
