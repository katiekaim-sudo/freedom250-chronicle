Card Networks Add Stablecoin Product Lines

Funding source × credential × settlement asset × merchant receipt

The dominant transition adds stablecoin funding, settlement, payout and wallet products around the card constitution. The card credential and merchant acceptance contract usually survive; native alternatives become a new rail only when authorization, routing, finality and remedies move with them.

One checkout, several monetary constitutions

The same merchant surface can carry an ordinary card funded by a stablecoin, a card whose interbank obligation settles in USDC, a merchant payout in stablecoin, or a genuinely native wallet transfer. Those are different products and different clocks.
