The SEC and CFTC turn

How are the market regulators rewriting crypto from inside their own rulebooks?

The braid so far

The market regulators are rewriting crypto from inside their own rulebooks: proposals, staff letters, time-limited exemptions, credential gates, a registered clearinghouse and, on October 1, 2026, a crypto-custody proposal. These objects do not share one maturity. A proposal is not permission; a no-action position is not a rule; registration is not recurring use.

In this Libra-ingress and Aries-Full-Moon window, Mercury–Mars is the lead archetypal strand. It moves from no direct major aspect in the ingress to an applying waxing square in the Full Moon. The image is the record pressing into action: text becoming a proposal, exemption, registration, gate or instruction. Constructively, the old rulebook learns to describe the new object precisely enough to govern it. In shadow, urgency lets staff positions and conditional permissions do work that deserves fuller law, public process or tested remedy.

Jupiter–Saturn is a companion strand. Their waxing First Quarter trine gives expansion a channel through inherited structure. This can be institutional competence: innovation admitted without erasing custody, capital, disclosure or loss rules. It can also be controlled expansion in which incumbents and credentialed actors receive the usable lane first. Venus–Pluto supplies the access-and-custody companion image through a waning square. The CPA credential notice and custody proposal both ask who is valuable, qualified and trusted enough to enter—and who holds power after admission.

Uranus–Pluto remains a background strand, not the owner of the regulatory story. Its active trine offers a flowing image between network innovation and concentrated power. The network may redistribute practical control; it may instead modernize the regulator's and incumbent's power to admit, monitor and exclude.

The constructive pole is a legible perimeter in which new market forms can operate and fail without destroying title, custody, cash finality or remedy. The shadow pole is regulation by a stack of conditional exceptions whose practical beneficiaries are clearer than its public constitution.

The factual test is downstream: final and effective rules, named relying firms, actual transactions, examinations, enforcement, custody failures, correction and appeal. Until then, this is a regulator opening and labeling lanes—not proof that the market has moved through them at scale.
