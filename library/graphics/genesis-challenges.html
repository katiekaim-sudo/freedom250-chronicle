What are the 33 science challenges the Genesis Mission is meant to solve?

DOE's July 22, 2026 document lists 33 science and technology challenges in five areas: Protecting the Nation from Emerging Threats (9), Extending the Frontiers of American Discovery (8), Delivering Reliable Infrastructure and Energy Affordability (7), Building American Industrial Strength (5), Helping Americans Live Longer, Healthier Lives (4). They include 'Delivering Nuclear Energy That is Faster, Safer, Cheaper', 'Scaling the Grid to Power the American Economy', 'Software Understanding for National Security', 'Ensuring American Space Superiority'. All 33 are proposed objectives in DOE's text, not measured results; only 2 have a profiled Genesis project assigned: GridFM 2.0 (to 'Scaling the Grid to Power the American Economy') and Prometheus nuclear AI (to 'Delivering Nuclear Energy That is Faster, Safer, Cheaper').

Membership, an announced commitment, selection, award, delivery, accepted operation and measured scientific result remain separate states.
