Federal Reserve, Congress, and courts

Federal Reserve leadership changed through three dated steps: Senate confirmation as a Board member on May 12, confirmation to lead the Board on May 13, and the oath plus FOMC selection on May 22. Powell’s term leading the Board ended May 15; his separate Governor term remained scheduled through January 31, 2028.

Congressional objects stayed at distinct legal states. H.R. 8312 was announced April 15, marked up with companion fraud bills April 29, and passed the House 240–181 on June 10; no Senate passage or enactment is recorded. H.R. 8957, the American Reserve Modernization Act, was introduced May 21 and referred to committee. H.R. 3633 received the May 14 Senate Banking committee vote described above. The GENIUS Act was inherited 2025 law; this window contains implementation proposals rather than passage of the act itself.

The in-window judicial record includes the March 31 Peterson II turnover judgment; the May 5 Cantero remand judgment; the May 13 Banco San Juan Internacional decision affirming that the Federal Reserve Act does not require a Reserve Bank to maintain an account; the May 22 Cantero certiorari petition; the May 29 extension of the Custodia petition deadline to July 11; and the June 19 limited remand in NTEU v. Vought. A judgment ordering or permitting a step is not recorded here as proof that a later transfer or operational change occurred.

Consumer agreements and fraud-payment controls

BofA’s March 23 online agreement still lacked arbitration; its May 18 revision inserted a broad arbitration section covering the enumerated digital-payment services and applications for online credentials. Truist revised its arbitration language April 8. PNC’s May 31 language extended to affiliates, predecessors, and assets of failed banks. These are contract changes, not statutes.

The fraud-payment record includes the April 15 announcement of H.R. 8312, an April 22 GSA routine-use notice permitting specified records to be shared with Treasury, the April 29 markup, the June 10 House vote, and Treasury’s June 17 fraud.gov system-of-record notice. The task-force reports due during the same period remain unpublished in the retrieved record.
