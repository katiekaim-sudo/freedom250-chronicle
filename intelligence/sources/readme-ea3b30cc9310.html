Major Bank Digital Transition Map — 2026

Governing question

How are the largest banks and systemically important incumbent asset managers rebuilding themselves for tokenized money, securities, custody, collateral and settlement—and which parts are research, announced construction, pilot, bounded operation, recurring production or demonstrated scale and recovery?

Controlling answer

The transition is not a single race toward “being on blockchain.” Incumbents are choosing different constitutional positions:

  • bank-money orchestrators issue or mirror deposits and connect them to existing account, payment and liquidity systems;
  • custody and record bridges preserve authoritative books while adding wallets, token mirrors and multi-network servicing;
  • capital-markets manufacturers issue bonds, funds or receipts and automate lifecycle events;
  • regulated public-chain issuers put fund shares or e-money claims directly on public networks; and
  • research-and-access institutions prepare clients for digital assets without yet showing a proprietary operating rail.

The strongest organizations are therefore not simply those with the most products. They are those that can join an exact legal claim, authoritative record, client route, funded cash leg, collateral treatment, correction power and failure path. No reviewed institution publicly proves that whole chain at recurring scale under stress.

This is a purpose-built watch cohort, not a ranking of size, safety, importance or investment merit.

Authority boundary

Institution-authored research establishes the institution's analysis and strategy framing. A press release establishes the launch or transaction the institution reports. Neither alone establishes every legal, customer, volume, finality, accounting, failure or recovery claim.

The package keeps the bank parent, issuing bank, regulated subsidiary, asset manager, custodian, network, CSD, transfer agent and settlement-money issuer separate. It does not infer group control from a partnership or consortium, does not treat a roadmap as operation, and does not convert gross notional into economic value or native-token value capture.

Wave 4 rechecked the original fifteen institutions against current official product, newsroom, filing and transaction surfaces. Wave 5 corrected the cohort omission by adding Fidelity as the sixteenth institution with the same seven-surface evidence test, then separated Fidelity's workplace retirement recordkeeping and trust roles from its digital-asset issuer and custody roles. The retirement-rails synthesis then separates fund investment exposure, the fund-share register, the plan trust's holding, the participant-benefit record and the contribution/settlement/distribution cash rails. It records the current live fund-level DLT models without treating them as proof of workplace-plan or participant-ledger migration. All three preserve the rule that a scoped negative is not proof of inactivity.
