1. What this astrology is

It is a language for reading meaning in time. The sky is a clock with symbols on its face. When you cast a chart you are asking one question: what is the shape of this moment — what is being asked of the people living in it?

That is a claim about meaning, not about mechanism. Nobody here thinks Saturn reaches down and closes a bank. The claim is that the same archetypal weather shows up in the sky and in the world at once, and that reading one helps you see the other — the way a good metaphor doesn't cause anything but lets you notice what was already true.

Mundane astrology is the branch that reads the collective: nations, governments, money, war, law, the mood of a people. It is the oldest branch — it was practiced for kings and cities for a thousand years before anyone cast a chart for a private person. It deals in governments and gold, not inner journeys. When Jupiter is exalted in the house of the nation's wealth, the question is not "how do I feel about abundance." It is who is the steward of the treasure, and are they wise or are they gorging.

The reading is bold. A chart is not a hedge. It is an unrepeatable whole, and its irreducibility is a feature — it is why it resists being flattened into a number. The math underneath must be flawless; the reading built on top is decisive, concrete, and never timid. Level 1 is where you are allowed to be magnificent (see §6).

2. What a chart actually IS

(Do not skip this. Everything downstream says "the chart" as if you already know. Here is the thing itself.)

A chart is a 360° circle — a map of the sky over one place at one moment. Not a picture of the heavens as they look, but a picture of what time it is, written in symbols. You pick a moment (the Sun entering Aries; a bill signed at 1:40 p.m.) and a place (Washington D.C.), and the chart freezes the whole sky over that spot as a flat wheel.

Everything in that wheel is measured as a position on the circle — 0° to 360°, starting at the spring equinox. That's all a "degree" ever means here. Two planets 90° apart on that circle are in square; a planet at 21.98° of Cancer is at a specific address on the rim.

The circle is not one thing. It is five layers stacked on the same axis — this is Huber's model, and it is the clearest picture of a chart anyone has drawn:

Embedded reference: The Chart — Five Layers.svg

Read from the bottom up, it is how the moment reaches the world. Read from the top down, it is what the moment is for:

Layer Huber's name (natal) What it is here (mundane)
Central core the soul the SOVEREIGN WILL — the free agent standing in the middle of it all, who may answer the chart rather than merely suffer it. In mundane work: a people's sovereignty — the collective's power to choose its own course
Aspects unconscious motivation the plot — who is fighting, helping, fusing. The structure of the moment, and the layer meaning actually lives in
Planets psychological drives the actors — ten motivations, ten gods with wants
Signs inherited qualities the toolshed — what equipment each actor has to hand
Houses environmental conditions the stage — where in the life of the nation it lands

⚠️ The core is NOT the root (DR-026). Get this right — it is the difference between a chart and a cage.

The root is a structural fact about the chart: where the dispositor chain bottoms out (§7.3). It lives down in the planets and signs, and it tells you the one story the placements all serve. It is the deepest thing in the wheel.

The core is not in the wheel at all. It is the soul — the sovereign, the one who is free to decide. The chart draws the field of forces; the core is what may answer them. A people can be handed a hard sky and choose well, or handed an easy one and squander it. The chart is not fate. It is the question put to a free will.

This is precisely why Kepler's caveat sits in this doc: aspects are not causal — they become "effective" only when someone is inwardly moved by them. The core is what does the moving. Take the core out and the chart collapses into a fate-machine, and every reading becomes an excuse.

And in mundane work this is not a technicality — it is the whole reason this project exists. The chart shows what history is asking of a people. Whether they answer it as sovereigns or as subjects is not in the chart. A 250-year-old republic arguing over whether it still governs itself is a nation with a live core, being asked a hard question. The sky sets the question. The people answer.

Why the order matters. Most readers work from the bottom — "Mercury is in the 10th house" — and stop there. That is the outermost, most superficial layer: the conditions on the ground. The deeper you climb, the closer you get to what the moment means — the houses tell you where, the aspects tell you what is happening, the root tells you what it all serves — and at the very top sits the one thing the chart cannot draw: the freedom to respond.

> "Sense can only be understood from the context, not by listing and describing the individual parts." — Huber. That one sentence is the whole method. A list of placements is not a reading. The relations are the meaning.

The four angles. The local horizon and meridian create four hinges — AC (ascendant, the eastern horizon: the face, the body, how it presents), DC (the western horizon: the other, the open enemy, the treaty partner), MC (midheaven, the ecliptic's upper-meridian intersection: the government, the standing, the visible office), IC (the opposite ecliptic point on the lower meridian: the land, the roots, the home ground). The MC/IC are not ordinarily the literal zenith/nadir. Angular planets — sitting on a hinge — shout. That is why the casting place matters so much: change the place and you change the angles, and the same sky says something different about a different nation. Earth's daily rotation moves this local frame; Earth's yearly revolution gives the Sun its roughly one-degree-per-day zodiacal progress. Philadelphia gives the US its natal angles; Washington gives the nation its mundane weather.

Whole Sign houses — this vault's convention. The house boundaries are the sign boundaries: the sign on the ascendant is the whole 1st house, the next sign is the whole 2nd, and so on. Clean, ancient, no argument about cusps.

Kepler's caveat, which Huber quotes and which this project takes seriously: aspects are not physically causal. They are geometric relationships that become "effective" only when someone is inwardly moved by them. The chart is not a fate-machine. It is a resonance field — it shows what the moment can be tuned into.

Four things, and they answer four different questions. Katie's ontology, which everything else in this vault rests on:

The signs — the toolshed

The sign a planet stands in tells you what tools he has, not how important he is. Dignity is the quality of his equipment, not the size of his role. A planet in fall can still command the whole chart; a planet exalted can be an idle guest.

  • Domicile / exaltation — he is at home, working with his own good tools.
  • Detriment / fall — he is working with the wrong tools. He still wants what he wants; he will just get it a stranger way.

The houses — the stage (the mundane meanings)

The house is where in the life of the nation the story lands. In mundane work the twelve are the anatomy of a country:

3b. Phase is universal — the clock with a hundred hands

(Katie's ruling, 2026-07-12. DECISION LOG → DR-020. Her words: "Everything is in a phase of everything at all times. All planets are in some phase from every synodic cycle. Every planet is in a phase from their homes, from their exaltations, from previous aspects. It's a never-ending clock with 100 layers and hands.")

She is right — and that is a problem as much as a truth. Ten planets make 45 synodic pairs. Add each planet's phase from its domicile and its exaltation, and its phase against its own natal place, and any given moment is about 150 true statements. A reading that names them all is §8.3 — the address trap — at cosmic scale.

So the rule has two halves, and you need both.

The grammar — every cycle, whatever it runs between, has the same three fields
Seed the conjunction, 0°. The thing is planted.
Fruit the opposition, 180°. The thing is fully lit — and therefore fully seen.
Direction waxing (0→180): building toward the fruit. waning (180→360): distributing it, dying back toward the next seed.

Eight phases, Rudhyar's lunation cycle applied to any pair: New 0–45 · Crescent 45–90 · First Quarter 90–135 · Gibbous 135–180 · Full 180–225 · Disseminating 225–270 · Last Quarter 270–315 · Balsamic 315–360.

That is the entire vocabulary. Any phase, of any kind, can now be said in one sentence-shape: what cycle, what phase, which direction.

Motion is the invariant — relationships carry arcs

(Katie's ruling, 2026-08-30. DECISION LOG → DR-068.)

Planets retain their archetypal motivations; they do not receive fixed plot assignments. Their relationships carry arcs. A Planet may perform several jobs at once, one storyline may recruit several relationships, and one pair may operate across several stories. Pair-to-story use is always many-to-many and must be earned in the actual chart and factual comparison.

Keep four identities separate:

  1. Planetary function — the actor's enduring motivation.
  2. Relationship identity — the stable pair and its enduring question.
  3. Relationship state — the pair's phase, direct Aspect, orb, and immediate motion at one exact chart snapshot.
  4. Relationship history — the ordered sequence of entries, perfections, separations, reapplications, phase crossings, and exact passes through time.

A chart is a frame; the relationship history is the film. The direct Aspect is the present scene, the synodic phase locates that scene within the longer relationship arc, and immediate motion says how the scene is being delivered. Retrogradation can reverse the directed hand, reopen an Aspect, or return the pair across a phase boundary. It cannot rewind chronological history. Every later state and exact pass has its own identity and carries what happened between encounters.

Ingress authority and relationship continuity are separate clocks. Aries may open the fresh mundane year and another cardinal ingress may lawfully succeed its governor, but neither event starts or resets a synodic relationship. The new frame meets every pair already in motion.

Plotlines are relationship braids

(Katie's ruling, 2026-08-30. DECISION LOG → DR-069.)

Several planetary relationships may be correct at the same time because they are doing different jobs in the same story. That is a braid, not a pile of significators. Correctness is role-specific and must survive the factual comparison; multiple plausible symbolic fits do not become extra votes.

Name each strand before interpreting it:

  • lead mechanism — the relationship that best describes the tested change;
  • co-mechanism — a distinct relationship doing another necessary job;
  • supporting context — inherited weather that conditions the development;
  • outcompeted — a plausible candidate the chronology or facts did not favor;
  • unresolved — still genuinely undecidable at the stated cutoff.

Also name how the strand is present: inherited substrate (the longer cycle continues), active dialogue (the pair is in a direct Aspect now), or seed echo (the chart contacts the conjunction family without recreating the pair's direct geometry). These modes may coexist, but they are not interchangeable.

Read the braid as a delta through time. Ask what continued, what entered or left direct dialogue, what crossed a phase boundary, what separated and reapplied, and what this chart newly foregrounded. A useful first hypothesis is often: which long relationship supplies the historical condition, which faster relationship carries the present exchange, and which governed lunation or eclipse makes it visible? This is a test structure, not a mandatory hierarchy.

Conjunction and persona charts remain subordinate seed dossiers. They may clarify a family's originating condition; they do not replace the continuing synodic history, rescue a miss, or add a convergence vote.

The four clocks

  1. Synodic — every pair of bodies, measured from the slower to the faster, so waxing always means "since the last conjunction."
  2. Dignity — Katie's layer, and the one that pays. A planet stands in a phase from its own exaltation. Saturn is exalted in Libra, so Saturn in Aries is the FULL phase of its own exaltation: the same want, the wrong tools, at maximum illumination. It is not a demotion. It is the harvest of the thing, seen from the far side. Saturn stress-tests before it goes home. Run it as two layers — the sign is the standing condition (Whole Sign; years long, no single day owns it), the degree is the exact hand (traditional exaltation degrees) and it perfects on a date you can compute. Condition and trigger — the same ladder as §4. Classical bodies only: Uranus, Neptune and Pluto have no traditional dignity, and we do not invent one. The workbook Dignities tab lists modern outer domiciles as a lookup. That is not this method. phases.py refuses them on purpose.
  3. Return — a transiting body against its own natal place. Conjunction = the return; opposition = the halfway house.
  4. Aspect-cycle — applying or separating, and which pass of a multi-pass transit. A transit is almost never one date: it hits, stations, comes back.

The discipline — what you may NAME (this is the load-bearing half)

Compute all of it. Narrate only what earns it. A phase may be spoken when any of these hold:

  • root — its cycle involves the chart's root planet(s) or the chart ruler (§7.3, one layer up). If the terminus is a loop, every member is the root — pass them all to phases.py;
  • era — both bodies are outer (Uranus, Neptune, Pluto): they set the age whatever the root says, and DR-003 already binds you to read them as cycle seeds;
  • exact — the phase sits within 1° of a perfection against a US natal point.

Everything else is background: true, computed, and not to be narrated. This is Katie's fence, in her words: "the more the better, but note it so other Claudes don't get confused with the bloat." If you find yourself naming a background row, stop — you have left the method and are listing addresses.

The aspects — the dialogue

Two gods in conversation. The aspect is the primary structure of a chart — the plot. Name who is talking to whom, not just the glyph. Silence is a finding: actors with no major aspect do not confer directly in this frame. Their synodic relationship still continues as background or between chapters under its own narration gate.

Workbook orbs (the drawer, not a flat 6°): conjunction 8° · sextile 5° · square 7° · trine 7° · opposition 8°. Read declination on every chart (parallel ≈ conjunction; contra-parallel ≈ opposition). Greer binaries (Sun/Moon, Venus/Mars, Jupiter/Saturn, Uranus/Neptune) are the usual political braid — if a pair is silent, say so.

  • Conjunction (0°) — fusion. They become one motive. The most powerful and the most ambiguous.
  • Opposition (180°) — confrontation, and therefore awareness. Two truths facing each other across a table.
  • Square (90°) — friction that forces action. The most productive hard aspect; nothing moves without it.
  • Trine (120°) — flow, ease, the open channel. Also laziness — what comes free is not always earned.
  • Sextile (60°) — the offered opportunity. It must be taken up.

Hard aspects are not "bad." They are where the work happens. Sun–Neptune in hard aspect is the single most dangerous configuration in mundane work (Greer): the leader dissolved, the crown made of fog, the office that cannot be seen clearly. It runs through every governing ingress of this chronicle.

4. The unit of mundane time — the ladder

This is the biggest structural fact in the whole method. Mundane astrology does not work off "today's transits." It works off a nested ladder, and you never skip a rung:

INGRESS      the season&#x27;s CLIMATE — the standing question
   ↓          (cardinal ingress, cast for Washington D.C., Whole Sign)
LUNATION     the month&#x27;s WEATHER — what fires, and when
   ↓          (New Moon seeds · Full Moon reveals and harvests)
ECLIPSE      the SUPERCHARGE — a lunation with a long fuse

The ingress — the moment the Sun enters a cardinal sign. Aries asks the year's question; it does not always own the year. Greer's validity rule keys off the rising sign's modality: Fixed rising = the full year; Cardinal rising = 6 months; Mutable rising = 3 months, then the next cardinal takes over. Cast it for D.C. That is the chart that governs for as long as it is valid. Everything else is commentary on it.

Ingress succession rule (Katie, 2026-08-30): Aries always opens a fresh mundane year, regardless of the prior ingress. Cancer, Libra, and Capricorn take over only when the currently governing ingress has completed the term allowed by its rising sign: Mutable = three months, so the next cardinal ingress takes over; Cardinal = six months, so the following cardinal ingress does not; Fixed = the year, subject to Aries's absolute annual reset. A pre-ingress New Moon may govern the opening lunar month across a seam, and synodic cycles continue without interruption, but neither changes which ingress lawfully governs.

The lunations — the New and Full Moons inside that season are the triggers. They don't introduce new stories; they time the one the ingress already set. A New Moon plants; the Full Moon lights it up and shows you what grew. The New Moon that governs the first weeks can sit before the ingress — days, not always one day (2026 Aries: 19 March, one day; 2026 Cancer: 15 June, six days). DR-021 still holds: that New Moon governs until the next New Moon. (Pessin: every story seeds at a New Moon and runs a roughly nine-month gestation to its harvest. Lunations group into Moon Families by degree band and by 273-day lineage — a subplot whose events cluster on one family is keeping time with it, and that's a story thread.) A lunation that does not hit the ingress (conjunction, opposition, square) is Greer-quiet. That is not “nothing happened.” Check the locked US natal before you call the month empty. An eclipse is still a New or Full Moon — name it as a solar or lunar eclipse, not as an ordinary lunation.

The eclipses — a lunation with the volume turned up and a fuse that burns for years. Keep three genealogies separately typed. A Saros return repeats the same evolving shadow-geometry series after 223 lunations; a Metonic return brings the same lunar phase back near the same calendar date and zodiac degree after 235 lunations, but may belong to a different Saros or may not be an eclipse at all; a Moon Family carries one New-Moon seed through its 273/546/819-day chapters. The Cancer Eclipse Spine is this vault's best Metonic finding: eight dollar-order events seeded by 24–30° Cancer eclipses landing on the US Moon–Pluto. The next strike is 2028-07-22. See The Shadow Has More Than One Ancestor — Saros, Metonic and Moon-Family Time.

A "chart for today" has no governing standing. It never replaces the ladder. The one sanctioned daily experiment is the Daybreak Almanac below: a subordinate hand-off frame, not a fourth governor.

4a. Daybreak Almanac — the daily hand-off experiment (DR-066)

Daybreak asks a narrower question than the governing method: how is the already-active hierarchy delivered from one morning threshold to the next? For each 2026 civil date, cast one Washington, D.C. Whole Sign frame at the exact geocentric Sun–Ascendant conjunction. The solar disc centre is used, refraction is disabled, and the solved Sun–ASC residual must be no more than 0.01 arcsecond. This frame owns its displayed houses. It does not govern the day, introduce a new storyline, or overrule an ingress or lunation.

Read it only in this order:

  1. The Long Clocks hold the era-level synodic geometry.
  2. The registered ingress holds the standing climate.
  3. The most recent registered New Moon holds the chapter, even when it opened just before an ingress. A later Full Moon or eclipse is a separately named phase strike, not a replacement chapter.
  4. Daybreak names the hand-off: exact Ascendant and MC, ruler and dispositor root, internal aspects, parent-chart contacts, ten factual clock hands, and active seed echoes.
  5. The Chronicle record is joined afterward. An exact-timestamp record is assigned to the Sun–ASC interval that actually contains it; a date-only record remains visibly assigned by civil date.

The experiment keeps two lanes separate. 2026-07-14 through 2026-08-15 is retrospective calibration because the record already existed. 2026-08-16 through 2026-09-14 is a 30-frame prospective watch whose factual cores and hashes were frozen on 2026-08-15 before the first interval opened. Grade a closed interval only as Lines up, Partial, Miss, Counterevidence, or Unresolved. Quiet, unchanged, failed, reversed, and contradictory days all count as evidence. There is no hit rate in the interface, no storyline score, no automatic story match, and no claim that the chart caused an event.

Lunation method rulings (DR-021 through DR-023)

The New Moon governs until the next New Moon (DR-021). The lunations after it (First Quarter, Full Moon, Last Quarter) are detail within that month. Bin NM-to-NM as the primary story-month; the ±3-day save-assignment is a finer layer inside it.

Full Moon = read BOTH ways (DR-022). The Moon-end landing on a natal point is one reading; the axis/directional reading ("the people delivering something to the struck point") is the other. Both are live. Surfacing that ambiguity is exactly the kind of astrological thinking the method asks for.

No Sabian analysis on quarter moons (DR-023). Sabians only for New Moon charts, Full Moon charts, and eclipses. The Moon moves ~13° per day — a quarter's degree symbolism is unreliable. Always compute the EXACT lunation moment (not noon-of-day) before any degree work.

5. How the astrology works inside Freedom 250

This matters as much as the technique — it is why the astrology is here at all.

It runs in parallel with the events; it does not filter them. The event layer (one note per bookmark, verbatim, plotline-tagged) must stand on its own and be defensible to someone who thinks astrology is nonsense. Nothing is captured because the sky said so. So the defensible claim is archetypal and era-level, not "this transit causes this event on Thursday." The bias is deliberate, and it is not a licence to be sloppy. Katie's lens is warm, opinionated, populist-right — on purpose. The discipline is that bias informs what you look at and how you say it, never what you are willing to see. Hold both poles even when you like one of them better.

6b. Comparison integrity — chart, record, comparison

(Katie's ruling, 2026-08-15. DECISION LOG → DR-063.)

A Storyline Comparison is a three-pass join, and the passes never rewrite one another:

  1. Chart reading: compute the bones, write the structural whole, follow it through the weather, and hold both poles. For a prospective test, freeze this text before its evidence window. If the text is written after the window, label the result retrospective calibration; if only part was frozen in advance, identify the exact prospective subtests.
  2. Record: capture the Chronicle independently of the astrology. Keep Attention (what Katie saved), Official (what was announced, proposed, approved, effective, argued, decided, or operating), and Entity (founding and computed phase state) in separate lanes. One lane cannot silently stand in for another.
  3. Comparison: join the dated record to the frozen reading with stable finding IDs. Every finding receives one disposition — Lines up, Partial, Miss, or Unresolved — and links to its exact evidence object where one exists. A date route is navigation; it is not an event identity.

A rhyme is not a cause. Co-occurrence, shared language, and timing may make a disciplined comparison finding; none proves that the sky caused an event. Chart comparisons receive no score. A literal prediction still belongs in the Forecast Ledger before its window under DR-004.

The Attention lane is qualitative (DR-083). Saved X posts are social commentary, and Katie often saves several perspectives on the same story. The number of saved notes therefore measures nothing about the world: Attention can show which stories were circulating and when, never how much happened. Volume tables, daily peaks and plotline counts are inventory only; a comparison may not use them as evidence, and may not call a chart's fit weak because it doesn't match the most-saved subject.

The editable Markdown remains the live reading authority. Its frozen baseline is archival comparison evidence, never a second live interpretation. Later prose may improve, but the app must show any drift and keep the original comparison copy intact. A factual correction is amended; it does not silently replace the baseline.

An open watch contains no findings waiting to be confirmed. Evidence enters the record first; findings are written only after their declared window. Close the comparison after the governing climate actually expires. A later non-Aries ingress may be a nested subframe without replacing a still-valid six-month or fixed-rising governor; Aries is never nested and always opens the fresh mundane year.

6c. Authority in motion — the State Transition Watch

(Katie's ruling, 2026-08-15. DECISION LOG → DR-064.)

Freedom 250 readings now carry a disciplined state-transition perspective: who has the legitimate right to decide here, by what authority, and is that right actually changing state? This is the thesis's governing question, not a required conclusion. Hear the chart first. Do not force the chart to announce a revolution merely because the project is studying revolutionary times.

For each prospective chart, add a separately frozen State Transition Watch after the living reading. It must name:

  1. the decision right in motion;
  2. its current holder and the claimant or constraint pressing it;
  3. a precise candidate from → toward transition;
  4. the named mechanism by which the change could occur;
  5. the state to distinguish — proposed, authorized, effective, operating, contested, reversed, failed, or unchanged;
  6. the constructive pole, shadow pole, and continuity / unchanged case;
  7. the Freedom 250 arcs that would need to synchronize for a system-level transition; and
  8. a falsifiable evidence test across Attention, Official, and Entity.

The chart role controls the scale. An ingress names the climate of authority; a lunation names a gate through which it may move; an eclipse names a possible discontinuity; an entity clock tests whether the institution itself changed legal or operating state. A proposal is not authorization, authorization is not effectiveness, and effectiveness is not operation. Never collapse the state ladder to make a rhyme look mature.

The watch is not appended into an already frozen reading baseline. It is a timestamped, immutable, hash-pinned addendum bound to that baseline's reading ID and SHA-256. Later Storyline Comparisons test the named transition rather than searching the record for a matching headline. Unchanged, failed, reversed, Miss, and Unresolved are valid results. A rhyme remains not a cause, and the watch receives no score.

The reusable output shape is 99 - Templates/Chart reading authoring template.md. The governing sequence is Chart → Authority → Candidate transition → Mechanism → Maturity → Counterpole → Evidence test.

7. How to read — the method

  1. Compute the bones first. Positions, dignities, houses, aspects, the chart ruler. Never from memory (§9). This is the one thing you can be 100% right about — get it perfect, then build.
  2. Planets as a cast that keeps its colors. Don't average them into gray; don't stack them as parallel keyword blocks. They blend like colors into a new hue.
  3. ★ TAKE IT TO THE ROOT. This is the master move. Trace every planet up its dispositor chain — planet → ruler of its sign → that ruler's sign-ruler → … — until it bottoms out in a final dispositor or a loop. That terminus is the chart's gravity well: the one story every planet ultimately serves. A loop is a circle of service, not a vacancy. If the terminus is a loop, every member is the root — name them all; phases.py must receive them all. Complicated but simple — ten placements, one root (or one closed circuit). Name the root, then read everything as service to it.
  4. Read motivation, not location. "Mercury in fall in the 10th" is an address, not a reading. Ask what the god wants, how he is equipped to get it, and what his role in the whole story is. Condition modifies the how; it never replaces the what and why.
  5. Use the myth for the register. Not "Mercury detriment" but "the herald reading the proclamation backward in a hall of mirrors." The god-stories are in Barz — Gods and Planets Study Notes §6. That is what makes a reading seen instead of computed.
  6. Both poles, short, alive. A few vivid paragraphs that hold many truths at once and resolve them to the root. Not an essay. Not a checklist.
  7. Then follow the root down into the lunations — the climate into the weather.

8. The four traps (every one of these cost a real conversation)

Now that you know what the thing is, here is how it gets broken. All four are ways of trading meaning for something that feels safer.

  1. The daily-chart trap. Mundane is not daily transits. Work the ladder (§4). A chart for today has no governing standing; the DR-066 Daybreak experiment may test delivery only after the hierarchy is fixed.
  2. The score trap. A number throws away the thing that makes it astrology. Katie: "I wanted to go through the mundane stuff, not get a score." No scores. Ever. (The Ledger scores calls, not charts — §5, level 3.) If you feel the pull to quantify, that pull is the mistake.
  3. The address trap — the big one. Listing where the planets are is not a reading. Motivation, not location (§7.4).
  4. The flat-doom trap. Reading only the shadow is its own amputation. Every symbol carries light and shadow by design. Hold both poles; let the rest of the chart decide which one is called forth.
