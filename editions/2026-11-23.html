US new-business applications stay above 500,000 for a seventh month

US businesses are forming at an unprecedented rate:

There were 501,609 US new business applications in October.

This marks the 7th consecutive month with business formation above 500,000.

This is also more than double the 235,730 recorded in April 2020, during the pandemic.

Since January 2025, new business applications have averaged 495,666 per month.

By comparison, the 2018-2019 pre-pandemic average stood at 292,344, or 41% lower.

The rapid adoption of AI is making it easier and cheaper to launch new businesses.

US entrepreneurial activity is skyrocketing.

The US has 5,159 data centers, more than the next nine countries

BREAKING: The United States now has 5,159 data centers, more than the next 9 countries with the most data centers combined.

This includes operational facilities as well as projects that are planned, under construction, or land-banked.

The US also accounts for 38.5% of the 13,384 facilities listed worldwide.

The UK and Germany rank next, with 608 and 583, respectively.

At the same time, China, France, and India have 488, 402, and 360 data centers, respectively.

Among US states, Virginia leads with 728 data centers, followed by Texas and Georgia with 605 and 308, respectively.

America is at the forefront of the global AI infrastructure expansion.
