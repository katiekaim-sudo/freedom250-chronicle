The Driverless — 6 legacy filings

One military counter-drone acquisition record and five civilian road-vehicle oversight, exemption and standards records share this legacy view but not an owning question.

The Airspace — 10 legacy filings

Ten civilian flight, spectrum, export, fixed-site and powered-lift permission records remain under transport and communications owners.

No record is admitted to W12 as owning research. Military or security relevance is contextual; rulemaking, permission, spectrum, export and demonstrated operation retain separate clocks.

The High Ground — 3 legacy filings

Three space records remain under the NSSTS science and company answer; Tech 63 carries a secondary military-space overlap while Tech 64–65 remain commercial-launch and Mars-science records.

The whole legacy group is not a Military Modernization package. Prototype goals, commercial authorization and a science agreement remain distinct from fielding, launch or accepted operation.

The Reactor — 13 legacy filings

Explore nuclear executive orders, reactor pilots, NRC rules, microreactors, fusion, fees and ownership requirements.

Fission and fusion follow different regulatory paths; an order, pilot or rulemaking does not establish an operating reactor.
