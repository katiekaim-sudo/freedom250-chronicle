Congress's push to check identity and risk before federal money goes out

Over 17 months the House Oversight Committee built a case for screening people, accounts and payments before Treasury pays. The July 15, 2026 hearing pushed that toward constant identity monitoring, but none of it is law: the key bills passed only the House.

What we found

  • The thread runs from the Feb 12, 2025 'War on Waste' hearing, to 'pay and chase is not the solution' (Mar 11, 2025), to Treasury named 'the last barrier before payment is sent out' (Jan 13, 2026).
  • The House passed H.R. 8312 (permanent Treasury fraud analytics and a fraud inspector general) 240–181 on June 10, 2026, with bills for pre-payment screening (H.R. 8463), Treasury power to pause or return payments (H.R. 8464) and rolling risk checks (H.R. 8467). The Senate has not acted.
  • July 15 witnesses described reused fraud setups: one phone number tied to 987 applications and 207 synthetic identities across 25 states. GSA documented in May 2025 that suspected fraud accounts passed Login.gov's strongest identity check.
  • Executive orders 14243 and 14249 (March 2025) opened agency data and pushed payment files to Treasury; EO 14395 (Mar 16, 2026) set up a Vice President–led task force with 30/60/90-day deadlines. The timing lines up with the House calendar, but the deadline deliverables are not public.
  • Numbers need care: GAO's $233–$521 billion is a modeled yearly range for FY2018–FY2022, not today's total. Treasury's official Death Master File pilot figure is $31 million in five months, against a vendor-cited $113.5 million.

Why it matters

This is the thesis on the payment side: a standing inspection layer is being placed between application and payment, using Treasury, existing payment systems and executive orders rather than a new agency. The open question is whether a risk flag starts a review or becomes the verdict.

What to watch

  • pending · Senate action on H.R. 8312 and the companion payment-control bills.
  • pending · Public release of the EO 14395 agency fraud maps, minimum anti-fraud rules and implementation plans (due April 15, May 15 and June 14, 2026).
  • pending · Whether any enacted text gives flagged people notice, human review, appeal and correction.

Research summary

The hearing supplied a front-end identity and continuing-risk lens for an emerging federated person, provider, account and payment-risk architecture. It did not create a national digital ID, enact continuous monitoring, change payment law or prove vendor efficacy; the House payment-control bills remained House-passed and not enacted at the cutoff.
