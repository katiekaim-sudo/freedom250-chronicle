Mars–Saturn Cycle Review

Enduring question: How is force disciplined, blocked, burdened, or made durable?
Disposition: consistently strong; 2026 is a primary pilot.

Family record

  • 2024 Pisces family: candidate +328.160 days.
  • 2026 Aries family: candidate +342.837 days.

What the charts add

The 2024 seed fuses Mars and Saturn in Pisces/7th. The candidate separates fallen Mars into Cancer/10th and Saturn into Pisces/6th. Public action and institutional labor/procedure are no longer the same job.

The 2026 seed is stark: domiciled Mars and fallen Saturn together in Aries/7th, with Mercury within 0.757°. The candidate places retrograde Mars in Leo/1st and Saturn still fallen in Aries/9th. The chart develops Grand Trine/Kite structure. Embodied force, command/legal doctrine, and the record carrying them separate clearly.

Narrowing use

For Iran, borders, enforcement, or institutional conflict, ask whether action is authorized, constrained, supplied, and made durable. The chart can distinguish command from force; it cannot prove an authorization or operation.
