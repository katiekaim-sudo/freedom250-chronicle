Hawaii bribery case: official records trace the money only part way

Official records show a $170.4 million COVID bridge appropriation, an existing testing partnership, campaign contributions and a July 2026 state indictment. They do not yet link a donor, an official act and a payment into one proven chain.

What we found

  • A state indictment was filed July 24, 2026 in Hawaii's First Circuit (case 1CPC-26-0000936) naming Tobi Solidum, Sylvia Luke, Ryan Yamane, Ford Fuchigami and Leodoloff Asuncion Jr. Its first page lists conspiracy to commit bribery and bribery counts. The full official text was not recovered; these are charges, not findings of guilt.
  • Act 5 (HB 2392), signed April 12, 2022, gave the Department of Health $170,433,565 over two years for COVID costs and named no vendor. The House version carried only a $12 million vaccination request; the larger testing and staffing amount entered in the Senate in March 2022.
  • DOH's worksheet showed $79.8 million in community-testing costs and $119.9 million in surge staffing, less about $29.3 million from FEMA reimbursement and a budget release. DOH's testing partner, the National Kidney Foundation of Hawaii, was in place about six months before the bill.
  • Campaign data shows $5,000 each from Tobi Solidum and Kristen Pae to Friends of Sylvia Luke (January 21, 2022 entries) and a $5,000 refund to Solidum in March 2022. A Campaign Spending Commission complaint (Docket 26-05) was on the July 8, 2026 agenda; its outcome was not found.
  • Earlier federal cases are separate and closed: former legislators J. Kalani English and Ty Cullen pleaded guilty in 2022 to honest-services wire fraud and were sentenced to 40 and 24 months.

Why it matters

The law, votes and totals are public, but the contract-and-payment trail behind the emergency money is not. It tests whether the state's own records, regulators and courts can trace and correct how emergency funds moved.

What to watch

  • pending · The official indictment text and court docket entries in case 1CPC-26-0000936.
  • pending · What the Campaign Spending Commission decided on Docket 26-05.
  • pending · DOH contracts, invoices, payment records and FEMA project records for the Act 5 money.
