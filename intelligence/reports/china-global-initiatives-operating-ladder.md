# China's Global-Governance Operating Map — 2026

**Wave:** China deepening wave 7
**Evidence cutoff:** 2026-09-24
**Question:** Where have the Belt and Road Initiative and China's four global initiatives produced funded, contracted, institutional or operating objects?

## Bottom line

China's external initiatives are not one program and do not share one legal or financial machine.

- The **Belt and Road Initiative (BRI)** is a broad cooperation umbrella. Its objects can be intergovernmental memoranda, bank loans, equity investments, commercial contracts, concessions, railways, ports, industrial parks, standards arrangements or training programs. The label itself does not finance or operate a project.
- The **Global Development Initiative (GDI)** has the clearest project machinery: a CIDCA-managed project pool, an incubation/achievement distinction, capital pools, implementing partners and project reports.
- The **Global Security Initiative (GSI)** is a concept and cooperation framework. Its operating objects include diplomatic agreements, forums, training, UN-financed projects and proposed or constituted institutions, but attribution must be explicit rather than retroactive.
- The **Global Civilization Initiative (GCI)** is chiefly a dialogue and normative platform. Its clearest multilateral institutional result is the UN General Assembly's consensus creation of the International Day for Dialogue among Civilizations.
- The **Global Governance Initiative (GGI)**, proposed in September 2025, is the newest. By the cutoff it had a concept paper and a Group of Friends with joint positions, but no general authority over existing institutions and no single GGI project-finance mechanism.

The right audit chain is:

`initiative language -> host request/endorsement -> legal instrument -> finance -> procurement -> construction/delivery -> ownership/control -> operation -> output -> debt service/remedy`

An object should stop at its last evidenced stage. A memorandum is not finance; finance is not procurement; construction is not operation; operation is not economic success; debt restructuring is not project failure; Chinese participation is not proof that an object belongs to a particular initiative.

## 1. Initiative constitution

| Initiative | Proposed | Core function | Operating machinery visible by cutoff | What the label does **not** prove |
|---|---:|---|---|---|
| Belt and Road Initiative | 2013 | connectivity, trade, investment and broad cooperation platform | bilateral cooperation documents; NDRC/MOFCOM/MFA coordination; policy and commercial banks; Silk Road Fund; firms; host agencies; project contracts and concessions; Belt and Road Forum secretariat | common contract terms, Chinese state ownership, sovereign guarantee, debt sustainability, construction or operation |
| Global Development Initiative | 2021 | accelerate UN 2030 Agenda across eight priority areas | Group of Friends; CIDCA Global Development Project Pool; incubation and achievement pools; capital pool; development funds; UN/host/Chinese implementing partners | that every older aid or BRI project is a GDI project; that a project-pool entry has secured finance or delivered output |
| Global Security Initiative | 2022 | common, comprehensive, cooperative and sustainable security; dialogue and political settlement | concept paper with twenty priorities; bilateral/multilateral consultations; forums; mediation; training; UN Peace and Development Trust Fund routes named in the concept paper | alliance, mutual-defense obligation, enforcement authority, conflict settlement or operational command |
| Global Civilization Initiative | 2023 | cultural exchange, plural modernization paths and inter-civilizational dialogue | party, state, academic and cultural forums; UN International Day for Dialogue among Civilizations | common values interpretation, binding cultural rules or measured attitude change |
| Global Governance Initiative | 2025 | principles for reforming global governance—sovereign equality, international rule of law, multilateralism, people-centered approach and results | concept paper; diplomatic coordination; Group of Friends of Global Governance | UN endorsement of the whole initiative, institutional reform, voting-right change, funding or delegated authority |

## 2. Who turns labels into objects

| Actor | Actual function | Boundary |
|---|---|---|
| CPC leadership / PRC president | proposes overarching concepts and gives political direction | speech is not implementing law, budget or contract |
| State Council / MFA | diplomacy, framework documents, forums, bilateral and multilateral coordination | endorsement language is not project delivery |
| NDRC | BRI planning, policy coordination, corridor and project alignment | coordination does not make NDRC financier or owner of every project |
| MOFCOM | trade, investment, overseas cooperation zones and commercial policy | commercial statistics do not isolate initiative causality |
| CIDCA | foreign-assistance coordination and GDI project-pool management | project-pool inclusion has distinct incubation and achievement states |
| Ministry of Finance / PBOC | sovereign finance, multilateral and monetary interfaces | public policy support differs from lender credit decision |
| China EXIM / China Development Bank / commercial banks | loans and financial contracts | creditor, guarantee, collateral, governing law and restructuring route differ by contract |
| Silk Road Fund and other funds | equity/debt investment through their own mandates and agreements | commitment is not disbursement or operating control |
| Chinese SOEs/private firms | engineering, procurement, construction, supply, equity, concession or operation | “Chinese project” does not identify state direction, financing or beneficial owner |
| Host government / SOE / regulator | requests, approves, borrows, guarantees, procures, owns, regulates and/or operates | host consent does not remove domestic accountability, fiscal or environmental questions |
| UN bodies / FAO and other implementers | approve, administer, procure, monitor and report multilateral projects | Chinese funding or proposal does not displace the institution's legal ownership and procedures |
| AIIB | treaty-based multilateral development bank governed by its Articles and boards | Beijing headquarters and Chinese sponsorship do not make each AIIB loan a PRC bilateral or BRI project |

## 3. Operating-object ladder

Use these states instead of “China has a project”:

| State | Minimum evidence |
|---|---|
| **G0 — proposal/label** | leader, ministry or initiative text names the idea |
| **G1 — host alignment** | host strategy, joint statement or memorandum accepts exploration/cooperation |
| **G2 — constituted** | treaty, company, project vehicle, fund, forum secretariat or formal project-pool entry exists |
| **G3 — financed/contracted** | signed financing/contract instrument, amount, parties and conditions |
| **G4 — under delivery** | procurement, mobilization, construction, training or disbursement receipt |
| **G5 — operating** | service, facility, institution or program is actually functioning |
| **G6 — output measured** | passengers, cargo, electricity, trainees, crop yield, cases mediated or another defined output |
| **G7 — outcome/remedy** | economic/social result, debt service, renegotiation, dispute remedy, transfer or closure |

Initiative support counts are orientation leads, not a substitute for this ladder.

## 4. Selected object audits

These cases are not a global inventory. They show how different operating forms must be read.

### A. Whoosh / Jakarta–Bandung high-speed railway — BRI transport object

**State:** G5 operating; G6 has service receipts, but a complete passenger/revenue/debt series remains a return gate.

Indonesia's presidency documented use of the Whoosh service in October 2023, and the Indonesian transport ministry later described scheduled daily operations and the phased addition of Karawang station service. Chinese official BRI records also identify the railway as built and operating.

The correct object is not “China built a railway.” It is a host-authorized Indonesian project company, Chinese-Indonesian ownership/contracting and lender structure, Indonesian regulation, constructed infrastructure and an operating passenger service. Evaluation requires current ridership, fares, operating cost, debt service, subsidy/guarantee exposure, land-development revenue, safety and local economic effects.

### B. Hambantota Port — loan, concession and operation are separate clocks

**State:** original port G5; 2017 concession G3–G5; throughput and fiscal/debt outcomes require continuing measurement.

Sri Lanka's Ports Authority records that:

- the port was built with government borrowing and had accumulated losses;
- Sri Lanka and China Merchants Port entered a 99-year public-private concession in 2017;
- two Sri Lankan companies divided commercial operations and port-service/security functions;
- the agreed investment was paid in tranches; and
- Sri Lankan public authorities retained specified harbor and security functions.

This evidence does **not** support the shorthand that China automatically seized the port when a loan defaulted. The construction loans, sovereign debt, concession payment, equity/control, port operations and naval/security authority are different legal objects. The wider Sri Lankan debt crisis involved domestic debt, bonds and multiple official and commercial creditors. IMF records show later separate restructurings with China EXIM, the Official Creditor Committee, bondholders and China Development Bank.

The serious questions remain: original project appraisal, procurement, debt terms, opportunity cost, concession valuation, traffic, fiscal receipts, land/port development, creditor coordination and long-run control—not a one-sentence morality play.

### C. Uganda FAO–China South-South agriculture — GDI development object

**State:** G3 funded, G4 delivered and G6 with bounded output receipts.

FAO, Uganda and China launched the third phase in January 2023 with **US$12.6 million**: nearly US$10 million committed by Uganda and US$3 million by China through FAO trust funds. It targeted more than 9,600 beneficiaries across twenty districts through rice/millet, livestock, aquaculture, training and technology-transfer hubs.

FAO's 2025 field reporting confirms actual hybrid-rice training and harvest activity. China's 2025 GDI report adds larger acreage, household and income claims. The FAO/host record proves a real tripartite program; the Chinese report's aggregate outcomes should be reconciled to FAO monitoring and host data before reuse.

This case also shows that GDI branding can sit on a longer-running cooperation line—the FAO-China-Uganda program began before the GDI. The current phase can be operated under the GDI without rewriting the origin date of the underlying relationship.

### D. Global Development Project Pool — machinery, not one project

**State:** G2 constituted; individual entries occupy different G0–G7 states.

CIDCA's provisional guidelines distinguish:

- an **Incubation Pool**, where feasibility or finance may still be unproven; and
- an **Achievement Pool**, for approved, feasible and funded projects in execution.

CIDCA reported in July 2026 that the pool had launched more than 1,800 projects and had a digital full-lifecycle management system. That aggregate is a Chinese administrative claim. It establishes a live management structure, not 1,800 independently verified successful outcomes. Reuse requires the named project, pool state, applicant, host, funding source, approval, implementer and result.

### E. UN Peace and Development Trust Fund — multilateral operating object with attribution limits

**State:** G2–G6 across individual UN projects.

The United Nations records that China pledged US$200 million over ten years to establish the fund in 2016, later extended it through 2030, and that the fund has supported more than 200 projects. The 2024 steering committee approved twenty-two 2025 proposals totaling about US$20 million; the 2025 meeting approved nineteen proposals totaling about US$18.6 million.

This is genuine funded multilateral machinery. It predates the GDI, GSI, GCI and GGI. Later initiative papers route through or cite it, but the whole fund must not be retroactively relabeled. The UN steering, sub-fund, implementing entity, budget, status and project result control each object.

### F. Saudi–Iran Beijing agreement — GSI diplomatic object

**State:** G3 signed political agreement; implementation receipts include the April 2023 foreign-ministers' statement and restoration steps, while durable regional-security effects remain open.

The March 10, 2023 trilateral statement records Chinese hosting/sponsorship and Saudi-Iran agreement to resume diplomatic relations, reopen missions within two months, restore ambassadors and implement earlier security and economic cooperation agreements. Chinese officials explicitly presented the outcome as a GSI practice case.

It is therefore a fair example of GSI-linked mediation. It is not a Chinese guarantee, peace treaty, alliance or proof that Saudi-Iran rivalry ended. Iraq and Oman hosted earlier rounds and must remain in the causal record.

### G. International Day for Dialogue among Civilizations — GCI institutional object

**State:** G2 constituted and G5 annually observable.

On June 7, 2024 the UN General Assembly adopted by consensus Resolution 78/286 declaring June 10 the International Day for Dialogue among Civilizations. China introduced the text with multiple cosponsors. By June 2026, UN- and China-linked events were being held for the second observance.

This is a real multilateral calendar and convening result. It does not mean the General Assembly endorsed every proposition in the GCI or delegated cultural-governance authority to China.

### H. Group of Friends of Global Governance — GGI coalition object

**State:** G2 constituted diplomatic grouping; policy adoption and institutional reform remain G0–G1.

The September 2025 concept paper defines the GGI's five principles and separates its purpose from the GDI, GSI and GCI. In May 2026 China convened a Group of Friends meeting at the UN, and a February 2026 joint statement is present in the UN statement system.

That proves an operating diplomatic coalition and a joint-position channel. It does not prove UN endorsement, charter reform, voting reform, new funding or compliance by nonmembers.

### I. International Organization for Mediation — adjacent, separately constituted institution

**State:** G2 treaty institution; operating-case record remains a return gate.

The UN Treaty Collection records that the convention establishing the International Organization for Mediation was concluded in Hong Kong on May 30, 2025 and entered into force on August 29, 2025, with China as depositary. It is a genuine multilateral legal object.

Because its negotiation predates the September 2025 GGI concept paper, it should be treated as **adjacent Chinese global-governance architecture**, not silently counted as a GGI delivery unless an authoritative originating or accession text makes that attribution. Ratifications, institutional opening, budget, staff, accepted cases and outcomes are separate clocks.

## 5. Finance, debt and control

### BRI finance is plural

A BRI object may be financed by:

- a host budget or sovereign borrowing;
- China EXIM or CDB;
- a Chinese commercial bank;
- the Silk Road Fund or another equity fund;
- a Chinese or host SOE balance sheet;
- project finance or a concessionaire;
- an MDB, including AIIB or the World Bank;
- mixed or third-country finance; or
- no secured finance yet.

Therefore “BRI debt” is not one creditor class. Each claim needs borrower, lender, guarantor, currency, interest, maturity, grace period, security, governing law, project cash flow and restructuring/remedy.

### AIIB is not a Chinese bilateral bank

AIIB was championed by China and is headquartered in Beijing, but it is a treaty-based multilateral development bank with its own international legal personality, member shareholders, board and policies. Its loans should be attributed to AIIB. A project may support connectivity or be publicly associated with BRI, but that does not convert the bank's legal decision into a PRC bilateral loan.

### “Debt trap” and “no debt problem” are both inadequate defaults

The World Bank found that BRI transport corridors can reduce travel time and raise trade and income, while warning that gains depend on complementary reforms and that debt, procurement, corruption, environmental and social risks can be material. Some projects may be highly valuable; others may impose net welfare costs.

Sri Lanka and Zambia demonstrate that Chinese claims can enter real sovereign-debt restructurings. China EXIM reached final amended agreements with Sri Lanka, while China co-chaired Zambia's official creditor committee with France. These are operating creditor-coordination receipts. They neither absolve original lending/project choices nor support a theory that every Chinese loan is designed to seize an asset.

## 6. How this reaches the U.S.–China relationship

The U.S. policy question is not whether China “wins” a country. It is which objects change choices or dependencies:

| Object | Relationship question |
|---|---|
| port, rail, grid or digital network | who finances, builds, owns, operates, maintains, supplies data/equipment and controls interruption? |
| sovereign or SOE loan | what fiscal space, collateral, restructuring and diplomatic coordination follow? |
| standards or training platform | which technical practices, vendors and professional networks become durable? |
| mediation or security forum | does it create communication, agreement, monitoring, enforcement or only convening? |
| development project | are host priorities, procurement, outputs and independent evaluation visible? |
| UN coalition/resolution | what text was adopted, by whom, with what legal effect and later voting behavior? |

U.S. alternatives should be compared on speed, financing terms, local capability, transparency, safeguards, operating reliability and host choice—not only on rhetoric or whether a project bears a Chinese label.

## 7. Claim audit

| Claim | Ruling | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| “BRI is a treaty organization.” | **False** | it is a cooperation umbrella implemented through many separate legal and financial objects |
| “A BRI memorandum means China will fund the project.” | **False** | host alignment, feasibility, finance, procurement and delivery are separate states |
| “AIIB loans are Chinese bilateral loans.” | **False** | AIIB is a treaty-based MDB with separate legal personality and governance |
| “Hambantota proves China seizes assets when borrowers default.” | **Unsupported by the cited transaction record** | construction loans, Sri Lankan fiscal distress, the later concession payment/equity and sovereign restructurings are separate objects |
| “Chinese overseas lending has no debt-sustainability consequence.” | **False** | borrower exposure, project return and restructuring are material and case-specific; Sri Lanka and Zambia show real creditor-workout clocks |
| “All 1,800 GDI projects are completed successes.” | **False** | the pool includes distinct states and the aggregate comes from Chinese administration; project-level approval, finance and output must be checked |
| “The GSI restored Saudi-Iran relations.” | **Partly supported but too causal if unqualified** | the Beijing agreement is real and China hosted/sponsored the final talks, while Saudi/Iran decisions and earlier Iraq/Oman rounds also mattered |
| “The UN adopted the Global Civilization Initiative.” | **False** | it adopted a consensus resolution creating an international day, not the GCI as a whole |
| “The GGI changed UN governance.” | **Not established** | it has a concept and operating diplomatic group; specific institutional reforms require separate adoption |
| “Every China-funded UN project is a global-initiative project.” | **False** | many mechanisms predate the initiatives; explicit attribution and the UN project's own legal custody control |

## 8. Return gates

1. A current public list of BRI cooperation documents with accession, withdrawal, amendment and expiration states.
2. Project-level finance/contract terms, procurement and beneficial ownership for current flagship BRI objects.
3. Host-audited ridership, cargo, revenue, operating subsidy, debt service and maintenance for Whoosh, Hambantota and other selected corridors.
4. Project-level reconciliation of the GDI's reported 1,800-plus objects by incubation/achievement state, funding source, host and result.
5. Independent/implementer confirmation for GDI outcome claims, including Uganda household income and beneficiary counts.
6. GSI object registry separating concept endorsement, forum participation, agreement, institution, finance, training and conflict outcome.
7. Saudi-Iran implementation after embassy restoration and the effect of later regional crises; do not freeze the 2023 agreement as permanent success.
8. GCI program funding, participation and repeat activity beyond observance counts.
9. GGI Group of Friends membership, joint texts and any adopted reform in the UN, MDBs or other institutions.
10. International Organization for Mediation membership, budget, staffing, accepted cases, awards/settlements and enforcement.
11. Chinese creditor contract and restructuring terms where disclosure is lawful, joined to IMF/host debt perimeters.
12. Comparative U.S./allied and multilateral offers at the same project stage, rather than comparing a delivered Chinese asset with a Western announcement.

## Governing boundary

Chinese sources establish initiative design, official attribution and reported outputs. Host-government sources establish local legal acts and operating claims. UN, FAO, IMF, World Bank and MDB sources establish their own institutional records and assessments. None is automatically neutral about the others.

This map preserves:

`proposal ≠ endorsement ≠ legal constitution ≠ financing ≠ procurement ≠ construction ≠ operation ≠ output ≠ durable outcome`
