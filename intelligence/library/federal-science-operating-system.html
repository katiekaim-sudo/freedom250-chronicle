Research summary

The doctrine-to-operation map for an AI-native scientific-industrial economy. The August National Security Science & Technology Strategy adds the focused, resilient, agile and secure mission-selection layer: capital follows mission-relevant capability through a security membrane of identity, provenance, research security, investment screening, export controls and trusted supply. It creates no funding or operating system. The fusion vertical continues to preserve each company, machine, capital, license, electricity and grid-delivery gate.
