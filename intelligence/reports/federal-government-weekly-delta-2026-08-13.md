## Reader contract

Signature, filing, public inspection, publication, effectiveness,
implementation, operation and observed outcome remain separate clocks. A plan
is not a contract; an application is not a license or project; a comment window
is not relief; a tariff proclamation is not customs collection; and a public
reporting duty is not proof that the reported transaction was wrongful.

## 1. Private cyber power moved under a federal approval chain

The August 12 presidential memorandum directs the National Coordination Center
to establish and manage a program in which vetted U.S. companies may conduct
cyber-surveillance and cyber-effects operations against foreign cyber-enabled
transnational criminal organizations. DOJ and DHS co-executive directors
oversee it; participants contract with DOJ or DHS; each operation requires a
written package, federal approval and federal direction; and the memorandum
includes legal, targeting, deconfliction, incident, and bond or escrow controls.

Procedures are due within 60 days and the first status report within 180 days.
The state reached is a command and approval architecture. No company, contract,
target, operation or effect was established by cutoff.

Atomic record: 2026-08-12-1200 - President Creates a Federally Controlled Private Cyber Program.  
Source: [White House memorandum](https://www.whitehouse.gov/presidential-actions/2026/08/expanding-capabilities-to-combat-transnational-cyber-enabled-crime/).

## 2. Shipbuilding and drones formed one industrial-mobilization stack

The August 13 Navy memorandum sets 60-, 90- and 120-day planning and review
gates around CVN-81 systems, acquisition competition, trade-deal investment,
repair capacity, NAVSEA reform and a possible fifth public Navy yard. Its
conditional “Finland Model” and delegated waiver route do not themselves create
a foreign-owned yard, waiver, contract or congressional clearance.

Atomic record: 2026-08-13-1200 - President Orders Navy and Shipbuilding Industrial Base Plans.  
Source: [White House memorandum](https://www.whitehouse.gov/presidential-actions/2026/08/rebuilding-the-united-states-navy-and-americas-shipbuilding-industrial-base/).

The same day, a section 232 proclamation scheduled additional UAS duties for
September 3, including nominal 100-percent and 25-percent bands subject to
product, country, origin, certification and other exceptions. A separate
25-percent component band begins February 9, 2027. Commerce must build the
administration/onshoring route and report on the market within 120 days.

Atomic record: 2026-08-13-1200 - UAS Import Duties and Onshoring Framework Are Proclaimed.  
Source: [White House proclamation](https://www.whitehouse.gov/presidential-actions/2026/08/adjusting-imports-of-unmanned-aircraft-systems-and-unmanned-aircraft-systems-components-into-the-united-states/).

BIS simultaneously removed the wind-gust criterion, raised a specified
national-security endurance threshold from 30 minutes to three hours and
removed associated controls for some software and technology, while preserving
military end-use/end-user controls. Its text says effective August 13; the
public-inspection filing at 8:45 a.m. ET was scheduled for August 14 publication.

Atomic record: 2026-08-13-0845 - Commerce Loosens Civil Drone Export Controls.  
Source: [Federal Register public inspection](https://www.federalregister.gov/public-inspection/2026-16628/streamlining-export-controls-for-drone-exports).

Together these objects widen domestic protection and planning while loosening a
band of civil export control. They do not prove an import, export, approved
onshoring plan, factory, shipment or domestic production result.

## 3. FinCEN narrowed the broad registry while tightening a local payment lens

FinCEN's Minnesota geographic targeting order is effective August 11 through
February 6, 2027. Covered banks and money transmitters with an office in
Hennepin or Ramsey County must report and retain specified information for
covered outgoing international transfers of at least $3,000. The order asks
about government-benefit and contract sources and carries a five-year retention
duty. It does not declare every covered payment fraudulent or prove recovery.

Atomic record: 2026-08-11-1200 - FinCEN Activates a Minnesota Payment GTO.  
Source: [Federal Register](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2026/08/11/2026-16365/geographic-targeting-order-imposing-recordkeeping-and-reporting-requirements-on-certain-financial).

On August 13 at 8:45 a.m. ET, FinCEN's BOI final text reached public inspection
for scheduled August 14 publication and effectiveness. It makes the March 2025
narrowing permanent: domestic reporting companies and U.S. persons remain
outside the operative reporting population; covered foreign companies still
report non-U.S. beneficial owners. A contemplated, NARA-coordinated deletion of
certain prior U.S. BOI has no completion date and was not complete at cutoff.

Atomic record: 2026-08-13-0845 - FinCEN BOI Final Rule Reaches Public Inspection.  
Source: [Federal Register public inspection](https://www.federalregister.gov/public-inspection/2026-16576/beneficial-ownership-information-reporting-requirement-revision).

The useful story is the contrast: a broad domestic-company registry remains
narrowed while a geographically targeted transaction-reporting program becomes
operative.

## 4. Health policy moved at both the recommendation and funding layers

Executive Order 14420, signed August 10, declares universal, high-risk and
shared-decision categories for federal childhood-vaccine recommendations,
recognizes separate MMR shots once such products are domestically available,
and orders an HHS implementation/research plan within 90 days. It does not by
itself make separate products available, change an operating schedule, move a
grant or file a lawsuit.

Existing captured record: 2026-08-10-1906 - POTUS For far too long America has recommended more.  
Source: [White House order](https://www.whitehouse.gov/presidential-actions/2026/08/delivering-gold-standard-childhood-vaccine-recommendations-for-americans/).

CMS's August 13 final rule bars federal Medicaid funding for the rule-defined
procedures for people under 18 and corresponding CHIP funding for people under
19. It is effective October 13, and permits up to six months of continued
federal matching for certain beneficiaries already receiving cross-sex hormone
therapy. It does not prohibit state-only or private funding, exclude every
provider, or establish that a claim was denied before effectiveness.

Existing captured record: 2026-08-11-2132 - Today at my direction Dr Mehmet Oz announced that.  
Source: [Federal Register](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2026/08/13/2026-16508/medicaid-program-prohibition-on-federal-medicaid-and-childrens-health-insurance-program-funding-for).

## 5. DTCC disclosed a deadline around an earlier settlement failure

Separate August 13 DTC and NSCC notices say that July 13 interest payments
followed a June 29 settlement rollover. A participant claiming direct losses
materially above those payments must submit substantiating evidence by August
31; later submissions will not be considered.

Atomic records: 2026-08-13-1200 - DTC Sets August 31 Rollover Loss Deadline and
2026-08-13-1200 - NSCC Sets August 31 Rollover Loss Deadline.  
Sources: [DTC B24712-26](https://www.dtcc.com/-/media/Files/pdf/2026/8/13/B24712-26.pdf) and
[NSCC A9807](https://www.dtcc.com/-/media/Files/pdf/2026/8/13/a9807.pdf).

The notices establish the rollover, prior interest payments and hard proof
deadline. They do not establish the number of claimants, a liability admission,
an additional loss amount or a new August incident. DTCC's official all-notices
RSS listed 25 notices dated August 10–13, correcting the earlier claim that the
site had no post-July 15 material; the JS-paginated UI remains unverified.

## 6. The sleeper was a federal device-control reversal

OMB Memorandum M-26-17, dated August 10, rescinds M-23-13 after a July DOJ OLC
opinion concluded TikTok was no longer a covered application under the statute.
The operative memo says TikTok may be used on government devices. This is a
government-wide control reversal, not proof that an agency installed or
authorized the app or accepted a particular risk.

Atomic record: 2026-08-10-1200 - OMB Rescinds the Federal TikTok Device Ban Memo.  
Source: [OMB M-26-17](https://www.whitehouse.gov/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/M-26-17-Rescission-of-M-23-13.pdf).
