2025 Aries lunar chapter — the claim must become a record and a bill

The period whole

The first six lunar hearings of the Aries year move a public claim through record, hidden need, law and value, public authority, collective voice, and finally the money/debt account. The annual ingress has a bright Sun–Neptune center but a Jupiter–Mercury–Mars–Moon operating root. Its first New Moon is an eclipse on Mercury. Its last Full Moon presses Saturn in the treasury while Jupiter's squares to Saturn and Neptune approach perfection.

The season therefore does not say “executive absorption” six different ways. It asks whether the claim can survive translation into an accountable record, a material structure, an operating body and a bounded shared cost.

March 29 — the first Aries seed enters through the record

The partial solar eclipse is the first New Moon wholly inside the Aries year. Aries rises, and the Sun and Moon meet in the first house with Mercury, Venus and Neptune concentrated in the twelfth. The seed is public identity; its terms, value and image are still being worked out behind the face it presents.

Moon and Mars form the root circuit. Mars is fallen and out of bounds in the fourth, while the eclipsed Moon is held in the exalted Sun's light in the first. National assertion and protective force need one another, but they use very different tools and stages. Saturn–Uranus is already an applying sextile, and Mars trines Saturn while sextiling Uranus, producing the same Small Talent mechanism the annual chart offered: difficult action, limit and material change have a usable route.

The eclipse lights conjoin annual Mercury within 1.111°. The first seed of the new year therefore enters through the national record, message, trade and transport channel. It does not prove that the message is clear. Mercury is conjoined with Neptune, and the eclipse's Jupiter–Pluto contra-parallel keeps scale and concentrated power opposed on the second sky.

The astronomy makes the public image more precise. Globally this is a partial solar eclipse of magnitude 0.938, Saros 149 member 21. At Washington the Sun is still below the true horizon at syzygy. Sunrise occurs while the eclipse is ending, leaving only about 2.442 minutes of modeled local visibility, with local magnitude about 0.045 and obscuration about 0.012. A globally large partial eclipse appears in Washington only as a tiny eclipsed sunrise. Local witness and global class are not the same claim.

The same-Saros predecessor is the March 2007 partial solar eclipse near 28° Pisces. The Metonic predecessor is the March 29, 2006 total solar eclipse at 8°35′ Aries, only 0.418° from the current degree but in Saros 139. The Pessin line begins here in the Great Harvest degree band and reaches Full phase at the September 26, 2026 Aries Full Moon. Those are three distinct ancestries.

April 12 — the eclipse seed receives its first Full hearing

The Libra Moon in the twelfth opposes the exalted Aries Sun in the sixth. The Moon-end landing makes hidden political feeling, confinement, institutional need and excluded voices visible. The axis asks whether the public service, labor, health or armed body carrying the new claim can hear what its visible order has put out of sight.

The Full Moon is Greer-quiet to the ingress, but it is not outside the March 29 eclipse month. Mercury and Mars trine within 0.215° while Mercury applies to Neptune and Venus remains joined to Saturn. The record can reach action; the terms remain entangled with limit and ideal. Jupiter and Pluto contra-parallel within 0.092°, and Mars–Pluto repeats the opposition-like second-sky link within 0.193°. The quiet Full Moon exposes what the direct ingress contact rule does not own.

It also carries an older Pessin job: this is the Full phase of the October 14, 2023 Libra solar-eclipse seed in the Quiet Sowing band. The April Full therefore belongs to the current March 29 eclipse month while maturing a different eclipse seed from 2023. Current chapter and older family overlap; neither becomes the other's Saros or Metonic return.

April 27 — a material seed sits on the missing leg

The Taurus New Moon falls in the ninth house under Virgo rising. Mars and Pluto are in a nearly exact opposition; the lights square both and become the apex of a fixed T-square. Law, courts, doctrine and long exchange carry the pressure between hidden force and the working/system side of concentrated power. The seed asks whether a rule can make material value durable without simply ratifying whichever pole can exert more leverage.

The chart has a wide root roster because several independent circuits and finals coexist. That is not extra convergence. Mercury–Uranus's 0.224° semisquare and the Venus–Neptune contra-parallel sharpen the problem of revised terms and uncertain value. The lunation squares the annual Ascendant but is otherwise quiet to ingress planets; its authority comes from its own monthly seed and exact figure.

This Threshold-band New Moon begins a Pessin lineage whose Full phase is the October 26, 2026 Taurus Full Moon. The later fixed T-square to Pluto is not a new unrelated plot. It is one mature chapter of the material seed planted here.

May 12 — public authority faces the ground

Leo rises. The Taurus Sun is in the tenth and the Scorpio Moon in the fourth. Read both directions: public authority and visible value face the land, opposition and buried emotional account; the fallen Moon reveals what apparent stability cannot settle by presentation alone.

Mercury squares Pluto within 0.033°, making record and power the sharpest dialogue. The Full Moon also contacts annual Uranus in the fourth, bringing the year's material/foundational change into the monthly axis. Saturn–Neptune has entered closer conjunction while Uranus remains linked to the pair. The public decision is therefore made inside inherited structural change, not above it.

This Full Moon is the harvest of the November 13, 2023 Scorpio New Moon in the Quiet Sowing band. An older hidden-ledger seed reaches the foundation/public- authority axis. Its older lineage and its current April 27 lunar month are both true; they do different jobs.

May 26 — the record becomes sole root

The Gemini New Moon seeds the fifth house under Capricorn rising. Mercury in its own sign is the sole final dispositor. Public confidence, speculation, children, culture and risk answer through the national record. The lights oppose the annual Moon within 0.263°, making the seed a direct hearing of the political class and collective hopes embedded in the annual root.

Mercury has sextile and trine routes to Neptune and Pluto; record can move through ideal and power without open friction. Mars–Pluto's 0.220° quindecile and Mars–Jupiter's septile keep action persistent and inspired at a narrower harmonic level. The Moon is out of bounds. A fluent message can therefore carry an unusually independent public response; it still has to be corrected by what the next Full reveals about the bill.

The Gemini Sun in the second opposes the Sagittarius Moon in the eighth. The slow pressure is now unmistakable. Jupiter squares Saturn within 0.767° and Neptune within 1.696°; Saturn and Neptune are within 0.929° of conjunction. The Full Moon directly squares annual Saturn in the treasury. Four days later the third Jupiter–Saturn square perfects; June 19 brings the Jupiter–Neptune square; June 21 opens the Cancer stage. The lunar chapter carries the annual claim to its resource, rule and ideal bill immediately before the nested handoff.

This Full Moon is the harvest of the December 12, 2023 Sagittarius Quiet Sowing seed, returning to its degree within 0.024°. The long family gives the monthly money/debt axis an older belief and legitimacy history without turning it into a literal outcome.
