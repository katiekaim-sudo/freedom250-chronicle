Which official government websites could the research not read, and why?

As of September 22, 2026, the research could not fully read 30 official sources. 11 blocked or refused access (cisa.gov, congress.gov, dsca.mil, exim.gov, among others); 6 kept changing as they loaded, so no complete list could be taken; 4 could be read only in part; 2 still need the exact document or an earlier version to compare; 1 was not yet published at the cutoff; and 6 were read but could not support a conclusion. For 10 of them the monitor keeps its old place until the missing item is recovered. None of this means the agency did nothing.
