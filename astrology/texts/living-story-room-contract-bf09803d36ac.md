# Living Story Room Contract

A Living Story Room lets Katie replay how one large story became legible without
turning its component braids into one factual or astrological object. It is a
guided route through existing owners, not a new research store or super-braid.

## Durable job

Select one lunar chapter and see what entered each lane during that window, at
that historical cutoff:

1. the primary-source and Research-knowledge objects added in the chapter;
2. adjudicated institutional and Executive Order movements;
3. Katie's saved-attention stream, labeled as attention only;
4. the computed sky governing that chapter;
5. any authored rope overlaps actually locked to that window;
6. unresolved deadlines and return gates;
7. later review dispositions, without rewriting the old window; and
8. the categorical delta from the previous chapter.

## Independent lanes

The factual record, Research-knowledge clock, institutional movement,
Executive Order lineage, saved attention, computed sky, authored overlap,
unresolved gate and later review remain separate lanes. Shared dates make them
comparable. No count, coincidence or visual alignment creates evidence,
causation, confidence, common command or another vote.

## Historical replay

Each chapter is an immutable projection bounded by its start and end dates.
Later knowledge may appear only in a later chapter or a separately labeled
review. A later correction may qualify the earlier record but never silently
changes what the Observatory knew at the earlier cutoff.

The chapter-change panel reports categorical additions only: new factual
records, Research updates, institutional movements, attention items, open gates
and authored hearings. It is not a progress, maturity or importance score.

## Tours are shelves, not mergers

A tour names several independently owned story braids and an order in which to
visit them. The tour owns its question, orientation and navigation. It owns no
member fact, institutional state, planetary strand, verdict or Forecast Ledger
call.

## Three Living Story replays, one Grand Tour and a fail-closed lunar hearing

Money's New Pipes, Rebuilding the Federal Machine and Capacity or Spectacle are
the three live replays. The internal Grand Tour explains their distinct jobs in
about three minutes without becoming a shell tab or common factual owner. A
scheduled lunar hearing must bind every current overlap-ledger owner exactly
once to its existing frame, closing chapter and review date. Before that date it
remains scheduled; when the date arrives automation may mark it review-due but
may never choose a disposition, migrate a strand or write the next overlap.

## Automation boundary

The nightly builder may collect, deduplicate and sort already-governed objects;
compute chapter boundaries and categorical deltas; attach exact source routes;
and flag reviews due. It may not author a factual conclusion, infer an
institutional movement, choose a planetary relationship, migrate an overlap,
resolve a gate, score a tour or write a prediction.

## Canonical owners

- This file owns the experience and replay contract.
- `Story Rooms/*.md` owns each tour's question, membership and authored guide.
- `Review Hearings/*.md` owns scheduled human review packets; it may never carry
  a pre-authored disposition.
- `Braids/*.md` owns each member story and authored overlap window.
- `Braid Overlap Ledger.jsonl` owns append-only overlap history.
- The Workbench Research Library owns factual research.
- The Movement Registry owns adjudicated institutional state transitions.
- `mundane_sky_events.json` and relationship history own computed geometry.
- `Mundane Astrology.html` is a generated projection only.
