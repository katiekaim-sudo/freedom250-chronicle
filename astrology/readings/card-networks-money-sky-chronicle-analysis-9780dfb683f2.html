Finding

The factual sequence is already differentiated:

  1. a customer credential can change while card authorization, clearing and settlement remain;

That is a coherent symbolic description. It is not yet discriminating evidence because many anchors are close together in time, 12 of 14 clocks are midnight conventions, and slow-planet geometry will also appear around unrelated events.
