# Wave 6D — The Sovereign Perimeter

**Research window:** Exact 2026 Aries ingress through exact 2026 Cancer ingress.  
**Evidence cutoff:** June 20, 2026, 11:59 p.m. EDT.  
**Question:** Where does the Aries-year regime claim a right to set the rule—and where does surviving law, private governance, state authority or voluntary participation still define the edge?  
**Finding:** The four remaining orders draw four different sovereign perimeters. College-sports policy attempts to make private governing-body rules relevant to federal grant and contract responsibility, but delays its operative sections and cannot itself invalidate state law. Cuba sanctions acquire immediate body through named designations, blocked-property rules, licenses and foreign-bank risk. Federal-land policy removes two executive-order criteria but leaves statutory and agency-specific law in place while rulemaking catches up. Frontier-model security binds federal and classified systems while making private pre-release participation voluntary and expressly rejecting a general model-license regime. The packet's thesis is **the regime is strongest where it can attach an existing federal lever—money, sanctions, land administration or classified-system control—and weakest where it mistakes influence over a perimeter for ownership of the institution beyond it**.

The institutional ladder is:

`claimed public interest → available federal hook → rule at the perimeter → implementing holder → concrete designation / contract / access decision → external counterparty or contest`

## 6D.1 — April 3: federal money is used to approach a private rule system

[EO 14400](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2026/04/09/2026-06961/urgent-national-action-to-save-college-sports) defines improper financial activity, fraudulent NIL arrangements, covered higher-education institutions and an interstate intercollegiate athletic governing body. It directs agencies to consider certain governing-body rules when judging the present responsibility of federally funded institutions; calls for OMB/GSA guidance, information collection and Education reporting; invokes FTC enforcement against agents; and directs Justice toward meritorious challenges to conflicting state laws.

The mechanism is indirect:

`private governing-body rule → institution conduct and report → federal grant/contract responsibility review → possible suspension/debarment or other action`

Sections 3 through 6 do not take effect until August 1, 2026. Agencies were to prepare immediately, but at the Cancer ingress the operative decision gate had not opened. No named university responsibility finding, debarment, federal-funds recovery, governing-body rule amendment, agent case or successful state-law challenge was isolated.

**Jurisdictional limit.** The EO does not turn the NCAA or another private body into a federal agency. It cannot by declaration invalidate a state statute or conclusively determine the scope of the Commerce or Contracts Clauses. Federal agencies may act only through their own lawful grant, contract, competition and litigation powers.

## 6D.2 — May 1 through June 11: Cuba policy becomes an operating designation surface

[EO 14404](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2026/05/07/2026-09173/imposing-sanctions-on-those-responsible-for-repression-in-cuba-and-for-threats-to-united-states) extends the January Cuba emergency into a broad person-specific sanctions constitution. State and Treasury may determine that a foreign person falls within a covered Cuban sector, ownership/control chain, support relationship, leadership role, human-rights or corruption category. A determination can produce property blocking and entry suspension; a separate Treasury determination can restrict a foreign financial institution's U.S. correspondent/payable-through accounts or block its property.

This order acquired a body quickly:

- May 7: State designated GAESA under EO 14404; OFAC issued General License 1 so activity already authorized or exempt under the Cuban Assets Control Regulations would not be accidentally interrupted;
- May 18: OFAC added four named Cuban officials under the new authority;
- June 4: OFAC added five named individuals and five entities, including MINFAR and the Cuban Institute of Friendship with the Peoples; and
- June 11: OFAC added another Cuban state-owned enterprise.

The operative chain is:

`EO criterion → State/Treasury determination → named SDN entry → U.S.-property and transaction prohibition → license / wind-down boundary → compliance or enforcement`

**Maturity.** Named legal classifications, live blocking perimeter and licensing instructions. The public record reviewed here does not establish the dollar value of property actually blocked, a particular correspondent-account restriction, seizure, forfeiture or later enforcement recovery.

**Counterevidence and limit.** Merely operating in a listed Cuban sector does not automatically make every person sanctioned. A separate determination is required. General License 1 preserves CACR-authorized or exempt activity rather than erasing the pre-existing Cuba rulebook. Classification, blocked property, frozen transaction and government receipt remain separate clocks.

## 6D.3 — May 29: an executive land overlay dies while the statutory floor survives

[EO 14408](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2026/06/03/2026-11181/removing-unnecessary-and-counterproductive-restrictions-on-access-to-federal-lands) immediately rescinds EO 11644 and EO 11989, which supplied federal off-road-vehicle designation and minimization criteria. War, Interior, Agriculture, TVA and other relevant agencies must then begin rulemakings to rescind or revise regulations adopted under those orders.

This is a real legal-state change, but not an instant route opening:

`prior presidential criteria rescinded → surviving statute and agency authority → agency rule revision → site or route designation → physical access / use`

NEPA, FLPMA, the Endangered Species Act, the National Historic Preservation Act and agency-specific land authorities remain. Existing regulations do not rewrite themselves merely because their executive-order parent has been removed. A named trail, energy project, timber action, utility route or permit needs its own agency object and environmental record.

**Maturity.** Executive overlay removed; regulatory transition directed. No universal public-land access right or automatic production permission follows.

## 6D.4 — June 2: federal AI security becomes mandatory inside and voluntary at the private edge

[EO 14409](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2026/06/05/2026-11415/promoting-advanced-artificial-intelligence-innovation-and-security) creates a joined federal cyber-defense design:

- national-security and War systems receive priority action;
- CISA is assigned binding operational directives/guidance for civilian federal systems and relevant services for state, local and critical-infrastructure users;
- Treasury, NSA, War, DHS and the National Cyber Director are assigned an AI cybersecurity clearinghouse;
- OMB must inspect relevant grant funding and OPM must expand cyber hiring;
- a classified process must benchmark advanced cyber capability and define a covered frontier model; and
- developers may voluntarily offer pre-release model access for up to 30 days under confidentiality, security and intellectual-property controls.

The order expressly says the framework does not authorize mandatory governmental licensing, preclearance or permitting for new AI models. This creates an asymmetric perimeter:

`federal / classified system → binding public security direction`

but:

`private frontier developer → voluntary confidential pre-release relationship → trusted-partner coordination`

At June 20 the 30- and 60-day clocks had not expired. GOLD EAGLE's July 14 clearinghouse launch is a Wave 7 maturity receipt. No completed CISA directive bundle, classified threshold, voluntary-access agreement, model designation or enforcement case was isolated inside the Aries cutoff.

**Continuity.** Private model ownership and release remain governed by existing law and voluntary agreement at this interface. DOJ's criminal-enforcement priority still requires an underlying offense, investigation and case.

## The perimeter theorem

| Field | What the regime can directly move | What it cannot simply absorb |
|---|---|---|
| College sports | federal responsibility review, funding/contract procedure, FTC action and federal litigation position | private rule authorship, state law or judicial judgment |
| Cuba | designation criteria, named blocking, licensing and U.S. financial-entry risk | every Cuban-sector actor without determination or every asset outside U.S. jurisdiction |
| Federal lands | prior presidential criteria and agency rulemaking direction | statutes, site-specific review or state/private land |
| Frontier AI | federal-system directives, classified benchmark, grants/hiring and voluntary agreements | a general private-model licensing regime not enacted by law |

The common structure is not unlimited command. It is **federal leverage applied exactly where a legal hook already exists**.

## Both poles

### Constructive pole — sovereign leverage is bounded and explicit

- federal funders can protect institutional responsibility without owning private sports;
- sanctions can target named officials and entities while licenses preserve lawful activity;
- removing duplicative executive criteria can return land decisions to statutes and accountable agency rules; and
- federal cyber systems can harden while private developers collaborate under protected voluntary terms.

### Shadow pole — every relationship becomes a jurisdictional pretext

- grants and contracts can be used to impose a favored private rule system;
- broad sanctions categories can chill lawful counterparties beyond named targets;
- rescission can create an enforcement gap before replacement land rules are tested; and
- “voluntary” frontier-model access can become practically coercive when government controls procurement, classification or trusted-partner status.

Both poles remain hypotheses. Agency guidance, named responsibility findings, sanctions transactions, land rules, model agreements, judicial decisions and operating results determine what matures.
