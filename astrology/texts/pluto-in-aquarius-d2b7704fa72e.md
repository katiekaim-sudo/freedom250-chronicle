The last time Pluto occupied Aquarius (1778–1798), humanity tore down its old monarchies and erected constitutional republics in their place — the American Revolution, the French Revolution, the U.S. Constitution. Pluto doesn't reform. Pluto *resurrects*. Whatever it touches dies and is reborn in an unrecognizable form.

In Aquarius, the death-and-resurrection is aimed at **the collective structure itself** — who gets to be part of the group, what the group believes, how power flows between the individual and the system. This is Pluto's Overwhelming & Catastrophic Extremes operating through Aquarius's Process of Individuation. The old group (the post-1989 liberal-globalist consensus) is being devoured from within, and the new human being — free-thinking, alienated, paradigm-shifting — is clawing its way out of the ruins.
