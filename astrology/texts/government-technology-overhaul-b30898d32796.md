# Government technology and the record overhaul

*Which federal AI and digital-record systems actually change public decisions or service, and who authorizes and corrects them?*

## The braid so far

This is the **government operator and record** story, distinct from [private AI and compute buildout](ai-buildout.md). The [October 4 Super Intelligence Force owner](<Research-Workbench/Research Packages/Federal Agency AI Plans and Infrastructure 2025-2026/SUPER_INTELLIGENCE_FORCE_AUTHORITY_MEMBERSHIP_AND_REPORT_GATE_2026-10-04.md>) finds a White House coordination and recommendation body, without a public charter, budget or transferred agency authority. Its announced leaders retain their separate DNI, FTC, Defense and OPM powers. Coordination is a real political layer, not yet a shared operating system.

The [October 5 NARA AI-records return](<Research-Workbench/Research Packages/NARA AI Records Layer and Buildout 2026-08-23/NARA_AI_RECORDS_RETURN_REFRESH_2026-10-05.md>) gives a narrower live example: semantic search is operating on special-topic record-release pages, with public warnings that results may be incomplete or inaccurate and source documents must be checked. General Archives.gov AI search remains a future pilot in the saved inventory. NARA was considering general records-schedule coverage for AI policy and training data, but no approved general schedule or AI-records enforcement case was located at that cutoff.

The [OPM structural audit](<Research-Workbench/Research Packages/Office of Personnel Management Structural Change Audit 2025-2026/OPM_STRUCTURAL_CHANGE_COVERAGE_AND_GAP_AUDIT_2025-01-01_TO_2026-08-29.md>) finds some real electronic retirement and personnel-record operation, while a governmentwide HR-platform award and shared-service center still need agency migration, acceptance, decommissioning, error and result proof. AI hiring guidance is not a deployed, validated individual selection. These records show why “the government adopted AI” is too coarse: a search feature, staffing system, advice body and legally consequential decision have different owners and remedies.

The forward question is whether a named agency tool moves from authority and procurement to accepted use with an authoritative record, human decision right, accuracy test, privacy/retention rule and correction after an error. Constructively, shared technology makes public service faster and more legible without losing agency accountability. In shadow, a fluent interface becomes the public's only view while the underlying record, model and decision maker are hard to find. Ordinary IT modernization, piecemeal by agency, remains a strong rival to one federal technology constitution.

This braid begins **without authored planetary strands**. [Rebuilding the Federal Machine's 2027–30 route](../Story%20Rooms/rebuilding-the-federal-machine-2027-30-forecast-route.md) hears its lawful work/record questions; [Capacity or Spectacle](../Story%20Rooms/capacity-or-spectacle-2027-30-forecast-route.md) cross-examines physical compute and private suppliers. A future chart relationship is earned only by a dated agency authorization, accepted system, consequential decision or correction, not by the word *AI*.
