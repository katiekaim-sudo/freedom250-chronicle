## Bottom line

Wave 3 adds 50 evidence-graded company edges and 43 primary-source objects.
The combined standalone map now contains **125 company edges, 113 source
objects and nine sector lanes**.

The three sectors occupy sharply different maturity structures:

- biotechnology has substantial funded activity, but the scarce proof is
  screening eligibility, repeatable scale, cGMP transfer, validated lots and
  customer or regulator acceptance;
- resilient PNT already contains production incumbents, flown hardware and
  active receiver designs, while fault-tolerant quantum computing remains in
  government verification or risk-reduction stages;
- space, hypersonics and missile sensing contain dense contract, task-order,
  launch and test rails, but launch, landing, flight and award states still do
  not automatically equal operational acceptance.

The governing path remains:

```text
CET priority
  -> mission
  -> acceptance bottleneck
  -> federal owner and program
  -> contract / prototype / test / qualification lane
  -> exact legal company
  -> validated scale, field result, launch checkout or operational acceptance
```

### Biomedical mission, regulatory evidence and manufacturing acceptance

**Question and scope:** A biomedical mission, credible evidence and a manufacturable therapy are complementary parts of the acceptance chain. EO 14355’s pediatric mission is maintained in [the AI official inventory](../sources/trump-ai-executive-order-official-inventory-2026-08-01-6f8e2def351e.html); the questions here are what supports a regulatory decision and what proves production. September 7 reconciliation follows the six named Tech records and directly identified returns; all original Wave 3 company counts and judgments retain their August 18 cutoff.

The [January 2025 FDA draft](https://www.fda.gov/media/184830/download) proposes a context-specific credibility process: define the question and model role, assess influence and consequences, plan and perform assessment, document results and determine adequacy. It discusses independent testing, uncertainty and ongoing model maintenance. The [January 7 notice](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-2025-01-07/html/2024-31542.htm) is draft availability, not an approval. Drug discovery and unrelated internal efficiency uses are outside its stated scope. Neither the “deregulate” label nor AI participation establishes accepted evidence or an approved medicine.

The saved GIVE record’s URL points to [THRIVE](https://arpa-h.gov/news-and-events/arpa-h-launches-groundbreaking-program-develop-affordable-precision-genetic), a separate precision-genetic-medicine program. The correct [GIVE launch release](https://arpa-h.gov/news-and-events/arpa-h-launches-program-ignite-innovative-biomanufacturing-cell-and-gene-therapies) describes a September 25, 2025 manufacturing funding opportunity, with distributed production and automated quality assurance. The [GIVE program](https://arpa-h.gov/explore-funding/programs/give) concentrates on automated RNA manufacturing and integrated quality control. Do not convert domestic first-production ambitions into proof that an existing overseas capability was re-shored.

A [September 1, 2026 ARPA-H return](https://arpa-h.gov/news-and-events/arpa-h-selects-teams-make-made-order-genetic-medicine-manufacturing-possible) announces five teams and up to $125 million for development. Centillion Biosciences, HDT Bio, Massachusetts General Hospital and Waterfall Scientific cover manufacturing plus quality control; the University of Utah team covers integrated quality control. This is a real change from opportunity to named performers. It does not add accepted production capacity to the August 18 company registry or establish disbursement, affordable delivered therapy or patient benefit. The earlier solicitation anticipated other transactions, while the later release calls them contract awards; exact award instruments were not reviewed. The original planning-versus-build tests below still apply.

### The planning-versus-build firewall

DBIMP planning awards identify important candidates but do not establish plant
construction. Checkerspot, Industrial Microbes and other planning recipients
move closer to the buildout only when a site, build instrument, equipment,
commissioning clock and qualification plan appear.

BioMADE membership is not used as PATH evidence. BioMADE, ARMI|BioFabUSA and
ATI remain noncompany program or consortium nodes.

### Maturity ruling

Resilient PNT is substantially more mature than quantum computing:

- Lockheed Martin is an operating GPS production incumbent.
- Vector Atomic hardware was delivered, integrated and launched on X-37B,
  although public on-orbit performance acceptance remains missing.
- BAE and Trimble hold current MAVRC design agreements, still before prototype
  downselection and certification.
- Microsoft and PsiQuantum entered the final government verification-and-validation phase of US2QC; that stage is not a published utility-scale validation result. [The maintained Quantum and Cryptographic Acceptance Map](../sources/nssts-quantum-cryptographic-acceptance-map-2026-08-19-141341f745ad.html), “Three federal architectures must remain separate,” distinguishes US2QC from QBI Stage C even though DARPA describes their technical goals as equivalent. This is a program-label clarification, not a new company-performance census.
- Atom, IBM, Quantinuum and QuEra remain in QBI Stage B or related risk-reduction
  and testbed lanes.
- The 2026 quantum CHIPS actions are letters of intent, not definitive
  agreements or qualified foundry output.
- IonQ has the broadest visible convergence path: computing and networking
  programs plus the optical-clock manufacturing contract. Its facility and
  qualified clocks remain future objects.

### Space policy, authorization and mission acceptance

**Question and scope:** Space strategy, permission to operate and a delivery agreement answer different parts of how a mission reaches acceptance. These three original Tech questions belong beside the existing launch, checkout and performance distinctions, not in a separate package. September 7 review is bounded to the named instruments and release; the company map’s August 18 observations remain dated.

[EO 14369](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2025/12/23/2025-23845/ensuring-american-space-superiority), signed December 18 and published December 23, 2025, joins exploration, national-security architecture, commercial acquisition and space-nuclear policy. Correct the capture’s “fielding” shorthand: §2(b)(i) targets prototype missile-defense development and demonstration by 2028. Section 2(d)(ii) targets a lunar reactor ready for launch by 2030. Prototype, readiness, launch and operation are different results. Its agency plans and reviews need their own completion records.

[EO 14335](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2025/08/19/2025-15822/enabling-competition-in-the-commercial-space-industry), signed August 13 and published August 19, 2025, directs lawful review of Part 450 and environmental burdens. It does not itself replace the licensing rule, waive required environmental law or license a particular launch. Commerce’s 150-day task is a proposal for individualized novel-space mission authorization excluding human spaceflight; coastal-consistency review, spaceport coordination and leadership changes are separate tasks. Subsequent rules, permits and decisions are the evidence of changed permission.

[NASA’s June 17, 2026 Aeolus release](https://www.nasa.gov/news-release/nasa-announces-public-private-partnership-to-advance-mars-science/), updated June 18, describes a six-year reimbursable agreement and planned 2028 launch with Relativity. NASA provides the four-instrument atmospheric payload, integration and science operations for at least one Martian year; Relativity provides spacecraft, rocket and cruise operations and maintains the spacecraft. The instruments address winds/ozone/temperature, clouds, energy balance and global images. This specifies a mission division of work; it does not show launch, science return or accepted performance. The executed agreement and reimbursement terms were not reviewed. These policy, permission and payload sources complement the company chains below without promoting their maturity.
