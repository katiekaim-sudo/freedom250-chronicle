## Question

What did the material federal committees, councils, boards, research bodies and
public-input surfaces mapped by the control-surface census actually say,
research, recommend or prepare during the window—and does that record support,
contradict, narrow or fail to bear on the Observatory's braided thesis?

## Present answer

Wave 0 is complete: 126 existing source objects are reconciled across the
financial/digital, management/public-input and science/industrial lanes. Wave
1 is open with nine newly verified official-source signal records. No final
program-level thesis verdict has yet been issued.

The test is deliberately adversarial. It preserves the possibility that the
observed pattern is ordinary constitutional adaptation, independent sectoral
modernization, incumbent absorption or selection bias rather than one coherent
institutional refactoring.

## Evidence ladder

```text
individual or invited-speaker statement
  -> meeting discussion
  -> draft or working-group product
  -> adopted body recommendation
  -> agency response
  -> legal or allocation instrument
  -> funded delivery or deployment
  -> recurring operation
  -> measured effect, failure or correction
```

No rung inherits the meaning of a later rung. Similar language across bodies is
not coordination unless an official actor, citation, instrument, standard,
contract, system or decision right supplies the join.
