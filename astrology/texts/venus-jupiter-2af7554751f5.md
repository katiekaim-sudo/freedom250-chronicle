# Venus–Jupiter

## The planets

**Venus** — *Magnetize, value, harmony, beauty of form.* MUNDANE: the women of the nation; women's vote and issues; the arts, culture, tourism, entertainment; diplomacy and social harmony. Venus shows where the nation seeks peace, pleasure, and economic ease.

**Jupiter** — *Zeus, expansion, faith, courage to hope.* MUNDANE: hierarchical organizations — government, corporate, nonprofit; the corporate sector; courts, judges, religion, charitable organizations. (Greer: "the planet of hierarchy; he rules every organization set up in a hierarchical manner.")

## The combination — mundane

Greer calls this "one of the best aspects in any mundane chart," and it is easy to see why. Under helpful aspects (conjunction, sextile, trine), Venus-Jupiter brings "peace and goodwill domestically and abroad; prosperity and settlement of quarrels; particularly beneficial for women's interests and Venus-ruled economic sectors." The courts settle disputes rather than inflame them. Charitable organizations flourish. Diplomacy works. The arts, entertainment, tourism, and luxury sectors expand. Women's issues advance — legislation, representation, economic participation all benefit. It is the mundane astrologer's favorite transit for a reason.

Under hostile aspects (square, opposition), Jupiter's expansive nature turns Venus's love of ease into "excessive government expenditures; public and private extravagance; taxes become a greater burden; fraud, scandal, excessive luxury" (Greer). The corporate sector over-leverages. Charitable organizations are exposed for waste or self-dealing. Government spending on social programs balloons without discipline. The scandals that erupt under hostile Venus-Jupiter tend to involve luxury, fraud, and the misuse of public funds — the nation discovers that its generosity has been exploited.

**As trigger:** Venus-Jupiter aspects are fast-moving (days to a couple of weeks) and mark the specific moments when economic optimism, diplomatic goodwill, or their shadows — extravagance and fraud — activate the broader outer-planet storylines.
