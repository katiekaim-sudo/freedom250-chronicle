Government · Who advises whom

Which advisory boards, research centers and councils belong to, or advise, which federal agency?

The library has 102 checked relationships for 42 boards, councils and research centers across 28 agencies or offices. 19 are federally funded research centers, all run day to day by private operators (MITRE Corporation 6, RAND Corporation 4, Institute for Defense Analyses (IDA) 3); the rest are boards and councils that advise or are set up inside an agency. Being sponsored or supported is not being owned or controlled.
