Federal Science Operating System

How does federal science doctrine move through mission selection, capital, security gates, industrial capability and operation without collapsing strategy into delivery?

Research through 2026-08-17

Home room: Innovation
The short version (draft, not yet reviewed by Katie)

How Washington plans to turn science strategy into funded work

The White House has issued a stack of science plans that set goals, budget guidance and security gates, but none of them alone creates money or a working system. Real change happens only when a law, award, permit or license attaches to a named project.

What we found

  • The July 21, 2026 White House report, 'Science: A New Golden Age', is an advisory design map, not a legal system. The August National Security Science & Technology Strategy (NSSTS) adds a security layer but supplies no funding, agency owners, deadlines or metrics.
  • The tools that do carry force are separate: NSTM-5 / OMB M-26-16 gives FY2028 budget guidance and 90-day agency implementation-plan instructions, and Executive Order 14363 charters the Genesis Mission and its American Science and Security Platform.
  • The security layer the strategy describes covers research security, screening of people and capital, export and data controls, supply-chain checks and cryptography. The study treats it as strategy until a specific legal or technical tool applies it to a specific object.
  • DOE's finalized Fusion Science and Technology Roadmap (June 9) is the first worked example. The eight Milestone companies are one federal funding group, not eight power plants, and the net-electricity and grid-delivery gates are still unmet.
  • The study finds no federal blockchain, no federal science token, no unrestricted spending by AI agents, no blanket preemption of opposition and no plan to eliminate universities.

Why it matters

This shows the government trying to rebuild how science is chosen, funded and secured by stacking reports, budget memos and executive orders on top of existing agencies and laws, rather than by new legislation. The study is careful that most of it is still direction, not operation.

What to watch

  • pending · The technology-specific plans the NSSTS says OSTP and the NSC will issue later.
  • pending · Agency implementation plans under OMB M-26-16 and how they shape the FY2028 budget.
  • 2027 · The fusion framework deadline that traces to NEIMA (2019) as amended by the ADVANCE Act; watch the NRC fusion-machine rulemaking against it.

Research summary

The doctrine-to-operation map for an AI-native scientific-industrial economy. The August National Security Science & Technology Strategy adds the focused, resilient, agile and secure mission-selection layer: capital follows mission-relevant capability through a security membrane of identity, provenance, research security, investment screening, export controls and trusted supply. It creates no funding or operating system. The fusion vertical continues to preserve each company, machine, capital, license, electricity and grid-delivery gate.
