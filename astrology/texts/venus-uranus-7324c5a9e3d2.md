# Venus–Uranus

## The planets

**Venus** — *Magnetize, value, harmony, beauty of form.* MUNDANE: the women of the nation; women's vote and issues; the arts, culture, tourism, entertainment; diplomacy and social harmony. Venus shows where the nation seeks peace, pleasure, and economic ease.

**Uranus** — *Prometheus, awakener, breakthroughs, liberation.* MUNDANE: democracy, countercultures, alternative movements; machines, factories, computers, the internet; radical change. Uranus disrupts whatever it touches — liberating what was trapped, destabilizing what was settled.

## The combination — mundane

Under helpful aspects (conjunction when well-placed, sextile, trine), Venus-Uranus is what Greer describes as "fortunate for prosperity and progress; beneficial changes in laws; especially favorable for women's issues." The economy benefits from technological disruption — new industries emerge, markets modernize. Laws change in ways that benefit women and expand social freedoms. The arts embrace innovation. Diplomatic alliances shift in constructive directions. Democracy and the counterculture find common cause with mainstream values.

Under hostile aspects (square, opposition), Uranus's disruptive force destabilizes Venus's domains. Greer warns this combination is "unfortunate for the economy; widespread dissatisfaction; family, community, and social troubles." Markets are disrupted suddenly — currency shocks, flash crashes, technology-driven dislocations. Alliances break without warning. Social harmony fractures as radical movements clash with traditional values. Family and community structures strain under the pressure of rapid change. Women's issues become flashpoints for cultural conflict rather than sites of progress.

**As trigger:** Venus-Uranus aspects are fast-moving (days to a couple of weeks) and mark the specific moments when market disruptions, sudden alliance shifts, or technology-driven economic change activate the broader outer-planet storylines.
