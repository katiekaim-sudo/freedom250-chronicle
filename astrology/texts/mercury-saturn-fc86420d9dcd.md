# Mercury–Saturn

## The planets

**Mercury** — *Communication, intellect, commerce, transport.* MUNDANE: the media; the transportation and communications infrastructure; trade and commerce; education and intellectual life; the postal system and telecommunications. Mercury's condition shows how information flows through the nation — freely or obstructed, truthfully or deceptively.

**Saturn** — *Limitation, structure, time, discipline.* MUNDANE: the established order; the conservative opposition; the laboring classes and the elderly; agriculture and mining; infrastructure and bureaucracy; borders and boundaries. Saturn shows where the nation encounters hard limits.

## The combination — mundane

Under helpful aspects (conjunction, sextile, trine), Mercury-Saturn is what Greer calls "a very favorable aspect." The result is that "transport and communications infrastructure provides lasting benefits; favors the laboring classes, higher wages." This is not flashy — it is the nation's communication and commerce systems being built or maintained with Saturnian durability. Infrastructure legislation passes and actually works. Trade agreements benefit workers. The media operates with discipline and seriousness rather than sensationalism. Education emphasizes rigor. The laboring classes see tangible improvements — higher wages, better working conditions — because Mercurial commerce is structured by Saturnian fairness.

Under hostile aspects (square, opposition), Saturn's weight falls on Mercury's domains. Greer describes "trade and commerce hindered; transportation delayed; conditions worsen for laboring classes." Information is controlled, censored, or suppressed. The media faces official constraints. Transportation systems break down or suffer delays. Trade stalls. The economy's commercial arteries constrict. The laboring classes — the people who depend on functional transport and fair commerce — bear the brunt. Official communications become stiff, punitive, and designed to limit rather than inform.

**As trigger:** Mercury-Saturn aspects are fast-moving (days to about two weeks) and mark the specific moments when communication limits, commercial constraints, and infrastructure stress activate whatever broader outer-planet storylines are in play.
