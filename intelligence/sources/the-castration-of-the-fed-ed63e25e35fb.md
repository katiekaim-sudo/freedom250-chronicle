# The Castration of the Fed

**The claim.** The biggest meta-narrative in this chronicle — the Monetary Reset — has always had a missing middle. We could see the old dollar dying (gold, Bessent, "controlled demolition") and the new dollar being born (stablecoins, tokenization, the XRP world), but the *mechanism* connecting them stayed fuzzy: how does a sovereign monetary system actually get changed? The answer was hiding in plain sight, in a committee room. The House Financial Services **Task Force on Monetary Policy, Treasury Market Resilience & Economic Prosperity** spent fifteen months (March 2025 – June 2026) holding nine hearings that, read in order, are not oversight. They are a **pre-legislative record being built to remake the Federal Reserve by statute** — lever by lever.

## Why "castration," not "teardown"

A teardown razes a building to put up another. That is not what this record describes, and the tell is the last hearing: the June 2026 field hearing in Oklahoma City is a *love-letter* to the Fed's structure — the twelve regional banks, Robert Owen, "Main Street's seat at the table." You do not venerate what you intend to demolish. The institution is being **preserved** — even flattered.

What is being removed is its *potency*. Across the reform witnesses (Lacker, Hoenig, Pollock, Michel, Nelson, Schrager) the same anatomy is excised every time:

- **The second mandate.** The Price Stability Act (Hill) strikes "maximum employment" from 12 U.S.C. 225a — one line, half the Fed's reason to act.
- **The toolkit.** Lacker's blueprint: the Fed sets *one* interest rate (strip the other four-plus), holds **bills only**, no QE discretion, no credit allocation.
- **The balance sheet.** Nelson and Schrager: shrink the post-2008 sheet from ~$9T back toward "a few hundred billion," end the floor system, possibly end interest on reserves. Quarles' phrase — an *"attractive nuisance... irresistibly appealing to... congressmen"* — is the honest description of what's at stake.
- **The independence from Treasury — the keystone.** "Revisiting the Treasury-Fed Accord" asks whether to undo the 1951 act that *gave* the Fed its independence in the first place.

None of this abolishes the Fed. It leaves a living institution — a chairman, twelve banks, a building — that can do nothing the Treasury does not allow. The power isn't destroyed; it's **transferred**: to the Treasury (Bessent becomes the potent actor; the Fed a gelding that sets one overnight rate and services the debt management handed to it) and to the private rails (stablecoins, tokenized Treasuries) that route the dollar *around* the neutered Fed. Pollock quoted McChesney Martin on why fiscal dominance is dangerous — *"you should not have the borrower be the boss of the lender"* — and the entire campaign is engineered to invert exactly that. That sentence is the castration.

The one place "teardown" survives: the post-2008 *operating apparatus* (ample reserves, the floor system, QE, interest-on-reserves, the $9T sheet) genuinely is being ripped out, not merely neutered. So the precise verdict: **a castration of the institution, carried out via a teardown of its post-2008 organs.**

## Read the titles in order — the blueprint

Each hearing works one statutory lever of the Federal Reserve Act:

1. **Mar 4 2025 — Examining Monetary Policy and Economic Opportunity.** *Framing:* establish the premise (Michel/Cato: "the Federal Reserve is not an indispensable part of the economy").
2. **Apr 8 2025 — U.S. Treasury Debt in the Monetary System.** Map the Fed-Treasury dependence; $52T of debt by 2035.
3. **May 15 2025 — Examining Treasury Market Fragilities.** Establish the fragility that justifies the cure. (Cetina: dollar "in a currency crisis relative to gold"; Duffie: tokenized Treasuries coming.)
4. **Sep 17 2025 — "Less Mandates. More Independence."** *First cut:* the single-mandate **Price Stability Act**.
5. **Dec 2 2025 — Primary Dealers and Balance Sheet Constraints.** The replacement buyer surfaces (Tabacchi: GENIUS-Act stablecoins now buy T-bills, $2–4T projected).
6. **Jan 14 2026 — Striking the Right Balance Sheet.** *Second cut:* dismantle the floor system / interest-on-reserves; the "attractive nuisance."
7. **Mar 18 2026 — Revisiting the Treasury-Fed Accord.** *The keystone:* re-subordinate the Fed to Treasury; "fiscal dominance" in the committee's own ink; Lacker's five-point blueprint; English names **Bessent** as the source.
8. **Apr 29 2026 — Derivatives' Role in the Treasury Market.** The surveillance watchdog (OFR) being defunded; tokenization/stablecoins normalized; the collateral "circularity."
9. **Jun 12 2026 — Examining the Structure of the Federal Reserve System.** The body preserved and venerated, while Davidson reads the demolition permits into the Q&A; "Chair Walsh" confirms Warsh now runs the Fed.

## The four converging tracks

The hearings are one rail of four:

1. **The Record** — this hearing series, building the statutory case.
2. **The Rails** — GENIUS Act (stablecoins, enacted) + CLARITY Act (crypto market structure), legalizing the replacement plumbing.
3. **The Capture** — Warsh nominated Jan 30 2026, seated by June, hiring Paul Winfree (author of Project 2025's Fed chapter); the CBDC-ban executive order.
4. **The Gate** — Custodia → SCOTUS, master accounts (Kraken, "skinny" accounts), the courts prying open the crypto rails.

Senate flank: Rand Paul's "Audit the Fed." Treasury flank: Bessent's gold-reset and "Gain-of-Function" essay.

## The through-line in the witness chairs

The reform argument is carried by a consistent bench — **Mercatus / former Reserve Bank presidents** (Hoenig, Lacker), **Cato** (Michel), **Mises** (Pollock), **Bank Policy Institute** (Nelson), **Manhattan Institute** (Schrager). Hoenig testifies twice (the Accord and the Structure hearings); English (Yale) is the recurring establishment counterweight. The defenders never argue the Fed is perfect — only that the cure is worse. That asymmetry is itself a tell: the reformers have a *program*; the defenders have a *caution*.

## What it builds toward

Stack the levers and the destination is a Fed **demoted from sovereign to utility**: single-mandated, audited, balance-sheet- and IOR-constrained, structurally rebalanced toward the regional banks, and re-tethered to Treasury via an Accord rewrite — while the dollar itself migrates onto stablecoin/tokenized rails that Congress just legalized and the courts just forced open. The vault's *"US Treasury note replacing the Federal Reserve note"* note is the symbolic capstone: the Fed stops *issuing* the dollar and starts *servicing* it.

## How this refines the chronicle's prediction

Prediction #3 (made 2026-06-04) said the Monetary Reset would be "gradual, not catastrophic — no discrete dollar-reset event." The hearing record **supports the gradual read and proves the mechanism**: this is being done by statute, hearing by hearing, not by an overnight repricing. But it sharpens the call — **the reset isn't a midnight event you'll catch on a price chart; it's a legislative package.** The thing to watch isn't a candle. It's a **markup.**

## What to watch next (procedural tells, in order)

- A Task Force **report** or a consolidated chairman's bill drawing the hearing record together.
- **House floor action, chamber passage or enactment** of H.R. 5396 or a successor Federal Reserve Act amendment. Committee markup and formal reporting have occurred; neither changed operative law.
- The **CLARITY Act** floor outcome (the rails).
- A **SCOTUS** cert decision on Custodia (the gate) and the *Trump v. Cook* removal case (independence).
- **Warsh's** first FOMC and any IOR / balance-sheet move (the capture in action).
- The **title of the next Task Force hearing** — it names the next lever.
