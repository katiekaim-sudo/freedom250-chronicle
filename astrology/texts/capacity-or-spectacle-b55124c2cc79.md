# Capacity or Spectacle?

## Why this room matters

AI, energy, industrial policy, defense procurement and science money all claim
to be rebuilding American capacity. This tour keeps announcement, funding,
award, construction, delivery, acceptance, operation and effect on separate
clocks so the Observatory can see what was actually built.

**Forward story route:** [2027–2030 forecast route](capacity-or-spectacle-2027-30-forecast-route.md) follows the four member braids through funding, physical inputs, acceptance and repair. It is a working archetypal forecast, not a combined research owner or Forecast Ledger call.

## Tour stops

1. Mandate and funded carrier.
2. Named project, supplier and physical dependency.
3. Delivered object and acceptance test.
4. Operating unit, user and repeated output.
5. Stress, failure, loss owner and remedy.

## Strongest ordinary rival

The stories may share rhetoric and resource pressure while remaining unrelated
programs with uneven delivery rather than one coherent capacity regime.
