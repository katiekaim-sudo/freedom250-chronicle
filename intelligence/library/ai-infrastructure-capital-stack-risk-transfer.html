Research summary

AI infrastructure uses several financing objects rather than one stack: GPU lease funds, ring-fenced GPU project collateral, AI-cloud operating companies, data-center property and dedicated power. Ownership, debt service and terminal loss therefore sit in different entities and must be proved transaction by transaction.
