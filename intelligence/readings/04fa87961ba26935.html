Question: What did Secretary Scott Bessent mean when he said Iran's shadow banking system was buckling, and what would it take to establish that Iran's banking system had actually been stopped?
Short answer
The August 7 public record does not establish that the United States switched off Iran's domestic banking system. It establishes a coordinated attack on Iran's external financial adapters: U.S.-jurisdiction property and transactions, foreign-bank correspondent access, shell-company and exchange-house accounts, oil-revenue conversion, and crypto exchanges and wallets.
That pressure can impair foreign-currency access, trade finance, oil-revenue repatriation and import settlement while domestic rial deposits, card authorization and Iranian clearing systems continue to operate. A literal domestic shutdown requires separate bank- or rail-level operating evidence.

Controlling boundaries
A designation is not an asset seizure.
A blocked asset is not vested government property.
A correspondent-account restriction is not revocation of a domestic bank license.
A SWIFT disconnection is a messaging restriction, not the movement or final settlement of money.
A listed wallet address is not proof that an issuer, exchange or custodian executed a freeze.
A rejected cross-border payment is not proof of a domestic card, ACH or RTGS outage.
A sanctions-pressure claim is not proof of bank insolvency, resolution or depositor loss.
A historical architecture source is not current operating telemetry.

Current adjudication
Supported: Treasury is constricting Iran's ability to turn oil and other foreign earnings into usable cross-border funding by targeting the banks, foreign accounts, exchange houses, shells and crypto adapters that perform the conversion. On August 24 it expanded the sectoral designation perimeter, made new immediate designations and suspended five authorization channels. On August 28 FinCEN proposed Section 311 Special Measure Five against Banque Misr's five UAE branches, while OFAC separately designated Reza Mohammad Taeedi and Kameng Trading Limited.

Treasury/Fed/IMF monetary-system change. Current CBUAE license and Banque Misr
UAE remittance pages show public operating availability, not a named executed
