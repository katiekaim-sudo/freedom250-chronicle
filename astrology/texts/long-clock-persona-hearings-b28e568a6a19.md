cycle seed seeks an operating body**: its derived interface, routing circuit,
or public aim. It is not retained as another timing layer or as corroboration.

is opened afterward and is allowed to add no more than one or two materially
new questions.

near its seed position is not news.** Within a month it is automatic carryover,
and proximity alone remains carryover at longer intervals. Only a verified
condition, relationship-lane, or genuinely new derived-angle change can qualify
for promotion.
