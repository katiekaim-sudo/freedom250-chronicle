Freedom 250 · Banks and the dollar · Settlement

Which private shared-ledger settlement networks settle in central-bank money?

Of the 12 market-infrastructure systems in the library, 1 is recorded as settling in central-bank money: the Sterling Fnality Payment System, run by Fnality UK Ltd (supervised by the Bank of England). 4 move banks' own tokenized deposits, which are commercial-bank money, and for the other 7 the library does not say what money they settle in.

What's still uncertain

  • A system is placed in the central-bank group only where the master's description says it settles in central-bank money; the others may be grouped differently once their settlement asset is recorded.
  • Swift's ledger records the payment commitment; the master says final settlement still happens afterwards through existing payment systems.
  • 7 of the 12 systems were not re-checked on the web; those cards say so.
