# Sun–Jupiter

## The planets

**Sun** — *Authority, illumination, "everything revolves around the Sun."* MUNDANE: the head of government (the President); the executive branch; the focus of executive power. The Sun's condition shows how the government will fare. (Greer: "the Sun represents the actual head of government — whoever officially calls the shots in the executive branch.")

**Jupiter** — *Expansion, faith, abundance, Zeus on the throne.* MUNDANE: the judiciary; organized religion; higher education; foreign relations and diplomacy; the nation's general prosperity and optimism; the banking system (in its expansive, lending mode). Jupiter shows whether the nation feels lucky, generous, and confident — or overextended and self-righteous.

## The combination — mundane

Under helpful aspects (conjunction, sextile, trine), Sun-Jupiter is what Greer calls "THE most favorable aspect in mundane astrology." The result is "peace and prosperity at home and abroad; the government receives significant good luck." Everything breaks the executive's way. The economy expands, foreign relations warm, legislation passes, and the judiciary cooperates with (or at least does not obstruct) the government's agenda. The President looks magnanimous and successful. Diplomatic summits produce real agreements. The national mood is confident and generous. This is the closest a mundane chart gets to an unambiguous blessing.

Under hostile aspects (square, opposition), Jupiter's excess shows its shadow side. Prosperity may still come, but it arrives alongside "lurid scandals and highly publicized crimes," as Greer notes. The government overreaches — too much spending, too much confidence, too many promises. The judiciary pushes back against executive overreach. Foreign relations are marked by arrogance rather than diplomacy. The economy runs hot but unevenly, generating resentment. The scandals that emerge under hostile Sun-Jupiter tend to be spectacular, theatrical, and impossible to ignore — Jupiter does nothing quietly.

**As trigger:** Sun-Jupiter aspects are fast-moving (days to about two weeks) and mark the specific moments when prosperity, judicial action, diplomatic developments, and national confidence activate whatever broader outer-planet storylines are in play.
