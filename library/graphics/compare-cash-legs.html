Freedom 250 · Crypto and digital money · Comparison across links

Compare the money used to settle payments

Open the new focused comparison → · This complete earlier form remains available as a reference.

30 systems and money instruments, side by side: 5 stablecoin, 10 commercial-bank deposit token, 3 central-bank unit, 1 off-ledger final settlement, 1 carries more than one kind, 10 kind of money not stated in the row. A stablecoin is a claim on its issuer, a deposit token is a claim on one bank, a central-bank unit is a claim on the central bank, and several ledgers only coordinate payments that finally settle somewhere else.

What's still uncertain

  • The masters carry no 'kind of money' field. This page reads it from the Crypto Hub's links and node kinds first; for 12 rows it falls back to a sentence in the row's own description (printed under 'How we know'). A field in the master would settle these.
  • Issuer liability, representation, orchestration and legal discharge remain separate.
  • 2 systems have a different status in the Crypto Hub than in the market-infrastructure master; both readings are printed, and the master is the home for build status.
  • FedNow, Fedwire and the other public rails are not rows: neither the hub's cash-leg ecosystem nor the market-infrastructure master carries them.
  • 10 rows do not say what kind of money they move: Citi Token Services / SCB, DBS Token Services, Lloyds / Agorá, UBS Digital Cash, DTC Tokenization Service, Canton Network, Broadridge Distributed Ledger Repo (DLR), Broadridge DLX, CME Group tokenized cash on Google Cloud Universal Ledger, Paxos Securities Settlement Company (PSSC).
