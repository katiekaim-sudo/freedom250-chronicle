Stablecoin Adapter Stack — Wallet × Processor × Acquirer

The conversion layer forming behind cards, checkout pages, merchant payouts and network settlement

Stablecoin adoption is entering commerce through controlled adapters, not through a universal crypto terminal. The firms that already own the wallet, merchant contract, payout instruction, network obligation or reconciliation ledger can change the funding or settlement asset while preserving the familiar checkout surface.

The controlled adapter stack

A payment is not one rail. Each product assigns these jobs to different companies and different legal clocks.
