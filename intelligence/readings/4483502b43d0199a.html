Status vocabulary
COMMERCIAL — VERIFIED SHIPMENT: a named company or customer says commercial product shipped.
COMMERCIAL — COMPANY REPORTED: company reports commercial operation, but no named shipment or audited output series was located.
COMMISSIONED / RAMP: equipment has entered commissioning or early production; do not infer nameplate.
PILOT / SAMPLE: first articles or samples; not bulk supply.
CONSTRUCTION: physical project work is underway.
ANNOUNCED / SELECTED: site or capacity target only.
MOU / PROPOSED: non-final partnership or transaction.
CONDITIONAL FINANCE: government or lender commitment still subject to conditions.
FINAL AGREEMENT: operative agreement exists; milestones and disbursements remain separate.
INCUMBENT — SCALE NOT REFRESHED: established commercial producer whose exact current tonnes were not verified in this sweep.
