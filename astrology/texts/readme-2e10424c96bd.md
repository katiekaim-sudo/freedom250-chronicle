## Method boundary

The research method defines a persona as the **first later geocentric tropical Sun crossing of the fixed radix target longitude**, calculated for the radix place. This pilot uses tropical longitude, geocentric positions, Whole Sign houses, traditional rulers, the true node, and the current Washington, D.C. convention.

Before interpretation, the package separates three kinds of information:

- **Guaranteed:** the Persona Sun repeats the seed degree, so its radix contacts merely restate the target.
- **Dependent:** Saturn, Neptune, and the slow planets remain near their seed positions only 28.67 days later. Persistence and near-returns receive no independent vote.
- **Potentially additive:** the derived Ascendant and ruler route, Whole Sign houses, Sun house, MC/10th governance, internal network, and tight contacts that are not mechanically forced.

Katie’s slow-body gate is explicit: an outer or slow planet being near its radix place within a month is not news. It becomes a focus only if it stations or changes direction, changes sign, or acquires/loses a genuinely new aspect or derived-angle relationship. The novelty belongs to that changed condition or relationship, never to the near-return itself.

Applied here:

- Saturn, Uranus, Neptune, and Pluto neither station nor change sign between seed and persona; their near-returns are carryover.
- The nearly exact MC–Uranus opposition qualifies because the **persona MC is new**. Persona Uranus conjunct radix Uranus does not qualify.
- Jupiter changes from retrograde at the seed to direct after stationing at **11 March 03:30 UTC / 10 March 23:30 EDT**, then forms the applying `0.472°` trine with Mars.
