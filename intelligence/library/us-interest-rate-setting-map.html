The short version (reviewed by Katie)

Us Interest Rate Setting Map

There is no single rate or rate setter. The FOMC sets a target range for overnight fed funds; EFFR is calculated from trades; the Board sets IORB; the Desk runs ON RRP/SRF; Reserve Bank directors establish discount rates subject to the Board; Treasury yields, SOFR, prime and loan rates have their own setters. Charging below market is generally lawful; tax (AFR/§7872), Regulation O, fair lending, safety-and-soundness, credit-union rules and public-credit budgeting decide the consequences.
