Research summary

The task force remains a federated operating architecture: the White House coordinates, agencies own program screening and action, DOJ houses the National Fraud Enforcement Division and multi-agency National Fraud Detection Center, and State nodes consume or contribute leads and records. The September 22 CMS event supplies a completed agency-action receipt: about 315,000 unauthorized Marketplace enrollments covering more than 760,000 people were cancelled after CMS/insurer review. It also makes agency use of pattern recognition and AI/ML at a prepayment eligibility gate explicit. But CMS says the cancellations are expected to return approximately $2.2 billion in APTC; no collected-recovery ledger or NFDC lead/model lineage is published. The NFDC Charter text, technical annexes, system/model records, lead-to-case lineage, false-positive and remedy measures, and reconciled money chain remain unresolved. Identity, eligibility, cancellation, expected recoupment, adjudication and collected recovery remain separate proof states.
