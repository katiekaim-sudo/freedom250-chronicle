## Executive readout

1. **CFTC is building a connected regulatory perimeter.** Its open suite spans
   product identity, reporting, continuous/perpetual trading, cross-margining,
   firm affiliations, private-pool registration and compute as a new underlier.
2. **Compute is the week's sharpest novel bridge.** CFTC asks whether a
   derivative can be permitted when cash pricing is opaque, the underlier may
   not be standardized or liquid, and settlement data may be outside regulatory
   observation and surveillance.
3. **The private-pool proposal exposes a reporting seam.** CFTC itself asks
   whether CPO relief plus a higher Form PF threshold could leave some pools
   outside both principal reporting systems.
4. **Treasury's GENIUS clock is now real.** The Section 3 NPRM was published
   August 18 and comments close October 19. The earlier package's unresolved
   publication-relative watch can be replaced; the proposal is still not a
   final stablecoin regime.
5. **SEC crypto moved one clock, not one legal state.** S7-2026-27 reached Public
   Inspection August 20 and is scheduled for publication August 21. It remains
   a proposal; its controlling comment deadline must be booked after actual
   publication.
6. **DOJ's National Fraud Enforcement Division is a real chart delta.** It has
   an earlier administrative/operating history, but the final organizational
   rule published August 18 becomes effective August 24. Those clocks must not
   be collapsed.
7. **The CFTC Innovation Advisory Committee is an overlay, not a subsidiary
   tree.** Its renewed charter is advisory only. Its private-market members do
   not become federal entities or owners.
8. **Other agencies exposed public-private operating designs.** DOT proposed a
   30–50-year corridor-manager concession architecture; NOAA opened a voluntary
   whole-of-government space certification pilot; CMS created a nursing-home
   workforce data/payment system; Treasury designated OpenCorporates data for
   Do Not Pay screening.
9. **Final authority also moved.** DOE issued time-bounded emergency generating
   orders; DOL published a final Wagner-Peyser staffing rule; CBP set a future
   importer-identity enforcement clock; OFAC made a new designation package.
10. **A rumored White House crypto meeting should not be overbooked.** CFTC
    remarks confirm an August 19 White House Innovation Meeting, but no official
    White House instrument or decision was found on the checked surfaces.
