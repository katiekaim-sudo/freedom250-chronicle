Stablecoins in Everyday Payments — Wallets, Processors and Merchant Banks

Who converts between stablecoins and dollars behind cards, checkout pages, merchant payouts and bank settlement

Stablecoins are entering everyday commerce through companies that already run payments, not through a universal crypto terminal. A firm that already holds the customer's wallet, the merchant contract, the payout instruction, a card network's duty to settle or the account-matching ledger can switch the funding or settlement asset. The checkout the customer sees stays the same.

The controlled adapter stack

A payment is not one rail. Each product assigns these jobs to different companies and different legal clocks.

Wallet / custody

A stablecoin wallet the merchant controls, or the payout recipient's wallet.

Merchant contract

Worldpay holds the merchant contract for card acceptance or business payouts.

Conversion / routing

What actually changed

Boundary

A merchant paid in stablecoins does not prove the customer paid in stablecoins. Nor does it prove a chip-card (EMV) terminal read a blockchain asset. Worldpay's live settlement product and its later BVNK payout relationship rest on evidence from different dates.
