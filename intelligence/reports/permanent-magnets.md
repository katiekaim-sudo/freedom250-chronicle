# Permanent Magnets — the hidden motor stack

**Status:** first source-first sweep  
**Cutoff:** 2026-07-22  
**Geographic frame:** global stack, with detailed United States reindustrialization and China-control lanes

## Short answer

The permanent-magnet field is in a real industrial transition, but the public story is usually one layer too shallow.

China still controls the system because it combines separation, metal and alloy production, magnet manufacturing, equipment, process knowledge and scale. The United States has restarted genuine commercial magnet production through Noveon, MP Materials and eVAC, with USA Rare Earth commissioning a fourth platform. But the flood of proposed American capacity for 2027–2030 is not yet equivalent to qualified, continuous output.

The decisive change is the role of the state. Federal policy has moved beyond grants into equity, warrants, conditional and final loans, price floors, guaranteed offtake, tariff protection, strategic inventory finance and contractor supply-chain mandates. MP Materials is the clearest national-champion model; USA Rare Earth, Vulcan/ReElement and Energy Fuels show variants of the same structure.

The near-term bottleneck is therefore not simply geology. It is the conversion and qualification chain:

> separated NdPr/Dy/Tb/Sm oxide → metal → alloy/flake → sintered or bonded magnet → coating and machining → motor or component qualification → sustained deliveries

## The factual stack

| 8. Inventory/logistics | stockpiles, traders, offtake and reserve finance | absorbs timing and price risk | Project Vault; Hartree; Mercuria Americas; Traxys; DLA stockpile |

The Department of Energy's permanent-magnet supply-chain assessment estimated China's 2020 shares at about 58% of rare-earth mining, 89% of separation, 90% of metal refining and 92% of magnet-alloy/manufacturing capacity. The report is old enough that current plant states must be refreshed, but the stage-by-stage concentration pattern remains the right baseline. ## What is actually live in the United States

### Commercial magnet production and shipments

- **Noveon Magnetics, San Marcos, Texas:** established commercial sintered-NdFeB production. GM deliveries began in July 2025; ABB monthly deliveries began in August 2025; a five-year Nidec agreement contemplates more than 1,000 total tonnes. Its facility is commonly described as having 2,000-tpa capacity, but a public audited sustained-output series was not located. [GM agreement](https://noveon.co/general-motors-noveon-magnetics-sign-multi-year-agreement) [ABB agreement](https://noveon.co/abb-selects-noveon-magnetics) [Nidec agreement](https://noveon.co/nidec-and-noveon-magnetics-enter-agreement)
- **MP Materials, Independence, Texas:** commercial metal production began in 2024; first alloy flake and finished magnets on commercial equipment followed in 2025. MP said 2026 would be the GM ramp year. The planned 10X campus would bring total U.S. nameplate to roughly 10,000 tpa, with commissioning targeted for 2028. [2025 results](https://investors.mpmaterials.com/investor-news/news-details/2026/MP-Materials-Reports-Fourth-Quarter-and-Full-Year-2025-Results/default.aspx) [10X site](https://investors.mpmaterials.com/investor-news/news-details/2026/MP-Materials-Selects-Northlake-Texas-as-the-Site-of-10X-a-New-U-S--Rare-Earth-Magnet-Manufacturing-Campus/default.aspx)
- **eVAC Magnetics, Sumter, South Carolina:** shipped its first U.S.-made commercial NdFeB magnets in December 2025. The company said the new plant would scale to 2,000 tpa in Q1 2026 and could expand further; sustained 2026 throughput has not yet been independently established in the reviewed public record. [eVAC first shipment](https://evac-magnetics.com/shared/eVAC-news-first-magnets)

### Commissioning, planned scale or adjacent output

- **USA Rare Earth, Stillwater, Oklahoma:** commissioned Phase 1a in March 2026 and targeted customer orders in Q2, a 600-tpa run rate by year-end 2026 and 1,200 tpa in Q1 2027. Those are separate clocks. Its June 2026 Commerce agreement supports eventual 10,000-tpa magnet and 10,000-tpa metal/alloy capabilities across Stillwater, Blacksburg and Round Top. [Commerce final agreement](https://www.nist.gov/news-events/news/2026/06/department-commerces-chips-program-finalizes-definitive-agreement-usa-rare)
- **Vulcan Elements, North Carolina:** operates a small commercial/R&D facility; its proposed Benson campus is a 10,000-tpa project. The $620 million Office of Strategic Capital loan commitment is conditional, not a disbursed final loan. ReElement's related $80 million commitment supports the refining side. [OSC announcement](https://www.war.gov/News/Releases/Release/Article/4339788/office-of-strategic-capital-agrees-to-joint-700m-conditional-loan-commitment-wi/)
- **Energy Fuels, Utah:** commercially produces separated NdPr oxide and is developing heavy-rare-earth separation and metallization. It is a midstream supplier, not presently a U.S. finished-magnet producer. Its $725 million OSC financing announced in June 2026 is conditional. [OSC announcement](https://www.war.gov/News/Releases/Release/Article/4520819/the-department-of-wars-office-of-strategic-capital-signs-725-million-conditiona/)
- **Niron Magnetics, Minnesota:** samples rare-earth-free iron-nitride magnets and is developing pilot/Plant 1 plus a proposed 10,000-tpa site. It is strategically interesting substitution technology, not yet a bulk substitute supply source. [Niron manufacturing plan](https://nironmagnetics.com/niron-magnetics-advances-u-s-permanent-magnet-manufacturing-plans/)

## The China control mechanism

China's leverage is not merely its mine share.

1. **Industrial concentration:** separation, metallization, alloying and magnet production are more concentrated than mining.
2. **Export licensing:** China's April 4, 2025 controls cover seven medium/heavy rare-earth families, including specified permanent-magnet materials containing controlled elements. A license requirement is not a total ban, but it creates transaction-level permission and delay risk. [MOFCOM Announcement No. 18](https://english.mofcom.gov.cn/Policies/AnnouncementsOrders/art/2025/art_0dd87cbee7b045bf93fabe6ab2faceee.html)
3. **Multiple legal clocks:** broader October 2025 controls were later suspended to November 10, 2026, while the April controls remained in force. General licenses for selected exporters eased some flows without ending the control architecture. [SCIO suspension explanation](https://english.scio.gov.cn/pressroom/2025-11/10/content_118168740.html) [April 2026 status](https://english.scio.gov.cn/m/pressroom/2026-04/10/content_118428943.html)
4. **Equipment and knowledge:** production equipment, recipes, yield learning, patents and a skilled workforce are part of the moat.
5. **Price power:** excess capacity and low prices can make non-China projects unfinanceable even while physical concentration makes them strategically necessary.

## The American state-market architecture

The United States is now actively manufacturing a market, not only subsidizing factories.

| Mechanism | Concrete example | Exact state |
|---|---|---|
| Equity and warrant | DoD's MP Materials preferred-equity/warrant package | executed 2025 transaction; conversion/exercise conditions matter |
| Price floor | MP NdPr floor of $110/kg for ten years | contract support beginning Q4 2025, subject to terms |
| Guaranteed outlet | 10X ten-year arrangement for 100% of production to defense or syndicated commercial buyers | future output support; factory not yet commissioned |
| Final grant/loan agreement | Commerce–USA Rare Earth, up to $277m incentives and up to $1.3bn loan | final agreement signed June 3, 2026; disbursement and milestones remain separate |
| Conditional loan | Vulcan/ReElement $700m combined; Energy Fuels $725m | commitments subject to closing conditions |
| Strategic inventory finance | Project Vault | EXIM approved up to a $10bn direct loan; roughly $2bn private capital was described separately |
| Tariff | Section 301 rate on China permanent magnets | scheduled at 25% in 2026 |
| Import-market design | Section 232 PCMDP proclamation | negotiations/price-floor policy active; no later public remedy located by the cutoff after the 180-day milestone |
| Contractor qualification | July 20, 2026 critical-materials EO | active order; rules, mapping and qualification clocks still ahead |

The MP arrangement is especially important. DoD did not just award a grant: it became a capital participant, supported price, supported demand and financed heavy-rare-earth work. [MP/DoD partnership](https://investors.mpmaterials.com/investor-news/news-details/2025/MP-Materials-Announces-Transformational-Public-Private-Partnership-with-the-Department-of-Defense-to-Accelerate-U-S--Rare-Earth-Magnet-Independence/default.aspx)

Project Vault adds a financial and logistical layer. EXIM described OEM demand indications from Clarios, GE Vernova, Western Digital and Boeing and supplier participation by Hartree, Mercuria Americas and Traxys. That makes commodity traders and inventory finance part of the magnet story. It is not accurately reduced to “the government created a $12 billion reserve.” [EXIM Project Vault](https://www.exim.gov/news/project-vault)

## The clock that matters now

The July 20, 2026 executive order moves the defense market from general sourcing preference toward supplier-level proof.

- Starting January 1, 2027, routine non-availability waivers under 10 U.S.C. 4872 are to cease unless a contractor accepts an approved mitigation plan.
- Within 180 days, the Department of War is to develop policy for mapping critical supply chains through all contractor tiers; implementing regulations follow within 90 days.
- The order defines an “indentured Bill of Materials” back to raw-material origin and orders accelerated testing and qualification.

This turns qualification records, bills of material, foreign ownership/control, feedstock origin and funded mitigation plans into immediate corporate variables. [July 20 order](https://www.whitehouse.gov/presidential-actions/2026/07/securing-americas-defense-supply-chains-and-ensuring-domestic-acquisition-of-critical-materials/)

## Demand: much wider than the EV headline

- **Defense:** actuators, guidance, radar, aircraft, submarines, drones, satellites and many motors.
- **Industrial/data centers:** ABB's Noveon agreement explicitly ties magnets to motors for cooling towers, pumps, building systems, HVAC and data-center growth.
- **Semiconductor fabrication:** motors and magnetic components in lithography, deposition, etch and vacuum equipment; Commerce used this as the CHIPS nexus for magnet awards.
- **Automotive:** propulsion, steering, pumps, speakers and auxiliary systems in both combustion and electric vehicles.
- **Robotics and automation:** compact high-torque motors.
- **Consumer electronics and storage:** speakers, haptics, cameras and hard-disk drives.
- **Energy:** direct-drive wind, generators and efficient industrial motors.

## Substitution and recycling

There are three different substitution claims:

1. **reduce heavy rare earths inside NdFeB** through grain-boundary diffusion and microstructure control;
2. **replace NdFeB in selected motor designs** with ferrite, wound-field or reluctance systems;
3. **replace rare-earth magnets as material** with candidates such as iron nitride.

They should not be combined. Proterial and Shin-Etsu are advancing heavy-rare-earth-free or saving NdFeB formulations; this reduces Dy/Tb exposure but still uses NdFeB. Niron is a genuine rare-earth-free material program but remains pre-scale. [Proterial technology](https://www.proterial.com/e/press/2025/n0722b.html) [Shin-Etsu magnet business](https://www.shinetsu.co.jp/en/products/electronics-materials/neodymium-magnet/)

Recycling also has two clocks: factory scrap can return quickly and predictably; end-of-life recovery depends on collection, disassembly, chemistry and product design. Noveon, MP/Apple and Shin-Etsu demonstrate meaningful routes, but recycling cannot be counted as free new supply.

## Strongest Freedom 250 joins

### 1. The state is becoming the market maker

The magnet stack joins the Observatory's broader state–private seam: public capital, private factories, price support, offtake guarantees, tariffs and mandatory qualification are being assembled into one industrial control system.

### 2. Project Vault is a strategic-liquidity mechanism

The reserve sits between Treasury/EXIM-style public finance, commodity traders and industrial users. It is inventory finance for supply continuity, not simply a pile of rocks.

### 3. The “processing” claim is correct but incomplete

The vault's Bessent and Burgum events correctly identify processing. The deeper version is: separation, metallization, alloy, magnet-making yield and customer qualification are different choke points.

### 4. Data-center growth has a physical motor layer

The stack links Technology/AI to mines and industrial motors through cooling, pumps, robotics, storage and semiconductor tools. “AI infrastructure” is not only chips and electricity.

### 5. Capacity announcements may outrun feedstock and customers

If every announced U.S. plant reaches nameplate, the late-2020s market could contain duplicated capacity, feedstock competition and dependence on price floors. The correct watch is qualified tonnes delivered by specification—not ribbon cuttings or aggregate nameplate.

## Bottom line

This is a real reindustrialization story, not just rhetoric. But it is still early, state-shaped and vulnerable to category errors. The most defensible current reading is:

> China retains system control; the United States has restarted real production; Washington is constructing a protected domestic market; and the decisive proof will be qualified, continuous, origin-traceable deliveries through 2027–2029.
