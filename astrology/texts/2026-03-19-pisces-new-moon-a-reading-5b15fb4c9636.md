Saturn's fall and Venus's detriment make relationship difficult, not impossible; success requires clearer limits than goodwill alone can provide.

This lunation is the winter chart that most fully returns to the Capricorn ingress. The seed becomes trustworthy when service, policy, and relationship name the same obligation.
