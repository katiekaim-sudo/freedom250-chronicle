# Institutional Regime Waves — Astrology and Executive Order Ledger

## Reading rule

Astrology is archetypal context, not proof, causation, or a day-level prediction engine. The ingress supplies the governing question. Lunations carry, emphasize, reveal, or remain quiet within that climate. The factual lane separately records what an order authorized, which legal person retained authority, and whether the change reached rule, operation, transaction, contest, reversal, or continuity.

The key comparison is therefore not “did astrology cause or predict this event?” It is: **what themes does the lunar chapter naturally endorse, and what kind of institutional change is independently visible in the record?**

The first entries of Neptune and Saturn into Aries and Uranus into Gemini are treated as preview passes. Their retrogrades back into Pisces and Taurus revisit unfinished older terrain before their durable 2026 entries. A first entry is not assumed to be consummation.

## Wave 1 — The Charter Stayed; the Control Plane Moved

### Governing sky

The 2024 Aries ingress remained the strict Greer governor until the 2025 Aries ingress. Its fixed rising sign made the year-long authority decisive; the 2024 Capricorn ingress was a nested winter frame, not a replacement governor.

The late-winter New Moon chapters moved from Capricorn through Aquarius and Pisces into the March 29 Aries solar eclipse. The February 28 Pisces New Moon contacted the 2024 ingress Venus and Saturn. The March 14 Virgo lunar eclipse was quiet to the outgoing governor but bridged into the approaching 2025 Aries Saturn. The March 29 Aries solar eclipse contacted the new ingress Mercury.

### Factual spine

There were 109 numbered executive orders from January 20 through March 31, including 93 before the 2025 Aries ingress. The strongest proved pattern was not wholesale abolition of legal persons. It was insertion of horizontal control surfaces:

- DOGE and USDS access and observation;
- OMB and OIRA review;
- Treasury screening and payment controls;
- personnel, hiring, probation, and reduction machinery;
- GSA and procurement consolidation proposals;
- anti-silo access to agency records.

Agency heads, certifying officers, appropriations, organic statutes, courts, and program-specific law continued to constrain consummation. Several statutory reduction targets later continued, were restored, or remained contested.

### Working synthesis

**The legal persons largely endured; the operating control plane moved.** The institutional order began crumbling most visibly at the level of inherited discretion, internal routing, and who could see, review, approve, staff, screen, and release.
