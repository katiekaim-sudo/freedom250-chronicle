Finding

The earlier planetary umbrellas largely hold. The missing specificity was the relationship clock.

“Saturn is involved in Crypto” is true but too broad. The pair layer separates at least five different questions: institutional promise (Jupiter–Mercury), record and rule under constraint (Saturn–Mercury), innovation meeting implementation (Uranus–Mercury), value promise versus backing (Neptune–Venus), and value enduring obligation (Saturn–Venus). The same Saturn can therefore participate in Crypto, Iran, and AI without making those plots interchangeable.

The assignments work best as temporary lenses on a many-to-many story network. They fail if converted into permanent pair-to-plot ownership.

Overall judgment

The relationship layer materially improves continuity:

  • Planetary umbrellas remain broad enough to cover reality.
  • Synodic phase says where the relationship is in its story.
  • Direct aspect says how the planets are speaking now.
  • Ingress/lunation hierarchy says which clock governs the season and which chart is the chapter.
  • Factual gates decide whether the real-world plot changed.

That is enough to make the plots “stick out” without pretending that astrology discovered a fact or permanently assigned a story to one planet or pair.
