What should change in the government-sweep job

The next iteration should produce a package before it writes event notes. The minimum durable contract is:

  1. Freeze an exclusive start cursor and inclusive cutoff; replay the full date for date-only and batch publishers.
  2. Write a reached-source manifest by source family, including access failures and the exact negative-claim boundary.
  3. Give every candidate an exact ID, primary URL, issuer, source/release clock, legal/effective/implementation clocks, state delta and claim limit.
  4. Search exact IDs and URLs across the vault, Workbench, Catalog and Registry before disposition.
  5. Record one disposition for every material candidate: atomic, fold, repair, duplicate, support, watch, reject or hold.
  6. Never place an inferred or sequencing time in the factual event clock. Leave date-only records date-only.
  7. Create pending Workbench research first. Do not treat visibility in the Catalog, or a generated/app view, as vault promotion.
  8. Reconcile saved-return gates and close with an explicit next cursor.

This adds very little prose to the actual notes. It adds the missing control plane around them.
