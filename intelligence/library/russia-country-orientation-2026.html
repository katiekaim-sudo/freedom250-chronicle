Research summary

There is no single U.S.–Russia relationship. Formal diplomacy survives under severe constraints; New START has expired without a successor; Ukraine diplomacy is active but ad hoc and unresolved; sanctions and export controls operate as object-specific permissions systems; ISS remains a narrow operating exception; and Russia's commodity and industrial connections have been rerouted toward Asia and third countries rather than eliminated. Russia's China and Iran partnerships, DPRK mutual-assistance treaty, CSTO defense obligation, EAEU economic integration and multilateral memberships have different legal weights. The orientation preserves actor, territory, instrument, activation, implementation and operating clocks.
