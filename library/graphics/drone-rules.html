Where does the drone beyond-visual-line-of-sight rule (Part 108) stand, and what other drone rules are pending?

Part 108 is still only a proposal: the FAA and TSA proposed it August 7, 2025, refused more comment time September 29, 2025 and reopened comments on one design question January 28, 2026; the record holds no final rule, although EO 14307 set a 240-day final-rule target ('as appropriate'). The other pending drone rule is the FAA's May 6, 2026 proposal to restrict drones near fixed-site facilities (comments extended to August 5, 2026). 3 items are notices, not rules: Electric Vertical Takeoff and Landing and Advanced Air Mobility Integration Pilot Program — Establishment and Request for Proposals; Implementing Section 927 Waiver Process for Certain Unmanned Aircraft Operations; Unleashing American Drone Dominance (FCC Notice of Inquiry). 2 are final: Integration of Powered-Lift: Pilot Certification and Operations (SFAR); Streamlining Export Controls for Drone Exports.
