Provisional cross-lane ruling

The existing evidence supports a narrower and stronger thesis than either “thousands of committees control government” or “one hidden system is taking over.”

The recurring architecture is a federated procedural substrate:

  1. advisory and research bodies define problems, classifications and possible designs;
  2. councils and standards bodies create common vocabularies, schemas, evidence requirements and review methods;
  3. internal boards and program offices apply portfolio, risk, credit or milestone gates;
  4. existing legal actors retain the final approval, instrument, funding, authorization, settlement or remedy; and
  5. public records become thinnest at the item-level handoff between internal review and final action.

This is meaningful support for inch-by-inch institutional refactoring. It is also meaningful support for the rival incumbent-absorption: many of the same records show inherited agencies, accounting systems, regulators, utilities and program authorities adapting new capability without surrendering their legal role.

Strongest repeated mechanisms

Conditional portability

Financial records repeatedly permit a token, record or interface to change form only if legal rights, eligibility, custody, capital, production, inspection, recovery, clearing and remedy survive. Longer execution hours and tokenized collateral do not automatically create continuous cash, legal finality or loss handling.

Procedural centralization without one command

CDO schemas, common assistance notices, business standards, FedRAMP evidence, TMF portfolio selection and internal acquisition or project gates create a more machine-readable and comparable administrative layer. Member agencies usually retain final approval, funding, risk acceptance and operation.

Mission-selected, stage-gated capacity

Science and industrial records organize work around missions, portfolios, shared compute/data/instruments, milestone finance and faster transition. Genesis and the NSB/NGF chain reach beyond doctrine, while PCAST, STIB and many DOE/NASA/CHIPS item decisions remain below the public-product or action gate.

Private expertise under public sponsorship

The 19 material FFRDC interfaces strongly support the structural proposition that private institutions operate analytical and technical capacity while a federal sponsor retains formal authority. A center publication is not sponsor acceptance, agency policy or operation.

Selective legibility

OIRA, FCC and CFPB expose relatively joinable public-contact records. By contrast, several internal councils, acquisition bodies and program boards publish their architecture but not item-level agendas, votes, recommendations or decision memoranda. This is a public-custody result, not proof of secret coordination or inactivity.

Strongest action joins already held

  • GMAC tokenized-collateral advice to CFTC guidance and a conditioned pilot;
  • GMAC CME–FICC cross-margining advice to paired CFTC/SEC conditional orders;
  • FASAB Technical Bulletin 2025-1 from exposure through issuance;
  • TBAC financing advice to Treasury decisions and auctions;
  • FSOC market-resilience recommendation to an operating interagency working group;
  • NSB committee and Board action to an NSF cooperative agreement and named operating facility;
  • DOT credit-board recommendation through Secretarial approval to an executed TIFIA agreement; and
  • TMF selections through transfer, obligation, deployment and self-reported effects in bounded projects.

Every chain retains its missing clock. Permission is not use; an award is not a payment; a self-reported effect is not independent evaluation.

Counterevidence retained

  • FASAB preferred a comprehensive principles-based statement over piecemeal software amendments.
  • The SEC IAC tokenization recommendation rejected blanket exemption and preserved ownership, intermediary, state-authority and notice protections.
  • Atomic settlement can sacrifice netting and raise funding needs.
  • FedRAMP standards do not replace agency risk acceptance.
  • NASA's APMC chair, not the advisory membership collectively, is the decision authority.
  • DHS acquisition-governance disruption shows institutional discontinuity, not a smooth march toward centralization.
  • Current PCAST has appointments but no located product; STIB has taskings but no located adopted recommendation.
  • Most FFRDC publications have no sponsor-side adoption receipt.
  • Common vocabulary remains a null or discovery signal when no actor, instrument, standard, system or decision right supplies the join.

Wave 1 opening sample

Nine newly verified official-source records open the financial/digital wave. The strongest are the CFTC tokenized-collateral and CME–FICC cross-margining chains. The sample also preserves SEC IAC, FASAB, FSOC and CFTC IAC records at their actual expression levels.
