Research summary

SWIFT says its blockchain-based ledger is ready for initial controlled use inside its infrastructure stack, and 17 named banks are preparing live pilots using bank-issued tokenised deposits. The operator question is therefore resolved at the service level, but no completed SWIFT-ledger transaction, named production corridor, external-cash finality event, public participant rulebook or stablecoin settlement role is established.
