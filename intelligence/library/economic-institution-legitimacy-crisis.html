Research summary

Waves 1–5 organize the legitimacy-crisis lens across 15 contrastive function cases and 119 dated events and feed a generated ten-mode private thesis instrument. The opening Institutional Sovereignty Map asks who retains the control rights over purpose, admission, authorization, authoritative record, execution, settlement and delivery, loss and failure, and correction and remedy, while routing each function into existing factual owners rather than creating a new truth store. The September 24 Braided Thesis defines the parent frame as 1776 2.0—a procedural rebellion attempting to found a new operating order from inside the old one—and compares eight thesis objects at different altitudes across eight shared strands, the original seven arcs, seven genuine tensions, a combined rival and eight return gates without merging their factual owners. The September 24 Growth Architecture makes the positive-case theory inspectable across ten nodes from the human-scale dollar and live records through faster credit, programmable dollar rails, risk-bearing capital, physical buildout, operating capacity, growth as stability and the debt denominator; facts, inference and five-percent belief remain separate, and stablecoin scale is not treated as new risk capital raised. Dedicated Under the Hood and Fed Constellation rooms expose 19 technology objects, five System operating layers and the distinct contributions of all twelve Reserve Banks. The September 8 whole-site Reserve Bank recertification materially deepens the Constellation's operating face with Bank-hosted accounting, Treasury, payment, technology, procurement, data, HR, finance, cash, credit-risk, supervision and contingency functions. The separate September 22 research-and-sensing face adds each Bank's conference contribution and six recurring cross-System fields across AI, digital money, labor, infrastructure, data authority and resilience, while preserving attention as distinct from policy, ownership and operation. The September 17 capital-markets specimen and September 18 mission-to-machine synthesis extend the thesis: institutions are restating retained public functions while execution is decomposed into selected portfolios, regulated claims, shared services, programmable records, private adapters, and new venue or settlement arrangements. The strongest recurring mechanism is that practical control can move faster than legal responsibility, authoritative records, supervision and remedy. Ordinary constitutional adaptation and successful incumbent absorption remain the strongest counter-reading, common architecture is not proof of common command, and no single function-level crisis state is treated as a proxy for the whole system. Katie has accepted the instrument as the project's living private orientation surface; it remains developing rather than a settled verdict.
