- **2026-07-21 — The Government Learns Who Is Dead — Payment Verification Goes Government-Wide** (Treasury/Bureau of the Fiscal Service · Press Release · The end of the paper check). Treasury and the Bureau of the Fiscal Service announce that a government-wide payment verification process to stop federal payments to deceased individuals is now implemented. No Federal Register document, no rule — an announcement that a screen has been switched on across the whole disbursement machine. It sits directly downstream of EO 14247 and the Do Not Pay system of records, and it is the same grammar as the check kill: the payment stops being a thing the government sends and becomes a thing the government first checks. - **2026-07-15 — FedNow's Third Birthday — 1,800 Banks, $271B a Quarter, and a Ceiling Raised Twentyfold** (FedNow Service · Report · Fedwire, FedNow & payment rails). ## 2025

- **2025-12-10 — Standing Repo Facility — Aggregate Limit Removed, Full Allotment (FedTrade Plus)** (Federal Reserve Bank of New York · Operating Policy Statement · Fedwire, FedNow & payment rails). The Desk removes the aggregate operational limit on standing overnight repo operations and moves them to a full-allotment format on the new FedTrade Plus platform (one proposition per security type per operation, $40B max each). The Fed's overnight dollar backstop, formerly capped, is now effectively unlimited — and rebuilt on new rails. Announced Dec 10, effective Dec 11, 2025; a companion note flags a move toward central clearing of the facility.
