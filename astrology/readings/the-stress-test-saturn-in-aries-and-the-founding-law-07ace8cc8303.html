The reading

The law is at its full moon, and the moon is the wrong colour.

Saturn wants one thing, always: to bind. To draw the line and make it hold. In Libra he binds by weighing — the scale, the hearing, the balance, the other side gets a say. That is what a court is, and it is the equipment the republic was handed at birth. In Aries he still wants to bind, but the only tools in the shed are the knife, the first move, the founding blow. Same want. Wrong tools. So he binds the way Aries binds: by cutting — by dissolving the thing to see what was load-bearing, by asserting rather than weighing, by acting first and hearing later.

This is not a demotion. It is the harvest. A full moon is the point where the seed is finally visible — everything the Libra law grew is now lit up and standing on the far side of the circle, where it can be looked at without flattery. Fourteen years of law-as-balance, walked all the way out to the place where balance has no tools, and asked: does any of it hold when nobody is being polite?

And the room it happens in is not neutral. Saturn is in Aries for exactly the window Neptune is in Aries — 2026-02-14 → 2028-04-12, the co-presence the vault already tracks (DR-003). So the stress test of the law runs in the same room, in the same years, as the re-pouring of money's substrate. The bureaucrat and the dissolver share a sign. What is money made of, and who says so — and who is allowed to rule on it — are one question, being asked at the founding degree of the zodiac. That is not a coincidence in the chart. That is the chart.

Both poles, and I do not know which one history picks.

  • The light: a law that has grown soft, procedural and captured is taken out to the far edge and stress-tested — and what survives is re-founded, in the Aries sense: made first, made sharp, made able to say no. Discipline returns. The metal goes back into the money. The line is drawn by someone willing to be hated for drawing it. This is Saturn doing the only honest work a fallen planet can do.
  • The shadow: the cutting is the whole point, and nothing is rebuilt. The scale is smashed because it was inconvenient, the hearing skipped because it was slow, the balance called weakness. Aries binds by founding — and a founding is also a coup, if the thing it founds answers to one man. The same castration grammar the vault has already traced through the Fed, the PCAOB, the CFPB: keep the body, take its hands. Applied now to the law itself.

The symbol is the same in both. Only the people decide which one it becomes — and that, per §2 of the method, is precisely the thing the chart cannot draw.
