Freedom 250 · Foreign relations and world money · International bodies

Is anything replacing the international bodies the U.S. is leaving?

Not wholesale: the registry counts 0 wholesale replacements among the 27 bodies (as of October 4, 2026). 17 bodies still do their job with no successor. 4 were left or cut with no successor at all: UN Human Rights Council, UNRWA, UNESCO, Paris Agreement. Only 3 show a changed default route, each for one bounded job: U.S. foreign health aid now goes through the America First Global Health Strategy bilateral network (the WHO's universal functions are retained); WTO appeals for participating non-U.S. Members go through the MPIA; and a U.S. side-by-side tax regime sits inside the OECD. Two pieces of new machinery exist (Artemis Accords, Board of Peace), and Kyoto Vision for a Golden Age of Science is doctrine only. The Board of Peace's Gaza field handoff was incomplete in September 2026.

How the United States participates in and can leave international organizations
The international order is changing one job at a time. Countries are leaving some bodies, rebuilding others and creating smaller groups for specific tasks; they are not replacing the whole system at once. Evidence through October 15, 2026

What's still uncertain

  • A new statement becomes a replacement only after machinery, resources, normal use and control of the frontier function move.
  • Two counts differ: the registry's own counts say 18 active or retained incumbents and 5 exits or contractions without a successor; the ladder places 17 bodies at level 0 and 4 at level 1 (the successor ledger counts the parent climate treaty, UNFCCC, as its own case).
  • The successor ledger (wave 6) words its own ladder differently (its level 1 is 'rhetoric'); this page uses the map's ladder, and both place every case at the same level.
  • Percentages are institution-specific and cannot be consolidated. Exit, defunding, internal refit, parallel doctrine, shared machinery, resource migration, default use and residual-incumbent status are separate clocks.
  • The registry and the successor ledger hold no web links; their official sources are cited in the package's wave ledgers (for the Board of Peace, Resolution 2803).
