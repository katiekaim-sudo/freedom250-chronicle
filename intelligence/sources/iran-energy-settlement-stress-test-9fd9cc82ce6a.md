# Iran Energy and Settlement Stress Test

**Current through:** 2026-08-09 America/New_York  
**Method:** keep asset, service, export, revenue, payment, account-access, legal-discharge and enforcement clocks separate.

## 1. The stress-test result

Iran demonstrates that all of the following can be true at once:

- a turbine is operable;
- AI identifies a likely repair;
- a work order is valid;
- a spare physically exists;
- oil or electricity revenue has been earned;
- a payment instruction or on-chain transfer is confirmed;
- the beneficiary cannot access the funds;
- the part export or technical service remains prohibited; and
- debt discharge, title and enforcement remain unresolved.

That is why technical atomicity cannot be treated as commercial or legal finality.

### Controlling non-collapse rule

GL X addressed specified Iranian-origin oil commerce. It did not authorize export or reexport of U.S.-origin turbine parts, control systems, software or technical services. An OFAC payment authorization also does not independently satisfy the Export Administration Regulations.

The sharpest overlap is therefore:

> A revenue rail may be temporarily open while the asset-maintenance supply rail remains closed.

## 4. Parts, software and technical service remain a separate constitution

BIS's current Iran guidance says a license is required for most Commerce Control List items, license exceptions are unavailable, and non-humanitarian applications are generally subject to denial; OFAC authorization may also be required. DOJ and OFAC enforcement records show U.S.-origin gas-turbine replacement parts and other equipment routed through intermediaries for Iranian power-sector customers.

- https://www.bis.gov/licensing/country-guidance/iran-export-controls
- https://www.justice.gov/archives/opa/pr/export-company-executive-pleads-guilty-violating-us-sanctions-against-iran
- https://ofac.treasury.gov/media/13901/download?inline=

The operative states are separate:

```text
part needed
  != part identified
  != part available
  != purchase order valid
  != export authorized
  != shipment delivered
  != import lawful
  != part accepted
  != service permitted
  != payment legally discharged
```

Domestic turbine manufacture does not prove that every controller, sensor, alloy, coating, software update or network component is domestically substitutable.

## 5. Energy revenue, restricted funds and usable money

The State Department's 2023 description of the Iraq electricity-payment arrangement said funds were transferred to restricted accounts, could be used only for approved humanitarian or other non-sanctionable third-party transactions, were not transferred directly to Iran, and remained subject to U.S. case-by-case approval.

- https://2021-2025.state.gov/briefings/department-press-briefing-june-26-2023/
- https://ofac.treasury.gov/faqs/topic/1551

**Current-state boundary:** the 2023 statement establishes the mechanism historically. It is not proof that the same waiver and account instructions remain operative in August 2026. The latest written waiver or non-waiver and exact account instructions are a return gate.

Treasury's current actions describe separate mechanisms for oil-sale receipt, CNY settlement, exchange-house conversion, shell-company invoicing, offshore accounts and foreign-bank restrictions.

- https://home.treasury.gov/news/press-releases/sb0483
- https://home.treasury.gov/news/press-releases/sb0596

Therefore:

```text
commodity delivered
  != revenue recognized
  != foreign account credited
  != FX converted
  != funds repatriable
  != beneficiary usable
  != sanctions-compliant legal discharge
```

## 6. Digital assets do not remove the legal control plane

OFAC states that Iranian digital-asset exchanges are blocked Iranian financial institutions even if not separately listed; sanctions obligations apply to digital and fiat currency; a U.S. holder of blocked digital currency must deny access; and release requires OFAC authorization or cessation of the legal prohibition.

- https://ofac.treasury.gov/faqs/1250
- https://ofac.treasury.gov/faqs/1257
- https://ofac.treasury.gov/faqs/560
- https://ofac.treasury.gov/faqs/646
- https://home.treasury.gov/news/press-releases/sb0598

**Ruling:** blockchain confirmation can establish a protocol event. It does not establish that the property is unblocked, transferable, legally usable or immune from freeze, seizure or forfeiture.

A DOJ complaint concerning alleged Iranian-oil proceeds also supplies the necessary adjudication boundary: a civil forfeiture complaint is not a final forfeiture judgment, and allegations remain to be proved.

- https://www.justice.gov/opa/pr/united-states-files-civil-forfeiture-complaint-47-million-proceeds-sale-1-million-barrels

## 7. Current physical-logistics boundary

Current official maritime records separate three different conditions:

- elevated missile, UAV, USV and navigation-interference risk in the Persian Gulf, Strait of Hormuz and Gulf of Oman;
- restrictions affecting traffic entering or exiting Iranian ports; and
- transit to and from non-Iranian ports.

- https://www.maritime.dot.gov/msci/2026-004-persian-gulf-strait-hormuz-and-gulf-oman-iranian-attacks-commercial-vessels
- https://www.centcom.mil/MEDIA/PUBLIC-RELEASES/Article/4457255/us-to-blockade-ships-entering-or-exiting-iranian-ports/

Do not collapse “the Strait is open,” “Iranian ports are restricted,” and “commercial transit is safe.” A smart contract cannot substitute for navigational, military, insurer, port, carrier and cargo-control states.

## 8. The required Iran management graph

For any claimed energy-asset transaction, trace:

| State | Minimum proof object |
|---|---|
| physical condition | identified asset and signed sensor/inspection record |
| operational authority | owner/operator/OEM/reliability instruction and work entitlement |
| part/service identity | BOM, origin, ECCN, software and technical-service scope |
| counterparty | legal entity, beneficial ownership and designation screen |
| license | exact authorization, effective time, expiry and prohibited residuals |
| contract | purchase/service terms, delivery, acceptance, warranty and dispute rule |
| payment | payer, payee, currency/asset, account or address, instruction and confirmation |
| access and FX | account restriction, custody/key control, conversion and withdrawal state |
| legal discharge/title | governing law, setoff, blocked-property effect and adjudication |
| enforcement | freeze, seizure, complaint, forfeiture judgment and distribution as separate clocks |

## 9. Design lesson

Any event-driven energy settlement system exposed to Iran or another fast-moving sanctions regime needs:

- time-versioned sanctions, export-control and license rules;
- screening of counterparty, beneficial owner, asset, part, vessel, bank, address and jurisdiction;
- a reversible compliance hold before legally consequential release;
- independent physical-operation continuity when the commercial rail is paused;
- evidence preservation for correction, setoff, restitution and adjudication; and
- no assumption that a technically final transfer is legally discharging.

Iran does not disprove programmable settlement. It shows the constitution it must have.
