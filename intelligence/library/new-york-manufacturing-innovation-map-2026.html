Research summary

New York has a real distributed industrial operating system: an I-90/CSX production spine connected to Great Lakes process water, bounded Niagara and St. Lawrence hydropower programs, specialized Hudson and Great Lakes port cargo, and major semiconductor, energy, materials, biotech, defense and research nodes. Micron is the largest forward anchor but remains under construction rather than operating production.
