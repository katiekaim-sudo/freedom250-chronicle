Research summary

CFTC MPD Letter 26-25 generalizes the March Phantom position to qualifying passive software providers, expressly including non-crypto providers. A PSP may supply a standalone or embedded front end, market particular derivatives, solicit users to specific registrants and earn revenue-share or transaction fees without MPD recommending IB/AP registration enforcement solely for the covered activity. The pathway remains anchored to DCM/FCM/IB/DCO onboarding, custody, execution and clearing; excludes asset custody, express trade signals and routing/execution discretion; imposes disclosures, IB-like marketing controls, records, notices, CFTC consent and PSP-registrant joint liability; and is interim staff forbearance rather than Commission law, a spot regime, a DeFi safe harbor or operating evidence.
