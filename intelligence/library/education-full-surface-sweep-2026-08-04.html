The short version (draft, not yet reviewed by Katie)

Education's August letter to colleges asks; it does not require

A recheck of the Education Department's sites on Aug. 4, 2026 found three things: a letter asking colleges to publish commitments by year-end, a planned move of Perkins loan servicing to a new company, and a delay in enforcing a student-loan reporting field. None is yet a funding condition, a finished transfer or a waiver.

What we found

  • Secretary Linda McMahon signed a three-page letter on Aug. 3, 2026 asking every college to post statements on seven topics, including admissions merit, free speech, cost and foreign influence, before the end of 2026. It cites no legal duty, form, penalty or funding tie.
  • Federal Perkins Loan servicing is moving from ECSI to GCR Servicing. ECSI stops taking new submissions Oct. 1, and the transfer is expected to finish by Dec. 1. That date is a plan, not a done deal.
  • The new 2026–27 Direct Loan field (Enrollment Status Effective Date) is still required. Federal Student Aid pushed enforcement past Aug. 8 to 'later in fall 2026' without a date; if a school leaves it blank, loan-limit rules still apply.
  • GAO's July 16 review of the STATS and earnings-accountability final rule: most of it takes effect July 1, 2027, with two parts effective Aug. 31, 2026.
  • No Education Department entry appeared in the Aug. 2–4 Federal Register.

Why it matters

The letter is only a request now, but its seven topics overlap tools the government already controls, such as student-aid eligibility, civil-rights reviews, accreditation and grants. The study treats a later link to funding as something to watch for, not a fact.

What to watch

  • 2026-10-01 to 2026-12-01 · Whether the Perkins servicing move from ECSI to GCR finishes on time and GCR opens intake.
  • by 2026-12-31 · College responses to the letter, and any later rule, grant term or finding that ties those responses to funding or eligibility.
  • pending · The new enforcement date for the COD reporting field.
