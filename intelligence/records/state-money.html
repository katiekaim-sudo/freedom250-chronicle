The Governor and Executive Council hear the Business Finance Authority's plan: a $100 million municipal bond secured by $160 million in bitcoin, borrower CleanSpark the miner, BitGo holding the coins, Moody's rating it junk at Ba2. The Texas Stock Exchange begins trading — test stocks first, live trading July 10, thousands of symbols phasing in over the month. ETP listings in Q3, corporate listings in Q4, IPOs in 2027. The Digital Financial Assets Law becomes mandatory: license or leave, $100,000 a day for operating without one. California becomes the second BitLicense state at the exact moment Washington deregulates. 2026-07-01 · FL · Gold & silver as money

Gold Is Legal Tender in Florida

The gold-and-silver legal tender law takes effect — the largest state ever to do it. Gold above 99.5 percent and silver above 99.9 percent recognized as money, sales-tax exempt, with a custodian regime for electronic specie payments. Signed in May 2025, effective the week of the 250th anniversary: while Washington tokenizes the dollar from above, the big states are re-monetizing metal from below.

Act 841 creates a state registry of certified electronic gold-payment platforms in the Treasury. Stacked on last year's Act 304, Louisiana now has the most complete gold-money architecture outside Texas: legal tender, debit instruments, depository access, and now a certified payments network — assembled unanimously and in silence.

Two laws, thirteen months, near-unanimous votes: the fullest custodian-depository-electronic-transfer plumbing any state has built for metal money. Live July 1.

2025-05-06 · NH · State bitcoin reserves

Ayotte signs HB 302 — the first state law in the nation letting the treasurer put public money into bitcoin, up to five percent of funds, assets over $500 billion market cap only. The treasurer's answer the next day: no plans to buy. Fourteen months later she still hasn't. First to pass, last to act — the law was the point.
