Research summary

A no-composite, six-lane system for grading the legal and operating system behind each currency, with IMF state, exchange regime, market function and trade pressure kept separate. The July 31 Japan–Korea cases show national authorities transacting, dealers repricing, stablecoin exchanges supplying a second dollar-linked quote and the IMF classifying or researching rather than making either price.
