# Fed accounts and bank access: dated filings

Dated filings on who gets into the Federal Reserve's payment system: master accounts, account-access rules, national charters and the anti-money-laundering rules that come with them.

Home room: Banking & the Dollar › Banks, charters & watchdogs. 21 dated entries, 2025–2026. Dates and facts only.


## 2026

- **2026-07-19 — The Only Crypto Firm Through the Fed's Gate Still Cannot Use the Door** (Federal Reserve Bank of Kansas City · Report · Fed accounts & bank access). Kraken Financial CEO Brian Mathena told Wyoming's Select Committee on Blockchain, Financial Technology and Digital Innovation that the firm is 'playing a bit of catch up, trying to get the account operationalized.' Four and a half months after the Kansas City Fed handed Kraken the first limited-purpose account ever given a crypto-native firm, customer wires still route through Dart Bank as correspondent. Two facts fill in the record: the Board's master-account database lists Kraken's date of access effective as Jan 28 2026 — five weeks before the March announcement — and of 53 Tier 3 or unclassified applicants, exactly three have ever been approved (Kraken, Numisma, a Puerto Rico cooperative). Governor Bowman called Tier 3 access 'unobtainium' and the KC approval a pilot 'only accessible for this one institution.' The gate opened once, quietly, ahead of schedule, for one firm — and the firm still cannot walk through it. No primary Fed or Kraken document; trade reporting of state-legislature testimony is the only record. Source: https://fintechbusinessweekly.substack.com/p/kraken-financials-skinny-master-account

- **2026-04-10 — Prohibition on the Use of Reputation Risk by Regulators** (Treasury Department — Comptroller of the Currency · Rule · Fed accounts & bank access). The Office of the Comptroller of the Currency and the Federal Deposit Insurance Corporation are adopting a final rule to codify the elimination of reputation risk from their supervisory programs. Among other things, the rule prohibits the agencies from criticizing or taking adverse action against an institution on the basis of reputation risk. The rule also prohibits the agencies from requiring, instructing, or encouraging an institution to close an account, to refrain from providing an account, product, or service, or to modify or terminate any product or service on the basis of a person or entity's political, social, cultural, or religious views or beliefs, constitutionally protected speech, or solely on the basis of politically disfavored but lawful business activities perceived to present reputation risk. The rule further forbids the agencies from taking any supervisory action or other adverse action against an institution, a group of institutions, or the institution-affiliated parties of any institution that is designed to punish or discourage an individual or group from engaging in any lawful political, social, cultural, or religious activities, constitutionally protected speech, or, for political reasons, lawful business activities that the agencies or its personnel disagree with or disfavor. ## 2025

- **2025-12-23 — Request for Information and Comment on Reserve Bank Payment Account Prototype** (Federal Reserve System · Notice · Fed accounts & bank access). The Board of Governors of the Federal Reserve System (Board) seeks public input on a special purpose Reserve Bank account prototype (a Payment Account) tailored to the risks and needs of institutions focused on payments innovation. A Payment Account holder would be expected to use its account for the express purpose of clearing and settling the institution's payment activity. Payment Accounts would be designed to pose limited risk to the Federal Reserve Banks (Reserve Banks) and the overall payment system, and Reserve Banks would generally conduct a streamlined review of requests for these accounts. Any institution that is legally eligible for Federal Reserve accounts or services (accounts and services) under the Federal Reserve Act would be eligible to request a Payment Account from a Reserve Bank. The Payment Account protype does not seek to expand or otherwise change legal eligibility for access to accounts and services. Source: https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2025/12/23/2025-23712/request-for-information-and-comment-on-reserve-bank-payment-account-prototype
