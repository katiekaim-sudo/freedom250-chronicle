The Pentagon and the industrial base

How is the military rebuilding its command, supply lines, power and the industries behind them?

The braid so far

The military is rebuilding command, supply, power, records and industrial capacity through named offices, vendors, standards, studies, energy projects and acceptance gates. Announcement, award, delivery, acceptance and fielded operation remain separate.

In this reading, Mars–Pluto is the lead strand. Their applying opposition tightens into the Aries Full Moon: force meets concentrated power face to face. Constructively, authority, custody and industrial capacity become explicit enough to test. In shadow, command and private capacity fuse faster than public responsibility and remedy can follow.

Mars–Neptune is a companion strand. Its flowing trine can align force with mission, imagination and strategic narrative; it can also make spectacle, secrecy or idealized readiness easier to confuse with accepted capability. Jupiter–Saturn supplies the institution-building companion: scale must pass through standards, procurement, validation and acceptance. Uranus–Pluto backgrounds the networked command question without owning it.

The evidence test is exact: signed instrument, funded carrier, delivered object, accepted test, operating unit, stress result, loss owner and remedy. The Joint Warfighter Evaluation announcement is a governed movement receipt, not proof of fielded capability.
