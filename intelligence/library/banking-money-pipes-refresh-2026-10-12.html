Official study title: What changed in banking and the money pipes after the Site build

What materially changed in banks, payment rails, clearing and tokenized settlement, and how do ACH, Securitize Stocks/USDC and DTC tokenized entitlements differ across capture, clearing, settlement, finality, availability, redemption, correction and loss?

The short version (draft, not yet reviewed by Katie)

A bank payment and a token trade do not settle in the same place

ACH settles banks at the Fed; Securitize trades a broker entitlement for USDC; DTC moves an incumbent entitlement without cash. The same word does not mean the same record, finality, correction or loss rule.

What we found

  • In an ACH credit, the Reserve Banks debit the sending bank's settlement account and credit the receiving bank's account; the consumer still holds a deposit claim against the receiving bank.
  • Returns, reversals, Regulation E recredit and final fraud loss are separate events. Nacha's October 8 first-party-fraud paper asks for evidence and proposes a clarification, but changes no rule yet.
  • Securitize Stocks launched through Securitize Markets on Solana with USDC and Jump Trading. The holder owns an Article 8 entitlement through the broker, not a direct issuer-registered share unless a supported conversion occurs.
  • USDC is Circle's conditional redemption promise, not an insured deposit. Its onchain transfer is irreversible even though Circle can block or freeze addresses under its terms.
  • DTCC's public October notices did not supply the terms, participants, fees, wallets, assets and first transaction needed to prove the broader DTC tokenization service opened.
  • Securitize has the stronger public launch claim; DTC has the stronger public record, reconciliation and correction constitution. DTC's token movement is free of value and initially has zero DTC collateral, Net Debit Cap, end-of-day settlement or default-management value.
  • The governed Settlement Language Lab compares capture, clearing, settlement, finality, availability, redemption, correction and loss across all three routes without treating any one state change as the whole transaction.

Why it matters

The same word—settlement—can describe bank-reserve movement, a broker-book change, a token transfer or legal discharge. Naming the record owner shows which step is final, who can correct it and who bears a loss.

What to watch

  • 2026-10-31 · DTC's general tokenization opening receipt: rules, fees, participants, wallets, assets and first post-opening transaction.
  • 2026-12-11 · Nacha first-party-fraud RFI/RFC close and any later adopted recredit rule.
  • On first public operating receipt · Securitize first trade/volume, exact custody and customer terms, reconciliation, corporate action or failed-trade result.

Research summary

ACH settles bank obligations at the Fed; Securitize trades a broker entitlement against USDC; DTC moves an incumbent participant entitlement free of value. Securitize has the stronger public launch receipt, DTC the stronger public correction constitution, and neither token movement merges title, cash, finality, correction and loss into one event.
