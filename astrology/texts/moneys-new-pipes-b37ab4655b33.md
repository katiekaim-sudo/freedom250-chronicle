# Money's New Pipes

## Why this room matters

This is the clearest place to watch a new operating order being assembled inside
old institutions. Tokenization changes representation and records. Regulators
decide who may enter and under what conditions. Stablecoin law defines which
private dollar promises can circulate and what public perimeter remains below
them. The three stories touch constantly but do different jobs.

## Tour stops

1. **The object:** What is represented on the ledger, and which old legal or
   accounting object still controls?
2. **The gate:** Which statute, rule, exemption, registration or no-action
   position admits it?
3. **The operator:** Who issues, holds, transfers, clears and reconciles it?
4. **The money leg:** What actually settles the obligation?
5. **The failure constitution:** Who corrects error, absorbs loss and supplies
   remedy when the elegant diagram breaks?

## Strongest ordinary rival

These may be separate modernization programs responding to the same technology
and political climate, not parts of one coordinated re-founding sequence.
Shared institutions and vocabulary do not prove shared command.

## What would materially change the story

A named operating route that joins permission, authoritative record, cash,
finality, reconciliation, failure and remedy at repeated scale would move this
story beyond promising fragments. A stress event with public recovery evidence
would be stronger still.
