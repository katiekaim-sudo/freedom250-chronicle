Tracing Iraqi oil: what is proven and what is only alleged

Iraqi oil moves through five separate records (physical, title, identity, permission and payment). Public records prove the lawful routes and the spot where they meet the alleged Iran-blending network, but not the cargo-by-cargo chain Treasury describes.

What we found

  • SOMO, Iraq's sole authorized oil marketer, reports Qayarah crude loaded from floating storage (VS87, ST1) near Khor al-Zubayr, for example 495,645 barrels on a July 29, 2025 bill of lading and 971,130 barrels on Feb 4, 2026.
  • OFAC lists sanctioned VS Oil Terminal FZE at Khor al-Zubayr, Jetty 41, and Treasury alleges its tanks and nearby ship-to-ship transfers mixed Iranian and Iraqi oil. In May 2026 Treasury alleged Ali Maarij authorized trucking Qayarah oil to VS Oil. These are official allegations; the manifests, tests and bank records have not been published.
  • At the same Berth 41, SOMO tendered Iraqi-origin fuel oil (posted June 14, 2026) and announced on June 18 a transaction-specific OFAC license to sell to non-sanctioned buyers. The same site carries both lawful and allegedly illicit traffic.
  • Lawful oil payments go to the Iraq 2 account at the New York Fed, managed by Iraq's central bank for the Finance Ministry, but no public record ties any single cargo to it. The 2021 $9.59 billion gap between SOMO and central-bank figures is an unreconciled accounting gap, not proof of theft.
  • The sanctioned tanker DIJILAH still has a current Gard P&I insurance entry (Feb 2026 to Feb 2027), and Treasury does not name which vessel met CASINOVA four times in April 2024.

Why it matters

It shows U.S. control over oil money working through existing tools (OFAC designations and licenses and the New York Fed account) rather than new institutions. It also shows how far sanctions claims can run ahead of the documents.

What to watch

  • pending · The full terms of OFAC license IRAN-EO13902-2026-1448903-1, plus the winning bidder and actual loadings for the June 2026 Berth 41 sale.
  • pending · Qayarah road-tanker awards and truck manifests that could confirm or rule out the alleged diversion.
  • pending · Any cargo-level link from SOMO invoice to buyer payment to an Iraq 2 credit.
