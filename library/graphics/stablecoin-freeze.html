Freedom 250 · Digital money · Stablecoins

Who can freeze, burn or block a stablecoin?

For 18 of the 36 crypto dollars here, the issuer can freeze or block coins, usually for suspected crime or on a court or government order. The GENIUS Act requires payment-stablecoin issuers to be able to do this.

What makes a legal stablecoin trustworthy
Under the GENIUS Act there is no single stamp that makes a stablecoin "trusted": the issuer's approval, the coin's code and keys, its reserves, the platforms selling it and the fixes for mistakes each have to be proven separately. Evidence through August 17, 2026

OCC final stablecoin rules: issuer duties, permissions and operating evidence
The OCC's final GENIUS rule takes effect January 18, 2027. Its requirements attach to specific issuers, claims and custody relationships; approval, backing, capital, reporting and completed redemption require separate evidence. Evidence through October 21, 2026

What's still uncertain

  • 'Not stated' means the terms we read did not say; it does not mean the issuer cannot.
  • DeFi protocol dollars may have governance or contract controls that are not one issuer's freeze button.
