# Nuclear and energy filings

Dated federal filings on nuclear power and energy: reactor licensing reform, NRC rules and fees, DOE reactor testing.

Home room: Energy, Data Centers & Big Projects › Power & energy. 13 dated entries, 2025–2026. Dates and facts only.


## 2026

- **2026-06-16 — Fee Schedules; Fee Recovery for Fiscal Year 2026** (U.S. Nuclear Regulatory Commission · Rule · Nuclear & energy). The NRC finalized fixed caps on service fees to implement Section 5(a) of EO 14300, directly attacking the hourly-billing model the order condemned for throttling reactor development. The caps are framed as delivering cost predictability and accountability to license applicants. Source: https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2026/06/16/2026-12067/fee-schedules-fee-recovery-for-fiscal-year-2026 · Study: library/federal-science-operating-system.html
- **2026-05-01 — Licensing Requirements for Microreactors and Other Reactors With Comparable Risk Profiles** (U.S. Nuclear Regulatory Commission · Proposed Rule · Nuclear & energy). The NRC proposed a new 10 CFR Part 57 framework for rapid, high-volume licensing of microreactors, explicitly responding to EO 14300's direction to consider regulating microreactors through general licenses. The rule would create flexible licensing pathways and reduce regulatory burden for mass-deployed small reactors. Source: https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2026/05/01/2026-08550/licensing-requirements-for-microreactors-and-other-reactors-with-comparable-risk-profiles · Study: library/federal-science-operating-system.html
- **2026-04-23 — Exceptions From Foreign Ownership, Control or Domination** (U.S. Nuclear Regulatory Commission · Proposed Rule · Nuclear & energy). The NRC proposed carving exceptions to the Atomic Energy Act's foreign-ownership bar for reactor licensees, implementing Section 301 of the ADVANCE Act of 2024. The change opens U.S. reactor projects to allied foreign capital and ownership the AEA had previously blocked. Source: https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2026/04/23/2026-07918/exceptions-from-foreign-ownership-control-or-domination · Study: library/federal-science-operating-system.html
- **2026-04-02 — NRC Reviews of Reactor Designs Previously Authorized by DOE or Department of War** (U.S. Nuclear Regulatory Commission · Proposed Rule · Nuclear & energy). The NRC proposed letting commercial reactor applicants directly leverage prior DOE or Department of War authorizations of demonstration reactors into NRC licensing reviews, building a bridge from the DOE pilot/test-reactor track to commercial licensing. A concrete mechanism for converting fast-tracked test reactors into licensed commercial plants. Source: https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2026/04/02/2026-06414/nrc-reviews-of-reactor-designs-previously-authorized-by-us-department-of-energy-or-department-of-war · Study: library/federal-science-operating-system.html
- **2026-03-30 — Risk-Informed, Technology-Inclusive Regulatory Framework for Advanced Reactors (10 CFR Part 53)** (U.S. Nuclear Regulatory Commission · Rule · Nuclear & energy). The NRC finalized 10 CFR Part 53, a new optional, technology-inclusive licensing pathway built for non-light-water advanced reactor designs, fulfilling a mandate of the Nuclear Energy Innovation and Modernization Act. It gives developers a third licensing route alongside the legacy Part 50 and Part 52 frameworks. Source: https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2026/03/30/2026-06048/risk-informed-technology-inclusive-regulatory-framework-for-advanced-reactors · Study: library/federal-science-operating-system.html
- **2026-03-03 — Streamlining Contested Adjudications in Licensing Proceedings** (U.S. Nuclear Regulatory Commission · Proposed Rule · Nuclear & energy). The NRC proposed revising its rules of practice to compress contested hearings in licensing proceedings, responding to both the ADVANCE Act of 2024 and EO 14300. The change tightens intervention and filing windows to keep reactor licensing on the EO-mandated accelerated timeline. Source: https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2026/03/03/2026-04187/streamlining-contested-adjudications-in-licensing-proceedings · Study: library/federal-science-operating-system.html
- **2026-02-26 — Worker Safety and Health Requirements To Support Reform of Nuclear Reactor Testing** (U.S. Department of Energy · Proposed Rule · Nuclear & energy). DOE proposed amending its 10 CFR Part 851 worker safety regulations to expedite review, approval, and deployment of advanced reactors — including the qualified test reactors in the Reactor Pilot Program — consistent with EO 14301. The comment period was reopened after an AFL-CIO extension request, signaling labor friction over the deregulatory pace. Source: https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2026/02/26/2026-03866/worker-safety-and-health-requirements-to-support-reform-of-nuclear-reactor-testing-reopening-of · Study: library/federal-science-operating-system.html
- **2026-02-26 — Regulatory Framework for Fusion Machines** (U.S. Nuclear Regulatory Commission · Proposed Rule · Nuclear & energy). The NRC proposed regulating commercial fusion machines under its existing byproduct-material framework — a lighter touch than fission licensing — with technology-inclusive requirements spanning the variety of anticipated fusion designs. The ADVANCE Act's relabeling of 'fusion reactor' to 'fusion machine' anchors the lighter posture meant to clear a path for commercial fusion. Source: https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2026/02/26/2026-03865/regulatory-framework-for-fusion-machines · Study: library/federal-science-operating-system.html

## 2025

- **2025-08-12 — DOE Announces Initial Selections for New Reactor Pilot Program** (U.S. Department of Energy · Press Release · Nuclear & energy). DOE selected 11 advanced-reactor projects from companies including Oklo, Radiant, Terrestrial Energy, Aalo Atomics, Last Energy, and Valar Atomics to build privately financed test reactors under the EO 14301 authorization process. The stated goal is to have at least three reach criticality by July 4, 2026, with companies bearing all costs and DOE providing a fast-track 'concierge' pathway. Source: https://www.energy.gov/articles/department-energy-announces-initial-selections-new-reactor-pilot-program · Study: library/federal-science-operating-system.html
- **2025-05-29 — Executive Order 14302 — Reinvigorating the Nuclear Industrial Base** (Executive Office of the President · Presidential Document · Nuclear & energy). Signed May 23, 2025, targets the full nuclear fuel supply chain — uranium mining, milling, conversion, enrichment, and spent-fuel recycling — and authorizes Defense Production Act voluntary agreements among domestic nuclear companies. It aims to quadruple U.S. nuclear capacity by 2050 and rebuild fuel-cycle independence. Source: https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2025/05/29/2025-09801/reinvigorating-the-nuclear-industrial-base · Study: library/federal-government-movement-atlas.html · Study: library/federal-science-operating-system.html
- **2025-05-29 — Executive Order 14301 — Reforming Nuclear Reactor Testing at the Department of Energy** (Executive Office of the President · Presidential Document · Nuclear & energy). Signed May 23, 2025, directs DOE to use its own Atomic Energy Act authorization process to fast-track construction and testing of advanced reactors — microreactors, SMRs, Gen III+/IV designs — at DOE sites outside the national labs. The legal foundation for the DOE Reactor Pilot Program, bypassing the slower NRC commercial-licensing path for test reactors. Source: https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2025/05/29/2025-09799/reforming-nuclear-reactor-testing-at-the-department-of-energy · Study: library/federal-government-movement-atlas.html · Study: library/federal-science-operating-system.html
- **2025-05-29 — Executive Order 14300 — Ordering the Reform of the Nuclear Regulatory Commission** (Executive Office of the President · Presidential Document · Nuclear & energy). Signed May 23, 2025, this order directs a wholesale overhaul of the NRC, faulting it for licensing only a fraction of reactors since 1978 and for fee structures that throttle development. It mandates fixed fee caps, an 18-month deadline for new reactor license decisions, and a culture shift away from radiation-risk conservatism toward enabling deployment. Source: https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2025/05/29/2025-09798/ordering-the-reform-of-the-nuclear-regulatory-commission · Study: library/federal-government-movement-atlas.html · Study: library/federal-science-operating-system.html
- **2025-05-29 — Executive Order 14299 — Deploying Advanced Nuclear Reactor Technologies for National Security** (Executive Office of the President · Presidential Document · Nuclear & energy). Signed May 23, 2025, orders deployment of advanced reactors — Gen III+, SMRs, microreactors, and mobile reactors — to power critical defense installations and AI data centers, framing energy disruption as a strategic risk. It tasks the Departments of Defense and Energy with siting reactors on federal and military land. Source: https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2025/05/29/2025-09796/deploying-advanced-nuclear-reactor-technologies-for-national-security · Study: library/federal-government-movement-atlas.html · Study: library/federal-science-operating-system.html
