When must U.S. Treasury trades be centrally cleared, and which clearinghouses can do it?

Under the SEC rule adopted December 13, 2023, eligible secondary-market Treasury cash trades must be centrally cleared by December 31, 2026, and repo by June 30, 2027; the SEC said in February 2025 it does not intend further extensions. Three clearinghouses are registered to do it: FICC (DTCC's subsidiary, the incumbent), CME Securities Clearing (registered December 1, 2025; its launch moved from Q2 to 'later in 2026') and ICE Clear Credit (its Treasury business approved January 30, 2026). The mandate does not apply to the Fed's own operations; clearing those is still only a proposal.
