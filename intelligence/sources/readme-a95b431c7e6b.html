Rand Paul COVID Origins Reading Room — Corpus and Wave Audit

This package preserves and audits the official Senate COVID Origins Reading Room published by Chairman Rand Paul. It treats the portal, its card summaries, the linked PDFs, the records inside those PDFs and any later committee interpretation as distinct evidence objects.

Short answer

The live portal currently resolves to fourteen distinct Senate-hosted PDFs:

  • 3,887 physical pages
  • 246,592,513 bytes
  • 14 SHA-256-identified source files
  • 14 page-addressable text extractions
  • 2 scan-heavy PDFs OCR'd locally
  • 0 empty text files
  • 0 extraction-error pages
  • 99 exact normalized-text duplicate groups
  • 109 excess repeated pages, including at least 96 inside Volume 1

This is too large and structurally irregular for a trustworthy one-pass summary. The audit therefore proceeds in bounded waves. No wave is called complete until its page-level findings are merged and independently checked against the prior ODNI, House, GAO and agency record.

Story-first route

Read the Reading Room story architecture for the overarching narrative being advanced, the evidence supporting each act, the missing causal bridges and the targeted research agenda.

The architecture now controls substantive next work. Completed page audits are evidence reservoirs, not a requirement to continue automatic page-by-page coverage.

Updated execution posture

Automatic diary progression is paused after the September 2020 audit. Remaining diary, Slack, prequel, intelligence and Volume 1 pages are reopened only when they can close a proof-chain link, test a private/public contradiction, authenticate a native object, resolve a disposition or correct a material official claim.

The active priority order is:

  1. proposed-versus-performed DEFUSE and related laboratory work;
  2. FBI/CBP Daszak stand-down reason and disposition;
  3. native recordkeeping, FOI and moderation evidence; and
  4. state-changing hearing, DOJ, policy-implementation or new scientific objects.

The completed Priority 1 test found a genuine late change in Proximal Origin framing and stronger public certainty, but no primary proof that Fauci or NIH dictated the conclusion or that Fauci's April 17 answer was knowingly false.

Evidence firewalls

  • Reading Room card copy is committee-authored framing.
  • “Released by Chairman Rand Paul” establishes the release custodian, not the original author, completeness or truth of every enclosed record.
  • A diary entry establishes what its author recorded, not independent truth of every event or scientific claim described.
  • Testimony establishes what a witness said under the stated conditions. It is not a laboratory notebook, funding record, finished intelligence judgment or adjudication.
  • Internal scientific uncertainty is evidence about deliberation. It is not by itself proof of engineering, release or pandemic causation.
  • A targeting or investigative record is not a charge, conviction, informant designation or obstruction finding.
  • A historical institutional relationship is not proof of a later covert agreement.
  • Publication date, underlying record date, hearing/interview date and legal disposition date remain separate clocks.
