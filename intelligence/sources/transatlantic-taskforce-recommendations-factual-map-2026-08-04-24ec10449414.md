# Transatlantic Taskforce Recommendations — Factual Map

Evidence cutoff: August 4, 2026 EDT.

## Controlling finding

The Taskforce produced a coordination constitution, not an operative common
market. Its verbs matter:

```text
establish / engage / seek / consider / support / explore / assess / reaffirm
  != enact / adopt / recognize / approve / authorize / launch / settle
```

The recommendations name real legal and operational seams. They generally do
not supply the instrument, accountable officer, budget, milestone, completion
test or public reporting date that would close those seams.

## Recommendation 2 — the load-bearing plumbing seam

The report puts four objects in one sentence that must remain separate:

```text
tokenized asset
  -> authoritative ownership / entitlement record
  -> legal settlement finality and correction rule
  -> collateral eligibility and valuation
  -> CCP margin account and default waterfall
  -> cash settlement asset and discharge
```

A token can be technically transferable while having no CCP collateral value.
A stablecoin or tokenized MMF can be eligible in a sandbox or private contract
without being generally eligible at a regulated CCP. A securities transfer can
be final on one ledger while the cash leg, legal discharge, books-and-records
control or insolvency treatment remains elsewhere.

The report supplies no:

- named CCP or clearing service;
- eligible stablecoin or tokenized fund;
- haircut, concentration limit or valuation source;
- custodian, control location or segregation rule;
- cash-asset, payment-rail or settlement-time definition;
- default, liquidation, correction or loss-allocation rule;
- transatlantic finality or governing-law opinion.

The UK Digital Securities Sandbox is a bounded experimental route. It is not a
general answer for U.S.-UK collateral or finality.

## Stablecoin statement — ten policy propositions

| Theme | Shared position | What the statement does not itself create |
|---|---|---|
| use | enable stablecoins in cross-border finance, payments and capital-market settlement | a permitted product, transaction or payment rail |
| money model | private stablecoins and tokenized deposits may coexist with public standard-setting | deposit status, central-bank access or deposit insurance |
| supervision | comparable outcomes for comparable risks; flexible tools | equivalent rules or mutual recognition |
| reserves | at least one-to-one backing with defined high-quality liquid assets | a common eligible-reserve list or valuation rule |
| localization | avoid inappropriately high local ring-fencing | permission to pool reserves across legal entities or insolvency estates |
| proportionality | avoid burdens that destroy viability or entry | exemption from prudential, AML, custody or consumer law |
| market access | fair, risk-based access to banking and financial markets | bank account, master account, CCP access or non-discretionary admission |
| custody/redemption | segregation, safeguarding, timely redemption and clear holder rights | an issued coin's contract or completed redemption |
| failure | protected reserve claim and priority ahead of other creditors, consistent with local law | a new statutory priority or cross-border resolution protocol |
| reciprocity | explore formal paths for each jurisdiction's coins to reach the other | a passport, comparability decision or registered foreign issuer |

Recommendation 3 expressly says the statement does not “supplant nor
presuppose” ongoing regulatory processes. Point 10 of the statement is subject
to each jurisdiction's laws, regulations and processes.

## Domestic stablecoin clocks

### United States

- GENIUS is enacted and supplies a federal statutory framework.
- Treasury, FinCEN, OFAC, FDIC and other regulators remain on separate proposed-
  and final-rule clocks.
- UK comparability, a foreign issuer's lawful U.S. access, institutional
  settlement use and CCP collateral treatment require their own operative
  objects.
- The bilateral statement supplies none of those objects.

### United Kingdom

- SI 2026/102 was made February 4, 2026.
- FCA final crypto/stablecoin rules were published June 30, but the main new
  regime does not take effect until October 25, 2027.
- The authorisation application window is September 30, 2026 through February
  28, 2027.
- The Bank of England's systemic-stablecoin Code remained draft at cutoff;
  consultation closes September 22, 2026 and finalization is intended by the
  end of 2026.
- HM Treasury recognition of a systemic stablecoin chain remains a separate
  act. No recognition or live systemic issuer was established by the report.
- Retail stablecoin payments remain entangled with future payments-services
  reform; the reviewed April amendment remained draft.

This is therefore an asymmetric transition: the U.S. statute exists but its
regulatory detail is still being filled in; the UK perimeter and FCA rules are
substantially made but their main operating date lies in 2027. Bilateral intent
does not synchronize those clocks.

## Capital-markets lane

Recommendations 6–10 are not crypto footnotes. They cover conventional market
access and information infrastructure:

- primary-market disclosure and cross-border offering friction;
- which non-U.S. issuers qualify for SEC foreign-private-issuer treatment;
- consolidated market-data tapes;
- swap-venue registration/substituted-compliance treatment;
- accounting and audit standard-setting.

At cutoff:

- the SEC FPI object remained a concept release, not a proposed rule;
- the UK bond tape was operating from June 22, 2026;
- the UK equity-tape framework had been published, but procurement and service
  operation remained future;
- no FCA-SEC tape cooperation agreement or technical object was located;
- CFTC staff relief remained temporary and nonbinding on the Commission;
- no new accounting or audit standard was identified.
