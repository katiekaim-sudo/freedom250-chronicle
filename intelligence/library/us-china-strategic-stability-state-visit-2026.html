Research summary

Trump and Xi's strategic-stability language remains a leader-level political framework rather than a standalone signed accord. The trade lane has now moved further: parallel White House/USTR and MOFCOM publications provide agreed Board of Trade procedures, named principals, a quarterly deputies' cadence, 30-for-30 terms of reference and two approved product lists valued at roughly $30 billion in each direction. The Board is a bilaterally evidenced mechanism and the product universe is settled, but the tariff changes remain pending domestic legal instruments, an effective date and customs instructions.
