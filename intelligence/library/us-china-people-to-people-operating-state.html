Us China People To People Operating State

Open, large and highly asymmetric, not one exchange system: 265,919 Chinese students in U.S. institutions (2024/25) vs 1,749 U.S. credit-bearing students in China (2023/24); Xi's youth invitation was 50,000 over five years, not 100,000, and China reports more than 50,000 visits by May 2026 without a participant-level ledger; air rights (50 weekly roundtrips per side) exceed operated service; U.S. nationals generally still need a Chinese visa; the 2024 STA protocol keeps basic-research cooperation alive through 2029; journalism is the most visibly constrained lane; subnational MOUs are real but nonbinding and unfunded. The relationship can become socially busy yet epistemically thinner.
