# Wave 3A — Production Acquires a Market

**Research window:** Exact 2025 Cancer ingress through the eve of exact 2025 Libra ingress.  
**Question:** Which spring production commands acquired a material, financial or project-selection body during the nested Cancer season?  
**Finding:** The first great conversion of the Cancer wave was not a new mine, power plant or fleet. It was a **state-manufactured market**. DOE published a grid methodology connecting reliability, data-center demand and dispatchable capacity. Defense and MP Materials executed a layered public-private package involving equity, a warrant, a price floor, financing and long-term outlet support. Apple added a private recycled-magnet demand agreement. Noveon began named commercial deliveries. DOE closed the first reactor-pilot application round and selected 11 projects. The old spring policy had therefore acquired measurable carriers—report, capital instrument, customer agreement, shipment and selection—without skipping qualification, construction, safety or sustained-output clocks.

The controlling ladder is:

`security classification → method/project route → capital + demand support → qualification → shipment/test → sustained output`

The Cancer wave advances the middle of that chain. It does not complete the end.

## The chain in one view

```text
Aries production doctrine
    ↓
Cancer names the body that must be protected or supplied
    ↓
grid method / public-private capital / customer agreement / project selection
    ↓
factory, fuel, site, safety analysis and qualification work
    ↓
```

The institutional change is deeper than “subsidy.” The state begins to join **ownership, price, demand, finance and technical qualification** around strategically chosen private carriers.

## 3A.1 — July 7: grid reliability becomes a common federal method

**Mover.** Department of Energy, reliability planners, NERC-derived regional records, generators, data-center developers and future §202(c) decision-makers.

DOE's [Resource Adequacy Report](https://www.energy.gov/sites/default/files/2025-07/DOE%20Final%20EO%20Report%20%28FINAL%20JULY%207%29.pdf) fulfilled a task from EO 14262. It supplied a uniform forward method for identifying at-risk regions and relating retirements, planned additions, firm capacity and new load. DOE's public framing estimated 100 GW of additional peak-hour supply need by 2030, half attributed to data centers, and stressed the mismatch between fast load construction and slower generation/interconnection.

**Maturity.** Published method and federal evidence record. This is more than an order, but less than a named generator-retention act.

**Counterevidence and limit.** A forecast is not an outage. An announced plant closure is not an accomplished retirement. Nameplate additions are not one-for-one firm replacement. The report does not itself establish the statutory emergency required for every later Federal Power Act §202(c) order, and it does not authorize a data center or compel a power transaction.

**Plotline.** The grid becomes the accounting surface on which the AI and reindustrialization story must prove it has a body. Compute ambition is now heard against reserve margin, generation type, interconnection and time.

## 3A.2 — July 10–11: MP Materials becomes a national-champion carrier

**Mover.** Department of Defense, MP Materials, future syndicated buyers, public shareholders and the domestic magnet supply chain.

On July 10, [MP Materials announced a public-private partnership with Defense](https://investors.mpmaterials.com/investor-news/news-details/2025/MP-Materials-Announces-Transformational-Public-Private-Partnership-with-the-Department-of-Defense-to-Accelerate-U-S--Rare-Earth-Magnet-Independence/default.aspx). The package is not accurately described as one grant. Its components have separate rights and clocks:

- preferred equity and a warrant positioned Defense to hold up to roughly 15 percent on an as-converted/as-exercised basis;
- the initial investment was scheduled to close July 11;
- a ten-year NdPr price floor supported a strategic input;
- a ten-year outlet arrangement was designed around future 10X magnet output;
- a $150 million loan supported heavy-rare-earth separation; and
- the 10X factory, production, buyer syndication and deliveries remained future milestones.

**Maturity.** Executed state–company capital and market-design architecture, subject to instrument conditions. This is the strongest Cancer-wave conversion from policy into an institutional body.

**Counterevidence and limit.** “Largest shareholder” is an instrument-dependent economic position, not proof Defense immediately exercised every share or controlled ordinary corporate governance. A price floor is not a support payment until its contractual conditions are met. Guaranteed future outlet is not delivered product. A planned 10X plant is not commissioned capacity.

**Plotline.** The state stops behaving only as regulator or grantor. It becomes capital participant, price stabilizer, lender and demand organizer around a private operating company.

## 3A.3 — July 15: private demand and recycling join the public structure

Apple and MP [announced a $500 million long-term agreement](https://investors.mpmaterials.com/investor-news/news-details/2025/MP-Materials-and-Apple-Announce-500-Million-Partnership-to-Produce-Recycled-Rare-Earth-Magnets-in-the-United-States/default.aspx) for magnets made in the United States from recycled rare-earth materials. The structure included a $200 million prepayment, expansion of the Fort Worth facility, a commercial-scale recycling line at Mountain Pass and a 2027 shipment target.

**Maturity.** Definitive customer agreement, prepayment and future capacity/delivery path.

**Counterevidence and limit.** The agreement did not prove Apple received magnets in 2025. Recycled feedstock does not remove every supply constraint, and end-of-life recovery differs from factory-scrap recycling. Customer qualification, yield, coating, geometry and sustained delivery remain separate.

**Institutional change.** The public national-champion architecture gains a private demand pole. Defense supports price, capital and strategic outlet; Apple supports recycled-product demand and a customer qualification target. Public and private terms converge without becoming the same account.

## 3A.4 — July–August: a policy lane reaches named commercial shipments

Noveon Magnetics had operated before the 2025 orders and had signed a five-year Nidec agreement on February 3. It therefore cannot be treated as a creature of the March mineral order. Its later receipts still matter:

- it began named GM magnet deliveries in July 2025; and
- it began monthly ABB deliveries in August.

**Maturity.** Commercial shipment to named customers.

**Counterevidence and limit.** Public sources establish deliveries, not a continuously audited 2,000-tonne annual run rate. A binding offtake ceiling is not volume already delivered. Noveon's prior operation is strong counterevidence to a simple policy-origin claim.

**Plotline.** The best evidence of reindustrialization is not the factory announcement. It is a qualified part entering a customer's actual supply chain.

## 3A.5 — July 21 to August 12: the nuclear pilot crosses from intake to named projects

DOE's Reactor Pilot Program request for applications opened June 18 under the Aries wave. Initial applications closed July 21. On August 12, DOE [selected 11 projects](https://www.energy.gov/articles/department-energy-announces-initial-selections-new-reactor-pilot-program) associated with ten companies for the new Department authorization pathway outside national laboratories.

The selection set included Aalo Atomics, Antares Nuclear, Atomic Alchemy, Deep Fission, Last Energy, Oklo (two projects), Natura Resources, Radiant Industries, Terrestrial Energy and Valar Atomics.

**Maturity.** Named project selection and DOE authorization pathway. The companies remained responsible for design, manufacturing, construction, operation and decommissioning costs.

**Counterevidence and limit.** Selection is not a DOE authorization, completed safety analysis, constructed reactor or criticality. DOE research/test authority is not NRC commercial licensure. The later 2026 Antares, Valar and Aalo criticality milestones must retain their own dates and do not turn the August selections into 2025 operation.

**Plotline.** The state creates a faster technical hearing without removing the technical proof. The object is no longer “advanced nuclear” in general; it is 11 named projects with owners, sites, safety documents and future authorization gates.

## What changed during the Cancer period

- Grid reliability acquired a common federal forecasting and intervention record.
- Permanent magnets acquired a layered state–company market structure rather than a one-off subsidy.
- Defense became a potential capital holder as well as buyer and policy sponsor.
- A private customer agreement joined the public magnet structure.
- Named domestic magnet shipments provided operating evidence independent of policy rhetoric.
- The Reactor Pilot Program moved from open intake to 11 named project selections.

## What did not change merely because these objects existed

- A grid report did not prove a statutory emergency or compel a power plant to run.
- Equity, warrant and price-floor instruments did not prove full conversion, exercise or support payment.
- A loan commitment did not prove disbursement.
- A customer agreement did not prove 2025 delivery.
- Shipment did not prove nameplate or sustained annual output.
- Reactor selection did not prove safety authorization, construction, criticality or grid power.
- Later successful milestones do not move backward into this wave.

## Institutional theorem

The Cancer wave reveals how the routing state manufactures a market around a strategic physical object.

It does not need to nationalize the company. It can instead distribute the missing market functions:

1. government defines the security object and future demand;
2. a public participant supplies patient capital, price support or a loan;
3. a private customer supplies qualification and outlet demand;
4. a company owns the operating and construction burden;
5. a regulator or technical agency preserves the safety/authorization gate; and
6. the public record can later distinguish instrument, shipment and output.

This is conformation, not simple replacement. Private legal persons remain private, but their economic environment is increasingly constituted by public terms.

## Both poles

### Constructive pole — public terms make an essential market investable

- price volatility and long development clocks no longer strand strategic capacity;
- public capital can bridge a missing midstream or factory;
- private customers impose real qualification discipline;
- project selection makes technical owners and gates visible;
- domestic shipments can be measured against promises; and
- grid planning forces compute and industrial ambition to face physical capacity.

### Shadow pole — the national champion becomes a protected private constitution

- one company can accumulate public capital, price support and guaranteed demand;
- market discipline can be socialized while upside remains concentrated;
- public mission can obscure dilution, voting, conversion and support-payment conditions;
- politically selected projects can outrun technical readiness;
- forecast emergency can become a standing rationale for intervention; and
- announcements can still substitute for qualified tonnes, criticality or megawatt-hours.

The evidence supports a state-manufactured strategic market. It does not yet establish a self-sufficient magnet, nuclear or grid system.
