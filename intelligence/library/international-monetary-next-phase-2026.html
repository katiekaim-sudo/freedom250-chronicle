Research summary

Four parts have to connect, and none is proven end to end: who legally holds deposits, export proceeds and token reserves and what they may become (captured liquidity); how a public budget becomes a restricted programmable payment; which rule wins in redemption, insolvency, outage or sanctions (failure and finality); and which insurer or guarantee makes the trade financeable (risk transfer). Indonesia supplies detailed placement and credit permissions, Europe an observed digital public-paper mobilization, Japan a bank-asset strategy; none establishes an automatic digital-money-to-government-debt rule.
