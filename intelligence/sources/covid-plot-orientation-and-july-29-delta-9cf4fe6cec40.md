inside an already-running COVID accountability and revisionism sequence. It

amplification and a precise event clock; it does not close the COVID-origin

## 4. What COVID.gov is

`https://covid.gov/` currently returns an HTTP 301 redirect to:

`https://www.whitehouse.gov/lab-leak-true-origins-of-covid-19/`

The White House page, *Lab Leak: The True Origins of Covid-19*, advances five
lab-origin arguments and sections concerning EcoHealth Alliance, gain-of-
function research, public-health guidance and alleged censorship. The page
identifies the House Select Subcommittee majority's 2024 after-action report
as its principal source and hosts a copy of that report. Its footer says the
page content is sourced from the House Oversight Committee website.

The White House had already used the same `COVID.gov` destination in an April
18, 2025 post, “DEFINITELY DON'T VISIT COVID.GOV 🤭.” The July 29, 2026 post
is therefore a replay/reactivation of an existing page and domain route—not
the page's first publication.

The current page is also not merely a frozen April 2025 object. It now:

- asserts that DOJ opened an investigation into EcoHealth's pandemic-era
  activities, without linking the operative DOJ source; and
- embeds or links a later HSGAC “lab-leak cover-up” video.

Those are material post-launch extensions. A complete archived HTML diff was
not completed, so the exact addition clocks and any smaller copy or image
changes remain unresolved.

### Institutional counter-record

The House majority's report and the Democratic minority's official final
report are separate source objects. The majority concluded that a
lab-related incident was the most likely origin and made findings about Fauci,
EcoHealth, research oversight and pandemic policy. The minority said the
origin remained unknown, described natural spillover and a laboratory accident
as plausible and disputed the claim that Fauci orchestrated the *Proximal
Origin* paper. The minority also documented its own concerns about EcoHealth
and research oversight.

Correct Freedom 250 treatment:

- preserve both reports;
- attribute each finding to its issuing committee faction;
- test underlying exhibits and independent authorities claim by claim; and
- leave the origin chain open unless a later primary object resolves a
  specific step.
