Freedom 250 · Digital money · Networks and protocols · Who controls what

What does each blockchain network, DeFi protocol and yield product control, where can it stop a payment, and how does it make money?

Very different amounts. On Ethereum, validators can censor or leave out transactions; on Stellar and Hedera each token's issuer sets its own freeze controls. Aave, Sky and Curve can change settings, caps or fees by governance vote, but Liquity's core design has no way to pause, upgrade or blacklist. Ethena and Ondo control who may mint and redeem; Morpho's governance cannot pause existing markets, while its vault curators choose where lenders' money goes. They earn from network fees, borrowing interest and protocol fees, and Ethena from derivatives funding payments.

What's still uncertain

  • The stablecoin competition is not one coin against another. Companies are racing to control the steps around the coin: legal liability to holders, reserves, ledgers, keys, risk, conversion to regular money and reaching customers.
  • Several statuses are the companies' own figures or plans ('figures are self-reported', 'some only announced'); each card says which.
  • The cards date from July 28, 2026; newer charter and product steps for some of the same firms are on the charters, issuers and freeze pages.
