How payments settle

In most of what the library holds, the cash still settles in money that already existed. Swift's ledger coordinates commitments among 17 listed banks, 8 of which have a completed pairing located; final settlement happens afterwards through existing payment systems. Of the 12 market systems, 1 settles in central-bank money by the master's own description (Sterling Fnality Payment System), 4 move banks' own tokenized deposits, and for 7 the master does not say. Who finances the interval before settlement: banks validated funding and managed the interval before external settlement. Customer availability can precede bank cash receipt.
