Research summary

Treasury's August 28 Operation Economic Outcast receipt partly resolves the forecast: FinCEN proposed, but did not yet impose, Section 311 Special Measure Five against Banque Misr UAE, while OFAC separately designated Reza Jalali Taeedi and Kameng Maritime Limited. The proposal would prohibit covered U.S. institutions from opening or maintaining correspondent accounts for or on behalf of the named UAE bank if finalized. It does not establish an operative ban, closure of a named account, failed payment, universal SWIFT or dollar exclusion, domestic Iranian banking shutdown, asset seizure, or any Federal Reserve or IMF change.
