# Office of Religious Affairs — Source Watchboard

**Status:** `current`
**Research ID:** `office-of-religious-affairs`
**Cutoff:** 2026-09-30, 10:00 p.m. EDT

## Watch purpose

Follow ORA inch by inch without turning a speech, appointment, budget request, charter, reported need or program proposal into a completed operating change. The [event ledger](../sources/office-of-religious-affairs-event-ledger-c38bdafa1ccc.html) owns state changes; this watchboard owns source routes and return triggers.

## Source owners and the clocks they can move

| Source owner | Watch for | State the source can move | It cannot prove by itself |
|---|---|---|---|
| Secretary / Department releases and memoranda | Director appointment, delegations, transfers, policy issuances and Secretary dispositions | appointment, authority, policy and disposition | implementation or result without a downstream receipt |
| Director of Administration and Management | ORA chartering directive, organization chart, billet allocation and establishment record | charter, organizational placement and initial staffing design | filled billets, permanent funding or service delivery |
| CAPE and Comptroller/CFO | baseline budget, FY2027 plan, reprogramming, budget justification and FYDP treatment | planning, request, transfer and execution clocks | congressional appropriation unless the legal funding source is shown |
| Military-department secretaries and chiefs of chaplains | 90-day requirements reports, service implementation guidance and facility/personnel requests | service need, submission and service policy | ORA acceptance, funding or Department-wide rule |
| Armed Forces Chaplains Board | conforming charter/issuance, meeting or recommendation records and Executive Director custody | board governance, advice and recommendation | Secretary acceptance or component implementation |
| ORA | policy, research and analytics programs, external engagements, grant/contract or program criteria, public reports | office action, program design and disclosed execution | neutral or effective operation without results evidence |
| Congress / appropriations committees | authorization, appropriation, report language, hearings and oversight requests | statutory permission, funding and oversight | executive implementation or outcome |
| GAO, Inspector General and service inspectors | audit, evaluation, program integrity, equal-treatment and resource findings | verified implementation or control finding | constitutional judgment unless a court decides it |
| Federal courts and administrative complaint systems | standing, merits, remedy, settlement or adjudicated discrimination/coercion records | legal challenge and adjudicated result | system-wide practice beyond the record |

## Exact priority watches

### 1. Director and transferred power

Promote only a named appointment or an exact delegation, transfer, organizational or funding instrument. A biography, speech appearance or unofficial title is discovery evidence only.

### 2. Sixty-day budget plan

Return at the calculated November 29, 2026 gate for the CAPE/Comptroller/ORA initial baseline budget and FY2027 funding plan. Keep submission, Secretary approval, budget request, congressional appropriation, reprogramming and execution separate.

### 3. Ninety-day charter, billets and requirements

Return at the calculated December 29, 2026 gate for:

- the DA&M organizational and chartering record;
- the initial SES, civilian and military billet allocation;
- permanent manning treatment in budget and FYDP requests; and
- each military department's comprehensive requirements report.

### 4. Policy and assessment authority

Watch any ORA policy issued on the Secretary's behalf and any research, technology or analytical tool used to assess spiritual fitness, readiness or resilience. Record the definition, instrument, population, data collected, scoring or inference rule, access controls, personnel consequences, validation, appeal/correction path and treatment of people of any faith or no faith.

### 5. Faith-based family and contracted programs

For homeschooling, chapel youth, suicide-reduction/resilience initiatives and contracted faith-based services, require the legal funding source, eligibility rule, provider-selection method, denomination/no-faith neutrality, safeguarding and privacy controls, contract or grant, delivery receipt and outcome measure.

### 6. Religious-liberty implementation

Keep the memorandum's non-compulsion/free-exercise clause and the release's non-favoritism promise distinct from actual command practice. Promote complaints, inspections, adjudications, audits and court records only with their scope and procedural state intact.

## False positives

Do not promote:

- the establishment memorandum as proof that a Director was appointed;
- a 60- or 90-day assignment as proof that the required object was submitted;
- a requirements report as an approved or funded program;
- a budget request as an appropriation or expenditure;
- a charter as filled staffing or operating capacity;
- an ORA engagement as a policy or contract;
- a Christian rhetorical statement as proof of formal denominational preference;
- a formal neutrality statement as proof of neutral implementation; or
- the absence of a public document as proof that no internal action occurred.
