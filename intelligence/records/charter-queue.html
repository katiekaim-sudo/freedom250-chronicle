2026-07-11 · OCC · Research · Fed account requests

The Ripple master-account claim is a conflation

The OCC's conditional-approval letter says the *proposed* Ripple National Trust Bank will apply for **Federal Reserve Bank stock** — a membership step. That is not a master-account request, and no Ripple or Standard Custody request was found in the Fed's public database. Membership, stock, a reserve account and a confirmed master account are four different things, and the retail story treats them as one.
