Card networks add stablecoin products, but the card stays

What funds the payment, what the customer pays with, what banks settle in, and what the merchant receives

Most of the change adds stablecoin products around the card system: card funding, settlement between banks, payouts and wallets. The card number and the merchant's card-acceptance agreement usually stay the same. A stablecoin payment becomes a truly separate payment network only when approval, routing, final settlement and dispute rights move with it.

One checkout, several different sets of money rules

The same merchant checkout can carry an ordinary card funded by a stablecoin, a card payment that banks settle with each other in USDC, a merchant payout in stablecoin, or a direct wallet-to-wallet stablecoin transfer. These are different products, and money moves on a different timeline in each.
