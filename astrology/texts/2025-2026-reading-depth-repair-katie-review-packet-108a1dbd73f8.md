### Reader key for the review packet

- **Root** means the final-dispositor seat or circuit through which the chart's
  motivations must keep returning; it does not mean “the strongest planet.”
- **Monthly owner** means the New Moon whose chapter remains in force until the
  next New Moon. A Full Moon reveals that chapter and may also mature an older
  Moon family.
- **Relationship film** means the same planetary pair followed through exact
  passes, stations and changing aspect phases rather than treated as a static
  placement.

  rule. It does not mean the chart is meaningless or detached from its chapter.

  series, date/degree echo and seed-to-quarter-to-Full maturation.
- **Zero evidence credit** means the astrology was heard without using later
