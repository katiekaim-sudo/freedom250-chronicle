FORTRESS America and Military Installation Energy Independence

In plain English

Question: What did FORTRESS America change, and what authority, scope, financing, construction and operating-test receipts are required before major military installations actually possess independent power?

Answer: FORTRESS America makes installation power a central military program, but it does not yet say which sites count, what loads must run, how long they must run or who will pay and operate the systems.

Short answer

FORTRESS America is a real department-wide program and governance change. Its September 30 energy objective is independent power for every major U.S. military installation so operations can continue when the civilian grid fails. It does not establish that every base is covered, that full installation load must be carried, that installations will disconnect from the grid during normal operations, or that the portfolio is funded, built and tested.

The decisive unresolved issue is the implementation constitution: covered sites, critical-versus-full load, outage duration, fuel assumptions, financing vehicle, ownership/operator, cyber standard, test method and acceptance record.

This package follows the installation-energy pillar deeply and maps the other four FORTRESS pillars only far enough to preserve the program boundary. Contested Logistics remains the parent package for fuel, strategic reserves, industrial supply and movement. Military Modernization remains a related owner for force and program modernization.
