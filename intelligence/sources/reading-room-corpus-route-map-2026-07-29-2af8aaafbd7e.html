2. The portal is a release layer, not one record

The Reading Room card date is the public-release clock. The underlying objects have their own clocks:

  • the DOJ OIG confidential-human-source inquiry letter is dated Apr. 15, 2025 even though its Reading Room card is dated Jul. 21, 2026;
  • the Baric interview occurred Apr. 10, 2026 at 9:00 a.m. in SVC-209 and was published Jul. 23;
  • the Daszak CBP package centers on a planned Feb. 5, 2021 arrival examination and later 2026 disclosure;
  • the Fauci “Prequel” was attached to an email dated Jul. 11, 2015 and retrospectively records events beginning in Apr. 2001;
  • the Senate's publication date for a record does not create a new historical event on the record's original date.

The live portal does not provide a corpus-wide production index, Bates map, transmittal set, redaction log, completeness declaration, Volume 1 constituent index, changelog or supersession history. Future portal checks should compare the resolved URL, file bytes and SHA-256 rather than relying on titles or card dates.

5. Known derivative and duplicate relationships

  • The 15-page Slack “Key Messages” file is a committee-curated excerpt/claim layer over the 1,123-page export. Exact normalized-text matching places thirteen of its pages in the full export: key-message PDF pages 2–7 match full-export pages 35 and 69–73; key-message pages 9–15 match full-export pages 999–1,005. The unmatched pages are packaging/curation layers. It is not a second independent corpus.
  • The Jul. 25 social thread and screenshots are promotion carriers for the 1,141-page diary PDF, not independent diary evidence.
  • The Daszak/CBP file is separately linked through HSGAC and the Reading Room; host duplication does not create another record.
  • The Apr. 15, 2025 DOJ OIG letter's Jul. 21, 2026 card is republication, not a new referral.
  • The Fauci/IC and Baric/IC packages overlap subjects and records already touched by the June ODNI COVID release and must be hash/page-deduplicated before final synthesis.
  • Reading Room Volume 1 is a mixed container. It must be decomposed into constituent record families before its page count is used as an evidence count.

Exact-page duplicate scan

The generated normalized-text comparison found:

  • 99 duplicate groups;
  • 208 duplicate-page occurrences;
  • 109 excess repeated pages beyond the first occurrence; and
  • 480 pages below the 80-character comparison threshold, including blank or image-dominant pages that were excluded from duplicate matching.

Volume 1 contains 182 pages participating in an exact duplicate group and at least 96 exact excess pages across 86 within-file groups. Several pages occur four times. Its 496 physical pages therefore cannot be reported as 496 unique evidence objects. The scan detects exact normalized extracted text only; near-duplicates, repeated email chains with changed headers and image-only duplicates require later review.
