IBRD + IDA = “World Bank.” Add IFC, MIGA and ICSID = “World Bank Group.” Those labels do not create a sixth Bank Group legal person.

Power is more than a loan

The IMF surveils the monetary system, holds approval jurisdiction over specified exchange practices, sets access to pooled finance and administers SDR decisions. The Bank bodies can set financing terms, invest, insure risk and administer binding dispute awards. BIS banks for central banks and hosts committees whose standards can travel into national rules. Each route has its own limits and domestic implementation key.

Five distinct routes
Two separate Bank clocks: an approved loan or guarantee sets terms; effectiveness, disbursement and observed results come later. IMF approval, access and actual drawing are separate too.

The Fed is a seam, not the parent.

The Fed has a U.S. policy seat, Reserve Bank account duties, an SDR certificate transaction, and direct BIS membership. The IMF also studies Fed policy. Click each connection to see the legal route and the books.

No IMF command over the FOMC
Books matter: Treasury holds the U.S. SDR position through the ESF. A Reserve Bank can hold a Treasury-issued SDR certificate asset and owe a deposit to Treasury. An IMF or IBRD deposit at a Reserve Bank is the Bank’s liability to that organization. These are not the same asset or owner.

Within a charter

Boards can change priorities, guidance and individual financing choices if the Articles permit them.

Beyond a charter

Changing a founding purpose or power requires the institution’s own amendment path and member acceptance.

In the United States

Congress controls reserved subscriptions, capital and amendments; Treasury represents the U.S.; Fed monetary-policy decisions stay with the Fed.

The live test: find the Board decision or governing text, then the signed country or financing instrument, then the money or operating receipt. Treasury’s statement points to the test; it does not close it.
