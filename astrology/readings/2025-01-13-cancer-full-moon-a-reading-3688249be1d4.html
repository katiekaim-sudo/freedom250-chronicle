The Full Moon lands in the national body itself. The people's significator is at home, full and on the rising sign, but a fallen and retrograde Mars rides with it, while the executive watches from the far side of the people-versus-government axis.

Cancer rises again, and this time the Moon is in her own sign at 24°00′ Cancer in the 1st, opposite the Sun at 24°00′ Capricorn in the 7th. This is the most self-possessed the public mood can be: dignified, visible, and fully illuminated. The Cancer–Capricorn axis is the classic line between the governed and the governing, and between protection of home and the demands of rule. The Moon holds the body while the Sun stands across the room as the counterpart. Whatever has been felt privately now becomes a visible fact of the national body.

Mars is the complication. He sits retrograde in fall at 27°05′ Cancer, in the same 1st house, applying to the Moon by 3.08°. Force in the people's house is inward-turned, defensive and frustrated with itself. It can defend the home ground with real feeling. It can also turn anger inward and experience every external demand as an intrusion. Mars trines Neptune in the 9th within 0.44°, so that force finds an easy channel into belief, law and long-distance ideals. It can be given a cause, which cuts both ways.

The chart has two roots at once. The Moon is a final dispositor in her own sign. The remaining circuit is a closed loop: Saturn in the 9th to Jupiter retrograde in detriment in the 12th, to Mercury in Capricorn in the 7th, and back to Saturn. The people's own mood is one sovereign center. The other is a triangle of law, hidden expansion and foreign-facing negotiation that governs itself without passing through the Moon. That is the structural shape of the month: a public that owns its feelings, and an institutional loop that does not need to consult them.

The Sun trines Uranus in the 11th within 0.61°, and the Moon sextiles it, so the illumination comes with sudden allies and fast coalition shifts. Exalted Venus in the 9th applies to square Jupiter within 0.94°: generosity of principle meets an overextended promise. Greer finds no hard contact with the ingress, but the U.S. chart takes the hit squarely. The Full Moon's Ascendant opposes U.S. Moon within 0.37°, its Mars opposes U.S. Pluto within 0.52°, and its MC opposes U.S. Saturn within 0.26°. It lands on the nation's natal Moon–Pluto line. Both poles are alive. The public can recover its voice as the owner of the house, or grievance can harden into the permanent posture of a people who feel governed from across the axis.
