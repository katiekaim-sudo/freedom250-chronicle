Campaign position: Wave 2 of the Federal Reserve structure research campaign
Question: Which Federal Reserve legal object acts for each function, and which review, records, liability and disclosure regime reaches it?
Status: current Workbench synthesis; primary-source and case-ranked; no Chronicle, watch, filing or app change
Bottom line
There is no legally complete answer to “Is the Federal Reserve public or private?” The useful answer is a status vector:
identify the actor, the act, the source of authority, the record, the remedy and the statute that supplies jurisdiction.
The Board of Governors is a federal agency. The FOMC is a separately named statutory committee and an “agency” for the Administrative Procedure Act and Freedom of Information Act. Each Reserve Bank is a congressionally chartered body corporate with public duties and private-corporate features. A Reserve Bank may be a federal instrumentality for one statute or function, outside the definition of a federal agency for another, an employer directly suable under a federal employment law, a “person” under the patent-review statute, or an agent handling government money for a False Claims Act inquiry. None of those answers automatically controls the next statute.
The same is true of records. A document physically stored at a Reserve Bank can be a Board record, an FOMC record or a Bank record. The controlling questions are why it was created or obtained, who controls it, and under whose statutory function it was held—not the building, email domain or server alone.
This is why the System can simultaneously possess strong public authority, corporate separateness, congressional accountability and operational insulation without becoming a literal fourth constitutional branch.
1. The authority hierarchy
For this report, propositions are ranked in this order:
constitutional and statutory text;
Supreme Court and controlling-circuit holdings;
current regulations;
other judicial decisions;
official executive-branch or agency interpretations;
official operating disclosures and policies; and
inference.
An actor’s label in a Federal Reserve explainer does not displace a statute-specific judicial test. A case holding that a Reserve Bank is not an “agency” for the Federal Tort Claims Act does not decide the Administrative Procedure Act, the Constitution, tax immunity, employment law, procurement or records custody.

4. The records-routing constitution
Route A — FOMC record
Current 12 C.F.R. §271.2(e) defines Committee records functionally. It reaches information in the possession and control of the FOMC, or in the possession and control of members, officers, employees or agents of the FOMC, the Board or a Reserve Bank in the performance of duties for or on behalf of the Committee or under its direction.
That means a record can be physically held by a Reserve Bank and still be an FOMC record. The question is whether the person possessed or controlled it while performing Committee-directed work. The FOMC—not a Bank’s voluntary-transparency office—is the proper FOIA starting point for such a record.
Route B — Board record
Current 12 C.F.R. §261.2(d) covers information created or obtained by the Board and under its control. It also includes two important Bank-created or Bank-obtained categories:
confidential supervisory information; and
records created or obtained in the performance of Board functions delegated to a Reserve Bank under 12 U.S.C. §248(k).
Records located at a Bank that do not fit the current definition are not Board records merely because the Board oversees the System.
Route C — Reserve Bank record
Reserve Banks are not generally subject to FOIA. In January 2024 the Banks adopted a common Transparency and Accountability Policy governing certain public requests. That is a voluntary Bank policy, not FOIA, and it contains exemptions and procedures of its own.
For a Bank record, the researcher should ask:
which Bank created or obtained it;
whether it was acting on its own statutory authority, as the Board’s delegate, for the FOMC, as Treasury fiscal agent, or as a host/shared-service operator;
whether the record migrated into Board or FOMC possession and control;
whether a separate statute, contract, discovery rule, congressional demand or inspector-general authority supplies another route; and
whether confidentiality law independently restricts disclosure.
Route D — a route other than public-record law
FOIA is not the universe of compulsory access. Depending on the object and purpose, records may be reached through:
statutory reports to Congress;
committee requests or subpoenas;
GAO or inspector-general authorities, subject to statutory limits;
civil discovery;
criminal, regulatory or supervisory process;
contractual audit and access rights;
Federal Records Act or archival requirements where applicable; or
a court order following a valid cause of action.
The existence of another route does not prove that the record is publicly releasable. Conversely, a FOIA exemption does not necessarily defeat an authorized congressional, supervisory or judicial demand.
5. The Fox rule must be read through the 2020 regulation
In Fox News Network, LLC v. Board of Governors (2d Cir. 2010), the court rejected the idea that every Reserve Bank lending record was automatically a Board record. Under the then-current rule, however, it required the Board to search Bank records that were maintained for the Board for administrative reasons under the regulation’s specified conditions.
The Board changed the definition in 2020. The current rule no longer uses the old “maintained for administrative reasons” formulation. It expressly captures confidential supervisory information and records made or obtained while a Bank performs delegated Board functions under §248(k), while excluding other Bank-held records.
Current research rule: Fox remains important for control, search and non-automatic custody, but its quoted regulatory text is historical. A present request must be tested under current §261.2(d).

7. The current master-account cases—and what they do not establish
Banco San Juan Internacional v. Federal Reserve Bank of New York
The Second Circuit’s May 13, 2026 decision held that the plaintiff had no statutory entitlement to continued master-account access and could not use mandamus, due process, Bivens or the pleaded contract theories to reverse the termination.
The district court had treated FRBNY as outside the APA definition of agency. The court of appeals did not adopt that categorical ruling. It assumed without deciding that FRBNY was an agency for the APA claim, then held that account termination was committed to discretion because there was no judicially manageable law to apply. The court also found the pleaded Board-control chain inadequate: the relevant decision was attributed to FRBNY, not the Board.
Custodia Bank, Inc. v. Federal Reserve Board
The Tenth Circuit held on October 31, 2025 that statutory eligibility does not create a mandatory right to a master account and that the Reserve Bank retains discretion. Rehearing en banc was denied March 13, 2026. As of this report’s cutoff, the Supreme Court petition is docketed as No. 26-62, with the response deadline extended to October 13, 2026; there is no grant or merits disposition.
The narrow rule
These cases strongly support a distinction between statutory eligibility and compelled access. They do not establish that every Reserve Bank act is private, unreviewable, immune from constitutional constraint or outside every federal definition of agency.
8. Litigation-routing checklist
Before asserting a claim involving “the Fed,” identify all eight elements:
Defendant: Board, FOMC, a named Reserve Bank, a named officer, the United States, or more than one?
Act: rule, order, denial, contract act, payment operation, employment decision, examination, lending decision or policy statement?
Authority: which statutory section, regulation, delegation, operating circular, contract or committee directive authorized it?
Cause of action: APA, FOIA, FTCA, employment statute, contract, tort, mandamus, constitutional remedy or another statute?
Waiver/capacity: sovereign-immunity waiver, §341 “sue and be sued,” or another express route?
Reviewability: final action, standing, exhaustion, committed-to-discretion bar, political-question concern or statutory preclusion?
Remedy: disclosure, injunction, vacatur, mandamus, reinstatement, contract damages or tort damages?
Record owner: Board, FOMC, Bank, Treasury, shared-service host or a mixed custody chain?
If those fields cannot be filled, “public” or “private” is functioning as rhetoric rather than legal analysis.
9. Corrected shorthand
Unsafe shorthand
Corrected formulation
“Reserve Banks are private.”
Reserve Banks are federally chartered instrumentalities with corporate form; their agency, person, employer and government status varies by statute and function.
“Reserve Banks are federal agencies.”
Some laws or functions treat them as federal instrumentalities or agents; other laws expressly or judicially place them outside federal-agency definitions. Name the regime.
“The Board has every Fed record.”
The Board has Board records as currently defined. Some Bank-created records are included; other Bank records are not.
“FOIA cannot reach records at a Reserve Bank.”
Physical location is not dispositive. FOMC records and qualifying Board records may be held by Bank personnel or systems.
“Fox makes all emergency-lending records public.”
Fox rejected automatic Board custody and applied a now-revised record definition. Exemptions and current §261.2(d) still govern.
“Banco held that FRBNY is not an APA agency.”
The Second Circuit assumed agency status without deciding and affirmed on committed-to-discretion and other grounds.
“Lewis decided the Banks are not federal.”
Lewis decided they are not federal agencies for the FTCA; it expressly recognized instrumentality status under other laws.
“Sue and be sued means no immunity ever.”
The clause supplies broad corporate capacity to litigate; the claim, function, governing law, preemption and any specific immunity still matter.
“A Bank employee is a federal employee.”
Generally not for the regimes examined here. That does not decide whether a president exercises federal officer power on the FOMC.
“Shared service means the twelve Banks merged.”
Shared governance and allocated operations can coexist with twelve separate corporations. The constitutive act and function must be traced.
10. What would prove a status change
A genuine change in legal status should be supported by one or more of the following:
an amendment to the Federal Reserve Act or another governing statute;
a new controlling judicial interpretation of the relevant statutory term;
an effective regulation changing agency, record or delegation definitions;
a valid charter, delegation or reorganization instrument changing the acting entity;
a new cause of action or sovereign-immunity waiver;
a contract or operating circular reallocating rights for a payment service; or
a documented transfer of custody and legal control over a record system.
The following do not, by themselves, prove a legal-status change:
a new public-facing brand;
shared email, software or hosting;
centralized procurement or cost allocation;
a consolidated financial presentation;
congressional criticism;
an inspector-general recommendation;
a voluntary transparency policy; or
a court’s statute-specific label copied into another legal regime.

12. Campaign ruling after Wave 2
The Federal Reserve’s mixed form is not a gap in the law waiting for one master label. It is a deliberately distributed legal architecture. Congress created a federal Board, a statutory monetary-policy committee and twelve separately incorporated Banks, then assigned different public powers, oversight channels, liabilities and record routes to each.
The practical consequence is strict routing discipline:
“Who controls the Fed?” becomes “Which actor exercised which authority, who could countermand it, who owns the resulting record, what law permits review, and what remedy exists?”
That formulation is strong enough to carry into appointments, shared operations, purse control, crisis command and the twelve-Bank foundation register without forcing the System into a false public/private binary.
