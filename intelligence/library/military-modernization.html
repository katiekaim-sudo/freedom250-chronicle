The military is reorganizing around shared data, with named vendors at the center

The Pentagon is rebuilding around one chain: sensors, networks, shared data, decisions, action and supply. The strongest evidence is new organizations and contracts, not new weapons, and several big September 2026 moves are still only studies or reviews.

What we found

  • On June 22, 2026 the Army announced a common data baseline for its Next Generation Command and Control program, led by Anduril, with Palantir and Raft. The Navy created a central integrator office called PAE Mission Systems.
  • The Space Development Agency awarded 36 more tracking satellites on July 13, 2026, linking Golden Dome missile defense to its satellite network. The 2026 AI strategy aims AI agents at battle management and 'kill chain execution', and wants commercial models deployable within 30 days of public release.
  • Not everything moves forward: the Space Force accepted the GPS OCX ground system in 2025, then cancelled it in April 2026 after testing found operational risk.
  • A review team (FAST) was set up by a memo signed August 4, 2026, released September 25, to recommend cutting or merging programs. On September 30 the Secretary created an Office of Religious Affairs with 60- and 90-day budget and charter clocks. No director, target list or savings has been named for either.
  • Also on September 30, Project Meridian was commissioned as a 120-day future-warfare study, with Elon Musk, Palmer Luckey and Newt Gingrich named as co-directors. No partner organization, contract, program or fielded system exists yet.

Why it matters

Defense is being rebuilt through its contracts, data rules and reporting lines, with private firms inside the system. The study notes vendors supply parts but do not own the mission, the data or targeting authority.

What to watch

  • 2027-01-28 · Project Meridian's report gate; a public unclassified report and a classified annex are intended.
  • pending · The Office of Religious Affairs' 60-day budget plan and 90-day charter and staffing steps, counted from September 30, 2026.
  • pending · The first FAST recommendation or named program cut.
