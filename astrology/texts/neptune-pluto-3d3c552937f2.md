## One chart, two focal lenses

**Neptune lens.** What imagined world, faith, medium, or ambiguity can bind a
collective strongly enough to become its shared environment? The 11th-house

infrastructure beneath it, and what must be excluded or transformed for it to
feel inevitable? Pluto's function is not a second chart; it is the pressure
inside the same collective vehicle.

Together they ask when a world-picture stops being an opinion and becomes the
substrate through which a society coordinates.
