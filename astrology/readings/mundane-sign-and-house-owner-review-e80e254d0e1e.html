The distinction governing this pass

Signs and Houses are cousins, not synonyms.

Sign = how movement is configured and what equipment that configuration supplies
House = where and in what national arena the chart acts

Modality supplies movement. Element shapes that movement. The unique Sign is an independently governed collective expression of both. A conventional Sign, Planet, or House analogy creates no semantic assignment among them.

Structural foundations for the Signs

Modality — how movement develops

  • Cardinal: opens a phase, mobilizes available material, and establishes direction across a threshold.
  • Fixed: consolidates what has formed and sustains its continuity, concentration, and development.
  • Mutable: modifies, redistributes, or releases an established phase toward transition.

Element — what shapes and carries the movement

  • Fire: ignition and animation.
  • Earth: practical and material reality.
  • Air: thought and exchange.
  • Water: emotion and feeling.

These seven inputs are intentionally spare. They should help us understand a Sign without importing civic topics, chart arenas, or a Planet's motivation.
