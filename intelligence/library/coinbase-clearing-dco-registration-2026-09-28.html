Coinbase gets its own derivatives clearinghouse

On September 28, 2026 the CFTC registered Coinbase Clearing LLC to clear fully collateralized futures, options on futures and swaps, but no first product, member or trade has yet been established.

What we found

  • This is a Commission registration order under Commodity Exchange Act section 5b, not a proposal, speech or staff no-action letter.
  • Coinbase Global now owns distinct venue, FCM and DCO affiliates; the regulated clearing actor is Coinbase Clearing LLC, held through The Clearing Company of San Francisco.
  • The application rulebook allows FCM-intermediated and qualifying self-clearing members, requires full collateral before accepting a trade and says the DCO therefore does not maintain a mutualized member-default financial-resource package.
  • Coinbase's current domestic perpetual-style futures still publicly name Nodal Clear and use initial and variation margin. No first Coinbase Clearing product, member, collateral notice, settlement institution or transaction was located.
  • The order does not approve spot crypto, securities clearing, USDC or Base settlement, current margined perpetuals, or Coinbase's separately pending single-stock-perpetual product and customer-margin filings.

Why it matters

Coinbase can now bring a regulated central-counterparty function inside its corporate group, giving it more control over admission, collateral, settlement operations and data. But the permission is confined to a fully collateralized model, and concentration, custody, liquidation, affiliate and operating risks remain.

What to watch

  • pending · First named product, clearing member and accepted transaction at Coinbase Clearing
  • pending · Eligible-collateral notice and identification of settlement banks or qualified custodians, especially any official USDC treatment
  • pending · Any Coinbase Derivatives filing that designates Coinbase Clearing or migrates products and open interest from Nodal Clear
  • pending · CFTC and SEC decisions on Coinbase's separate single-stock-perpetual product and customer-margin filings
