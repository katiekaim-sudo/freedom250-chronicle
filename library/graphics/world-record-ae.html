United Arab Emirates: what does the saved digital-money record establish?

The UAE has a layered regulatory architecture rather than one crypto licence: CBUAE owns payment tokens, Digital Dirham and licensed financial activities regardless of technology; SCA owns the federal investment-type VASP perimeter; VARA covers Dubai outside DIFC; DFSA covers DIFC; and FSRA covers ADGM.
