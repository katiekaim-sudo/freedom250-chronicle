# Neptune on the OCC as Custodia's BIO comes due

## The story (research side — facts only)

- On **October 13, 2026** the government's brief in opposition is due in *Custodia Bank, Inc. v. Federal Reserve Board of Governors*, Supreme Court No. 26-62. The petition was filed July 10 and docketed July 14. A first extension moved the response from August 13 to September 11; a second motion filed September 8 and granted September 9 set October 13 for all respondents. A BIO is not a cert grant, a conference result, or master-account access. (`99 - Templates/watch_calendar.json` watch `w-2026-09-11-custodia-bio`; vault Primer; `02 - Research/Judicial Money Map — Source Document.md` JM-06)
- The same day, comments close on the OCC/FDIC Community Reinvestment Act NPRM (FR 2026-16454, Docket OCC-2026-0694). The Board is not a co-proposer. A proposal and a requested judgment against the 2023 CRA rules are not a final rule or an entered judgment. (`watch_calendar.json` watch `w-2026-10-13-occ-fdic-cra-nprm`)
- The same day, Treasury announced enforcement actions imposing more than **$1.21 billion** in penalties on Bank of America for anti-money-laundering and sanctions violations, the third-largest penalty in Treasury history and, Treasury said, the largest in OCC history. The OCC's own notice is a **$750 million** civil money penalty for Bank Secrecy Act violations, which the OCC called the largest in its history. These are announced penalties, not a collected judgment and not a charter decision. (`01 - Events/2026-10-13-2306 - Treasury fines Bank of America $1.21 billion.md`; `01 - Events/2026-10-13-2032 - OCC fines Bank of America $750 million, its largest penalty.md`)
