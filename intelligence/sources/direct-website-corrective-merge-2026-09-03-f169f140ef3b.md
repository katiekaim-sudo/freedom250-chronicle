## Crypto and market-structure correction

The CFTC direct product database is the key addition. Seventeen Bitnomial
perpetual futures appeared as certified on September 2: Bitcoin, Ether, Solana,
XRP, Bitcoin Cash, Litecoin, Dogecoin, Avalanche, Chainlink, Polkadot, Stellar
Lumens, Hedera, Cardano, Aave, Tezos, Tron, and Shiba Inu. Separately, two
KalshiEx perpetual proposals—EUR/USD and U.S. 10-year Treasury—appeared as
`Approval Pending (45)` on August 31. The combination is evidence that
perpetual-style product design is entering regulated derivatives records across
crypto, FX, and rates. It is **not** evidence that all products are approved,
listed, liquid, cleared, or available to every customer.

CFTC also published a final CORRA/Overnight TIIE clearing rule, reporting relief
for Electron Exchange, and leadership remarks describing programmable,
composable, continuous markets and their liquidation, rehypothecation,
procyclicality, oracle, interoperability, and cyber risks. The speech is
official context but expressly personal views, not Commission policy.

The SEC added three staff beneficial-ownership interpretations and set a
September 17 operational roundtable on 24-hour trading. These direct-site
objects materially strengthen the existing thesis that market authorities are
working through the operating requirements of continuous and tokenized market
infrastructure. They do not prove official adoption of any cryptocurrency as
money, settlement finality, or a replacement for existing rails.
