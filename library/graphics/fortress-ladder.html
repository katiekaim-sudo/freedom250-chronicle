What's still uncertain

  • A stated 14- or 21-day design capability is not an observed test; the package says an independence claim is only as strong as its load boundary, duration and operating-test record.
  • The projects have different boundaries (full base, critical load, a gate, a tenant facility), so they are not counted toward one readiness score.
  • The full saved FORTRESS source ledger is available. It lists controlling, framework and project sources with their authority, access state and limits; the signed memorandum remains under the September 30 access hold.
  • Source gap (package README): The official release links the signed memorandum, but the publisher PDF was not retrievable through the available browser, HTTP or PDF-reader paths on September 30. Search indexing exposed the memorandum text and a secondary mirror reproduced it, but this package keeps the memorandum in official_link_access_hold state. The accessible official release controls the confirmed public announcement; memorandum-only wording must be rechecked against the signed PDF before quotation.
  • Candidate primary source, not yet checked: 10 U.S.C. §2920 (https://www.govinfo.gov/link/uscode/10/2920)
