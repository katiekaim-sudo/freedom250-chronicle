Which new nuclear-power rules are final, and which are still only proposed?

Of the 8 saved nuclear filings since February 2026 in the record, 2 are final: NRC Part 53, the advanced-reactor licensing framework (published March 30, 2026, effective April 29, 2026) and the NRC's fiscal-2026 fee rule (published June 16, 2026, effective August 17, 2026). 5 are still proposals only: Worker Safety and Health Requirements To Support Reform of Nuclear Reactor Testing; Regulatory Framework for Fusion Machines; Streamlining Contested Adjudications in Licensing Proceedings; NRC Reviews of Reactor Designs Previously Authorized by DOE or Department of War; Licensing Requirements for Microreactors and Other Reactors With Comparable Risk Profiles. The exceptions from foreign ownership, control or domination rule is filed as a proposal, but the factual spine records that a companion direct final rule was confirmed and took effect July 7, 2026. This is the saved filing set and its documented companion instruments, not a current census of every nuclear rulemaking.
