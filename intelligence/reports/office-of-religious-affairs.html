Start here

The Office of Religious Affairs (ORA) is now its own maintained research question. The September 30, 2026 memorandum establishes the office and its Director effective immediately, places the Director directly under the Secretary and Deputy Secretary without intervening authority, assigns policy and resource powers, realigns the Armed Forces Chaplains Board, and starts 60- and 90-day implementation clocks.

The office is established. Its Director, transferred authorities, operating staff, budget, charter, service requirements, funded programs and observed constitutional effects remain separate states.

Read order
