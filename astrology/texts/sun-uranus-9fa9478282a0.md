# Sun–Uranus

## The planets

**Sun** — *Authority, illumination, "everything revolves around the Sun."* MUNDANE: the head of government (the President); the executive branch; the focus of executive power. The Sun's condition shows how the government will fare. (Greer: "the Sun represents the actual head of government — whoever officially calls the shots in the executive branch.")

**Uranus** — *Disruption, revolution, the lightning bolt.* MUNDANE: radical movements; revolutionary politics; sudden upheavals; technology and innovation; strikes and civil unrest; the urge to overthrow the established order. Uranus is the planet of the unexpected — in a mundane chart it shows where the status quo will be broken, willingly or not.

## The combination — mundane

Under helpful aspects (sextile, trine), Sun-Uranus brings "successful reforms and changes for the better; new ideas enter public conversation," as Greer describes. The government innovates. Reforms that would normally face institutional resistance pass smoothly. The President appears forward-thinking, even visionary. Technology policy advances. The public appetite for change is met with constructive legislative action rather than chaos. Alliances are restructured in ways that benefit the nation.

Under hostile aspects (conjunction, square, opposition), Uranus strikes the executive like lightning. Greer warns of "sudden unexpected troubles for government; bad decisions; abuses of power; alliances break down." The government is blindsided — by a crisis, a scandal, a technological disruption, or a popular revolt it did not see coming. Executive decisions made under these aspects tend to be impulsive and poorly considered. Abuses of power become visible and generate backlash. International alliances fracture without warning. The nation lurches rather than pivots, and the disruption tends to leave lasting instability.

**As trigger:** Sun-Uranus aspects are fast-moving (days to about two weeks) and mark the specific moments when sudden disruption, reform impulses, and unexpected events activate whatever broader outer-planet storylines are in play.
