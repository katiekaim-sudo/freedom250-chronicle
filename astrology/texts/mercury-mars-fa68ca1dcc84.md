# Mercury–Mars

## The planets

**Mercury** — *Communication, intellect, commerce, transport.* MUNDANE: the media; the transportation and communications infrastructure; trade and commerce; education and intellectual life; the postal system and telecommunications. Mercury's condition shows how information flows through the nation — freely or obstructed, truthfully or deceptively.

**Mars** — *Action, aggression, the cutting edge.* MUNDANE: the military; police and law enforcement; violent crime; industrial disputes; fires and accidents; surgeons. Mars energizes whatever it touches — constructively through decisive action, destructively through conflict and force.

## The combination — mundane

Under helpful aspects (conjunction, sextile, trine), Mercury-Mars is "favorable for all Mercurial affairs," as Greer puts it. "Technological innovations favored; education, literary activities, sciences flourish." The national conversation is energized and productive. Media does investigative work rather than just generating heat. The transportation and communications sectors see real improvements — new technology deployed, infrastructure upgraded, scientific breakthroughs announced. Military communications function well. Education policy is pushed forward by decisive action rather than stalled by committee.

Under hostile aspects (square, opposition), the combination becomes "unfavorable for the communication and transport sector; strikes, protests, disturbances." Speech is weaponized. Media confrontations escalate past argument into something closer to combat. Transportation strikes or disruptions make headlines. Protests — especially those organized through media and communications channels — become heated and potentially violent. The sciences and education suffer from politicization. Military communications may be compromised or leak at the worst moment. Legal arguments turn into courtroom combat where rhetoric overwhelms reason.

**As trigger:** Mercury-Mars aspects are fast-moving (days to about two weeks) and mark the specific moments when aggressive communication, media conflict, or transportation disruptions activate whatever broader outer-planet storylines are in play.
