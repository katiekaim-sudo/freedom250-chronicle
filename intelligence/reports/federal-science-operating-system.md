## Pre-2025 statutory hinge

**September 7 legal attribution correction:** the 2027 framework deadline originated in [NEIMA section 103(a)(4), 132 Stat. 5572](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/PLAW-115publ439/pdf/PLAW-115publ439.pdf). [ADVANCE section 205, 138 Stat. 1460–1462](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/PLAW-118publ67/pdf/PLAW-118publ67.pdf) amended definitions and related provisions and required a separate report; it did not originate that deadline. The source chronology and forward clock below now use that attribution. This targeted correction does not advance other evidence cutoffs.

### 2024-07-09 — ADVANCE Act supplies fusion definitions within the existing NEIMA framework

**Entity:** Congress; President; Nuclear Regulatory Commission
**Clock:** statute / federal rulemaking
**State after:** Atomic Energy Act definitions amended; existing NEIMA December 31, 2027 framework deadline retained

Section 205 of Public Law 118-67 added the term `fusion machine` to the Atomic
Energy Act and brought radioactive material produced by a fusion machine into
the byproduct-material definition. The December 31, 2027 optional-framework
deadline already existed in NEIMA section 103(a)(4), enacted in 2019; ADVANCE
section 205(b) amended the related provisions and terminology. Section 205(c)
separately required a mass-manufactured-fusion licensing report within one year.
These provisions do not themselves create a final rule or project license.

**Sources:** [Public Law 118-67](https://www.govinfo.gov/app/details/PLAW-118publ67) · [NRC — Fusion and the ADVANCE Act](https://www.nrc.gov/materials/fusion/mass-production-status) · [NRC fusion FAQ](https://www.nrc.gov/materials/fusion/faq)

## 2025

### 2025-01-20 — national energy emergency declared

**Entity:** President; executive departments and agencies  
**Clock:** emergency / energy infrastructure  
**State after:** active national-emergency framework

Executive Order 14156 identifies inadequate energy production, transportation and infrastructure as an unusual and extraordinary threat. It directs agencies to use lawful emergency permitting and other authorities to facilitate energy supply. The order repeatedly limits action to what existing law permits.

**Source:** [White House — Declaring a National Energy Emergency](https://www.whitehouse.gov/presidential-actions/2025/01/declaring-a-national-energy-emergency/)

### 2025-04-23 — accreditation and institutional-value clock separated from research funding

**Entity:** President; Department of Education; recognized accreditors  
**Clock:** accreditation / Title IV institutional eligibility  
**State after:** policy direction toward student-oriented quality and value

Executive Order 14279 directs the Education Department to realign recognition of accreditors toward high-quality, high-value programs and new educational models. This is an institutional-eligibility and student-aid lane. It is adjacent to, but legally distinct from, agency R&D awards.

**Source:** [White House — Executive Order 14279 PDF](https://www.whitehouse.gov/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/eo-14279.pdf)

### 2025-07-23 — qualifying data-center infrastructure receives an acceleration lane

**Entity:** President; Commerce; Energy; Interior; Defense; EPA; CEQ; Permitting Council  
**Clock:** siting / finance / permitting  
**State after:** federal priority and support pathway

Executive Order 14318 defines qualifying projects using alternative thresholds: at least $500 million in committed capital expenditure, more than 100 MW of incremental electric load, protection of national security, or designation by a named Secretary. It directs an initiative that may use loans, guarantees, grants, tax incentives and offtake agreements, plus federal permitting and land actions.

The order covers associated power and technology components. It does not identify every qualifying project or approve any particular parcel.

**Source:** [White House — Accelerating Federal Permitting of Data Center Infrastructure](https://www.whitehouse.gov/presidential-actions/2025/07/accelerating-federal-permitting-of-data-center-infrastructure/)

### 2025-08-07 — discretionary grant oversight shifts toward priority and progress review

**Entity:** President; OMB; grantmaking agencies  
**Clock:** award design / annual review / termination  
**State after:** senior-appointee review and stronger progress controls directed

Executive Order 14332 directs pre-issuance senior-appointee review of funding opportunities and discretionary awards, annual review for consistency with agency priorities and substantial progress, and revised terms permitting termination when an award no longer advances agency priorities or the national interest. It also directs tighter drawdown authorization and limits on facilities-and-administration costs.

**Source:** [White House — Improving Oversight of Federal Grantmaking](https://www.whitehouse.gov/presidential-actions/2025/08/improving-oversight-of-federal-grantmaking/)

### 2025-11-24 — Genesis Mission chartered

**Entity:** President; DOE; OSTP; OMB; NSTC; participating agencies  
**Clock:** platform / mission / interagency integration  
**State after:** operative executive direction

Executive Order 14363 establishes the Genesis Mission and directs DOE to establish the American Science and Security Platform. The platform is to integrate:

- federal and commercial high-performance computing;
- secure cloud AI environments;
- AI agents and scientific foundation models;
- federal, proprietary, open and synthetic datasets;
- autonomous and AI-augmented experimentation and manufacturing tools.

The order also directs metadata and provenance work; coordinated funding opportunities and prizes; fellowships, internships and apprenticeships; standard partnership, data-use and model-sharing agreements; and policies for ownership, licensing, trade secrets and commercialization of AI-directed experimental results.

Every major command is bounded by applicable law, security requirements and available appropriations.

**Source:** [Federal Register — EO 14363](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2025/11/28/2025-21665/launching-the-genesis-mission)

**Clock:** proposed rule / federal licensing framework

### 2026-03-17 — Genesis RFA defines the fusion DCP work program

**Entity:** DOE; Office of Science; Genesis Mission
**Clock:** funding opportunity / application process
**State after:** cross-domain RFA opened; no recipient award or platform access

DOE announced an anticipated $293.76 million across all Genesis subjects, not
fusion alone. The RFA describes Phase I awards of $500,000–$750,000 for nine
months and Phase II awards of $6–$15 million over three years. It makes the

non-laboratory awards may use Other Transaction Authority, so one federal award

## Clock rule

The companion
`FEDERAL_FUSION_DATE_TIME_PLACE_LEDGER_2020-2035.md`
preserves each source clock, timezone, locus, precision defect and later D.C.
chart convention without calculating or interpreting a chart.

The object-level states, eight-company field map and award-accounting chain
live in
`FEDERAL_FUSION_PROJECT_CAPITAL_REGULATORY_REGISTRY_2022-2035.md`.

Never compress these into one “launch” date:

`report -> memorandum -> agency plan -> budget request -> appropriation -> funding opportunity -> award or contract -> platform integration -> test -> operating capability -> verified result -> payment or commercialization`

For fusion, preserve interacting but parallel clocks:

- `communications -> roadmap -> funded mechanism -> verified milestone`;
- `federal statutory direction -> proposed rule -> final rule -> effective rule`;
- `Agreement State framework` and `project application -> completeness -> review -> license -> inspection -> operating authorization`;
- `site control -> permits -> construction -> equipment -> commissioning`;
- `queue/request -> studies -> interconnection agreement -> upgrades -> synchronization -> meter -> export`;
- `reaction -> ignition -> separately measured target/plasma gain -> engineering gain -> heat -> gross electricity -> net electricity -> sustained operation`.

Those lanes converge at a named project's licensed, interconnected and measured
commercial operation; none is evidence that the others have completed.
