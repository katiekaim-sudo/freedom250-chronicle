## Why P1 is proposed, not silently promoted

The pass-chart audit found no principled reason to replace inception with a later pass simply because the later chart looks stronger:

- Mercury–Mars 2023 begins hidden in Scorpio and completes with exalted Mars in public Capricorn.
- Mercury–Neptune 2025 begins at late Pisces in the 10th and later crosses the Aries threshold.
- Venus–Saturn and Venus–Neptune begin with exalted Venus in Pisces, then revise under retrograde motion before Aries re-expression.

Choosing the most dignified, angular, or narratively convenient later pass would substitute culmination for origin. The package therefore proposes earliest chronological P1 as the deterministic coordinate while keeping the field explicitly pending Katie's disposition.

## Mercury–Venus: charts without a persona

Mercury–Venus proves why a visible exception is better than forced symmetry. Its six conjunction crossings calculate cleanly, including four inside the governed chart window. But the pair cannot reach geocentric opposition and its directed hand reverses. Without a separate inferior-planet doctrine, it has no unique conjunction-to-opposition family and therefore no family persona.

The crossing charts remain available as exact geometry. The absent persona preserves the truth of what the current method cannot yet say.
