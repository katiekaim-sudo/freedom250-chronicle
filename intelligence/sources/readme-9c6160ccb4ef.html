Federal Government Whole-Site Recertification — 2026-08-01

This package deepens the incorporated July 25 whole-federal sweep. It is not a first government sweep and it does not reopen the older, narrower July 18 Government-Wide Audit as though that were whole-government coverage.

The new question is site architecture and evidence reach: what federal domains and publishing parents exist now, which are live and canonical, what non-news source surfaces they expose, what their sitemaps and archives actually cover, and what those surfaces add or correct across the Chronicle's current storylines.

Operational meaning of “entire websites”

The campaign does not pretend that a human or agent has read every byte on every federal server. It tests the discoverable public corpus through:

  • the daily CISA .gov registry;
  • the U.S. Government Manual and USAGov agency index;
  • root redirects, robots directives, XML sitemap indexes and nested sitemaps;
  • site search, document libraries, publications and archive indexes;
  • rules, notices, dockets, adjudications and public-inspection objects;
  • strategies, policy memoranda, performance plans and reports;
  • budgets, audited financial statements, grants, loans, contracts and awards;
  • datasets, APIs, statistics, dashboards and operating-status pages;
  • organization, leadership, delegation, workforce and reorganization pages;
  • OIG, GAO, courts and congressional validation surfaces.

Anything blocked, absent, stale, dynamic-only, classified or not separately sweepable is recorded as such. “Not found on the reached surfaces” never becomes “did not happen.”

Evidence boundary

The CISA domain registry proves registration, not a live website, canonical status, agency authority, publication completeness or current content. The Government Manual is an official organization handbook, not a live change log. An XML sitemap is a discovery map, not proof that its URLs are current or that unlisted content does not exist.

This workbench package remains outside-model research. No vault note, live queue, watch calendar, entity topology or app build changes merely because a candidate page is interesting. Promotion follows merge, dedup, claim audit and Katie's review.
