Mercury–Mars Cycle Review

Enduring question: How does message, transport, or trade become action, enforcement, or conflict?
Disposition: keep the conjunction anatomy; keep the 2025–26 persona candidate as a high-yield pilot; treat 2023–24 as a restraint control.

Family record

  • 2023–24: three passes; proposed P1 at 11°54′ Scorpio; candidate +6.001 days; two later family recontacts remain, and the candidate stays mostly fused/hidden.
  • 2025–26: five passes; proposed P1 at 19°11′ Scorpio; candidate +22.054 days; four later recontacts remain.
  • 2028: five-pass first-after bracket; no successor boundary and no persona candidate.

What the charts add

The 2023 seed places Mercury and domiciled Mars together in Scorpio/12th with Sagittarius rising. Six days later they remain there with the same Ascendant and nearly the same governing structure. This is a useful negative control: the persona does not earn a new story.

The 2025 seed places Mercury and domiciled Mars in Scorpio/3rd under Mercury-ruled Virgo rising. At the candidate, both are in Sagittarius/3rd, but Mercury has stationed retrograde and is in detriment while Mars remains direct. ASC–Mars is 0.116° and MC–Mars 0.538°. The pair is still about signal becoming action, but the two jobs are no longer identical: message is under revision while action continues.

Narrowing use

For Iran, this asks whether orders, logistics, transport, or rhetoric are being revised while force continues. For AI, it asks whether a claim turns into engineering/deployment work. For investigations, it asks whether information becomes an executable enforcement act. It does not prove a strike, deployment, leak, or prosecution.
