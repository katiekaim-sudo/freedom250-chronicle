December 27, 2024–January 2, 2025 Rule Perimeter and Operating Identity Window

Which late-December 2024 reporting, credit and identity perimeters survived long enough to become operating machinery?

Research summary

The fixed publication window produced three sharply different perimeter outcomes. Congress disapproved the Treasury/IRS DeFi broker-reporting rule and the CFPB overdraft-lending rule, so those rules have no force or effect; the SEC's EDGAR Next rule instead became the mandatory credential, permission, delegation, MFA and optional API control plane for electronic filers. The comparison is about which perimeter acquired durable operating identity, not generic regulation or deregulation.
