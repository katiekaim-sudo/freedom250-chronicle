Research summary

D.V.D. is the cleanest same-case judicial return from the March 2025 window, but it is not the only same-object maturation. The same action-date window also produced an operating and amended automobile-tariff system, a federal-labor exclusion regime that was implemented, expanded and legislatively contested, a D.C. task force later described as an operating multi-agency structure, a Smithsonian review that became a report and successor order, and a Commerce investment office that acquired leadership, operating examples and a $100 million FY2027 budget request. The shared factual pattern is command to authority container to operating machinery to counterparty, record, budget or court test—not one coordinated program or proof of policy success.
