# Libra New Moon as the diesel deal and the hang-up

## The story (research side — facts only)

- On **October 9–10** Katie's Russia/Ukraine folder spiked around one deal. Axios, via a BRICS Info save, said President Trump decided on a **diesel deal with Russia after Zelensky ignored six US requests** to stop attacking Russian refineries (`01 - Events/2026-10-10-0402 - Trump diesel deal after Zelensky ignored six US requests.md`).
- A Hormuz Letter save the same night said the US **lifted sanctions on Russian diesel**, authorizing imports **through April 2027**, after Trump announced Russia would supply **4.8 million tons**. Ukrainian military sources said they will **escalate drone and cruise missile strikes** on Russia's energy infrastructure through the winter, and that more than **51% of Russia's oil refining capacity is already offline** (`01 - Events/2026-10-09-2245 - Ukraine to escalate winter strikes on Russian oil.md`). Visegrad24 posted video of **Ukrainian drones striking an oil terminal in Rostov** (`01 - Events/2026-10-10-0042 - Ukrainian drones strike Rostov oil terminal.md`).
- The peace-track desk saved the other half of the bargain: the Kremlin said neither Trump nor Putin wanted to hang up first from a **90-minute “very friendly” call** (`01 - Events/2026-10-10-1416 - Kremlin neither Trump nor Putin wanted to hang up.md`), and Russian agencies said Putin had received Trump's **Ukraine de-escalation proposals with “understanding”** (`01 - Events/2026-10-10-0944 - Putin received Trump Ukraine proposals with understanding.md`). Trump, asked about Zelensky calling him weak over the oil deal, said “Okay.” (`01 - Events/2026-10-09-2112 - Trump shrugs off Zelensky calling him weak over oil.md`).
- The same window's China save is a separate ledger item: WatcherGuru reported China officially calling for a **national blockchain network** as economic strategy (`01 - Events/2026-10-10-1321 - China calls for a national blockchain network.md`). Bessent named Judy Shelton as an adviser on issues including **China's financial system** (`01 - Events/2026-10-09-1640 - Bessent names Judy Shelton as China-finance adviser.md`).
- On **October 11** the bargain moved from a call to a public order. Rapid Response posted Trump's Truth Social: **energy ceasefire** in the Russia-Ukraine war, **effective immediately**, both parties agreed, do not break it (`01 - Events/2026-10-11-1524 - Trump energy ceasefire Russia Ukraine effective immediately.md`). Minutes later Axios, via Kobeissi, quoted Zelensky: the ceasefire **“is news to me”** and “If President Trump negotiated it with Putin, please tell us when the ceasefire begins” (`01 - Events/2026-10-11-1448 - BREAKING Ukraine s President Zelensky says the energy ceasefire.md`).
- FT, via Kobeissi, said the White House is **threatening to halt weapons supplies** and **cut off intelligence sharing** to pressure Zelensky to stop striking Russian energy infrastructure, and is lobbying Europe to end those attacks (`01 - Events/2026-10-11-1954 - BREAKING The Trump Administration is now threatening to halt.md`). Bessent, answering critics of the diesel surge, said **Europe has given Russia more money since the start of the war than it has given Ukraine** (`01 - Events/2026-10-11-1327 - HOLY CRAP Treasury Sec Scott Bessent just MIC DROPPED.md`). Elon Musk replied to ZeroHedge that it is **time for Ukraine to get a new leader** (`01 - Events/2026-10-11-0501 - I agree time for Ukraine to get a new.md`). These are saved posts and named press reports, not a filed treaty text.
