Research summary

A retained July 13, 2026 interpretation: the observed transition looked less like one replacement currency than a federated, programmable system organized around conversion points—regulated claims, collateral, liquidity, settlement, cross-border adapters, insurance and loss allocation. It is neither proof of one coordinated plan nor a current factual master; route every present-tense claim to the maintained component objects.
