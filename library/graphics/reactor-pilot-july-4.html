Did the DOE reactor pilot program get three test reactors to criticality by July 4, 2026?

Yes, by DOE's own count. DOE selected 11 projects on August 12, 2025 with a goal of at least three test reactors reaching criticality by July 4, 2026. DOE reported Deployable Energy's Unity as the pilot's third criticality (June 30, 2026, announced July 1, 2026). DOE reported Aalo Atomics' Aalo-X as the pilot's fourth criticality (July 4, 2026, announced July 6, 2026). DOE later called Oklo's Groves (early August) the fifth that summer. This saved comparison does not profile the first two, and the count is DOE's, not an independent census; criticality is a test milestone, not a commercial license or power delivery.
