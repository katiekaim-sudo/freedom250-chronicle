Research summary

An eleven-entity implementation and dependency map beneath OMB M-25-21/M-25-22: agency AI is becoming a federated layer of rights-, service-, regulatory- and mission-affecting systems rather than one federal platform. It preserves high-impact labels versus functional materiality, human-review limits, prime/reseller/provider separation, contract and acceptance clocks, recurring commercial infrastructure, NARA evidence custody, and exact return objects without treating inventories as complete or internal AI as a downstream legal outcome. A September 3 targeted award refresh confirms VA exercised IBM Pension Optimization Option 4 on August 24 for $14,941,471.41, extending the current period through August 31, 2027; this proves contractual continuity, not payment, system operation or outcomes.
