# Libra New Moon Braid Review Hearing

This packet prepares the first governed return to the September 26 Aries Full
Moon overlaps. It contains no review outcome. Before October 10 every owner
remains **scheduled** and every existing snapshot remains **current**.

## The hearing order

For each braid or comparison:

1. Read the frozen Aries Full Moon snapshot exactly as authored.
2. Read the October 10 Libra New Moon as a new whole chart; do not treat it as
   an update patch to the old chart.
3. Review any primary-source factual or institutional receipts that arrived
   after the prior authored cutoff.
4. Ask whether each old strand is retained, revised, released, outcompeted or
   unresolved. Several relationships may continue in different jobs.
5. If a new overlap is useful, author it as a new window with a new review date.
   Never move the old strand forward in place.

## Questions for every strand

- Did the relationship remain in direct dialogue, become inherited substrate,
  appear only as a seed echo or leave the new chapter entirely?
- Did the whole chart strengthen the same job, assign a different job or favor
  a stronger ordinary rival?
- Did any new factual receipt change the institutional story? If not, leave the
  factual state unchanged even when the symbolism is vivid.
- Are the constructive and shadow poles still honest?
- Is this a literal testable call? If yes, register it separately in the
  Forecast Ledger. Ordinary review creates no prediction.

## Allowed dispositions

- **retain** — still useful in the same bounded job;
- **revise** — still useful, but its role or question changes;
- **release** — no longer useful in the new window;
- **outcompete** — a stronger whole-chart explanation wins;
- **unresolved** — the chart or record does not decide.

## Fail-closed rule

The builder may change this packet from `scheduled` to `review due` when the
date arrives. It may not choose a disposition, author a new strand, rewrite the
historical ledger or register a Forecast Ledger call.
