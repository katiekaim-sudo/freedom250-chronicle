Freedom 250 · Banks and the dollar · Congress

What did each House hearing on the Fed set out to change, and who testified?

The library records 11 House Financial Services hearings on the Fed, from March 4, 2025 to June 25, 2026: 9 by the Task Force on Monetary Policy and 2 by the full committee or a subcommittee. The Task Force heard 45 witnesses, and the library reads 12 of them as 'Analytical'; each card gives the witness's affiliation, a quote, and links to the committee memo and transcript.

What's still uncertain

  • Stance labels are an interpretive reading of each prepared statement. The labels are the library's, not the witnesses' or the committee's.
  • All 11 hearing rows are marked 'needs primary source' in the master: the committee's own hearing pages are not linked yet. The video links are the committee's YouTube recordings; the memo and transcript links open the vault's saved copies.
  • 'What it was about' is the library's reading of each hearing's purpose, carried from The Hearings page; it is not the committee's wording.
  • The committee's September 2026 schedule listed a tenth Task Force hearing, 'Examining the Future of Central Banking', for September 23, 2026. The master has no row for it and no hearing after June 25, 2026, so whether it (or any later Task Force hearing) was held is unconfirmed.
  • No caption transcript is on file for the hearings of January 14, 2026 and April 29, 2026.
