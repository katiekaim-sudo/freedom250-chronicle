2026 Full Moon family triage

Every registered 2026 Full Moon is also the Full phase of a New Moon planted about 546 days earlier. The Chronicle had calculated those families but mostly heard only their seeds and harvests. This audit restores the missing First Quarter as a contextual hinge for all thirteen families before deciding which ones deserve a full companion reread.

The main result is not that every quarter is secretly a main-character chart. It is that the quarter often tells us how the family will reach its harvest: which degree it holds, which house receives the pressure, which root temporarily commands the field, which later body the lights configure, and whether the spatial container is already in place.

No family is ranked by political importance here. Structural flags route human attention only. The audit makes no forecast, story verdict, causation claim or factual comparison and does not alter any locked reading.

Four eclipse transitions, not one generic “eclipse family”

The eclipse state divides the year into three clean groups:

  • Shadow arrives at harvest: Virgo and Pisces begin as ordinary New Moons and culminate as lunar eclipses.
  • Shadow releases before harvest: Libra and Aries begin as solar eclipses and reach ordinary Full Moons.
  • Ordinary seed and harvest: the other nine families have no eclipse at either endpoint.

This gives eclipse narration a direction. Virgo and Pisces ask how an ordinary process enters shadow as it becomes fully visible. Libra and Aries ask what an eclipsed seed looks like when its harvest is no longer itself eclipsed. The other nine are controls: Moon-family structure exists without an eclipse and must not be narrated as less important merely because there is no shadow.

The First Quarter is not an eclipse in any of the thirteen audited families. Its usefulness is therefore structural rather than sensational.

A three-family spatial corridor: Leo, Virgo and Libra

This is a raw-distribution fact, not a claim that the three charts have one meaning. It shows a stable spatial container spanning three consecutive families while house, root and eclipse state change inside it. Virgo acquires shadow; Libra releases it; Leo remains ordinary. That makes this corridor a useful astronomical control. Some chart-shape continuity is produced by slow orbital arrangement rather than by one event. The astrology belongs in what each family does inside that inherited field, not in pretending the repeating boundary was newly created three times.

Root authority usually changes between seed and harvest

Only two families retain the same terminal structure at seed and Full phase:

  • January Cancer: final Moon at both endpoints; and
  • September Aries: Mars–Moon circuit at both endpoints.

The other eleven change their final dispositor or bounded circuit before the harvest. That is the rule, not the exception. A Moon-family reading that tracks only the repeated sign can therefore miss the more important development: the same family question is being answered by a different authority structure.

The First Quarter often acts as a temporary simplifier. Seven of the thirteen quarter charts have at least one final dispositor; the Virgo quarter is a sole Mercury final, the Libra quarter a sole Venus final, the Scorpio quarter a sole Sun final, and the Capricorn quarter a sole Mars final. That temporary clarity may survive into the Full phase, be divided among multiple finals, or collapse into a new loop.

The harvest itself has a final dispositor in nine of thirteen families, compared with five of thirteen seeds. Full phase therefore tends more often to expose a named terminal authority—but four harvests remain irreducibly circular. This is an observed 2026 pattern, not a universal law.

Quarter lights can assemble a later non-light mechanism

In four families, a First-Quarter light aspects a later Full-phase non-light planet within one degree:

  • Scorpio;
  • Aquarius;
  • Pisces; and
  • Aries.

Pisces is the clearest multi-contact example: the quarter lights already configure both the later Mercury and Uranus, assembling the eclipse's mutable release before the Full phase. Aquarius carries two such contacts; Aries carries one. Scorpio's quarter Sun separately configures later Saturn and Venus within 0.37°, while quarter Venus squares Saturn within 0.044° and Full Venus sextiles Saturn within 0.063°. Its completed companion therefore shows the quarter preparing a later agreement mechanism rather than merely repeating a light degree. These are cross-chart relays, not additional transits and not separate votes.

Virgo shows another form of quarter bridge. Its lights hold the later axis within 0.063°, while quarter Neptune conjoins later Saturn within 0.119°. The quarter can therefore carry the harvest mechanism even when its lights do not touch a later non-light body inside the one-degree filter.

Declination is not an optional decoration

Twelve of the thirteen Full phases contain at least one parallel or contra-parallel within 0.25°. Taurus on October 26 is the only exception.

The tightest examples include:

  • Libra Saturn–Neptune contra-parallel at 0.005°;
  • Leo Mars–Uranus contra-parallel at 0.009°;
  • Aquarius Venus–Saturn parallel at 0.022°;
  • Capricorn Sun–Pluto contra-parallel at 0.047°;
  • December Cancer Venus–Jupiter contra-parallel at 0.050°;
  • Virgo Jupiter–Pluto contra-parallel at 0.055°; and
  • Pisces Mars–Pluto contra-parallel at 0.067°.

This does not mean every tight declination deserves narration. It means the coordinate system is structurally active often enough that omitting it from the normal audit can erase a major dialogue. The individual reading must still decide whether the pair belongs to the chart's root, ruler, era field or actual story braid.
