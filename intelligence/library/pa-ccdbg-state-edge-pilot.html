Research summary

The public payment joins to a real CCDBG award, and Pennsylvania documents a detailed PELICAN-to-SAP/provider accounting trail. Public evidence still does not join the federal payment to one State receipt, SAP journal, provider payment, correction or ACF-696 line. The gap is a cross-system identity and reconciliation problem, not proof that the downstream records do not exist.
