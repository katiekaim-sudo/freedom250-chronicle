Freedom 250 · Digital money · Licenses

Which license does each job in a tokenized market need, and which firms hold one?

Each of the 26 jobs in a tokenized market has at least one legal route. The library maps 52 routes, each with its legal basis, the conditions and its limit, and names 57 firms or public bodies documented on them. A license covers the exact company that holds it and the activity it names; a technology vendor or parent company does not inherit it.
