## 2024

- **2024-11-17 — Chase replaces its deposit-account arbitration section** (Chase · Occurred · Arbitration & dispute rules). ## 2020

- **2020-11 — The Cross River origination chain enters the public record** (Cross River · Occurred · Who really owns the debt). The program agreements filed as SEC exhibits set out the shape that later becomes standard. Cross River is the named lender. Affirm purchases most of the loans, becomes the owner of the associated customer information, and acts as the contractually designated servicer. Upstart processes applications under bank-controlled guidelines and maintains the register of who owns the note. The borrower deals with a brand that is neither the lender nor, ultimately, the owner. ## 2018

- **2018-11 — Comenity's notice-and-cure machinery already in place** (Bread / Comenity · Occurred · Arbitration & dispute rules). The November 2018 forms already required at least thirty days' pre-suit notice and a cure period, an individually retained lawyer, and a personally signed arbitration rejection. They also offered a $5,100 special payment to a claimant who beat the bank's rejected pre-demand offer — an incentive the current form no longer contains. The baseline matters: the hardening did not begin in 2025. It deepened.
