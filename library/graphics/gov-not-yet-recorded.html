What's still uncertain

  • The current movement vocabulary cleanly covers authority through operation, delivery, effect, source custody and return gates, but it does not cleanly type court adjudication, congressional procedure, oversight findings or internal organization changes.
  • Do not force-fit those object classes into an existing stage. Add typed stages only after method review, or explicitly keep those classes outside the movement lane.
  • 'Accepted' means the research kept the change for write-up. It is not yet a recorded change with a stage on the ladder.
  • A date is the date the official record shows. Effective dates after the research cutoff are still future or conditional.
