Mercury–Saturn Cycle Review

Enduring question: What information becomes rule, restraint, infrastructure, or durable record?
Disposition: moderate and consistent focal differentiation; useful for record-versus-structure questions.

Family record

  • 2025 Pisces family: candidate +13.098 days.
  • 2026 Aries family: candidate +342.374 days, 21.696 days before the successor.
  • 2027: first-after bracket, no persona gate.

What the charts add

In 2025, Mercury begins debilitated with Saturn in Pisces/1st. The candidate moves Mercury into Aries/12th while Saturn remains Pisces but moves to the 11th. Information becomes hidden or preparatory while institutional structure sits in the collective field.

In 2026, both begin in Aries/12th, with Saturn fallen. The candidate separates debilitated Mercury into Pisces/7th and Saturn into Aries/8th. The record/counterparty interface and the shared-liability or custody structure are no longer the same job.

Neither family produces a spectacular focal angle. Their value is the consistent separation of the record from the institution carrying or limiting it.

Narrowing use

For Crypto, ask whether documentation becomes enforceable custody, infrastructure, or rule. For AI, ask whether the technical claim becomes a durable standard or operating procedure. For borders or censorship, distinguish a hard record/control from mere rhetoric.
