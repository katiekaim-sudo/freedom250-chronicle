Freedom 250 · Banks and the dollar · The Fed

Which Reserve Bank runs which national payment and Treasury service?

The Fed's national services are spread across the twelve Reserve Banks, each a separate legal corporation. Boston runs FedNow; New York runs Fedwire Funds, Fedwire Securities and the National Settlement Service and carries out the Fed's open-market operations; Atlanta runs check and FedACH; Chicago runs FedLine access; Cleveland builds Treasury's Pay.gov; Minneapolis supports TreasuryDirect; Richmond hosts the System's national IT; Kansas City decides account requests.
