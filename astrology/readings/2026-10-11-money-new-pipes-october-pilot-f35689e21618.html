Factual spine, frozen at October 5

The institutional path

The CFTC can frame the market-regulatory lane. A later public text must say which transactions and assets qualify, whether the venue is an elective DCM subtype or something else, what CAM designation and rulebook require, and which actor holds the customer. Clearing, financing, custody/keys, customer-property priority, cash settlement, 24/7 liquidity, records, failure, correction and appeal are open design questions, not terms established by the announcement. The legal test is especially sharp where an integrated platform might list, execute, finance, hold and settle a transaction while a customer assumes that federal registration settles the loss question. It does not.

A simple rival explanation remains live: this is a bounded adaptation of existing DCM/DCO architecture for retail leverage, with ordinary spot, tokenized securities and stablecoin payment systems on their own tracks. No common operating refounding follows from the shared word crypto.
