Stablecoin Law — Charter × Reserve × Rail

A comparative card plot for the legal objects now forming behind familiar payment surfaces

The terminal transition and the stablecoin-law transition rhyme because both separate what the customer presents from who owes the money, what backs it, who routes it, and what finally settles. The laws widen the adapter layer; they do not make every NFC terminal a native crypto terminal.

The common adapter stack

Each law assigns a different constitution to the same six jobs. The terminal touches only one edge.

Active law European Union EUR / referenced fiat active

MiCA turns stablecoins into licensed e-money or asset-reference objects

Europe regulates by legal token class. It is the clearest taxonomy-first model.

IssuerAn e-money-token issuer must be a credit institution or electronic-money institution. Asset-referenced tokens use a separate authorized issuer regime.
ReserveReserve, liquidity, segregation, custody and permanent redemption duties vary by class. Holders of e-money tokens have a par-redemption claim; interest is prohibited.
Terminal joinStrong adapter foundation; no claim that EMV contactless kernels natively understand MiCA tokens.
open legal object + rail reading
Legal clock

Stablecoin titles apply from 30 Jun 2024; remaining MiCA regime from 30 Dec 2024.

Distribution

Crypto-asset service providers operate inside a harmonized EU authorization and conduct perimeter.

Settlement boundary

The token may move on-chain, but issuer redemption and bank/central-bank money remain separate finality clocks.

Currency Watch join

The euro architecture prioritizes monetary singleness and regulated private claims rather than an unbounded token market.

Counterweight

MiCA authorization is not an ECB guarantee, deposit insurance, or universal merchant acceptance.

Active law United Arab Emirates AED / registered foreign payment tokens active

The UAE writes the merchant endpoint into the token law

UAE proves that the decisive stablecoin acceptance layer is legal routing and merchant permission, not NFC capability.

IssuerA UAE entity may be licensed to issue a Dirham Payment Token; a bank uses a subsidiary or affiliate. Foreign-token issuance, conversion, custody and transfer have distinct permissions.
ReserveThe default is 100% same-currency cash in a segregated escrow account at an unrelated UAE bank. A qualifying bank-owned issuer may use at least 50% cash with the balance in short UAE government or CBUAE instruments, with added capital and controls.
Terminal joinThis is the sharpest terminal comparator: an ordinary UAE merchant may accept only a licensed Dirham Payment Token, while registered foreign tokens are confined to virtual-asset purchases.
open legal object + rail reading
Legal clock

The CBUAE Payment Token Services Regulation has been in force since 31 Aug 2024.

Distribution

Issuance, custody/transfer and conversion are separate regulated services.

Settlement boundary

The wallet transfer is the customer leg; reserve cash and redemption remain separate commercial-bank or directed central-bank custody legs.

Currency Watch join

The law protects the dirham payment perimeter by controlling the legal token at the merchant endpoint—not by controlling the radio hardware.

Counterweight

Generic contactless support does not satisfy token licensing, conversion, custody, merchant or redemption rules.

Enacted · clock running United States USD enacted · not generally operative

GENIUS writes a private-dollar issuer constitution

GENIUS is the strongest sovereign-debt-reserve flywheel in the set—and the cleanest proof that stablecoin law is also banking architecture.

IssuerPermitted issuers are insured-depository subsidiaries, federal qualified issuers, or state qualified issuers under the statutory split and threshold.
ReserveOne-to-one reserves in cash, central-bank balances, insured deposits, Treasury bills of 93 days or less, qualifying repo, government money funds and related liquid government assets. Monthly reporting; no holder interest; no holder FDIC insurance.
Terminal joinGENIUS supplies the lawful issuer and reserve object that a card, wallet or acquirer can route. It does not itself update a terminal.
open legal object + rail reading
Legal clock

Public Law 119-27 was signed 18 Jul 2025. Final implementing rules were due 18 Jul 2026; none was located on official final-rule pages through this 28 Jul cutoff. The outside general effective date remains 18 Jan 2027 unless final rules start the earlier 120-day clock.

Distribution

Wallets, card networks, processors and distributors can remain separate from the legal issuer. A 2028 service-provider restriction creates a later market-access clock.

Settlement boundary

On-chain transfer, card-network obligation, mint/redemption, bank deposit movement and central-bank finality remain distinct.

Currency Watch join

The reserve schedule creates a direct short-Treasury demand channel while preserving the dollar unit and private distribution.

Counterweight

Enacted is not effective. A proposal, charter application or OCC conditional approval is not a live GENIUS issuer authorization.

Active law Hong Kong HKD / referenced fiat active

Hong Kong makes fiat-referenced issuance an HKMA-licensed activity

Hong Kong is a regulator-gate model: a tight issuer doorway around an international financial center.

IssuerA license is required for in-scope fiat-referenced stablecoin issuance in Hong Kong and for specified Hong Kong-linked issuance or offering activity.
ReserveHKMA supervision centers reserve-asset management, redemption, risk management, governance, AML/CFT and disclosure.
Terminal joinA licensed issue can become a funding or settlement asset behind an existing wallet or card surface; the legal bridge still needs an authorized distributor and processor.
open legal object + rail reading
Legal clock

The Stablecoins Ordinance and HKMA supervisory framework took effect 1 Aug 2025.

Distribution

Only specified regulated institutions may offer in-scope stablecoins to retail investors; the issuer license and distribution permission remain separate.

Settlement boundary

The ordinance governs the issuer and token promise, not the merchant acquirer or the final bank-money leg.

Currency Watch join

Hong Kong protects the monetary perimeter through licensing and local supervisory control rather than by treating every token as bank money.

Counterweight

A sandbox participant, application, or public announcement is not a license.

Enacted · clock running United Kingdom GBP / qualifying referenced fiat law made · regime scheduled

The UK creates a qualifying-stablecoin activity but stages payments reform

Britain is the best example of an enacted legal doorway whose operational room is still under construction.

IssuerIssuing a UK qualifying stablecoin becomes a regulated activity. Systemic payment stablecoins add Bank of England oversight to FCA supervision.
ReserveQualifying stablecoins reference fiat and hold backing assets; detailed FCA rules are expected to provide full backing and redemption rights.
Terminal joinPromising adapter law, but the payment-services bridge is still being completed.
open legal object + rail reading
Legal clock

The 2026 cryptoassets regulations create regulated issuing and safeguarding activities; the broader regime is expected to begin in late 2027, with FCA rules and application steps still required.

Distribution

Safeguarding and other crypto activities have separate permissions. The government is still rationalizing dealing and payment-service perimeters.

Settlement boundary

The UK is explicitly designing stablecoins as both cryptoassets and potential payment instruments, with separate systemic oversight.

Currency Watch join

The UK is sequencing perimeter first, payment assimilation second, tax treatment third.

Counterweight

Do not label the late-2027 regime live; current policy notes explicitly describe additional rules and payments reform.

1 Jun 2026
Active law Japan JPY / qualifying referenced fiat active

Japan opens a bounded sovereign-debt reserve lane

Japan’s debt-funding rhyme with GENIUS is real but narrower: only the trust lane gets bounded short-sovereign reserve flexibility.

IssuerBanks, registered funds-transfer providers, and trust companies or trust banks issue different legal claims. A lighter non-custodial intermediary may broker for a full provider.
ReserveCovered trust stablecoins may place up to 50% in qualifying government bonds of no more than three months or callable term deposits without principal loss; at least 50% remains in demand deposits. Daily fair-value and shortfall-cure rules apply.
Terminal joinJapan is building the issuer, intermediary and redemption adapter. A PSP could preserve the tap while changing funding or payout behind it.
open legal object + rail reading
Legal clock

The 2022 electronic-payment-instrument regime has applied since 1 Jun 2023. Act No. 66 of 2025 and its final implementing package took principal effect 1 Jun 2026.

Distribution

Full EPI providers handle sale, exchange, intermediation or custody. The lighter intermediary cannot hold customer assets.

Settlement boundary

Trust redemption, deposit-token settlement, bank reserves and a public-chain transfer are different objects and clocks.

Currency Watch join

JPY Currency Watch: underlying order B; exchange order B; current-payment usability A; systemic service A. This is orderly absorption, not evidence of a broken payment system.

Counterweight

JPYSC’s controlled-venue production is not open-wallet merchant circulation. Tokenized bank deposits are not trust stablecoins.

Adjacent rail law Japan Crypto market perimeter enacted · not yet operative

Japan moves crypto markets—not stablecoins—toward securities-style law

The split itself is the signal. Japan is refusing to collapse ‘crypto,’ ‘stablecoin,’ and ‘bank deposit’ into one object.

IssuerThis is not a new stablecoin issuer charter. Stablecoin electronic payment instruments stay under the Payment Services Act.
ReserveNo stablecoin reserve change on this card.
Terminal joinThe law helps institutions classify crypto investment activity but does not make crypto a contactless tender.
open legal object + rail reading
Legal clock

The Diet enacted the FIEA/PSA reform 15 Jul 2026; principal commencement awaits a Cabinet-set date within the statutory window.

Distribution

Trading, offerings, lending, management, advice, disclosure, intermediary duties and unfair-trading controls move into a Financial Instruments and Exchange Act perimeter.

Settlement boundary

Market regulation and payment-money settlement remain deliberately separated.

Currency Watch join

Japan is building two doors: a payments-law door for redeemable money claims and a market-law door for investable cryptoassets.

Counterweight

Do not say Japan made every cryptoasset a security, legal tender, or bank deposit.

Japan plans to close the paper-check exchange

Japan is modernizing both ends at once: retiring paper claims while legalizing new tokenized claims.

IssuerBanks remain the obligors; the payment instruction changes form.
ReserveNo stablecoin reserve rule.
Terminal joinIt is the cleanest historical echo in the set: the paper instrument disappears while the bank relationship survives.
open legal object + rail reading
Legal clock

The plan targets zero paper bills and checks through the electronic clearinghouse by FY2026 end and abolition of exchange from FY2027 start.

Distribution

Electronic recorded monetary claims and internet transfers replace paper presentment.

Settlement boundary

This is a direct cash/check-to-electronic-rail transition, separate from stablecoin law.

Currency Watch join

A mature currency system can retire an old instrument without changing the unit of account.

Counterweight

This is an official transition plan, not a stablecoin statute and not proof every check disappears on one day.
