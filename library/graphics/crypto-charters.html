What's still uncertain

  • A conditional approval is not a charter: the firm must meet the conditions before it can open.
  • A charter and a Fed account are separate decisions; see the Fed accounts page.
  • State charters (such as Wyoming's crypto banks) are included where the firm is also in the federal line.
