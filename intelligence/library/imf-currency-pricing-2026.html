Research summary

The IMF does not ordinarily set the dealer quote for a currency. Its power sits around the quote: it recognizes the member's regime, classifies the actual system, models the external position, polices specified official restrictions and multiple exchange rates, and, when Fund money is involved, can make a new price-formation set-up a condition of access. Program cases cover Ethiopia, Egypt, Pakistan and Argentina; 22 bounded rulings.
