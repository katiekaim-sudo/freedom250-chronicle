## Controlling rulings

1. **Primary sources only.** Statute, code, Federal Register, slip opinion, special message, organic act, agency instrument. A briefing, SAP, or fact sheet may name an object; it does not replace the object. News is not a source.
2. **Four clocks never collapse.** Statutory existence · personnel · function transfer · appropriations (obligation / outlay). A press release is not a repeal. An interagency agreement is not a charter amendment. A House-passed bill is not law.
3. **Control, not ownership.** Consolidation follows the leash (who appoints, who removes, who apportions, who may sue). SFFAS 47 already draws that line on the entity chart.
4. **The legal person stays separate** from the operating actor, the data holder, the payment rail, and the recipient. Do not write "the government" when the text names OMB, USAID, SSA, or a district court.
5. **A charter is binding.** What the creature is, what it agreed to, how it is supposed to operate. Charter-library expansion is a later pass; these maps cite the organic hook and do not ingest new verbatim acts unless a package cannot stand without a short extract.

## How these notes are built

Each map has: short answer · entity/control table · legal stack with clocks · claim audit · Entity Theory read · open clocks · sources with retrieval dates.

**Claim-audit words.** `established` means the named official object says it. `not established` means it was not found in a primary object at cutoff. `different object` means the claim names one thing and the document is another.

Do not promote a White House briefing into a holding. Do not promote a special message into an enacted rescission. Do not promote an EO into a repealed statute.
