# 2025-04-12 Libra Full Moon — A Reading

> **Retrospective calibration.** Written 2026-09-25 from the computed chart alone, after the window closed. It carries no forecast credit; the record and the comparison live in separate files (DR-063).

**The Full Moon stretches across the axis of service and undoing. The exalted executive works through the civil and armed services while the political class is lit up in the house of hidden enemies. The chart's value-planet turns forward within the hour.**

The Sun at 23°20′ Aries in the 6th opposes the Moon at 23°20′ Libra in the 12th. The executive is exalted and at work in the house of the civil service, the armed forces, labor and public health. The political class, Greer's Moon, is illuminated in the 12th: prisons, intelligence, hidden opponents, and the things that undo a government from within. Aries against Libra is initiative against balance, command against consensus. What was begun at the New Moon becomes visible here as a question about who serves and who is being quietly undone.

Scorpio rises, so Mars rules, and Mars is in fall at 27°56′ Cancer in the 9th, trining Mercury within 0.21°. The chart's force is expressed through law, doctrine and foreign relations, and it speaks fluently even from a weak sign. The root is the Mercury–Jupiter mutual reception that governed the winter. Mercury at 28°09′ Pisces in the 5th is in detriment and fall; Jupiter at 17°58′ Gemini in the 8th is in detriment. Markets and debt, message and credit, keep handing authority to one another. Mercury has only just turned direct after its retrograde through the Aries threshold, and it is still inside its shadow.

Venus is the chart's most exact event. She stations direct at 24°37′ Pisces less than an hour after the Full Moon, exalted in the 5th, conjunct Saturn within 1.27° and sextile Uranus in the 7th within 0.72°. Venus has spent six weeks retrograde. Here she turns forward: prices, alliances and agreements that were under review begin to move again, carrying Saturn's weight and Uranus's surprise. Saturn and Uranus themselves are only a week past their exact sextile. The structural order and the disruptor are cooperating, uneasily, underneath the Full Moon.

Neptune has just entered Aries and sits at 0°30′ in the 6th alongside the Sun. The new dream is entering the house of public service. Greer finds no hard hit on the ingress. The U.S. chart does not stay quiet. Neptune squares U.S. Venus within 0.16°, Mars opposes U.S. Pluto within 0.33° and U.S. Moon within 0.41°, Mercury opposes U.S. MC within 0.77°, and the Full Moon's MC falls on the U.S. North Node. Force presses on the nation's natal Moon–Pluto line once more. The constructive pole is a reorganized service ethic in which the hidden house is opened to light. The shadow is a public mood that learns more from what is being undone than from what is being built.
