Research summary

The control architecture now has two linked halves: identity and eligibility before Treasury releases a payment, and a still-fragmented transaction record after the dollar reaches a State, NGO, subrecipient or vendor. Public feeds are operating in bounded form; reconciliation, downstream traceability, shared-ledger use and public-chain adoption remain separate proof states.
