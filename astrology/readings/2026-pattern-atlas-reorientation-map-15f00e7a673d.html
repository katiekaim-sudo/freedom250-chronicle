Reorientation map

We have crossed an important threshold. The problem is no longer that the 2026 mundane corpus lacks context. We now have enough context that continuing to add techniques one by one risks hiding the whole chart beneath a pile of individually correct findings.

The project therefore turns from technique-by-technique discovery toward question-driven whole-chart synthesis.

The governing goal is:

> Consolidate the completed 2026 mundane-astrology waves into a governed > pattern map and whole-chart analytical route, then use that structure to > reread the priority stories—especially the Aries Full Moon/Mars–Pluto > sequence and the Barbault Basket/AI arc—adding new calculations only when a > specific interpretive question requires them.

This Atlas is the map for that turn. The generated companion is 99 - Templates/pattern_atlas_2026.json.

What this Atlas is—and is not

Three objects must remain distinct:

Object Job
mundane_method_atlas.json Owns chart types, owner families, interfaces, gates and method boundaries.
pattern_atlas_2026.json Indexes the completed cross-chart findings, coverage, priority questions and missing-evidence queue.
whole_chart_packet_index.json Routes all thirty registered packets to their exact chart, reading, governor, lineage, motion and applicable patterns without copying chart payloads.
Whole-chart packet Keeps one exact chart, snapshot, house frame, root, relationships and reading context intact under their existing owners.

The Pattern Atlas does not become a composite chart, a second semantic owner or a vote counter. It tells us which lenses may matter to a question and routes us back to the intact chart that must still be read as a whole.

Political records remain independent factual objects. Astrology meets them only in an explicit comparison layer. Forecasts remain in the Forecast Ledger.

The whole-chart packet becomes the unit of analysis

Every serious reread should begin from one intact packet containing or routing to:

  • chart, snapshot, exact clock, locality and one house frame;
  • governing ingress and lunation or eclipse lineage;
  • positions, angles and the whole Aspect web;
  • dispositor routes, final roots or bounded loops;
  • raw distribution and declination context;
  • applicable pattern records from this Atlas; and
  • the locked reading plus any clearly labeled companion rereads.

The Atlas stores applicability and routes. It must not copy the positions, houses or judgment into a flattened master row. That is how we keep context without making the computer pretend that the whole chart is merely the sum of its tags.

That route now exists in 99 - Templates/whole_chart_packet_index.json: five ingress governors, twelve New Moons or solar eclipses, and thirteen Full Moons or lunar eclipses. The Astrology Hub projects those thirty entries at the front door; choosing one opens its exact authored reading, while the index preserves the deeper routes for analysis.

The new working rhythm

For each priority question:

  1. open the intact chart packet;
  2. state the governing period and the exact question;
  3. load only the applicable Atlas records;
  4. separate fact, calculation, interpretation, political comparison and Forecast Ledger state;
  5. account for controls and counterevidence;
  6. return to the whole chart for Judgment; and
  7. add a new calculation only if a named question remains unanswered.

This is the reorientation: fewer free-floating discoveries, more complete stories whose parts remain inspectable.
