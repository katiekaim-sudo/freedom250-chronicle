Research summary

The open committee AI agenda is moving through distributed translators rather than one AI regulator: the SEC IAC, FSOC, CFTC IAC, ETAAC, SSAB and PTAC formulate sector disclosure, risk, records, recourse and payment proposals, while the CAIO and CIO Councils, FedRAMP Board and FSCAC develop reusable governance, architecture and cloud-assurance procedures. Most consequential items remain between gates: draft, working-group synthesis, adopted recommendation, parent-agency disposition, implementation, operation and effect are separate. The broader national framework is retained as context, and the underlying eleven-entity census remains frozen at August 23.
