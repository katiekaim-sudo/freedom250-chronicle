## Source-separated political maturation: the enforcement record becomes a confidential remedy process

The official political object was selected independently from the astrology.
On September 3, 2024—the UTC date of the seed—the SEC entered six final
recordkeeping orders against credit rating agencies. The firms admitted the
orders' facts and violations, accepted censures and cease-and-desist relief,
and incurred penalties totaling more than $49 million.

The failure concerned communications used to initiate, determine, maintain,
monitor, change or withdraw credit ratings. In the Fitch order, the SEC found
that missing off-channel communications likely impaired examinations and
investigations. This is therefore a factual record about whether the regulator
could reconstruct how an institution reached consequential judgments.

Four firms were required to retain independent compliance consultants. The
orders created review, report, implementation, one-year evaluation,
internal-audit, discipline-notice and certification steps. But the main
consultant reports and related communications were expressly intended to
remain nonpublic. SEC staff could also extend procedural dates.

That state sequence is:

`final order and admissions → mandatory remediation process → nonpublic
consultant findings → later evaluation and certification`

By the March 3, 2026 Full phase, the public record establishes the first two
rungs. It does not establish the contents of the consultant findings or prove
successful completion. The factual maturation therefore fits the family only
if the limit is preserved:

> illuminate the existence of a remedy process while the decisive remediation

That is a stronger comparison than a headline coincidence because it also
