# 2026-10-10 Libra New Moon — A Reading

This New Moon begins a cycle of agreement, coalition, and public positioning—but it begins after the terms have already become difficult. The Libra lights gather in the 11th house of assemblies, alliances, and collective aims. They want a workable form of relationship. The chart does not let that desire remain abstract: Venus, ruler of the lunation, is retrograde in Scorpio in the 12th and applying to a nearly exact square with Mars in Leo in the 9th. What a group says it shares has to survive a hidden argument about value, authority, belief, and who gets to set the terms.

Venus and Mars can describe creative tension: attraction strong enough to force an honest revision, or competing interests that discover they need a common form. Retrograde Venus makes revision essential. Old prices, loyalties, resentments, and obligations return to the room. In the harder pole, the public alliance is built over a private contest, and courtesy becomes a way to postpone disclosure. The seed remains viable, but only if the parties account for what has been withheld.

The Sun and Moon sextile Jupiter in Leo, giving the undertaking confidence, visibility, and a sense that something larger can be made. Mars also has constructive routes to Uranus and Saturn: experimentation can acquire form, and force can be disciplined into a durable procedure. Yet Mars remains opposed to Pluto, while Venus remains square Pluto. The new beginning sits inside a fixed pressure pattern. No one gets everything; an arrangement that pretends otherwise is likely to store the conflict rather than resolve it.

The chart’s root runs Sun → Venus → Mars and back toward the lights. Collective purpose, value, and assertion are inseparable. This can be excellent for turning an ideal of fairness into an actual decision. It can also produce an agreement whose real function is to decide who must yield. The useful question is not “Did everyone use the language of balance?” It is “What changed hands, what was conceded, and can the arrangement still be named honestly once those facts are visible?”

Mercury in Scorpio keeps the record investigative and exacting. The New Moon falls within range of the governing Libra ingress Mercury in the 7th, linking the seed directly to the season’s contracts, negotiations, public opponents, and named counterparts. The words chosen now become part of the architecture. Ambiguity may create room for movement; it may also create the next dispute.

## Timing

The cycle opens October 10 and reaches its first full disclosure at the October 26 Taurus Full Moon. Treat the interval as negotiation under revision: the seed is planted now, while its actual power structure becomes visible at the Full Moon.
