Research summary

Crypto infrastructure is a system of systems, not one vertically owned machine. Nineteen governed profiles, three process maps and nine asset traces connect Ripple/XRPL, Securitize, major public and institutional networks, DTC tokenization, Circle/USDC/Arc, Coinbase/Base/Tokenize, Wormhole and tokenized bank-money rails. Tokenization, Stablecoin and FX process maps show which exact ecosystem controls, operates, integrates or depends on each lifecycle stage without collapsing technical finality, the authoritative legal record, cash settlement, correction or public backstop. Ethereum, Hedera, Solana, Avalanche, Stellar, Algorand and XDC are explicitly mapped as native-USDC execution and transport rails while Circle retains issuer, reserve and redemption authority. Ripple's underlying/collateral cell is limited to Fubon Bank's historical property-lien pilot as an external dependency: Licuido is collateral-market buildout, while Evernorth and Pathfinder are separate XRP treasury and custody actors whose pending combination and proposed uncollateralized XLS-66 participation do not establish tokenized-asset control. Hedera's FX appearance is limited to one collateral pilot; Canton separately records one Treasury-for-USDCx event with the exact issuer and redemption chain unresolved.
