Two orders rename AI and set up America.gov as a front door

On September 29, 2026 the President signed two orders: one tells agencies to call AI 'Super Intelligence' in their own documents without changing what it means, and one makes America.gov a single front door to federal services while agencies keep their records and decisions.

What we found

  • The Super Intelligence order tells the executive branch to use 'Super Intelligence' or 'SI' instead of 'AI' in non-statutory documents. It borrows the existing legal definition of AI (15 U.S.C. 9401(3)) and sets no test for superhuman ability. Statutes, past regulations, contracts and grants do not change.
  • The America.gov order has GSA, the National Design Studio and OMB run the site, link it to Login.gov, and require agencies to expose existing services used by more than 100,000 people a year. IRS tax filing, the Department of War and intelligence services are excluded.
  • The originating agency keeps custody of records and the final decision. America.gov's own terms say it does not submit, decide or approve filings.
  • Today the site works as a question-and-answer search tool. The White House fact sheet says passport renewal and Medicare enrollment come 'later this year', while the site's Coming Soon page says early 2027.
  • At cutoff neither order had an Executive Order number or Federal Register citation. The study's wider census of AI plans across eleven federal entities is frozen at August 23 and was not updated.

Why it matters

This is the government changing its own vocabulary and building a shared front door on top of existing agencies, through orders and OMB memos rather than new law. The study is clear that the front door is direction for now, not working cross-agency transactions.

What to watch

  • ~2026-11-28 · The legislative proposal on a durable Super Intelligence definition, due within 60 days. It would still be only a proposal.
  • ~2026-12-28 · OMB's America.gov implementation memo, due within 90 days.
  • early 2027 · Whether passport renewal and Medicare enrollment actually work through America.gov.

Research summary

The September 29 actions form a two-order stack. The first directs the executive branch to use Super Intelligence or SI in non-statutory documents, but imports the existing statutory AI definition and establishes no superhuman-capability threshold. The second establishes America.gov as a GSA-led front door integrating Login.gov and existing agency interfaces while preserving originating-agency records, authority and adjudication. White House texts are confirmed; Federal Register numbers, the 60-day legislative proposal, the 90-day OMB memorandum, covered-service integrations, transactions and effects remain distinct open receipts. The underlying eleven-entity census remains frozen at August 23.
