One story, three connected movements

Living thesis

Our living thesis asks how economic institutions remain legitimate as technology, private networks and public restructuring change who can actually make things happen. The recurring question is whether practical control, legal responsibility, authoritative records and the ability to correct a result still line up. Money and payments, physical buildout, and public authority are three connected parts of that story. Read the research as background, worked cases, historical context and tests of the thesis—including evidence of successful adaptation and competing explanations. The three families below are connected movements within that same story: money and routes, physical buildout, and public authority. A study can belong to more than one movement because the thesis follows relationships across the system.
