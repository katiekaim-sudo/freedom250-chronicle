Research summary

Payment middleware can already hide the selected settlement rail from the requesting agent or end user, and XRPL can use XRP as a bridge between issued assets. The September 1 transfer-agent extension shows how Ripple's custody, prime, stablecoin, XRPL and transfer-agency-technology relationships could fit a regulated stack, but Ripple is not thereby the recordkeeping transfer agent and Securitize/Wormhole relationships do not prove control or XRP use. XRP's distinct inflection still requires route share, depth, financed inventory, holding-duration, haircut and hedge evidence; Ripple, XRPL, RLUSD and XRP remain four separate success ledgers.
