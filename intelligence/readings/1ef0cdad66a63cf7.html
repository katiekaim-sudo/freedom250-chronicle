Controlling ruling

Transportation Policy. The White House posted the controlling memorandum at
16:54:58 EDT on August 20, after the prior research cutoff. It revokes the 2013
policy, assigns a new APST-coordinated implementation overlay and opens 90-,
120-, 180- and 240-day deliverable clocks. It is a policy and coordination
instrument, not a launch, appropriation, site, contract, construction project,
export-control result or proof of the stated 2030 capacity goal.

closed three proposal clocks carried from August 20; DOL/OFCCP published three

not support a claim that all of those layers are already operating or that the
