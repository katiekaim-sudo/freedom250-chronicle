Neptune–Pluto

The planets

Neptune — The Ocean. Dissolution, ideals, transcendence. MUNDANE: collective movements, mass sentiment, propaganda, speculative bubbles; the force that dissolves existing national structures and replaces them with a new collective image. Almost always malefic in mundane charts — Neptune favors unity, but the political and economic spheres punish unity-seeking with mob behavior and lowest-common-denominator politics. The ONLY exception: when Neptune is unafflicted and in favorable aspects, he benefits the masses, favors democratic causes, and indicates peace. In any other condition: chaos, scandal, deception, the collapse of the government's legitimacy.

Pluto — God of the Underworld. Deep purgation, resurrection, transformation. MUNDANE: collective power wielded from below or behind the scenes; the compulsion to purge the body politic of corruption; emerging nations and mass movements that overthrow existing power structures. (Greer caution: "treat Pluto with healthy skepticism — more than any other planet, Pluto invites projection." Keep him in the chart but don't overweight his effects relative to empirically stronger cycles.)

The combination

The deepest, slowest cycle in mundane astrology — half a millennium per revolution. This is not the rise and fall of governments or even of national orders; this is the rise and fall of entire civilizations and the collective ideals that animate them. When the dissolving tide of mass consciousness (Neptune) meets the civilizational purge-force (Pluto), the foundational myths of an age are born or die. The 1891-92 conjunction in Gemini seeded the current movement toward global culture, mass communication, and the information age — the very infrastructure through which nations now wage their wars, conduct their economies, and govern their peoples.

Greer does not give a specific Neptune-Pluto entry (Pluto skepticism applies doubly at this timescale). The civilizational-scale framing from Barbault and Baigent remains the best lens: this cycle operates beneath and behind all the shorter cycles, shaping the assumptions that governments, corporations, and the laboring classes take for granted. The helpful expression means the deep civilizational ideals and the transformative mass forces cooperate; the hostile expression means entire paradigms of governance and economic life collapse and must be rebuilt from scratch over centuries.

Current (long sextile, ~1941-2035): This sextile has been in effect since the early 1940s and persists until roughly 2035. The ENTIRE modern era — WWII and the creation of the post-war international order, the Cold War contest between the superpowers, the digital revolution that restructured every nation's economy and military — unfolds within this single harmonious phase. It is the background hum beneath all the shorter cycles. Every government, every corporation, every popular movement in the Freedom 250 chronicle operates within this window of opportunity: the chance for the nations to put deep civilizational ideals into constructive, practical expression before the sextile fades.
