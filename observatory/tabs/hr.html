 The lunar engine — the harvest and the seed

 The hearings' real clock isn't the slow outer planets — it's the Moon . Each hearing is timed by a pair of lunations: a New Moon that plants and a Full Moon that reveals . And from the first cut onward, those two lunations fall — every single time — into the chronicle's two overlapping Moon Families .

 Great Harvest · 8–16° · the bloom
 THE family of this chronicle — seeded mid-2024 to early 2025 (the gold-reset campaign, the whole monetary-reset story). Its bloom runs Sept 2025 → June 2026, which is the hearing window. Every cutting hearing carries a Great Harvest Full Moon: the long-planted reset coming up for air, all the cards on the table.

 Eclipse Storm · 24–32° · the seed
 The next family — seeding 24–32° New Moons across the same window, to harvest in 2027. Every cutting hearing also carries an Eclipse Storm New Moon: each cut planted now, to be reaped as law in 2027. The hearing isn't the candle. It's the seed of the markup.

 Each hearing's seed (New Moon, bottom) and bloom (Full Moon, top), placed by Moon Family. The first three are the setup act (off-family). From #4, the first cut , the machine locks in: an Eclipse Storm seed below, a Great Harvest bloom above, every time. Two eclipses fall on the hinges — the first cut (#4) is bracketed by an eclipse pair; the keystone (#7) is seeded by the Feb-17 2026 North-Node solar eclipse — "the nation initiates."

 And the deeper chain — the same-degree lineages
 Each bloom is the Full Moon phase of a New Moon planted ~18 months earlier at the same degree (Pessin’s true gestation chain). Trace them back and the convergence shows: the keystone (#7) and the structure hearing (#9) both bloom seeds planted by XRP/Ripple New Moons — the Oct-2-2024 Libra solar eclipse and the Dec-2024 Capricorn New Moon. The Fed’s castration is harvesting the very crypto-rails its hearings keep pointing at. And each cut plants its own seed that harvests across 2027 (May → Nov) — the markup calendar, sown now.  Underneath, the slow sky (the weather, not the clock) 
 The outer planets set the mood the whole way: Pluto crawls through early Aquarius (money-power draining out of the central house and into the network); Saturn meets Neptune at 0° Aries — first time since 1702 — bracketing the series and dissolving the old structure (that's the castration, written in the sky); Uranus hands Taurus off to Gemini (the currency itself to the new rails). They set the weather. The Moon sets the clock.
