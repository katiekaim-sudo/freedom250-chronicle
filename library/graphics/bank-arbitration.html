Freedom 250 · Banks and the dollar · The fine print

How are big banks limiting group lawsuits in their customer contracts?

Through their customer agreements: across 12 dated records from November 2018 to June 4, 2026, the library documents 8 banks and card issuers (Bank of America, Bread / Comenity, Chase, Citi, Cross River, PNC, Synchrony, Truist) using individual arbitration, waivers of class and group claims, and short mailed opt-out windows, and 3 records add special rules for mass filings. Each card links the contract or notice it comes from.
