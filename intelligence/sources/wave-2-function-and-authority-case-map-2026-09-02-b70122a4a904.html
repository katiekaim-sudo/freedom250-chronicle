Cross-case finding

The cases do not divide cleanly into “old institutions” and “new technology.” They form a spectrum:

  1. technology absorbed inside an identifiable constitution — Fedwire's ISO 20022 migration;
  2. technology operating while a constitution is being written — GENIUS, tokenized securities, shared ledgers, and AI governance;
  3. a proposed constitution attempting to preserve accountable authority — the SEC transfer-agent proposal;
  4. a shared machine signal inside distributed legal judgment — Do Not Pay;
  5. a surviving legal person with a redistributed operating body — Education, USAID, and parts of Treasury/IRS;
  6. an announced replacement body without a completed legal charter — AHA;
  7. centralization with active legal and administrative checks — OPM;
  8. international coordination without supranational operating authority — G20/FSB cross-border payments; and
  9. a mature institution whose different instruments produce different degrees of leverage — IMF surveillance and conditional lending.

The strongest cross-case variable is not public versus private. It is the gap between control and responsibility:

Who can shape the decision or route in practice, who remains legally responsible, which record governs, and whether the affected party can obtain an intelligible correction and remedy?

Where those answers remain joined, technology can strengthen institutional legitimacy. Where they split, the change creates a legitimacy test. Where the split is contested and produces direct adverse effects that ordinary processes cannot resolve, it may mature into a legitimacy crisis.

No case in this Wave 2 map presently supports an S5 legitimacy crisis finding under the proposed method.

That classification does not suspend or weaken the overarching legitimacy- crisis lens. The lens asks whether cases at different states reveal a connected system-level pattern; it does not require each source to use the word legitimacy or each function to have reached crisis independently. Observable authority conflicts, reliance shifts, bypasses, failures of correction, selective burdens, and capability–structure gaps are the evidence from which the interpretation is built.

Comparative state key

  • S0 settled authority
  • S1 governance lag
  • S2 legitimacy test
  • S3 legitimacy contest
  • S4 legitimacy strain
  • S5 legitimacy crisis
  • S6 rupture

The state is provisional and function-specific. A case can carry a primary state plus a narrower observed condition, such as output shortfall or judicial contest.

Case 1 — IMF: advice, clocks, exceptions, and money

Cutoff: 2026-07-30
Owner: international-monetary-transition

Read first

  • Research Packages/International Monetary Transition/06 - Who Gets Adjusted IMF Symmetry Audit 2026/WHO_GETS_ADJUSTED_IMF_SYMMETRY_AUDIT_2026-07-30.md
  • Research Packages/International Monetary Transition/06 - Who Gets Adjusted IMF Symmetry Audit 2026/IMF_SURVEILLANCE_VS_CONDITIONALITY_CONTROL_MAP_2026-07-30.md

Authority chain

member policy authority
  -> IMF staff analysis and negotiation
     -> Executive Board consultation or program review
        -> recommendation, condition, waiver, modification, or purchase
           -> national implementation and household/market incidence

The mature capability is not merely advice. For borrowers, selected policies can become prior actions, quantitative criteria, structural benchmarks, review clocks, waiver requests, target modifications, and financing decisions.

The direct legitimacy evidence is unusually strong for this corpus: the IMF's own evenhandedness framework states that actual and perceived lack of evenhandedness harms credibility and legitimacy. The sampled records also show the real constitutional asymmetry: nonborrowers receive candid advice without an IMF purchase gate, while borrowers can face operationalized adjustment.

Counter-record: the distinction follows the lawful difference between surveillance and use of Fund resources; powerful members are criticized; some social protections carry hard program rank; waivers are disclosed and rule-governed. The sample does not establish motive or geopolitical favoritism.

Next return: a defined 2020–2026 cohort coding every criterion miss, waiver, modification, purchase, social-protection rank, and later result, plus repeated Article IV recommendations and observable implementation in systemic states.

Case 2 — Federal Reserve access: who gets a direct account?

Cutoff: 2026-08-15 18:29 ET
Owner: fed-clearing-treasury

Read first

  • Research Packages/Fed, Clearing and Treasury/PAYMENT_ACCOUNT_PRIORITY_COMMENT_DECISION_RIGHTS_MATRIX_2026-08-15.md
  • Research Packages/Fed, Clearing and Treasury/PAYMENT_ACCOUNT_FULL_COMMENT_CORPUS_CONSTITUTIONAL_SYNTHESIS_2026-08-15.md

Authority chain

Congressional eligibility
  -> Board policy and general supervision
     -> primary-supervisor evidence
        -> District Reserve Bank independent risk judgment
           -> bilateral account terms, permitted services, monitoring, closure

Kraken's disclosed account is a narrow one-year, Fedwire-only limited account without interest, intraday credit, discount-window access, or broader payment services. The national Payment Account remains proposed. Eligibility does not create a right to approval.

This case contains direct institutional contest: Governor disagreement, fifteen priority comments, and congressional oversight ask whether standards are nationally consistent, charter-neutral, transparent, and paired with reasons and redress, while preserving District judgment and the credit frontier.

Counter-record: the dual lock may itself be a legitimacy-preserving design: national standards plus local adjudication, primary-supervisor input plus an independent Reserve Bank judgment, and strict prefunding rather than access to public credit.

Next return: final OP-1878 disposition, Tier 3 policy state, Kraken term and actual use, incidents or escalation, and evidence of reasons, appeal, and cross-Bank consistency.

Case 3 — GENIUS: law arrives before the complete operating regime

Primary cutoff: 2026-08-17
Owner: private-monetary-stack

Read first

  • Research Packages/Fintech and Private Rails/Retail Payment Terminal Transition 2026-07-28/genius-implementation-gap-2026-08-17/GENIUS_ACT_IMPLEMENTATION_GAP_AUDIT_2026-08-17.md
  • Research Packages/Fintech and Private Rails/Retail Payment Terminal Transition 2026-07-28/genius-stablecoin-issuer-readiness/GENIUS_ACT_STABLECOIN_ISSUER_READINESS_2026-07-28.md

Authority chain

Congressional issuer perimeter
  -> federal, certified State, or foreign-comparability gate
     -> named issuer and redemption obligation
        -> reserve manager and custodian
           -> wallet, exchange, processor, merchant, and network distribution
              -> redemption, enforcement, correction, or insolvency priority

Continuous programmable transfer and modular distribution are genuine capabilities. Multiple coins operate under preexisting regimes. The missing object is a complete GENIUS approval, supervision, reporting, and stress chain. The reserve trust or custodian does not become a second issuer; statutory reserve priority is not federal insurance or guaranteed immediate par recovery.

Legitimacy test: who may issue a dollar-like claim, what makes par credible, which holders can actually redeem, who may freeze or correct, and which public regime makes the promise acceptable across jurisdictions?

Counter-record: the law may make new technology legally legible while keeping the issuer, reserves, supervision, and coercive powers named. That is compatible with bank/public institutional absorption, not only displacement.

Next return: qualifying final rules, actual effective date, first exact issuer approval, State certification, foreign comparability/registration, issuer or reserve novation, and first tested redemption, enforcement, insolvency, mistaken freeze, correction, or appeal.

Case 4 — SEC transfer agents: distributed execution, concentrated responsibility

Cutoff: 2026-09-01
Owner: sec-transfer-agent-modernization

Read first

  • Research Packages/Fintech and Private Rails/Transfer Agent Modernization Proposal 2026-09-01/SEC_TRANSFER_AGENT_MODERNIZATION_RECORD_CHAIN_AND_CONTROL_MAP_2026-09-01.md

The proposed design permits electronic, outsourced, and linked-DLT systems while retaining one registered recordkeeping transfer agent that is responsible for and in exclusive control of the authoritative master securityholder file. Recognized positions must reconcile to authorized supply and the master file.

This is a direct attempt to close the capability–structure gap:

multichain technical state
  -> reconciled control book
     -> accountable registered recordkeeper
        -> correction, regulatory examination, and enforcement

Legitimacy test: can the market receive the benefits of distributed execution without losing recognizable title, correction, and accountable record authority?

Counter-record: this may become one of the strongest examples of successful institutional adaptation. Concentrated accountability is not necessarily anti-technology or evidence of lost legitimacy.

Next return: Federal Register/comment clock, coded affected-party comments, final one-agent or joint-responsibility rule, first registered multichain operation, and a public fork, bridge, overissuance, correction, or cash/title finality case.

Fedwire completed a production message migration while the Reserve Banks remained the legal operators, FRFS remained the national business line, Board supervision remained visible, and Reserve Bank account books remained the authoritative monetary record. ISO changed message structure; it did not become the money, legal operator, access authority, or finality rule.

This is the necessary control against treating technology as legitimacy loss. It demonstrates that a major technical migration can occur inside an identifiable constitution.

Cases 7–9 — Three different forms of institutional hollowing

These cases must not share one status label merely because functions or staff move.

Department of Education — distributed operation, retained owner

Source: Freedom 250 Chronicle/02 - Research/Federal Group Control/Department of Education — Consummation Map.md
Cutoff: 2026-08-15

ED and Federal Student Aid retain statutory responsibility and debt ownership. Treasury and other agencies perform bounded work under interagency agreements. The no-title-transfer clause and continuing appropriations are direct legal- continuity evidence. The full phase state, service outcomes, borrower notice, error, complaint, and remedy chain remain unproved.

Rival explanation: lawful shared-service modernization that preserves policy authority and improves continuity.

USAID — statutory person with a severely reduced operating body

Source: Freedom 250 Chronicle/02 - Research/Federal Group Control/USAID — Statutory Person and Operating Death.md
Cutoff: 2026-08-15

USAID remains a statutory independent establishment while State asserted operating leadership over retained foreign assistance and other USAID functions were to cease. Congress retains charter and money authority. The current function, asset, grant, contract, liability, beneficiary, and remedy succession is incomplete.

Rival explanation: lawful policy reprioritization and consolidation rather than institutional illegitimacy.

HHS/AHA — proposed replacement before legal creation

Source: Freedom 250 Chronicle/02 - Research/Federal Group Control/HHS and AHA — Proposed Person and Statutory Remainder.md
Cutoff: 2026-08-15

HHS, SAMHSA, and HRSA remain statutory. AHA is an announced and budget-requested operating box whose different public descriptions do not fully agree. Congress funded existing component headings rather than AHA, and a court preliminarily enjoined parts of the restructuring for named components and programs.

The direct evidence is a provisional judicial authority finding, not a final merits judgment or a broad public-legitimacy measure.

Next returns across all three: exact operating charts, staff and function states; service and beneficiary outcomes; lawful transfer/closeout instruments; appropriations and charters; complaints and correction; final court rulings.

Cases 10–11 — OPM centralization and its positive control

Source: Research Packages/Office of Personnel Management Structural Change Audit 2025-2026/OPM_STRUCTURAL_CHANGE_COVERAGE_AND_GAP_AUDIT_2025-01-01_TO_2026-08-29.md
Cutoff: 2026-08-29

OPM as personnel governor

OPM and OMB increasingly standardize hiring, ranking, appraisal, suitability, appeals architecture, personnel records, retirement intake, benefits integrity, and shared HR systems. Employing agencies still own their lawful personnel actions; OPM OIG, MSPB, Congress, and courts remain checking institutions.

Policy, platforms, awards, and shared-service creation are documented. Signed migrations, production acceptance, legacy decommissioning, service outcomes, AI validation, and appeal outcomes are not.

The AFGE litigation supplies a concrete leash: the challenged OPM-directed probationary-separation program was enjoined while agencies' own lawful authority was preserved. This is a contest over the locus of personnel authority, not evidence that all centralization is illegitimate.

Digital retirement as a positive-control candidate

OPM's Online Retirement Application moved paper routing toward shared digital intake and case records without transferring the retirement mandate. Use became mandatory for covered agency/payroll groups, and OPM reported high electronic intake and substantial throughput. That is real operating adoption, though it remains issuer-reported.

The decisive test is whether the technology reduces delay, lost records, and handoff ambiguity while preserving accurate adjudication, privacy, accessibility, notice, reconsideration, appeal, correction, and continuity during outage or vendor failure.

Case 12 — FAR: fast deviation, slower public-law clock

Source: Freedom 250 Chronicle/02 - Research/Federal Group Control/Revolutionary FAR Overhaul — Procurement Control Map.md
Cutoff: 2026-08-15

Model class deviations can change operative agency rules before the formal FAR rewrite completes. Detailed material is also moving from codified regulation into expressly nonmandatory Companion Guides. Statute, applicable FAR, solicitation/contract, and issued deviation remain authoritative; GAO and the Court of Federal Claims remain protest forums.

This creates a procedural-legitimacy test because implementation can outrun notice-and-comment and because a Companion Guide may shape practice without being independently enforceable. No retrieved protest decision yet tests the new boundary.

Counter-record: rapid deviations can simplify procurement while statutes, protest rights, and eventual rulemaking retain the legal floor.

Next return: final rules, agency adoption map, first GAO/CFC decisions, and measured effects on speed, competition, protest, price, and performance.

Case 14 — Do Not Pay: machine eyes, distributed judgment

Cutoff: 2026-08-29
Owner: treasury-do-not-pay-governmentwide-map

Read first

  • Research Packages/Fraud and Payment Integrity/TREASURY_DO_NOT_PAY_GOVERNMENTWIDE_STRUCTURAL_MAP_2025-2026.md

Treasury operates a shared screening fabric. Source owners retain their source records and correction duties. Paying programs retain eligibility, adverse action, notice, contest, appeal, recovery, and substantive decision authority. A DNP match is a signal, not a blacklist or fraud judgment.

This is a paradigmatic legitimacy test for machine-assisted government:

source data
  -> shared match or alert
     -> independent verification
        -> program decision
           -> notice and evidence
              -> appeal and source correction
                 -> payment, recovery, or reversal

The legal firewalls are real counterevidence: DNP alone cannot be the basis for the new OPM retirement action, and Privacy Act/source-owner correction remains available. The missing evidence is whether the firewalls work in practice.

Next return: the first independently documented match-to-decision-to- appeal chain, false-positive/negative rates, decisional weight, access logs, correction latency, State implementation, and audited savings/recovery.

Case 15 — Federal AI and NARA: decision power depends on the surviving record

Cutoff: 2026-08-23
Owners: federal-agency-ai-plans-infrastructure and nara-ai-records-compute-transition

Read first

  • Research Packages/Federal Agency AI Plans and Infrastructure 2025-2026/FEDERAL_AGENCY_AI_TRANSITION_CROSS_AGENCY_SYNTHESIS_2026-08-23.md
  • Research Packages/NARA AI Records Layer and Buildout 2026-08-23/NARA_AI_RECORDS_AND_BUILDOUT_DEEP_DIVE_2026-08-23.md

Agencies are using AI for search, extraction, triage, anomaly detection, ranking, evidence processing, benefits support, identity checks, drafting, and recommendations. Adoption is real but uneven; a declared “human in the loop” does not disclose model weight, default acceptance, time pressure, evidence shown to the reviewer, or the affected person's ability to challenge it.

The agency remains responsible for the legal action. Yet model, data, and log custody can sit with private providers, and AI materials become federal records only through the applicable capture, control, circulation, use, and reliance tests. NARA's AC 11.2026 is therefore not bureaucratic background. It is an attempt to preserve the evidence from which a future action can be reconstructed and contested.

Positive-control possibility: AI can improve consistency, access, and speed when the authoritative human or legal action remains identifiable and inputs, models, tests, outputs, overrides, notice, correction, and appeal are captured.

Next return: actual decision chains, impact/subgroup tests, model and data versions, override rates, notice and evidence, appeal outcomes, incidents, vendor log/export rights, NARA schedule treatment, and independent performance and rights audits.

Wave 2 synthesis boundary

The cases currently support four bounded propositions:

  1. Capability–structure gaps are real and vary by function.
  2. Legal responsibility often survives the movement of interfaces, execution, data, standards, or operating work.
  3. Direct legitimacy evidence is strongest where a body itself names the issue, a court or oversight body adjudicates authority or control, or an operating program misses a measurable outcome.
  4. The clearest constructive path is not institutional stasis. It is technology joined to named authority, an accepted record, supervision, transparent correction, meaningful remedy, and observable public or participant value.

They do not establish one coordinated replacement system, a completed Federal Reserve displacement, general public rejection of economic institutions, or a system-wide legitimacy crisis.

Wave 3 handoff

Wave 3 should build one dated chronology while preserving the separate clocks. The timeline should include only events that change at least one of:

  • technical capability;
  • adoption or reliance;
  • legal authority;
  • standard or supervision;
  • authoritative record;
  • failure, correction, or remedy;
  • accepted legitimacy; or
  • a case's provisional legitimacy state.

Each event must name its component owner, evidence state, affected function, institution, changed clock, unchanged clocks, and claim limit. A date shared by several domains is not evidence that the events share an author or plan.
