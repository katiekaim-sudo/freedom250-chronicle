# Public money, identity and service delivery

How do legal eligibility and public-payment controls become decisions, records and services for people and recipients?

The delivery branch follows public money from authority and identity checks through agency or state administration, while preserving the boundary between screening, eligibility, payment execution, recovery and observed service result.

## Payment integrity is a checkpoint, not a blacklist

Treasury Do Not Pay is a governmentwide shared screening network and federal payment checkpoint. Treasury operates the Working System and controlled access, but each federal or State-administered program must verify a match and apply its own eligibility, notice, contest and recovery authority. A match is therefore not itself a conclusive blacklist, adverse decision, payment stop or recovered dollar.

The fraud and payment-integrity study joins pre-payment identity and eligibility to the weaker post-payment transaction record. Public feeds operate in bounded form, while State, NGO, subrecipient and vendor records remain fragmented. The delivery question is who had authority to decide, which record controlled, what payment actually moved and what remedy followed.

By September 22, the White House fraud task force had produced a clearer agency-action receipt: CMS says it cancelled about 315,000 unauthorized Marketplace enrollments covering more than 760,000 people after CMS/insurer review, while HHS officials publicly described pattern recognition and AI/ML near the prepayment eligibility gate. CMS describes the associated approximately $2.2 billion as an expected APTC return, not collected recovery, and the event supplies no NFDC lead or platform lineage. The public record still lacks a complete 30/60/90-day EO output set, the data-sharing instruments and methods, lead-to-case lineage, false-positive and remedy measures, and a reconciled collected-recovery chain.

Keep the public-delivery clocks explicit: a match is not a decision; an award is not a payment; a payment is not settlement; a correction is not recovery; and a policy or notice is not a recipient outcome. The hearing, OBBB, Pennsylvania, Hawaii and Southeast studies test different links in that chain and cannot be collapsed into one fraud story.

## Eligibility architecture changes through records and agencies

The September 1, 2026 OLC opinion changes the Executive Branch position on PRWORA reporting from benefit-agency reporting toward a participating State-wide component-agency duty, while rejecting the older formal-decision/final-order knowledge threshold. The saved study does not establish a replacement reporting procedure, State transmission, benefit termination or enforcement result.

Census/USPS, Education funding and election-integrity materials show the same pattern: an operating test, award condition, data route or public release can be real without becoming one joined enforcement database or a governmentwide outcome. Identity, citizenship, recipient status, award conditions and service access remain distinct records and authorities.

Education remains three questions beneath one router: university funding and programmable compliance; the August 4 Department source sweep; and the August 17 research-security audits. Institution-level review is not a governmentwide suspension, Title IV decision, award loss or Treasury payment stop, and PRWORA adjacency does not create a joined education-enforcement database.

## Service delivery is measured at the recipient and operating edge

Education funding is a multi-entity junction in which recipient identity, award conditions, servicing, reporting and research-security eligibility operate before or around payment; it does not establish a public universal funding ledger. Election-integrity review similarly separates voter data, intelligence products, election technology, registration and eligibility, and keeps capability, occurrence and outcome apart.

The branch therefore treats delivery as complete only when a named program or recipient record shows the operative decision, money or service, and the relevant result. A policy release, screening match, postal channel, award, or technology capability is a source object and route to further evidence, not a substitute for delivery.

## AI can influence a service without becoming the authoritative act

Federal agency AI can flag, rank, retrieve, summarize or recommend inside a public service or enforcement workflow without becoming the authoritative decision. An inventory is not accepted operation; a flag or recommendation is not an authoritative act; a human-review label does not prove meaningful independence; and internal agency AI does not establish payment, legal outcome or recipient effect.

Delivery still requires the agency's own authority, notice, appeal and remedy records. The eleven-entity synthesis therefore belongs in this branch as evidence about where AI enters public work, while its deployment, compliance and operating-effect limits remain explicit.

## How this fits together

Public delivery is a chain from identity and eligibility authority through payment execution and recipient-level evidence. A match is not a decision; an award is not a payment; a payment is not settlement; a correction is not recovery; and a policy or notice is not a recipient outcome.

The branch combines dated records through 2026-09-14. The September 14 task-force update is the newest operating-architecture answer; EO outputs, agreement terms, case lineage, replacement procedures, State operation and downstream outcomes remain unresolved where the sources say so.
