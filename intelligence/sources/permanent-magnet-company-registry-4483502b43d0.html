Permanent-magnet company registry

Core registry as of: 2026-07-22
New York extension as of: 2026-09-04 ET; see PERMANENT_MAGNET_NEW_YORK_FOOTPRINT.md
Purpose: identify the legal/industrial function actually performed and prevent proposed capacity from being reported as production

Required distinctions

For each company or project, keep these objects separate:

  1. parent and operating subsidiary;
  2. mine/resource and its current permit/construction/production state;
  3. concentrate, mixed carbonate, separated oxide, metal/alloy and magnet products;
  4. site name and country;
  5. announced, financed, under construction, commissioned and commercial states;
  6. nameplate capacity, current run rate, customer-qualified capacity and reported shipments;
  7. company claim, counterparty confirmation, regulator filing and measured output;
  8. grant, conditional commitment, final agreement, disbursement and repayment;
  9. memorandum, binding offtake, customer nomination and completed delivery.

Status vocabulary

  • COMMERCIAL — VERIFIED SHIPMENT: a named company or customer says commercial product shipped.
  • COMMERCIAL — COMPANY REPORTED: company reports commercial operation, but no named shipment or audited output series was located.
  • COMMISSIONED / RAMP: equipment has entered commissioning or early production; do not infer nameplate.
  • PILOT / SAMPLE: first articles or samples; not bulk supply.
  • CONSTRUCTION: physical project work is underway.
  • ANNOUNCED / SELECTED: site or capacity target only.
  • MOU / PROPOSED: non-final partnership or transaction.
  • CONDITIONAL FINANCE: government or lender commitment still subject to conditions.
  • FINAL AGREEMENT: operative agreement exists; milestones and disbursements remain separate.
  • INCUMBENT — SCALE NOT REFRESHED: established commercial producer whose exact current tonnes were not verified in this sweep.
