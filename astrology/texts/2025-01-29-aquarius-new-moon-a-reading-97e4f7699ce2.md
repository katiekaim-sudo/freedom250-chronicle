# 2025-01-29 Aquarius New Moon — A Reading

**This New Moon resets the national identity directly on the body. The lights rise in Aquarius with the herald fused to Pluto, so whatever is announced carries the force of a purge or a rebirth, while the treasury holds both the chart ruler and a dreaming Venus.**

Aquarius rises, and the Sun and Moon meet at 9°51′ Aquarius in the 1st. The Ascendant is less than five degrees ahead of them. A new beginning planted on the rising sign is a new beginning of self-image: what the nation says it is, what future it claims, and whom it claims to represent. The Sun is in detriment in Aquarius. Leadership presented as the voice of the collective still has to operate in a sign that distrusts a single center. The Moon is cazimi, fully empowered inside the light.

Mercury joins them at 2°17′ Aquarius in the 1st, combust and conjunct Pluto within 0.32°. The herald carries the voice of the underworld. Speech in this chart does not merely describe; it moves power, exposes, compels, or claims to reveal what was hidden. The constructive form is language that finally names deep structural facts. The shadow is language used as a lever, where each message becomes an instrument of pressure and the audience is asked to accept a transformation before it understands it.

The root is the loop that has held this winter's lunations: Saturn to Jupiter to Mercury and back to Saturn. Saturn, the chart ruler, is at 17°09′ Pisces in the 2nd. Jupiter, retrograde and in detriment, is at 11°20′ Gemini in the 5th. Mercury is in the 1st. Order, expansion and message pass authority around a closed circuit that runs through the treasury, the markets and the body itself. With the ruler in the treasury, what is being governed here is ultimately money.

Exalted Venus at 25°26′ Pisces shares that 2nd house with Neptune, the North Node and the America point. She applies to Neptune within 2.46°. Value becomes an image of abundance, a currency of trust or faith. It can be generous and inspired, or impossible to audit. The lights trine Jupiter within 1.48°, applying, which lends the new beginning optimism and appetite. Greer finds no hard hit on the ingress. The U.S. contacts cluster on the national MC: the chart's America point opposes U.S. MC within 0.20°, the nodal axis crosses it within 0.55°, and Jupiter opposes the U.S. Ascendant within 0.61°. The question put to the sovereign core is whether a renewed identity is being chosen or merely announced.
