# NSSTS Transaction, Failure and Return-to-Service Trigger Map

**Evidence cutoff:** 2026-08-19  
**Status:** pending Workbench evidence; not vault doctrine  
**Scope:** 31 cases across four trigger layers  
**Automatic monitoring:** false

## Governing finding

The next useful company search is not another capability sweep. It is a search
for the instruments that appear when a planned facility becomes controlled
land, a partner becomes a seller, a system becomes accepted, or a failed asset
becomes eligible to return to service.

```text
announcement
  -> binding land / capital / utility instrument
  -> exact buyer and seller
  -> delivery and evidence custody
  -> permit / commissioning / claim / bond disposition
  -> owner, AHJ, regulator or contracting-officer decision
  -> accepted operation
```

The new registry adds **31 cases across four trigger layers**. It does not rank
companies as likely award winners. It ranks the evidence objects that would
allow a company to move from adjacent capability into a defensible target role.

## 1. Arizona: the hidden object is site control

NIST's public announcement remains unusually precise about what it does *not*
establish: Natcast accepted a **nonbinding** letter of intent from ASU and the
Arizona Commerce Authority, while the final site contract remained subject to
diligence and negotiation. The $1.1 billion Natcast operating award funds the
PPF capability; it does not itself execute a lease.

The exact land chain is therefore pivotal:

| Node | Public role | What is still missing |
|---|---|---|
| Arizona Board of Regents | University-property controller and master lessor | Parcel-specific disposition or delegated approval |
| Arizona State University Research Park, Inc. | Legally separate nonprofit component unit and master lessee through 2101 | PPF sublease/development instrument and improvement owner |
| Natcast, Inc. | Federally funded operator and prospective site counterparty | Executed site contract |
| Arizona State University | Named construction, launch and operating partner | Exact procurement and acceptance delegation |
| Sunbelt Holdings | Park asset manager | Exact legal entity and PPF-specific scope |
| SRP / Air Products / Southwest Gas | Park-level utility presence | Reserved PPF capacity and accepted service |

This creates a clean promotion sequence:

1. legal description or parcel/APN;
2. executed lease, sublease, development or facility-use agreement;
3. named improvement owner and permitting authority;
4. electric, gas, water and discharge commitments;
5. design, construction and tool contracts;
6. occupancy, tool SAT and qualified baseline-flow evidence.

### Same-campus false positives

The Materials-to-Fab renovation is real and names **JE Dunn Construction
Company** and **Diamondback Design Group, LLC**. The MTW partnership renovation
is also real and names **Applied Materials, Inc.** in the approved scope. These
are valuable delivery comparators, but neither project is the Natcast PPF. A
shared campus cannot substitute for a target contract.

## 2. Argonne: use Aurora as the acceptance standard

The Genesis Center RFI closes on 2026-08-21 at 3:00 p.m. CDT. It asks about
equipment, software, infrastructure, use, reserved capacity and private
financing, but expressly creates no award commitment. Its category mirrors are
distribution paths for one inquiry, not separate competitions.

Aurora supplies the mature comparison:

| Stage | Aurora public evidence | Genesis Center public evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Buyer | UChicago Argonne, LLC under DOE M&O | Same procurement node is likely, but exact vehicle unannounced |
| Seller | Intel Federal LLC; Cray Inc. lower tier | No Center seller disclosed |
| Instrument | CORAL2 Subcontract 8F-30005 | RFI only |
| Performance | Exascale benchmark and operational reporting | No accepted Center configuration |
| Use | Opened to users; more than 60 million first-year node-hours | No Center user opening |
| Closeout | Project reported closed | No system-level IOC or closeout |

This comparison changes the evidentiary bar. A collaboration announcement,
active endpoint or GPU count may be important, but it is not equivalent to the
Aurora chain.

### Current commercial shadows

- **World Wide Technology LLC** is a named Minerva integration collaborator;
  Minerva is active, but the Argonne order is not public.
- **SambaNova Systems, Inc.** supplies the active Metis platform; its procurement
  and enterprise AmSC acceptance records are not public.
- **Commonwealth Edison Company** is the physical 138-kV service gatekeeper;
  no Genesis load study, service upgrade or capacity reservation is public.
- **Oracle** and **NVIDIA** are formal Solstice/Equinox partners; Argonne still
  calls both systems early-stage, and the exact commercial affiliate, price,
  commissioning criteria and IOC are not public.

The Argonne M&O clock also requires care. DOE's July 2026 public table lists
2026-09-30 as the current and ultimate end date for DE-AC02-06CH11357. That live
record supersedes older shorthand that treated the contract as already running
to 2031; a successor or extension action must be captured separately.

## 3. The acquisition-to-award entity-clock test

The strongest new calibration is the NIST silane-distribution purchase.

1. CVD Equipment Corporation signed an asset sale for Stainless Design
   Concepts on 2026-03-23.
2. **Edwards Semiconductor Solutions LLC** acquired the SDC business on
   2026-04-01; Atlas Copco placed it in its Semiconductor Equipment division.
3. NIST changed the intended source after the acquisition.
4. NIST awarded Edwards Semiconductor Solutions LLC notice
   `1333ND26PNB680185` on 2026-07-31 for $125,505.

The business being purchased—ultra-high-purity gas delivery, gas cabinets,
panels, valve manifold boxes and liquid chemical delivery—did not disappear,
but the award-date legal seller changed. The case proves why a pre-award brand
or market-research vendor cannot be copied into the final registry without an
entity-clock check. It also requires keeping **Edwards Semiconductor Solutions
LLC** separate from **Edwards Vacuum LLC**.

Other 2026 NIST and federal orders—SkyWater CMOS wafers, a Teledyne in-vacuum
EUV camera, a Laserspec mid-IR laser and a Xenturis cleanroom modification—show
how an operating laboratory stack fragments into modest exact purchase orders.
They are current delivery comparators, not Natcast assignments.

## 4. Failure reveals a second company map

When a project slips, equipment fails or a contractor defaults, another supply
chain becomes visible:

| Layer | Representative companies | Evidence produced | Final decision remains with |
|---|---|---|---|
| Insurance / loss prevention | Factory Mutual Insurance Company; HSB | Policy response, survey, inspection, cause and loss record | Carrier under wording; court/arbitrator if disputed |
| Claims / forensics | Sedgwick Claims Management Services; EFI Global; Exponent | Custody, origin/cause, remediation and quantum | Carrier/self-insured owner/tribunal |
| Surety completion | Zurich American; F&D; Travelers Casualty and Surety | Bond remedy, completion estimate and takeover agreement | Owner or federal contracting officer |
| Qualification | NTS within Element Materials Technology | Accredited test result and raw data | Customer or government program |
| Electrical recommissioning | Electrical Reliability Services | NETA and functional-performance dossier | Owner switching authority, utility and AHJ |
| Delay / prudence | J.S. Held | Critical-path, allocation and damage analysis | Commission, owner, insurer or tribunal |

### Role compression

Several corporate families can occupy multiple steps. Sedgwick owns EFI
Global; HSB combines equipment-breakdown expertise with inspection functions;
FM combines insurance with loss-prevention engineering; Element owns NTS and a
large testing network. This is a structural fact, not evidence of misconduct.
It raises four concrete controls:

1. identify the exact retaining and reporting entity;
2. preserve raw data and joint evidence access;
3. state who may perform destructive testing;
4. name the independent owner/AHJ/regulator who authorizes return.

## What now deserves the closest watch

1. **Executed PPF site-control instrument.** This would resolve parcel,
   counterparty, improvement owner and likely procurement/AHJ path.
2. **Genesis RFI disposition after 2026-08-21.** Watch for a respondent list,
   solicitation, capacity agreement, private-finance vehicle or subcontract.
3. **ComEd load/capacity object.** A study or service agreement would be much
   stronger than another computing partnership announcement.
4. **Solstice/Equinox delivery and IOC dossier.** Require exact affiliates,
   configuration, utility boundary, security acceptance and user release.
5. **PPF builder's-risk, performance bonds and commissioning contracts.** These
   can reveal the real construction team before a promotional announcement.
6. **Any loss, default or delay record.** The adjuster, surety, forensic expert
   and recommissioning firm may expose the project's actual dependency chain.
