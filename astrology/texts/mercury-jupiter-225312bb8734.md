# Mercury–Jupiter

## The planets

**Mercury** — *Communication, perception, reason.* MUNDANE: the press, media, internet, transport, treaties, the national conversation; intellectuals and talking heads. Mercury's condition shows how information flows through the nation — freely or obstructed, truthfully or deceptively.

**Jupiter** — *Zeus, expansion, faith, courage to hope.* MUNDANE: hierarchical organizations — government, corporate, nonprofit; the corporate sector; courts, judges, religion, charitable organizations. (Greer: "the planet of hierarchy; he rules every organization set up in a hierarchical manner.")

## The combination — mundane

Under helpful aspects (conjunction, sextile, trine), Mercury-Jupiter is one of the most broadly positive combinations for the nation's economic and intellectual life. Greer describes it as "favorable for every department of the economy; prosperity increases; favorable for new legislation." The media carries good news because there is good news to carry. Trade and commerce expand. New legislation passes and is well-received. The courts communicate clearly. The press covers institutional reform fairly. Intellectual life flourishes because Jupiter's expansive faith meets Mercury's capacity for articulation — big ideas find their voice.

Under hostile aspects (square, opposition), Jupiter's amplification distorts Mercury's information function. Greer warns of "unexpected economic difficulties; rising prices in stock markets may drop even faster than they rose." The media over-hypes, the markets over-extend, and the correction is sharper than the rally. Legislation is poorly drafted or poorly communicated. The press amplifies institutional scandals. Trade agreements collapse under scrutiny. The national conversation becomes grandiose and detached from reality — talking heads promise what hierarchical organizations cannot deliver, and the gap between rhetoric and results produces backlash.

**As trigger:** Mercury-Jupiter aspects are fast-moving (days to a couple of weeks) and mark the specific moments when economic optimism, legislative announcements, or media amplification activate the broader outer-planet storylines.
