## Question and finding

If a monetary change begins outside the United States, what would it mean for a
region to initiate it? The studies distinguish a changed legal permission, a
bank balance sheet, a pilot, live transactions, a settlement arrangement and
control over admission or loss. Those changes can occur in different places
and on different clocks. No source establishes one coordinated conversion or
a single jurisdiction completing every part of the proposed architecture.

Use this document for the regional comparison. The bank and state comparison
owns the detailed funds/claims/collateral mechanisms, and the regional annex
owns the entity and operating-object maps. The [Next Phase study](../sources/international-monetary-next-phase-research-package-804f4b36d10e.html)
asks a complementary question: how do captured funds become assets, public
payments, legally final transfers and insured obligations? Its later position
in the folder sequence does not make it a replacement for every regional fact.

## The central distinction: initiation is not dominance

A jurisdiction can start a phase without controlling its final form. Production use may begin in a modest bilateral QR corridor. The standard may be set later by a wholesale central-bank system. Scale may arrive later still through dollar balance sheets and private networks. The initiating event, the legal settlement asset and the eventual dominant control point can therefore sit in different countries.

## Regional evidence and hypotheses at the original comparison cutoff

### Japan — the stress-and-collateral opening

Japan matters because sovereign debt, bank reserves, the yen funding market and decades of accumulated cross-border positions meet in the same system. Separately, official work is testing tokenized BOJ money and digital use of JGB collateral. That makes Japan a plausible place for **stress to force faster adoption** even if another jurisdiction supplies the interface.

The official record supports an active laboratory plus a bounded production foothold, not a completed monetary replacement:

- SBI Shinsei Trust Bank issued JPYSC on 2026-06-24, but circulation remains inside SBI VC Trade accounts and cannot yet move to external wallets;
- MUFG Bank, Mizuho Bank and SMBC target commercial transactions through a separate trust-stablecoin arrangement during FY2026;
- Japan Post Bank plans Yucho DCJPY tokenized deposits while separately building a JGB ladder from balances previously held largely at the BOJ;
- the BOJ has described internal sandbox work involving tokenized central-bank money and its participation in Project Agorá;
- JPX/JSCC, Mizuho and Nomura announced a proof of concept for 24/7 digital JGB collateral transactions on Canton;
- the 2025 Payment Services Act amendment and rules effective 2026-06-01 permit specified trust structures to hold up to 50% of their covered reserve pool in qualifying short-dated sovereign bonds, subject to valuation and cure rules.

The bank evidence does not show a coordinated JGB order. Japan Post has the clearest BOJ-cash-to-JGB ladder, while Mizuho remains short-duration, MUFG and SMBC are changing classification/hedges as well as holdings, Resona plans gradual yen-bond purchases and Fukuoka FG remains cautious. The likely balance-sheet inflection is a yield threshold at which multiple banks independently lengthen duration.

The saved claim that Japanese pension funds were officially directed in July 2026 to buy more domestic assets was not located in a primary government or fund record during this pass. GPIF did rebalance materially into its broad domestic-bond bucket in FY2025, but the category includes yen-hedged foreign bonds and short-term assets. It does not prove equivalent JGB buying or a political directive and cannot carry the thesis.

### South Korea — the adapter state

Korea’s importance is not that it has declared a new national money. It is that the same policy complex is connecting tokenized deposits, central-bank settlement, securities-token law, longer wholesale settlement hours and bilateral local-currency QR payments.

Project Hangang’s first phase was a live pilot and Phase II was launched in March 2026. The National Assembly also passed the securities-token framework in January 2026, with effect scheduled for February 2027. This is an integration pathway, not evidence that a general CBDC is in production.

The sharper next test is fiscal and sovereign: a ₩30bn EV-charging subsidy is scheduled for programmable deposit-token disbursement, with a stated 2030 policy goal for one quarter of Treasury disbursement. A real public-funds payment or tokenized KTB settlement would promote Korea beyond another merchant pilot. KB Kookmin's production US$100m digital bond proves asset issuance but remains offshore dollar debt.

### Indonesia / ASEAN — the quiet production lead

Within this comparison, these live corridors show that a production payment change need not originate in the United States. Indonesia’s cross-border QR arrangements already let users make live payments while participating central banks and appointed dealers support direct local-currency conversion and settlement. Indonesia–Japan and Indonesia–South Korea links are among the live corridors, alongside the wider ASEAN payment-connectivity program.

This is not de-dollarization by proclamation and not a common ASEAN currency. It is narrower and more operational: some retail cross-border flows can bypass a third-currency conversion leg. That is a genuine production change.

Indonesia now has a deeper bank-sovereign mechanism. From 2026-06-01, covered natural-resource export FX must enter state-owned-bank special accounts under sector-specific retention rules, with permitted placement in bank/BI instruments and FX government bonds/sukuk. Mandiri's direct CIPS connection and the prospective RMB-clearing arrangement extend outward from that domestically controlled liquidity base. This is not compulsory sovereign buying, but it is a legally explicit trade-to-bank-to-sovereign channel.

Digital Rupiah should remain separate. Bank Indonesia’s wholesale work has completed a PoC phase, but the central bank describes the project as exploration and experimentation rather than a public production launch.

### Europe — the wholesale standard setter

Europe’s most consequential near-term path is wholesale, not the retail digital euro. In June 2026, EIB DLT-native commercial paper was mobilized through Clearstream triparty services and ECMS for Bundesbank financing. The ECB’s Pontes initiative is designed to connect DLT platforms to settlement in TARGET Services central-bank money, with an initial product launch scheduled for September 2026. Appia is the longer-run integrated design.

The completed EIB circuit makes Europe more than a paper-design story, but it preserved existing CSD, T2S, ECMS and collateral-eligibility controls. If Pontes moves from schedule to reliable live operation, Europe may establish the first broad public template in this group for tokenized wholesale assets settling against central-bank money. That is different from issuing a new currency and different from the digital euro, which still requires legislation and an issuance decision.

The private cash leg is already plural: SG-FORGE has a production euro electronic-money token, HSBC has production corporate tokenized deposits, and Qivalis now has 37 participating banks but still awaits authorization and issuance. Europe is building public settlement and private money together.

### BRICS — corridor, not coin

Official BRICS declarations support local-currency financing and payments, technical work on payment interoperability, study of BRICS Clear and review of the Contingent Reserve Arrangement. They do not establish a supranational monetary authority or announce a gold-backed common currency.

The correct object is therefore a bundle:

- bilateral and regional local-currency arrangements run by member states;
- the New Development Bank’s financing choices;
- the Contingent Reserve Arrangement;
- the BRICS Payment Task Force and proposed interoperability work;
- feasibility work around clearing and settlement infrastructure.

BRICS can accelerate demand for alternatives and give political cover to national rails. It cannot, on the current official record, switch on one bloc-wide money.

Russia's statutory 2026-09-01 digital-ruble access, China's CIPS/e-CNY architecture, India's UPI, Brazil's Pix/Drex work and NDB's separate-currency funding are real national or institutional clocks. None becomes BRICS money by political association. NDB's February 2026 US$2bn bond also disproves a binary dollar-exit reading.

## Original sequencing hypothesis — not an observed plan

**Dated analytical scenario:** the ordering below was an interpretive proposal in the original study, not an announced plan, measured probability or established dependency. Regions can develop in parallel; the later corrections above do not validate the sequence.

1. **Indonesia captures trade FX domestically and demonstrates production local-currency corridors.**
2. **Japan supplies a bank-balance-sheet, trust-money or collateral catalyst.**
3. **Korea joins fiscal spending, deposits, securities and wholesale components.**
4. **Europe supplies a central-bank-money and sovereign-collateral wholesale standard.**
5. **Russia/China/India/Brazil and other BRICS states widen national production rails without one bloc currency.**
6. **The United States scales, collateralizes or weaponizes the winning architecture**, even if it did not originate it.

In compact form: **Indonesia can start the balance-sheet capture and usable rail. Japan can start the bank/trust transition or the stress. Korea can connect public spending to bank money. Europe can start the wholesale standard. BRICS states can widen the corridor without a bloc coin. America can still control scale and leverage.**

## Evidence that would test the sequencing hypothesis

- Japan’s stress remains domestically contained and its digital-collateral work does not pass PoC.
- Cross-border QR growth remains tourism-sized and never reaches meaningful trade, treasury or wholesale flows.
- Pontes is delayed, stays narrow, or fails to attract interoperable DLT platforms and settlement volume.
- Korean pilots do not proceed to durable commercial or public-sector use.
- BRICS technical work remains declarations without implementable governance, operators or rulebooks.
- United States regulation and private infrastructure move first at production scale, collapsing the timing gap.
