A catalog of saved hearing memos, testimony and bills, from late 2024 to mid-2026

Katie's vault keeps a wide shelf of congressional hearing documents beyond the House Oversight set. The study keeps each source type's limits attached: a committee memo, prepared testimony and a bill text are different kinds of evidence, not transcripts of what was said.

What we found

  • The saved hearings run from November 13, 2024 (UAP) to July 15, 2026 (Emerging Fraud Threats), and include committee memos, written testimony, at least one bill and other source notes.
  • A monetary and Treasury-market run covers Monetary Policy (March 4, 2025), Treasury Debt (April 8, 2025), Treasury Market Fragilities (May 15, 2025), Primary Dealers (December 2, 2025), the Treasury-Fed Accord (March 18, 2026) and Future of Payments (June 24, 2026).
  • A fraud run covers the War on Waste (February 12, 2025), Minnesota Fraud Parts I and II (January 7 and March 4, 2026), Fraud in State-Run Federal Programs (April 15, 2026), a nine-bill fraud markup (April 29, 2026) and SNAP Fraud (June 25, 2026).
  • Records and declassification hearings covered the John F. Kennedy files in April and May 2025, Martin Luther King Jr. records in January 2026, unidentified anomalous phenomena in November 2024 and September 2025, and the MKUltra program in June 2026.
  • The Price Stability Act of 2025 bill is saved with the September 17, 2025 hearing on Fed mandates and independence.

Why it matters

Hearings are where Congress publicly frames changes to money, fraud controls and records before or alongside law. This shelf is the raw material for tracking that framing.

What to watch

  • pending · New hearings added after the July 25, 2026 graph audit, especially later steps in the fraud and Treasury-market series.
