Freedom 250 · Banks and the dollar · Settlement words

What do 'settled' and 'final' really mean for an ACH payment, a tokenized stock and a DTC token?

They mean different things in each system, and "final" in one record does not settle the others. ACH: The Reserve Bank debits the sending bank and credits the receiving bank at the scheduled time. For a credit item, the receiving bank's settlement credit is final and available under Operating Circular 4. Securitize tokenized stock: The product is designed to exchange the CET and USDC atomically on Solana. Solana can make the token transfer technically confirmed while Securitize determines the governed entitlement state. DTC token: The entitlement representation moves between registered participant wallets free of value. DTC's official record recognizes the participant wallet location of the entitlement.
