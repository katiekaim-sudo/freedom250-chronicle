Where does U.S. diesel go, and who replaced Russian diesel?

In June 2026 U.S. diesel went mostly to Mexico (8.7 million barrels), Brazil (4.4 million barrels), Netherlands (4.3 million barrels); when Russian diesel left Northwest Europe, the February 2023 replacements came from Saudi Arabia (0.202 mb/d), India (0.161 mb/d), China (0.119 mb/d), with the United States averaging 0.107 mb/d over October 2022 to February 2023.
