## C. Authority response

| Field | Entry |
|---|---|
| Response state | `PARTIAL` |
| Exact propositions answered | The Chinese Executive Director stated that the accommodative monetary-policy direction was consistent with staff advice and that further RRR/rate cuts remained available. He rejected staff’s preliminary finding that the 2025 external position was stronger than fundamentals, citing trade restrictions, export frontloading and EBA-model limits. He asserted that market forces play the decisive role in RMB formation, that stability is maintained at an adaptive/equilibrium level, and that China has neither need nor intent to obtain trade advantage through depreciation. The statement separately described the financial system as generally sound and risks as under control. |
| Source/page/date | IMF CR 26/44, Executive Director statement dated `2026-02-13`: monetary policy, PDF p.115; external assessment/exchange policy, PDF p.116; financial-system response, PDF pp.117–118 |
| Attribution boundary | Statement by Zhengxin Zhang, Executive Director for China, presenting the Chinese authorities’ position. It is not IMF staff analysis or the Executive Board summing-up. |
