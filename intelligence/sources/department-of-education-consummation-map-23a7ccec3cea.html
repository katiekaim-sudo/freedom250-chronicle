Department of Education — Consummation Map

Updated: 2026-08-15
Status: factual legal map
Rule: An EO cannot repeal an organic act. An interagency agreement is not a deed to the loan book. A stay is not a merits holding.

Short answer

The Department still exists. House OLRC prints 20 U.S.C. § 3411 as law in effect on August 14, 2026: “There is established an executive department to be known as the Department of Education.” No retrieved statute repeals that sentence.

What moved is control, not the charter. EO 14242 tells the Secretary to facilitate closure “to the maximum extent appropriate and permitted by law.” The March 19, 2026 ED–Treasury IAA is an Economy Act buy of collection work. Attachment 1 says the referrals “do not transfer ownership of the debt from Education to Treasury.” P.L. 119-75 still appropriates to the Department, including Program Administration “For carrying out … the Department of Education Organization Act,” and orders staffing “necessary to fulfill its statutory responsibilities.”

Four clocks. None of them is the others.

2. Four clocks

Clock 1 — statutory existence. Live. § 3411 still names the Department. § 3473(a)(2) bars the Secretary from abolishing “organizational entities established by this chapter.” § 3503 killed HEW’s Education Division on May 4, 1980. It did not kill ED. No 2025–26 repeal of chapter 48 was retrieved.

Clock 2 — personnel. Cut, not erased. ED’s March 11, 2025 release: workforce 4,133 at inauguration; after the RIF, “roughly 2,183”; “final mission.” Same release: ED “will continue to deliver on all statutory programs.” Official 2026 headcount after that: not retrieved. McMahon stayed the district court’s bar on the RIF. A stay is not a merits blessing.

Clock 3 — IAA function transfer. Work moved. Authority did not. ED’s hub (reviewed July 13, 2026) posts the IAA PDFs and says the agreements “do not change funding, eligibility, or legal protections.” The Treasury IAA (March 19, 2026) is the student-loan piece. Vehicle: 31 U.S.C. § 1535 and § 3711(g). Three phases: (1) revoke the 2001 Cross-Servicing exemption and collect defaulted FSA debt; (2) operational support for non-defaulted debt “to the extent practicable”; (3) review of general admin — review, not a grant of statutory power. § VIII.G: nothing in the IAA “limit[s], supersed[es], or otherwise affect[s] either party’s … statutory or regulatory duties.” An IAA is not a charter amendment.

Clock 4 — appropriations. Still pointed at the Department. P.L. 119-75 (Feb. 3, 2026) includes Student Financial Assistance $24,615,352,000; Student Aid Administration $2,058,943,000; Program Administration $399,407,000 “For carrying out, to the extent not otherwise provided, the Department of Education Organization Act,” and: “the Department of Education shall support staffing levels necessary to fulfill its statutory responsibilities.” Congress still writes the check to this person.

3. EO 14242 — what it orders and what it cannot

Signed March 20, 2025. Published March 25, 2025. 90 FR 13679.

Orders. Sec. 2(a): the Secretary shall, “to the maximum extent appropriate and permitted by law, take all necessary steps to facilitate the closure of the Department of Education and return authority over education to the States and local communities while ensuring the effective and uninterrupted delivery of services, programs, and benefits.”

Cannot. Sec. 3(a): does not impair authority granted by law. Sec. 3(b): implement “consistent with applicable law and subject to the availability of appropriations.” An EO cannot repeal § 3411. It cannot repeal § 3473. It cannot move title to the loan book. “Facilitate” plus “permitted by law” is the tell.

4. The student-loan book — two objects

Object A — collections. IAA phase 1: Fiscal Service collects defaulted federally held student-loan debt through Cross-Servicing. IAA text: DRG serves nearly six million borrowers; outstanding value “over $120 billion.” Education pays Fiscal Service. Education still certifies the debt. Attachment 1, III.3, word for word: “Referrals made pursuant to this Attachment are for collection purposes only and do not transfer ownership of the debt from Education to Treasury.”

Object B — title. Direct Loans arise under Title IV of the HEA. 20 U.S.C. § 1018 keeps the PBO in the Department. The IAA’s own § VIII.G leaves both parties’ statutory duties in place. Phase 2 is operational support “to the extent practicable.” Phase 3 is a review. A review is not a deed.

The March 19, 2026 press release says Treasury will “assume operational responsibility for collecting on defaulted” debt. Press is not the IAA. The IAA wins.

6. Entity Theory read

The group still consolidates a Department of Education. Control inside the group shifted. Labor, HHS, Interior, State, DOJ, and Treasury now do work the Department used to do with its own hands. That is control of operations. It is not ownership of the charter, and it is not title to the loan book.

The Secretary can order an Economy Act service. The Secretary cannot, by that order, amend 20 U.S.C. § 3411. A hollowed department is still a legal person if the Code says it is. The entity-chart status “dissolving” names the intent clock. It does not flip Clock 1.

Third emptying: HHS and AHA — Proposed Person and Statutory Remainder. Do not merge.
