Freedom 250 · Digital money · Who does what

When you buy into BlackRock'S BUIDL fund, send USDC or buy a tokenized share, which company is responsible at each step?

A different company at almost every step, and the money step is mostly unmapped. In a BUIDL subscription, Securitize Markets, LLC gives eligible investors access, the fund (BlackRock USD Institutional Digital Liquidity Fund Ltd.) issues the shares, Securitize, LLC keeps the share register, and The Bank of New York Mellon must confirm the subscription money first. For USDC, Circle Internet Financial, LLC is the issuer behind each documented step, but a token transfer alone is not a bank payout. For the Coinbase tokenized share, Coinbase Onchain SPV Ltd issues the certificate, Alpaca Securities LLC holds the underlying Apple share, Base carries the token and Onchain Marketplace Limited keeps the Legal Register that decides ownership. Across the three, 10 of 12 money steps are not mapped in the library's evidence.
