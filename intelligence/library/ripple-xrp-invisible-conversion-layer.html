Research summary

Payment middleware can already hide the selected settlement rail from the requesting agent or end user, and XRPL can use XRP as a bridge between issued assets. Ripple has assembled a vertical payments, custody, stablecoin, prime-brokerage and treasury stack, while GOLD EAGLE is a horizontal federal vulnerability-prioritization and distribution layer. They can intersect at software, operator and critical-infrastructure surfaces, but no public source through September 17 names Ripple or XRPL as a GOLD EAGLE participant. The February XRPL Batch incident is a strong functional discovery-to-patch analogue, not evidence of a federal join. XRP's distinct inflection still requires route share, depth, financed inventory, holding-duration, haircut and hedge evidence; Ripple, XRPL, RLUSD and XRP remain four separate success ledgers.
