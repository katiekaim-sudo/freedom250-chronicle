Mars–Uranus

The planets

Mars — Ares, conflict, action, capacity to assert. MUNDANE: the military; war and violent crime; epidemic disease; natural disasters. Also: the men of the nation, men's vote and issues. Mars angular = classic war warning. Mars shows where the nation's combative energy is directed.

Uranus — Prometheus, awakener, breakthroughs, liberation. MUNDANE: democracy, countercultures, alternative movements; machines, factories, computers, the internet; radical change. Uranus disrupts whatever it touches — liberating what was trapped, destabilizing what was settled.

The combination — mundane

Mars-Uranus is the lightning strike of mundane astrology — sudden, powerful, and impossible to predict in its specifics. Under helpful aspects (conjunction when well-placed, sextile, trine), Greer describes results "very favorable for constructive change and beginnings; energy and success; legislation is successful." Revolutionary energy is channeled constructively. New technologies emerge with military precision. Counterculture movements gain decisive momentum. Legislation passes that actually changes things. The factories, computers, and internet infrastructure (Uranus) are energized by Mars's drive and the result is rapid, effective modernization.

Under hostile aspects (square, opposition), the same energy turns destructive. Greer warns of a "disruptive influence; unexpected crises; disputes amplified; quarrels ignited; turbulence and lawlessness; an important figure may die." This is the aspect of the sudden strike — cyberattacks, terrorist incidents, assassination attempts, revolutionary violence. Technology weaponized. Machines and factories become sites of conflict. Democratic movements turn violent. The "important figure may die" warning is specific and sobering — Mars-Uranus hostile aspects have historically correlated with sudden, violent deaths of public figures. Lawlessness spikes because Uranus rejects authority and Mars provides the force to act on that rejection.

As trigger: Mars-Uranus aspects are fast-moving (days to a couple of weeks) and mark the specific moments when sudden crises, technological disruption, or revolutionary violence activate the broader outer-planet storylines.
