The Reading

The whole: a hidden exchange over money, steered by a ruler walking backward through the house of government

Scorpio rises, so the nation presents itself through Mars's fixed water: intense, private, strategic, concerned with what is owed and who holds leverage. Mars, the chart ruler, is retrograde at 4°45′ Leo in the 10th, in the house of government, and only two weeks past his December 6 station at 6°10′ Leo. The steering hand of the season is the executive's own force, and it is moving backward across the house it rules. Retrograde Mars in the 10th reverses and re-litigates. It goes back over ground already taken, revisits old fights, and presents a new government through the undoing of a previous one. Retrograde does not mean weak. It means the force is aimed at what came before.

Mars opposes Pluto at 0°45′ Aquarius in the 4th by 4.00°, applying. The government's force faces the underworld power rising from the land, the opposition and the foundations. Pluto has only just taken up permanent residence in Aquarius. The deepest transformation in the chart sits under the nation's feet while the executive's force pushes against it from the top of the wheel. That is a purge axis. It can be a necessary clearing of rot from the foundations, or a war of attrition between the state's will and the ground it stands on.

The root: Mercury and Jupiter, in mutual reception, both in detriment

Take everything to the root and it drains into one closed circuit. Mercury at 8°34′ Sagittarius in the 2nd and Jupiter retrograde at 14°28′ Gemini in the 8th are in mutual reception: Mercury is in Jupiter's sign and Jupiter is in Mercury's, so each rules the other. Both are in detriment. The chart's gravity well is an exchange between the treasury (2nd) and debt, shared finance and foreign money (8th). The herald and the great benefic are each working in the other's house with the other's tools.

A mutual reception is a pact. Each planet can call on the other; neither fully owns its own affairs. Here the pact is conducted by two debilitated partners. Mercury in Sagittarius speaks in large principles with poor precision. Jupiter in Gemini multiplies options, deals and talking points and struggles to deliver one coherent expansion. Everything in the winter serves this: the nation's revenue and the nation's obligations referring back and forth to one another, value explained by promises and promises backed by explanations. Mercury trines Mars within 3.83°, so the root has a direct line to the chart ruler. The executive's force can speak straight into the money circuit.

The T-square: the political class against the establishment, with Jupiter as the apex

Jupiter is not only a root member. It is also the apex of a mutable T-square. The Moon at 13°09′ Virgo in the 11th opposes Saturn at 13°48′ Pisces in the 5th within 0.65°, applying, and the two are contra-parallel within 0.08° as well. Jupiter squares both: Saturn within 0.66° (the Jupiter–Saturn square is exact on December 24), and the Moon within 1.31°. The political class in the legislature (Moon, 11th) stands against the establishment's structure in the arena of markets and national pleasure (Saturn, 5th). Both tensions discharge through Jupiter in the 8th, the root planet in the debt house.

Greer's Jupiter/Saturn pair is corporate expansion against bureaucratic inertia, the hierarchy that grows against the hierarchy that endures. Their square here is the season's central institutional argument: whether the structure should be enlarged or constrained, and what it is going to cost. Because the apex is the retrograde, debilitated root planet, that argument is fought over obligation and credit rather than in plain view.

The Sun: the executive in the press house, square Neptune

The Sun at 0° Capricorn falls in the 3rd house, the press, communications and the national conversation. It squares Neptune at 27°11′ Pisces in the 5th by 2.82°. Greer names hostile Sun–Neptune aspects the most unfortunate indication in a mundane chart, and they bracket this entire era (the 2025 Aries ingress will carry the conjunction). At 0° Capricorn the executive arrives as the builder of institutions, but through the square its message passes through a medium of fog, spectacle and competing illusions. The information environment is not neutral ground; it is the stage on which the executive is seen, and it distorts as it transmits.

  • Venus at 16°05′ Aquarius in the 4th, trine Jupiter within 1.62°: value and alliance anchored in the land and the opposition party, with an easy channel into the root. There is a constituency for the deal.
  • Uranus retrograde at 23°56′ Taurus in the 7th: the disruptor stands in the house of foreign powers and open opponents, on the Descendant sign. Shocks arrive from the counterpart, and they arrive through money and material things (Taurus).
  • Pluto in the 4th, Neptune and Saturn in the 5th: the long clocks are placed under the nation's feet and in its markets and spectacles, not in its government.
  • MC 1°32′ Virgo in the 11th, ruled by Mercury: the chart's public summit belongs to the legislature and answers to the root planet.

The verdict

This is a chart of leverage and reversal. The steering force is the executive's own Mars moving backward through the house of government, opposed by a newly arrived Pluto at the foundations. The chart's meaning drains into a pact between the treasury and the debt house, run by two debilitated partners who rule each other. The political class and the establishment are locked in a T-square that discharges into that same debt planet. The Sun, meanwhile, speaks through a fogged press.

Both poles are fully alive. At the constructive pole, the reversal clears what had calcified, the money pact is renegotiated in the open, and the T-square forces a real decision about the size and cost of the state. At the shadow pole, the reversal becomes an end in itself, the money circuit governs out of sight, and the national conversation is so saturated with image that the country cannot tell a restructuring from a performance. The chart sets the question. The people answer it.
