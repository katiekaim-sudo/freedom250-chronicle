# Federal Reserve restructuring — factual timeline

Updated: 2026-08-20  
Status: working factual spine  
Scope: Federal Reserve entity structure, Reserve Bank operational authority, account access, payment infrastructure, Treasury-market clearing, balance-sheet operations, stablecoin implementation, and litigation over Federal Reserve independence.

## Inclusion rules

- Entries record a dated public action, publication, speech, filing, decision, operating change, or formally scheduled deadline.
- Descriptions state what the cited source says. They do not assign motives or characterize the events as a unified program.
- `Occurred` means the event has taken place.
- `Proposed` means a public proposal or request for comment remains subject to further action.
- `Scheduled` means a future date has been formally announced or established by rule or statute.
- Speeches and research are included as public positions or evidence of institutional work; they are not treated as adopted policy.

## 2017

### 2017-06-27 — Tenth Circuit splits three ways over Fourth Corner's account

**Status:** Occurred
**Entities:** Fourth Corner Credit Union; Federal Reserve Bank of Kansas City; Tenth Circuit
**Track:** Account access / courts

The Tenth Circuit issued a fractured judgment in the cannabis credit union's master-account suit — one judge saw statutory entitlement, one would allow a lawful-purpose amendment, one rejected relief. No binding majority established an unconditional right to an account. The first crack of the gate question that Custodia later answered. (Judicial Watch JM-08; the Judicial Watch carries the courthouse-anchored chart.)

**Source:** [Tenth Circuit — Fourth Corner opinion page](https://www.ca10.uscourts.gov/opinion/16-1016)

## 2020

### 2020-03-25 — TNB's narrow-bank account suit dismissed as unripe

**Status:** Occurred
**Entities:** TNB USA Inc.; Federal Reserve Bank of New York; S.D.N.Y.
**Track:** Account access / courts

The narrow bank's suit over a delayed interest-bearing master account was dismissed because FRBNY had never issued a final denial. No right established, no denial reviewed — the gate question kicked down the road to Custodia. (Judicial Watch JM-09.)

**Source:** [TNB dismissal](https://law.justia.com/cases/federal/district-courts/new-york/nysdce/1:2018cv07978/500217/52/)

**Entities:** Board of Governors; twelve Federal Reserve Banks  
**Track:** Account access / regional authority

The Board adopted final Guidelines for Evaluating Account and Services Requests. The guidelines establish a three-tier review framework for legally eligible institutions seeking Reserve Bank accounts and services. Individual Reserve Banks process account requests under the framework.

## 2023

### 2023-07-24 — Banco San Juan sues to keep its master account

**Status:** Occurred
**Entities:** Banco San Juan Internacional; Federal Reserve Bank of New York; Board of Governors; S.D.N.Y.
**Track:** Account access / courts

### 2023-11-09 — Preliminary injunction denied in Banco San Juan

**Status:** Occurred
**Entities:** S.D.N.Y.; Banco San Juan Internacional; Federal Reserve Bank of New York
**Track:** Account access / courts

The court declines to stop the termination: contracts authorize it, the statutes preserve discretion, and FRBNY is not an APA agency on the theory advanced. The gate may close under the Bank's own risk process. (Judicial Watch JM-07.)

**Source:** [District opinion](https://law.justia.com/cases/federal/district-courts/new-york/nysdce/1:2023cv06414/602767/106/)

### 2023-07-20 — FedNow launches

**Status:** Occurred  
**Entities:** Federal Reserve Financial Services; Reserve Banks  
**Track:** Payments

The Federal Reserve launched the FedNow Service for instant interbank clearing and settlement, operating continuously on a 24-hour, 365-day basis.

**Source:** [Federal Reserve Board — Federal Reserve announces FedNow Service is live](https://www.federalreserve.gov/newsevents/pressreleases/other20230720a.htm)

### 2023-12-13 — SEC adopts Treasury central-clearing rules

**Status:** Occurred  
**Entities:** SEC; covered Treasury clearing agencies; Treasury-market participants  
**Track:** Treasury clearing

The SEC adopted rules requiring covered clearing agencies to require their direct participants to submit specified cash Treasury and Treasury repo transactions for central clearing. Transactions with a central bank as counterparty are excluded from the definition of eligible secondary-market transactions.

**Source:** [SEC — Final rules on Treasury clearing](https://www.sec.gov/newsroom/press-releases/2023-247)

**Status:** Occurred
**Entities:** D. Wyo.; Custodia Bank; Board of Governors; Federal Reserve Bank of Kansas City
**Track:** Account access / courts

Summary judgment for the Fed defendants: eligibility is not entitlement; the denial stands. The gate holds at the district level; the appeal carries the question to the Tenth Circuit. (Judicial Watch JM-06.)

### 2024-11-13 — New York Fed announces FedTrade Plus

**Status:** Occurred  
**Entities:** Federal Reserve Bank of New York; SOMA Desk  
**Track:** Open-market operating platform

The New York Fed announced that it would replace FedTrade with FedTrade Plus and move open-market operations to the new platform in phases. It identified repo and reverse repo as the first phase, followed by securities lending, Treasury outright operations, and agency mortgage-backed-securities operations. ## 2025

### 2025-10-31 — Tenth Circuit affirms: no right to a master account

**Status:** Occurred
**Entities:** Tenth Circuit; Custodia Bank; Board of Governors; Federal Reserve Bank of Kansas City
**Track:** Account access / courts

Published opinion: the statutes give Reserve Banks discretion to deny eligible institutions; §342 says the Banks “may” receive deposits, and risk discretion remains. Judge Tymkovich dissents. The strongest circuit law yet on the gate — eligibility is not entitlement. (Judicial Watch JM-06; courthouse chart in the Judicial Watch.)

**Source:** [Tenth Circuit opinion](https://www.ca10.uscourts.gov/sites/ca10/files/opinions/010111327582.pdf)

### 2025-02-18 — Presidential order addresses independent-agency supervision

**Status:** Occurred  
**Entities:** Executive Office of the President; independent regulatory agencies; Federal Reserve Board  
**Track:** Executive control / agency independence

President Trump issued an executive order titled “Ensuring Accountability for All Agencies.” It directed independent regulatory agency chairs to consult with the Executive Office of the President and provided for White House review of certain regulatory actions. The order separately stated that it did not apply to the Federal Reserve's conduct of monetary policy but did apply to its supervision and regulation of financial institutions.

**Source:** [White House — Ensuring Accountability for All Agencies](https://www.whitehouse.gov/presidential-actions/2025/02/ensuring-accountability-for-all-agencies/)

### 2025-02-25 — SEC extends Treasury-clearing deadlines by one year

**Status:** Occurred  
**Entities:** SEC; Treasury clearing agencies; market participants  
**Track:** Treasury clearing

The SEC extended compliance for eligible cash Treasury transactions to December 31, 2026, and eligible repo transactions to June 30, 2027.

**Source:** [SEC — Extension of Treasury-clearing compliance dates](https://www.sec.gov/rules-regulations/2025/02/s7-23-22)

### 2025-05-22 — Supreme Court distinguishes the Federal Reserve from other independent agencies

**Status:** Occurred  
**Entities:** Supreme Court; executive branch; Federal Reserve  
**Track:** Constitutional status

In an order concerning removal protections at the National Labor Relations Board and Merit Systems Protection Board, the Supreme Court stated that the case did not implicate the constitutionality of for-cause protections at the Federal Reserve. The order described the Federal Reserve as a “uniquely structured, quasi-private entity” following a distinct historical tradition.

**Source:** [Supreme Court — Trump v. Wilcox order](https://www.supremecourt.gov/opinions/24pdf/24a966_5h25.pdf)

### 2025-07-18 — GENIUS Act signed

**Status:** Occurred  
**Entities:** President; Congress; Treasury; federal banking regulators; state regulators; payment-stablecoin issuers  
**Track:** Stablecoins / chartering

The GENIUS Act became law, establishing a federal and state framework for permitted payment-stablecoin issuers, reserve assets, redemption, supervision, foreign issuers, and certification of state regulatory regimes. Its effective date is the earlier of 18 months after enactment or 120 days after primary federal regulators issue final implementing regulations.

**Sources:** [Treasury — Statement on enactment](https://home.treasury.gov/news/press-releases/sb0197); [OCC — GENIUS Act implementation summary](https://occ-gov.cms.awsdev.treasury.gov/news-issuances/bulletins/2026/bulletin-2026-3/)

The FOMC announced the change on December 10. The operations use FedTrade Plus and settle on BNY's tri-party repo platform.

## Open verification items

- **Centrally cleared Fed operations:** no FOMC vote, pilot, small-value exercise, CCP choice, or launch notice was found through August 20. The timeline records speeches, counterparty survey work, and Perli's statement that Fed staff are still weighing the issue.
- **Cook after June 29:** the D.C. Circuit had earlier ordered the parties to file motions governing future proceedings within 14 days after the Supreme Court disposed of the stay request. As of August 20, an authoritative public order setting the next hearing or merits deadline was not found. Track D.C. Circuit No. 25-5326 and D.D.C. No. 1:25-cv-02903.

The Bank did publish official supplemental account terms on May 8. Those terms establish the legal entity, Fedwire Funds-only service and restrictions; they do not establish a completed direct transaction or the controlling one-year term-start clock. A second congressional letter followed from Senator Blunt Rochester on May 20.
- **Agency MBS cutover:** tests occurred, but no full FedTrade Plus cutover notice was found through August 20.
