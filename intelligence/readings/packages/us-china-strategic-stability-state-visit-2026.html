## Bottom line

**September 28 update:** The trade lane has now moved beyond the missing-object state described in the original September 24 files. The United States and China have published parallel official versions of the Board of Trade working procedures, the “30-for-30” terms of reference and both approved product lists. The Board is now a bilaterally evidenced mechanism. The tariff reductions remain pending domestic legal implementation; no effective date or customs instruction was located by the package cutoff. Read `US_CHINA_BOARD_OF_TRADE_AND_EIGHTH_ROUND_OUTCOME_AUDIT_2026-09-28.md` first for the current trade answer.

The public record supports a **leader-level political consensus and organizing framework**, not a newly signed, self-executing agreement announced at the September 24 state dinner.

President Xi's dinner formulation points backward and forward at the same time:

1. In May 2026, Trump and Xi publicly agreed to build a “constructive China-U.S. relationship of strategic stability.”
2. On September 24, Xi said the leaders reached “common understanding on many issues,” adding “new substance” and “new strategic guidance” to that framework.
3. Neither government had published, by this cutoff, a standalone bilateral instrument attaching defined obligations, signatures, entry-into-force language, termination rules, dispute procedures or an implementation schedule to the phrase **strategic stability**.

This does not make the leaders' consensus meaningless. It makes it a political framework whose practical content must be tested against later joint texts, agency actions, agreements and operating receipts.

A separate Chinese meeting readout says the economic teams reached a **“new joint arrangement.”** That may be a concrete trade package, but the governing text and complete terms were not public at this cutoff. It must not be silently merged with the broader strategic-stability framework.
