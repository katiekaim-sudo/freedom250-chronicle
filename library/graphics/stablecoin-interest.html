Where does stablecoin interest go if the law bans paying it?

The GENIUS Act bars an issuer from paying holders interest just for holding the coin. 10 of the 17 issuers here say in their terms that holders get nothing, and 7 don't say. The interest earned on the reserves stays with the issuer, which shares much of it with distributors such as exchanges. Some of those distributors pay their own customers 'rewards', and whether that is allowed is the open question.
