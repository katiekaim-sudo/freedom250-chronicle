Research summary

No. The Upbit KRW-USDT price fell during the defense window, consistent with the won strengthening, but that is a parallel price, not a measured exit: it does not show a response to an intervention order, resident capital flight or stablecoin net flow. Japan's limited production stablecoin rails are not shown to have supported intervention, and Japan's JPYSC is a trust claim, not a deposit.
