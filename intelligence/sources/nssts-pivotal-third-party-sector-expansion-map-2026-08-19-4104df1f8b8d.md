# NSSTS Pivotal Third-Party Sector Expansion Map

**Evidence cutoff:** 2026-08-19  
**Status:** active Workbench research; not vault doctrine; not an investment screen  
**Controlling registry:** `NSSTS_PIVOTAL_THIRD_PARTY_SECTOR_EXPANSION_REGISTRY_2026-08-19.json`

## Ruling

The second third-party sweep adds 40 exact candidate entities or entity seams
across four research waves. None has been promoted to a Science Strategy target
relationship. The named federal awards, laboratory installations, regulator
records and operating contracts are comparables or access paths. They do not
establish a target award, subcontract, purchase order or classified role.

The expansion exposes the same architecture in very different sectors:

```text
headline system
  -> hidden operating environment
  -> calibrated or traceable evidence
  -> independent or delegated review
  -> government / owner decision
  -> field operation and continuing custody
```

The company that builds the headline system may control none of the middle
steps. Those steps can still determine whether the system is testable,
licensable, safe to operate, qualified for procurement or admissible as federal
evidence.

## Wave 1 — quantum and resilient PNT

The hidden quantum chain is not one category called “cryogenics.” It is at least
four separate gates:

1. **environmental readiness** — Bluefors supplies and commissions the cold
   environment, SAES controls residual gas, and Lake Shore supplies traceable
   temperature and magnetic-field measurement;
2. **device correlation** — FormFactor can screen devices at 4 K, but a 4 K
   result must be correlated to actual millikelvin behavior;
3. **network and timing acceptance** — EXFO can establish classical fiber
   condition and Calnex can establish packet-timing behavior, while neither
   proves quantum security or phase-coherent quantum performance; and
4. **test truth and metrology** — Spirent Federal and Talen-X can create
   authorized NAVWAR scenarios, while Menlo frequency combs support optical
   traceability.

The sovereign-supply-chain seam is especially visible in **PHIX B.V. / PHIX
Inc.** Photonic packaging may be technically necessary but still fail a U.S.
target's export, IP, source-approval, reliability or domestic-second-source
requirements.

Highest-value return questions:

- What device measurement must correlate from 4 K to the installed operating
  temperature?
- Who owns the threat library and raw I/Q data in a resilient-PNT test?
- Does classical fiber acceptance sit in the same signed handover as quantum
  metrics, or are they separate acceptance packages?
- Which entity, facility and accredited scope issues the serial-specific
  calibration record?

## Wave 2 — space, hypersonics and advanced sensing

This wave corrects two stale incumbency assumptions:

- **Range Generation Next LLC is no longer the Eastern/Western Range prime.**
  The current Space Force Range Contract was announced to Jacobs Technology
  Inc. and current 2026 records identify Amentum Technology, Inc. on the rail.
- **National Aerospace Solutions, LLC is the predecessor AEDC incumbent.**
  Beyond New Horizons, LLC holds the current TOS II operating contract.

The 10 registry candidates sit below the visible launch and missile primes:

| Control object | Hidden company | Actual role |
|---|---|---|
| hazardous operations and FTS compliance | Metis Technology Partners | inspection and compliance evidence; Space Force retains waiver and launch authority |
| White Sands test execution | Southwest Range Services | staff and test evidence; ATEC retains certification and acceptance |
| wind tunnel and environmental test availability | Beyond New Horizons | operates AEDC facilities; Air Force technical authorities accept results |
| launch-range communications continuity | Full Spectrum Operations | maintains voice, video and data paths; does not give launch permission |
| launch and payload facility readiness | NetCentric Technology | base-facility O&M and engineering; mission attribution still needs a task |
| environmental qualification | National Technical Systems | vibration, thermal and energetic test evidence; performing lab and scope control |
| flight-test data custody | Teletronics / Curtiss-Wright | acquisition, display and analysis support; government owns validity and anomaly disposition |
| energetic and separation hardware | Ensign-Bickford Aerospace & Defense | critical component and lot evidence; target BOM remains missing |
| structural model correlation | ATA Engineering | modal-test and analytical evidence used in flight-clearance decisions |
| modeled public-safety risk | ARCTOS | risk-analysis inputs; current contract continuity remains unresolved |

The principal lesson is that “range access” is not a single award. Hazardous
procedures, facility readiness, FTS compliance, communications, instrumentation,
modeled risk and data custody can be held by different companies before a
government mission director makes the final decision.

## Wave 3 — communications and directed energy

This wave contains the cleanest example of a genuine private formal gate:
**Key Bridge Wireless LLC**, as an FCC-authorized Spectrum Access System
administrator, can assign, restrict or revoke CBRS channel access within its
defined regulatory scope. That is narrower than an FCC license, but more than a
mere advisory report.

Most other companies produce blocking evidence without holding permission:

- **SURVICE** integrates directed-energy test and survivability evidence.
- **MZA Associates** measures atmosphere and predicts target-plane HEL
  performance.
- **Applied Physical Electronics** supplies the pulse environment.
- **Advanced Fiber Sensors** measures what field actually reached the test
  article.
- **Plymouth Grating Laboratory** may cap laser power and schedule through
  grating yield, thermal behavior and damage threshold.
- **NSI-MI Technologies** creates calibrated antenna, radome or RCS measurement
  environments.
- **Advanced Programs** can produce TEMPEST and RED-BLACK evidence, but its
  current NSA facility status must be independently reconfirmed.
- **KSAT Inc.** can commission and operate the ground segment without owning
  mission acceptance or command authority.
- **EN Engineering, LLC operating as ENTRUST Solutions Group** prepares fiber
  route, permit, make-ready and inspection packages; landowners, pole owners and
  agencies grant the rights.

For laser and HPM work, the government range safety officer retains the
permission-to-fire decision. A contractor may calculate hazard distances,
prepare procedures, calibrate sensors or characterize atmosphere; those records
should not be shortened to private launch or firing authority.

## Wave 4 — assurance, minerals, nuclear and fusion

The assurance stack separates the assessor from the authority:

- **Schellman Compliance, LLC** can perform the independent FedRAMP assessment;
  the government certification and workload authorization remain separate.
- **atsec information security corporation** can test a cryptographic module;
  NIST and the Canadian authority validate it, and the certificate applies to
  the module rather than every product that embeds it.
- **Covergent Solutions Inc dba Exiger Government Solutions** can illuminate
  beneficial ownership and supplier risk; the contracting officer or program
  makes the exclusion or mitigation decision.
- **LabVantage Solutions Inc.** can preserve laboratory lineage while **Iron
  Mountain Information Management, LLC** can preserve records custody; neither
  proves the scientific truth of the underlying measurement.

The materials and nuclear side reveals equally consequential permission
objects:

- **SRK Consulting (U.S.), Inc.** prepared the current Mountain Pass reserve,
  metallurgy, mine-plan and economic technical record used by mapped company MP
  Materials. That relationship is real, but it is a company-retained S-K 1300
  object, not a federal Science Strategy assignment.
- **MPR Associates, Inc.** supplies owner-side siting and feasibility analysis;
  **Jensen Hughes, Inc.** supplies PRA, licensing, fire, cyber and risk records.
- **NAC International, Inc.** can supply licensed packaging and transport
  infrastructure, while **EnergySolutions, LLC** can provide treatment and
  disposal capacity. Neither can be assigned to a target before the material or
  waste profile, package, route, task and final receipt exist.

Fusion should not inherit fission claims automatically. Tritium, activation
products, magnets, vacuum systems and future decommissioning may use parts of
the nuclear assurance and waste chain, but the exact material classification,
license, package and disposal route remain system-specific.

## Evidence that promotes a candidate

Capability pages do not promote any row. The useful records are:

1. target BOMs, approved-source lists and long-lead purchase orders;
2. mission-specific range, PSSC, test-facility or instrumentation tasks;
3. signed FAT, SAT, calibration and environmental-qualification reports;
4. approved threat libraries, truth-oracle custody and raw-data terms;
5. fiber, timing, spectrum, TEMPEST and cryptographic assessment packages;
6. owner-engineer, qualified-person, lender or regulator reliance records;
7. radioactive-material characterization, certified package, route and custody
   documents; and
8. final owner, agency or government acceptance—not merely contractor test
   completion.
