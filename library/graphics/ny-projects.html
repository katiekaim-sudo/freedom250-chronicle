Which big New York factory and lab projects are still under construction or only proposed?

Of the 156 sites on the New York map, 12 are under construction or commissioning, 3 have an award but no construction yet and 3 are only proposed or in permitting; the biggest by stated value are Micron White Pine campus (up to $100B), Project Double Reed ($19.46B), Chobani Rome megafactory ($1.2B).

What's still uncertain

  • The map keeps operating, building, pilot, awarded and proposed sites apart; an incentive, permit or power allocation is not production.
  • The editorial review counted 22 under-construction or proposed sites from the old map's filter; the registry has 18 with these three statuses (plus 9 research or pilot sites, not shown). The registry count is used here.
  • Target dates in the scale text (for example 2027 openings) are the companies' own targets.
  • Project-state labels are saved registry classifications. For Chobani Rome, the linked April22,2025 announcement alone does not establish later construction; its saved stage and September4,2026 cutoff remain separate from that source proof.
