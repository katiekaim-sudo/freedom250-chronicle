Governing finding
The next useful company search is not another capability sweep. It is a search
for the instruments that appear when a planned facility becomes controlled
land, a partner becomes a seller, a system becomes accepted, or a failed asset
becomes eligible to return to service.
announcement
-> binding land / capital / utility instrument
-> exact buyer and seller
-> delivery and evidence custody
-> permit / commissioning / claim / bond disposition
-> owner, AHJ, regulator or contracting-officer decision
-> accepted operation
The new registry adds 31 cases across four trigger layers. It does not rank
companies as likely award winners. It ranks the evidence objects that would
allow a company to move from adjacent capability into a defensible target role.
1. Arizona: the hidden object is site control
NIST's public announcement remains unusually precise about what it does not
establish: Natcast accepted a nonbinding letter of intent from ASU and the
Arizona Commerce Authority, while the final site contract remained subject to
diligence and negotiation. The $1.1 billion Natcast operating award funds the
PPF capability; it does not itself execute a lease.

Same-campus false positives
The Materials-to-Fab renovation is real and names JE Dunn Construction
Company and Diamondback Design Group, LLC. The MTW partnership renovation
is also real and names Applied Materials, Inc. in the approved scope. These
are valuable delivery comparators, but neither project is the Natcast PPF. A
shared campus cannot substitute for a target contract.

The Argonne M&O clock also requires care. DOE's July 2026 public table lists
2026-09-30 as the current and ultimate end date for DE-AC02-06CH11357. That live
record supersedes older shorthand that treated the contract as already running
to 2031; a successor or extension action must be captured separately.
3. The acquisition-to-award entity-clock test
The strongest new calibration is the NIST silane-distribution purchase.
CVD Equipment Corporation signed an asset sale for Stainless Design
Concepts on 2026-03-23.
Edwards Semiconductor Solutions LLC acquired the SDC business on
2026-04-01; Atlas Copco placed it in its Semiconductor Equipment division.
NIST changed the intended source after the acquisition.
NIST awarded Edwards Semiconductor Solutions LLC notice
1333ND26PNB680185 on 2026-07-31 for $125,505.
The business being purchased—ultra-high-purity gas delivery, gas cabinets,
panels, valve manifold boxes and liquid chemical delivery—did not disappear,
but the award-date legal seller changed. The case proves why a pre-award brand
or market-research vendor cannot be copied into the final registry without an
entity-clock check. It also requires keeping Edwards Semiconductor Solutions
LLC separate from Edwards Vacuum LLC.
Other 2026 NIST and federal orders—SkyWater CMOS wafers, a Teledyne in-vacuum
EUV camera, a Laserspec mid-IR laser and a Xenturis cleanroom modification—show
how an operating laboratory stack fragments into modest exact purchase orders.
They are current delivery comparators, not Natcast assignments.

Role compression
Several corporate families can occupy multiple steps. Sedgwick owns EFI
Global; HSB combines equipment-breakdown expertise with inspection functions;
FM combines insurance with loss-prevention engineering; Element owns NTS and a
large testing network. This is a structural fact, not evidence of misconduct.
It raises four concrete controls:
identify the exact retaining and reporting entity;
preserve raw data and joint evidence access;
state who may perform destructive testing;
name the independent owner/AHJ/regulator who authorizes return.
What now deserves the closest watch
Executed PPF site-control instrument. This would resolve parcel,
counterparty, improvement owner and likely procurement/AHJ path.
Genesis RFI disposition after 2026-08-21. Watch for a respondent list,
solicitation, capacity agreement, private-finance vehicle or subcontract.
ComEd load/capacity object. A study or service agreement would be much
stronger than another computing partnership announcement.
Solstice/Equinox delivery and IOC dossier. Require exact affiliates,
configuration, utility boundary, security acceptance and user release.
PPF builder's-risk, performance bonds and commissioning contracts. These
can reveal the real construction team before a promotional announcement.
Any loss, default or delay record. The adjuster, surety, forensic expert
and recommissioning firm may expose the project's actual dependency chain.
