The stablecoin law is passed, but the rules aren't done

GENIUS is law but not in force until January 18, 2027; the agencies have proposals, not final rules, and three required reports are missing.

What we found

  • The July 18, 2026 rule deadline passed with proposals only; with no final rule by September 30, January 18, 2027 is the practical start date.
  • The Fed's September 24 proposals would require 2% capital up to $20 billion of coins, redemption within two business days for any holder, and daily 1:1 reserves.
  • A federal committee must vote unanimously to approve a state's stablecoin regime; Florida's own license took effect October 1.
