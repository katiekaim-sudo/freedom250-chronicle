 Layer One

 The Structure — Who Runs the Money

 The United States government is not one thing. It is a consolidated group
 of 397 legal entities — departments, agencies, government-sponsored enterprises,
 trust funds, Reserve Banks — each one founded on a specific date, by a specific act,
 with a specific mandate. Some are older than the republic itself. Some were created last year.

 This layer maps all of them: who they are, when they were born, who owns whom,
 and which ones are doing something right now . Think of it as the org chart that
 the government never published — the one that treats every entity as a living thing
 with a founding date and a developmental clock.

 Layer Two

 The Story — What They Are Doing

 Every day, these entities act. They issue rules, file notices, hold hearings, sign executive
 orders, pass state laws, and build the rails that money will run on for the next century.
 The Chronicle tracks 9,663 events across
 46 plotlines and 159 subplots — sorted into five machines
 that cover the whole terrain:

 The Money Machine tracks federal payment rails — stablecoins, clearing,
 the gate between banks and tech.
 The State Machine tracks the states building their own monetary
 systems (Texas gold, New Hampshire bitcoin bonds, the TXSE).
 The World Machine tracks the same fight playing out across 10 regions globally.
 The Tech Machine tracks government technology policy — AI, quantum, drones, chips.
 And The Federal Group binds them together: every filing maps back to the
 entity that issued it.

 Layer Three

 The Pattern — Why It Rhymes

 The events layer tells you what is happening. The pattern layer asks when 
 it has happened before — and whether it is happening on schedule.

 The Chronicle uses mundane astrology — the oldest timing system
 for collective events — not as prophecy, but as a pattern language. Every monetary break
 in American history lands on a small number of recurring degree-zones. The 1863 National
 Banking Act and the 1913 Federal Reserve Act share the same zodiacal chair to within 0.6°.
 The founding of Social Security, Bretton Woods, and the Nixon Shock all cluster on another.
 These chairs repeat on long planetary cycles — and the next pass is
 November 2026 .

 The astrology layer does not tell you what will happen. It tells you
 what kind of moment this is, measured against 250 years of the same kind of moment.
 Registered in advance. Scored after the fact. Held to the discipline of a
 Forecast Ledger that tracks every pre-registered call.
