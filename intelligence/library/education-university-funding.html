Research summary

The Education corpus shows a multi-entity federal funding junction in which recipient identity, award conditions, servicing, reporting and research-security eligibility increasingly operate before or around payment. It does not establish a government-wide public-blockchain mandate, that every university is under one control regime, or that an audit notice by itself proves misconduct, award termination or a payment hold.
