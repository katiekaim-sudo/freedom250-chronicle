## 2025

- **2025-11-20 — Texas Buys the First State Bitcoin** (TX · State bitcoin reserves). The Comptroller spends about $5 million of the $10 million SB 21 appropriation on BlackRock's IBIT fund, with bitcoin at $91,336 — the first state in the nation to actually fund a strategic bitcoin reserve. A placeholder, they say, until a custodian is hired. New Hampshire passed its law first and bought nothing; Texas passed second and bought first — through BlackRock.
- **2025-10-11 — Newsom Signs the Crypto Welcome Mat** (CA · State crypto laws & bans). The same governor whose regulator built the licensing wall signs AB 1052 and SB 822: crypto is 'valid and legal consideration' for any Californian, cities and counties are barred from taxing or restricting its use as payment, and unclaimed crypto now escheats to the state in kind — making California a bitcoin holder by operation of lost-property law. Wall and welcome mat, same state, same season.
- **2025-09-30 — The SEC Blesses the Texas Stock Exchange** (TX · Exchanges & registries). The SEC approves TXSE as a national securities exchange — the first new major independent US exchange since Nasdaq in 1971. Dallas, $275 million raised, BlackRock and Citadel Securities behind it. Wall Street's landlords are funding the move out of Wall Street.
- **2025-09-03 — Real Shares Go On-Chain** (DE · Exchanges & registries). Galaxy Digital becomes the first public company to tokenize its actual SEC-registered common stock, on Solana, through Superstate's transfer agent — legal ownership recorded on-chain in real time under Delaware's 2017 blockchain-ledger law. Eight years after Delaware wrote the law, somebody finally used it. The securities register going on-chain from below, while the DTC builds the same thing from above.
- **2025-08-06 — New York Fines Paxos $48.5 Million** (NY · State crypto laws & bans). NYDFS settles with Paxos Trust over anti-money-laundering failures in its Binance partnership — $48.5 million. The state cop still swings the stick. What happens five days later shows what the stick is now worth.
- **2025-07-10 — Missouri Is the Eighth Gold State** (MO · Gold & silver as money). Kehoe signs the Constitutional Money Act: gold and silver legal tender, capital-gains exemption, anti-confiscation language. Eighth state in the belt after Utah, Wyoming, Oklahoma, Arkansas, Louisiana, Idaho, and Alabama. The wave is no longer news in the states where it happens — which is the news.
- **2025-06-11 — Louisiana Makes the Gold Debit Card Legal Tender** (LA · Gold & silver as money). Landry signs Act 304: gold and silver specie and gold-backed debit instruments are Louisiana legal tender, with authority to use another state's bullion depository — Texas's, by name. It passed 84-0 and 39-0. Nobody was watching the quietest second gold state in the nation.
- **2025-05-15 — The Pioneer Sells Its Bitcoin** (WI · State bitcoin reserves). Wisconsin's investment board — the first state pension into spot bitcoin ETFs — discloses it dumped its entire stake, some $300 million of BlackRock's IBIT, in the first quarter. Days before the tariff shock, as it turned out. The first state in was the first state out, right as the reserve laws passed everywhere else.
- **2025-05-07 — Arizona Takes the Side Door** (AZ · State bitcoin reserves). Four days after vetoing the real bitcoin-reserve bill as 'untested,' Hobbs signs HB 2749: a digital-asset reserve built from unclaimed property — abandoned crypto and its staking rewards, no taxpayer money. The second state reserve framework in the nation, built from lost coins. The bigger bill is back on her desk this summer, with reelection in November.
- **2025-05-06 — New Hampshire Passes the First Bitcoin Reserve Law** (NH · State bitcoin reserves). Ayotte signs HB 302 — the first state law in the nation letting the treasurer put public money into bitcoin, up to five percent of funds, assets over $500 billion market cap only. The treasurer's answer the next day: no plans to buy. Fourteen months later she still hasn't. First to pass, last to act — the law was the point.
- **2025-05-05 — Montana Bans the Fed Coin and Invents the Network Token** (MT · State crypto laws & bans). Gianforte signs SB 265, the Financial Freedom and Innovation Act: a CBDC ban, self-custody and mining protections, and the nation's first 'network token' framework — state certification for tokens under a $250 million sales cap. The bitcoin reserve died on the House floor in February; this is what Montana built instead.
