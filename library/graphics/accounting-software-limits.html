Freedom 250 · Innovation · Accounting

Which accounting jobs can software do, and which still need a person?

Of the 16 accounting jobs in the library's map, software working from reliable event records can do 6 strongly: identity; authority; settlement; internal control; reconciliation; correction and recovery. Three more are strong once a condition holds: classification, when facts are complete; allocation, for fixed rules; reporting and disclosure, after judgments. Six can be done only partly: entity and perimeter; occurrence / existence; rights and obligations; recognition; close and consolidation; assurance. Measurement is mixed. The map's rule of thumb: the closer a function is to identity, authorization, arithmetic, sequencing and reconciliation, the more programmable it is. The closer it is to economic substance, legal rights, uncertain measurement and materiality, the more judgment remains.

What's still uncertain

  • This is the library's own analytical map, not a ruling by FASB, the PCAOB or the SEC.
  • Standards checked through September 28, 2026: None supplies a general real-time GAAP or continuous-public-assurance regime.
  • Continuous monitoring is management activity, not independent assurance.
  • A smart contract does not know GAAP.
