Short answer

At 10:05:45 a.m. EDT on July 29, the White House posted, “The truth. COVID.GOV 👀.” COVID.gov redirects to the White House page Lab Leak: The True Origins of Covid-19. That page was already public and had already been promoted by the White House on April 18, 2025. The July 29 post therefore reactivated an existing—now extended—executive narrative surface during the Senate hearing for Anthony Fauci; it did not itself publish a new scientific study, intelligence assessment, declassification, legal instrument or adjudication.

The page principally repackages findings from the Republican-led House Select Subcommittee on the Coronavirus Pandemic's December 2024 final report. Its current version also contains at least two later additions: a statement that DOJ opened an EcoHealth investigation and a later Senate committee video. The page does not link a primary DOJ object for that investigation claim. The committee minority's own official final report disagreed with several of the majority's conclusions and said the origin remained unknown. Those are different institutional findings, not a resolved scientific or judicial state.
