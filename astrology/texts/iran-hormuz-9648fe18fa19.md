# Iran, Hormuz and the oil routes

*How is pressure on Iran playing out through sanctions, shipping, oil and force?*

## The braid so far

Pressure on Iran runs through sanctions, banks, shipping chokepoints, oil routes, Iraqi intermediaries and military logistics. The factual map keeps a designation, blocked account, announced agreement, financed route, cargo movement and use of force separate.

In this reading, **Mars–Pluto** is the lead strand: force approaching a face-to-face test with concentrated power. The image fits route closure, seizure, deterrence and survival without forecasting which literal act will embody it. **Mars–Neptune** is a companion strand. Its trine can align action with a coherent strategy; it can also let sea, oil, secrecy and image blur the difference among threat, operation and result.

**Saturn–Neptune** is background: an inherited security and sanctions order is being reseeded, but the pair has no direct major dialogue in this window. **Jupiter–Pluto** remains unresolved. Financial pressure can change behavior, enlarge underground routes or magnify enforcement claims; the current geometry and factual record do not decide which.

The constructive pole is a legible route constitution with clear authority, proportional force, protected commerce and an accountable remedy. The shadow pole is escalating control whose factual route, loss and end state disappear behind strategic language. The evidence test is vessel, cargo, payment, blocked property, military order, actual route use, loss and settlement—not rhetoric or proximity.
