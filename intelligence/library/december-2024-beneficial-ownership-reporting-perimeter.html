December 2024 Beneficial Ownership Reporting Perimeter

How did the December 2024 interruption of BOI reporting mature into the foreign-entity-focused federal reporting perimeter in force by September 2026?

Research summary

The December 3, 2024 nationwide injunction interrupted FinCEN's broad domestic-and-foreign BOI reporting rule but did not permanently repeal the CTA. After several judicial posture changes, Treasury suspended domestic enforcement and FinCEN used separate exemption authority to narrow the rule to qualifying foreign entities. FinCEN adopted that foreign-entity-focused perimeter as final on August 14, 2026, with additional U.S.-person exemptions.
