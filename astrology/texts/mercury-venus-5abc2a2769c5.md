# Mercury–Venus

## The planets

**Mercury** — *Communication, intellect, commerce, transport.* MUNDANE: the media; the transportation and communications infrastructure; trade and commerce; education and intellectual life; the postal system and telecommunications. Mercury's condition shows how information flows through the nation — freely or obstructed, truthfully or deceptively.

**Venus** — *Attraction, harmony, pleasure, value.* MUNDANE: the economy (especially consumer spending, luxury goods, and entertainment); the arts; women's issues and social welfare; diplomacy and friendly foreign relations; tourism; national morale and the public's sense of contentment.

## The combination — mundane

Under helpful aspects (conjunction, sextile, trine), Mercury-Venus is "mildly favorable for national peace, contentment; beneficial for prosperity, decreased unemployment; favorable for women's issues," as Greer describes. Trade negotiations proceed smoothly. Economic messaging from the government is well-received. The media covers culture, the arts, and human interest stories rather than conflict. Unemployment ticks down. Consumer sentiment improves. Women's issues gain favorable media attention. Diplomatic communications are warm and productive. This is a gentle, pleasant influence — nothing dramatic, but the nation feels a little more at ease.

Under hostile aspects (square, opposition), the effect is "mildly unfavorable; existing grievances remain; warns of troubled economic conditions." Trade talks stall or produce unsatisfying results. Economic messaging falls flat — the public is not reassured. Media coverage turns sour on cultural or social topics. Unemployment holds steady or worsens slightly. Existing economic grievances are not resolved and may be amplified by media attention. The mildness of this hostile aspect is notable — Mercury-Venus friction rarely produces crises, but it does produce a nagging sense that the economy is not working for people and the national conversation is not addressing it.

**As trigger:** Mercury-Venus aspects are fast-moving (days to about a week) and mark brief windows when trade sentiment, cultural communication, and economic mood activate whatever broader outer-planet storylines are in play.
