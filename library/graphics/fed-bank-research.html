Freedom 250 · Banks and the dollar · The Fed

What is each Reserve Bank studying in 2026?

Each Reserve Bank's 2026 conference records cover a different mix of topics. Across the six themes the library tracks, AI adoption and agentic controls is the widest (8 Banks: Boston, Cleveland, Richmond, Minneapolis, Dallas, San Francisco, New York, St. Louis); digital money, clearing and finality has 6; labor flows and regional distribution has 5; data governance and authority has 4; fraud, cyber and operational resilience has 4; power and physical infrastructure has 3 (Dallas, Minneapolis, San Francisco). Philadelphia falls under none of the six: its records are on mortgage transmission and intermediation. A conference topic shows what a Bank is paying attention to, not that it owns the function or has adopted a policy.

What's still uncertain

  • A conference topic is evidence of attention, inquiry or regional sensing. It is not proof that the host Bank owns the function, adopted a policy, funded a program or placed a design into operation.
  • Another reading: Conference specialization may reflect ordinary regional research comparative advantage and public engagement rather than a coordinated institutional refactoring program.
  • This reading weakens if: This reading weakens if conference themes do not recur in later official research, governance, supervision or operations, or if the apparent Bank differences disappear under a complete event census.
