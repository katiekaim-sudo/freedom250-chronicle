# Federal Science Operating System

**Evidence cutoff:** 2026-08-17
**Status:** pending workbench package; the NSSTS full-reading is selectively incorporated into the live vault
**Scope:** the federal funding, infrastructure, workforce, data, experimental and transaction architecture proposed for an AI-native scientific-industrial economy

## Short answer

The July 21, 2026 White House report does not itself create a new legal system. It supplies the design doctrine for reorganizing American science around individuals, mission portfolios, shared compute and laboratories, AI agents, industrial translation and new reward mechanisms.

The architecture becomes more concrete when four distinct objects are read together:

1. **`Science: A New Golden Age`** — a presidential advisory report and design map.
2. **The National Security Science & Technology Strategy** — the mission-selection, resilience and protection layer supporting the 2025 National Security Strategy.
3. **NSTM-5 / OMB M-26-16** — FY2028 budget guidance plus 90-day implementation-plan instructions for agencies meeting the stated R&D threshold.
4. **Executive Order 14363** — the operative charter for the Genesis Mission and its American Science and Security Platform.

The NSSTS makes the system explicitly national-security-facing without merging
these instruments. It defines a `focused, resilient, agile, secure` posture and
promises later OSTP/NSC technology-specific plans. It does not itself supply
funding, agency owners, deadlines, metrics or operating capability.

Existing education, grant-oversight, energy-emergency, data-center permitting, grid-mobilization and tribal-development authorities form adjacent implementation lanes. They do not merge into one blanket authority.

DOE's finalized Fusion Science and Technology Roadmap supplies the first
sector-specific vertical in this package. It joins appropriated research,
national laboratories, public-private milestone programs, AI/HPC and digital
twins, facilities, supply chains, regulation and an eventual grid-delivery
target. DOE's August 15 `Energy Without Limits` thread is a communications
activation of that June 9 roadmap, not a new scientific result.

The project/capital/regulatory registry resolves the roadmap into named
machines, sites and instruments. It shows that the eight Milestone companies
are one federal funding cohort, not eight equivalent U.S. power plants, and
tracks each object from agreement and obligation through hardware, licensing,
commercial contracts and the still-unmet net-electricity and grid gates.

The fusion date/time/place ledger preserves the physical, publication, legal,
effective and future-target clocks separately for later astrology. It records
exact times, timezones and loci when primary evidence supports them, preserves
unknowns when it does not and applies the live one-capital D.C. chart convention
only in a separate chart-readiness overlay.

## The three control surfaces

| Control surface | What is being made portable or callable | Controlling gates |
|---|---|---|
| **Capital follows capability** | fellowships, grants, prizes, compute access, research placements | appropriation, agency authority, award terms, metrics, accreditation or Title IV where applicable |
| **Speed-to-power** | land, generation, transmission, substations, data centers, user facilities | site control, tribal or landowner consent, interconnection, permits, financing, environmental and cultural review |
| **Scientific rails** | identity, data, models, instruments, experiments, provenance, credit, IP and rewards | platform rules, access authorization, cybersecurity, export controls, authoritative records, appropriations, payment law |

The NSSTS adds a **national-security membrane** around all three surfaces:
research security, participant and capital screening, export and data controls,
supply-chain assurance, cryptography and trusted-partner rules. The membrane is
strategy until a named legal or technical implementation assigns access,
restriction, monitoring or mitigation to a specific object.

## Core boundary

> The federal government is building an operating environment in which scientific capacity can be discovered, accessed, measured and coordinated more like a platform. The record does not yet establish a mature federal blockchain, a federal scientific token, unrestricted AI-agent spending, blanket preemption of opposition, or the elimination of universities.

## Package joins

- **Sovereign Compute:** land, power, tribal counterparties, large-load integration and project clocks.
- **Emergency Monetary Policy:** authority-specific activation; no single emergency switch.
- **Fintech and Private Rails:** wallets, programmable payments, identity and settlement remain separate legal objects.
- **Federal Cryptographic Trust Stack:**
  Genesis identity, provenance and PQC bridge
  preserves the operating
  Globus/ModCon identity and provenance rail, the DOE-published cryptographic
  attestation proposal, adjacent PQC mandates and the missing system-level join.
- **Scientific Trust Stack:**
  [`architecture and evidence map`](../sources/scientific-trust-stack-architecture-and-evidence-map-2026-08-17-b9b86dfc5e76.html)
  joins that cryptographic base to research-security disclosures, human and
  agent authorization, scientific provenance, authority-specific capital and
  partner gates, continuing eligibility, correction and appeal. Its governing
  object is a typed, scoped and expiring eligibility receipt—never a permanent
  `trusted: true` flag.
- **Military Modernization:** national-security compute, test ranges, autonomous systems and industrial mobilization.
- **Insurance and Reinsurance:** physical, cyber, performance and liability risk for autonomous laboratories and critical infrastructure.
- **Education:** researchers and credentials become more portable while institutional funding becomes more performance- and mission-sensitive.
- **Energy / fusion:** the roadmap instantiates capital, physical infrastructure
  and scientific rails in one commercialization chain; route official fusion
  events to `Energy dominance & the nuclear revival` and use Technology/AI only
  when compute, models, data, digital twins or autonomous control are the object.
