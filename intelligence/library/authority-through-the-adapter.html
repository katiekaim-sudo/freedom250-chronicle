Research summary

The strongest selected cases support constitutional refactoring: transaction execution, interfaces and data movement can become more distributed while claim constitution, authoritative records, collateral eligibility, final liquidity, correction, emergency authority and loss allocation remain with or become more explicitly embedded in states, regulated issuers, central banks, market utilities and courts.
