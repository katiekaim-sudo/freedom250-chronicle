DeFi Dollar Constitutions — Base Coin × Yield Layer × GENIUS Perimeter

Sixteen systems showing who owes the dollar, what stabilizes it and who can change the rules

A one-dollar target does not create one legal category. Protocol dollars, synthetic dollars, payment coins, vault receipts and tokenized securities can share a wallet while carrying different issuers, collateral, control powers and regulatory paths.

Synthetic dollarHedges, venues and custody replace a narrow reserve.
