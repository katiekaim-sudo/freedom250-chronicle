Crypto around the world: many licenses, no proven cross-border use

Across 22 jurisdictions, we found no verified cross-border payment route that depends on crypto from end to end. The token layer still settles through ordinary banks and national payment systems.

What we found

  • Singapore's Paxos USDG is the strongest live stablecoin found.
  • China's e-CNY balances became interest-bearing bank deposits under e-CNY 2.0.
  • 71.7% of Brazil's declared crypto trades were stablecoins.
