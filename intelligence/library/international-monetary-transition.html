Research summary

Reserve diversification, regional settlement and trade invoicing remain separate from actual monetary substitution. Bessent's April 2026 IIF scorecard adds the incumbent strategy and a three-way legitimacy contest. The IMF symmetry audit finds candid surveillance of systemic members but operationalized, finance-linked adjustment for borrowers. The July 31 Japan–Korea cases confirm two pricing perimeters: national authorities and dealers make the wholesale FX event, while a stablecoin exchange supplies a separate dollar-linked quote. Korea's observed token price transmission is not net capital flow; Japan's limited production rails are not shown to have supported intervention. The September 1 Asheville G20 Chair's Statement adds agreed work on digital assets and payment modernization, but China expressly objected to four paragraphs and the statement itself is not implementation or monetary substitution.
