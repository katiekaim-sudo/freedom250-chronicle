SEC-registered clearing agency

Clear trades and manage obligations

Legal basis: Exchange Act §17A; registration and applicable clearing rules

When it applies: The entity performs securities clearing-agency functions.

Named firms: National Securities Clearing Corporation (registered or chartered); Fixed Income Clearing Corporation (registered or chartered); The Depository Trust Company (registered or chartered)

Who can use it: Clearing agencies registered with the SEC for the relevant services

ConditionsRegistration must cover the entity and function. Identify CCP or depository service, asset and risk framework. Membership and transaction acceptance remain additional gates.

The limitDTC’s registration does not make it the CCP for every service; the function must be identified.

Derivatives clearing organization

Clear trades and manage obligations

Legal basis: CEA §5b and applicable CFTC rules

When it applies: The activity falls within the CEA derivatives-clearing function.

Named firms: Chicago Mercantile Exchange, Inc. (registered or chartered); ICE Clear Credit LLC (registered or chartered)

Who can use it: Derivatives clearing organizations with the required registration or specific applicable relief

ConditionsClassify the contract and clearing activity. Establish DCO registration or applicable relief and scope. Review membership, margin and default terms.
