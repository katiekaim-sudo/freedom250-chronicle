Mars–Uranus Cycle Review

Enduring question: How does action become rupture, strike, invention, or uncontrolled release?
Disposition: consistently high focal separation; valuable for distinguishing breakthrough from physical deployment.

Family record

  • 2024 Taurus family: candidate +305.384 days.
  • 2026 Gemini family: candidate +325.031 days.
  • 2028: first-after bracket.

What the charts add

The 2024 seed joins detrimented Mars and Uranus in Taurus/9th. The candidate moves Mars into Leo/10th while Uranus remains Taurus/7th. Public action and disruptive counterpart/technology become distinct.

The 2026 seed joins both in Gemini/2nd and is woven tightly to Neptune and Pluto. The candidate separates Mars into Virgo/7th and Uranus into Gemini/4th, changes multiple circuits to one, and introduces a new unstable-Yod structure. The Persona Sun remains with Uranus while Mars squares both the Sun and Uranus.

Narrowing use

For AI, this is the move from disruptive information capability to physical deployment, operations, or contested counterpart. For war, it distinguishes sudden technology/rupture from the actor executing force. It predicts neither a strike nor an accident.
