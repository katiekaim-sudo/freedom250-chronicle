2026-11-09 Scorpio New Moon — A Reading

This New Moon begins in the part of the chart where information becomes consequential. The Scorpio lights gather in the 3rd house with Mercury retrograde: records, testimony, messages, local systems, and the terms by which one fact is connected to another. The Moon is in fall but held closely by the Sun, so feeling is concentrated inside the act of naming. This can seed a disciplined inquiry. It can also make the account so intent on penetrating the surface that suspicion begins substituting for proof.

Mercury rules the Virgo Ascendant and therefore the chart’s outward method. Retrograde in Scorpio, it returns to material that was incomplete, private, buried, or badly understood. Its applying square to Pluto insists that the revision is not trivial. Words can expose power, and power can narrow what may be said. The best expression is an account strong enough to revise itself when the evidence changes. The harder expression is a contest over the record in which each correction is treated as capture.

Venus is the chart’s strongest stabilizer: retrograde but domiciled in Libra in the 2nd house. Value, proportion, and reciprocity can be recovered and made concrete. Her applying sextiles to both Mars and Jupiter create a route from a careful valuation to coordinated action and meaningful support. Yet Mars and Jupiter are conjunct in Leo in the hidden 12th. Confidence, force, and institutional backing can grow outside the visible frame. That concealed concentration may protect an important effort; it may also inflate an action before the public knows what is being authorized.

The New Moon squares both the governing ingress Moon and Jupiter in the 11th and 5th. A message or body of evidence can interrupt the season’s collective story and its confident performance. This is not automatically a debunking, nor automatically a confirmation. It is a demand that the public claim answer to the recovered particulars. Because the chart’s root ultimately settles through a strong Venus, the most reliable test is proportion: do the stated terms match the actual exchange, obligation, and benefit?

The seed is therefore investigative but not merely adversarial. Something can be repaired by returning to the record and pricing its consequences honestly. Something can also be driven underground if force is allowed to define truth. Watch who can amend the account, which evidence survives scrutiny from more than one side, and whether the resulting action remains accountable to the value it claims to protect.

Timing

The inquiry opens November 9 and culminates at the November 24 Gemini Full Moon, when Uranus joins the Moon and the informational field becomes much harder to contain. Retrogrades make revision part of the process; allow the record to develop before treating its first form as final.
