# Mundane Organism — Circular Reading Build Plan

**Saved:** 2026-08-11  
**Status:** approved working direction; implementation has not begun  
**Authority:** this is a workbench plan, subordinate to Katie's current rulings
and the live Freedom 250 vault  
**Companion:** `MUNDANE_ORGANISM_BUILD_HANDOFF.md`

## Decision

Preserve the donor organism's whole → part → whole → part reading rhythm.
Simplify natal-specific content and visible navigation, not the owner discipline
that keeps the astrology intact.

Preserve motion as the temporal invariant. Planets retain their archetypal
motivations without becoming fixed plot assignments; relationships carry arcs.
A chart freezes one state of an arc, while the organism must allow Katie to
move backward and forward through its ordered history without pretending that
retrogradation, repeated exactness, or an ingress boundary erases lived time.

The computer architecture must distinguish two very different kinds of
circularity:

```text
Astrological topology and navigation
    may be circular and reversible.

Facts, authority, permissions and invalidation
    must remain acyclic, versioned and fail-closed.
```

This permits repeated exploration of the same complete chart without allowing
the loop itself to manufacture facts, meaning, Evidence, prominence or
Judgment.

## The reading spiral

```text
Period whole
↕
Chart whole
↔ intact part in context
↔ current relationship state and its prior/next chapters
↔ local relationships and routes
↔ provisional understanding inside the same whole
↔ another useful part or scale
↔ same chart whole
↕
Same period whole
```

Every movement retains the same canonical identities:

- `period_ref`, where applicable;
- `chart_ref`;
- `snapshot_ref`;
- `house_frame_ref`;
- selected `subject_ref`;
- ordered `traversal_stack`.

Focus changes projection and visibility only. It does not change chart scope,
facts, relationship counts, owner authority, Evidence state or the current
permission boundary.

## Three maps that must remain separate

### 1. Astrological topology

Planets, Signs, Houses, relationships, figures, axes, ruler routes, dispositor
loops, phases, continuous pair histories and the whole chart coexist in one
field. This topology may be cyclic.

### 2. Navigation

Katie may move freely among the whole chart, one focused actor, its Sign
equipment, House arena, relationships, governance routes, symbolic lenses and
back to the whole. Navigation doors carry focus and context, never meaning or
Evidence.

### 3. Authority and dependency

```text
verified inputs
→ immutable facts
→ governed semantic owners
→ chart-specific contributions
→ whole-chart structure
→ candidate claims
→ Evidence and counterevidence
→ Katie Judgment
→ Translation
→ optional separate Forecast
```

This route is one-directional. Judgment may request another inspection but may
not rewrite facts, doctrine or the canonical snapshot.

## What remains structurally separate

Keep these distinctions even when the interface groups them behind fewer
doors:

- chart/period identity and exact clock authority;
- immutable coordinates and one Whole Sign house frame;
- exact angles as points rather than substitutes for complete Houses;
- mundane Planet functions;
- modality, element and unique-Sign records;
- mundane House domains;
- canonical Aspect relationships and whole Aspect web;
- stable planetary-pair identity, time-indexed relationship state, seed-family
  and pass identity, and ordered relationship history;
- condition and essential dignity;
- traditional rulership, House governance and dispositors;
- whole-chart structure;
- symbolic and specialist extensions;
- Evidence, Judgment, Translation and Forecast permissions;
- technical proof, provenance and unresolved states.

These layers answer different questions. Combining them would make the
interface shorter while making the astrology less coherent.

## What can be simplified or deferred

Remove or defer donor material whose job is specifically natal or personal:

- personal-development and counseling questions;
- natal personality wording;
- personal ASC/DSC psychological semantics;
- natal Moon-state meaning unless independently adopted for mundane work;
- founder/client voice machinery;
- personal timing techniques not separately governed for mundane use;
- any donor room that exists only to support an individual-person reading.

The first visible Mundane Chart Room can group the remaining depth behind seven
primary doors:

1. Whole chart
2. Civic actors — Planets
3. Operating fields — Signs
4. National arenas — Houses
5. Relationships — current dialogue and arc through time
6. Governance and condition
7. Symbols and specialist layers

The deeper owner rooms remain inspectable through progressive disclosure.

## Build sequence

### Step 1 — Lock six structural contracts

Before application implementation, govern:

1. **Chart identity** — target, event, clock, locality, technique, snapshot and
   house frame.
2. **Relationship-time identity** — stable pair identity; exact-instant,
   coordinate/ephemeris-frame and engine-version state identity; seed-family
   and exact-pass identity; ordered chronology; the five-Aspect direct-dialogue
   policy; chart-specific DR-020 phase-narration admission; separate seed-echo
   projection; and chart/period reference behavior.
3. **Period identity** — bounded period, governing ingress, referenced member
   charts, and referenced slices of relationship histories that began before or
   continue beyond the period.
4. **Circular navigation** — chart, selected part, local neighborhood,
   traversal stack and return-to-whole behavior.
5. **Owner contract** — separate ownership of facts, meanings, contributions,
   Judgment and Translation.
6. **Extension contract** — the controlled entry route for Sabians, midpoints,
   nodes, declination, 916 America and later layers.

This step locks schemas and permissions. It does not require final prose for
all thirty-four core semantic records.

### Step 2 — Build a semantic-closed skeleton

Create the mundane Lobby, Chart Room, Period Weather Room and Organism map with
the complete intended room roster visible but semantic execution closed.

Prove these navigation loops with identity-only or factual placeholders:

```text
Whole → Planet → Sign → House → Aspect → Whole
Whole → House → ruler/governance → Planet → Whole
Whole → Aspect → both actors → whole web → Whole
Whole → relationship state → prior/next chapters → both actors → Whole
Period → ingress → lunation → focused part → chart whole → Period
Period → relationship-clock slice → chart state → same relationship history → Period
```

Every unavailable meaning displays `provisional`, `unresolved` or `unbuilt`.
Nothing falls back to natal wording.

### Step 3 — Build core semantics in thin complete passes

#### Pass A — structural roster

Create lightweight governed records for all ten Planets, twelve Signs and
twelve Houses. Every record receives provenance, lifecycle state, dependencies
and Katie disposition even when its semantic prose remains provisional.

#### Pass B — Sign grammar and dignity

Implement the current working construction:

```text
Modality supplies the movement.
Element shapes and carries the movement.
The unique Sign is the irreducible collective expression of both.
Dignity describes the Planet's condition within that operating environment.
```

Build three modality records, four element records, twelve distinct Sign
foundations and the dignity-condition interface together. Modality and element
constrain a Sign but do not generate its complete meaning.

#### Pass C — mundane Planets

Author each Planet independently as a collective motivation, civic actor or
function, institutional expression, gift, shadow and polarity. Archetypal
continuity may be source evidence; natal owner wording is not inherited.

#### Pass D — mundane Houses

Author the twelve national and institutional arenas, including their axes,
families, governance boundaries and exact-angle distinction. Keep derivative
routes dependent on the twelve radical House owners rather than creating 144
new authorities.

#### Pass E — shared relationships and structural routes

Connect the adopted Aspect operators, applying/separating facts, whole-web
patterns, and the continuous relationship-time contract: stable pair identity,
exact snapshot state, seed-family/pass identity, and ordered transitions. Then
connect conditions, rulership, House governance and dispositors. None of these
facts assigns final importance or fixed plot ownership by itself.

### Step 4 — Prove one complete chart spiral

Run one exact mundane chart through:

```text
chart whole
→ one actor
→ Sign equipment
→ House arena
→ condition and dignity
→ current relationships
→ one relationship's phase, motion and accumulated history
→ governance and root
→ return to same whole
→ inspect another useful part
```

The return-to-whole surface may maintain a separate provisional synthesis
workspace showing:

- what the focused part contributed;
- what tension or ambiguity remains;
- which other part may deserve inspection;
- what the organism cannot yet conclude.

This synthesis references facts and semantic owners. It never writes backward
into either one.

### Step 5 — Add the extension framework

Use four extension families:

| Extension family | Examples | Job |
|---|---|---|
| Factual geometry | declination, midpoints, planetary nodes, 916 America | Add separately owned computed facts |
| Symbolic lenses | Sabian symbols | Add optional symbolic imagery |
| Timing adapters | returns, progressions, Moon Families, dignity and other non-pair phase clocks | Place the chart in a governed time system; planetary relationship time remains core structure rather than an optional decoration |
| Evidence bridges | Chronicle events and observed outcomes | Reference independent real-world records |

Every extension declares:

- `extension_id` and version;
- extension family;
- eligible chart types and points;
- factual input references;
- canonical source and semantic owner;
- admission and narration rules;
- Evidence, prominence and Forecast credit;
- invalidation dependencies;
- interface projection;
- unavailable and unresolved behavior.

An extension may enrich the reading without redefining Planet, Sign, House or
Aspect.

### Step 6 — Use Sabian symbols as the first extension

Sabians are the ideal extension test because the factual lookup and symbol
corpus already exist while their interpretive permission must stay bounded.

The mundane Sabian extension must:

- read the canonical 360 symbols from the Freedom 250 method workbook;
- use the governed degree-band lookup
  `symbol number = floor(degrees within sign) + 1`;
- preserve exact longitude and never round to the visually nearest degree;
- keep symbol, keynote, poles and provenance in its own dossier;
- appear as an optional symbolic lens for an eligible selected point;
- enter synthesis only after technical chart structure is understood;
- carry no automatic Evidence, prominence, convergence or Forecast credit;
- respect DR-023: no Sabian analysis for quarter moons, and exact syzygy is
  required for New Moon, Full Moon and eclipse degree work.

Do not mint a competing JSON symbol corpus. The workbook owns the atomic
lookup; the prose method owns how and when the symbols may be used.

The donor's focused-Sabian interface can be adapted as a visual reference: a
full zodiac field preserving exact angular position plus a synchronized point
inspector. Its natal payload and permissions do not cross automatically.

### Step 7 — Add the thin Period Weather loop

The first period container requires only:

Eclipses, Moon Families, era context, national/entity overlays, institutional
clocks, Chronicle events and saved-period libraries enter later as separately
gated adapters. The first period release does not wait for every timing layer.

After the circular exploration and core owners are stable:

3. account for counterevidence;
4. return to the whole chart or period;
5. send a complete candidate to Katie for `keep / revise / reject / hold`;
6. produce the symbolic mundane reading;
7. produce concrete civic Translation;
8. optionally register a separate falsifiable Forecast Ledger call.

## Thin-spiral delivery strategy

Do not build one enormous subsystem to completion before the rest of the chart
exists. Deliver several complete but increasingly deep passes:

```text
all rooms as truthful placeholders
→ one complete factual chart
→ one complete whole-part-whole loop
→ core semantic breadth
→ semantic depth
→ one symbolic extension
→ one governing period
→ Judgment and Translation
→ Observatory integration
```

Visible concentric rings and rooms are navigation, not construction order.
Construction proceeds base-to-derived through durable owners and several thin
full passes.

## Acceptance tests for circular reading

The circular architecture is ready only when all of these hold:

- returning from a part restores the exact same chart and snapshot;
- the traversal stack preserves the route without duplicating meaning;
- selecting a different part changes visibility, not facts or counts;
- the whole view can show a new provisional synthesis without mutating owners;
- a ruler loop or Aspect circuit cannot become a permission or invalidation
  loop;
- one relationship has one canonical identity across all projections;
- co-temporal charts may reference one astronomical relationship state while
  retaining distinct chart, snapshot, locality, and house-frame identities;
- co-temporal charts may make different DR-020 phase-narration decisions while
  sharing that state; direct geometry and seed echoes remain separate lanes;
- the whole-chart Aspect web may retain quincunx without admitting it to the
  five-Aspect relationship-clock direct-dialogue roster;
- a retrograde return or repeated perfection receives a new state/pass identity
  while preserving the same pair and seed-family history;
- Aries resets the mundane year and non-Aries succession follows the incumbent
  rising-modality validity rule; neither kind of boundary resets the
  relationship arc;
- no planet or pair becomes the permanent owner of a plot;
- no focused part becomes a miniature chart or independent Evidence vote;
- an optional extension can be disabled without damaging the core chart;
- unavailable extension material fails closed;
- technical proof remains available without leading the reading experience.
