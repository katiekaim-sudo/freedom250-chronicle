Which crypto networks and companies would the Fed'S stablecoin rules reach, and how?

The Federal Reserve Board's two GENIUS Act proposals of September 24, 2026 reach the 20 crypto networks and companies the library maps in four different ways: 2 would face them directly as stablecoin issuers (Ripple / XRPL and Circle / USDC / CPN / Arc / Tazapay); 4 as custody, distribution or payout services (Securitize, Cash legs & tokenized bank money, Coinbase / Base / Tokenize and SWIFT ledger & bank obligations); 12 as technical systems an issuer would run on; and 2 sit outside the direct issuer rules (Bitcoin and DTC / DTCC tokenization). The proposals are not final, and they approve no company, network or token.

What's still uncertain

  • Every row is a proposal-overlap finding only. It does not establish that an ecosystem is a permitted payment stablecoin issuer, an eligible reserve asset, an approved custodian, a Board-approved subsidiary, compliant with a final rule or operating in a Board-supervised lane.
  • Two Board proposals; not final or operative at the saved cutoff.
  • This worked case is a proposed legal architecture. It does not establish a current Board-approved issuer, open application lane, eligible tokenized reserve, final capital requirement or production operating receipt.
  • Comment window: Awaiting exact calendar deadline. The release date alone does not fix the due date.
