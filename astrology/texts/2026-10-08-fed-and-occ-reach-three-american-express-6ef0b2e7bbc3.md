# Fed and OCC reach three American Express companies

## The story (research side — facts only)

On **October 8**, the Federal Reserve issued a consent cease-and-desist order to American Express Company and American Express Travel Related Services Company. The order names American Express National Bank as the service company's subsidiary national bank. The Fed supervises the two holding companies. The OCC supervises the bank on its own. Within 90 days the Fed wants board-oversight, enterprise BSA/AML, and OFAC-compliance plans, plus quarterly progress reports. The OCC wants an independent-majority compliance committee within 15 days and a 90-day action plan covering customer due diligence, suspicious-activity monitoring, audit, training, and staffing. The OCC also imposed a **$350 million** civil penalty payable to Treasury. It found the bank failed to timely identify, evaluate, and sufficiently report about **$13 billion** of suspected trade-based money-laundering activity from June 2014 through May 2025. That figure is processed suspected activity, not a bank loss, a government recovery, or proven criminal proceeds. The orders are live supervisory consequences. Completed remediation is still ahead. Source: `Research Packages/Federal Government Weekly Delta 2026-10-12/waves/WAVE_1_FINANCIAL_CONTROL_STACK.md`.

The same October 3–12 spine records two other control moves that are not the same act. On October 8, OFAC designated additional vessels, owners, and facilitators under EO 13902 as part of Operation Economic Outcast. A designation is a list change. It does not itself prove a vessel stopped or a payment failed (`waves/WAVE_9_FOREIGN_AFFAIRS_SANCTIONS_AID.md`). In that same window, USTR invoked the USMCA Rapid Response Labor Mechanism for GLM Components in Mexico and directed Treasury to suspend liquidation of unliquidated entries from the facility. Mexico has a 10-day acceptance clock and, if it accepts, 45 days to review. Invocation and a customs suspension are process states, not a final denial-of-rights finding or a tariff (`WAVE_10_MERGE.md`; `waves/WAVE_9_FOREIGN_AFFAIRS_SANCTIONS_AID.md`).
