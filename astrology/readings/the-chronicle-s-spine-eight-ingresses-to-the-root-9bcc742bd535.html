The Chronicle's Spine — Eight Ingresses to the Root

Read down the line, the eight cardinal ingresses are one story of a root that builds, settles, and then lets go. Here is each season taken to its gravity-well. Sister note (same sequence, not a second canon): The Moving Root — Ingresses 2025–2026.

2025 Aries — the Sun eats the center

Aquarius rising, fixed — the master chart of the whole opening year. The dispositor loop (Mars→Moon→Jupiter→Mercury) has no anchor, and that is the tell: there is no strong planet at the root because one star has devoured the room. The Sun, exalted at the Aries Point, sits fused to Neptune (0.4°) with four planets combust around it — the press (Mercury), value (Venus), the establishment (Saturn) and the dream (Neptune) all burnt up under its beams. The executive doesn't answer to the root; it has absorbed it. Read the motivation: a Sun that wants to be everything — to pull the message, the money, the structure and the ideal into one body. Both poles: a galvanizing leader who unifies a scattered nation, or a consuming one who leaves nothing standing on its own. The year opens with the center eaten.

2025 Cancer — the one clean root (value in the land)

Aquarius rising, fixed. Every planet drains to a single final dispositor: Venus, in her own sign, in the 4th — and the Moon is exalted right beside her. The cleanest, strongest root in the two years, and it's value rooted in the land and the home: currency, worth, women, belonging, all standing on solid ground. Sun sextile Moon (government and people in rare harmony). Venus wants to bind and to make-worth, and here she does it at full strength from the soil itself. Both poles: genuine prosperity and belonging — or Taurus's shadow, "having overpowers being," worth hoarded into property. This is the high-water mark of the root's strength.

2025 Libra — value and voice fall in the halls of power

Sagittarius rising, mutable — a 3-month nuance quarter. The root (Venus→Mercury) has no anchor: Venus in fall in the 10th, Mercury combust in the 11th — worth and the message both broken, precisely in the government and the legislature. The Sun is in fall too (opposite Neptune, the revelation phase). Meanwhile the only strong planet, Mars in his own Scorpio, hides in the 12th, and the exalted ruler Jupiter works in the 8th (foreign money, debt). So the official value-and-voice is hollow while the real force broods covert and the real money is foreign. A brief, hollow quarter with power moved off-stage.

2025 Capricorn — strength gathers in the ranks

Aquarius rising, fixed. Root: Saturn→Jupiter→Moon, anchored by Jupiter exalted — but in the 6th, the house of the armed forces-as-service, labor, and public health. The benevolent guardian arrives and lands on the ranks: strength pooling in the military and the workers, while the establishment (Saturn) holds the economy (2nd) and the political class fades, weak, into the hidden 12th (Moon in detriment). Jupiter wants to expand and protect, and does it here through the uniformed and working body of the nation. The root starts climbing toward power again — through service.

2026 Aries — the root settles on the treasure

Gemini rising, mutable. Everything drains into a Mars→Jupiter→Moon loop whose only dignified heart is Jupiter exalted in the 2nd — the treasure. All the season's half-blind force (Mars underwater in Pisces) and the people's raw will (Moon in Aries) circle the guardianship of the nation's wealth. The spine has walked from the land (Venus, 4th) to the ranks (Jupiter, 6th) to the money (Jupiter, 2nd) — eighteen months building a strong house around value. 2026 Cancer — the anchor lets go (now)

Gemini rising, mutable — live at the June 21 solstice. No dignified root: a bare Mercury↔Moon mutual reception — the voice and the people — on the money (2nd) and the land (4th). Jupiter is still exalted in the 2nd but is no longer the root; the treasure now serves the people-and-voice circuit instead of commanding it. The guardian steps off the wheel. The chart is hard (Sun on Mars/Pluto, ruthless force; the public ground between purge and sudden restriction), and from the state's chair the people fall into its 7th — the house of open war. The strong house, handed to a restless crowd the state watches like an enemy. Full reading: 2026 Cancer Ingress — A Reading.

2026 Libra — force and order fall on the people

Aries rising, cardinal — 6 months, the autumn. Root: Mars→Moon→Saturn, and two of the three are in fall — Mars (the chart ruler) fallen on the land (4th), Saturn fallen on the people themselves (1st). The war-god rules with the worst tools, stuck on home ground; the establishment, fallen, crushes the populace without authority. The Sun is in fall too, in the 7th (foreign affairs, open war). Turn it to the government's chair and Mars lands in its 7th — its force pointed at war abroad — while covert Pluto grips the economy. A hard, weak, war-leaning autumn bearing down on the people and the land. Both poles: a necessary purgation, or simple misrule with force and order both failing.

2026 Capricorn — dissolution into foreign debt

Gemini rising, mutable. The most diffuse root of all — a five-planet chain (Sun→Saturn→Mars→Mercury→Jupiter) with no anchor anywhere. The executive Sun sinks into the 8th (international finance, debt, mortality); the establishment is fallen (Saturn, 11th); the voice is combust in the 7th (captured abroad). The one dignified planet, the Moon exalted, sits at the anaretic 29° — a last gasp — hidden in the 12th. Nothing holds the center; the year ends turned outward and owing. Both poles: a letting-go into a larger reset, or dissolution with no one holding the line.

The whole spine, in one breath

The Sun devours the center (Aries '25) → the root finds its one clean anchor, value in the land (Cancer '25) → value and voice fall hollow in the halls of power (Libra '25) → strength gathers in the ranks (Capricorn '25) → and settles on the treasure (Aries '26). Eighteen months building a strong house around the nation's worth. Then at the solstice the guardian steps off the wheel (Cancer '26, now) → force and order fall on the people (Libra '26) → and the year dissolves into foreign debt (Capricorn '26). A root that climbed from land to ranks to treasury under a consuming Sun — then lost its anchor and slid, through fallen force, into owing. The treasure was built and guarded; then its guardian withdrew, and it scattered.
