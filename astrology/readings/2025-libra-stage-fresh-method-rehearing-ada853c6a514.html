The fresh hearing

The kite, not inevitability

The dominant figure is a Kite. Sun, Uranus and Pluto form a Grand Trine across Libra, Gemini and Aquarius. Neptune opposes the Sun and sextiles both Uranus and Pluto, supplying the handle. The figure is capable, but it is not inevitable and not automatically good. The Grand Trine can move authority and change with very little resistance. The opposition is what keeps the figure conscious: the public center has to face what it is projecting rather than letting the easy circuit validate itself.
