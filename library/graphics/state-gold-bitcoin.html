Freedom 250 · Digital money · States · Gold and bitcoin

Which states have made gold money or put bitcoin in a state reserve, and when does each take effect?

Gold, yes; bitcoin, not as money. Florida's gold-and-silver legal-tender law took effect July 1, 2026; in Texas, the law makes gold and silver specie legal tender from September 1, 2026, and the state's electronic gold currency may start no sooner than May 1, 2027. Louisiana (June 11, 2025, with a gold-payment registry added June 23, 2026) and Missouri (July 10, 2025) signed gold legal-tender laws whose start dates are not in the record. For bitcoin the rows show reserves, not money: New Hampshire and Arizona passed reserve laws in May 2025, Texas made the first state purchase on November 20, 2025, and Wisconsin's pension sold its stake. Every row is a reconstructed summary without an official link.
