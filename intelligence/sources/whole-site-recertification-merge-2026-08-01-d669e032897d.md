# Federal Government Whole-Site Recertification — Whole-Federal Merge

## Result

This recertification turns the July 25 whole-federal sweep into a broader,
reproducible site-architecture campaign. It inventories the current federal
`.gov` registry, tests roots/robots/sitemaps, supplies a manual publishing-parent
layer for priority hosts that a root-domain CSV cannot represent, and replays
the Chronicle's major federal storylines across non-news surfaces for the July
26–August 1 delta plus older backfill.

The campaign found substantive new objects and several corrections, but it does
not support a claim that every federal page was read. It supports the narrower
statement that the discoverable public corpus was systematically enumerated,
priority architectures were deep-probed, named source surfaces were manually
reviewed, exceptions were preserved, and promotion candidates were checked
against their controlling primary objects.

No vault note, entity topology, live watch, queue or Observatory build was
changed. This is the controlling workbench merge pending Katie's review.

## 1. Project and research posture

Freedom 250 is a personal, real-time chronicle of 2025–2026 as a contested
American inflection point. Its atomic layer is one defensible source/event per
artifact; plotlines and subplots organize the record; authored synthesis is
where the project's viewpoint lives. Related synthesis runs alongside the event layer; interpretive or speculative
claims remain visibly separate from factual and computed states.

The generated July 31 Primer counted **9,600 notes**, including **8,306 event
notes**, **46 plotlines**, **159 subplots**, **489 executive orders**, **379
mapped federal entities** and **23 Observatory views**. The seven top-level arcs
are:

1. The Monetary Reset
2. The Information War
3. The Great Reckoning
4. Border & Homeland
5. Great-Power Realignment
6. War Footing — Iran & the Mideast
7. Fringe, Disclosure & Health

The hottest current threads included Fauci/Covid, stablecoin law,
tokenization/DTCC/RWAs, voter rolls, urban crime, the Strait of Hormuz,
deportation, 2028 succession, Q drops and AI labs. Those threads supplied the
campaign's review priorities; they did not lower the evidence bar.

### Research controls used here

- one primary object per claim, with exact date and time when the source gives
  them;
- filing, publication, signature, effective, implementation, operating and
  observed-effect clocks kept separate;
- actor, legal entity, owning organization and publishing parent kept separate;
- statute/order/rule/permit/award/commitment/advance/spend/operation/result not
  collapsed into one state;
- news/release pages used as discovery routes, not substitutes for controlling
  rules, dockets, data, agreements, audits or case records;
- existing-corpus ownership and dedup recorded before promotion;
- access failures and `not found on reached surfaces` retained as bounded
  negatives, never universal absence claims;
- future triggers and falsifiers attached to unresolved stories.

## 2. What “whole-site” reached

The dated [CISA federal-domain dataset](https://get.gov/about/data/) contained
**1,338 federal root-domain rows** assigned to **147 registered organizations**.
The census checked all 1,338 roots and deep-audited **164 priority `.gov`
domains**. A 32-domain higher-cap replay replaced lower-cap rows where the first
pass hit its sitemap ceiling.

Combined priority coverage:

| Measure | Result | Boundary |
|---|---:|---|
| Registered federal root rows checked | 1,338 | Registration is not operation, authority or canonical status. |
| Priority `.gov` roots deep-probed | 164 | Priority set, not every registered domain at the same depth. |
| Sitemap files reached | 1,030 | A sitemap file is a discovery object. |
| Sitemap URL entries enumerated | 2,083,812 | Entries are not unique pages read or current publications. |
| Priority roots returning 2xx | 126 | A reachable root is not proof of complete corpus access. |
| Priority roots returning 4xx | 34 | Many are WAF/client outcomes, not downtime. |
| Priority roots with no HTTP status | 4 | Requires manual/browser adjudication. |
| Explicit higher-cap truncations remaining | 6 | `cms.gov`, `govinfo.gov`, `loc.gov`, `nasa.gov`, `nist.gov`, `usgs.gov`. |

The root-domain census was supplemented with **34 priority publishing hosts**
outside its rows: **17 child/subdomain publishers, 10 `.mil` roots, 2 `.org`, 1
`.com`, 1 `.edu`, and 3 requested standalone `.gov` names absent from the dated
snapshot**. Important canonical corrections include:

- `taxcourt.gov` → `ustaxcourt.gov`;
- `pcaobus.gov` → `pcaobus.org`;
- `nnsa.gov` → `energy.gov/nnsa`;
- `aspr.hhs.gov` → `aspr.gov`;
- `fhwa.dot.gov` → `highways.dot.gov`;
- `fra.dot.gov` → `railroads.dot.gov`.

Those are publishing relationships, not silent entity mergers. The full
supplemental manifest
preserves owner, canonical publisher, non-news substitutes, coverage mode and
claim limit for every row.

## 3. Highest-value state changes

### P0 — factual promotion candidates

1. **ICE Clear Credit Treasury rules approved; the August 24 decision watch is
   stale.** The SEC approved SR-ICC-2026-002 on July 24; the order was published
   July 29. This changes the legal rule state, not service launch, participant
   onboarding, operational readiness or cleared volume. The separate
   SR-ICC-2026-005 risk-model approval should travel with it.
   [SEC approval order](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2026/07/29/2026-15249/self-regulatory-organizations-ice-clear-credit-llc-order-approving-proposed-rule-change-relating-to)

2. **The OCC exposed a concrete GENIUS application-form layer—and an apparent
   one-year statutory-date error.** FR Doc. 2026-15088 proposes PRA collections
   for issuer applications and foreign registrations. It prints January 18,
   2026 as the Act's effective date, but the enacted July 18, 2025 statute's
   18-month no-final-rule backstop is January 18, 2027. A PRA proposal is not a
   final licensing regime or an approval queue.
   [OCC notice](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2026/07/27/2026-15088/agency-information-collection-activities-proposed-information-collection-comment-request)
   · [Public Law 119-27](https://www.govinfo.gov/app/details/PLAW-119publ27)

3. **The July 29 FOMC decision is now the controlling policy/operating packet.**
   The Committee held the target range at 3.50–3.75 percent by a 9–3 vote; the
   July 30 operating rates belong to the implementation clock. Neither object
   proves a particular Desk transaction.
   [FOMC statement](https://www.federalreserve.gov/newsevents/pressreleases/monetary20260729a.htm)
   · [implementation note](https://www.federalreserve.gov/newsevents/pressreleases/monetary20260729a1.htm)

4. **OPM's post-appointment suitability system crossed into an operative
   package on July 30.** The effective rule is now joined by a new manual,
   referral addendum, checklists, secure intake and agency reporting routes.
   That establishes authority and operating infrastructure, not referral
   volume, targeting or an adjudicated case.
   [OPM implementation hub](https://www.opm.gov/suitability/suitability-executive-agent/)

5. **DOJ OLC's voter-roll opinion is an important non-news legal backfill.**
   The May 12 opinion supports CRT demand authority and a DOJ-to-DHS sharing
   theory, but describes sharing as proposed; it does not document a completed
   transfer, match, removal, charge or adjudication. It should join the voter-
   rolls storyline as a control object, not proof of an operating matching
   system.
   [OLC opinion PDF](https://www.justice.gov/olc/media/1440346/dl)

6. **FDA component reorganization/delegation continuity changes the entity
   map.** The object corrects an overly broad `HHS reorg only proposed`
   shorthand while leaving the larger HHS-plan completion question open. Route
   it as a component-level authority/function change, not whole-department
   implementation.

7. **VA Dole Act section 403 implementation is live from July 29 through its
   September 30, 2027 sunset.** It creates direct assistance authority for
   homeless veterans. Authority and a plan do not establish obligation,
   delivery to a named veteran or an outcome.

8. **Zoox received a two-year monitored ADS exemption.** The July 31 order
   permits a capped legal pathway for the passenger-carrying vehicle; it does
   not prove manufacturing, deployment, rides, local operating permission or
   safety performance.
   [NHTSA exemption](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2026/07/31/2026-15485/zoox-grant-of-temporary-exemption-from-portions-of-various-requirements-of-the-federal-motor-vehicle)

9. **The FCC–FAA Upper C-band chain now has joined legal and operating clocks.**
   General rules take effect September 29; auction completion is due by July 4,
   2027; wireless authorization begins December 31, 2030; aircraft compliance
   reaches into 2034. Rule publication is not auction, licensing, retrofit or
   active service.
   [FCC rule](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2026/07/31/2026-15598/upper-c-band-398-42-ghz-expanding-flexible-use-of-the-37-to-42-ghz-band)
   · [FAA rule](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2026/07/31/2026-15585/requirements-for-interference-tolerant-radio-altimeter-systems)

10. **OFAC opened a sharp Hormuz/insurance/digital-payment object on July 29.**
    The designations connect maritime insurance, shipping risk, shadow-fleet
    entities and a stated Bitcoin/digital-asset payment rail. They establish a
    legal blocking state—not the number or value of payments, blocked assets,
    independent truth of every allegation or observed transit effect.
    [Treasury release](https://home.treasury.gov/news/press-releases/sb0581)
    · [OFAC list action](https://ofac.treasury.gov/recent-actions/20260729)

11. **The Office of Strategic Capital signed an up-to-$820 million conditional
    loan commitment for Performance Drone Works.** It is pre-financial-close
    and subject to further diligence. It is not an advance, outlay, construction
    start, qualified throughput, delivery or operating capacity.
    [official release](https://www.war.gov/News/Releases/Release/Article/4561771/the-office-of-strategic-capital-signs-820-million-conditional-loan-commitment-w/)
