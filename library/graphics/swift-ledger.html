Which banks have used Swift's blockchain ledger, and for what?

Swift lists 17 banks for its blockchain ledger. 8 of them appear in 6 bilateral cases the banks themselves published, which they call live pilots. Swift says most of the 17 have transacted, so some have not been named.

Evidence and source scope

Six means published bilateral cases/pairings, not an exact transaction count. DBS publication-date discrepancy: the official raw HTML and embedded publication field say September 7, 2026; its rendered public dateline shows September 6. The Library retains its saved September 7 label and records both source displays without inferring a timezone or correcting the issuer. Execution on September 5 is a separate clock.

What's still uncertain

  • 17 roster banks (Swift, Jul 9, 2026); 8 of them appear in 6 bank-published cases. Swift's Sep 28 statement that 'most of the 17' have transacted implies at least 9 banks, so some transacting banks are not yet named in any source.
  • Real money has moved in bank-reported controlled transactions, but banks call them 'live pilots' or a 'controlled proof of concept' and Swift calls this an 'initial controlled go-live phase'. No general availability date.
