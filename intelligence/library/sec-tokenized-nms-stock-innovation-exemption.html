Research summary

The March 2026 Commission interpretation is Project Crypto's classification and transaction hinge: it separates a crypto asset's status from an investment contract surrounding that asset, supplies a five-category taxonomy and gives bounded treatment to mining, staking, receipts, wrapping and no-consideration airdrops. On September 25, Corporation Finance issued nine nonbinding staff FAQs applying that framework to functionality, decentralization, staking receipts, marketing, successor promises, continuing development, central-party absence, buybacks and platform-promoter status. Release 34-106402 separately became an effective, temporary Commission order through September 17, 2031, creating conditional exchange- and dealer-definition exemptions for Tokenized Securities Venues and qualifying liquidity providers. The sequence shows the SEC constitutionalizing the securities side of onchain finance through modular uses of existing authority; it does not establish comprehensive spot-market law, a live TSV, custody, clearing, bank cash, legal settlement finality or selection of any named token, chain or company.
