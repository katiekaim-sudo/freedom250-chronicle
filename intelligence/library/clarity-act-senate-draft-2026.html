The crypto market-structure bill stalled, so the agencies moved

The Senate failed to end debate 49–50 on September 15; the CFTC sent its own crypto rule to the White House two days later.

What we found

  • The September 14 draft made a crypto ethics rule for officials permanent.
  • It added a brake if stablecoin rewards drain small banks.
  • The SEC and CFTC already hold most of the tools; the gap is spot trading of non-security crypto.
