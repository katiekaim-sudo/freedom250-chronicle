Research summary

The asymmetry is narrower than 'powerful countries are never criticized.' Systemic non-borrowers receive candid policy prescriptions; borrowers receive an extra layer of dates, conditions, reviews, exceptions and access to money. The audit covers selected 2025–26 records across eight countries and five waiver cases, with 18 bounded rulings.
