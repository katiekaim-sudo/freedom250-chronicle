# Gold Balance of Payments and Currency Adjustment 2025-2026

## Controlling question

How does gold move through a country's current account, financial account,
reserve position and currency market—and which resident/nonresident transaction,
settlement currency, central-bank operation or valuation change actually carries
each link?

The packet begins from one accounting fact: a balance of payments balances by
double entry. A bilateral gold surplus or deficit is therefore not the final
answer. The research target is the counterpart:

```text
gold production, title or custody
  -> resident/nonresident ownership change
  -> current account, financial account, other-volume change or valuation
  -> invoice, payment, funding and hedge currencies
  -> conversion, retention or central-bank intermediation
  -> reserve accumulation, FX sale, debt service or import financing
  -> observed currency pressure and IMF program measurement
```

No arrow is automatic.

## Non-negotiable boundaries

- BOP is economy-wide resident/nonresident accounting, not a bilateral
  settlement scoreboard.
- A bar movement is not necessarily an ownership change or BOP transaction.
- A customs import is not necessarily a BOP import, reserve acquisition or
  currency conversion.
- A dollar customs value is not evidence of invoice, payment, funding or hedge
  currency.
- Domestic central-bank purchase from resident miners is not initially an
  external transaction.
- Gold export receipts, retained monetary gold, valuation gains and pledged
  liquidity are separate objects.
- Reserve value is not reserve availability. Encumbered gold cannot silently
  finance the BOP or satisfy an IMF NIR test.
- Current-account contribution does not mechanically determine the nominal
  exchange rate; the financial counterpart and policy response matter.
