Freedom 250 · Banks and the dollar · Fed accounts

Who has a Federal Reserve account, and who is still waiting?

No crypto firm is confirmed to hold a full Federal Reserve master account. Kraken has a limited-purpose account; Anchorage Digital is waiting for a decision; Custodia Bank is fighting its denial in court.

What's still uncertain

  • The Fed's public account database loads only in a browser with JavaScript, so some counts come from press readings of it; those are marked in the sources.
  • A firm can hold a bank charter without a Fed account; the two are separate decisions by different agencies.
