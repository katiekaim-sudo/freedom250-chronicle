Mercury–Jupiter Cycle Review

Enduring question: Which message, law, trade claim, or doctrine gains hierarchical scale and public reach?
Disposition: useful when the persona separates message from institution; restrained when the short interval keeps them fused.

What the charts add

The 2024 seed fuses domiciled Mercury with detrimented Jupiter in Gemini/1st. Its candidate separates Mercury into Taurus/6th while Jupiter remains Gemini/7th and lies 0.717° from the derived Ascendant. Message becomes procedure; hierarchy becomes counterpart or public interface.

The 2025 candidate keeps both planets together in Cancer/9th only twelve days after the seed. Jupiter's condition changes from detriment in Gemini to exaltation in Cancer, but focal separation is low. The chart supports a doctrine/law/meaning question more than a new operating anatomy.

The 2026 candidate separates Mercury in Leo/11th from detrimented Jupiter in Virgo/12th. The large promise and the institutional body no longer occupy the same visible place.

Narrowing use

For Crypto or AI, ask whether a public claim gains a legal, organizational, budgetary, or user-bearing body. For courts and legislation, distinguish message from effective hierarchy. Announcement, price enthusiasm, or prestige alone does not satisfy the question.
