# Mars–Jupiter

## The planets

**Mars** — *Ares, conflict, action, capacity to assert.* MUNDANE: the military; war and violent crime; epidemic disease; natural disasters. Also: the men of the nation, men's vote and issues. Mars angular = classic war warning. Mars shows where the nation's combative energy is directed.

**Jupiter** — *Zeus, expansion, faith, courage to hope.* MUNDANE: hierarchical organizations — government, corporate, nonprofit; the corporate sector; courts, judges, religion, charitable organizations. (Greer: "the planet of hierarchy; he rules every organization set up in a hierarchical manner.")

## The combination — mundane

Under helpful aspects (conjunction when well-placed, sextile, trine), Mars-Jupiter channels martial energy into organizational reform. Greer describes "constructive changes for organizations — government, corporate, nonprofit; reforms less difficult; resistance to needed change less effective." The military operates efficiently under clear hierarchical command. Courts act decisively. Corporate restructuring succeeds. Nonprofit organizations expand aggressively and effectively. The key insight is that Mars's force serves Jupiter's hierarchy — reform happens because the energy to push through resistance is available and the institutional framework to direct it is functioning.

Under hostile aspects (square, opposition), the combination produces what Greer calls "crisis and turmoil for organizations; scandals and hostile criticism; proposed changes not well thought out; can warn of epidemics." Hierarchical organizations face aggressive challenges — the military overreaches, the courts provoke fury, corporate scandals erupt. Reforms are attempted with more force than wisdom and backfire. Religious institutions face hostile scrutiny. The epidemic warning is specific to Mars-Jupiter: Mars rules disease and Jupiter amplifies whatever it touches, so poorly-aspected Mars-Jupiter periods bear watching for public health crises.

**As trigger:** Mars-Jupiter aspects are fast-moving (days to a couple of weeks) and mark the specific moments when organizational reform, military expansion, or institutional scandal activate the broader outer-planet storylines.
