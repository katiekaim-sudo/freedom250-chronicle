## Political maturation: who counts as a reporting company?

The factual lane was researched independently. It follows one authority object
from the December 2024 seed week: the federal beneficial-ownership information
reporting perimeter under the Corporate Transparency Act.

Two days after the New Moon, the Eastern District of Texas entered a nationwide
preliminary injunction in *Texas Top Cop Shop*, interrupting CTA enforcement
and staying reporting deadlines. That order did not repeal the statute or
finally decide its constitutionality. The judicial posture changed repeatedly:
the Fifth Circuit first stayed and then restored the injunction; the Supreme
Court stayed it on January 23, 2025; and a separate Eastern District of Texas
order continued to interrupt implementation until February 17.

The durable perimeter change then traveled through a separate authority lane:

| Family stage | Factual BOI state |
|---|---|
| Seed · Dec. 2024 | broad domestic-and-foreign reporting duty interrupted by nationwide preliminary injunction |
| Before First Quarter | Treasury suspended domestic enforcement; FinCEN's Mar. 26, 2025 interim final rule exempted domestic entities and retained a foreign-entity-focused perimeter |
| Full · May 2026 | narrowed interim perimeter operating; final-rule disposition still pending |
| By Sep. 25, 2026 cutoff | Aug. 14 final rule made the narrowed perimeter permanent and added further U.S.-person exemptions |

The factual lifecycle is:

`broad duty → court interruption → reopened deadlines → enforcement suspension
→ foreign-only interim perimeter → final narrowed perimeter`

This is a remarkably literal example of the family's question without being
proof of astrology. A national ownership-disclosure obligation begins as a
contested foundational rule, passes through courts and administrative
machinery, and matures as a final definition of who counts as a reporting

The negative controls matter:

- a stay is not a merits judgment;

Astrology receives no factual, causal, corroborative or convergence credit.
