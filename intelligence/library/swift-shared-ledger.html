Research summary

SWIFT's controlled rollout has bank-reported live transactions, including September 10 domestic SGD activity among DBS, OCBC and UOB, while customer availability, bank-specific deposit obligations and final settlement remain separate. Oracle's September 28 announcement adds a bank-side implementation route: Oracle can supply the EVM ledger, wallets, commitment contracts, event workflows and ISO 20022 payment processing while SWIFT coordinates shared state and the banks retain their liabilities, keys, funding and settlement choices. No named Oracle bank, Oracle-facilitated transaction, general-availability receipt, public rulebook or measured scale is established; Digital Assets Data Nexus remains planned for fiscal 2027.
