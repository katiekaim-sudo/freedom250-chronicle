How much money is behind Genesis, and how much of it is real cash?

Genesis carries more than $5 billion in announced federal commitments, more than $800 million in partner support and $2.4 billion in October company pledges, but none of it is shown as cash paid: only 1 line includes obligated money, 2 are selections for award and 2 are open calls.
