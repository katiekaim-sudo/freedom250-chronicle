Which companies are Genesis members, which only pledged, and which have named project roles?

Of the 63 companies in the Genesis record, 46 are on the consortium's private-sector roster (checked October 13, 2026), 11 pledged support in October (5 of them without being on the roster), and 20 have a named role on a specific project.
