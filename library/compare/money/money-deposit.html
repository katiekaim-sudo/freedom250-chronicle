Swift blockchain-based shared ledger

A shared ledger run by Swift on which participating banks record and move tokenized deposits that they themselves issue, around the clock including weekends. Swift coordinates the payment commitments; final settlement still happens afterwards through existing payment systems, and each bank stays responsible for its own deposits. Operator: S.W.I.F.T. SC (Swift, a bank-owned Belgian cooperative company)
