Freedom 250 · Foreign relations and world money · International bodies

Is anything replacing the international bodies the U.S. is leaving?

Not wholesale: the registry counts 0 wholesale replacements among the 27 bodies (as of October 4, 2026). 17 bodies still do their job with no successor. 4 were left, given notice to leave, or reduced participation in with no successor at all: UN Human Rights Council, UNRWA, UNESCO, Paris Agreement. Only 3 show a changed default route, each for one bounded job: U.S. foreign health aid now goes through the America First Global Health Strategy bilateral network (the WHO's universal functions are retained); WTO appeals for participating non-U.S. Members go through the MPIA; and a U.S. side-by-side tax regime sits inside the OECD. Two pieces of new machinery exist (Artemis Accords, Board of Peace), and Kyoto Vision for a Golden Age of Science is doctrine only. The Board of Peace's Gaza field handoff was incomplete in September 2026. UNESCO remains under notice until December 31, 2026; pending departure is not effective exit.

What's still uncertain

  • A new statement becomes a replacement only after machinery, resources, normal use and control of the frontier function move.
  • Two counts differ: the registry's own counts say 18 active or retained incumbents and 5 exits or contractions without a successor; the ladder places 17 bodies at level 0 and 4 at level 1 (the successor ledger counts the parent climate treaty, UNFCCC, as its own case).
  • The successor ledger (wave 6) words its own ladder differently (its level 1 is 'rhetoric'); this page uses the map's ladder, and both place every case at the same level.
  • Percentages are institution-specific and cannot be consolidated. Exit, defunding, internal refit, parallel doctrine, shared machinery, resource migration, default use and residual-incumbent status are separate clocks.
  • The registry and the successor ledger hold no web links; their official sources are cited in the package's wave ledgers (for the Board of Peace, Resolution 2803).
