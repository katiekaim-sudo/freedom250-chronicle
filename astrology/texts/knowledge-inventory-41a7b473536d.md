## Primary semantic owners

### Planets — strong existing coverage

The live workbook already defines each major Planet as a mundane actor, its
motivation, gift, shadow, rulership, dignity, cycle, practical caution, and
mythic register. The ten-Planet foundation is ready to seed the mold.

The important mundane distinctions are already explicit:

- Sun: executive power and the actual governing center;
- Moon: political class and public mood, distinct from the ordinary people;
- Mercury: press, national conversation, transport, trade documents, and the
  record;
- Venus: women, diplomacy, social peace, art, value, and currency;
- Mars: military, police, force, war, unrest, and decisive reform;
- Jupiter: courts, corporations, banks, clergy, wealth, growth, and law;
- Saturn: establishment, bureaucracy, limit, austerity, consequence, and what
  endures;
- Uranus: revolution, individual freedom, technology, and automation;
- Neptune: ideology, propaganda, mass movements, oil/sea, dissolution, and
  speculative fog;
- Pluto: covert power, debt, purge, seizure, and transformation at the root.

Chiron, the lunar nodes, planetary nodes, and asteroid 916 America belong in
separately typed point or special-object owners rather than being silently
treated as additional Planets.

### Signs — complete candidate roster, deliberate review lane

The live workbook provides a complete twelve-row candidate shelf with element,
modality, polarity, ruler, mundane verb, field behavior, gift, and shadow. Its
field wording often absorbs civic arenas, so the mold now holds twelve
independently governed candidates whose semantic center is movement
configuration and collective provisions.

However, the independent mundane source shelf is thinner here than it is for
Planets and Houses. The Master Reference contains strong Katie language, while
Campion, Pessin, Rose, and the other sources supply selective historical,
modality, lunation, or cohort evidence rather than twelve complete mundane Sign
owners. Each candidate Sign therefore remains a deliberate Katie review lane.

The mold preserves Sign as equipment and operating style. It does not let a
Sign replace the Planet's motivation or the House's arena.

Katie's current design ruling gives Sign construction a mundane priority that
is less central in the natal organism:

```text
Modality = how the Sign supplies movement
Element  = how that movement is shaped and carried
Sign     = the unique collective expression of that movement-in-medium
```

Cardinal, fixed, and mutable therefore become first-class movement owners;
fire, earth, air, and water become first-class shaping-medium owners. The
twelve Signs remain irreducible identities rather than mechanical sums, but
each Sign must visibly reference both structural inputs.

### Dignity — condition of Sign-supplied equipment

Traditional dignity facts are shared. Their mundane role is to describe how
usable or fitting the Sign's movement-and-element equipment is for the civic
Planet using it.

Dignity modifies **how** the actor operates. It never replaces the Planet's
motivation, determines its prominence, or supplies a final Judgment. Domicile
and exaltation indicate fitting tools; detriment and fall indicate awkward or
costly tools; neutral indicates no asserted essential dignity condition.

### Aspects — shared rather than rewritten

The Aspect is the pair's current dialogue or scene. Conjunction, opposition,
square, trine, sextile, and quincunx retain their whole-chart astrological
operators. Applying/separating state, dissociate contact, pattern membership,
and exactness remain shared texture. Plot participation is many-to-many and is
earned only in the whole chart and factual comparison; neither Aspect nor pair
owns a plot.

Mundane work changes the actors at the endpoints and the civic Translation of
their interaction. It does not require a second square or opposition.

### Relationship time — first-class rather than optional geometry

The instantaneous Aspect web and the continuous relationship history answer
different questions. The first says who is speaking in this chart. The second
says where that relationship came from, which developmental chapter it occupies,
whether it is entering, perfecting, separating, returning, or crossing a phase
boundary, and what history has accumulated between repeated encounters.

Retrograde motion can reverse the directed hand and reopen an Aspect or phase
sector. It cannot reverse chronological history. Each state and exact pass must
therefore retain a distinct time identity even when the geometry resembles an
earlier encounter. A relationship state is keyed to pair, exact instant,
coordinate/ephemeris frame, and engine version; charts reference it rather than
duplicate it. The whole-chart web keeps quincunx, while direct relationship-clock
dialogue uses only conjunction, sextile, square, trine, and opposition.

Chart-dependent visibility remains a separate projection. Direct five-Aspect
geometry stays visible regardless of narration; phase prose is admitted or
withheld per chart under DR-020 with its root, ruler, era, or exact-U.S.-natal
ground; seed echoes remain a third independent lane. Co-temporal charts may
therefore share one astronomical state but differ in phase-narration admission.

Aries always resets the mundane year. Cancer, Libra, and Capricorn succeed only
when the incumbent ingress's rising-sign validity term permits it. Every ingress
observes the pair already in motion; none supplies a new synodic origin.
