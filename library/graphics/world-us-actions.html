What has the U.S. government done abroad with sanctions and money since 2025?

The world record holds 12 U.S. government actions since 2025 that changed money abroad, February 26, 2025 to June 1, 2026: sanctions and takedowns aimed at Russia's crypto routes and Venezuela's oil, a Section 301 trade case against Brazil's Pix payment system, a $20 billion Treasury swap line for Argentina, a tariff deal with Indonesia, a U.S.-UK markets taskforce and the G20 boycott. 5 rest on a U.S. government document; the other 7 link news or law-firm pages and are marked 'needs primary source'.
