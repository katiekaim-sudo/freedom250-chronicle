# The world's money

*How are other countries' currencies, the IMF and the dollar's role shifting?*

## The braid so far

Other countries are changing different pieces of the money system on different clocks, and none is replacing the dollar outright. Japan disclosed ¥15.399 trillion of intervention from July 30 to August 26, 2026, the IMF conditions only its borrowers, and stablecoins showed a parallel price rather than an escape route.

**The rhythm.** The story is loudest on the **Cancer–Capricorn axis**, home against state and the domestic currency against the authority that defends it. Its peaks are New Moons (Cancer, June 25, 2025; Libra, October 21, 2025; Gemini, June 15, 2026), so it moves in seedings rather than climaxes. The charts it lights up are the U.S. Venus (Neptune and Saturn squares), the archetype of the dollar's attractiveness being questioned, and Saturn opposing the Treasury's Mercury, the department's messaging held to account.
