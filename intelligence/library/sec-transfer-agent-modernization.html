Research summary

The SEC's September 1 proposal would permit an authoritative master securityholder file to use multiple linked electronic systems, including a blockchain or DLT as the file or a component, while retaining one registered recordkeeping transfer agent responsible for and in exclusive control of each fungible issue. It turns tokenization into a reportable, governed operating model rather than approving any chain, company or token. The exact comment deadline remains pending Federal Register publication.
