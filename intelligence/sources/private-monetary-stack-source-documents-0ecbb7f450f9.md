## 3. Corrections applied to the vault (2026-07-12)

Katie's ruling: *"if we have official documentation from these primary sources overriding a tweet — let's correct."* Method — **annotate, never delete.** The bookmark's quoted text and title stay verbatim (a bookmark carrying a false claim is a true fact about what was being said, and it is evidence in the Information War). What changes is a `claim_status: disputed` frontmatter field plus a **Correction** block naming the primary source, so nothing downstream — the Subplot Map, global search, the story threads — can mistake the claim for fact.

Three claim families corrected:

- **The "ISO 20022-compliant coin"** — the ISO 20022 Registration Authority states cryptocurrencies are not inherently ISO 20022 compliant, are not managed or registered by ISO 20022, and that **no ISO 20022 certification authority exists**. (CA-01.)
- **"Fed research validated BFT for XRP, XLM, HBAR"** — false; verified against FEDS 2025-012 in full. (CA-08.)
- **"DTCC chose Stellar" / "the record moves to a blockchain"** — overstated. Stellar is one named chain in an explicitly multi-chain program targeting **H1 2027**; DTC/Cede & Co. remain the authoritative record and XLM is not the settlement asset. (CA-02, CA-05, CA-11, CA-13.)
