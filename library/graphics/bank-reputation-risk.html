Freedom 250 · Banks and the dollar · Bank supervision

Did the ban on 'reputation risk' debanking become law, and at which agencies?

It became agency regulation, not a law passed by Congress: the OCC and FDIC adopted a final rule (April 10, 2026); the NCUA adopted a final rule (June 25, 2026); the Federal Reserve Board has only proposed one (February 26, 2026). The NCUA's rule says it implements Executive Order 14331 (signed August 7, 2025 · published August 12, 2025).

What's still uncertain

  • The dates are publication dates; effective dates are not in the master.
  • The OCC and FDIC rule appears under the OCC's name in the master; the rule text it quotes says both agencies adopted it.
  • No bill in Congress on this point is recorded in the masters.
