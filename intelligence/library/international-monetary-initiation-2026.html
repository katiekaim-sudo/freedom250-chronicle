No region is switching the money system on its own

Different countries are changing different pieces (law, banks, pilots, live payments) on different clocks; no one has done all of it.

What we found

  • 'Starting' a monetary change is six separate clocks, and they move in different places.
  • Japan's crypto-market law passed July 23 with only a narrow piece in force.
  • The BOJ is raising rates toward neutral while slowing its retreat from government bonds.
