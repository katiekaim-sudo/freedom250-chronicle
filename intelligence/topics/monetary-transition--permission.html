Who permits the change: law, charter, regulator and court

Which legal permissions allow a firm, network or public body to issue, hold, route or settle a monetary claim, and what remains only proposed?

Permission is a chain of distinct decisions. A statute or hearing can set a policy direction; a rule can define conditions; a charter or license can authorize a legal person; account access and supervision can permit a service; courts can define the perimeter. None of those states is the same as production operation or observed outcome.

Congress and the rulemaking path

Charters, access and operating state

The Charter Queue's core rule survives consolidation: application, conditional approval, final approval and actual operation are separate milestones. Its durable contribution is the legal-clock vocabulary, while later private-stack work updates individual entities and states. A preliminary conditional OCC approval does not authorize commencement before final approval, and a planned transfer or product architecture remains future-state until the operating receipt exists.

The SEC transfer-agent proposal shows how permission can be specific rather than ideological: a registered agent may use linked electronic systems, including DLT, while retaining responsibility and exclusive control of the authoritative master file. The proposal does not approve a chain, company or token in the abstract.

The September 17 Tokenized NMS Stock Innovation Exemption is operative rather than proposed, but its operating clock remains separate. A qualifying venue must publish a complete notice at least 30 calendar days before operation. The order opens permissioned AMM pools on public, permissionless DLT and permits a non-security crypto or tokenized-money-market-fund pair leg; it does not identify an operating venue, authorize issuance or select a chain or token.

CFTC Letter 26-25 opens a different adapter at the distribution edge. A qualifying passive software provider may market particular derivatives, refer users to named registrants and receive transaction-based compensation without MPD recommending introducing-broker or associated-person enforcement solely for that activity. The staff position keeps onboarding, custody, execution and clearing inside the registered market and excludes trade signals, asset custody and routing or execution discretion; it is interim no-action relief, not Commission law or proof that any provider has launched.

Institution-level evidence prevents a common charter announcement from becoming a common operating status. The saved matrix observes Paxos and BitGo active by April 30 and records Circle’s July 10 final-approval announcement, while Anchorage’s separate access request remained pending through the May 31 database cutoff. Kraken Financial’s limited account is a specific exception to any universal claim that the Federal Reserve door remained closed. These observations do not update every institution to the same date.

The Ripple record illustrates a different limit: a planned Federal Reserve Bank stock application by proposed Ripple National Trust Bank does not verify a master-account request by Standard Custody & Trust Company. CA-09 owns the dated claim disposition. The Treasury timeline preserves its unsupported June 26 application row as correction history, rather than keeping it in the factual action table. The saved database search is not a current absence finding.

The SEC Investor Advisory Committee is a Dodd-Frank §911 advice body. Its March 12, 2026 tokenization recommendation, adopted 12 in favor with two abstentions, told the Commission not to grant a blanket innovation exemption and to keep ownership-right disclosure, regulated intermediaries and best terms for orders. That text is Committee advice. Release 34-106402 remains the later, separate permission object.

Courts and emergency perimeter

Judicial money research follows the cases that decide whether an actor may stand inside a payment system: master-account access, preemption, sovereign assets, agency survival, bank failure and remedies. The case map is the controlling factual route; companion readings and older timing expectations remain interpretation or history.

Emergency authority can contract, pre-position, control or protect private payment and digital-asset rails under named statutes, but an emergency authority map is not proof that a rail was activated. Authority, order, operation and observed effect retain separate clocks.

What permission does not prove

A forum commitment, proposed rule, legal permission or charter does not prove an operating corridor, funded liquidity, completed settlement, adoption or outcome. The permission branch therefore connects to records-and-routes and accounting: the next evidence must show who operated, what claim moved, which record controlled, what settled and how correction or loss worked.
