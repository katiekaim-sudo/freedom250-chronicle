When does the GENIUS Act take effect, and are any of its rules final?

The GENIUS Act takes effect on January 18, 2027. As of October 21, 2026, 1 of its rules is final and 12 are still proposals.

Every date, in order

The Act takes effect on the earlier of January 18, 2027 (18 months after enactment) or 120 days after the primary regulators issue final implementing rules. No final rule existed by September 20, 2026 (120 days before January 18, 2027), so any later final rule lands after January 18 and the effective date is January 18, 2027.
