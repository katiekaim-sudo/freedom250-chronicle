Which international bodies has the U.S. left, and when does each exit take effect?

Saved evidence through October 4, 2026. The U.S. has completed withdrawal from 1 treaty regime: the Paris Agreement (effective January 27, 2026). Its exit from the World Health Organization is claimed effective January 22, 2026 but disputed: WHO still treated the U.S. as a member in arrears in May 2026. UNESCO exit takes effect December 31, 2026. The parent UN climate treaty (UNFCCC) exit takes effect February 27, 2027. On February 3, 2025 it stopped taking part in the UN Human Rights Council and cut its money to UNRWA, without leaving the UN. For 10 other bodies an exit clause exists but no U.S. notice was found.

Evidence and source scope

WHO legal/financial closure is disputed. UNESCO membership in September and a pending December 31 effective exit are compatible states; notice is not completed departure.
