# New York Science and Technology Company Expansion Pass — 2026-09-04

## Answer

Yes. A second company-focused pass materially expands the statewide picture.
The original 28-node map was a strong strategic backbone, but it was not a full
company ecosystem. This pass identifies **26 additional high-confidence
physical company nodes** and **five site-located forward projects** now included
with non-operating clocks. One unresolved supplier lead remains off-map because
it lacks an exact verified New York site.

The additions show five especially dense systems:

1. **Central New York:** quantum cryogenics, 5G hardware, radar, electronic
   warfare and semiconductor power modules around Syracuse–Utica.
2. **Rochester/Finger Lakes:** precision optics, photonics, laser systems,
   tactical communications and advanced test/measurement equipment.
3. **Hudson Valley:** semiconductor fabrication, superconducting quantum chips,
   vaccine and biologics R&D/manufacturing, and high-voltage electrification.
4. **Western New York:** aerospace motion control, space propulsion, battery
   systems, vehicle powertrains and bioprocessing inputs.
5. **New York City/Long Island:** deployable quantum-network hardware and a
   large proposed-to-commissioning electronics reshoring project.

This is not a generic list of technology companies headquartered in New York.
Every admitted row has evidence of a physical New York lab, fab, pilot line,
test center, manufacturing operation or active facility expansion. Software-only
offices and accelerator membership alone do not qualify.

## Admission rule

A company enters the strong-candidate table only when a company, government,
SEC or equivalent primary record establishes all three:

- a named New York location;
- a physical technical function such as manufacturing, fabrication, assembly,
  test, pilot production or laboratory R&D; and
- a current-enough operating or project-state receipt.

`Operating` means the cited New York capability is presently in use.
`R&D/pilot` means physical technical work is occurring without a claim of
commercial volume. `Construction/commissioning` means a project is being built,
equipped, validated or ramped but its promised new output is not yet proven.

## Ticker rule for the interactive card

- Show a ticker only when the mapped operating company or its controlling public
  parent has a verified current public-market relationship.
- Label the relationship explicitly: `company`, `public parent`, or `ADR`.
- Do not infer that a strategic partner, customer, investor or project developer
  is the node company's ticker.
- Do not show a proposed symbol before a listing closes. SEEQC's pending direct
  IPO proposes Nasdaq symbol `SEQC`, but the company had not begun trading by
  this pass's cutoff.
- Toyota Industries was delisted effective June 1, 2026. Its former Tokyo
  ticker `6201` is therefore not shown on the Raymond nodes, and Toyota Motor's
  ticker is not substituted for a different parent company.
- Foreign primary listings are acceptable when they are clearly labeled with
  the exchange. A U.S. OTC or ADR symbol is a presentation aid, not a claim that
  the operating New York subsidiary itself is separately public.

## What this changes in the statewide thesis

The New York system is broader and more internally connected than the first map
showed. Micron is a gravitational anchor, but the near-term advantage is the
combination of already-operating specialist firms: cryogenics for quantum
systems, power modules and wafer fabs, laser and precision-optics manufacturing,
radar and tactical communications, aerospace actuation and propulsion, and
bioprocessing inputs and advanced therapeutics.

The waterway thesis remains an **infrastructure and geography thesis**, not a
literal claim that these firms move their finished products by canal. The
company layer reinforces the I-90/Mohawk–Hudson and Great Lakes industrial
spines, while actual benefits vary by site among hydropower access, process
water, brownfield industrial space, ports, freight, workforce and research
institutions.

## Implemented map architecture

The governed registry now contains all 59 map-ready nodes in one default-on
view. A multi-select Industry control lets the user show any union of 11 broad
industries while the project-state filter remains independent. Cross-cutting
nodes can belong to more than one industry and appear only once in the result.

Status remains the marker-color encoding. Industry is controlled through the
filter and written in the detail card rather than represented by an unreadable
11-color marker palette. Deterministic collision spreading and leader lines
keep dense Rochester, Syracuse, Buffalo, Albany and New York City clusters
selectable without replacing individual companies with aggregate bubbles.
