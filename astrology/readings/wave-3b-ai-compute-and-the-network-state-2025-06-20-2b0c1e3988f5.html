Wave 3B — AI Compute and the Network State

Research window: Exact 2025 Cancer ingress through the eve of exact 2025 Libra ingress.
Question: Did the July AI/data-center stack create an operating national network, or did it chiefly define the land, power, procurement, export and security interfaces through which one might later emerge?
Finding: The Cancer wave gave the AI thesis a coherent full-stack constitution. The July 23 AI Action Plan and three executive orders joined data-center land and power, federal LLM procurement, full-stack exports, hardware, models, applications, standards, cybersecurity and federal finance into one national strategy. That was a major change in governing architecture, but not an operating network. Before Libra, DOE selected four federal sites and opened the first site-specific land solicitation; NIST formed an industry consortium and released draft secure-software guidance; FAA proposed a BVLOS rule and opened an eVTOL pilot process. The federal LLM contract conditions and AI export program matured only later. The physical data-center leases, permits, construction and foreign deployments came later still.

The controlling chain is:

strategy → legal route → site/standard/program → proposal or contract → authorization/finance → construction/deployment → operation

Cancer establishes the organism's interfaces. It does not prove the organism is alive at national scale.

The stack in one view

    ↓

This is the first wave in which the regime consistently treats a technological system as an exportable constitution, not merely a product.

3B.1 — July 23: the Action Plan defines one national stack

The White House released Winning the Race: America's AI Action Plan in response to EO 14179's 180-day planning command. It organized more than 90 proposed federal actions under three pillars:

  1. accelerate innovation;
  2. build American AI infrastructure; and
  3. lead in international diplomacy and security.

The plan explicitly joined chip factories, data centers, energy, federal land, permitting, workforce, model adoption, security and full-stack exports.

Maturity. Presidential strategy/report and a router to later objects.

Counterevidence and limit. The Action Plan has no EO number and is not itself a permit, appropriation, acquisition rule, export license, federal contract or project. Its importance lies in the topology it makes explicit, not in independent legal force.

Plotline. “AI” ceases to mean a model alone. It becomes a sovereign stack whose physical and legal interfaces must be aligned.

3B.2 — EO 14318: the physical stack is defined from power plant to storage layer

EO 14318 defined a Data Center Project around more than 100 MW of new AI load and defined Covered Components to include:

  • transmission, substations, transformers and protective equipment;
  • gas, coal, nuclear, geothermal and other dispatchable generation and backup systems;
  • semiconductors and semiconductor materials;
  • switches, routers and networking equipment; and
  • storage hardware, data-management software and integrated cloud services.

A project could qualify through a $500 million capital commitment, more than 100 MW of load, national-security protection or a Secretary's designation. Commerce and OSTP were directed to coordinate possible finance; agencies were directed toward categorical-exclusion, FAST-41, EPA, water/biological and federal-land routes. The order also revoked EO 14141.

Institutional change. The controlled object is no longer “a data center.” It is the power, component, network, storage and land system upon which compute depends.

Counterevidence and limit. The order did not appropriate money, issue a project permit, grant a lease, secure water, interconnect power or begin construction. The presumption concerning federal financial assistance and NEPA does not erase project-level environmental law or every other approval.

3B.3 — July 24 and September 9: federal land moves from category to site and solicitation

The next day, DOE selected four federal locations for future public-private data-center and generation solicitations:

  • Idaho National Laboratory;
  • Oak Ridge Reservation;
  • Paducah Gaseous Diffusion Plant; and
  • Savannah River Site.

On September 9, DOE issued the first reviewed site-specific request for applications at Idaho National Laboratory, seeking applicant-funded long-term lease proposals.

Maturity. Named federal sites, followed by a real solicitation at one site.

Counterevidence and limit. Site selection is not sponsor selection. A solicitation is not an executed lease. Neither is a permit, power-service agreement, financing close, construction start or operating data center. The later Portsmouth lease, Savannah River/Paducah selections, Army negotiations and FAST-41 projects retain their 2026 clocks.

Plotline. The federal estate is repurposed as an industrial-development surface, but the private sponsor still has to acquire a legal realty instrument and every other project gate.

3B.4 — EO 14319: federal procurement becomes a model-governance interface

EO 14319 required future federal LLM procurement to implement “truth-seeking” and “ideological neutrality” principles. It assigned OMB a 120-day guidance task, contemplated vendor documentation rather than routine model-weight disclosure, and directed contract terms, cure periods, vendor-borne decommissioning costs and later agency procedures.

Maturity at Libra ingress. Policy and future contract architecture. No OMB implementation guidance was due or issued inside the Cancer wave.

Later body. OMB M-26-04 issued December 11, 2025. Agencies then had a 90-day procedure clock. Even that guidance did not find a named vendor/model compliant, terminate a contract or charge decommissioning costs.

Counterevidence and limit. This is a federal procurement perimeter, not a nationwide private-model speech code. “Truth” and “neutrality” remain administratively difficult evaluation objects; a vendor disclosure or benchmark is not proof that a model is free of error or politics.

Plotline. The model's hidden system prompt, evaluation and behavior become procurement records on which federal eligibility can turn.

3B.5 — EO 14320: the export object is a whole operating system

EO 14320 directed Commerce to create an American AI Exports Program and solicit industry-led consortia. A proposal would need:

  • hardware, servers, accelerators, storage, cloud and networking;
  • data pipelines and labeling systems;
  • models and applications;
  • cybersecurity measures;
  • a target country or regional bloc;
  • an ownership and operating model for data centers and infrastructure;
  • requested federal support; and
  • compliance with export controls, outbound-investment rules and end-user policy.

The Economic Diplomacy Action Group could coordinate loans, guarantees, equity, co-financing, political-risk insurance, technical assistance and diplomacy for selected priority packages.

Maturity at Libra ingress. Program direction only.

Later body. Commerce announced implementation on October 21, 2025. The first public proposal call did not open until April 1, 2026. Application, priority designation, export-control approval, finance commitment, contract, shipment and foreign deployment remain distinct.

Plotline. The export is not a chip or model. It is an American technical, financing and governance environment intended to become another country's operating layer.

3B.6 — July 30: inherited cyber architecture acquires a consortium and draft

EO 14306 had been signed in the Aries wave. On July 30, NIST announced the Software Supply Chain and DevSecOps Security Practices Consortium, with 14 industry participants, and released a preliminary draft of SP 1800-44 for comment.

Maturity. Named public-private consortium and draft technical guidance.

Counterevidence and limit. A consortium is not a regulator. Draft guidance is not an agency security control, contract compliance finding or proof deployed software is secure. Later SP 800-53 and SSDF work must keep its own clock.

Institutional change. Standards production itself becomes a networked public-private process. The federal center defines the problem and convenes the record; industry helps compose the technical practice.

3B.7 — August 6 and September 12: mobility becomes authorization-by-network

FAA's August 6 BVLOS proposal responded to EO 14307 by proposing a performance-based system for routine beyond-visual-line-of-sight operations, aircraft/manufacturer rules, separation, service providers, security, reporting and records.

On September 12, FAA launched the eVTOL Integration Pilot Program and requested state/local/tribal/territorial applicants with private-sector partners.

Maturity. Proposed rule and public-private pilot intake.

Counterevidence and limit. The BVLOS object was proposed, not final general authority. The eIPP launch was not a selected project, certified aircraft, passenger operation or scaled air-mobility network. Eight selections arrived in March 2026 and still required operational agreements and aviation approvals.

Plotline. Mobility is no longer governed only aircraft by aircraft. It begins to require a digital ecology of identity, traffic management, separation services, risk metrics, data exchange and repeatable authorization.

What changed during the Cancer period

  • AI acquired one joined physical, procurement, export and security topology.
  • Data-center policy acquired exact qualifying thresholds and a covered-component definition.
  • Four federal sites were named and one site-specific lease solicitation opened.
  • Federal LLM behavior became a future contract-eligibility object.
  • The export object expanded into hardware + data + models + applications + finance + governance.
  • Secure-software standards acquired a public-private consortium and draft carrier.
  • BVLOS and eVTOL acquired a proposed rule and pilot-intake path.

What did not change merely because the stack existed

  • The Action Plan was not an operating code.
  • A qualifying-project definition was not a designation, finance award or permit.
  • A federal site was not a lease or data center.
  • A procurement principle was not a named model evaluation or contract remedy.
  • An export program direction was not a program selection, loan, export license or deployment.
  • Draft cyber guidance was not compliance.
  • A proposed aviation rule was not routine permission.

Institutional theorem

The network state is not one government platform. It is a constitution of interfaces.

The state begins to govern the technical stack by deciding:

  1. what counts as the complete system;
  2. which land, power and component projects enter an accelerated route;
  3. which model records and behaviors determine federal procurement eligibility;
  4. which industry consortium writes usable technical practice;
  5. which operator, manufacturer or service provider may use distributed airspace; and
  6. which foreign consortium receives American finance, diplomacy and standards.

The legal persons remain dispersed—DOE, Commerce, OMB, GSA, NIST, FAA, private sponsors, model developers and foreign partners. The regime's innovation is the common topology through which they are asked to coordinate.

Astrological hearing

Venus roots a network in physical relationship

The 2025 Aries ingress remains the fixed annual governor. The nested Cancer ingress again rises Aquarius but routes through Saturn → Mars → Mercury → Moon to Venus in Taurus, the sole root in the 4th.

That makes the factual question concrete: can an information network acquire land, power, ownership, customer relationship and terms durable enough to hold it?

  • Venus in domicile in Taurus emphasizes the physical and contractual base—site, asset, utility relationship and public/private bargain.
  • Moon exalted in Taurus places communities, local/state bodies and the territorial foundation inside the answer. The data-center landscape later proves this vividly: federal acceleration cannot occupy every utility, water, zoning or Tribal gate.
  • Jupiter exalted but combust in Cancer in the 6th describes large public-service and technical capacity close to executive purpose. It can shelter development; it can also obscure where public support ends and private risk begins.
  • Saturn and Neptune co-present in Aries in the 3rd frame the attempt to give structure to a technological vision through standards, policies, evaluations and plans. Their exact conjunction is still future.

Uranus in Gemini changes the object from machine to system

Uranus entered Gemini on July 7 and remained there through this Cancer wave before its later Taurus retrograde. The AI stack's factual form is a natural archetypal match:

  • compute is distributed across chips, servers, storage and networks;
  • power becomes a forecast and control problem;
  • model behavior becomes an evaluation record;
  • airspace becomes identity, separation and traffic-management data; and
  • export becomes a consortium and standards system.

The constructive pole is interoperability and rapid learning. The shadow is governance by technical interface: whoever defines the standard, list, model evaluation or API can shape the whole system without visibly owning every institution.

Both poles

Constructive pole — interoperable public capacity

  • land, power and component dependencies become visible before projects fail;
  • federal procurement can require transparency and acknowledge model uncertainty;
  • standards emerge through practical public-private work;
  • export proposals must name owners, operators, security and finance;
  • aviation rules can replace one-off waivers with repeatable safety evidence; and
  • local, state, Tribal and utility gates remain legible rather than being wished away.

Shadow pole — the interface becomes the sovereign

  • a qualifying-project designation can privilege favored infrastructure;
  • model “neutrality” can become political adjudication through procurement;
  • private consortia can write de facto public standards;
  • full-stack export can bind foreign partners to American vendors, finance and governance;
  • identity and traffic records can expand surveillance; and
  • speed can move costs and environmental risk onto communities that do not control the federal route.

The evidence supports a coherent network constitution. It does not support an operating national AI/data-center/export/mobility system by September 21, 2025.
