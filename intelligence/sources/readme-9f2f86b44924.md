## In plain English

**Question:** Which education funding investigations exist, and how do they relate without becoming one universal university-control system?

**Answer:** Federal education money increasingly checks who the recipient is, what conditions apply, how it reports and whether it passes research-security review, before or around payment. That does not mean every university is under one control system.
