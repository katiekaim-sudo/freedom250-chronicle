Government · Money

How does money move between Treasury, the Fed, the GSEs, the trust funds and the IMF?

The federal tree records 16 money relationships among 20 bodies. 10 of them run through the Treasury: 8 point into it (revenue in, customs receipts / approved refunds, profits remitted, holds the public debt, intragovernmental IOUs, conservatorship dividends) and 2 point out (quota / dues (up & out), backstop / guarantee). The other 6 are seized assets, internal lending, quota / dues (up & out), ownership capital & dividends, state equity stakes. None of the 16 is dated in the master.

What's still uncertain

  • None of the 16 flows carries a date or a source link in the master. The 'evidence through' stamp is the newest founding date among the bodies drawn (March 31, 2025), so the list is at least that recent; when each flow was last checked is not recorded.
  • A flow says money can move this way; it does not give amounts or prove a particular payment.
  • The External Revenue Service is proposed and does not legally exist yet; the US Sovereign Wealth Fund has no body of its own.
