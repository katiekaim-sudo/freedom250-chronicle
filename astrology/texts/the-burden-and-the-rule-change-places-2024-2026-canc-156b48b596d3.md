`shared obligation → governing rule → shared obligation → revised rule`

## The seed: contingent burden enters the shared ledger

The Cancer New Moon perfects July 5, 2024 at 14°23′ Cancer in the eighth house
with Sagittarius rising. The Sun and Moon share the eighth house of debt,
dependency, mortality, insurance, tax, pooled resources and what one party
must hold for another. Every dispositor route ends at the domiciled Moon.

That gives the seed a simple but demanding authority statement: the contingent
human burden cannot be delegated outside the chart. It is the final owner.

The closest non-lunar aspect is Mars sextile Saturn within 0.118181°. Action
and administration have a workable channel into structure, rule and endurance.
The seed is not yet a public confrontation. It is the installation of a
container: who must report, recover, refrain, block access or qualify for
relief when a burden becomes real?

The chart is ordinary, not eclipsed. Its Moon lies 86.389679° from the nearest
nodal axis. The family's later approach toward the nodes never converts this
question into an eclipse story.

## First Quarter: the burden acquires a legal mechanism

The missing First Quarter perfects April 4, 2025 at 15°33′ Cancer. Scorpio rises
and the Moon moves into the ninth house. The Sun moves into the sixth house of
workers, administration, health, service and armed capacity.

The stage change is precise:

`8H contingent obligation → 9H legal or doctrinal authorization`

The Moon remains final, but it is no longer the only terminal structure. A
Jupiter–Mercury circuit appears beside it. Law, scale, doctrine and legitimacy
now route through the record, classification, filing or instruction that makes
the rule executable.

The First Quarter also contains a nearly exact institutional geometry:

- Mars trine Saturn within **0.010670°**;
- Saturn sextile Uranus within **0.029212°**; and
- Mars sextile Uranus within **0.039882°**.

This is one connected mechanism, not three votes. Action, boundary and
structural change become mutually accessible while the Cancer Moon occupies
the legal house. Venus is conjunct Saturn within 0.927734° and Mercury within
2.106339°; the agreement and the record both enter the Saturnian container.

The cross-chart bridge confirms that the quarter is preparing the harvest's
institutional field. First-Quarter Venus conjoins the later Full-phase Saturn
within 0.407386°. Quarter Mercury conjoins that Saturn within 0.771218° and
sextiles the later Uranus within 0.800878°. The missing chapter does not predict
one policy outcome. It shows how care becomes a document, agreement, standard
or administrative rule capable of surviving into the Full phase.

## Full phase: the seed returns as executive action

The Full Moon perfects January 3, 2026 at 13°02′ Cancer–Capricorn. Sagittarius
rises again. The Cancer Moon returns to the eighth house and again becomes the
sole final dispositor. The Capricorn Sun, Mercury, Venus and Mars concentrate
in the second house of treasury, valuation and owned resources.

The family has returned from the governing rule to the burden itself:

`What did the rule actually require someone to fund, absorb, insure, block or
carry?`

The most exact seed-to-harvest return is not the Moon's coordinate. The Full
Moon is 1.355962° from the seed Moon, but the seed lights oppose the later Full
Mars within only **0.154843°**. The family question planted at 14°23′ Cancer is
answered by exalted, combust Mars at 14°32′ Capricorn.

That changes the reread materially. What was seeded as a shared Cancer burden
returns as a concentrated act of Capricorn allocation, enforcement or
severance. Mars can make the resource decision effective, but combustion keeps
its action inside the solar purpose. The public sees the obligation; the
executive or treasury side decides how forcefully to account for it.

Declination makes the value/action merger tighter than longitude alone. Venus
and Mars are parallel within **0.079667°**. The Full chart's 2H value function
and action function are operating on nearly the same celestial latitude even
though their longitude conjunction is wider at 2.293895°.

The locked reading's central conflict therefore remains intact but becomes
more exact:

- a domiciled eighth-house Moon owns the final answer;
- exalted retrograde Jupiter enlarges the shared-resource side;
- a concentrated second-house Capricorn field tries to define value and act;
- Venus and Mars join terms and force in declination; and

The family does not ask whether protection or dependency is morally good. It
