What does Securitize actually run, and which of its companies holds which license?

Securitize runs the official ownership record for tokenized funds and stocks, not the money. Through 6 separate companies it acts as an SEC-registered transfer agent, a broker-dealer with a trading system, an investment adviser, a fund administrator and an EU trading-and-settlement operator; each license belongs to that one subsidiary, not to the listed parent.

What's still uncertain

  • Group consolidation does not merge the licenses, appointments or duties of the named subsidiaries. A token can be part of an official shareholder register in one product, a representation reconciled to a transfer-agent control book in another, or a secondary layer attached to an incumbent registrar in a third.
  • Still being checked: obtain or observe a public FINRA/BrokerCheck description that directly reflects the reported 2026 custody, underwriting and selling-group expansion.
  • Still being checked: track production evidence and volumes for the NYSE digital-platform, Computershare and Continental relationships rather than treating partnership announcements as completed market migration.
  • Still being checked: track BRSRV assets, supported chains, stablecoin-issuer use and network-outage or correction evidence separately from its launch.
  • Still being checked: track the EU TSS opening, admitted instruments, members, settlement asset and production volumes separately from CNMV authorization.
  • Still being checked: resolve product by product whether the official register is an off-chain control book, a composite blockchain-plus-identity register, or a linked incumbent register.
