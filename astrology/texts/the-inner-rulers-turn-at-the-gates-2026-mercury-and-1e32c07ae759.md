## 2026 Mercury and Venus solar motion

The 2026 readings often say that Mercury or Venus is retrograde, direct,
morning-side, evening-side or near a station. Until now, however, those facts
were heard one chart at a time. The Libra-season visibility hearing went much
deeper, but it could not answer the control question:

> **Is the late-year inner-planet motion actually distinctive inside the whole
> registered year, and where else does an inner ruler physically turn while it
> is carrying the chart?**

The answer is more organized than a list of retrogrades. Mercury marks the two
cleanest exact station gates of the registered year. Venus then takes over the
slower late-year descent-and-return story.

The compact finding is:

> **Mercury turns the annual frame forward at the Aries ingress and turns the
> Cancer season's first Full-Moon account backward. Venus then carries the
> Libra season from evening visibility through retrograde, inferior
> conjunction and a November root-seat recovery.**

This is an astronomy-context reading. It does not create a combustion doctrine,
change chart governance, add political evidence or turn stations into event
predictions.

## The normalized comparison

The producer preserves all **twenty-nine** registered 2026 ingress and New/Full
Moon charts and generates **fifty-eight** Mercury/Venus snapshots. Each snapshot
keeps five lanes separate:

1. geocentric longitude, signed speed and direct/retrograde motion;
2. signed solar side and true spherical elongation;
3. phase angle, illuminated fraction and apparent magnitude;
4. exact station and repeated-longitude shadow history; and
5. Washington civil-twilight altitude and rise/set timing.

No composite score is produced. A planet east of the Sun is geometrically on
the evening side; that does not prove it is visible. A planet above the
geometric horizon at civil twilight may still be lost to glare, atmosphere,
weather or the actual local horizon. The existing modeled-visibility hearing
remains [[The Rulers Enter Twilight — Mercury and Venus in the 2026 Libra
Season]].

### Aries: the annual frame opens before its ruler can move forward

The Aries ingress perfects March 20 at 10:45:58 a.m. EDT. Gemini rises, so
Mercury rules the three-month frame. Mercury is retrograde at 8°29 Pisces and
moving only −0.01977° per day. It stations direct at 3:32:54 p.m., **4.782
hours later**.

The locked ingress reading already hears a retrograde, nearly stationary
Mercury in the public office. The year-wide audit sharpens the clock: the
season does not merely contain a generally slow ruler. Its governor changes
direction inside the same Washington afternoon.

At civil dawn on the ingress date, Mercury is 4.92° above the true horizon and
rises 56.64 minutes before the Sun. It is on the morning side, 21.53° from the
Sun and only 22.6% illuminated. Those are separate physical facts; they do not
establish whether a human observer actually saw it.

The preceding Pisces New Moon had occurred about thirty-seven hours earlier.
Mercury was still retrograde and 42.16 hours from its direct station. The lunar
chapter therefore hands the annual frame a messenger already deep in its
return passage; the ingress catches the turn itself.

### Cancer: the next season's ruler reverses at its first Full Moon

The Cancer ingress perfects June 21 at 4:24:31 a.m. EDT with Gemini rising.
Mercury again rules the frame and now also belongs to the Mercury–Moon root.
It is direct but slowing, inside its pre-retrograde shadow, 8.38 days from its
turn.

At the first Full Moon of that season, June 29 at 7:56:42 p.m. EDT, Mercury is
inside the Moon–Saturn–Mars–Mercury root and moving −0.02033° per day. It had
stationed retrograde at 1:35:59 p.m., **6.345 hours earlier**.

The two exact station gates form a clean motion reversal:

```text
Aries ingress       Mercury rules the frame → turns direct +4h47m
Cancer first Full   Mercury belongs to root → turned retrograde −6h21m
```

This is not a claim that the Full Moon replaces the Cancer ingress. The ingress
governs the quarter; the Full Moon exposes the first major consequence inside
it. What the motion layer adds is that the same planetary function carrying
the season's treasury–foundation conversation physically reverses at the first
culmination.

The local sky also makes a useful mirror. At the Aries ingress Mercury occupies
the morning side and rises before the Sun. At the June Full Moon it occupies
the evening side, stands 3.71° above the true horizon at civil-twilight end and
sets 57.35 minutes after the Sun. The messenger has crossed from morning to
evening and from forward turn to backward turn.

Sun–Venus–Mars root. Its true altitude is already −2.95° at civil-twilight end;
it sets only 14.48 minutes after the Sun. The season ruler has moved from a
bright evening crescent at the Full-Moon revelation into the root while
physically disappearing into the solar side of the sky.

## The two inferior-conjunction passages form one late-year handoff

Venus reaches inferior conjunction October 23 at 11:44 p.m. EDT. Mercury turns
retrograde only 3h29m later. The October 26 Taurus Full Moon then finds root-
member Venus only 6.95° from the Sun, **0.695% illuminated**, retrograde and
already on the morning side. At civil dawn it is still 4.41° below the true
horizon and rises about eight minutes before the Sun.

Mercury reaches its own inferior conjunction November 4. At the November 9
Scorpio New Moon:

- Mercury rules the chart, remains retrograde and is only 8.70% illuminated;
- Venus belongs to the root, remains retrograde and has re-entered its
  body-relative station corridor;
- Mercury rises 54.22 minutes before the Sun and stands 4.02° above the true
  horizon at civil dawn;
- Venus rises 114.67 minutes before the Sun and stands 14.86° above the true
  horizon at civil dawn; and
- Mercury and Venus turn direct on the same Washington civil day, November 13,
  with Venus's exact UTC station falling just after local midnight November 14.

This does not make the November New Moon a guaranteed event date. It supplies a
precise chapter condition: the record and value functions are both returning
from the near-side solar passage, but neither has yet completed its turn at the
seed itself.
