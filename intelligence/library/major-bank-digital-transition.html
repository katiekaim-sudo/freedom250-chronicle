Research summary

Sixteen major incumbents are not converging on one bank-on-blockchain model. Fidelity adds a national-trust-bank digital custodian, the live FIDD stablecoin, a bounded FYOXX OnChain fund-share record, collateral accounts and a plan-sponsor-gated workplace retirement route. Its retirement reach is distribution leverage, not universal authority over plan menus or assets. The quiet retirement transition can occur beneath an unchanged participant interface: fund-share DLT, the plan trust's holding, the participant-benefit record and contribution or settlement cash are separate layers. Live Fidelity and Franklin fund-record models do not yet establish a tokenized U.S. workplace target-date fund or CIT, plan-level DLT record, or digital-cash benefit rail. No institution proves recurring scale plus public stress, correction, recovery and legacy retirement across all seven surfaces.
