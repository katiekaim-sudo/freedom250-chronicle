Timeline finding

The transition has three movements:

  1. Prepared access: legacy reserve, retainer and emergency programmes join public authority to commercial capacity.
  2. Integration: fuel governance, transport data, payments and predictive planning are being pulled into common management layers.
  3. Capital mobilization: federal land, federal credit, public priority rights and private investment are being combined to build new power, mineral, manufacturing and maritime capacity.

The story becomes vault-worthy when one of those instruments closes, activates, produces verified throughput or fails.
