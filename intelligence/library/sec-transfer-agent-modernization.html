SEC proposes blockchain-based official shareholder records

On September 1, 2026 the SEC proposed letting a blockchain serve as all or part of a company's official shareholder list, as long as one registered transfer agent stays legally responsible for it.

What we found

  • The proposal would require an electronic master shareholder file and allow it to run across several linked systems, including one or more blockchains. It is proposed, not final: no final rule and no effective date exist.
  • Whatever technology is used, one registered transfer agent must stay responsible for, and in exclusive control of, each class of shares. The SEC is regulating who answers for the record, not picking a chain.
  • New reporting and procedure rules would come with it: firms must disclose tokenization and outside tech vendors, adopt compliance procedures, and handle restricted-share legends. The SEC also asks how corrections work on a blockchain that can't be edited.
  • Ripple, Securitize and Wormhole are separate players with separate roles. Ripple's investments and partnerships don't make it anyone's transfer agent, and Wormhole just moves data between chains; it doesn't own or keep the record.
  • Several chains can coexist, with one legally responsible record sitting above them. Other countries follow a similar pattern: shared technology, but legal title and money stay under local control.

Why it matters

The SEC isn't banning or blessing crypto here; it is rewriting a decades-old recordkeeping rulebook so a blockchain can quietly become part of the legal shareholder ledger. That is the old system rebuilding its own plumbing through ordinary rulemaking.

What to watch

  • pending · Federal Register publication, which starts the 60-day comment clock and sets the exact comment deadline
  • pending · Final SEC decision on keeping one responsible transfer agent per share class, and final definitions of blockchain records and 'exclusive control'
  • pending · First registered company actually running a multi-chain shareholder record with a named official file
