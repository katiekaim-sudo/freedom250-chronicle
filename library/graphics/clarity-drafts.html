Where does the CLARITY market-structure bill stand, draft by draft?

It is not law. The CLARITY Act (H.R. 3633) passed the House, and Senate sponsors released three public drafts: EHF26654 (616 pages, July 22, 2026), EHF26718 (630 pages, September 10, 2026) and EHF26724 (635 pages, September 14, 2026). None was formally filed as an amendment. On September 15, 2026 the Senate voted 49 to 50 on taking the bill up, short of the 60 votes needed, and the library's web check on October 19, 2026 found no later step.
