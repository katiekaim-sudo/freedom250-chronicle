Government · Congress

What has each congressional committee actually done in the 119th Congress, and which committees were never checked?

The library tracks 226 congressional committees and subcommittees of the 119th Congress. It has checked the calendars of 32; 194 were never checked, so a blank for them means not looked at, not idle. It holds 276 dated, officially verified outcomes for 19 of them (bills marked up, reported or passed, nominations, subpoenas and released transcripts), most from Committee on Oversight and Government Reform (101). The newest is from July 22, 2026.

What's still uncertain

  • The committee calendar scan ran to July 26, 2026; nothing later has been added, so the newest outcome is from July 22, 2026.
  • 'Never checked' is incomplete coverage, not a finding of no activity (the coverage audit's own rule).
  • A released transcript is a record, not a decision; a committee vote is not passage; a nomination reported is not a confirmation.
  • Permanent Select Committee on Intelligence: H.R. 9624 rests on an official summary only, not the committee's own result record.
  • Select Committee on Intelligence: Nomination of Walter Jay Clayton III (director of national intelligence): sources disagree (William Clayton III / Walter Clayton); the library follows Walter Clayton because PN1092 and Executive Calendar No. 833 identify Walter Clayton for the same office and July 21 SSCI report.
  • Select Committee on Intelligence: Nomination of Joshua M Rudd (army general): sources disagree (Lt. Gen. Joshua M. Rudd to be Director of the National Security Agency / PN711: Army officer for appointment to the grade of General under 10 U.S.C. 601); the library follows PN711: Army officer for appointment to the grade of General under 10 U.S.C. 601 because congress.gov records that PN711 itself was sequentially referred to SSCI and ordered reported favorably on the same date as the 14-3 result object.
