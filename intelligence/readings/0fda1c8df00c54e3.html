# Genesis Mission

<!-- plain-english-entry:start -->
## In plain English

**Question:** What is Genesis building, how do laboratories and researched companies contribute, and what has actually been demonstrated?

**Answer:** Genesis connects national labs, scientific data, computing and AI tools through a DOE-led platform. The October science summit added project selections, proposed research tools, industry compute credits and a real graduate-fellowship funding opportunity. DOE also reports several lab-level systems already working, but the July project graphic is an earlier snapshot and the shared platform is not yet shown working end to end.

**Evidence checked through:** 2026-10-13 (targeted official-source return after the summit; earlier system and company profiles retain their own dates)

The detailed record below keeps the legal wording, source limits and open questions.
<!-- plain-english-entry:end -->

Maintained research guide · original system and company pass September 9, 2026; summit check October 9 and targeted return October 13, 2026.

A national effort to connect scientific data, AI models, advanced computing and laboratories so researchers can tackle challenges in health, energy, industry, discovery and security.

Established in November 2025, Genesis is coordinated by the White House across federal agencies. DOE builds the shared scientific platform; Under Secretary for Science Darío Gil directs the mission at DOE. Its aim is to double the productivity and impact of American science and engineering within a decade.

[Executive Order 14363 — Launching the Genesis Mission](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2025/11/28/2025-21665/launching-the-genesis-mission) · [White House: More than $5 billion in federal commitments](https://www.whitehouse.gov/releases/2026/07/45502/)

## What changed at the October 8 science summit

The summit moved several **named projects and resources** forward. It did not turn the whole Genesis platform into a demonstrated, shared operating system.

The twelve Phase II selections include a Commonwealth Fusion Systems digital twin for SPARC, an RNA-structure project led by UC San Diego, MIT lattice-QCD work and an Argonne scientific-software project. These are named research aims, not completed fusion, biological, particle-physics or computing results.

## What the October 13 return added

No later dated Genesis release was located on the reached official surfaces after October 9. The return did find details in the summit material that this answer had not yet captured:

- **Graduate fellowship:** DOE opened `DE-FOA-0003673`, with $100 million expected across current and future years, about four to eight institutional awards and an initial target of roughly 100 fellows. The November 9 letter-of-intent and January 12, 2027 application clocks are real; no institution or fellow has been selected.
- **Quantum user facility:** DOE now describes a planned National Quantum Supercomputing User Facility. No site, appropriation, procurement, award, construction or operating-access receipt was located.
- **More working pieces:** DOE reports Osprey installed at eight accelerator facilities; Jefferson Lab's HAIDIS inverse solver; two Brookhaven biomolecules that capture terbium; bounded SLAC accelerator-AI speedups; and ORBIT water forecasts. These are component claims, not proof of one completed Genesis platform.
- **More visible collaborators:** the standalone Genesis site displays OpenAI for Government and Google. It does not disclose an exact legal entity, agreement, assigned work, value or accepted result for either.

Read the dated October 13 return for amounts, clocks, source links and evidence limits.

## What Genesis is doing

The charter directs an integrated American Science and Security Platform. American Science Cloud and ModCon connect data, AI and shared services with laboratory computing and facilities. Each project has its own access conditions, scientific purpose and implementation state.

[Executive Order 14363 — Launching the Genesis Mission](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2025/11/28/2025-21665/launching-the-genesis-mission) · [DOE ModCon architecture and resources](https://www.energy.gov/undersecretaryforscience/genesis-mission/modcon-transformational-ai-and-data)

### OPAL plant science workflow

**Demonstrated workflow.** A reported production workflow couples ORNL plant imaging, Genesis services and Frontier.

A 40-day campaign of 360 pennycress plants; ordinary data queries 3–10 seconds, provenance overhead under 1%.

Next evidence: Complete shared provenance/lakehouse integration and test further laboratories.

Author-reported preprint. Cold inference with queue contention takes 30 minutes–2 hours; not all science is instantaneous.

[OPAL production workflow paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.08844v1)

### GridFM 2.0

**Research development.** Brookhaven-led three-year project for faster grid planning.

Named research/utility collaboration; open-source program context.

Next evidence: Two planned utility deployments; evaluate performance against stated targets.

BNL reports $14.2m and OE $11.5m; no reconciliation assumed. Billion-scenarios and acceleration figures are goals.

[Brookhaven GridFM Phase II project](https://www.bnl.gov/newsroom/news.php?a=123136) · [DOE GridFM 2.0 announcement](https://www.energy.gov/oe/articles/does-office-electricity-announces-115m-genesis-mission-project-meet-growing-electricity)

### Genesis-Science-1

**In development.** Arcee and DOE are developing an open scientific model.

Contribution portal and described workbench approach.

Next evidence: Post-training contributions due September14; verify model release and evaluations.

No downloadable released weights established in reviewed official pages.

[Genesis Open Models contribution portal](https://genesisopenmodels.anl.gov/) · [Genesis-Science-1 planned operating design](https://genesisopenmodels.anl.gov/about-gs1/)

### Prometheus nuclear AI

**Phase II selection.** INL-led work on reactor engineering and licensing workflows.

Named lab/industry team and NVIDIA collaboration.

Next evidence: Execute research, demonstrate validated improvements and retain human oversight.

Distinct from the private company named Project Prometheus and historical NASA project; no name-based merge.

[INL Prometheus Phase II selection](https://inl.gov/news-release/genesis-mission-funds-ai-innovation-to-speed-up-safe-affordable-nuclear-energy/) · [INL NVIDIA Prometheus collaboration](https://inl.gov/news-release/idaho-national-laboratory-to-accelerate-nuclear-energy-deployment-with-nvidia-ai-through-the-genesis-mission/)
