## 1. Authority moved at the citizenship boundary

On August 6 the President signed [Ending Birth
Tourism](https://www.whitehouse.gov/presidential-actions/2026/08/ending-birth-tourism/),
delegating INA §215(a) authority to State and DHS and directing an operating
response to the conduct defined by the order. A second order,
[Continuing to Protect the Meaning and Value of American
Citizenship](https://www.whitehouse.gov/presidential-actions/2026/08/continuing-to-protect-the-meaning-and-value-of-american-citizenship/),
sets a federal document-recognition policy for specified categories and requires
every department and agency to publish implementation guidance within 30 days,
by **September 5**.

Those objects change executive policy, delegation and the guidance clock. They
do not establish that any individual lost or was denied citizenship, a document,
a visa or entry, or that a removal or final judicial decision occurred.

DHS separately filed a final rule that will extend the existing $4,000 H-1B and
$4,500 L-1 9-11 biometric fee to all same-employer extension-of-status petitions
by covered 50-plus employers with more than half of their U.S. employees in H-1B
or L-1 status. Public inspection occurred August 7; publication is August 10 and
effectiveness **September 9**. Collection, account transfer and biometric-system
deployment remain later clocks.

## 2. Three physical states crossed cleanly

### Oklo Groves reached criticality

DOE reported that Oklo's Groves Isotope Test Reactor at Lockhart, Texas,
successfully achieved criticality under the Reactor Pilot Program. Oklo operated
the experiment; DOE was the authorizer and official publisher. This is a real
physical operating milestone, not continuous operation, electricity generation,
commercial/NRC reactor approval, isotope production or product delivery.  
Source: [DOE Office of Nuclear Energy](https://www.energy.gov/ne/articles/office-nuclear-energy-celebrates-fifth-advanced-reactor-criticality)

### Hanford completed A-106 retrieval

DOE Environmental Management reported that workers transferred about 79,000
gallons of waste from Single-Shell Tank A-106 to double-shell storage in less
than seven months. The completed retrieval raised the reported total to about
3.5 million gallons removed from 24 single-shell tanks. The waste was moved to
continued storage; it was not thereby treated, vitrified, grouted, disposed or
placed in a closed tank state.  
Source: [DOE Hanford completion record](https://www.energy.gov/em/articles/hanford-marks-24th-tank-retrieval-continuing-momentum-toward-accelerated-cleanup)

### ISS power preparation finished

NASA recorded a 6-hour, 27-minute August 6 spacewalk that completed preparation
of the station's 3B power channel. The future roll-out solar array was not
installed by that work. Preparation complete and future array installation are
separate objects.

## 3. Critical-minerals control became an operative legal architecture

Commerce/BIS published [temporary final rule FR
2026-16078](https://www.federalregister.gov/d/2026-16078), implementing the
President's July 30 Defense Production Act determination through the Defense
Priorities and Allocations System. Beginning **August 27**, covered U.S. sellers
of black mass and tungsten waste/scrap must allocate 100 percent of monthly sales
to U.S. persons unless BIS grants an advance adjustment or exception, and export
is restricted without a license.

Friday public inspection separately exposed three final DOE Nuclear Fuel Cycle
Consortium plans of action: Human Mobilization, Market-Integrated Fuel
Utilization and Material Sufficiency. They create DOE-chaired committee and
subcommittee structures with DOJ/FTC notice or oversight and bounded DPA §708
antitrust protection for compliant supervised action.

The command vehicle and committee architecture are real. A material sale,
license, adjustment, delivery, new fuel capacity or production effect had not
been established by cutoff.

## 4. Human systems showed operating changes, not just proposals

- **FAFSA:** FSA reports that limited 2027–28 Beta 1 launched with community
  partners in ten jurisdictions, including Washington, D.C. Participating students
  use real forms, and institutions/state agencies may receive official ISIRs.
  Events continue through August 20. This is not general public access, Beta 2,
  an aid award or a disbursement.  
  Source: [FSA Beta testing plan](https://fsapartners.ed.gov/knowledge-center/library/electronic-announcements/2026-07-21/2027-28-fafsa-beta-testing-plan-updated-august-6-2026)
- **Home health:** CMS Transmittal R245SOMA was issued, effective and implemented
  August 5. It adds survey guidance for HHA acceptance-to-service policies and
  accurate public service/limitation information. It does not prove that an
  agency has been cited or a particular referral accepted or denied.  
  Source: [CMS transmittal](https://www.cms.gov/medicare/regulations-guidance/transmittals/2026-transmittals/r245soma)
- **FDA Red No. 3:** FDA denied objections and lifted the stay while preserving
  future compliance dates. That is not immediate market-wide compliance or an
  observed health outcome.
- **Head Start and HUD:** the parental-engagement and disparate-impact/Title VI
  objects remain proposals. Their comment clocks are live; present duties have
  not been replaced by final rules.

## 5. Treasury and market infrastructure fixed future control clocks

### Treasury refunding

Treasury's August 5 [quarterly refunding
statement](https://home.treasury.gov/news/press-releases/sb0590) fixes a $125
billion package: $58 billion three-year notes, $42 billion ten-year notes and
$25 billion thirty-year bonds. Treasury says it will refund about $96.3 billion
and raise about $28.7 billion in new cash. Auctions are August 11–13; settlement
is August 17. The plan is not completed issuance, settlement, buyback execution
or a realized TGA path.

### SEC accounting-enforcement unit

On August 5 the Division of Enforcement created a specialized Financial
Reporting and Accounting Unit, staffed by attorneys and accountants and led by
Timothy Zimmerman. The durable factual and theory boundary is in
SEC Financial Reporting and Accounting Unit — Source Document; the atomic
official event is 2026-08-05 - SEC Creates Financial Reporting and Accounting Unit.

The unit centralizes accounting/audit case capacity. It does not make accounting
standards, replace Corp Fin filing review or PCAOB inspection, and the release
does not disclose headcount, budget, data tools, a first case or new authority.

### Overnight and clearing controls

- SEC approved Phase 1 overnight Limit Up-Limit Down price bands for protected
  hours from 9:00 p.m. to 4:00 a.m. ET. Commencement is expected December 6,
  subject to processor changes; operation has not begun.
- NSCC announced a $15 billion absolute pend threshold for ETF create/redeem
  instructions, effective August 17, and an August 24–October 9 CNS output-test
  phase. The latter validates receipt/layout, not economic outputs or production.
- FICC announced August 13 and 17 participant activations and advanced proposal
  clocks for Treasury-clearing submission and a separate GSD Guaranty Fund.
  Activation is not first use, volume, novation or settlement.

## 6. Congress changed procedural states, not law

The Senate passed H.R. 6500 as amended at 3:37 a.m. August 8, substituting a
FY2027 continuing-resolution package. It also passed H.R. 5334 as amended on
August 7, substituting a broad Russia/Iran sanctions bill. The House had passed
materially different texts and must act again.

No December 11 continuing appropriation and no H.R. 5334 tariff/sanctions clock
is operative until identical text passes both chambers and is enacted. The next
House session was scheduled for August 10 at 11:00 a.m. ET.

The Senate also confirmed Todd Blanche as Attorney General, Erica Schwartz as
CDC Director, Douglas Schiess as the next Chief of Space Operations and a
74-nomination cohort. The bulk vote is one institutional control object, not 74
separate stories. Confirmation does not establish commission, oath, office
assumption, command transfer, quorum or resulting policy.
