# Who permits the change: law, charter, regulator and court

Which legal permissions allow a firm, network or public body to issue, hold, route or settle a monetary claim, and what remains only proposed?

Permission is a chain of distinct decisions. A statute or hearing can set a policy direction; a rule can define conditions; a charter or license can authorize a legal person; account access and supervision can permit a service; courts can define the perimeter. None of those states is the same as production operation or observed outcome.

## Congress and the rulemaking path

The Congressional Monetary Infrastructure package organizes committees, hearings, bills and market utilities as an assembly line from proposal toward operation. Hearings preserve arguments and proposed design; markup and bill text preserve legislative state; enacted law and agency implementation supply different evidence. The committee control plane remains a factual representation of jurisdiction and proceedings, not an executive operating hierarchy.

The historical GENIUS reading remains source context for licensing, reserve and implementation questions. Its early activation scenario is not current authority; later legal-state work controls effective dates, conditions and operating claims.

## Charters, access and operating state

The Charter Queue's core rule survives consolidation: application, conditional approval, final approval and actual operation are separate milestones. Its durable contribution is the legal-clock vocabulary, while later private-stack work updates individual entities and states. A preliminary conditional OCC approval does not authorize commencement before final approval, and a planned transfer or product architecture remains future-state until the operating receipt exists.

The SEC transfer-agent proposal shows how permission can be specific rather than ideological: a registered agent may use linked electronic systems, including DLT, while retaining responsibility and exclusive control of the authoritative master file. The proposal does not approve a chain, company or token in the abstract.

## Courts and emergency perimeter

Judicial money research follows the cases that decide whether an actor may stand inside a payment system: master-account access, preemption, sovereign assets, agency survival, bank failure and remedies. The case map is the controlling factual route; companion readings and older timing expectations remain interpretation or history.

Emergency authority can contract, pre-position, control or protect private payment and digital-asset rails under named statutes, but an emergency authority map is not proof that a rail was activated. Authority, order, operation and observed effect retain separate clocks.

## What permission does not prove

A forum commitment, proposed rule, legal permission or charter does not prove an operating corridor, funded liquidity, completed settlement, adoption or outcome. The permission branch therefore connects to records-and-routes and accounting: the next evidence must show who operated, what claim moved, which record controlled, what settled and how correction or loss worked.

## How this fits together

The decisive permission question is always specific: which authority authorized which entity to perform which function, under which conditions, at what clock, with what record and remedy?

Congressional and judicial package cutoffs run through 2026-09-03; SEC proposal through 2026-09-01; emergency package through 2026-07-15. GENIUS and Charter Queue materials retain their own dated boundaries and source roles.
