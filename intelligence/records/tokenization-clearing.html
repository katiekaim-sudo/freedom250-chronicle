2026-07-17 · Federal Reserve Bank of Kansas City · Research · Tokenization & clearing

Kansas City Builds the Doctrine: Buying Bonds Without Calling It Easing

Research Working Paper 26-05, Andrew Lee Smith and Victor Valcarcel: operationally identical Fed asset purchases do different work depending on why they are made. Purchases for accommodation move yields; purchases for market functioning mainly buy liquidity — cutting dealer inventories and compressing bid-ask spreads when trading turns disorderly. Strip the arithmetic and this is a licence. It gives the Fed a published, respectable way to buy Treasuries in size and say the words 'this is not stimulus.' The paper lands five weeks before Jackson Hole, whose posted theme is financial innovation and payments, at the district bank that hosts it.

2026-07-13 · Federal Reserve Bank of Philadelphia · Research · Tokenization & clearing

The Ample-Reserves Machine Can Run Away From Its Operators

Working Paper 26-34, by Philadelphia EVP Roc Armenter: the 'ample' level of reserves is itself a function of liquid deposits, so reaching ample is not the same as being normalized. Feed reserves in, banks create deposits, deposits raise the ample bar, the Desk feeds more in — and at modest deposit persistence the loop goes explosive. His fix is to attenuate the Desk's response and accept small deviations between the effective funds rate and interest on reserves. Context the paper supplies: QT ended December 2025, the balance sheet is over 20 percent of nominal GDP against roughly 5 percent in 2006, and the Desk now runs Reserve Management Purchases under the April 29 2026 implementation note. A sitting Reserve Bank executive publishing that the floor system carries an unpriced instability, two weeks before the meeting.
