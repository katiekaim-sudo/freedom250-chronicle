What the government is building around crypto and the dollar

The United States is not building one crypto system; it is constructing a stack of separate gates for issuers, reserves, Fed access, collateral, title, accounting and failure while keeping the dollar and central-bank settlement at the core.

What we found

  • GENIUS implementation is now a distributed rulemaking program across the Fed, OCC, FDIC, NCUA, Treasury and states. The law exists, but the first complete operating population of approved issuers, reserves, redemptions and enforcement does not.
  • Federal Reserve advisory councils favor tokenized bank deposits over stablecoins as stores of value and want interbank tokenized-deposit settlement to remain in central-bank money. The Board has not been shown adopting that design.
  • CFTC tokenized-collateral and CME/FICC cross-margining work and the SEC tokenized-venue order have reached legal-permission states. Named reliance, recurring use, settled transactions, customer effects and failure evidence are still the key missing receipts.
  • The quiet control point is the official record: the SEC transfer-agent proposal, DTC model and CFTC recordkeeping guidance allow distributed records only inside accountable recordkeeping, custody, correction and examination systems.
  • Strategic Bitcoin Reserve policy, anti-CBDC legislation and the Fed's limited Payment Account proposal are different sovereignty questions: government asset custody, direct public-money issuance and access to central-bank settlement must not be collapsed.

Why it matters

This is the Observatory thesis in miniature: a genuine financial transition is being built through distributed translation and gate layers, but the highest-consequence powers remain concentrated at claim definition, issuer admission, central-bank access, authoritative title, sanctions and failure remedy.

What to watch

  • 2026-10-19 · Treasury GENIUS issuance, offer and sale proposal comment close.
  • October 2026 · Expected general launch of DTC's Tokenization Service after the July bounded production event.
  • 2026-11-03 · SEC transfer-agent modernization proposal comment close.
  • 2027-01-18 · GENIUS Act general effective-date backstop, absent an earlier qualifying statutory trigger.
