Federal Government Whole-Site Recertification — Whole-Federal Merge

Result

This recertification turns the July 25 whole-federal sweep into a broader, reproducible site-architecture campaign. It inventories the current federal .gov registry, tests roots/robots/sitemaps, supplies a manual publishing-parent layer for priority hosts that a root-domain CSV cannot represent, and replays the Chronicle's major federal storylines across non-news surfaces for the July 26–August 1 delta plus older backfill.

The campaign found substantive new objects and several corrections, but it does not support a claim that every federal page was read. It supports the narrower statement that the discoverable public corpus was systematically enumerated, priority architectures were deep-probed, named source surfaces were manually reviewed, exceptions were preserved, and promotion candidates were checked against their controlling primary objects.

No vault note, entity topology, live watch, queue or Observatory build was changed. This is the controlling workbench merge pending Katie's review.

1. Project and research posture

Freedom 250 is a personal, real-time chronicle of 2025–2026 as a contested American inflection point. Its atomic layer is one defensible source/event per artifact; plotlines and subplots organize the record; authored synthesis is where the project's viewpoint lives. Related synthesis runs alongside the event layer; interpretive or speculative claims remain visibly separate from factual and computed states.

The generated July 31 Primer counted 9,600 notes, including 8,306 event notes, 46 plotlines, 159 subplots, 489 executive orders, 379 mapped federal entities and 23 Observatory views. The seven top-level arcs are:

  1. The Monetary Reset
  2. The Information War
  3. The Great Reckoning
  4. Border & Homeland
  5. Great-Power Realignment
  6. War Footing — Iran & the Mideast
  7. Fringe, Disclosure & Health

The hottest current threads included Fauci/Covid, stablecoin law, tokenization/DTCC/RWAs, voter rolls, urban crime, the Strait of Hormuz, deportation, 2028 succession, Q drops and AI labs. Those threads supplied the campaign's review priorities; they did not lower the evidence bar.

Research controls used here

  • one primary object per claim, with exact date and time when the source gives them;
  • filing, publication, signature, effective, implementation, operating and observed-effect clocks kept separate;
  • actor, legal entity, owning organization and publishing parent kept separate;
  • statute/order/rule/permit/award/commitment/advance/spend/operation/result not collapsed into one state;
  • news/release pages used as discovery routes, not substitutes for controlling rules, dockets, data, agreements, audits or case records;
  • existing-corpus ownership and dedup recorded before promotion;
  • access failures and not found on reached surfaces retained as bounded negatives, never universal absence claims;
  • future triggers and falsifiers attached to unresolved stories.

2. What “whole-site” reached

The dated CISA federal-domain dataset contained 1,338 federal root-domain rows assigned to 147 registered organizations. The census checked all 1,338 roots and deep-audited 164 priority .gov domains. A 32-domain higher-cap replay replaced lower-cap rows where the first pass hit its sitemap ceiling.

Combined priority coverage:

Registered federal root rows checked 1,338 Registration is not operation, authority or canonical status.
Priority .gov roots deep-probed 164 Priority set, not every registered domain at the same depth.
Sitemap files reached 1,030 A sitemap file is a discovery object.
Sitemap URL entries enumerated 2,083,812 Entries are not unique pages read or current publications.

The root-domain census was supplemented with 34 priority publishing hosts outside its rows: 17 child/subdomain publishers, 10 .mil roots, 2 .org, 1 .com, 1 .edu, and 3 requested standalone .gov names absent from the dated snapshot. Important canonical corrections include:

  • taxcourt.gov → ustaxcourt.gov;
  • pcaobus.gov → pcaobus.org;
  • nnsa.gov → energy.gov/nnsa;
  • aspr.hhs.gov → aspr.gov;

  • fra.dot.gov → railroads.dot.gov.

Those are publishing relationships, not silent entity mergers. The full supplemental manifest preserves owner, canonical publisher, non-news substitutes, coverage mode and claim limit for every row.

3. Highest-value state changes

P0 — factual promotion candidates

  1. ICE Clear Credit Treasury rules approved; the August 24 decision watch is stale. The SEC approved SR-ICC-2026-002 on July 24; the order was published July 29. This changes the legal rule state, not service launch, participant onboarding, operational readiness or cleared volume. The separate SR-ICC-2026-005 risk-model approval should travel with it. SEC approval order

  2. The OCC exposed a concrete GENIUS application-form layer—and an apparent one-year statutory-date error. FR Doc. 2026-15088 proposes PRA collections for issuer applications and foreign registrations. It prints January 18, 2026 as the Act's effective date, but the enacted July 18, 2025 statute's 18-month no-final-rule backstop is January 18, 2027. A PRA proposal is not a final licensing regime or an approval queue. OCC notice · Public Law 119-27

  3. The July 29 FOMC decision is now the controlling policy/operating packet. The Committee held the target range at 3.50–3.75 percent by a 9–3 vote; the July 30 operating rates belong to the implementation clock. Neither object proves a particular Desk transaction. FOMC statement · implementation note

  4. OPM's post-appointment suitability system crossed into an operative package on July 30. The effective rule is now joined by a new manual, referral addendum, checklists, secure intake and agency reporting routes. That establishes authority and operating infrastructure, not referral volume, targeting or an adjudicated case. OPM implementation hub

  5. DOJ OLC's voter-roll opinion is an important non-news legal backfill. The May 12 opinion supports CRT demand authority and a DOJ-to-DHS sharing theory, but describes sharing as proposed; it does not document a completed transfer, match, removal, charge or adjudication. It should join the voter- rolls storyline as a control object, not proof of an operating matching system. OLC opinion PDF

  6. FDA component reorganization/delegation continuity changes the entity map. The object corrects an overly broad HHS reorg only proposed shorthand while leaving the larger HHS-plan completion question open. Route it as a component-level authority/function change, not whole-department implementation.

  7. VA Dole Act section 403 implementation is live from July 29 through its September 30, 2027 sunset. It creates direct assistance authority for homeless veterans. Authority and a plan do not establish obligation, delivery to a named veteran or an outcome.

  8. Zoox received a two-year monitored ADS exemption. The July 31 order permits a capped legal pathway for the passenger-carrying vehicle; it does not prove manufacturing, deployment, rides, local operating permission or safety performance. NHTSA exemption

  9. The FCC–FAA Upper C-band chain now has joined legal and operating clocks. General rules take effect September 29; auction completion is due by July 4, 2027; wireless authorization begins December 31, 2030; aircraft compliance reaches into 2034. Rule publication is not auction, licensing, retrofit or active service. FCC rule · FAA rule

  10. OFAC opened a sharp Hormuz/insurance/digital-payment object on July 29. The designations connect maritime insurance, shipping risk, shadow-fleet entities and a stated Bitcoin/digital-asset payment rail. They establish a legal blocking state—not the number or value of payments, blocked assets, independent truth of every allegation or observed transit effect. Treasury release · OFAC list action

  11. The Office of Strategic Capital signed an up-to-$820 million conditional loan commitment for Performance Drone Works. It is pre-financial-close and subject to further diligence. It is not an advance, outlay, construction start, qualified throughput, delivery or operating capacity. official release

P1 — promote selectively or open/update a watch

  • SEC S7-2026-26 opens an August 31 comment choice between gross customer-by- customer and net omnibus Treasury-clearing margin delivery; the exemption is not granted.
  • The Options Clearing Corporation may establish an up-to-$1 billion commercial-paper liquidity program tied to its Chicago Fed account; authorization is not issuance, receipt or use of proceeds.
  • Treasury's June 2026 ESF statement closes a missing-object watch and records an Uzbekistan SDR sale; it does not supply a reliable posting clock or a broader intervention claim.
  • GAO-26-109077 leaves 39 DHS priority recommendations after adds, closures and implementations. That is GAO's independent control state, not 39 new DHS actions or failures. GAO report
  • Cameron County's B&M Bridge permit changes ownership/operation/maintenance permission, not construction, funding or crossing volume.
  • ACF–VA and CMS–OPM matching programs create eligibility/payment-control architecture with mandatory verification before adverse action; raw matches are not lawful adverse outcomes.
  • Commerce's engine-parts tariff-offset procedure creates an application path, not an approved credit, cash payment or production effect.
  • USDA's wetland-certification interim rule, MARSSIM Revision 2, and staged CMS DMEPOS prior authorization warrant targeted factual notes with their court, science/security and payment-control boundaries intact.
  • PURSUE supplies a government-wide UAP records/declassification spine with four release tranches through July 10. It does not authenticate every record, prove an extraordinary origin or complete the search.
  • DDTC's public OGL 1/2 text ended July 31; no successor was located on reached surfaces. Preserve the bounded negative and continue the instrument watch.
  • USPS RIN 3210-AA00 reached OIRA as a ballot-mail final rule on July 27, but remains pending; no public final text or effective clock was found.
  • The July 31 cross-code judiciary proposed-rules notice creates an August 14 source-text recovery watch and a February 15, 2027 comment clock.
  • DOJ's 28 CFR 50.18 petition-for-rulemaking procedure is operative July 31; intake, acknowledgement, disposition and later rulemaking are distinct.
  • NARA's July 28–31 schedules feed is a candidate discovery cluster, not proof of item-level approval or disposition until the PDFs, items and signatures are audited.
