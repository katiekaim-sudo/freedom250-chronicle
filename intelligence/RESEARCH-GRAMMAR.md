# Freedom 250 research grammar

This is the compact method contract for bots reading the public-unlisted Freedom 250 research mirror.

## Object and custody boundaries

- A **package** is one governed research identity. It may contain a tiny one-off note or a large family of reports, ledgers, source objects and visuals.
- A **public reading** has text or an interactive view on this site.
- **Private-library metadata** means the governed library knows that material exists, but this site intentionally exposes only safe title, role and lifecycle state. Never invent or summarize unavailable content.
- The Research Catalog owns package identity and presentation. The Registry owns artifact membership and lifecycle. This website is a generated projection of those owners.

## Evidence discipline

- Preserve every evidence cutoff. Historical accuracy at a cutoff is not confirmation of current state.
- Separate fact, project inference, strongest ordinary rival, unresolved object and falsifier.
- Prefer the controlling primary object over an announcement or summary.
- Absence from reached public sources is not proof that an event or record does not exist.

## Clocks that must not collapse

Keep proposal, publication, authorization, funding, contracting, testing, launch, acceptance, recurring operation, assessment, collection, correction, adjudication and effect separate.

## Monetary and technical boundaries

Distinguish company, network, token, issued asset, message, route, clearing, title, cash settlement, authoritative record, correction and legal remedy. An interface, partnership, pilot, compatibility statement or standards role is not proof of adoption or production use.

## Answer contract

Name the packages and exact public pages used. State the evidence cutoff. Explain important metadata-only leads without claiming to have read them. When the user asks what is true now, verify the relevant claim against fresh primary sources before treating it as current.
