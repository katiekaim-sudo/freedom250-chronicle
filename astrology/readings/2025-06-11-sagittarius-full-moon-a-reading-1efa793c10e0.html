Taurus rises and Venus rules, and Venus is in her own sign at 5°09′ Taurus in the 1st. For the first time this season the chart ruler is dignified and standing in the national body itself. The country presents as grounded, possessive of its own worth and concerned with security and value. The Sun at 20°39′ Gemini in the 2nd opposes the Moon at 20°39′ Sagittarius in the 8th. The illumination crosses the treasury and the debt house: national revenue against shared obligation, domestic money against foreign money, what the country holds against what it owes.

The root has two parts. Venus is a final dispositor, ruling herself from the 1st. The Moon and Jupiter are in mutual reception: the Moon in Sagittarius, Jupiter's sign; Jupiter at 0°20′ Cancer, the Moon's sign and its exaltation, entered only two days earlier. Value on the national body is one center of gravity. The other is a pact between the political class in the debt house and a newly empowered Jupiter in the 3rd, the house of communications, transport and neighbors. The benefic has just arrived at its best sign and in reception with the public mood. There is real capacity here for protection, provision and credible reassurance.

That capacity meets resistance. Jupiter squares Saturn at 1°06′ Aries in the 12th within 0.77° and Neptune within 1.70°, and Saturn and Neptune are conjunct within 0.93° in the hidden house. The expansive, protective benefic is pitted against the fallen order and the dissolving ideal together, a cardinal square at the Aries Point. Mars at 26°39′ Leo in the 4th applies to square Uranus on the Ascendant sign within 2.04°. Force at the foundations collides with sudden change in the national body. Venus squares Pluto in the 10th within 1.65°. The country's sense of its own worth is pressed by power concentrated in the government house.

The lights square the ingress Saturn in the 2nd within 2.42°, so this Full Moon times the Aries year's combust establishment planet in the treasury. The U.S. chart receives Jupiter conjunct U.S. Venus within 0.33°, Saturn square U.S. Venus within 0.44°, the lights across U.S. Mars within 0.64°, and the nodal axis across U.S. Neptune within 0.70°. The nation's natal Venus receives both the great benefic and the great limiter at once. The constructive pole is value brought home: a dignified sense of national worth and a benefic newly able to protect. The shadow is a reassurance offered against a hidden crisis of structure, with the account settled later in the 8th house.
