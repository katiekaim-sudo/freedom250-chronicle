Research summary

Japan officially confirmed a coordinated July 31 (U.S. Eastern) yen purchase, and its Ministry of Finance disclosed ¥15.399 trillion of intervention from July 30 to August 26 (direction not yet disclosed, after ¥11.735 trillion of confirmed yen buying in April–June); the exact daily reconciliation stays open. Korea's intervention rests on market-source reports, with no official amount. A formal joint Japan–Korea operation is not officially confirmed, a U.S. rate check is reported but not confirmed, and no record shows the IMF authorizing or executing any trade.
