Freedom 250 · Energy, Data Centers & Big Projects

Which New York industrial sites depend on which water, power and freight infrastructure?

Of the 156 sites on the New York map, 143 are tied to the power grid and 76 to the I-90 rail corridor; 24 depend on road freight, 16 on the Hudson corridor and 14 on other rail. 12 sites name a water tie (process water, water supply, wastewater, groundwater or water systems) and 7 name hydropower. The registry uses 26 kinds of tie; most sites have two or three. A tie is the registry's tag, not a measured share of the site's supply.
