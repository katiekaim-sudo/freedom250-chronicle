# Mars–Pluto

## The planets

**Mars** — *Ares, conflict, action, capacity to assert.* MUNDANE: the military; war and violent crime; epidemic disease; natural disasters. Also: the men of the nation, men's vote and issues. Mars angular = classic war warning. Mars shows where the nation's combative energy is directed.

**Pluto** — *God of the Underworld, resurrection, transformation.* MUNDANE: treat with healthy skepticism per Greer; collective power, plutocratic forces, the masses-as-force. Don't overweight. (Greer does not include Pluto in his system; the following draws on Barbault and general practice with appropriate caution.)

## The combination — mundane

Mars-Pluto brings military and enforcement power face-to-face with transformative and plutocratic forces. Greer offers no specific delineation for this pair — Pluto was outside his framework — but the mundane logic is straightforward from Barbault and observed correlations. Under helpful aspects, the military and law enforcement operate as instruments of genuine transformation: corrupt power structures are dismantled, plutocratic influence is checked by force of law, and the masses-as-force are channeled into constructive confrontation with entrenched power. Enforcement actions succeed because they target the right structures.

Under hostile aspects, the combination is one of the most volatile in the mundane toolkit. The military and enforcement apparatus either serves plutocratic interests at the expense of the people, or collides with plutocratic power in ways that produce extreme violence. Financial warfare — sanctions, asset seizures, economic weaponization — escalates. Power confrontations become existential rather than negotiable. The masses-as-force turn violent. The transformation Pluto demands is resisted with Martian force, and the resistance itself becomes destructive. The Pluto caveat applies throughout: these delineations carry less empirical weight than the traditional-planet pairs and should be treated accordingly.

**As trigger:** Mars-Pluto aspects are fast-moving (days to a couple of weeks) and mark the specific moments when enforcement confrontations, financial warfare, or power struggles activate the broader outer-planet storylines.
