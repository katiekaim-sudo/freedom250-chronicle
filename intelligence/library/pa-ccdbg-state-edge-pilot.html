One $37 million child-care payment can be traced into Pennsylvania, but not to providers

An April 17, 2025 federal payment of $37,065,525.14 ties to a real Pennsylvania child-care grant, and the state's manuals show a detailed accounting trail inside its own systems. But public records cannot link that payment to a specific state receipt, ledger entry, provider payment or federal report line. That is a missing-ID problem, not proof the records do not exist.

What we found

  • The payment matches award 2501PACCDD (Child Care and Development Block Grant, listing 93.575), signed October 16, 2024. Four obligation actions total $271,627,973; none of them is the April 17 cash event.
  • Public data had errors: the Defend the Spend feed called it a Head Start grant (93.600), used a different recipient name and award ID suffix than USAspending, and used a different date format.
  • Pennsylvania's plan says money flows through its PELICAN system to regional offices, providers invoice monthly, PELICAN feeds the state's SAP accounting system, and the federal ACF-696 report is built from SAP coding. PELICAN reports carry SAP line, fund, cost-center and general-ledger fields.
  • Pennsylvania's FY2025 Treasury-State Agreement names Wells Fargo as fiscal agent for this grant and classes 96% of one component as reimbursement and 4% as payroll. The bank account that received this money is not publicly identified.
  • A negative number in public data is not necessarily returned cash. A Georgia May 8, 2025 case showed a shift between funding streams, not a recovered debt.

Why it matters

To follow federal money to the end, every system from Treasury to the state to the provider has to share ID numbers. This pilot shows where that chain breaks today, which is the layer any rebuild of payment controls would have to fix; the study says a public blockchain alone would not.

What to watch

  • pending · Whether Pennsylvania keeps the federal award number (FAIN) or Treasury settlement IDs in SAP.
  • pending · A current PELICAN report extract joined to SAP document numbers and ACF-696 lines.
