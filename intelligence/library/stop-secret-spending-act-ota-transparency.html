Research summary

Public Law 119-106, approved September 11, 2026, adds other transaction agreements to FFATA's Federal-award definition and creates staged Treasury, OMB, agency, inspector-general and GAO duties. GAO's strongest current measurement was $77.5 billion in DOD OTAs recorded in SAM.gov for FY2021-FY2025 but absent from USAspending, not money missing from every federal record. The law does not ban OTAs, declassify awards, prove fraud or establish payment or performance. At the September 15 cutoff, USAspending exposed no distinct OTA award type or centralized view; one-year reporting, list, IG and GAO gates fall on September 11, 2027, the fallback plan on September 11, 2028, and automatic transmission plus a centralized view on September 11, 2029.
