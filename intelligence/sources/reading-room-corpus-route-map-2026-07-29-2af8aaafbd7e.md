## 2. The portal is a release layer, not one record

The Reading Room card date is the **public-release clock**. The underlying
objects have their own clocks:

- the DOJ OIG confidential-human-source inquiry letter is dated Apr. 15, 2025
  even though its Reading Room card is dated Jul. 21, 2026;
- the Baric interview occurred Apr. 10, 2026 at 9:00 a.m. in SVC-209 and was
  published Jul. 23;
- the Daszak CBP package centers on a planned Feb. 5, 2021 arrival examination
  and later 2026 disclosure;
- the Fauci “Prequel” was attached to an email dated Jul. 11, 2015 and
  retrospectively records events beginning in Apr. 2001;
- the Senate's publication date for a record does not create a new historical
  event on the record's original date.

The live portal does not provide a corpus-wide production index, Bates map,
transmittal set, redaction log, completeness declaration, Volume 1 constituent
index, changelog or supersession history. Future portal checks should compare
the resolved URL, file bytes and SHA-256 rather than relying on titles or card
dates.
