Research summary

Emergency monetary response is a sequence of distinct operating and balance-sheet tools: continuity and finality, liquidity and collateral, guarantees and resolution, controls, external finance, and eventual exit or loss allocation. The comparative package preserves national and multilateral authority differences rather than treating crisis response as one switch.
