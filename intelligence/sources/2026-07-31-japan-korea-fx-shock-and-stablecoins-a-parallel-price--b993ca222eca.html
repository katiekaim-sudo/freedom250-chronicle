The finding

The saved Upbit KRW-USDT quote fell during the reported currency-defense window. That nominal movement is consistent with the won's appreciation and a connection between FX and local token prices. It does not identify a causal response to an intervention order, establish resident capital flight or measure stablecoin net flow. A falling token quote can coexist with a rising relative premium if wholesale USD/KRW falls faster. The relative premium remains not computed.

Japan's overlap is a different question: distinct trust, bank-deposit and central-bank claims are being issued, offered or tested under different rules. A settlement option or controlled pilot is not proof of an observed transaction through that option, and no reviewed source connects these digital-money projects to the intervention's execution, collateral or settlement.

Read the saved exchange-event probe for the complete observations, the authority and object map for issuer and legal objects, the claim audit for bounded claims, and the evidence-gap index for the remaining evidence questions. The source ledger holds the primary routes and source provenance.

Why price is not a premium or a flow

A stablecoin-implied exchange rate has units KRW/USD:

(KRW per USDT) / (USD per USDT)

A relative deviation would compare that result with synchronized wholesale KRW/USD. All three inputs need matched times and comparable trade or executable sides and sizes; spreads, fees and conversion or transfer frictions matter. The saved record lacks the synchronized wholesale reference. It also reports USDT/USD near, but not exactly, one on Coinbase without a recovered exact observation and capture route. Do not assume USDT equals USD 1 or manufacture a premium from an unmatched closing rate.

The observations therefore do not establish:

  • a relative scarcity premium, or the absence of one;
  • resident or beneficial-owner net inflow or outflow;
  • token minting, redemption or reserve movement;
  • capital-flight motive or an established Korean Bitcoin refuge;
  • causal lead-lag between official orders, wholesale quotes and exchange orders;
  • intervention success or a digital intervention rail.

Turnover is gross matched trading and can include repeated cycling. Resident capital flight requires attributed net cross-border flows and the relevant fiat/token conversion legs. An exchange transfer can be internal reallocation; global minting does not identify Korean demand. Different claims need different evidence, not an arbitrary count of indicators.

Korea's legal and pilot perimeter

The Bank of Korea's January 2026 Money in the Digital Era: Balancing Innovation and Trust report distinguishes existing controls from future design risks. Regulated exchange conversion commonly meets real-name bank-account and VASP/FIU controls. A prospective direct KRW-token-to-USD-token route could weaken owner linkage and parts of FX-control observability; that is a scenario, not a finding that all such controls have disappeared or that residents used the route in this event.

The BOK proposes bank-led sequencing, a statutory BOK–finance ministry–FSC consensus mechanism and clarification under the Foreign Exchange Transactions Act. Those proposals are not enacted general stablecoin issuance law. In its January report, the BOK assessed broad dollarization of Korean-won demand as unlikely; that dated assessment is not a measurement of the July event. Its concerns also include deposit funding, reserve segregation, redemption and run risk, short-term safe assets and monetary-policy transmission.

Project Hangang is a different monetary object. The FSC's July 15 designation expanded the permitted Phase II pilot scope for participating banks, transfers, limits and treasury projects. Designation or permission does not show that every feature had been used. Participating-bank deposit tokens are distinct from public-chain won stablecoins; their bank claim and applicable deposit protection must be read within the pilot's terms. The January report described follow-up tests after a first test ending in June 2025; the later July designation is a separate source. The expansion preceded the July 30 market shock and is not evidence of a crisis activation.

What IMF staff research adds

The March 2026 staff working paper Stablecoin Inflows and Spillovers to FX Markets studies four dollar stablecoins and 27 fiat currencies over 2021–2025. The saved research summary attributes to the paper an estimated one-percent exogenous stablecoin net-inflow shock associated with roughly:

  • 40 basis points of additional parity deviation;
  • five basis points of local-currency depreciation; and
  • a five-to-ten-basis-point short-lived dollar premium.

Those are the paper's model estimates, not Korean event measurements or a formula for inferring local flow from turnover. Exact shock normalization, horizons, identification assumptions and intermediary balance-sheet constraints belong to the paper's method; this consolidation did not independently re-read that paper. Its authors' views are not an Executive Board or IMF-management ruling. The source ledger also distinguishes institutional publications from staff working papers.

Singleness, access and visibility

2026-04-14 - The Bretton Woods Legitimacy Crisis — Who Gets to Define Stability asks who has authority to define stability. Here that question reaches the monetary claim itself.

The BOJ's singleness of money concerns whether different claims denominated in one unit remain acceptable at par, including under stress. Issuer failure, cyber risk, fraud and concentrated private infrastructure can threaten that acceptance. The BOK emphasizes conversion, identity, reserves, cross-border observability and monetary transmission. U.S. Treasury doctrine, as quoted in the BOJ speech, emphasizes dollar stablecoins as support for dollar reserve status and Treasury demand. That is attributed policy doctrine, not measured incremental Treasury buying caused by these projects.

Stablecoins create privately operated price, custody and settlement points around state currencies. They can extend dollar use while banks, issuers, regulators and central banks retain different redemption, reserve, AML, FX-law and settlement powers. A functional shift must identify which dependency has changed; neither a public token nor continued regulation answers every question about displacement of state functions.
