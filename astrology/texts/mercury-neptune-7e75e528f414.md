# Mercury–Neptune

## The planets

**Mercury** — *Communication, intellect, commerce, transport.* MUNDANE: the media; the transportation and communications infrastructure; trade and commerce; education and intellectual life; the postal system and telecommunications. Mercury's condition shows how information flows through the nation — freely or obstructed, truthfully or deceptively.

**Neptune** — *Dissolution, the Ideal, oceanic depths of the unconscious.* MUNDANE: collective movements, mass sentiment, propaganda, popular causes; the unity of the people (from divine to mob). Almost always malefic in mundane charts unless completely unafflicted.

## The combination — mundane

Under helpful aspects (sextile, trine), Mercury-Neptune is "favorable for the collective imagination; new ideas, aspirations, efforts in arts and literature," as Greer describes. The national conversation turns toward idealism and creative possibility. Arts and literature flourish. Media produces work that inspires rather than deceives. Science communication reaches the public in ways that spark genuine wonder. Health messaging resonates. The collective imagination is engaged constructively — people dream together, and the dreams are at least adjacent to reality.

Under hostile aspects (conjunction, square, opposition), Mercury-Neptune produces what Greer vividly describes as "remarkable frauds and manias; speculative bubbles; apocalyptic frenzies; lurid crimes seize public imagination." This is the misinformation aspect par excellence. Fact and fiction become indistinguishable in the public discourse. Speculative bubbles inflate as Mercury's commerce is dissolved by Neptune's fantasies. Apocalyptic narratives — whether about the economy, public health, or the end of civilization — grip the national conversation. The media amplifies rather than debunks. Lurid crimes or scandals dominate headlines not because of their importance but because Neptune makes the public unable to look away. Propaganda operates at peak effectiveness because critical thinking has been dissolved.

**As trigger:** Mercury-Neptune aspects are fast-moving (days to about two weeks) and mark the specific moments when propaganda, collective fantasy, misinformation, or inspired imagination activate whatever broader outer-planet storylines are in play.
