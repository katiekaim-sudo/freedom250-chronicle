Ruling

The second third-party sweep adds 40 exact candidate entities or entity seams across four research waves. None has been promoted to a Science Strategy target relationship. The named federal awards, laboratory installations, regulator records and operating contracts are comparables or access paths. They do not establish a target award, subcontract, purchase order or classified role.

The expansion exposes the same architecture in very different sectors:

headline system
  -> hidden operating environment
  -> calibrated or traceable evidence
  -> independent or delegated review
  -> government / owner decision
  -> field operation and continuing custody

The company that builds the headline system may control none of the middle steps. Those steps can still determine whether the system is testable, licensable, safe to operate, qualified for procurement or admissible as federal evidence.

Wave 1 — quantum and resilient PNT

The hidden quantum chain is not one category called “cryogenics.” It is at least four separate gates:

  1. environmental readiness — Bluefors supplies and commissions the cold environment, SAES controls residual gas, and Lake Shore supplies traceable temperature and magnetic-field measurement;
  2. device correlation — FormFactor can screen devices at 4 K, but a 4 K result must be correlated to actual millikelvin behavior;
  3. network and timing acceptance — EXFO can establish classical fiber condition and Calnex can establish packet-timing behavior, while neither proves quantum security or phase-coherent quantum performance; and
  4. test truth and metrology — Spirent Federal and Talen-X can create authorized NAVWAR scenarios, while Menlo frequency combs support optical traceability.

The sovereign-supply-chain seam is especially visible in PHIX B.V. / PHIX Inc. Photonic packaging may be technically necessary but still fail a U.S. target's export, IP, source-approval, reliability or domestic-second-source requirements.

Highest-value return questions:

  • What device measurement must correlate from 4 K to the installed operating temperature?
  • Who owns the threat library and raw I/Q data in a resilient-PNT test?
  • Does classical fiber acceptance sit in the same signed handover as quantum metrics, or are they separate acceptance packages?
  • Which entity, facility and accredited scope issues the serial-specific calibration record?

Wave 2 — space, hypersonics and advanced sensing

This wave corrects two stale incumbency assumptions:

  • Range Generation Next LLC is no longer the Eastern/Western Range prime. The current Space Force Range Contract was announced to Jacobs Technology Inc. and current 2026 records identify Amentum Technology, Inc. on the rail.
  • National Aerospace Solutions, LLC is the predecessor AEDC incumbent. Beyond New Horizons, LLC holds the current TOS II operating contract.

The principal lesson is that “range access” is not a single award. Hazardous procedures, facility readiness, FTS compliance, communications, instrumentation, modeled risk and data custody can be held by different companies before a government mission director makes the final decision.

Wave 3 — communications and directed energy

This wave contains the cleanest example of a genuine private formal gate: Key Bridge Wireless LLC, as an FCC-authorized Spectrum Access System administrator, can assign, restrict or revoke CBRS channel access within its defined regulatory scope. That is narrower than an FCC license, but more than a mere advisory report.

Most other companies produce blocking evidence without holding permission:

  • SURVICE integrates directed-energy test and survivability evidence.
  • MZA Associates measures atmosphere and predicts target-plane HEL performance.
  • Applied Physical Electronics supplies the pulse environment.
  • Advanced Fiber Sensors measures what field actually reached the test article.
  • Plymouth Grating Laboratory may cap laser power and schedule through grating yield, thermal behavior and damage threshold.
  • NSI-MI Technologies creates calibrated antenna, radome or RCS measurement environments.
  • Advanced Programs can produce TEMPEST and RED-BLACK evidence, but its current NSA facility status must be independently reconfirmed.
  • KSAT Inc. can commission and operate the ground segment without owning mission acceptance or command authority.
  • EN Engineering, LLC operating as ENTRUST Solutions Group prepares fiber route, permit, make-ready and inspection packages; landowners, pole owners and agencies grant the rights.

For laser and HPM work, the government range safety officer retains the permission-to-fire decision. A contractor may calculate hazard distances, prepare procedures, calibrate sensors or characterize atmosphere; those records should not be shortened to private launch or firing authority.

Wave 4 — assurance, minerals, nuclear and fusion

The assurance stack separates the assessor from the authority:

  • Schellman Compliance, LLC can perform the independent FedRAMP assessment; the government certification and workload authorization remain separate.
  • atsec information security corporation can test a cryptographic module; NIST and the Canadian authority validate it, and the certificate applies to the module rather than every product that embeds it.
  • Covergent Solutions Inc dba Exiger Government Solutions can illuminate beneficial ownership and supplier risk; the contracting officer or program makes the exclusion or mitigation decision.
  • LabVantage Solutions Inc. can preserve laboratory lineage while Iron Mountain Information Management, LLC can preserve records custody; neither proves the scientific truth of the underlying measurement.

The materials and nuclear side reveals equally consequential permission objects:

  • SRK Consulting (U.S.), Inc. prepared the current Mountain Pass reserve, metallurgy, mine-plan and economic technical record used by mapped company MP Materials. That relationship is real, but it is a company-retained S-K 1300 object, not a federal Science Strategy assignment.
  • MPR Associates, Inc. supplies owner-side siting and feasibility analysis; Jensen Hughes, Inc. supplies PRA, licensing, fire, cyber and risk records.
  • NAC International, Inc. can supply licensed packaging and transport infrastructure, while EnergySolutions, LLC can provide treatment and disposal capacity. Neither can be assigned to a target before the material or waste profile, package, route, task and final receipt exist.

Fusion should not inherit fission claims automatically. Tritium, activation products, magnets, vacuum systems and future decommissioning may use parts of the nuclear assurance and waste chain, but the exact material classification, license, package and disposal route remain system-specific.

Highest-priority companies to watch

  1. Metis Technology Partners LLC — hidden hazardous-operations and flight-termination compliance at the active range rail.
  2. Spirent Federal Systems, Inc. / Talen-X, Inc. d.b.a. Safran Federal Systems — competing ownership and truth-oracle paths for resilient-PNT testing.
  3. Bluefors Oy, Lake Shore Cryotronics, FormFactor and SAES Getters — four non-interchangeable cryogenic and vacuum gates.
  4. The SURVICE Engineering Company, MZA Associates and Advanced Fiber Sensors — directed-energy instrumentation, atmosphere and field evidence.
  5. Key Bridge Wireless LLC — a narrow but real automated spectrum-permission function.
  6. Schellman Compliance and atsec — assessment evidence needed before cloud and cryptographic claims can cross federal authorization boundaries.
  7. SRK Consulting (U.S.), Inc. — the clearest hidden third party already attached to a mapped strategic-materials company.
  8. NAC International and EnergySolutions — downstream transport and waste routes that become visible late but can block nuclear or fusion deployment.

Evidence that promotes a candidate

Capability pages do not promote any row. The useful records are:

  1. target BOMs, approved-source lists and long-lead purchase orders;
  2. mission-specific range, PSSC, test-facility or instrumentation tasks;
  3. signed FAT, SAT, calibration and environmental-qualification reports;
  4. approved threat libraries, truth-oracle custody and raw-data terms;
  5. fiber, timing, spectrum, TEMPEST and cryptographic assessment packages;
  6. owner-engineer, qualified-person, lender or regulator reliance records;
  7. radioactive-material characterization, certified package, route and custody documents; and
  8. final owner, agency or government acceptance—not merely contractor test completion.
