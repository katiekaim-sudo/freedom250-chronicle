# Defense R&D, drones and airspace filings

Dated federal filings on defense research and procurement reform, drones, air taxis and the airspace.

Home room: Innovation › National-security tech. 20 dated entries, 2024–2026. Dates and facts only.


## 2026

- **2026-07-23 — EO 14415 — Securing America's Defense Supply Chains and Ensuring Domestic Acquisition of Critical Materials** (Executive Office of the President · Presidential Document · Defense research & buying). Signed July 20, published July 23 (91 FR 46693), the first executive order in twenty-five days. From January 1, 2027 the Secretary of War stops issuing the routine 10 U.S.C. 4872 waivers that let contractors buy sensitive materials from adversaries — a waiver now needs a formal mitigation plan naming the source, proving exhaustive effort, and setting a hard timeline, and lying on one gets referred to the Attorney General. Every prime and subcontractor at any tier must hand over a complete indentured Bill of Materials tracing every part back to the raw ore, vet suppliers for financial distress, foreign ownership and single points of failure, and report significant risks within fifteen days. The Department of War will map the resulting national-security chokepoints 'using any tools and technologies to include artificial intelligence.' Project Vault — the Strategic Critical Minerals Reserve, with Ex-Im as lender — is carved out and protected. Source: https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2026/07/23/2026-15003/securing-americas-defense-supply-chains-and-ensuring-domestic-acquisition-of-critical-materials · Study: library/military-modernization.html
- **2026-05-07 — DFARS: Mitigating Risks Related to Foreign Ownership, Control, or Influence (DFARS Case 2021-D011)** (Defense Acquisition Regulations System · Proposed Rule · Defense research & buying). Proposes new DFARS requirements implementing NDAA provisions to screen beneficial ownership and foreign ownership, control, or influence in the defense supply chain. It tightens who can build sensitive defense technology — a guardrail counterweight to the broader deregulatory thrust. Source: https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2026/05/07/2026-09067/defense-federal-acquisition-regulation-supplement-mitigating-risks-related-to-foreign-ownership · Study: library/military-modernization.html
- **2026-05-06 — Designation — Restrict the Operation of Unmanned Aircraft in Close Proximity to a Fixed Site Facility** (Federal Aviation Administration · Proposed Rule · Drones, aviation & airspace). The FAA proposed to implement Section 2209, creating a process for operators of critical fixed-site facilities to request unmanned-aircraft flight restrictions on safety or security grounds. It is the security-side complement to BVLOS expansion — the airspace-protection layer the broader drone-integration push depends on. Source: https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2026/05/06/2026-08943/designation-restrict-the-operation-of-unmanned-aircraft-in-close-proximity-to-a-fixed-site-facility
- **2026-04-16 — Unleashing American Drone Dominance (FCC Notice of Inquiry)** (Federal Communications Commission · Notice · Drones, aviation & airspace). The FCC sought comment on spectrum and licensing reforms to support drone operations — dedicated drone spectrum, streamlined experimental licensing, and new drone innovation zones and testbeds — in service of EO 14307's goals. It extends drone-dominance policy beyond the FAA into the radio-spectrum layer drones need to fly BVLOS. Source: https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2026/04/16/2026-07381/unleashing-american-drone-dominance
- **2026-04-01 — Implementing Section 927 Waiver Process for Certain Unmanned Aircraft Operations** (Federal Aviation Administration · Notice · Drones, aviation & airspace). The FAA explained how it will use FAA Reauthorization Act Section 927 authority to enable UAS operations through waivers rather than full rulemakings or exemptions, expressly invoking EO 14307's drone-dominance goals. It creates a faster, case-by-case path to commercialize drone operations. Source: https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2026/04/01/2026-06297/implementing-section-927-waiver-process-for-certain-unmanned-aircraft-operations
- **2026-02-11 — Establishing an America First Arms Transfer Strategy** (Executive Office of the President · Presidential Document · Defense research & buying). Sets a formal arms-transfer strategy to expand production capacity, harden the defense industrial base, and pull nontraditional and new-entrant defense companies into the supply chain via arms sales. It reinforces the push to use exports as a lever to fund and scale domestic defense innovation. Source: https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2026/02/11/2026-02814/establishing-an-america-first-arms-transfer-strategy · Study: library/military-modernization.html
- **2026-01-28 — Part 108 BVLOS — Reopening of Comment Period** (Federal Aviation Administration · Proposed Rule · Drones, aviation & airspace). The FAA reopened the Part 108 BVLOS comment period to take additional input on electronic conspicuity and right-of-way provisions. The reopening shows the agency refining the highest-friction technical pieces of the drone rule as it moves toward a final rule. Source: https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2026/01/28/2026-01644/normalizing-unmanned-aircraft-systems-beyond-visual-line-of-sight-operations-reopening-of-comment
- **2026-01-21 — Streamlining Export Controls for Drone Exports** (Bureau of Industry and Security · Rule · Drones, aviation & airspace). Acting under EO 14307, Commerce/BIS issued an interim final rule easing export controls on civil UAVs, allowing less-sensitive commercial drones to ship license-free to most Wassenaar partners and certain cargo and agricultural-spray drones to allies under License Exception STA. A concrete deregulatory follow-through on the drone-dominance order. Source: https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2026/01/21/2026-01059/streamlining-export-controls-for-drone-exports

## 2025

- **2025-09-29 — Part 108 BVLOS — Denial of Extension of Comment Period** (Federal Aviation Administration · Proposed Rule · Drones, aviation & airspace). The FAA and TSA denied requests to extend the Part 108 BVLOS comment period, citing EO 14307's 240-day final-rule deadline and the extraordinary measures needed to meet it. The denial signals the administration's push to fast-track the drone rule rather than slow the rulemaking. Source: https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2025/09/29/2025-18873/normalizing-unmanned-aircraft-systems-beyond-visual-line-of-sight-operations-denial-of-extension-of
- **2025-09-16 — Electric Vertical Takeoff and Landing and Advanced Air Mobility Integration Pilot Program — Establishment and Request for Proposals** (Federal Aviation Administration · Notice · Drones, aviation & airspace). The FAA formally established the eVTOL/AAM Integration Pilot Program directed by EO 14307, pairing state, local, tribal, and territorial governments with private eVTOL operators to develop frameworks for safe air-taxi operations. The notice opened a request for proposals to select participating projects. Source: https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2025/09/16/2025-17844/electric-vertical-takeoff-and-landing-and-advanced-air-mobility-integration-pilot
- **2025-09-10 — DFARS: Assessing Contractor Implementation of Cybersecurity Requirements (DFARS Case 2019-D041)** (Defense Acquisition Regulations System · Rule · Defense research & buying). Finalizes the DFARS rule operationalizing the Cybersecurity Maturity Model Certification (CMMC) program, requiring contractors to meet and verify cybersecurity levels to win DoD work. It sets the compliance backbone for software, AI, and microelectronics vendors building into the defense ecosystem. Source: https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2025/09/10/2025-17359/defense-federal-acquisition-regulation-supplement-assessing-contractor-implementation-of · Study: library/military-modernization.html
- **2025-08-07 — Normalizing Unmanned Aircraft Systems Beyond Visual Line of Sight Operations (Part 108 NPRM)** (Federal Aviation Administration · Proposed Rule · Drones, aviation & airspace). The long-awaited FAA/TSA 'Part 108' proposed rule laid out performance-based regulations to enable routine low-altitude BVLOS drone operations without visual observers, plus third-party UAS Traffic Management services. Issued under EO 14307's mandate, it is the foundational rulemaking for normalizing commercial drone flight in U.S. airspace. Source: https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2025/08/07/2025-14992/normalizing-unmanned-aircraft-systems-beyond-visual-line-of-sight-operations
- **2025-07-09 — Notice of Request for Comments on Executive Order 14265, Modernizing Defense Acquisitions** (Department of Defense · Notice · Defense research & buying). DoD opened a public comment docket soliciting industry input on which acquisition regulations to cut or rewrite to implement EO 14265. It operationalizes the acquisition-reform order and feeds the two-phase DFARS overhaul with class deviations effective Feb 1, 2026. Source: https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2025/07/09/2025-12756/notice-of-request-for-comments-on-executive-order-14265-modernizing-defense-acquisitions-and · Study: library/military-modernization.html
- **2025-06-11 — Executive Order 14307 — Unleashing American Drone Dominance** (Executive Office of the President · Presidential Document · Drones, aviation & airspace). Signed June 6, 2025, EO 14307 directed the FAA to issue a proposed rule enabling routine beyond-visual-line-of-sight drone operations within 30 days and a final rule within 240 days, and to accelerate UAS and eVTOL integration into the National Airspace System. BVLOS normalization was the order's first priority, and it also launched an eVTOL Integration Pilot Program. Source: https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2025/06/11/2025-10814/unleashing-american-drone-dominance
- **2025-04-18 — Executive Order 14275 — Restoring Common Sense to Federal Procurement** (Executive Office of the President · Presidential Document · Defense research & buying). Directs a top-to-bottom rewrite of the 40-year-old Federal Acquisition Regulation to delete provisions not required by statute and impose a ten-for-one deregulatory rule on new supplemental rules. Because the FAR governs all federal buying including major weapons systems, this is the broadest procurement-barrier teardown of the term. Source: https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2025/04/18/2025-06839/restoring-common-sense-to-federal-procurement · Study: library/military-modernization.html
- **2025-04-15 — Restoring America's Maritime Dominance** (Executive Office of the President · Presidential Document · Defense research & buying). Directs a national Maritime Action Plan to rebuild U.S. shipbuilding, including acquisition-process reforms, modular and commercial design practices, and fewer approval layers for vessel procurement. It pairs a build mandate to revive the shipyard base with deregulatory fixes relevant to autonomous and crewed naval platforms. Source: https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2025/04/15/2025-06465/restoring-americas-maritime-dominance · Study: library/military-modernization.html
- **2025-04-15 — Reforming Foreign Defense Sales To Improve Speed and Accountability** (Executive Office of the President · Presidential Document · Defense research & buying). Orders streamlining of the Foreign Military Sales process and a consolidated list of priority partners to move U.S. weapons and defense technology to allies faster. It eases the export and transfer barriers that gate allied access to American defense tech, tied to industrial-base expansion. Source: https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2025/04/15/2025-06464/reforming-foreign-defense-sales-to-improve-speed-and-accountability · Study: library/military-modernization.html
- **2025-04-15 — Executive Order 14265 — Modernizing Defense Acquisitions and Spurring Innovation in the Defense Industrial Base** (Executive Office of the President · Presidential Document · Defense research & buying). Orders the Secretary of Defense to deliver a 60-day plan to overhaul DoD acquisition around commercial solutions, Other Transaction Authority, and Rapid Capabilities Office mechanisms, and to strip out unnecessary supplemental regulations. The keystone deregulatory order driving the 2026 DFARS overhaul and the Pentagon's transformation strategy. Source: https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2025/04/15/2025-06461/modernizing-defense-acquisitions-and-spurring-innovation-in-the-defense-industrial-base · Study: library/military-modernization.html
- **2025-02-03 — Executive Order 14186 — The Iron Dome for America** (Executive Office of the President · Presidential Document · Defense research & buying). Signed Jan 27, 2025, this order commands a next-generation layered homeland missile shield against ballistic, hypersonic, and cruise threats — later branded 'Golden Dome' — calling for space-based sensors and interceptors plus directed-energy tools. It is the founding charter of the largest U.S. missile-defense build in a generation and the single biggest defense-R&D demand signal of the term. Source: https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2025/02/03/2025-02182/the-iron-dome-for-america · Study: library/military-modernization.html

## 2024

- **2024-11-21 — Integration of Powered-Lift: Pilot Certification and Operations (SFAR)** (Federal Aviation Administration · Rule · Drones, aviation & airspace). The FAA's final powered-lift rule established pilot-certification and operating rules for eVTOL and air-taxi aircraft, including a ten-year Special Federal Aviation Regulation tailored to their varied designs. It is the foundational framework enabling powered-lift integration into the National Airspace System.
