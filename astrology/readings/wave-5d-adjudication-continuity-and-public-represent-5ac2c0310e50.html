Wave 5D — Adjudication, Continuity, and Public Representation

Research window: Exact 2025 Capricorn ingress through exact 2026 Aries ingress.
Evidence cutoff: June 20, 2026, 11:59 p.m. EDT. Later facts are quarantined as handoffs.
Question: What joins a railroad emergency board, a FEMA review council, a national motor race and Made-in-America enforcement—and what could each institutional carrier actually decide?
Finding: All four objects govern representation under constraint. EO 14374 created a temporary neutral board that selected a final labor offer and then terminated; it held conditions steady but could not impose the eventual contract. EO 14378 preserved a FEMA advisory body and delegated its FACA administration, yet the body required another extension and no public final report was isolated before its May end. EO 14381 moved national spectacle beyond announcement into a public 1.7-mile route, dates and intergovernmental/private operating coalition, but not a completed race or public spending ledger. EO 14392 assigned origin-claim review, marketplace procedure, procurement removal and False Claims Act referral paths, but no named EO-attributable enforcement action was public by the cutoff. The packet's thesis is the state builds bounded interpreters of public truth—offer, diagnosis, national image, country of origin—while the underlying legal and operating holders remain outside them.

The shared ladder is:

contested public claim → authorized interpreter → procedure / record → recommendation or verification → external decision holder → operation → contest / settlement

5D.1 — January 14–16: the second board selects, reports and disappears

EO 14374 created Presidential Emergency Board 254 at 12:01 a.m. EST on January 16 under Railway Labor Act section 9A. The parties had not all accepted the first board's October recommendations, so the statute required a second hearing.

The design is precise:

  • a chair and two neutral members;
  • final offers submitted within 30 days;
  • selection of the most reasonable offer within the following 30 days;
  • status quo through 60 days after the report;
  • presidential records transferred to National Mediation Board custody; and
  • automatic termination when the report is submitted.

The PEB 254 report was delivered March 16 after hearings and meetings. The Board selected among final offers and ceased to exist. The later record—already traced in Wave 3G—shows that the cooling-off period ended, workers struck on May 16, a tentative agreement was reached May 19 and service returned.

Maturity. Full statutory body → hearing → report → termination lifecycle, followed by bargaining, self-help and operating settlement outside the board.

Counterevidence and limit. The Board did not own LIRR, represent the unions, impose a contract or make the tentative agreement. NMB record custody is not institutional ownership. The eventual strike is evidence that even final-offer selection did not consummate the relationship.

5D.2 — January 23, March 24 and May 29: FEMA review survives by borrowed time

EO 14378 continued the FEMA Review Council until March 25 and assigned the President's applicable Federal Advisory Committee Act functions to Homeland Security under GSA procedures. It preserved an advisory carrier created in January 2025; it did not extend FEMA's operational charter or transfer FEMA powers to the council.

The council's original information-gathering machinery was real: public meetings and a Federal Register request for public experiences and programmatic or organizational recommendations. But its need for continuance reveals an unfinished report clock.

In the next ingress period, EO 14397 extended the body until ten days after report submission or May 29, whichever came first, and superseded EO 14378's continuation terms. No public final report was isolated by the evidence cutoff.

Maturity. Existing advisory body preserved, administered and granted a final bounded runway.

Counterevidence and limit. Continuation is not completed diagnosis or FEMA reorganization. The council could recommend, not execute disaster assistance, abolish FEMA, move appropriations or supersede statutory authorities. Its later extension belongs to the Aries period even though it matures a Capricorn-wave object.

5D.3 — January 30 through May: national image acquires a route

EO 14381 announces the Freedom 250 Grand Prix near the National Mall and assigns Interior and Transportation the route, permit and maintenance interface. DOT may use available funds; FAA handles aerial-photography permissions; D.C. coordination remains necessary; Interior can consider the race under the National Capital Region special-event rule.

The DOT announcement names August 21–23, free public attendance, FOX coverage and an operating coalition of INDYCAR, the Freedom 250 task force, DOT, Interior and the District. By May, DOT's Freedom 250 surface displayed a 1.7-mile National Mall circuit and virtual course tour.

That is a material public-interface maturity: date, place, route, organizer and audience. It is still pre-event.

Maturity. Scheduled event, public route and named intergovernmental/private planning carrier.

Counterevidence and limit. No complete permit packet, federal obligation/outlay ledger, road-maintenance contract, security plan, audience count, broadcast receipt or completed race existed by June 20. “Free to the public” does not mean costless to the government or organizer.

5D.4 — March 13: origin becomes a verification and referral problem

EO 14392 does not rewrite the Made in USA Labeling Rule. It directs FTC enforcement priority where existing law is violated, asks FTC to consider marketplace-verification rules, asks origin agencies to consider voluntary-labeling rules, and requires government-wide acquisition holders to review origin claims.

For government procurement, the route is especially clear:

vendor origin claim → contract-holder review → product removal if misrepresented → DOJ referral → possible False Claims Act case

Each arrow requires an actual record. No named seller, marketplace procedure, procurement removal, DOJ referral, complaint, proposed rule or penalty explicitly attributable to EO 14392 was isolated by the cutoff.

Maturity. Effective review and enforcement priority with an assigned interagency route.

Counterevidence and limit. Patriotic branding is not legal proof of origin. The EO does not make every imported component deceptive, establish a new origin test by itself, remove a product without agency findings or turn referral into liability.

The bounded-interpreter theorem

The four carriers are powerful precisely because they are narrow:

  • a board can keep labor peace long enough to hear and select;
  • a council can collect evidence and frame institutional alternatives;
  • an event coalition can render national identity into place, route and audience; and
  • an enforcement network can test whether commercial identity matches production fact.

Their limitation is not failure. It is the legal architecture. Recommendation still needs bargaining; diagnosis still needs an operational holder; spectacle still needs permits and performance; verification still needs a case and remedy.

The crumbling-and-conforming thesis therefore becomes more exact: old institutions are often not replaced; they are surrounded by temporary interpreters that define the terms under which their next decision becomes publicly legible.
