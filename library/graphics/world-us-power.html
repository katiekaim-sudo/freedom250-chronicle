Freedom 250 · Foreign relations and world money · International bodies

What can the U.S. block or decide in each international body, and what can't it?

Across 27 bodies, U.S. power sits in five different places: a veto, consensus seat or blocking share in 5 (United Nations, NATO, International Monetary Fund, ITER, Board of Peace), money leverage in 8, domestic implementation in 8, command or facility access in 5 and agenda power in 6. A gate can stop specified action but does not give command: at the UN Security Council, procedural matters require nine votes and are not vetoable; affirmative action still needs nine votes.
