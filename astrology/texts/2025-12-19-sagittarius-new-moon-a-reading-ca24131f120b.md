# 2025-12-19 Sagittarius New Moon — A Reading

The seed is a new cycle of confidence and appetite: what the nation is willing to wager, celebrate or believe about its future. It is begun at a threshold, the last word of one sign before the institutional sign begins.

The constructive pole is a new cycle of national confidence that accepts its debts honestly. The shadow is a wager made at the threshold, on credit held in the hidden house, against a fog the year never cleared.
