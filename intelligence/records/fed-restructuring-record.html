2026-06-29 · SCOTUS · Occurred · Courts and removal

Supreme Court decides Trump v. Cook stay application

The Supreme Court denied the government's request to stay the injunction protecting Cook. The Court held that the government's legal position would effectively convert for-cause tenure into at-will employment; required notice and an opportunity to respond before removal; left the ultimate factual cause determination unresolved; and held that the Fed's removal protection is constitutionally consistent with a distinct historical tradition of independent central banking.

2025-07-18 · Congress · Occurred · Stablecoins and tokenization

The GENIUS Act became law, establishing a federal and state framework for permitted payment-stablecoin issuers, reserve assets, redemption, supervision, foreign issuers, and certification of state regulatory regimes.
