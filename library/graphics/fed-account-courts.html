Freedom 250 · Banks and the dollar · Courts

What have courts decided about who gets a Fed account?

So far the courts side with the Fed. Two appeals courts (the Tenth Circuit for Custodia in 2025 and the Second Circuit for Banco San Juan in 2026) held that being eligible for a Fed account does not entitle a bank to one. Custodia has asked the Supreme Court to take the case, and the Fed opposed review on October 13, 2026.
