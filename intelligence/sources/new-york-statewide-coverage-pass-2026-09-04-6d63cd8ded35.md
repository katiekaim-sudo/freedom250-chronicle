# New York Statewide Manufacturing and Innovation Coverage Pass — 2026-09-04

## Answer

The 59-node map is a useful strategic backbone, not a complete statewide
establishment census. A county-first and region-first rerun found **110
additional site-level candidates** that clear the current physical-function
rule and are absent from the 59-node registry. Of these, **75 belong in the
Science & Innovation core** and **35 are Industrial interest**. The split is a
research lens, not a quality ranking.

The map implementation applies a narrower **company-only admission rule** to
this statewide expansion. It adds **97 company sites**: **64 Science &
Innovation** and **33 Industrial interest**. Twelve university, nonprofit,
government and shared-user research sites remain in this report as context but
were not added as new nodes; two Hollingsworth & Vose location references were consolidated into
one physical company site. With the original layer preserved, the governed map
now contains **156 nodes: 150 companies and six clearly labeled research or
industrial-infrastructure context nodes**.

The pass found **108 current operating, laboratory, test, pilot or educational
technical sites**. Two additional site-located projects remain on forward
clocks: Niagara Bottling at Tryon is pre-operating, and the proposed data-center
reuse of the former Remington campus is proposal/remediation only. AngioDynamics
Queensbury remains an operating medical-device site, but its product-transfer
and outsourcing transition must be visible.

Fulton and Herkimer were false negatives in the earlier anchor-led search. They
contain coherent physical capability: Fulton in RF/electromagnetic systems,
medical devices, controls, specialty materials and production; Herkimer in
photonics, turbine machining, titanium/superalloy recovery, technical textiles,
sanitary process equipment and medical products.

## Two independent dimensions

### Research lens

- `science_innovation_core` — the site's defining function is a laboratory,
  pilot/test platform, advanced scientific material, enabling equipment,
  semiconductor/photonics/quantum system, high-technology electronics,
  medical/biotech platform, aerospace/defense technology or energy system.
- `industrial_interest` — the site is useful for understanding New York's
  production base, workforce, freight, water, supply chain or industrial
  geography, but the reviewed receipt does not establish a distinct
  science/R&D/pilot function as the reason for admission.

### Project state

Project state remains separate: `operating`, `rd_pilot`,
`construction_commissioning`, `awarded_preconstruction` or `proposal_permit`.
An industrial-interest plant can be operating; a core-science project can still
be only proposed. Neither lens upgrades its clock.

## Map ruling

The September 4 implementation:

1. adds a `research_lens` field to every node and keeps that dimension separate
   from industry and project state;
2. adds an independent node-type filter, defaulted to **Companies**;
3. admits 97 operating companies and named company projects from this pass;
4. preserves the six previously mapped research or industrial-infrastructure
   context nodes without treating them as companies;
5. preserves separate physical plants of one company while consolidating the
   duplicated H&V location reference; and
6. retains holds and the 12 newly reviewed institutional/public research sites
   off-map.

## Boundary

- Evidence cutoff: **2026-09-04 ET**.
- The pass is statewide but is not proof that every New York manufacturer has
  been found. Negative county findings mean no admission-grade result was found
  in the bounded pass, not that the county has no relevant business.
- Company, SEC, agency and government records establish physical functions;
  they do not establish customer relationships, utilization, market share or a
  Micron/GlobalFoundries/Wolfspeed supply relationship unless separately cited.
- Ticker labels describe the mapped company or a named controlling public
  parent. They are not attributed from customers, investors or partners.
- The governed registry and standalone map now carry the company-only expansion
  as a 156-node projection. The Chronicle, watch calendar and installed app
  were not changed by this pass.
