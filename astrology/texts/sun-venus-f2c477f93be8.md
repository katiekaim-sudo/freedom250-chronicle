# Sun–Venus

## The planets

**Sun** — *Authority, illumination, "everything revolves around the Sun."* MUNDANE: the head of government (the President); the executive branch; the focus of executive power. The Sun's condition shows how the government will fare. (Greer: "the Sun represents the actual head of government — whoever officially calls the shots in the executive branch.")

**Venus** — *Attraction, harmony, pleasure, value.* MUNDANE: the economy (especially consumer spending, luxury goods, and entertainment); the arts; women's issues and social welfare; diplomacy and friendly foreign relations; tourism; national morale and the public's sense of contentment. Venus shows whether the people feel prosperous and at ease.

## The combination — mundane

Like Sun-Mercury, Sun-Venus stays close, so the conjunction is the primary aspect. Greer describes it as favorable for "national prosperity; tourism, entertainment, consumer expenditures driving growth; friendly foreign relations; favorable for women's issues." This is the soft-power conjunction — the nation looks attractive, diplomacy runs on charm rather than threats, and the economy benefits from people spending money on things that make them happy. Trade agreements reached under this influence tend to be mutually beneficial. The entertainment and tourism industries do well.

The only hostile aspect possible between Sun and Venus is the semisquare (45 degrees), which Greer treats as minor. Even at its worst, Sun-Venus friction is mild — a diplomatic faux pas, a consumer confidence dip, a cultural moment that lands awkwardly rather than gracefully. There is no Sun-Venus square or opposition to worry about.

This makes Sun-Venus one of the most reliably positive triggers in mundane work. When it activates, it brings a window of goodwill, economic buoyancy, and successful diplomacy. Women's issues and social welfare legislation get favorable attention. The President benefits from personal likability rather than fear or respect.

**As trigger:** Sun-Venus conjunctions are fast-moving (days to about a week of orb) and mark brief windows when soft power, economic sentiment, and diplomatic goodwill activate whatever broader planetary themes are in play.
