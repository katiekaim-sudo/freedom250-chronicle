# WHO and IHR — Membership Exit Map

Updated: 2026-08-15  
Status: factual legal map  
Rule: Notice is not membership end. Membership end is not a settled assessment. IHR and the Pandemic Agreement are not the WHO Constitution.

## Short answer

EO 14155 (Jan. 20, 2025) says the United States “intends to withdraw from the WHO,” revokes the January 20, 2021 letter that had retracted the 2020 notice, and tells State to inform the UN Secretary-General and WHO leadership. 22 U.S.C. § 290c is the U.S. reservation: Congress reserved the right to withdraw “on a one-year notice,” provided “the financial obligations of the United States to the Organization shall be met in full for the Organization's current fiscal year.” The WHO Constitution, as Congress itself noted in that section, has no withdrawal clause.

HHS and State announced on January 22, 2026 that the United States had completed withdrawal. CDC’s official fact sheet: “following the required one-year notice period, the United States Government formally exited from WHO on January 22, 2026.”

EO 14155 § 4 is a separate clock: while withdrawal is in progress, State “will cease negotiations on the WHO Pandemic Agreement and the amendments to the International Health Regulations, and actions taken to effectuate such agreement and amendments will have no binding force on the United States.” That is a claim about two other instruments. It is not the membership exit.

## 2. Legal stack

**22 U.S.C. § 290c** (text in effect Aug. 14, 2026):

> In adopting this subchapter the Congress does so with the understanding that, in the absence of any provision in the World Health Organization Constitution for withdrawal from the Organization, the United States reserves its right to withdraw from the Organization on a one-year notice: *Provided, however*, That the financial obligations of the United States to the Organization shall be met in full for the Organization's current fiscal year.

Three objects inside that sentence:

1. Congress knew the WHO Constitution has **no** withdrawal clause.
2. The U.S. reserved a **one-year notice**.
3. Current-year **financial obligations** still have to be met.

**EO 14155** is the executive instrument that starts that notice and directs the pause, the personnel recall, and the depositary letter.

**IHR and the Pandemic Agreement** are not the Constitution. § 4 of the order says negotiations cease and that actions to effectuate those instruments “will have no binding force on the United States.” That is the Executive’s position on two other texts. It is not a judicial holding. It is not membership.

**Paris / UNESCO** are sibling exits (EO 14162; State UNESCO notice with a Dec. 31, 2026 effective date under the UNESCO Constitution). Different persons. Different clocks. Do not merge.

## 3. EO 14155 — what it orders

Signed January 20, 2025. Published January 29, 2025. 90 FR 8361.

- § 2(a): the United States intends to withdraw. The Jan. 20, 2021 retraction letter is revoked.
- § 2(d): State and OMB, with all practicable speed — pause future transfers to WHO; recall and reassign U.S. personnel or contractors working with WHO; identify other partners.
- § 3: State shall immediately inform the UN Secretary-General, any other depositary, and WHO leadership.
- § 4: cease IHR-amendment and Pandemic Agreement negotiations; those instruments “will have no binding force on the United States.”
- § 5: implement consistent with law and appropriations.

## 6. Entity Theory read

A membership exit is the outward analogue of a domestic dissolution. The group stops consolidating this supranational person. Control of the health-diplomacy function comes home — or goes bilateral — while the WHO Constitution still has no exit clause. The U.S. reserved one anyway, in 1948, and wrote a money proviso into the reservation. The January 2026 HHS/State announcement is Clock 2 (membership). Clock 3 (assessments) is still a ledger question. Do not flip the node to “gone” on a press headline from day one of the term.
