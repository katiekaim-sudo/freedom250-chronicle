## 2024–2026 Leo Moon family

The February 1, 2026 Leo Full Moon is not merely a Full Moon with the public in
the first house. It is the return of an August 4, 2024 Leo seed within
**0.494124°**, with Leo rising at both endpoints and the Moon returning to the
first house. Between them, the May 4, 2025 First Quarter installs the same
Moon-to-Pluto empty-arc boundary that the Full Moon later retains, returns Pluto
within **0.092201°**, and places Mars almost exactly opposite its later
position.

The family therefore carries two simultaneous movements:

1. the seed's national-body degree returns almost exactly; and
2. the quarter builds a public-versus-concentrated-power container before the
   harvest arrives.

But the astronomical sky adds a necessary correction. At exact Full phase in
Washington, the Leo Moon is still **1.815° below** the true horizon. It rises
13.311 minutes later. The Sun remains **2.814° above** the horizon and sets
18.924 minutes later. Symbolically, the Moon is angular in the first house;
physically, the public light has not yet appeared. The Full Moon perfects in the
short threshold before moonrise and sunset exchange places.

That distinction changes the title of the story. The public is not already
fully visible. It is reaching the horizon.
