# Conjunction and Persona Method for the Inside Delivery Clocks

**Posture:** proposed extension under the governed Long Clock persona method  
**Scope:** 17 phaseable non-luminary relationships led by Mercury, Venus, or Mars; Mercury–Venus held separately  
**Authority:** conditional research review only; zero factual-evidence or convergence vote

## 1. The identities must not collapse

Four objects coexist:

1. **The relationship clock** — the continuous conjunction-to-opposition-to-conjunction phase story.
2. **The conjunction family** — the whole seed episode, which can contain one or several exact passes.
3. **An exact pass chart** — the chart of one conjunction recontact inside the family.
4. **The family persona candidate** — the first later geocentric tropical Sun crossing of one reviewed display coordinate.

The family is the seed. A pass is not a second seed merely because it is exact. A persona is not a second chart vote, second clock, event timer, or successor seed.

## 2. Proposed display coordinate

For this review package, the earliest chronological exact pass supplies the deterministic provisional display/persona coordinate.

- In a single-pass family, the coordinate is unambiguous, although the delivery clock itself is still only proposed.
- In a multipass family, the earliest-pass choice remains pending Katie's disposition.
- Every later exact pass retains its own conjunction chart.
- Later passes describe revision, development, or completion within the same seed family. They do not receive another persona or another vote.

The seven multipass families were reviewed against their complete pass charts. No later pass was substituted merely because it was stronger, more angular, more dignified, or more visibly operative; that would replace inception with hindsight. Five of those families govern the current chart window and therefore have provisional persona candidates. Two are future brackets and remain withheld.

## 3. Candidate calculation and successor gate

The technical candidate is:

> the first later geocentric tropical crossing of the fixed provisional conjunction longitude by the Sun.

This is called a **first-later solar crossing**, not a solar return. The candidate is rendered only when it precedes the next family's first exact conjunction pass.

| Result | Meaning |

A missing persona is an explicit gate result. The calculation does not by itself prove a metaphysical lifecycle claim.

## 6. Seed-first reading order

Read a family in this order:

1. **Relationship question.** What enduring dialogue does the pair carry?
2. **Family conjunction anatomy.** Sign, Whole Sign house, condition of both planets, chart ruler, tenth-house ruler, actual MC ruler, dispositors, major relationships, configurations, and whole-pattern structure.
3. **Complete pass series.** What changes across revision and completion without becoming a new origin?
4. **Candidate gate.** Did the first-later solar crossing occur before the successor family, and is the coordinate itself reviewed?
5. **Persona arena.** Persona Sun house, Ascendant, chart ruler, tenth-house/public routes, and actual MC.
6. **Two focal lenses.** Where and how does each conjunction planet now work? Do they remain fused or acquire different jobs?
7. **Condition and topology changes.** Direction, sign, dignity, dispositors, aspect-lane entries/exits, configuration change, and derived angle relationships.
8. **Adjacency audit.** Disclose the interval to the next exact family pass and to the next governing ingress or lunation. Close adjacency does not invalidate the candidate, but it downgrades any claim of independent novelty.
9. **Seed/persona overlay.** Tight non-mechanical contacts only, with dependency classes preserved.
10. **Ingress/lunation test.** Only after the parent chart hierarchy already earns the relationship should the persona narrow the question.
11. **Factual disposition.** Transition evidence and continuity evidence remain outside astrology.

## 7. Novelty classes

### Guaranteed or astronomically bounded

- The persona Sun repeats the fixed target longitude.
- The seed conjunction is exact.
- A slow focal can remain near its prior degree over a short interval.
- Persona-Sun contact to the slow focal often restates the target.

These are structural facts, not new interpretive votes.

### Dependent carryover

- Later exact passes in the same family.
- Persistent slow-body geometry without a changed condition or relationship.
- Ordinary dissolution of the original exact conjunction.
- House movement produced only by the new derived Ascendant.
- Very-short-interval repetition of sign, house, route, and configuration.
- Apparent novelty duplicated by an adjacent exact family pass or governing ingress/lunation.

### Potentially differentiating, still zero-vote

- the two focal planets separating by sign, house, condition, direction, or dispositorship;
- an exact focal station or direction change near the candidate;
- a material dignity or sign change;
- a changed chart ruler or public-governance route;
- a genuine whole-pattern or configuration change;
- a tight new focal relationship to the persona ASC or MC;
- an overlay contact that is neither the target repetition nor slow-body carryover.

### Unsafe inferences

- treating a candidate as proof that the pair manifested;
- treating persona absence as failure, doom, or automatic renewal;
- deriving event dates from the persona;
- giving each pass or chart feature a separate vote;
- letting a pair permanently own Crypto, Iran, AI, or any other plot;
- using an astrological match as factual corroboration;
- promoting a provisional anchor, delivery clock, or package without review.

## 8. Storyline use

The persona layer is most useful as a narrowing device:

- the relationship clock says **which developmental chapter**;
- the current direct aspect says **how the planets speak in the governing chart**;
- the conjunction chart says **what condition and structure seeded this family**;
- the persona candidate may say **where, through whom, or under what hidden term that seed operates**;
- the factual ledger says **whether reality changed at all**.

All pair-to-plot mappings remain many-to-many and question-led.
