The Charter Queue — Factual Timeline

This note is the canonical source. The Observatory view (The Charter Queue.html, sub-view of obs-om) is derived — edit here, then re-run build_charter_seed.py → build_charter_astro.py → build_charter_timeline.py. Never hand-edit the JSON or the HTML.

What it tracks: the legal operating clock of the private monetary stack — application → conditional approval → final approval → consummation → account access. Not the company build clock (founding → product → pilot → production). A press release moves the first; only a regulator moves the second.

The seven tracks

  • The Door — the OCC charter queue. Who applied, who was conditionally approved, who consummated.
  • The Gate — Federal Reserve account access. Master accounts, Reserve Bank stock, Custodia. The door nobody has walked through.
  • The Ledger — clearing and depository permission. PSSC, DTC's tokenization service, the Collateral AppChain.
  • The Rulebook — the incumbents absorbing the new rails while keeping the constitution. Swift, Visa, Mastercard, the capital-treatment guidance.
  • The Coin — the issuers and their obligors. Who owes the holder money, and who can freeze it.
  • The Interior — the permissioned bank rails. Fnality, Partior, Canton, Kinexys, Broadridge — where regulated balance sheets actually meet.
  • The Stack — the acquisitions. Vertical integration is moving faster than any protocol merger.

Status vocabulary: Occurred · Proposed · Scheduled · Public position · Research. Month-precision dates get no chart — month is not a moment, and the vault does not fake one.

The record

2021 — Anchorage Digital Bank, N.A. receives the first digital-asset national trust charter

Status: Occurred Entities: OCC; Anchor Labs; Anchorage Digital Bank, N.A. Track: The Door

The OCC charters the first national trust bank built for digital assets, under a 2021 operating agreement. Five years later it is still the only member of the cohort that has been a chartered, operating national bank the whole way through. A trust charter is not a commercial-bank charter: no insured deposits, no lending, no discount window, and no Federal Reserve master account.

Source: OCC interpretations and decisions

2019-05 — Fnality International is established

Status: Occurred Entities: Fnality International Limited; shareholder banks Track: The Interior

A private company limited by shares, England and Wales, owned by a consortium of banks and market utilities. Its purpose is the one thing no stablecoin can offer: a settlement asset that is central-bank money, held in a pooled omnibus account at the Bank of England.

Source: Fnality

2021 — Partior is founded by DBS, J.P. Morgan and Temasek

Status: Occurred Entities: Partior; DBS; J.P. Morgan; Temasek; Standard Chartered Track: The Interior

Out of the Monetary Authority of Singapore's Project Ubin. The settled asset is tokenized commercial-bank money — a liability of the participating settlement bank, not of Partior, and not of any central bank. Whoever the money is owed by is the whole question.

Source: Partior

2022-08-31 — The sterling Fnality Payment System is recognized as a UK payment system

Status: Occurred Entities: Fnality UK Limited; HM Treasury; Bank of England Track: The Interior

Formal recognition. The pooled central-bank funds sit in trust in a Bank of England RTGS omnibus account. This is the only place in the whole sweep where a private tokenized rail settles in genuine central-bank money — and it took an act of national recognition to get there.

Source: Bank of England — FMI supervision

2023-12 — The sterling Fnality system makes its first payments

Status: Occurred Entities: Fnality UK Limited; Lloyds; Santander; UBS Track: The Interior

Three banks, an initial proving phase. Live — in the narrowest of the five senses of that word.

Source: Fnality

2024-07 — Canton Coin genesis

Status: Occurred Entities: Canton Network; Canton Foundation; Digital Asset Track: The Interior

No premine, no presale, no founder or venture allocation. The network is not owned by the company that builds its technology — a distinction the sweep insists on and most reporting collapses.

Source: Canton Network

2024-12 — The sterling Fnality system operates under Bank of England limits

Status: Occurred Entities: Fnality UK Limited; Bank of England Track: The Interior

The Bank's own classification, and it controls over any marketing language. The scaling gate is a supervisory permission, not a technical milestone — one regulator's decision stands between this and the whole "central-bank money on chain" pitch.

Source: Bank of England FMI report 2025-26

2025-02-04 — Stripe completes its acquisition of Bridge

Status: Occurred Entities: Stripe; Bridge Track: The Stack

The merchant-payments giant pulls stablecoin orchestration inside the house. Wallet infrastructure (Privy), a proprietary chain (Tempo) and a proposed trust bank follow. The pattern of the whole era in one deal: buy the missing layer, do not build a coalition.

Source: Stripe

2025-05-17 — Kinexys, Chainlink and Ondo complete a cross-chain delivery-versus-payment test

Status: Occurred Entities: J.P. Morgan Kinexys; Chainlink; Ondo Track: The Interior

Ondo's tokenized Treasuries formed the asset leg; J.P. Morgan deposits formed the payment leg. A transaction path was proved. A production service was not.

Source: J.P. Morgan

2025-06-23 — Fiserv announces FIUSD

Status: Occurred Entities: Fiserv Track: The Coin

A stablecoin marketed into the core banking and merchant systems Fiserv already runs. Thirteen months later the public record still does not name the issuer, the redemption obligor or the reserve custodian. A coin with no stated debtor is not yet a coin.

Source: Fiserv FIUSD

2025-06-24 — Digital Asset raises $135 million

Status: Occurred Entities: Digital Asset; DRW; Tradeweb; DTCC; Goldman Sachs; Citadel Securities; BNP Paribas; Circle Ventures; Paxos; Virtu Track: The Stack

The dealers, the market makers, the banks, the depository itself, and the crypto firms all buy into the company that builds Canton. When DTCC is an investor in the ledger that will carry DTC's tokens, "disruption" is not the word for what is happening.

Source: Digital Asset

2025-08-07 — Ripple and the SEC dismiss their appeals; the judgment stands

Status: Occurred Entities: Ripple Labs; SEC; U.S. District Court (S.D.N.Y.) Track: The Coin

The district court's final judgment survives: a $125,035,150 civil penalty and an injunction against unregistered institutional offers and sales. The long war ends not in vindication but in a paid fine and a standing injunction — and Ripple turns immediately toward the charter.

Source: SEC v. Ripple docket

2025-08-07 — NYDFS fines Paxos $26.5 million over the Binance relationship

Status: Occurred Entities: NYDFS; Paxos Trust Company Track: The Coin

A $26.5M penalty plus $22M of mandated compliance investment, for historic anti-money-laundering and transaction-monitoring failures around the BUSD partnership. The lesson the sweep draws is the one that matters: a regulated issuer can keep its reserves perfectly and still fail at watching its distributor. Backing is not oversight.

Source: NYDFS enforcement

2025-08-28 — Anchorage files a Federal Reserve access request

Status: Occurred Entities: Anchorage Digital Bank, N.A.; Federal Reserve Track: The Gate

Tier 3. It is the most advanced request in the entire cohort, filed by the only member with a five-year-old national charter. As of the Fed database's currency date of 2026-05-31 it is still pending — nine months, no answer, no denial. This single line is the whole private stack's position in one fact.

Source: Federal Reserve access-request database

The neutral connector takes a governance seat on the network it connects. The middleware vendor is now a governed market participant with a vote. The sweep flags the conflict plainly; nobody has resolved it.

2025-09-25 — Deutsche Bank completes the first live euro cross-border payment on Partior

Real money, real corridor. Each settlement bank still issues its own liability — the ledger is shared, the debt is not.

Source: Partior

2025-10-24 — Ripple closes the Hidden Road acquisition; it becomes Ripple Prime

Status: Occurred Entities: Ripple; Hidden Road; Ripple Prime Track: The Stack

$1.25 billion for a prime brokerage. With Metaco and Standard Custody (custody), Rail (payments) and GTreasury (corporate treasury), Ripple has bought itself an end-to-end institutional money circuit. What it still does not have is an account at the Federal Reserve.

Source: Ripple

2025-11-25 — Paxos announces the acquisition of Fordefi

Status: Occurred Entities: Paxos; Fordefi Track: The Stack

Institutional wallet and custody infrastructure, pulled inside. The missing layer, bought rather than built — again.

Source: Paxos

2025-12-01 — Anchorage's USAT issuance policies take effect

Status: Occurred Entities: Anchorage Digital Bank, N.A.; USAT Track: The Coin

The reserves sit in a segregated South Dakota express trust — legally separate from the OCC-chartered bank whose name is on the coin. Even inside one brand, the obligor is a different body from the bank.

Source: Anchorage Digital

2025-12-12 — The OCC conditionally approves five digital-asset trust banks in a single day

Status: Occurred Entities: OCC; Circle / First National Digital Currency Bank; Ripple National Trust Bank; BitGo; Fidelity Digital Assets; Paxos Track: The Door

Five charters, one day, one agency — the same agency that chartered the National Banks in 1863. Conditional approval is not final approval, and final approval is not an open bank: each of the five must still satisfy pre-opening conditions, and each must separately apply for Federal Reserve Bank stock, which is a membership step and not an account.

Source: OCC, 2025-12-12

2025-12-12 — Paxos Trust Company, N.A. — conversion effective at 5:00 p.m.

Status: Occurred Time: 17:00 Entities: OCC; Paxos Trust Company, N.A. Track: The Door

The New York limited-purpose trust becomes a national trust bank at a stated hour — one of the very few births in this corpus that carries a clock. Not FDIC insured. No deposits. Expected to apply for Federal Reserve Bank stock. Marketed as "GENIUS-ready"; what is actually true is the conversion.

Source: OCC, 2025-12-12

2025-12-17 — DTCC and Digital Asset announce a DTC Treasury-token path on Canton

Status: Occurred Entities: DTCC; DTC; Cede & Co.; Digital Asset; Canton Network Track: The Ledger

The design, read carefully, is the opposite of the headline. The conventional position is immobilised in a Digital Omnibus Account; the securities stay registered to Cede & Co.; DTC mints token representations of participant entitlements and treats its LedgerScan record as part of DTC's own official books. Settlement is not digitised at launch. The chain is a transport surface. Title never leaves the building.

Source: DTCC

2025-12 — DTCC and Euroclear become co-chairs of the Canton Foundation

Status: Occurred Entities: DTCC; Euroclear; Canton Foundation Track: The Interior

The incumbent depositories do not merely connect to the new network. They take the chair of the body that governs it.

Source: Canton Foundation

2026-01-15 — The Canton working group demonstrates a cross-border multi-asset repo

Status: Occurred Entities: Canton Network; LSEG Digital Settlement House; European government bonds; U.S. Treasuries Track: The Interior

European government bonds and U.S. Treasuries against euro and dollar cash, including tokenized commercial-bank deposits supplied through LSEG. Staged, real assets, working-group only — not an open market. Collateral is where the new stack becomes load-bearing, and this is what the frontier actually looks like.

Source: Canton Network

2026-01-22 — BitGo Holdings lists on the NYSE

2026-02-09 — The OCC terminates Anchorage's 2021 operating agreement

The bank is found compliant with all ten actionable articles; the corrective actions are judged effective and sustainable. The charter is not terminated — the leash is. A supervisory release, and a real one. It changes nothing about the Federal Reserve request still sitting in the queue.

2026-02-12 — Bridge National Trust Bank receives preliminary conditional approval

Status: Occurred Entities: OCC; Bridge Ventures LLC; Stripe Track: The Door

Stripe's stablecoin arm asks for a national trust charter and gets a conditional yes. The letter is explicit: pre-opening conditions must be met, the proposed bank must apply for Federal Reserve Bank stock, and none of this confirms a master account or authority to commence business.

2026-02 — Ripple receives a full Electronic Money Institution licence in Luxembourg

Status: Occurred Entities: Ripple; Luxembourg (CSSF) Track: The Door

The European door opens before the American one. Note what it is and is not: an EMI licence is not a bank charter, and Ripple's own aggregate count of "75+ licences globally" should never be read as 75 bank charters.

2026-03 — The federal banking agencies clarify capital treatment for tokenized securities

Status: Occurred Entities: OCC; Federal Reserve; FDIC Track: The Rulebook

Tokenized securities generally take the same capital treatment as the non-tokenized form. A technical clarification, not a product approval — and precisely the kind of quiet ruling that lets an incumbent adopt the new wrapper without changing anything else.

Source: Interagency guidance

The bank exports a commercial-bank deposit liability onto a public network for eligible institutional clients — while keeping the deposit claim, the compliance perimeter, the client relationship and the ledger inside the bank. Nine chains, 130+ stablecoin-linked card programmes, 50+ countries, up 50% quarter on quarter. Visa-defined programme metrics, not audited totals — and not one gram of membership, netting, fraud or dispute authority has moved. The settlement asset became pluggable. The rulebook did not.

2026-05-12 — DTCC and Chainlink collaborate on 24/7 collateral management

The Collateral AppChain is a third object — not DTC's entitlement-token service, not the Stellar connection. They may become interoperable. They do not share a ledger, a validator set, a legal operator, a cash asset or a rulebook merely because DTCC is in all three.

2026-05-27 — The SEC grants Paxos Securities Settlement Company temporary clearing-agency registration

Status: Occurred Entities: SEC; Paxos Securities Settlement Company, LLC (PSSC) Track: The Ledger

Temporary — up to eighteen months — for a central securities depository and securities settlement system, limited to the securities and services in its application. It is not a central counterparty. 2026-05-27 — SoFiUSD launches — the first stablecoin issued by a U.S. national bank

The one clean vertical in the whole sweep: charter-holder, issuer and consumer brand are the same body. And the coin it issues is not an insured deposit. 2026-07-11 — Kraken's claimed Federal Reserve account is not in the public record

Status: Research Entities: Payward; Kraken Bank (Wyoming SPDI); Federal Reserve Track: The Gate

Kraken's group states that its Wyoming special-purpose depository holds a limited-purpose Federal Reserve account. The Fed's public access database does not corroborate it under any known Kraken or Payward name. Either Kraken is the first crypto-adjacent body through the door in American history, or the claim inflated in the telling. Unconfirmed, and checkable — which makes it a watch, not a finding.

2026-07-11 — The Ripple master-account claim is a conflation

Status: Research Entities: Ripple; Standard Custody & Trust Company; Federal Reserve; OCC Track: The Gate

The OCC's conditional-approval letter says the proposed Ripple National Trust Bank will apply for Federal Reserve Bank stock — a membership step. That is not a master-account request, and no Ripple or Standard Custody request was found in the Fed's public database. Membership, stock, a reserve account and a confirmed master account are four different things, and the retail story treats them as one.

Source: OCC conditional approval 1359
