2026 conjunction-radix relays

A conjunction does not seed only a zodiacal degree. It also arrives through a complete Washington chart: horizon, meridian, ruler, ruler house, pair house, dispositor roots and internal geometry. This companion asks whether those larger structures reappear in the twenty-nine registered 2026 ingress and lunation charts.

The answer is yes, but sparsely and in different ways. One current chart may inherit an old frame, another an old command root, and another a reciprocal exchange between the seed degree and the seed chart's horizon. None of those relations gives the conjunction chart a second vote. The current ingress or lunation remains governor.

What the computer compares

The audit loads only the canonical radix member of each of the ten Long Clock conjunction dossiers. The later persona charts are not loaded and retain zero independent vote.

For each conjunction radix the projection preserves:

  • ASC and MC coordinates and signs;
  • chart ruler and chart-ruler house;
  • the conjunction pair's Whole Sign house;
  • final dispositors and bounded-loop members; and
  • the horizon and meridian as axes rather than four independent points.

Each 2026 chart remains a complete chart object. The smaller comparison tables are reversible views joined by chart ID; they do not replace the whole chart. Lunations contribute their light, horizon and meridian axes. Ingresses contribute the ingress Sun, horizon and meridian. Exact axis strength remains the standing ≤0.25° band.

This is therefore a practical example of the right computer architecture for the astrology corpus: preserve the whole chart, then generate typed projections for specific questions.

That is a coherent archetypal chronology. It is not evidence that the three conjunctions caused or secretly governed one historical program.

The Aries ingress is a two-radix splice

> The 2026 Aries ingress repeats the 2026 Saturn–Neptune conjunction radix's > Gemini rising · Mercury ruler · Mercury in the 10th house frame.

But its command root comes from somewhere else. The ingress's exact Jupiter–Mars–Moon root is the root of the 1965 Uranus–Pluto conjunction radix.

The ingress is therefore not “the Saturn–Neptune chart again.” It is a two-radix splice:

Saturn–Neptune outer frame + Uranus–Pluto command root

That is a more precise inheritance claim than either a degree hit or a broad outer-planet analogy. The seasonal chart itself still governs the season.

The raw axis count is not the result

After collapsing the near-identical Saturn–Pluto and Jupiter–Saturn frames, the ten radices become nine axis corridors. The exact audit finds:

  • 22 axis receipts;
  • spread across 16 of 29 charts;
  • touching all nine frame corridors.

A 1,439-offset whole-frame rotation control places twenty-two receipts at only the 83.74th descriptive percentile. The control median is eighteen. The raw count is therefore not rare enough to carry the interpretation.

The useful information is relational:

  • which old chart supplies a current frame;
  • which supplies the command root;
  • whether seed degree and seed frame form a reciprocal exchange; and
  • whether several historically different frames concentrate in one current chart.

That is also why the pair-house scan is retained only as dependent context. For example, Saturn and Neptune repeat their conjunction-radix 11H placement in several 2026 charts, but their continuing proximity and similar horizons make those repetitions structurally dependent. They are not additional evidence votes.

What changes in the method

The conjunction chart now has four separately named ways to recur:

  1. Degree inheritance — the conjunction coordinate contacts a current body, light or angle.
  2. Frame inheritance — ASC sign, ruler and ruler house recur.
  3. Command inheritance — the complete dispositor-root signature recurs.
  4. Axis inheritance — a seed horizon or meridian contacts a current light, horizon or meridian.

A fifth, rarer label—reciprocal seed/frame relay—is admitted only when the same cycle and current chart carry both a seed-degree hinge and a seed-axis hinge.

These labels describe different structures and cannot be added as votes. They let the computer preserve the whole-chart context while asking bounded questions of it.

Controls and limits

  • The ten canonical conjunction radices are admitted; persona charts are excluded and have no independent vote.
  • Saturn–Pluto and Jupiter–Saturn remain separately named charts but count as one axis corridor where their nearly identical frames would inflate totals.
  • Every axis is counted once; its opposite endpoint remains inspectable.
  • Pair-house recurrence is dependent context, never a receipt.
  • Prospective registered charts after the September 25 cutoff are structure, not occurred events.
  • No recurrence supplies factual evidence, corroboration, causal proof or an automatic Forecast Ledger call.
  • Locked chart readings remain unchanged. This is a companion layer.
