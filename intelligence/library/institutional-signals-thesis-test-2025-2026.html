Research summary

The completed fixed-window program contains 145 unique normalized official-source signals across 87 of 114 governed surfaces, with all 114 surfaces terminal. Its companion dated buildout map contains 90 object-specific builds across 67 surfaces: 84 active and 6 paused, stale or item-level access-limited. The strongest live clusters are common data grammar, machine-readable assurance, conditional permission, public-service records, shared-service migration, capital gates and testbed-to-deployment chains. Together they support incremental, incumbent-contained institutional refactoring through a distributed translation-and-gate layer while agencies, Boards, Secretaries and statutory officials usually retain adoption, funding, finality and remedy authority. Incumbent absorption and ordinary modernization remain strongly supported rivals. Founding-scale authority transfer, one hidden command structure and broad measured public benefit are not established; effect and affected-person remedy remain the decisive gaps.
