Treasury Do Not Pay — Governmentwide Structural Map

What does Treasury Do Not Pay screen, how far does it reach, and who owns the actual eligibility or adverse-action decision?

Research through 2026-08-29

Home room: Government ChangesAlso in: Citizenship, Voting & Immigration
The short version (draft, not yet reviewed by Katie)

Do Not Pay flags payments, but each program makes the call

Treasury's Do Not Pay is a shared screening network that checks payees against death, debt, exclusion and other records before money goes out. A match is only a signal; the agency or state program must check it and make its own legal decision.

What we found

  • Treasury runs the system, its data links, portal, API, state hubs and a payment-verification checkpoint. Treasury's own help pages say a match does not stop a payment by itself.
  • States can use it for federally funded programs through PARIS (Medicaid, TANF, SNAP, child care and more) and a Labor Department hub for unemployment insurance. That does not reach purely state-funded spending.
  • OMB memo M-25-32 (August 20, 2025) gave a four-year waiver from separate computer-matching agreements. Public Law 119-77 (February 10, 2026) strengthens state death-data sharing, effective December 27, 2026.
  • A May 29, 2026 OMB proposed rule would require states to screen before paying out federal funds. It was still proposed, not final, at the August 29 cutoff.
  • OPM and Treasury published a retirement-payment matching notice on August 26, 2026, with a legal start of September 25. OPM may not suspend or cut benefits on a Do Not Pay match alone. Treasury's $11.7 billion FY2025 figure mixes categories and is not audited fraud savings.

Why it matters

This is the thesis at work in plain view: Treasury is becoming a common checkpoint before federal money moves, built on an old law through executive orders, memos and matching notices, not a new agency. Legal decisions still sit with each program.

What to watch

  • 2026-09-25 · Whether OPM's retirement-payment matching actually began on schedule, was changed or was delayed.
  • 2026-12-27 · Death-data changes under Public Law 119-77 take effect; look for the sharing method and first use.
  • pending · Final or withdrawn version of OMB's May 29 proposed rule requiring state prepayment screening.

Research summary

Do Not Pay is a governmentwide shared screening network, not a conclusive blacklist: Treasury operates the Working System, controlled data access and a federal payment checkpoint, while each federal or State-administered program must verify a match and apply its own eligibility, notice, contest and recovery authority.
