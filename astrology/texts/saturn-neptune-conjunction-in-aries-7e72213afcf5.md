## Conjunction window

Saturn and Neptune are within orb of conjunction for much of 2025-2026 due to overlapping ingress sequences. The exact aspect at 0°45' Aries on February 20, 2026 is the headline moment; the planets remain within a few degrees of each other for several months before and after.

## Historical parallels

Previous Saturn-Neptune conjunction in Aries: 1702. Era of the early Enlightenment, the War of the Spanish Succession beginning, and Queen Anne's reign.

More recent Saturn-Neptune conjunctions (in other signs):
- 1989 in Capricorn — fall of the Berlin Wall, collapse of Soviet Bloc
- 1952-53 in Libra — Korean War armistice, beginning of Cold War order
- 1917 in Leo — end of WWI's middle phase, Russian Revolution
