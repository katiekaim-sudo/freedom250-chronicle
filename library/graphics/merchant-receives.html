Freedom 250 · Digital money · Payments

When a shop "accepts stablecoins," what does the shop actually receive?

A shop can accept a customer's stablecoin payment and still receive ordinary money. The saved Stripe product credits the merchant in USD, while Shopify defaults to its normal payout currency and lets eligible merchants claim USDC instead. Stablecoin-funded cards also pay shops through ordinary card settlement. Separately, some processors offer stablecoin settlement after shoppers pay normally. The saved records mix live products, limited offerings, pilots and infrastructure; they do not show how most shops are paid.

What's still uncertain

  • The 27 records include checkout, card funding, network settlement, provider infrastructure, payroll, payouts, treasury, Bitcoin and no-product controls. They are not 27 evidenced shop stablecoin products.
  • A merchant currency choice, a payment-provider configuration choice and an interbank settlement option are different decisions.
  • Provider USD balances need not mean immediate receipt into a merchant bank account.
  • Saved production, rollout, pilot and no-evidence labels keep their own cutoffs; this display review does not reverify operation or measure actual merchant elections.
