# Sabian images in the sky story

**Yes: some images add a useful last sentence to a chart.** They are most helpful when they expose a tension already earned by the whole wheel. They cannot choose a political outcome, repair weak geometry or make an older Moon-family return a second vote. The chart's root, actors, houses, aspects and motion still come first; the Sabian image is a caption **after** that hearing.

The canonical lookup is the workbook's `Sabian 360` tab. The key is **floor(degree within sign) + 1**: 5°08′ Taurus is **Taurus 6**, not Taurus 5. [[2027–30 Sabian Luminary Points — COMPUTED DRAFT.json]] applies that rule to the Sun and Moon in **all 100 distinct exact New/Full Moon wheels** already admitted in the 2027–30 annual corpus, including its five early-2031 closing strikes. A New Moon's two lights share **one** image; an exact Full gives two images across the axis. No quarter Moon is given a Sabian caption here. The index is lookup data, not 100 interpretations.
