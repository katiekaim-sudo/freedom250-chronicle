Research summary

Reserve diversification, regional settlement and trade invoicing remain separate from actual monetary substitution. Bessent's April 2026 IIF scorecard adds the incumbent strategy and a three-way legitimacy contest. The IMF symmetry audit finds candid surveillance of systemic members but operationalized, finance-linked adjustment for borrowers. The July 31 Japan–Korea cases and saved August Japanese confirmation distinguish national intervention and dealer prices from a separate stablecoin quote; the disclosed aggregate does not resolve every trade date, allocation or U.S. funding leg. Korea's observed token price transmission is not net capital flow; Japan's limited production rails are not shown to have supported intervention. The September 1 Asheville G20 Chair's Statement adds agreed work on digital assets and payment modernization, but China expressly objected to four paragraphs and the statement itself is not implementation or monetary substitution. Masu's September 10 BOJ speech strengthens the hawkish-normalization direction while preserving separate rate-decision, JGB-stock-runoff, market-stability and digital-rail clocks. The Japan Monetary Constitution build uses balance-sheet movement as its empirical spine. Layer 0 finds that the 2021–2026 JGB–HKD link synchronized BOJ-recorded JGB title with HKMA central-bank money without creating a new claim or merging finality; the September 11 retirement is established, while utilization, rationale and successor remain unknown. Layer 1 finds embedded BOJ independence inside a braided Diet–Cabinet–MOF–BOJ–FSA authority structure: the BOJ can operate a JGB auction without buying the debt, execute MOF intervention without deciding it, and settle government funds without authorizing expenditure. Layer 2 finds a multi-book transition: the BOJ's audited fiscal-2025 balance sheet contracted by ¥66.742 trillion; reserve remuneration more than doubled and the Treasury payment fell; sovereign debt and interest expense rose on separate government clocks; and current MOF/FEFSA intervention is material while the latest monthly operation's direction remains undisclosed. Layer 3 finds asymmetric private transmission: bank core margins improved, but shinkin net income fell and regional banks realized ¥1.136 trillion of bond losses; floating borrowers repriced on staged clocks; GPIF absorbed ¥14.753 trillion of domestic bonds while its bond sleeve lost 5.11 percent; and capital, stress tests and delinquency data still show an orderly system. Layer 4 finds private JGB risk-bearing inside public finality: MOF shortened issuance toward investor capacity, recent benchmark auctions remained covered, and repo and JSCC intermediate funding and default risk, while BOJ-NET retains title and yen settlement and the BOJ retains collateral, scarcity-lending and contingent-purchase authority. Layer 5 finds singleness without sameness: cash, BOJ reserves, bank deposits, safeguarded provider balances, trust stablecoins and tokenized deposits share a denomination but not a debtor, ledger, asset or failure path. JPYSC is live but closed-loop and tiny; DCJPY remains an issuing-bank deposit; both BOJ CBDC tracks remain pre-production. The concrete digital-money balance-sheet change is the 2026 capacity to move up to half of a trust-stablecoin reserve from demand deposits into qualifying short sovereign securities or term deposits, adding valuation, liquidity and contribution clocks without giving tokenholders title to the reserve asset. Layer 6 finds many interfaces but few finality rooms: BOJ-NET supplies wholesale yen finality and JGB title; Zengin's large-value RTGS, small-value DNS and More Time clocks can put customer credit ahead of interbank finality; and its collateral and private mutualized loss rules keep liquidity distinct from loss. Zengin-Net's own 2026 diagnosis supports constitutional refactoring under infrastructure strain, but the conditional fiscal-2030 replacement and unresolved settlement asset do not establish crisis or a completed successor. Layer 7 finds mobility without monetary succession: Agorá's real-value tests, Swift's shared commitments, DCJPY, stablecoins, tokenized JGBs and the live Indonesia–Japan QR corridor move coordination and programmable control faster than they move debtor identity, final yen settlement or statutory title. Retail CBDC would create a direct public BOJ claim but remains unauthorized and simulated. The evidence supports constitutional refactoring through adapters, not current monetary replacement or an established legitimacy crisis. Layer 8 finds public continuity and layered loss: BOJ finality and Articles 33/37/38 liquidity sit at the public center, but BOJ credit is not intended to compensate solvency loss; DICJ insurance and resolution, TLAC, JSCC/Zengin waterfalls, fraud restitution and industry contributions allocate cost before legally gated fiscal support. The evidence supports concentrated classification power and bounded legitimacy pressure, while April 2026 capital, funding and stress results argue against a current systemic or legitimacy crisis. The final Japan proof follows six transaction and failure paths and rules for constitutional refactoring without monetary succession: plural risk-bearing inside public finality. It strongly supports concentrated finality and distributed balance-sheet burden, but not current monetary replacement, systemic crisis or an established legitimacy crisis; unequal protection and burden support only a bounded, falsifiable legitimacy-pressure inference. The governed six-lens visual makes the whole constitution, balance-sheet migration, JGB life cycle, payment-finality chain, digital adapters and failure/loss ladder inspectable while preserving the source owners and bounded states. The September 14 UK delta identifies collateral mobility as the near-term centre of the joint FCA–Bank wholesale-tokenisation program and opens a bounded FCA inquiry into eligible physical-gold ownership tokens, while preserving separate title, custody, collateral, settlement, finality, prudential, perimeter and failure clocks. The records support burden migration and constitutional refactoring, but not a present solvency or legitimacy-crisis claim.
