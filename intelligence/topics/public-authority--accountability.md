# Records, disclosure and accountability

Which record counts, how are official claims checked, and where do disclosure and accountability systems stop?

The accountability branch treats records, disclosures, hearings and sweeps as evidence architectures with their own custody, release, version and correction clocks. It connects those records to the thesis question about control and remedy without turning visibility into truth.

## Records authority and compute are part of the control system

NARA is both the governmentwide records authority for federal AI evidence and a federal customer assembling a brokered hybrid compute stack. CTAC carries the procurement rail; AWS, Azure, Google and specialist integrators occupy different platform and archival roles. A platform or cloud contract does not itself establish records custody, lawful retention, public access or evidentiary completeness.

The accountability question is therefore not only whether an AI system ran, but which record was captured, which authority retained it, what provenance survives, and who can correct or disclose it.

## Disclosure packages preserve source families, not one merged truth

The Disclosure Architecture method compares selection, declassification, bundling, framing and amplification while enforcing a firewall: similarity is not evidence of shared actors, causes or operation. The COVID parent now routes five distinct questions rather than presenting one mega-answer: the 2020 authority baseline, ODNI disclosure, HHS life-sciences policy, White House publication state and Senate reading-room corpus.

Hearings, interviews and presidential remarks are source-bearing records with different evidentiary tiers. Official transcripts or replay controls govern exact wording; speaker assertions remain claims until matched to law, filings, agency action, funding, implementation or observed effect. The broader hearing shelf and the Epstein statement-accountability reading therefore remain separate from the House Oversight source corpus.

## Sweeps are versioned evidence, not completeness claims

The whole-government collections move from broad source coverage to materiality-controlled adjudication. The August 22 full-freshness sweep retired its original 392-entity completeness claim and closed 55 deduplicated review units into atomic candidates and non-atomic dispositions. The August 29 control audit and September 3 corrective delta update the evidence window with explicit folds, repairs, rejects and watches.

Weekly deltas, hearings and source harvests therefore belong together as versioned evidence of what changed at each cutoff. They do not become a timeless government census, automatic vault ingestion, or proof that every agency endpoint or operating outcome was inspected.

Public Law 119-106 makes the award-record boundary itself inspectable. It adds other transaction agreements to FFATA and stages the agency list, data feed, public view and audit cycle on separate clocks. GAO's $77.5 billion finding concerned DOD OTAs visible in SAM.gov but absent from USAspending, so fragmented reporting is neither total secrecy nor proof of fraud.

## Accountability is tested through remedy and competing explanations

The economic-institution legitimacy work supplies a working thesis lens: control, responsibility, visible records and remedy can diverge as institutions adapt. Its strongest counter-thesis is institutional absorption—established institutions can adopt useful technology while retaining accountable records and public authority. The branch should keep both readings visible and use new legal, operating and correction receipts to test them.

Accountability is therefore not satisfied by publication, immutable storage, a dashboard or a shared signal. It requires an authoritative record, a named decision-maker, a correction path and evidence that the person, program or counterparty affected could obtain a remedy.

## How this fits together

Accountability requires source custody, versioned evidence, an authoritative decision record and a remedy path. Replace any current explanation that treats a release, sweep, transcript, dashboard or immutable record as proof of shared causation, complete coverage or achieved outcome.

Named sources range from 2026-07-21 through 2026-09-15. Sweep and disclosure claims retain their individual version and cutoff boundaries; no exhaustive government census is claimed. The September 15 Public Law 119-106 reading is a question-led implementation check, not a new whole-government sweep.
