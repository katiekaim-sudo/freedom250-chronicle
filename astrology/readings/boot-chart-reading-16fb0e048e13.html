Boot card: Reading or interpreting a chart

What you are doing (read this before the rules)

Mundane astrology reads the collective — nations, governments, money, war, law, the mood of a people. You are asking one question of a chart: what is the shape of this moment; what is being asked of the people living in it? It deals in governments and gold, not inner journeys. The math beneath must be perfect; the reading on top is bold — decisive, concrete, never timid.

What a chart IS (don't skip — everything below says "the chart" as if you know). A 360° circle: the sky over one place at one moment, flattened into a wheel. Not a picture of the heavens — a picture of what time it is, written in symbols. Every position is a degree on that circle. Its four hinges are the angles — AC (the face), DC (the other), MC (the government, straight up), IC (the land, straight down); a planet on a hinge shouts, which is why the casting place changes what the same sky says. Whole Sign houses: the sign on the ascendant IS the whole 1st house.

The circle is five layers on one axis (Huber's model — diagram in 03 - Astrology/The Chart — Five Layers.svg). Bottom = most superficial; top = what it's all for:

Layer Mundane meaning
Central core the SOVEREIGN WILL — the soul; a people's power to choose. ⚠️ NOT the root.
Aspects the plot — who fights, helps, fuses. This is where meaning lives.
Planets the actors — motivations, gods with wants
Signs the toolshed — how well equipped (dignity = tool quality, not importance)
Houses the stage — where in the nation's life it lands

⚠️ The core is not the root, and confusing them turns a chart into a cage. The root is structural — where the dispositor chain bottoms out; the deepest thing in the wheel. The core is not in the wheel at all: it is the soul, the one who is free to decide. The chart draws the field of forces; the core is what may answer them. Kepler (via Huber): aspects are not causal — they become effective only when someone is inwardly moved by them. The core is what does the moving. The chart is not fate; it is the question put to a free will. The sky sets the question — the people answer.

> "Sense can only be understood from the context, not by listing and describing the individual parts." — Huber. A list of placements is not a reading. Most readers stop at the bottom layer ("Mercury in the 10th"); the work is to climb.

The unit of time is a ladder, and you never skip a rung:

   ↓      Greer validity: Fixed rising = year; Cardinal = 6 months; Mutable = 3 months
LUNATION  the month&#x27;s WEATHER — what fires and when (New Moon plants · Full Moon reveals)
   ↓      Opening NM can sit *days* before the ingress, not always one day. Name eclipses as eclipses.

The master move: trace every planet up its dispositor chain until it bottoms out in a final dispositor or a loop. That terminus is the root — the one story every planet serves. A loop is a circle of service, not a vacancy; every member is the root (phases.py must receive them all). Name the root; read everything as service to it.

Three levels of speech (DR-013 — the rule that governs how bold to be)

  1. Symbolic reading — name the question the sky puts to history, boldly. No hedging, no scores.
  2. Mundane translation — concrete actors, institutions, stakes, and both poles. You don't know which one history picks.
  3. Registered call — a literal event in a literal window is a separate act: it goes in the Forecast Ledger, where it is scored and allowed to be wrong (DR-004).

> The chart speaks boldly about what history is being asked, without pretending to know which literal event will embody the answer.

State Transition Watch (DR-064)

For a prospective Freedom 250 reading, hear the complete chart first and then freeze a separate addendum around the question: who has the legitimate right to decide here, by what authority, and is that right actually changing state? The thesis is a lens, not a conclusion. Never make the chart say “revolution” on command.

The addendum names the current holder, claimant or constraint, candidate from → toward transition, mechanism, maturity, constructive and shadow poles, continuity case, synchronization across the seven arcs, and the evidence test. Keep the maturity ladder exact: proposed → authorized → effective → operating, with contested / reversed / failed / unchanged preserved as real states. Keep Attention / Official / Entity evidence separate. No score; a rhyme is not a cause.

Existing frozen baselines are never rewritten. The watch is timestamped, immutable, and bound to the baseline ID and hash. Use 99 - Templates/Chart reading authoring template.md for the full shape.
