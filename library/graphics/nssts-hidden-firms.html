Which lesser-known contractors, testers and insurers sit underneath the big national-security tech programs?

The science plan's structural map names 48 firm entries (45 different names; a few firms sit in more than one corridor), and 19 kinds of firm, beneath the headline programs in 8 corridors. In AI compute: WWT, SambaNova and the engineers Bridgers & Paxton, Burns & McDonnell and Sargent & Lundy. On the grid: study and test firms such as Qualus, PowerGEM, Doble and Shermco. In quantum and encryption: Bluefors, FormFactor, Entrust and SafeLogic. In minerals and nuclear: SRK, NAC International and the insurer-engineer FM Approvals. 46 of the 48 entries match a row in an NSSTS registry (by name, or by row id where no name matched). The map says these firms do not inherit final authority, and it cites no source per corridor.
