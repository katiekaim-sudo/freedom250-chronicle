What “complete” means here

Retrospective-complete means that the Full phase has occurred and the route has a bounded factual object or fixed-window denominator. Prepared means the factual reservoir exists but the Full-Moon chapter is still future. Neither state means:

  • that every seed produced a successful policy;
  • that every factual object survived;
  • that the astrology predicted the object;
  • that the date correspondence proves causation;
  • that several objects from one window are several votes; or
  • that a shared theme can replace a same-object state transition.

The reservoir contains reversals and non-maturations on purpose. Capricorn's DeFi rule dies. Leo's longer-hours permission has not yet become exchange operation at its Full phase. Virgo's public orders do not yield public completion receipts. Those are stronger controls than a catalog made only of apparent successes.
