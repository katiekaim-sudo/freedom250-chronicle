Scientific Trust Stack

Which authority-specific identity, disclosure, ownership, access, provenance, security, data and investment gates route federally supported science?

Research through 2026-08-17

Home room: Innovation
The short version (draft, not yet reviewed by Katie)

No single pass lets federally funded science through

Federally funded science is routed through many separate checks on identity, disclosure, ownership, access, security, data and investment, each run by its own authority. No single credential, signature, list or score gives blanket permission or proves the science is true.

What we found

  • Each gate answers a different question. A CFIUS clearance, DOE research-security review, DCSA facility clearance, BIS license, FASCSA order, CHIPS award condition, identity credential or valid signature is not a universal green light.
  • The study maps the chain: identified people, organizations and artifacts, then disclosures and certifications, then agency-specific eligibility decisions, then scoped access, then logged work and provenance, then independent scientific checks, then ongoing monitoring, correction and appeal.
  • Its main new finding is that security gates do not only keep people out. Known-investor profiles, routing through cleared-investment vendors and federal capital programs can also steer approved money toward trusted capabilities.
  • The study sets hard limits: proving identity is not permission, permission is not scientific validity, a record of origin is not truth, and monitoring without notice, correction and appeal is not a complete trust system.

Why it matters

Federal science is being re-plumbed through existing agency powers over investment, export, security clearance and grant conditions, not one new law. The result is a patchwork of authorities, not one central platform.

What to watch

  • pending · Any move to fold these separate gates into one shared credential, list or trust score, which the study says does not exist today.
  • pending · Signs that security screening is steering federal or private investment toward approved science projects.
