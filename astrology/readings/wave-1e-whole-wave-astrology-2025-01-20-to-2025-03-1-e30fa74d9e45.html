Wave 1E — The Control Plane Enters the Body

Research window: January 20 through March 19, 2025.
Factual question: What common institutional act joins the command-and-personnel, money-and-records, rules-and-procurement and pressured-entity packets?
Astrological question: What do the outgoing fixed-rising 2024 Aries governor, the nested 2024 Capricorn winter room and the New Moon chapters naturally endorse before the 2025 Aries handoff?
Whole-wave finding: The installation wave did not principally replace the legal persons of government. It built a horizontal control plane across them. The new center sought earlier visibility and approval over who could enter or leave the workforce, which rule could publish, which payment file could pass, who could hold a contracting warrant, which program could justify its continued existence and which statutory function had to survive. The old entities largely remained responsible for the legal act; the new regime moved upstream into the record, classification, review and permission layer.

The astrology does not prove that institutional change. It supplies a coherent archetypal hearing: a fixed governing year asks whether executive force can rework the state’s underlying architecture without dissolving the lawful relationships and public obligations that hold it together.

The institutional thesis at the ingress boundary

By March 19 the regime had changed who could see, stop, classify, review and demand explanation more decisively than it had changed who owned the charter.

That is not a minor prelude. Control over thresholds can transform an organism before the corporate diagram changes:

  • a hiring gate changes which future state can be staffed;
  • a data-access gate changes which transaction can be inspected;
  • a publication gate changes which rule can become public law;
  • a warrant gate changes who may bind the government;
  • a statutory-minimum report changes which function must defend its existence; and
  • a funding-request gate changes which entity can ask for future body.

But threshold control is not consummation. The same record preserves contrary clocks:

  • OMB’s categorical funding pause was rescinded after immediate legal challenge;
  • Treasury did not inherit the agencies’ statutory payment decisions;
  • OIRA review did not become rule authorship;
  • DOGE teams did not become departments;
  • statutory bodies did not disappear on an executive signature; and
  • FMCS, IMLS and other institutions later demonstrated the fourth act of contest, restoration or restructuring.

The precise thesis is therefore not “the old state was abolished.” It is:

> The old state was made to operate through a new observation-and-permission membrane before the new regime had proved that it could transfer the underlying legal organs.

The lunar sequence

Inherited Capricorn New Moon — hold and inventory the inherited structure

The December 30 Capricorn New Moon governs the inauguration and the first nine days of the new administration. It naturally endorses the initial act of taking possession of inherited machinery:

  • freeze hiring and rules;
  • name the command rooms;
  • inventory personnel and DEIA structures;
  • suspend selected funding and foreign-aid pathways for review; and
  • define which appointee must approve the next move.

It is Greer-quiet to both the strict governor and nested frame. The lack of a hard contact does not make the chapter empty; it reads as the structural backdrop rather than a timed trigger.

January 29 Aquarius New Moon — turn the bureaucracy into a network of control surfaces

The Aquarius chapter is where the installation becomes a system:

  • deferred resignation creates a scalable exit lane;
  • CIO, SES and performance records become policy-bearing data;
  • Schedule Policy/Career becomes a petition mechanism;
  • DOGE teams and OMB/OPM begin future-state planning;
  • Treasury access and payment files become litigated technical objects;
  • OMB installs a regulatory cost budget; and
  • independent agencies enter an explicit White House review network.

The theme is not “technology” in the abstract. It is coordination by shared visibility and interoperable gates. Constructively, the system can make a fragmented state legible. In shadow, the network lets a small center see and interrupt actions whose lawful responsibility remains elsewhere.

The New Moon is Greer-quiet to both frames. Its endorsement is thematic and cumulative, not a claim that one aspect caused every February move.

February 12 Leo Full Moon — reveal the command question

The Leo Full Moon makes the center visible. During its chapter:

  • the workforce reduction architecture is announced;
  • the analytical ruler for regulations is switched;
  • independent regulators receive OIRA, performance, budget and liaison leashes;
  • named entities are declared unnecessary; and
  • a card-audit pilot and rulebook review begin.

The polarity is vivid: accountable executive authorship versus personalized command; public leadership versus the absorption of institutions into one political image. It is quiet to the strict governor but lies within 0.17° of nested Capricorn Uranus, making the seasonal institutional interface unusually resonant without supplying proof of any event.

February 27 Pisces New Moon — dissolve the boundary into records, functions and permissions

The Pisces chapter carries the wave toward its limit:

  • ARRP plans convert doctrine into future-state agency maps;
  • payment explanations move in front of covered transactions;
  • contracting policies, personnel and warrants enter review;
  • the second roster of statutory bodies is ordered toward a minimum lawful presence; and
  • the public begins to see a bounded payment feed while the true legal decision remains distributed.

Pisces does not only mean confusion. It asks what must be surrendered so the organism can change form. The constructive expression is permeable coordination and the removal of obsolete compartments. The shadow is an intentionally blurred boundary in which reviewer, adviser, operator and legal decision-maker can no longer be cleanly distinguished.

This New Moon conjoins strict-governor Venus by 0.50° and squares nested Mercury by 1.10°. Value, contract, relationship and governing language become the live interfaces. That is an especially natural rhyme for a chapter carried by payment justifications, contract authority and statutory-function reports.

March 14 Virgo total lunar eclipse — audit what the new membrane has actually changed

The total lunar eclipse closes the wave with an institutional audit. Its civil date coincides with EO 14238, but the reading does not depend on day-level causality. Its longer value is that the wave’s apparently simple claims—efficiency, fraud reduction, legal minimum, accountability—must become testable in the details:

  • Was a person actually separated or only listed?
  • Was a payment stopped lawfully, returned for correction or merely displayed?
  • Was a rule repealed or placed on an agenda?
  • Was an entity dissolved, stripped, restored or restructured?
  • Which official still held the decision and the appeal?

The eclipse is quiet to the outgoing governing frames but bridges forward: its Sun conjoins and Moon opposes incoming 2025 Aries Saturn within 0.87°. The factual rhyme is not “the eclipse caused the March order.” It is that the next governing year inherits an unresolved Saturnian test: can the new architecture acquire durable, lawful form?

Both poles of the completed wave

Constructive pole — a legible state

  • fragmented responsibility is made visible;
  • obsolete nonstatutory carriers are separated from statutory duties;
  • payment, rule and contract actions acquire explicit explanations and reviewers;
  • political principals become answerable for the structure they direct; and
  • future operating designs can be compared against real charters and accounts.

Shadow pole — a permission state

  • visibility becomes surveillance of the administrative body;
  • review becomes an unreviewable veto;
  • the legal person survives while staff and operating capacity are emptied;
  • the same center defines efficiency, lawfulness and acceptable policy;
  • classification substitutes for adjudication; and
  • responsibility remains with the old officer after practical control has moved elsewhere.

The record supports the existence of the membrane. It does not let us declare one pole victorious across every lane.

The handoff into 2025 Aries

The 2025 Aries ingress does not begin from a blank state. It receives:

  1. a cross-agency command network;
  2. future-state personnel maps and active reduction machinery;
  3. a bounded but real payment-and-record control path;
  4. a regulatory and procurement review architecture;
  5. an unresolved statutory-minimum experiment;
  6. multiple court clocks; and
  7. an eclipse question about what can take durable form.

The first 2025 Aries research packet should therefore ask which of these design objects acquires an operating carrier. Education closure, GSA/common-goods consolidation, the FAR rewrite, payment justification, entity reductions and emerging strategic vehicles should be traced as separate maturity chains rather than treated as one continuous “dismantling” story.
