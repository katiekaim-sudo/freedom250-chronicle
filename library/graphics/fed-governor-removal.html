Freedom 250 · Banks and the dollar · Courts

Can the President fire a Fed governor?

Only for cause, and only after notice and a chance to respond. On June 29, 2026 the Supreme Court (5-4) kept Governor Lisa Cook in office, holding that courts can review the 'cause' and that she got no notice or hearing. On October 7 the President set up a committee to try again, with a hearing set for November 5, 2026.
