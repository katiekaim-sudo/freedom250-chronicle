Arizona Takes the Side Door

May 7, 2025 · Arizona

Reconstructed (not an official filing)

Four days after vetoing the real bitcoin-reserve bill as 'untested,' Hobbs signs HB 2749: a digital-asset reserve built from unclaimed property — abandoned crypto and its staking rewards, no taxpayer money. The second state reserve framework in the nation, built from lost coins. The bigger bill is back on her desk this summer, with reelection in November.

The Pioneer Sells Its Bitcoin

May 15, 2025 · Wisconsin

Reconstructed (not an official filing)

Wisconsin's investment board — the first state pension into spot bitcoin ETFs — discloses it dumped its entire stake, some $300 million of BlackRock's IBIT, in the first quarter. Days before the tariff shock, as it turned out. The first state in was the first state out, right as the reserve laws passed everywhere else.

Texas Buys the First State Bitcoin

November 20, 2025 · Texas

Reconstructed (not an official filing)

The Comptroller spends about $5 million of the $10 million SB 21 appropriation on BlackRock's IBIT fund, with bitcoin at $91,336 — the first state in the nation to actually fund a strategic bitcoin reserve. A placeholder, they say, until a custodian is hired. New Hampshire passed its law first and bought nothing; Texas passed second and bought first — through BlackRock.

New Hampshire Kills the Bitcoin Bond

July 8, 2026 · New Hampshire

Reconstructed (not an official filing)

The Executive Council votes down the $100 million Bitcoin-backed municipal bond. Over-collateralized 150-160% via BitGo cold storage, borrower CleanSpark, Moody's rating Ba2 (junk). Governor Ayotte supported it. Councilor Karen Liot Hill cast the decisive opposition: "We are being asked to lend legitimacy to a volatile asset class." The first state crypto-backed bond attempt is dead. New Hampshire — Live Free or Die

New Hampshire Weighs a Bitcoin Bond

July 8, 2026 · New Hampshire

Reconstructed (not an official filing)

The Governor and Executive Council hear the Business Finance Authority's plan: a $100 million municipal bond secured by $160 million in bitcoin, borrower CleanSpark the miner, BitGo holding the coins, Moody's rating it junk at Ba2. The first time a US state would tie public debt to a crypto reserve asset. The state that wouldn't buy bitcoin may lend its name against it instead.
