Is DTC's tokenization live, and on which blockchains?

Partly. DTC ran its first production trades with tokenized assets on July 15, 2026, in a limited form. As of October 19, 2026, DTCC still describes the full service as an October 2026 launch and has not announced it. Blockchains named: LFDT Besu (DTCC's private network) (live, limited, since 2026-07-15), Canton Network (public) (live, limited, since 2026-07-15), Stellar (public) (announced only, expected first half of 2027), Arc (Circle) (announced only, expected 2027).
