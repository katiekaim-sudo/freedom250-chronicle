2026-07-23 · Executive Office of the President · Presidential Document · Defense research & buying

EO 14415 — Securing America's Defense Supply Chains and Ensuring Domestic Acquisition of Critical Materials

Signed July 20, published July 23 (91 FR 46693), the first executive order in twenty-five days. From January 1, 2027 the Secretary of War stops issuing the routine 10 U.S.C. 4872 waivers that let contractors buy sensitive materials from adversaries — a waiver now needs a formal mitigation plan naming the source, proving exhaustive effort, and setting a hard timeline, and lying on one gets referred to the Attorney General. Every prime and subcontractor at any tier must hand over a complete indentured Bill of Materials tracing every part back to the raw ore, vet suppliers for financial distress, foreign ownership and single points of failure, and report significant risks within fifteen days. The Department of War will map the resulting national-security chokepoints 'using any tools and technologies to include artificial intelligence.' Project Vault — the Strategic Critical Minerals Reserve, with Ex-Im as lender — is carved out and protected.

The FAA proposed to implement Section 2209, creating a process for operators of critical fixed-site facilities to request unmanned-aircraft flight restrictions on safety or security grounds. It is the security-side complement to BVLOS expansion — the airspace-protection layer the broader drone-integration push depends on.

2026-01-21 · Bureau of Industry and Security · Rule · Drones, aviation & airspace

Streamlining Export Controls for Drone Exports

Acting under EO 14307, Commerce/BIS issued an interim final rule easing export controls on civil UAVs, allowing less-sensitive commercial drones to ship license-free to most Wassenaar partners and certain cargo and agricultural-spray drones to allies under License Exception STA. A concrete deregulatory follow-through on the drone-dominance order.

2025-06-11 · Executive Office of the President · Presidential Document · Drones, aviation & airspace

Executive Order 14307 — Unleashing American Drone Dominance

Signed June 6, 2025, EO 14307 directed the FAA to issue a proposed rule enabling routine beyond-visual-line-of-sight drone operations within 30 days and a final rule within 240 days, and to accelerate UAS and eVTOL integration into the National Airspace System. BVLOS normalization was the order's first priority, and it also launched an eVTOL Integration Pilot Program.

2025-04-18 · Executive Office of the President · Presidential Document · Defense research & buying

Executive Order 14275 — Restoring Common Sense to Federal Procurement

Directs a top-to-bottom rewrite of the 40-year-old Federal Acquisition Regulation to delete provisions not required by statute and impose a ten-for-one deregulatory rule on new supplemental rules. Because the FAR governs all federal buying including major weapons systems, this is the broadest procurement-barrier teardown of the term.

2025-02-03 · Executive Office of the President · Presidential Document · Defense research & buying

Executive Order 14186 — The Iron Dome for America

Signed Jan 27, 2025, this order commands a next-generation layered homeland missile shield against ballistic, hypersonic, and cruise threats — later branded 'Golden Dome' — calling for space-based sensors and interceptors plus directed-energy tools. It is the founding charter of the largest U.S. missile-defense build in a generation and the single biggest defense-R&D demand signal of the term.

Integration of Powered-Lift: Pilot Certification and Operations (SFAR)

The FAA's final powered-lift rule established pilot-certification and operating rules for eVTOL and air-taxi aircraft, including a ten-year Special Federal Aviation Regulation tailored to their varied designs. It is the foundational framework enabling powered-lift integration into the National Airspace System.
