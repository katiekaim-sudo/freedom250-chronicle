## What the charts add

In 2025, Mercury begins debilitated with Saturn in Pisces/1st. The candidate moves Mercury into Aries/12th while Saturn remains Pisces but moves to the 11th. Information becomes hidden or preparatory while institutional structure sits in the collective field.

Neither family produces a spectacular focal angle. Their value is the consistent separation of the record from the institution carrying or limiting it.
