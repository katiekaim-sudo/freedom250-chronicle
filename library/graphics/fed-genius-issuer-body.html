Which legal roles would surround a Board-supervised stablecoin issuer?

The proposal map places one legal issuer among supervisors, its parent, reserve claims, custodians, service providers, holders and payout banks. These are different legal relationships, not a chart of an approved company or a transfer of the issuer’s duties.
