Research summary

After the September 22 baseline, the strongest new federal movement was procedural rather than a single government-wide program: DOT finalized individualized DBE/ACDBE eligibility, OFAC consolidated sanctions-penalty procedure, the FTC changed practice and ethics-clearance rules, USPS updated NEPA procedure and the Supreme Court kept Missouri's 2022 congressional map controlling for the underway election. EXIM's up-to-$7-billion Argentina framework, CMS's 37-state voluntary quality pledge, VA's planned partial Compact-health implementation and ATF's future-effective explosives rule are real but do not yet prove commitments, payments, operation or outcomes. Current Fed, SEC, Treasury, UNGA, defense and country work remains with existing owners.
