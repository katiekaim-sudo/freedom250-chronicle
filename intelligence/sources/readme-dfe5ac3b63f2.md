## Evidence boundary

- Prepared testimony controls exact witness wording where filed.
- Proceeding transcripts include questions and answers; written statements do not.
- House transcript text is normalized automatic captioning attached to official
  committee video.
- Senate transcript text is normalized from the official Senate caption feed.
  The Senate does not disclose whether the feed was human or automatic.
- Caption text is a discovery and analysis layer. Verify exact quotations against
  the official video or a later formal transcript.
- A hearing, testimony, discussion draft or committee memo does not itself change
  a statute, rule, effective date or operating system.
