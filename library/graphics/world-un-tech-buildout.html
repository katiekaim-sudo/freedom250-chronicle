Where does the UN's AI and digital-technology build-out stand?

Of the 12 layers the UNGA81 package tracks (as of September 30, 2026), 3 are operating (Mandate and focal point; Evidence, dialogue and implementation lab; Capacity centres and diffusion network), 6 are under way, 2 are proposed only and 1 is only a panel that was held. The money is not there yet: the Secretary-General proposes a $3 billion starting fund and the September 21 closed design session was held; no governing or capitalization instrument was located at cutoff. Cross-border AI sandboxes: the Valencia Lab operates and the September 21 co-design session was held, but no public charter, standards work item, shared-protocol network or pilot was located. Tokenization as public infrastructure: the September 21 panel was held; Pakistan publicly framed tokenization around remittances, SME finance, sovereign debt, access, safeguards and standards participation, while no UN standard, legal model or pilot was located.

What's still uncertain

  • The $3 billion figure and financing options are proposals, not appropriations, commitments or an adopted levy.
  • A UN-convened panel does not endorse a company, token, architecture or legal classification.
  • States are the package's own (operating, under way, proposed, held); a launch, a meeting or a draft is not a funded, adopted or operating system.
  • Some sources are not UN or government sites (a video recording, initiative websites); each is labelled by kind on its card.
