Sabian images in the sky story

Yes: some images add a useful last sentence to a chart. They are most helpful when they expose a tension already earned by the whole wheel. They cannot choose a political outcome, repair weak geometry or make an older Moon-family return a second vote. The chart's root, actors, houses, aspects and motion still come first; the Sabian image is a caption after that hearing.
