Updated execution posture
Automatic diary progression is paused after the September 2020 audit. Remaining
diary, Slack, prequel, intelligence and Volume 1 pages are reopened only when
they can close a proof-chain link, test a private/public contradiction,
authenticate a native object, resolve a disposition or correct a material
official claim.
The active priority order is:

The completed Priority 1 test found a genuine late change in Proximal Origin

Evidence firewalls
Reading Room card copy is committee-authored framing.
“Released by Chairman Rand Paul” establishes the release custodian, not the
original author, completeness or truth of every enclosed record.
A diary entry establishes what its author recorded, not independent truth of
every event or scientific claim described.
Testimony establishes what a witness said under the stated conditions. It is
not a laboratory notebook, funding record, finished intelligence judgment or
adjudication.
Internal scientific uncertainty is evidence about deliberation. It is not by
itself proof of engineering, release or pandemic causation.
A targeting or investigative record is not a charge, conviction, informant
designation or obstruction finding.
A historical institutional relationship is not proof of a later covert
agreement.
Publication date, underlying record date, hearing/interview date and legal
disposition date remain separate clocks.
