## Evidence ladder

```text
individual or invited-speaker statement
  -> meeting discussion
  -> draft or working-group product
  -> adopted body recommendation
  -> agency response
  -> legal or allocation instrument
  -> funded delivery or deployment
  -> recurring operation
  -> measured effect, failure or correction
```

No rung inherits the meaning of a later rung. Similar language across bodies is
not coordination unless an official actor, citation, instrument, standard,
contract, system or decision right supplies the join.
