# 2025 Cancer lunar chapter — protection becomes an account that must be seen

one in Washington can literally see.** The annual Aries governor remains in

That is the period's larger lunar plot: old obligations become visible as new
protection claims are made.

The Moon is domiciled, cazimi, out of bounds and one of two final dispositors;

tools and independent seats. That is not proof that the country is nourished or
that its institutions agree on what protection means.

The lights and Jupiter square the near-exact Saturn–Neptune conjunction in the
tenth. Mars in Virgo sextiles the lights within 0.440°. The seed has an
operating outlet and a government/ideal problem. Constructively, a strong need
can be turned into practical work and force a public rule to become definite.
In shadow, the felt completeness of the need becomes a reason to skip the
account or to treat the government's restructuring as self-justifying.

The New Moon strikes annual Venus within 0.195° and sits inside the nested
Cancer field. Sun and Jupiter parallel while both contra-parallel Pluto. The
protective promise and concentrated power face one another in declination even
though the public prose may sound benefic. The root of value remains silent in
longitude; the power question is not.

This Threshold-band seed begins a Pessin line whose Full phase is the December
24, 2026 Cancer Full Moon. The later hidden-care and operating-burden hearing is
