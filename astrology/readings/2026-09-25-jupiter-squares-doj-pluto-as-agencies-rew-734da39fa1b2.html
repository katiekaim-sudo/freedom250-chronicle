Jupiter squares DOJ Pluto as agencies rewrite their procedures

The story (research side — facts only)

The September 21–25 federal weekly delta does not show one government-wide program. Its strongest completed movement is a cluster of procedural control changes: who qualifies, who reviews, which record governs, how a penalty or appeal is routed. (~/Documents/Freedom 250 Observatory/Research Packages/Federal Government Weekly Delta 2026-09-25/WAVE_10_MERGE.md)

  • September 24 — FTC. Two final rules took effect the same day. One revises Second Request modification, ALJ assignment, brief limits, public-record listings, FOIA methods, quorum calculation and commissioner-disqualification motions. The other eliminates the FTC-specific post-employment-clearance rule while leaving government-wide ethics law in place. These are internal process changes, not case outcomes. (…/waves/WAVE_7_COMMERCE_SCIENCE_COMMUNICATIONS.md)
  • September 25 — DOT. The DBE/ACDBE final rule made the October 2025 interim rule final: race- and sex-based presumptions are out; individualized disadvantage determinations are in. Effective that day. The rule changes eligibility procedure. It does not prove a firm's certification, a contract award, or a payment. (…/waves/WAVE_4_INDUSTRIAL_ENERGY_LOGISTICS.md)
  • September 25 — OFAC. New 31 CFR part 505 consolidates sanctions penalty procedures that had been repeated across programs. OFAC states the rule makes no substantive change to the penalty provisions. A separate rule updates Terrorism List Government regulations after Syria's removal from the State Sponsors of Terrorism list. Procedure and source-custody, not a blocked transaction or a collected penalty. (…/waves/WAVE_1_FINANCIAL_CONTROL_STACK.md)
  • September 25 — USPS. An interim final rule updates NEPA procedures after CEQ's government-wide regulations were rescinded; comments remain due October 26. It does not decide any one project's environmental review.

Implementation-planned objects in the same package (CMS/37-state Medicaid quality pledge; VA pursuing partial Compact health authorities) are plans, not operating programs.
