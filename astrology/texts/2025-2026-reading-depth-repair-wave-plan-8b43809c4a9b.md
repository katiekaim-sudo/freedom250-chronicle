  a registry source until Katie dispositions it.
- No synthesis rebuild, upload, activation or release is implied by this work.

## Audit vocabulary

Each mandatory method family receives one of four states:

- `changes_hearing` — the layer changes the whole, its proportions or the actor
  carrying the pressure and must enter the revised reading;
- `supports_hearing` — the layer materially supports the whole but adds no
  independent vote;
- `heard_backstage` — it was inspected and does not alter the whole, with the
  reason recorded;
- `unresolved` — facts, owner authority or interpretation are not ready.

`Calculated`, `available` and `screened` are not audit dispositions.
