Great-Power Realignment

The claim. A mid-sized arc now (620 notes, sixth of seven after a deep Europe backfill on 2026-06-05) and the one with the widest geographic reach: the international board is being rearranged. The throughline is a shift away from a US-led liberal order toward direct great-power dealing — Trump negotiating personally with Xi, allies (especially Canada) in visible crisis, and the post-WWII institutions (NATO, the EU, the UN) treated as spent. The arc now runs in two waves: a winter surge (Oct 2025–Jan 2026) carried by Europe's unraveling — January 2026 alone holds 107 notes — and a second peak in May 2026 (116 notes) just as the Iran war winds down, as if attention rotated from the Mideast to the wider chessboard. Europe (269 notes) is now by far the arc's largest plotline, ahead of Other International (82), China (73), Canada (64), India (56), Russia/Ukraine (46), Mexico (19) and Cuba (11) — which together make up the arc's 620.

The sub-threads

The dominant beat is now the unraveling of Europe (269 notes), a story the chronicle tracks through seven sub-threads of its own (see the Europe — Subplots index): mass migration & Islamization (52) — boats, asylum, no-go zones, the "great replacement" framing; Starmer's Britain (33) — a Labour government in permanent crisis; Brussels, the ECHR & sovereignty (33) — EU overreach, the cash-payment cap, a von der Leyen no-confidence push; the nationalist resurgence (24) — AfD, Le Pen's RN, Orbán, Reform UK, Meloni, Wilders; Germany's crisis (19) — economy, energy, security; two-tier Britain & speech arrests (8) — grooming-gang fallout and jail for social-media posts; and Europe's war footing (3) — NATO rearmament. The reading the saves advance: the post-war European order is failing from within, and a national-populist right is rising to replace it. The second beat is US–China détente and Trump's Beijing visit — Trump arriving in Beijing with Musk and Huang in tow, a 200-plane Boeing order, direct Xi diplomacy that reframes China from adversary to counterpart. Then Canada's crisis: an Alberta separatism referendum, Carney accused of "overplaying his hand," and lurid "UK selling Canada to China" claims — Canada is mentioned far more than its size would predict (64 notes). An India strand — offshored credit data, the H-1B overlap with the Homeland arc. Ukraine corruption and Yermak — Yermak's arrest, alleged Soros connections, Gabbard's biolabs probe, a pivot from "the war" to "the graft." And Venezuela, Maduro and the cartels — Maduro's fall, Venezuelans celebrating, Mexico pressed to pay a "blood price" against the cartels (this strand touches both the war and homeland arcs).

The timeline

A long European winter wave underlies the arc from October 2025 through January 2026 (the Europe backfill ingested 2026-06-05 — grooming-gang arrests, AfD/RN/Reform gains, Starmer's troubles, EU cash limits — peaking with January's 107-note month). Over the top of it, two great-power clusters bracket the arc. The opening beat is January 3, 2026 — Maduro's fall and Venezuelans in the streets, plus Alberta separation talk — and January 16, with Carney's "new world order" framing, the EU no-confidence move, and the "UK selling Canada to China" claim. Then a long quiet stretch during the Iran war, and a sharp revival in mid-May: May 11, Merz calling out the EU, an Arcadia mayor charged as a PRC foreign agent, Gabbard's Ukraine biolabs probe; May 13, Trump landing in Beijing with Musk and Huang while an Alberta separatist vote is blocked by a court; and May 14, the peak day (13 notes) — Trump and Xi trading "declining nation" lines, the Boeing deal, and Yermak's corruption arrest.

What I'm watching

Whether Europe's decline narrative — now the arc's center of gravity — actually converts into the national-populist takeover the saves anticipate (AfD, RN, Reform UK) or stalls against incumbent EU institutions; the seam to watch is Europe × Border & Homeland, since the migration story is the same story told on two continents. Also whether the Beijing visit marks a genuine US–China realignment or a one-off photo-op, and whether the Canada crisis (Alberta, Carney) is a real fracture or noise amplified by a few accounts. The seam to watch is China × Economy — the Boeing deal and trade terms are where this arc and the Monetary Reset collide.
