BUIDL subscription/access gate

Admit customers and participants

Legal basis: The March 14, 2024 filed BUIDL agreement allocates subscription, issuer and registration functions; the current offering and subscription documents remain controlling.

When it applies: A prospective investor seeks to acquire an interest in the named fund product.

Named firms: BlackRock USD Institutional Digital Liquidity Fund Ltd. (role documented)

Who can use it: The issuer or authorized appointee empowered to accept subscriptions under the fund documents

ConditionsApply the operative offering, subscription, eligibility, sanctions/AML, and transfer documents for the relevant share class and jurisdiction. Identify whether the result is direct registration, intermediary-held beneficial interest, or another contractual record arrangement.

The limitThe named fund is the issuer controlling the subscription arrangement, not the investor. Current eligibility terms and acceptance authority still require the operative offering documents.

DFS-supervised stablecoin holder redemption gate

Admit customers and participants · Issue or redeem a claim

Legal basis: NYDFS requires a covered issuer policy to confer a timely par-redemption right on a lawful holder, subject to reasonable conditions including successful onboarding.

When it applies: A lawful holder requests direct redemption from a DFS-supervised issuer.

Named firms: Circle Internet Financial, LLC (registered or chartered)

Who can use it: DFS-supervised issuers and authorized onboarding agents applying the covered product redemption policy

ConditionsThe particular coin is issued under DFS supervision and applicable issuer policy applies. Holder/designee successfully onboards and meets compliant-order conditions. Issuer has required DFS approval and current product terms.

The limitCircle is issuer-context only, never the holder. Guidance does not prove that a particular Circle holder has direct redemption rights under current terms.

FICC/GSD sponsored participation

Participate through an account or member

Legal basis: Rule 3A permits a relationship between approved Sponsoring Member and person accepted by FICC as its Sponsored Member. DTCC states the entry conditions as approved-jurisdiction legal entity plus an available sponsor.

When it applies: A qualified legal entity seeks GSD access through a Sponsoring Member rather than direct netting membership.

Named firms: None named yet

Who can use it: legal entity in FICC-approved jurisdiction; eligible GSD Netting Member applying as Sponsoring Member

ConditionsSponsoring applicant meets Rule 3A/current standards and is approved. FICC accepts entity as that sponsor's Sponsored Member. Parties comply with current rules, guaranty, and service documents.

The limitSponsored access is separate from direct Netting Membership; a service page does not replace GSD Rules.
