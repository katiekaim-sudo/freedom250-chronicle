The question

Which reserve, settlement, invoicing, FX and stablecoin changes constitute diversification, pricing or actual monetary substitution?

What the research currently says

Reserve diversification, regional settlement and trade invoicing remain separate from actual monetary substitution. Bessent's April 2026 IIF scorecard adds the incumbent strategy and a three-way legitimacy contest. The IMF symmetry audit finds candid surveillance of systemic members but operationalized, finance-linked adjustment for borrowers. The July 31 Japan–Korea cases confirm two pricing perimeters: national authorities and dealers make the wholesale FX event, while a stablecoin exchange supplies a separate dollar-linked quote. Korea's observed token price transmission is not net capital flow; Japan's limited production rails are not shown to have supported intervention.
