Third-party custodial tokenized-security entitlement

Role in the SEC order: supporting layer

Oasis Pro Markets joined DTCC Fund/SERV on September 16, and Ondo Stocks added live case-by-case institutional in-kind conversion through Alpaca on Ethereum and BNB Chain on September 21.

live named use · 2026-09-21

Where the proof stops: Fund/SERV membership and an approved-institution conversion route do not prove general retail access, recurring volume, shareholder title onchain or Ondo Network production.

Venue + operator (capability); Access + identity (capability); Stock + rights record (capability); Post-trade completion (support)

  • Venue + operator · Operator identity, Venue authority, Participant rules: Oasis Pro Markets is a named broker-dealer/ATS, a different legal route from TSV exemption reliance.
  • Access + identity · Admission / KYC, Transfer restrictions: Broker-dealer, transfer-agent and custodian controls enforce participant and transfer restrictions.
  • Stock + rights record · Product issuer, Mint / burn authority, Title / entitlement record, Corporate actions + proxy: Oasis Pro TA issues tokenized entitlements while underlying shares stay in the incumbent custody chain and Broadridge supports holder communications and proxy.
  • Post-trade completion · Custody, Securities settlement, Reconciliation + remedy: The model depends on regulated custody and traditional securities records; the complete TSV settlement chain is not established.

The ATS and transfer-agent registrations do not make Ondo or an affiliate a TSV, and the U.S. custodial entitlement model is not the same product as Ondo's global synthetic/custodial offerings or the proposed Ondo Network.

Watch: Exact operator, stock pool, Notice, chain contracts, custodian, liquidity provider and two-leg completion records if the stack is used under the order.
