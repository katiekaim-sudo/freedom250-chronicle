OPM Structural Change — Coverage and Gap Audit

Bottom line

Katie's hunch was right, with one important qualification: we did not lack the OPM story entirely. We had many of its legal and executive-control pieces, but they were distributed across unrelated packages and were not discoverable as one OPM process map. The largest substantive omissions were the operating systems and implementation chains beneath those legal changes.

The change is bigger than workforce reduction. Across the audit window OPM was being repositioned as:

  1. a central hiring, ranking, appraisal, suitability and appeals governor;
  2. a governmentwide HR platform and shared-service operator;
  3. a digital record, retirement and workforce-data utility; and
  4. an eligibility and improper-payment control node joined to Treasury, SSA, CMS, carriers and the Postal Service.

That architecture is material to both Politics / administrative-state control and Government Fraud / how the government is cut, measured and watched. It does not prove that every ordered migration, staffing plan, appeal, match, firing, savings claim or performance distribution occurred.

Highest-priority missing or underdeveloped structures

1. Retirement changed from paper routing to a shared digital operating record

OPM's May 7, 2025 directive moved new retirement intake toward the Online Retirement Application. NFC- and IBC-serviced agencies became mandatory June 2, 2025, and OPM intended to stop accepting paper in July. The stronger operating clock is June 30, 2026: OPM reported that more than 95 percent of applications were electronic and that the system had processed more than 155,000 applications over the prior year.

This is a material process change, not merely a website launch: agency, payroll-provider and OPM case records now share an electronic path. The issuer's speed and quality claims still require separate evidence.

  • Primary: May 7, 2025 OPM directive
  • Operating state: July 1, 2026 Last Day of Paper release
  • Disposition: promotion_review; upgrade the existing thin July 1 event rather than create a competing event.

2. Federal HR 2.0 is a central system-of-record program, not only an Oracle award

The December 10, 2025 OPM–OMB directive told agencies to stop duplicative Core HCM projects unless OPM/OMB granted an exception and set a phased path toward a single commercial governmentwide system of record. The June 10, 2026 Oracle award was already captured. The missing middle is OPM's March 17 HR Shared Service Center: a voluntary fee-for-service operation offering benefits, classification, compensation, labor relations, payroll, vetting, performance, recruitment, retirement and workforce-reshaping services, with a named cohort expected to begin transitions in FY2027.

  • Primary: Federal HR 2.0 directive
  • Primary: OPM HR Shared Service Center
  • Existing award: Oracle Core HCM announcement
  • State limit: policy, procurement and service-center creation are proved; production migration, signed IAAs, decommissioning, cost savings and service outcomes are not.
  • Disposition: promotion_review as one joined modernization/control object.

3. OPM became a larger digital personnel-record and data utility

OPM reported that a cloud eOPF platform replaced its legacy service in May–June 2025 after migration of more than 400 million files and five million accounts. On January 8, 2026, Federal Workforce Data replaced FedScope with monthly public data, downloads and new categories. A direct Login.gov document-request route for separated employees followed in August 2026; that last page had already been screened as a current-state object.

  • Primary: eOPF launch
  • Primary: Federal Workforce Data launch
  • Operational surface: Federal Workforce Data
  • Disposition: promotion_review for the joined infrastructure spine. Preserve the issuer-report boundary on migration completeness, data quality and use.

4. AI is now explicitly encouraged across the hiring lifecycle

The August 27, 2026 memorandum advises agencies to use AI in hiring while applying OMB M-25-21's high-impact analysis. It covers job-document drafting, résumé screening and summarization, eligibility, assessment, ranking and selection use cases. It also preserves validation, veterans' preference, privacy, accessibility, auditability, reconsideration and human-review duties.

OPM says AI features will be added across USA Staffing, USAJOBS, USA Hire, USA Class and USA Performance. That is a forward product direction, not proof that any feature was deployed or valid in a specific selection.

  • Primary: Use of Artificial Intelligence in the Federal Hiring Process
  • Disposition: high-priority promotion_review.

5. Hiring discretion and staffing control were rebuilt in several layers

The May 2025 Merit Hiring Plan supplied skills-based assessments, shared certificates, leadership involvement, a two-page résumé gate and reporting. The Rule of Many final rule then made numerical ranking available: agencies may predefine a sufficient group and selecting officials may choose anyone on the resulting certificate, while category rating remains available and veterans' preference remains applicable. The rule became effective November 7, 2025, with full compliance due March 9, 2026.

EO 14356 and its existing Workbench owner added Strategic Hiring Committees and Annual Staffing Plans. OPM's July 30, 2026 implementation memo makes September 30 the FY2027 plan deadline, but the related 5 CFR 250 proposal was not final at cutoff. Separately, probation changed toward affirmative public-interest certification instead of passive completion; no individual separation follows from the rule alone.

  • Primary: Rule of Many final rule
  • Primary: Annual Staffing Plans guidance
  • Primary: Probationary-period final rule
  • Disposition: Rule of Many promotion_review; probation and Annual Staffing Plans historical_repair into their existing executive-order chains.

6. Performance management acquired new distribution, review and certification rules

The September 2025 SES rule removed the regulatory prohibition on prescribed rating distributions and authorized OPM to impose limits. The July 7, 2026 general-workforce rule removed Level 2, authorized standardized distributions, ended universal mandatory higher-level review of Level 1, changed the grievance boundary, required a supervisory critical element and added biennial OPM system certification. It became effective August 6; the standardized fiscal-year cycle starts October 1, 2026, and specified compliance is due January 1, 2027.

These rules establish authority and minimum architecture. They do not establish an actual rating quota, distribution, demotion, removal or agency certification.

  • Primary: SES final rule
  • Primary: General-workforce final rule
  • Disposition: promotion_review as a joined multi-clock performance object; fold the July-sweep mention rather than duplicate it.

7. Retirement payment integrity now has an explicit weakness-to-match chain

OPM OIG's June 23, 2026 evaluation found largely manual projects and unresolved centenarian cases, and left 12 recommendations open at issuance. Its August 5 PIIA audit found OPM met statutory reporting criteria but could not validate parts of the retirement-control data and identified Do Not Pay scorecard errors.

On August 26 OPM published a new Retirement Services–Treasury Do Not Pay matching program. The legal start is September 25, 2026, not the notice date; the agreement is scheduled through September 10, 2029 unless ended or superseded. Results may not be the sole basis for an adverse action without independent verification and applicable process.

  • Primary: OPM OIG retirement improper-payment evaluation
  • Primary: OPM OIG FY2025 PIIA audit
  • Primary: OPM–Treasury Do Not Pay matching notice
  • Disposition: OIG/control chain promotion_review; September 25 first-operation state remains watch_only. No match, adverse decision or recovery existed by the audit cutoff.

8. PSHB is a real new operating benefits system with identified control risks

The Postal Service Health Benefits Program began operation January 1, 2025, moving postal employees and annuitants from the ordinary FEHB structure into a separate OPM-administered program. OPM OIG's July and November 2025 reports identified continuity/funding risk around the Health Benefits Data Platform and an open eligibility-documentation issue while also recording corrected controls.

  • Operating surface: OPM PSHB
  • Primary oversight: Critical PSHB Resource Issues
  • Primary oversight: PSHB Capstone Report
  • Disposition: promotion_review as one operating system plus OIG descendants. The reports do not prove a system failure or universal enrollment error.

9. A court imposed a material boundary on OPM-directed probationary separations

In AFGE v. OPM, the Northern District of California held on September 12, 2025 that OPM lacked authority to order other agencies' probationary separations through the challenged January/February directives and entered a permanent injunction plus record-correction requirements. The order did not bar agencies from taking their own lawful personnel actions and did not order final reinstatement.

The Ninth Circuit appeal, No. 25-5875, was argued August 28, 2026. No appellate decision was located by cutoff. The April 2025 Supreme Court emergency stay had addressed an earlier preliminary injunction and standing posture, not the later merits order.

  • Primary: authenticated district-court order
  • Appeal clock: Ninth Circuit oral-argument audio
  • Disposition: promotion_review with an active appellate watch; court objects, not OPM releases, control this boundary.

Source and completion receipt

The official-site lane reached the full 2025 and 2026 OPM CHCO memo index, the news archive through August 28, hiring, performance, suitability, retirement, benefits, eOPF/shared-system and workforce-data surfaces. The independent legal lane screened all 154 OPM Federal Register documents in the fixed window and cross-checked White House/OMB, GAO, OPM OIG and relevant federal-court objects.

This is not a claim that every unlinked attachment, transient page, internal agency plan, individual appeal or personnel action was captured. It is a materiality-filtered structural audit with exact known access limits.
