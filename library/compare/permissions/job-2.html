Direct registered holder of a security

Hold the claim or registered title

Legal basis: SEC describes direct registration as the investor's name appearing on the issuer's books, maintained by the issuer or its transfer agent.

When it applies: An investor elects direct registration for a security whose issuer offers it.

Named firms: None named yet

Who can use it: natural person; legal entity

ConditionsIssuer offers direct registration. Investor meets issuer/transfer-agent onboarding and account requirements. For a DRS transfer, the security and transfer agent are DTC DRS eligible.

The limitThis is a direct-registration route; it does not establish that every issuer offers it, that a token ledger is the issuer record, or that the issuer is itself the holder.

Sources (1)
  • SEC Investor Bulletin: Holding Your Securities · 2023-07-12

Beneficial holder through a street-name intermediary

Hold the claim or registered title

Legal basis: SEC describes the intermediary as recorded on issuer books and the intermediary as maintaining the record identifying the customer as beneficial owner.

When it applies: A customer holds securities through a broker-dealer or bank in street name.

Named firms: None named yet

Who can use it: natural person; legal entity; account customer of an intermediary

ConditionsIntermediary accepts the customer and account under its applicable program terms and legal requirements. Security is available through that intermediary's custody/nominee arrangement.

The limitBeneficial interest is distinct from appearing as registered owner on issuer books. This does not make an issuer, token administrator, or transfer agent the beneficial holder.

Manage reserve assets

Legal basis: NYDFS stablecoin guidance sets backing, custody, segregation and liquidity conditions for covered issuers.

When it applies: A DFS-supervised issuer maintains backing assets; where Circle Reserve Fund is used, its disclosed manager manages fund investments and operations.

Named firms: None named yet

Who can use it: DFS-supervised stablecoin issuers subject to the guidance, acting through properly appointed reserve providers

ConditionsIdentify the covered issuer and approved product. Establish the reserve policy, permitted assets, segregation, custody and reporting arrangements. Separate issuer policy responsibility from the fund manager and custodian appointments.

National-bank fiduciary/trustee route

Administer a trust

Legal basis: 12 USC 92a and implementing 12 CFR Part 9 distinguish fiduciary powers from the governing instrument and account duties.

When it applies: A national bank seeks to act as trustee or other fiduciary for a particular instrument or estate.

Named firms: None named yet

Who can use it: National banks with applicable OCC fiduciary authority, validly appointed to the particular fiduciary account

ConditionsEstablish applicable fiduciary permission and any exceptions under Part 9. Recover the instrument or court order appointing the bank and defining duties. Apply fiduciary asset, recordkeeping and administration controls.

The limitCharter, custody service, or listing does not establish trustee appointment. This does not determine state-chartered trust company or Delaware-law mechanics.

DTC nominee registered-holder route

Hold the claim or registered title

Legal basis: SEC staff guidance states that Cede & Co. appears as sole registered owner on the shareholder list for securities deposited with DTC by DTC participants.

When it applies: A security is deposited with DTC and the issuer or its transfer agent maintains the shareholder list showing DTC's nominee.

Named firms: Cede & Co. (DTC nominee) (role documented)

Who can use it: DTC nominee for securities deposited with DTC

ConditionsIdentify the particular security and confirm that it is deposited with DTC. Recover the applicable issuer or transfer-agent shareholder list or equivalent record. Keep the DTC nominee position separate from DTC participants and their customers' beneficial interests.

The limitThis route establishes a registered nominee category only. It does not establish ownership by a participant or customer, securities entitlement, voting authority, or a particular token ledger's legal effect.
