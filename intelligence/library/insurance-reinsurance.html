Research summary

Insurance links contractual access to risk transfer and assets supporting long-dated promises. Named policies, treaties, collateral, public programs and claim records determine who owes what; government loss sharing is case-specific rather than inevitable. The September Japan comparison finds real AIR convergence with the U.S./Bermuda machine, but not identity: Japan retains a national supervisor, newly live ESR regime, JGB-heavy life balance sheets, major mutuals and an explicit government earthquake-reinsurance tail, while current AIR stock is concentrated in foreign-owned and listed carriers.
