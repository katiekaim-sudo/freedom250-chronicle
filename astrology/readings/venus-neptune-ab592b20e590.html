Venus–Neptune

The planets

Venus — Magnetize, value, harmony, beauty of form. MUNDANE: the women of the nation; women's vote and issues; the arts, culture, tourism, entertainment; diplomacy and social harmony. Venus shows where the nation seeks peace, pleasure, and economic ease.

Neptune — Dissolution, the ideal, oceanic depths. MUNDANE: collective movements, mass sentiment, propaganda; popular causes; unity (divine to mob). Almost always malefic unless completely unafflicted. Neptune dissolves whatever it touches — sometimes into transcendence, more often into confusion.

The combination — mundane

Under helpful aspects (conjunction when well-placed, sextile, trine), Venus-Neptune "promotes peace, contentment, general goodwill; lessens class conflict; benefits laboring classes; charitable projects flourish" (Greer). This is one of the few conditions where Neptune's dissolving power works constructively — the walls between social classes soften, charitable impulses translate into real action, and the nation experiences a genuine moment of collective goodwill. The arts reach their highest expression. Diplomatic idealism produces actual agreements. The laboring classes benefit because the collective mood favors generosity over competition.

Under hostile aspects (square, opposition), the combination becomes what Greer calls "unfortunate for every aspect of social life; encouraging class conflict and hatred; warns of scandals, fraud, embezzlement." Value becomes illusion — financial bubbles inflate, currencies lose coherent meaning, Ponzi schemes proliferate. Class conflict intensifies as Neptune's mob energy targets Venus's domains of wealth and beauty. Scandals involving fraud and embezzlement dominate the news, especially in the arts, entertainment, or charitable sectors. Diplomatic agreements turn out to be illusory. The laboring classes are deceived by false promises.

As trigger: Venus-Neptune aspects are fast-moving (days to a couple of weeks) and mark the specific moments when financial bubbles, charitable impulses, or their dark twins — fraud, scandal, and class resentment — activate the broader outer-planet storylines.
