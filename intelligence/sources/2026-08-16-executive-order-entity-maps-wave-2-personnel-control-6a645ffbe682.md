## Hard boundaries carried

- A directed report or plan is not a completed action.
- Reduction to statutory minimum is Clock 2/3 intent, not Clock 1 repeal.
- DOGE is the USDS re-flag, not a new department.
- 14171 does not place the 14410 Appendix.
- GSA consolidation is not an organic-act transfer.
- Chapter-71 exclusion is not a firing.
