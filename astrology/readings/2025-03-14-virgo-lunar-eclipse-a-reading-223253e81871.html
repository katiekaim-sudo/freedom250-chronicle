2025-03-14 Virgo Lunar Eclipse — A Reading

> Retrospective calibration. Written 2026-09-25 from the computed chart alone, after the window closed. It carries no forecast credit; the record and the comparison live in separate files (DR-063).

The Moon of the governing class is eclipsed in the 10th house while the Sun and Saturn sit together at the nation's foundations. The official mood goes dark in public, the establishment is buried in the ground, and the chart's messenger stops to turn back.

This is a total lunar eclipse. The Moon at 23°57′ Virgo in the 10th passes through the Earth's shadow for sixty-five minutes of totality, and it does so high over Washington, forty-seven degrees above the horizon at greatest eclipse. In mundane terms the Moon is the political class and the public mood. Placed in the 10th, it is that class in the house of government: the official temperament, the administrative voice of the state. Virgo asks for accounting, efficiency and correction. The eclipse turns that audit dark at the point of greatest visibility, which can reveal what the audit could never see or expose the auditors themselves.

Across the axis, the Sun at 23°57′ Pisces sits in the 4th, conjunct Saturn within 1.62° and with Neptune at 29°23′, both combust. The executive light and the establishment are sunk in the land, the opposition and the foundations. Saturn under the beams is structure absorbed by the executive. Neptune under the beams, on the final degree of Pisces two weeks before leaving the sign, is an old dream at its exhaustion. The Moon opposes Saturn from the 10th: the governing mood against the buried order. The Sun sextiles Uranus within 0.10° and the Moon trines it, so the break arrives through the working body of the state (Uranus in the 6th) as a technical, sudden rearrangement rather than a slow reform.

The root is a closed loop: Mercury to Mars to Moon and back to Mercury. Mercury at 9°31′ Aries in the 5th is less than a tenth of a degree from stationing retrograde, so the messenger is at a standstill. Mars is in fall in Cancer in the 8th, working on debt and shared obligation from a weak hand. The eclipsed Moon closes the loop. Message, force and mood each serve one another, and at this moment all three are compromised. The announcement stops, the force works in the debt house, and the mood is in shadow. Venus, retrograde and in detriment, sits beside Mercury in the 5th: the market and the arena are revisiting their prices.

Sagittarius rises with Jupiter, the chart ruler, at 13°34′ Gemini in the 7th in detriment. The nation's steering answers to its counterparts. Greer finds no hard hit on the ingress; this eclipse belongs to the seam between winter and the Aries year. It is the Full Moon chapter of the September 15, 2023 New Moon in The Quiet Sowing band, so something planted eighteen months earlier comes due here. The U.S. chart takes a near-exact hit: eclipse Saturn opposes U.S. Neptune within 0.06°, and eclipse Neptune opposes U.S. MC within 0.48°. The binding force of the moment falls on the nation's founding ideal and its public standing. The constructive pole is an honest reckoning in which the governing class loses a flattering image and gains a truthful one. The shadow is an eclipse of accountability itself, in which the lights go out on the audit just as it becomes most visible.
