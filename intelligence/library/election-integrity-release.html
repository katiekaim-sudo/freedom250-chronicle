White House election files show real risks, not proof of changed results

The July files document real risks without proving a changed result. CISA's September bundle turns them into agency-owned reference material for a Regional Director, fusion-center and platform blueprint; the first proposal or instrument citing it is the next real change clock.

What we found

  • Voter data: reporting describes a 204,822,241-record U.S. voter dataset and Chinese analysis involving at least 18 states. Some later state data was bought or downloaded from commercial sites; the rest of the sourcing is partly redacted.
  • Intelligence: internal emails show a real dispute over how China reporting was described, and also show FBI analysts challenging the minority view's sourcing.
  • Election systems: CISA found serious patching, login and remote-access weaknesses, and authorized testers took full control of some state and local networks within hours or days. Its September bundle makes those findings agency-owned predicate material for a Regional Director, fusion-center and platform blueprint, without supplying the downstream proposal, jurisdiction, remediation or production ledger.
  • Registration: Michigan records show possibly fake registration applications and a multi-year investigation. DHS match figures on noncitizens and deceased registrants have no published method; reported maxima in DHS letters total 256,463.
  • On June 22, 2026, a federal court in Washington vacated the 2025 Homeland Security and Social Security records notices and the modified Systematic Alien Verification for Entitlements system. The court denied a stay on July 8, and the appeal remains pending. The SAVE America Act is not law; the live bill is the House amendment to S. 1383, passed February 11, 2026.

Why it matters

The story has moved from disclosure into administrative groundwork. CISA has created the reference stack a later rule, manual change, grant condition, procurement, MOU or operations plan can quote; that downstream instrument, not this plan alone, will show whether the proposed overlay gains authority, money and operating effect.

What to watch

  • pending · D.C. Circuit ruling in No. 26-5243 on the SAVE system and any replacement records notices.
  • pending · Senate action on the House amendment to S. 1383.
  • pending · DHS's matching method, data dates and state-by-state outcomes behind its registration figures.
  • pending · The CISA election platform's name, operator, data rules, enrollment roster and first live election use.
  • pending · A changed EAC or state patch-certification route and a named product/version fix that is approved, deployed and independently retested.
  • pending · The first official proposal, grant term, procurement, MOU, manual change or operations plan that cites the September CISA bundle.
