## Saros, Metonic and Moon-family time

## Answer first

The eclipse method has been carrying three different kinds of ancestry under
the loose word **family**. They are not interchangeable:

1. **Saros family:** the same evolving shadow-geometry series after 223
   synodic months, about 18 years 11 days 8 hours;
2. **Metonic echo:** the same lunar phase near the same calendar date and
   tropical-zodiac degree after 235 synodic months, about 19 years;
3. **Pessin Moon Family:** one New-Moon seed developing through First Quarter,
   Full and Last Quarter near 273, 546 and 819 days later.

The overlooked pattern is the relationship between the first two clocks.
Because `235 − 223 = 12`, one ancestral eclipse can return through its **Saros
branch** and then return again about twelve lunations later through its
**Metonic branch**. The two later events can sit at nearly the same historical
moment while belonging to different Saros series and doing different
astronomical work.

That braid is unusually visible from 2024 through 2026. It explains why the
eclipse years feel like a rolling return corridor without requiring us to call
every eclipse the same story.

## Three clocks, three permitted questions

| Clock | Astronomical identity | What it legitimately asks | What it cannot establish |
|---|---|---|---|
| Saros | 223 synodic months; same series and node geometry | How has this shadow family evolved from one eclipse member to the next? | Same calendar date, same zodiac degree, same locality or same political event |
| Metonic | 235 synodic months; same phase near the same calendar date and degree | What is returning to nearly the same seasonal and zodiacal address? | Same shadow path, same eclipse type or even another eclipse |
| Moon Family | New-Moon seed with 273/546/819-day phase chapters | Where is one seeded storyline in its growth, revelation or release cycle? | A 19-year historical return, a shadow series or a second evidence vote |

NASA describes the Saros as approximately 6,585.3 days and emphasizes the
similar geometry of members in one series. The additional eight hours also
mean Earth has rotated roughly 120°, so a Saros return does not normally put
the shadow over the same longitude. NASA's eclipse-period table separately
identifies the Metonic interval as 235 lunations and shows that, when both
events are eclipses, the later series is generally `s + 10`.

The practical reading rule is therefore:

> **Use Saros for shadow ancestry, Metonic for date-and-degree resonance, and
> Moon Family for the phase of one seed. Name the clock every time.**

## The 2025 lunar eclipses are hinge events

March 14 and September 7, 2025 each carry two different long ancestors at
once.

- **March 14, 2025:** the Saros descendant of March 3, 2007, and the Metonic
  echo of March 14, 2006;
- **September 7, 2025:** the Saros descendant of August 28, 2007, and the
  Metonic echo of September 7, 2006.

Then the 2007 ancestors return by Metonic rhythm as the March 3 and August 28,
2026 lunar eclipses. The 2025 lunar eclipses are therefore not isolated middle
points. They are **relay hinges** where one Metonic return lands while the next
Metonic return is being carried forward through the same-Saros ancestry.

This does not make the two years one political event. It does give the research
hearing a more exact structure:

1. inspect the 2006 same-date/degree window for symbolic recurrence;
2. inspect the 2007 same-Saros window for shadow-family development;
3. keep the 2024–26 return events in chronological order;
4. require object-level political receipts before claiming that any historical
   subject actually returned.

## Research protocol for future readings

Every eclipse reading should now open three separate retrospective windows:

1. **Moon-family window:** the actual New-Moon seed and its quarter phases;
2. **Saros window:** the immediately previous member of the same series;
3. **Metonic window:** the exact same-phase return about nineteen years
   earlier, explicitly recording whether it was an eclipse and which Saros it
   belonged to.

If a previous Metonic eclipse exists, calculate its intervening same-Saros
return as the **braid partner**. That event will normally lie about twelve
lunations before the current eclipse. Keep all four records separately dated.

Political research then asks four different questions:

- Did the same object or institution exist in the old Metonic window?
- Did the same shadow-family window carry a materially similar process?
- Did the braid partner alter, implement or contradict that process?
- What did the current Moon-family seed actually plant, reveal or release?

No affirmative answer may be inferred from geometry alone.
