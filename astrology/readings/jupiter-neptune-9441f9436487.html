Jupiter–Neptune

The planets

Jupiter — Zeus, expansion, faith, courage to hope. MUNDANE: hierarchical organizations — government, corporate, nonprofit; the corporate sector; courts, judges, religion, charitable organizations. (Greer: "the planet of hierarchy; he rules every organization set up in a hierarchical manner.")

Neptune — Dissolution, the ideal, oceanic depths. MUNDANE: collective movements, mass sentiment, propaganda; popular causes; unity (divine to mob). Almost always malefic unless completely unafflicted. Neptune dissolves whatever it touches — sometimes into transcendence, more often into confusion.

The combination — mundane

Barbault links Jupiter-Neptune strongly to idealistic and socialist economies and to France specifically. It is the cycle of faith meeting the collective dream — humanitarian, religious, utopian. When the hierarchy (Jupiter) encounters the dissolving tide of mass sentiment (Neptune), the result is either genuine humanitarian progress or "pie in the sky" that collapses into inflation, sectarian quarrels, and fighting for ideals that were never grounded in reality. The hard aspects historically precipitate rampant inflation when not well earthed.

Greer's delineation sharpens the mundane picture. Under helpful aspects, Jupiter-Neptune brings "peace and goodwill; benefits the people, especially the unfortunate/disadvantaged; humanitarian movements succeed; disputes find peaceful settlement." Hierarchical organizations — government, nonprofit, religious — turn their institutional power toward the collective welfare. Courts settle disputes with wisdom. Charitable organizations achieve real impact. The disadvantaged benefit because Jupiter's hierarchy serves Neptune's popular causes rather than exploiting them.

Under hostile aspects, Jupiter-Neptune produces "confusion, misunderstanding, charges of bad faith; conflict between political class and masses; conspiracy theories flourish" (Greer). The hierarchy's promises to the people ring hollow. Religious and nonprofit institutions face accusations of hypocrisy. The political class and the masses talk past each other — both convinced the other acts in bad faith. Conspiracy theories proliferate because Neptune's fog obscures Jupiter's institutional workings, and the people fill the gap with suspicion. Barbault adds that the hard aspects bring sectarian conflict and inflationary bubbles — faith inflated beyond all rational proportion.

Current: The 2022 Pisces conjunction (Neptune's home sign) seeded maximum idealism — the hierarchy's promise to the collective was cast in Neptune's strongest possible terms. The 2025 square = the ideal hits reality; hierarchical organizations face the masses' disillusionment. The trine later in 2025 = the ideal finds constructive expression; humanitarian movements and charitable organizations gain traction.
