Research summary

The six audited project-finance stacks did not establish crypto, stablecoin, tokenized debt, tokenized collateral or a deposit-token rail as a funding mechanism. Wholesale tokenization may be an adjacent operating rail, but a named borrower, instrument, investor cash, draw, settlement and loss path are required to connect it to a project.
