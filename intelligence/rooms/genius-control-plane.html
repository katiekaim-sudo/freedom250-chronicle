GENIUS Control Plane — Issuer Handoff × Reserve Custody × Distribution Whitelist

Twenty control events behind a payment surface that may barely change

A stablecoin can keep the same ticker, wallet and terminal experience while its issuer, reserve controller or U.S. distribution permission changes. These cards identify the legal controller, the object it can change and the event that activates that power.
