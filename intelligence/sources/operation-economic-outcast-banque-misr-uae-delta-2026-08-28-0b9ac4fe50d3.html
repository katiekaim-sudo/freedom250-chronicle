Operation Economic Outcast — Banque Misr UAE delta

Question and answer

Did Treasury's August 24 forecast produce a named financial-institution action?

Partially. On August 28, FinCEN found the five UAE branches of Banque Misr, collectively defined as Banque Misr UAE, to be a financial institution of primary money-laundering concern and proposed Section 311 Special Measure Five. The proposal was published September 1 at 91 FR 56085, document 2026-17871, docket FINCEN-2026-0232, RIN 1506-AB76. Comments close October 1.

If finalized as proposed, covered U.S. financial institutions would be barred from opening or maintaining correspondent accounts for Banque Misr UAE and would have to guard against indirect use of their foreign correspondent accounts. Through the cutoff, this was a proposal—not an operative prohibition. FinCEN says three direct U.S. correspondent relationships exist but does not name the institutions or establish that an account closed.

Separately, OFAC made operative blocking designations of Iranian national Reza Mohammad Taeedi, general manager of Bank Melli's Dubai branch, under E.O. 13224 as amended, and Hong Kong company Kameng Trading Limited under E.O. 13902. OFAC did not newly designate Banque Misr UAE itself.
