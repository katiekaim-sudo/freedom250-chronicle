The military moves on commercial ships, rail and fuel, and now aims for off-grid bases

U.S. wartime movement relies on private carriers, ports, rail and fuel companies held ready by contracts and activation deals. On September 30, 2026 the Department set up FORTRESS America, aiming for independent power at every major base, but which sites, how much power, the funding and the tests are still undefined.

What we found

  • USTRANSCOM calls commercial air carriers, sealift carriers and logistics firms its 'Fourth Component.' The Civil Reserve Air Fleet, Maritime Security Program and Tanker Security Program keep commercial capacity on retainer for a surge. This is not nationalization.
  • The Army's June 2026 energy request for proposals asks private partners to finance, build and run power plants on seven installations. They sell to the grid normally but must power the base for at least 14 days after an outage. The September 18 selections are conditional lease awards, not built plants.
  • FORTRESS America is a five-pillar program. The announcement sets no covered sites, load, endurance, funding, test standard or completion date. The existing legal standard is 10 U.S.C. § 2920's FY2030 critical-mission availability goal.
  • The Army's April 2026 industrial-base strategy treats its 23 depots, arsenals and ammunition plants as one system. The public record does not confirm the goal of 100,000 complete 155 mm shots per month has been met.
  • USTRANSCOM's Joint Transportation Management System aims to join movement and payment data for more than 100,000 users, with full capability targeted for FY2030. The February 2026 Maritime Action Plan proposes a Strategic Commercial Fleet and a Maritime Security Trust Fund; these are proposals, not law.

Why it matters

The military is gaining capacity by contracting, leasing its land and writing priority rights into private networks rather than owning everything. That fits a rebuild done through existing law, leases and payment systems, though most pieces are still solicitations or plans.

What to watch

  • FY2030 · 10 U.S.C. § 2920 critical-mission energy availability standard and full JTMS user capability.
  • pending · FORTRESS America naming covered sites, load and endurance standards, funding and tests.
  • pending · Army energy leases closing, plants built and islanding tests passed.
