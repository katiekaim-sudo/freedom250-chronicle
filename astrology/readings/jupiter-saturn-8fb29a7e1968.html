Jupiter–Saturn

The planets

Jupiter — Zeus. Expansion, faith, abundance. MUNDANE: every organization set up in a hierarchical manner — government departments, corporations, nonprofits, churches. Especially powerful in the corporate sphere with its drive for constant expansion and its focus on national wealth. Also: religion, clergy, courts, judges, charitable organizations. When dignified, the nation enjoys peace, prosperity, profit, and orderly growth. When debilitated, the corporate sector stagnates, corruption flourishes in the judiciary and the legislature, and the government ignores problems while they are still small enough to manage. (Greer: "of great importance in economic life — always watch Jupiter for clues to the nation's economic condition.")

Saturn — Kronos. Structure, limits, endurance. MUNDANE: the bureaucracy, civil service, the establishment; institutional inertia; the things in national life that endure. When dignified, the government enjoys stability and wise management. When debilitated, the nation suffers poverty, decline, and the unwanted consequences of past policy. (Greer: "the Greater Malefic — negative Saturn placements bring more grief than almost anything else in a mundane chart.")

The combination

Greer: "The Chronocrators — the Time Lords." The most fundamental social cycle in mundane astrology, governing the ground-base of the national economy, the corporate order, and the government's relationship to its own bureaucracy. Every 20 years, the corporate-expansive impulse (Jupiter) meets the bureaucratic-conservative impulse (Saturn) and the nation's social architecture is reset. The December 2020 conjunction in Aquarius was the Great Mutation — the first Air conjunction since 1186, shifting the element-basis of civilization from Earth (materialism, industry, land-based wealth, ~1842-2020) to Air (information, networks, ideas, digital wealth, ~2020-2220). Greer's own "Great Mutation" element-shift framing confirms this reading.

Greer's delineation for the pair is direct. Helpful Jupiter-Saturn aspects are "very favorable for the national economy; wealth + hard work" — the corporate sector and the civil service cooperate, the legislature passes sound economic legislation, the executive branch governs competently, and the laboring classes benefit from stable employment. Hostile Jupiter-Saturn aspects bring "some form of economic trouble; the government faces difficulties" — the national economy contracts or overheats, the corporate sector and the bureaucracy work at cross-purposes, and the executive branch struggles to maintain public confidence.

Current: The first square (crisis/test) of what was seeded at the 2020 Great Mutation. The Air-era social structures — the digital economy, the information-based corporate order, the restructured federal bureaucracy — face their first real challenge. The national economy is tested; the government must prove it can manage the new order. The corporate sector's expansion meets the civil service's institutional resistance, and the legislature must arbitrate between them.
