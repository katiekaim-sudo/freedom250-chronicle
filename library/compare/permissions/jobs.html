Federal securities or derivatives-market authority

Supervise and authorize

Legal basis: Federal securities laws and the Commodity Exchange Act, each within its statutory perimeter.

When it applies: A legally classified securities-market or derivatives-market activity calls for the applicable SEC or CFTC registration, designation, supervision, or rule route.

Named firms: U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission (public body); Commodity Futures Trading Commission (public body)

Who can use it: U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission; Commodity Futures Trading Commission; SEC or CFTC offices acting under delegated authority; Self-regulatory organizations only within their separate statutory rulemaking roles

ConditionsClassify the instrument, contract, and activity. Identify the exact entity and applicable SEC, CFTC, or SRO route. Recover current registration, designation, order, exemption, rulebook, and effective-date evidence.

The limitNeither tokenization nor a technical ledger decides securities/commodity classification or creates SEC/CFTC permission.

When it applies: An investor elects direct registration for a security whose issuer offers it.

Named firms: None named yet

Who can use it: natural person; legal entity

ConditionsIssuer offers direct registration. Investor meets issuer/transfer-agent onboarding and account requirements. For a DRS transfer, the security and transfer agent are DTC DRS eligible.

The limitBankruptcy is not a catch-all: domestic banks are excluded by section 109, and specialized regimes can govern other regulated entities. A token ledger does not by itself identify estate property, perfection, custody, or priority.

CFTC swap reporting counterparty or registered-entity route

Report trades and retain regulatory records

Legal basis: CEA swap-data framework and 17 CFR Parts 45 and 49.

When it applies: The transaction is a CFTC-regulated swap and Part 45 assigns a reporting counterparty or reporting task to a SEF, DCM, or DCO.

Named firms: None named yet

Who can use it: swap dealer; major swap participant; other reporting counterparty as allocated by Part 45; swap execution facility; designated contract market; derivatives clearing organization; third-party reporting facilitator acting for the responsible reporter

ConditionsClassify the instrument as a CFTC-regulated swap and identify the reporting hierarchy or registered-entity rule. Select an appropriate SDR and meet its data, validation, timing, and access requirements. A counterparty may contract with a facilitator to submit data, but preserve the assigned reporting party's responsibility and evidence of successful acceptance.

The limitA delegate submits or facilitates only under the responsible party's arrangement; it is neither automatically the reporting counterparty nor an SDR. A generic blockchain log or oracle feed is not an SDR report.
