March 26–31, 2025 Aries Eclipse Authority Window

Answer first

The D.V.D. third-country-removal dispute is not the only material object from the March 2025 Aries-eclipse window that acquired a later operating form. It is the cleanest same-case judicial return, because the same DHS guidance, procedure and parties can be followed from temporary restraint to final judgment, appellate review and a September 2026 Supreme Court application.

Five other independently material command objects from March 26–31 also became later machinery:

  • the automobile-tariff proclamation became an administered 25-percent duty system and was amended with a temporary import-adjustment offset;
  • the federal labor-relations exclusion order was implemented through agency determinations, expanded by a successor order and met a proposed appropriations constraint;
  • the D.C. Safe and Beautiful order created a multi-agency task force that later became an attributed operating enforcement structure;
  • the American-history order became a document review and report, followed by a successor order directing use of available authorities and public signage; and
  • the United States Investment Accelerator became a Commerce office with an executive structure, operating examples and a later $100 million budget request.

These are not one program and do not mature to the same legal or operating level. Their common factual grammar is narrower: a presidential command named an authority container, and later records show whether that container acquired an operator, procedure, enforcement route, budget request, successor instrument or judicial remedy.

Research question

Which material federal authority objects were signed or initiated from March 26 through March 31, 2025, and what verifiable operating, legal, budget or successor state had each reached by September 25, 2026?

Scope and method

This is an action-date window around the March 29, 2025 eclipse, not a claim that every object was published in the Federal Register during the same six days. The related Federal Register publication census for March 26 through April 1 contains 506 documents: 432 notices, 40 rules, 25 proposed rules and nine presidential documents. Several executive orders signed inside the action window were published after April 1. Signature, filing, publication, effectiveness, implementation, operation, budget request and court disposition therefore remain separate clocks.

The six objects were selected independently for material authority and same-object lifecycle traceability. Astrology supplied neither the search terms nor the selection test. This is not an exhaustive substantive review of all 506 published documents or every presidential action in the period.

1. D.V.D.: the command meets a judicially enforceable procedure

The March 28 temporary restraining order and March 30 DHS guidance created the strongest same-object chain in the set. By September 18, 2026, the First Circuit had vacated two sequencing declarations for lack of standing but otherwise affirmed the district court's final judgment setting aside the challenged guidance. The appellate court dissolved its stay on September 23. DHS filed Supreme Court application No. 26A406 the next day, and Justice Jackson requested a response by September 28.

The same removal authority is present throughout, but the contested decision right changes state:

provisional restraint → agency procedure → stay → final APA vacatur → appellate review → appellate stay dissolved → emergency application

The government's operational assertions about flights, cost and diplomatic engagement remain filing assertions rather than independently reconciled operating records. The complete legal spine is preserved in Research Packages/DVD Third-Country Removal Authority 2025-2026/README.md.

2. Automobile tariffs: a proclamation becomes an administered price boundary

Proclamation 10908 of March 26 imposed an additional 25-percent tariff on covered automobile imports beginning April 3, 2025 and on automobile parts on the date specified through Federal Register administration, no later than May 3, 2025. It also assigned implementation functions to Commerce and Customs and Border Protection and created a process for adding parts.

The April 29 amendment did not merely repeat the policy. It modified the same tariff system by creating import-adjustment offsets for automobiles assembled in the United States: an amount equal to 3.75 percent of aggregate MSRP for the first year and 2.5 percent for the second. The factual maturation is therefore command → collection architecture → operating adjustment. Later 2026 Canada-specific additional duties are a separate authority layer and are not backdated into Proclamation 10908.

3. Federal labor relations: exclusion becomes an implementation and funding contest

Executive Order 14251 made national-security findings and amended the list of agencies and subdivisions excluded from federal labor-management programs. It also delegated limited suspension decisions to the Defense and Veterans Affairs secretaries. Later Federal Register notices record agency use of that delegated mechanism.

On August 28, 2025, a successor order added additional components and extended the deadline for certain Defense and Veterans Affairs orders. Separately, the Administration's September 2025 statement on the House FY2026 NDAA objected to a proposed funding limitation, stating that it would prevent the Defense Department from operationalizing EO 14251. This is evidence of an active implementation and appropriations conflict, not proof that every exclusion was finally effective, judicially upheld or funded.

4. D.C. task force: a named coordination body becomes an attributed operating structure

Executive Order 14252 established the D.C. Safe and Beautiful Task Force, assigned a White House chair, named participating agencies and authorized coordination with local and transportation police. August 2025 presidential actions expanded the federal enforcement posture.

By August 2026, the U.S. Attorney's Office for the District of Columbia was describing the Task Force as the coordinating structure for an ongoing federal law-enforcement surge. Its release reported case, prosecution, firearms and crime figures. Those figures establish what the agency publicly claimed about operation; they are not an independent causal evaluation. A task-force charter, an arrest, a filed charge, a guilty plea, a trial verdict and a change in a crime series remain different factual states.

5. Smithsonian and public history: an instruction becomes a record demand, report and successor order

Executive Order 14253 directed federal historical-site and Smithsonian-related review and policy work. An August 12, 2025 White House letter initiated an internal review of Smithsonian exhibitions and materials. A December 18 letter said requested records remained missing and renewed the demand.

Executive Order 14416 of July 24, 2026 is a direct same-object successor. It expressly cites EO 14253, says the Domestic Policy Council completed a months-long review and issued a report, and directs the Interior Secretary, OMB, GSA and the Domestic Policy Council to use available authorities. It also directs temporary public signage. The lifecycle is unusually inspectable:

policy command → document demand → incomplete-record complaint → review/report asserted complete → successor authority and signage order

The successor order proves the institutional continuation. It does not make the report's evaluative claims neutral findings or establish that every directed change had been delivered by the cutoff.

6. Investment Accelerator: a named office acquires staff, cases and a proposed budget

Executive Order 14255 directed Commerce to establish the United States Investment Accelerator within 30 days, assist investments above $1 billion, staff the office under an Executive Director and place the CHIPS Program Office within its responsibility.

Later Commerce records identify the office, executive leadership and operating investment-facilitation examples. The FY2027 Commerce budget submission asks for $100 million and 75 positions for the Accelerator. The President's budget describes the same $100 million request under EO 14255. That is a stronger institutional state than an unassigned aspiration, but it remains important to distinguish the layers: the office is not a fund; a personnel announcement is not a statutory appointment; an announced investment is not delivered capital; and a budget request is not enacted authority, obligation or payment.

What the six branches show

The March window did more than publish a large volume of orders. Its durable objects repeatedly follow this sequence:

identity or command announced → authority container named → operating machinery appears → a counterparty, record, budget or court tests the boundary

At the cutoff, the branches occupy different states:

  • D.V.D. has the deepest same-case judicial chain;
  • the auto proclamation has the clearest price-and-administration chain;
  • EO 14251 has an implementation, expansion and legislative-resistance chain;
  • EO 14252 has an attributed multi-agency operation;
  • EO 14253 has the clearest record-to-successor-order chain; and
  • EO 14255 has the clearest office-to-staffing-to-budget-request chain.

The common maturation is not policy success. It is authorship becoming an institutional burden: someone must administer the tariff, defend the procedure, coordinate the enforcement, produce the record, run the office or fund the instruction.

Non-findings and boundaries

  • The 506-document Federal Register count is a publication denominator, not a claim of complete substantive review.
  • No causal relationship among the six objects was located.
  • The window is action-date based; it does not collapse later Federal Register publication into the signature date.
  • An executive order can create direction or an organizational container without proving completed implementation.
  • A successor instrument proves institutional continuation, not policy success.
  • An Administration statement of policy is a position in a legislative contest, not the final enacted state of the bill.
  • An agency press release establishes an official assertion, not independent validation of outcomes or causation.
  • A proposed budget does not create enacted authority, obligation, award, expenditure, delivery or acceptance.
  • No astrology interpretation is part of this factual package.
