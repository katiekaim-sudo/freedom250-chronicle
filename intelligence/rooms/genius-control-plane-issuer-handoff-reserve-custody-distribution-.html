GENIUS Act: Who Issues a Stablecoin, Who Holds Its Reserves and Who May Sell It

Twenty legal events that can change a stablecoin while the payment app barely changes

A stablecoin can keep the same ticker, wallet and payment terminal while its issuer, its reserve holder or its permission to be sold in the U.S. changes. Each card names who holds the legal power, what that party can change and what event lets it act.

Legal role

After the general effective date, only a permitted payment stablecoin issuer (PPSI) may issue in the United States.

Action

The regulator approves, denies or sets conditions on the exact legal entity that issues the coin.

Downstream effect

Minting must happen in an approved entity, even if the brand, blockchain, wallet and processor stay the same.

Customer sees

Possibly no visible change beyond revised terms.

Merchant sees

Merchants can keep the same payment method if the coin behind it stays eligible.

Failure mode

Mistaking a licensed affiliate, custodian or parent company for the issuer.

Proof needed

A regulator approval naming the exact issuer, plus token terms in force naming that same entity.

Do not overclaim

A charter alone is not PPSI approval under GENIUS.

Legal role

A digital-asset service provider (DASP) may offer or sell only a permitted coin, or one that meets the foreign exception.

Action

List, keep, restrict, wind down or remove each asset.

Downstream effect

Wallets, exchanges and issuance-service firms set coin eligibility in their product settings.

Customer sees

The coin may vanish from buy and sell menus, while self-custodied balances remain.

Merchant sees

The payment method can disappear from a processor dashboard without replacing the terminal.

Calling the rule a protocol ban, or assuming it covers every piece of software.

Proof needed

Provider listing policy, supported-asset records, customer notices and regulator guidance.

Do not overclaim

The statute's definitions expressly exclude protocols, self-custody software, validators and liquidity-pool participation.
