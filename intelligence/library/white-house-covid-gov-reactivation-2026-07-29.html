White House re-promoted its COVID lab-leak page during Fauci's hearing

On July 29, 2026 the White House posted a link to COVID.gov during Anthony Fauci's Senate hearing. The page already existed and mainly repackages a December 2024 House majority report; the post added no new study, finding or legal action.

What we found

  • The White House posted 'The truth. COVID.GOV' at 10:05:45 a.m. EDT, about 1 hour 36 minutes after the hearing's scheduled 8:30 a.m. start. The timing is verified; any planned coordination with the committee is not.
  • COVID.gov redirects to a White House page, 'Lab Leak: The True Origins of Covid-19', which the White House also promoted in April 2025. The page has since added a claim that DOJ opened an EcoHealth investigation, with no DOJ source linked.
  • Committee chair Rand Paul said Fauci invoked the Fifth Amendment and announced plans for a contempt vote. At cutoff no transcript count was reviewed and no contempt vote had happened.
  • Earlier records cited include a GAO finding of $1,413,720 sent to the Wuhan Institute of Virology through NIH and USAID channels. The House majority and minority reports disagree on origin.
  • A separate July 2026 federal policy bars federal support for dangerous gain-of-function research, with 90-, 120- and 180-day implementation steps. It notes privately funded research is not covered.

Why it matters

It shows official government channels, a White House domain and a Senate committee's rolling releases, being used to rewrite the public record on COVID, alongside a new research-funding policy. The study treats a single coordinated campaign as unproven.

What to watch

  • 2026-10-18 to 2026-10-26 · The first 90-day step of the high-risk life-sciences policy (date depends on whether July 20 or July 28 starts the clock); later steps fall Nov 17-25, 2026 and Jan 16-24, 2027.
  • pending · A committee notice, resolution text and roll-call on the announced Fauci contempt vote.
  • pending · A primary DOJ record confirming the EcoHealth investigation the White House page claims.
