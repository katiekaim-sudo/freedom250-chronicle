# Saturn meets Neptune as the Court ends the IEEPA tariffs

*An example crossover, filed by Claude on 2026-10-03 to set the tone for the section.*

## The story (research side — facts only)

- On **February 20, 2026** the Supreme Court held, in *Learning Resources v. Trump*, that IEEPA did not authorize the broad tariffs; termination and refund clocks began. (Workbench study timeline, Treasury / IRS taxation layer, `treasury-irs`.)
- The same day the President signed **EO 14389, *Ending Certain Tariff Actions*** (published Feb 25, 91 FR 9437). It ends the additional IEEPA duties imposed under EOs 14193, 14194, 14195, 14245, 14257, 14323 and others. (`03 - Executive Orders/2026-02-20 - EO 14389 - Ending Certain Tariff Actions.md`)
- He also signed **EO 14388**, continuing the suspension of duty-free *de minimis* treatment for all countries. (`03 - Executive Orders/2026-02-20 - EO 14388 - ….md`)
- Katie's X feed spiked on the Supreme Court that day ("US Supreme Court shoots down President Trump's sweeping…").

## The archetypal reading

Saturn is the law, the limit, the court and the boundary that says "this far and no further." Neptune is the ideal, the image and the tide that dissolves whatever has hardened. Katie's reading of this conjunction is that every 36 years the negatively crystallized ideal dies and a new collective image descends. On the very day the two met, at the first degree of Aries, the country's highest Saturnian body told a signature economic instrument that it had no legal ground to stand on. The tariff wall, which had worked as much as an *image* of national strength as an instrument, dissolved through the most Saturnian channel there is: a holding, a citation, a refund clock.

It is pure Aries-point theatre. The reset arrives procedurally, from inside the old machinery, which is exactly Katie's thesis of a revolution rebuilt through its own laws and agencies rather than torn down. Saturn squaring the national Venus adds the price: the Venus things (trade, value, what the country holds dear) meet a hard limit and a bill to settle, and the refunds begin. The Aquarius eclipse three days before sets the stage as a collective, systemic turning, and the executive answers the same day by signing its own unwinding (EO 14389) while keeping a different border on goods (*de minimis*, EO 14388). The ideal shifts shape rather than disappearing.
