The Money Crosswalk — Four Machines, Five Poles

Companion to The Whole Money Story. That note reads the narrative as one thing; this note maps the same story's moving parts across four angles of observation.

The thesis in one sentence

The domestic story is not "the U.S. is changing its money." It is: the unit of account remains recognizable, but the pipes — settlement, clearing, tokenization, reserves, sanctions, and state control — are being fought over worldwide. Four machines track four angles of that fight.

The five poles (from the World Machine taxonomy, applied here to all four)

TOKEN — dollar/private stablecoin rails (the Neptune pole of Saturn–Neptune). STATE-LEDGER — CBDCs and government-issued digital currencies. METAL — gold, silver, physical reserves (the Saturn pole). WALL — bans, licenses, sanctions, regulatory perimeters. PLUMBING — clearing, settlement, messaging rails (FedNow, SWIFT, DTC).
