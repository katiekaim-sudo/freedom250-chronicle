Later GENIUS Act and OCC implementation study and sources

The library tracks 17 dollar coins that are, or want to be, payment stablecoins. Those with a cited size total about $271.6 billion, and USD₮ (USDT) and USDC are 94% of it. Reserves are mostly short-term Treasuries, Treasury repos, money-market funds and bank cash, held by a few large custodians named on each card.

What's still uncertain

  • Market sizes are issuer-published figures only (the issuer's own page or the attestation report it publishes), each with its own date and basis. DefiLlama and other aggregators are not used (Katie's primary-source rule, 2026-10-19).
  • genius_route is the path the issuer is on or most likely to use; it is never a finding that approval was granted. No GENIUS approvals exist yet because the implementing rules are not final.
  • Attestations are point-in-time reports by accountants, not audits, unless the card says audit.
