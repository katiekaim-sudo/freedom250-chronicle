Fed Payment Stack Control Finality And Clock Gap

There is no single payments stack but five control layers on different clocks. Findings: the clock gap (clearing, guarantee and token hours extend faster than securities-transfer and central-bank cash hours, so private prefunded liquidity bridges the gap); the access paradox (the proposed Payment Account is strictly prefunded, no credit, $1,000,000,000 closing cap); and the accounting-control gap (no application-level ledger).
