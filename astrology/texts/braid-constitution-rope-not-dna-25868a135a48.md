# Braid Constitution — Rope, Not DNA

This is the durable design lens for Freedom 250 story braids. It governs how
factual research, narrative organization, institutional lineage, computed sky
and authored astrology may overlap without becoming one fused object.

## The governing image

**A braid is rope, not DNA.** Independent ropes may lie alongside one another,
twist together for one chapter, separate, return later or cease to be useful.
Nothing is genetically encoded into anything else.

- A factual event does not acquire astrological meaning as a source fact.
- A plotline does not own a planet or planetary relationship.
- A planetary relationship does not cause or explain a factual development.
- A shared image does not create evidence, confidence or an extra vote.
- A temporary overlap does not become a permanent storyline assignment.

## The six independent lanes

1. **Factual lane** — primary-source acts, records, dates, stages, effects and
   limits. Research owners control this lane.
2. **Narrative lane** — arcs, plotlines, subplots and braid questions. These
   organize Katie's story; they do not prove the story's interpretation.
3. **Institutional lane** — entities, decision rights, Executive Order routes,
   authoritative records, money, finality, correction, loss and remedy.
4. **Computed-sky lane** — ingress, lunation, eclipse, entity-chart contact and
   ordered planetary-relationship geometry. It contains no plot ownership.
5. **Authored-overlap lane** — a dated archetypal reading that places selected
   independent ropes beside one another for one explicit chart window.
6. **Forecast lane** — a separate registered literal call that can be wrong.
   Ordinary braid interpretation creates no Forecast Ledger entry.

The lanes may meet only through exact dates and explicit authored references.
No lane silently upgrades another.

## The project-wide question

Freedom 250 asks whether many individually procedural changes are cumulatively
founding a new operating order from inside inherited institutions. The factual
research already establishes repeated procedural refactoring across domains.
Durable control-right convergence and a founding-scale settlement remain tests,
not settled conclusions.

The institutional comparison uses eight recurring questions:

1. Who defines purpose and mandate?
2. Who or what is admitted?
3. Which record controls when systems disagree?
4. Who operates the route or interface?
5. Who declares finality and supplies the backstop?
6. Does capital reach physical capacity and operation?
7. Who corrects error, hears appeal and absorbs loss?
8. Do people recognize and materially benefit from the result?

These questions compare stories. They do not merge them or prove common command.

## The five parts of one story braid

### 1. Factual spine

The dated story from saved research, official records, adjudicated movements,
study timelines and social-attention context. Social attention is labeled as
attention only; volume is not worldly importance.

### 2. Institutional path

The entities, legal objects, Executive Order routes, stage transitions,
authoritative records, funding, finality, correction and observed results.
Direction, authority, implementation, operation and effect remain separate.

### 3. Sky packet

The governing ingress, the lunar chapter or eclipse, relevant entity-chart
contacts and the independent ordered history of planetary relationships.

### 4. Authored overlap window

A short, human-supervised reading names only the ropes that earn a place. It
must bind itself to one exact ingress and chapter, record an authored date and
carry a review date. It holds both poles and the strongest ordinary rival.

### 5. Historical return

When the review date arrives, the overlap remains frozen as a historical
reading. A new hearing may retain, revise, release, outcompete or leave a strand
unresolved. No old strand migrates automatically into a new chart.

## Strand language

User-facing roles are:

- **lead strand** — the strongest archetypal image in this dated reading;
- **companion strand** — a different relationship illuminating another job;
- **background strand** — inherited sky that conditions the chapter without
  claiming current direct action;
- **outcompeted strand** — plausible symbolism the whole chart or record did
  not favor;
- **unresolved strand** — genuinely undecidable at the stated cutoff.

Presence modes remain independent from roles:

- **inherited substrate** — the longer relationship continues;
- **active dialogue** — the pair is in a direct aspect in the authored window;
- **seed echo** — a chart contacts the conjunction family without recreating
  the pair's direct geometry.

The builder validates geometry. It never chooses the strand, role or meaning.

## Lifecycle

```text
factual timeline present
  -> institutional receipts identified
     -> chart packet available
        -> overlap authored for one ingress + chapter
           -> current authored window
              -> review due
                 -> retained / revised / released / outcompeted / unresolved
                    -> preserved historical window
```

These are separate states, not a maturity score. A braid may have excellent
research and no authored astrology. That is healthy. A vivid reading may still
have little institutional movement. That must remain visible.

## Braid families

Families are shelves, not super-braids:

- **Money and rails** — Tokenization; Stablecoin law; SEC and CFTC; Fed,
  Treasury and gold; Banks and credit; The world's money; XRP and Ripple.
- **The federal constitution** — Rebuilding the federal machine; Courts and the
  law; Immigration and who counts; Fraud and missing money.
- **Capacity and production** — AI buildout; Pentagon and industrial base;
  Tariffs and factories; Universities and science money.
- **Force and world order** — Iran and Hormuz; China, Russia and great-power
  deals.
- **Disclosure and legitimacy** — Epstein files; COVID reckoning.

Cross-listing identifies adjacency. It never transfers evidence, factual state
or an astrological strand between braids.

## Automation boundary

Nightly automation may refresh factual beats, research custody, computed
geometry, attention inventory, return dates and review-due flags. It may not:

- assign a planetary relationship from keywords or plotline membership;
- author or revise a strand role;
- move an authored overlap into the next chart;
- rewrite historical interpretation;
- promote a factual stage;
- register a literal prediction; or
- combine several rhymes into evidence or confidence.

Automation prepares the hearing. Authorship performs it.
