## 2026 Long-Clock seed-degree corridors

The ten canonical conjunction families were already present in the Long
Clocks engine, and individual seed-degree relays had already appeared in the
2026 readings. What had not been done was one governed census across **all
twenty-nine registered 2026 ingress and lunation charts**.

That census changes the emphasis. The interesting pattern is not that many
planets make some major aspect to some old degree. With ten seeds, fourteen raw
chart points and five admitted major aspects, a broad one-degree search almost
guarantees contacts. The distinctive pattern is that the old conjunction
coordinates repeatedly occupy the **lights, horizon and meridian**—the places
where a chart begins, culminates or becomes visible.

This companion does not replace any locked reading, conjunction dossier or
Long Clock phase chapter. A seed hinge is continuity, not a new governor.

## Ten histories make nine coordinate corridors

The ten canonical display anchors do not make ten independent places. The
January 2020 Saturn–Pluto conjunction at 22°46 Capricorn and the November 2020
Jupiter–Pluto conjunction at 22°52 Capricorn are only **0.087792° apart**.
They remain two different relationship histories, but they share one degree
corridor for contact counting.

The audit therefore carries:

- ten separately named conjunction families;
- nine deduplicated degree corridors;
- twenty-nine registered 2026 chart frames;
- the five standing major aspects; and
- an exact-strength band of 0.25° or less, with one degree retained only as
  strict context.

The Sun–Moon, ASC–DSC and MC–IC pairs are each counted as one axis. Both
endpoints remain visible in the data, but an opposition to the MC and a
conjunction to the IC do not become two votes.

## The result is a hinge pattern, not an everything pattern

Inside the 0.25° band the audit finds **40 deduplicated receipts** across 23 of
the 29 charts and eight of the nine corridors:

| Receipt type | Count |
|---|---:|
| Light, horizon or meridian axis | 17 |
| Independent body crossing an older seed corridor | 20 |
| A cycle-owner body returning to its own corridor | 3 |

Only three receipts are the obvious case of one of the conjunction's own
bodies returning to its seed geometry. Most body contacts are carried by a
third body, and almost half of the entire exact-strength set lands on chart
hinges.

The wide one-degree lane is useful mainly as a warning: all twenty-nine charts
and all nine corridors receive at least one contact somewhere. That lane is too
populated to prove importance by itself. At exact strength, six charts remain
clean controls and the shared Saturn–Pluto/Jupiter–Pluto Capricorn corridor is
the only corridor with no receipt.

The total contact count is elevated but not extraordinary, and the body count

Four of the seventeen hinge receipts are one mathematical family: the 1988
Saturn–Uranus seed at 29°55 Sagittarius lies on the major-aspect cross of every
cardinal ingress Sun. Aries and Libra square it, Cancer opposes it and
Capricorn conjoins it, all within 0.081°. Those four seasonal charts are real
separate clocks, but the geometry receives **one annual cardinal-cross unit**,
not four interpretive votes. After that collapse, fourteen hinge units remain;
that family-deduplicated result still sits above 96.5 percent of the rotated
placements.

Katie's live thesis—that the Barbault Basket may ultimately tell the story of

must remain separate at the source and meet only in an explicit comparison

## What this changes in the normal reading sequence

Seed-degree contacts deserve a small factual lane after authority, lunation or
ingress ownership has already been established:

1. name the canonical conjunction family and exact display anchor;
2. state the live chart point, major aspect and orb;
3. identify whether it is a body crossing or a light/angle hinge;
4. collapse co-located seed corridors and opposite axis endpoints;
5. collapse repeated structural families such as the four cardinal ingress
   Suns; and
6. ask whether the contact materially deepens Judgment without creating a new
   vote.

The result is a continuity instrument. It tells us which old relationship
history is standing at the door of a newer chart. It does not tell us, by
itself, what event will walk through.
