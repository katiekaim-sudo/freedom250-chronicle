Research summary

On September 28, 2026 the Commission registered Coinbase Clearing LLC as a derivatives clearing organization for fully collateralized futures, options on futures and swaps. The order creates a real affiliated clearing permission, with FCM and self-clearing membership routes, but does not approve a named product, USDC or blockchain settlement, spot or securities clearing, migration from Nodal Clear, or an operating launch. Current state: DCO registration effective; fully collateralized clearing permission live; first-product and first-clearing operating receipt not yet located.
