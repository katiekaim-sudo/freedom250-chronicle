The post-noon replay added SEC proposals, FSA's lagged official cohort-default-rate release, a resolved CFTC judgment and a noon New York Fed operation. An October 2 boundary correction later recovered FHFA's OIG budget allocation, OCC's credit-concentration handbook replacement and FDIC's monitorship closure without moving the original cutoff. None proves a government-wide final-day payment sweep.
