Object rule
CNT powder tonnes and dispersion or slurry tonnes/litres are not
additive. A commercial slurry can be more than 95 percent carrier and
dispersant. This registry therefore separates synthesis from formulation and
separates nameplate, measured output, ramp, qualification and serial supply.

Concentration ruling
The disclosed MWCNT figures above cannot support a global market-share number:
they mix measured output with nameplate and exclude several undisclosed plants.
They do establish a direction. Cnano reported 3.62 kt of 2024 powder output;
LG lists 2.9 kt/y and JEIO 2.0 kt/y. The disclosed U.S. synthesis figure is
CHASM's roughly 0.05 kt/y pilot, while Nano-C's scale is undisclosed. East Asia
therefore holds the deepest transparent operating base in this evidence set.
SWCNT supply is thinner and less transparent. OCSiAl Serbia has the clearest
battery-audited disclosed nameplate at 60 t/y. Zeon operates at undisclosed
scale; Cnano reported batch shipments while a dedicated line remained under
construction; Nano-C's U.S. output is not quantified.

Dependency map
Reactor and catalyst. Fluidized-bed CVD, CoMoCAT, Super Growth and other
routes are controlled process systems, not generic furnaces. Catalyst
formulation and reactor control can be the protected capability.
Purification. Catalyst metals and non-CNT carbon must be controlled;
Cnano expressly discloses external pre-purification for some material.
Dispersion. Cathode dispersions often use NMP while anode products can be
aqueous. Dispersant, milling, solvent recovery and contamination control are
separate choke points.
Metrology. Wall count, diameter, length, defects, catalyst residue,
moisture and bundle size determine whether two nominal grades substitute.
Qualification. The accepted object is usually exact grade + site +
dispersion recipe + electrode process. A new local source may require long
validation even if the datasheet looks equivalent.
Ownership and license. A U.S. building can remain dependent on foreign
IP, catalyst, powder or governance; a U.S.-controlled company can produce its
CNT in China.
Promotion gates
announced -> construction: site, permit, contractor, committed capital and
physical work.
construction -> commissioned: mechanical completion and startup/acceptance
record.
commissioned -> operating: measured output, shipments, utilization or
recognized production revenue.
battery-directed -> battery-qualified: exact grade and site evaluated by a
named cell maker, with sample, audit, approved-vendor and full-cell states
kept separate.
qualified -> serial supply: repeated shipment, purchase order, customer
filing, revenue or named vehicle/cell program.
domestic formulation -> sovereign chain: auditable powder, catalyst,
precursor, purification, dispersion and controlling-IP origin plus a viable
alternate source.
