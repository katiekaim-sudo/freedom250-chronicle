SeatThe questionIts parts

1. Venue + operator

Who lawfully operates the trading container and controls admission?

Operator identity; Venue authority; Participant rules

  • Operator identity: Exact legal person, U.S.-person status and affiliates.
  • Venue authority: Registration, exemption or other authority for the exact activity.
  • Participant rules: Published criteria, agreements and removal or denial powers.

2. Public DLT

Which public, permissionless ledger hosts the relevant applications?

Public read/write model; Exact deployment; Network governance

  • Public read/write model: Who can inspect, submit and validate state transitions.
  • Exact deployment: Named chain, contracts, versions and addresses.
  • Network governance: Upgrade, validator and emergency-control structure.

3. Applications + AMM

Which auditable code prices, routes and executes the trade?

Pool / pricing logic; Router / solver; Code control

  • Pool / pricing logic: Curve, auction or matching rule that forms execution.
  • Router / solver: Offchain intent, routing, filler or solver layer.
  • Code control: Source, audits, upgrade authority, pause keys and parameter setters.

4. Access + identity

Who admits the person, controls the wallet and enforces transfer eligibility?

Admission / KYC; Wallet + key control; Transfer restrictions

  • Admission / KYC: Identity, eligibility, AML-CFT and sanctions checks.
  • Wallet + key control: Key custody, transaction policy and recovery authority.
  • Transfer restrictions: Allowlist, credential and token-level compliance logic.

5. Stock + rights record

Who creates the representation and preserves the shareholder claim?

Product issuer; Mint / burn authority; Title / entitlement record; Corporate actions + proxy

  • Product issuer: Legal issuer or sponsor of the stock, entitlement or certificate.
  • Mint / burn authority: Who creates and destroys the digital representation.
  • Title / entitlement record: Which register, custodian or intermediary record controls the claim.
  • Corporate actions + proxy: Voting, disclosures, distributions, splits and communications.

6. Pair asset

What is delivered on the other side of the pool?

Issuer / obligor; Redemption / exit; Market depth

  • Issuer / obligor: Who owes the paired claim or administers the paired fund.
  • Redemption / exit: How the holder exits into bank money or the underlying.
  • Market depth: Observed inventory, spreads and executable depth for the named pair.

7. Liquidity provider

Which named firm commits or actually supplies own-account inventory?

Named Covered Firm; Inventory + hedge; Economics + custody boundary

  • Named Covered Firm: Exact U.S.-person legal actor and notice state.
  • Inventory + hedge: Own-account stock inventory, financing and risk transfer.
  • Economics + custody boundary: Compensation, agreements and proof customer assets are not held.

8. Data + market controls

Who supplies price inputs, transaction records, surveillance and regulatory reports?

Price / oracle; Transaction publication; Surveillance + halts; Regulatory reporting

  • Price / oracle: Reference markets, data sources and stale-price handling.
  • Transaction publication: Public and regulator-facing execution records.
  • Surveillance + halts: Manipulation detection, alerts, escalation and synchronized halts.
  • Regulatory reporting: CAT or other required event reporting and acceptance receipts.

9. Post-trade completion

Who completes custody, clearing, securities delivery, money settlement and repair?

Custody; Clearing / novation; Securities settlement; Cash settlement; Reconciliation + remedy

  • Custody: Who controls the security, token and keys, under which legal capacity.
  • Clearing / novation: Who nets, substitutes or guarantees obligations.
  • Securities settlement: Which CSD, register or entitlement book completes delivery.
  • Cash settlement: Which bank or settlement asset discharges the money obligation.
  • Reconciliation + remedy: Mismatch correction, failed delivery, default and recovery.

10. Interoperability + services

Which systems connect the venue to external records and rails?

Messaging / bridge; API / integration; Failure authority

  • Messaging / bridge: Transport of instructions or token state between systems.
  • API / integration: Interface to brokers, banks, custodians, recordkeepers and applications.
  • Failure authority: Canonical supply, pause, retry and loss responsibility when systems disagree.
