COVID and Biolab Disclosures

Workbench research packages for official COVID-origin, intelligence-process, research-funding, whistleblower and overseas-laboratory disclosures.

Packages

  • Rand Paul COVID Origins Reading Room — corpus and wave audit — source-preserved 14-PDF/3,887-page Senate corpus with hashes, page-addressable extraction, targeted OCR, completeness audit and bounded diary, Slack, testimony, enforcement and historical-architecture waves.
  • White House COVID.gov Reactivation — 2026-07-29 — primary-source orientation for the exact White House post, pre-existing COVID.gov page, July 29 Fauci hearing, open contempt procedure, majority / minority report boundary and bounded watch triggers.
  • HHS High-Risk Life Sciences Research Policy — 2026-07-28 — post-July 25 source-first delta for the July 20-dated / July 28-released government-wide DGOF and international-research policy, its federal-funding reach, implementation clocks, private-funding gap and vault-promotion map.
  • 2020 COVID Authority Spine — bounded 20-object primary-source baseline for emergency authorities, guidance changes, origins/funding records, countermeasures and vaccine authorization chains; preserves distinct date, time, timezone and place clocks for later comparison.
  • ODNI COVID and Biolab Disclosures — 2026-06-12 to 2026-06-18 — source-preserved audit of the June 12 biolab slides and June 18 67-item/391-page COVID-origin release.

Handling rule

Keep funding award, subaward, reported foreign-component spending, laboratory activity, origin hypothesis, intelligence consultation, analytic judgment, whistleblower allegation, inspector-general disposition, congressional testimony, declassification and public-release framing separate.
