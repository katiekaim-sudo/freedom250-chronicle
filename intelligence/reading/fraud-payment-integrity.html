Fraud and Payment Integrity

For Treasury's governmentwide prepayment-screening layer, read TREASURY_DO_NOT_PAY_GOVERNMENTWIDE_STRUCTURAL_MAP_2025-2026.md. It separates the DNP Working System, products, source datasets, program eligibility judgment and Treasury payment gate; maps federal, State, territorial, tribal and local/recipient reach; and carries the OPM Retirement Services September 25, 2026 activation boundary. It is retained in Official Research through an August 29, 2026 evidence cutoff and is not Chronicle canon.

Start with EMERGING_FRAUD_HEARING_RESEARCH_PACKAGE.md. Read the OBBB legal map as a separate enacted-text control; fraud, improper payment and payment error are not interchangeable categories.

For the open-accounting / blockchain thesis, read FEDERAL_FUNDS_LEDGER_ADOPTION_WATCHBOARD_2026-07-23.md. It joins the live on-chain-state forecast to the OMB Uniform Guidance proposal, Treasury payment systems, the live Defend the Spend public API, the 2021–2023 JFMIP permissioned grant-ledger prototype, State award conditions, disaster-reconstruction use cases, adoption branches and pre-registered falsifiers. It is workbench-only pending review.

FEDERAL_TRANSACTION_RECORD_DEEP_DIVE_2026-07-23.md takes the next step: which record controls at each stage from appropriation through recovery, whether one live payment can be traced into a State and downstream expenditure, which identifiers and clocks break, what fraud a ledger can and cannot address, and what operative instrument could require adoption.

Pennsylvania CCDBG State-Edge Pilot 2026-07-27/README.md tests the next unresolved seam. It carries FAIN 2501PACCDD and the April 17, 2025 public payment into Pennsylvania's published PELICAN, SAP, ELRC/provider-payment and ACF-696 architecture. It identifies the exact public proof boundary, preserves the correction/amount-state firewall, crosswalks the existing Observatory settlement corpus, identifies Wells Fargo's bounded CMIA fiscal-agent role, separates State and ELRC reimbursement and bank-account layers, and contains dormant records-request modules. Nothing has been filed; the pilot is workbench-only pending review.
