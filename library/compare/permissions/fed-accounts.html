Kraken (Kraken Financial)

Limited-purpose account

crypto exchange group with a state-chartered crypto bank (Wyoming SPDI)

Exact legal entity: Payward Financial, Inc., doing business as Kraken Financial (a Wyoming special purpose depository institution); parent Payward, Inc.

Record cutoff: 2026-10-19

Account: limited-purpose "skinny" account

Reserve Bank: Federal Reserve Bank of Kansas City

What it allows: Only the Fedwire Funds Service (large-value wire transfers), and only in a way that rejects any payment that would overdraw the account. No intraday credit, no discount-window loans, no interest on balances, and a cap on the end-of-day balance (amount not published). Only Kraken Financial may use it; the Kraken exchange and other Payward companies may not. Initial term of one year. Kraken told Reuters it also has no FedNow or ACH access. As of mid-July 2026 Kraken's CEO said the account was not yet operating; no later public evidence of use was found.

What this does not establishA charter and a Federal Reserve account are separate permissions. Neither, by itself, establishes every product’s operating use.

Decided: 2026-03-04
