DTC Tokenized Entitlements — Record-Sovereignty Legal Map

Current saved study

'The token is a programmable representation inside DTC's entitlement ledger, not an autonomous bearer share and not an issuer-register substitute.' The issuer/transfer-agent register (Cede & Co.) controls registered ownership. DTC's books hold the participant entitlement, with the Digital Omnibus Account and LedgerScan as DTC's official token records. The participant's books hold the customer interest. The chain records movement, but DTC can mint, burn, pause, freeze, claw back and force-transfer without keys. Key control is not legal entitlement. Exit to direct registration requires detokenization, then DRS/FAST/DWAC. SEC action is a revocable staff no-action position, and the contest between issuer-sponsored and DTC-entitlement tokenization is unresolved.

Exchange Tokenized-Trading Rule Crosswalk

Current saved study

No. The Commission approved Nasdaq's rules on 2026-03-18, and five NYSE-family exchanges plus 24X filed immediately effective rules. Tokenization is an optional post-trade settlement-form flag: same CUSIP, symbol, order book, priority and fees; market data does not distinguish forms; settlement stays T+1. DTC decides eligibility, and anything ineligible falls back to traditional settlement. No operative start notice was located. About 11% of DTC participants were excluded per the 24X filing. 'The most important word is ... fungible.'
