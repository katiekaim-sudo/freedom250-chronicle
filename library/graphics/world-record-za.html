South Africa: what does the saved digital-money record establish?

South Africa has an operating but layered crypto-service regime: the FSCA licenses covered FAIS advice, intermediary and investment-management services; FIC Item 22 and Directive 9 impose separate AML and travel-rule duties; SARS applies tax and CARF reporting; and SARB and FinSurv retain currency, exchange-control and settlement authority.
