Revolutionary FAR Overhaul — Procurement Control Map

Updated: 2026-08-15
Status: factual legal map
Rule: A class deviation is the buying rule now. A Companion Guide is not a rule. A proposed FAR is not a final FAR.

Short answer

EO 14275 (Apr. 15, 2025, 90 FR 16447) orders the FAR cut back to what statute requires or what sound procurement still needs. OMB M-25-26 (May 2, 2025) splits the work into two clocks: Phase I class deviations now, Phase II notice-and-comment later under 41 U.S.C. § 1707.

Acquisition.gov issues model deviation text part by part. Agencies adopt it by class deviation. The FAR Companion and buying guides are non-mandatory. They are not the FAR.

FR Doc. 2026-12559, 91 FR 37550 (June 23, 2026), is one of twelve proposed rules (FAR Case 2026-001). Comments closed July 23, 2026. A final rule was not retrieved at cutoff.

EO 14402 pushes fixed-price as the default. EO 14398 writes a DEI-ban clause into contracts and calls compliance material for the False Claims Act. Neither order repeals Title 41. Neither order is the FAR.

This is the buying rulebook under the payment record. Defend the Spend can show a payment. It cannot show whether the award was made under the old FAR, a class deviation, or a rule that has not yet been issued.

2. Binding FAR vs Companion Guide

The FAR is the single Government-wide procurement regulation. 41 U.S.C. § 1303(a)(1); 48 C.F.R. ch. 1. A protest lies for “an alleged violation of a procurement statute or regulation.” 31 U.S.C. § 3552(a). That is the protest hook.

A class deviation is an authorized departure from the FAR for more than one contract action. Once an agency issues the RFO class deviation, that text is the agency’s FAR for new solicitations. Acquisition.gov FAQ: “The model deviation text for a particular FAR part is not applicable to an agency until the agency has issued a class deviation.”

The FAR Companion, buying guides, and Practitioner Albums are the other half of what M-25-26 calls Strategic Acquisition Guidance. Acquisition.gov FAQ, in terms:

  • Buying guides “are non-mandatory resources.”
  • They “will remain outside the FAR.”
  • They “will not include mandates or become part of the FAR.”

A Companion practice is therefore not a procurement regulation under § 3552. Failure to follow a buying-guide tip is not, without more, a protestable violation. Failure to follow a statute, the still-codified FAR, or the agency’s issued class deviation is.

The test for what stays in the FAR (EO 14275 § 4): required by statute, or necessary to simplicity, efficacy, or economic or national-security protection.

Sunset (EO 14275 § 6): non-statutory FAR that remains is to expire four years after the final rule unless the Council renews it. That clock does not start on a proposed rule.

§ 1707 is why Phase I deviations and Phase II rules are different legal objects. A deviation can run a procurement tomorrow. A proposed rule cannot.

Sister orders (same buying book, different clauses):

  • EO 14402 (Apr. 30, 2026, 91 FR 24325). Fixed-price is the default “to the maximum extent consistent with law,” with written exceptions. Ninety days to try to convert the ten largest non-fixed-price contracts. This is a contract-type leash, not the RFO rewrite itself.
  • EO 14398 (Mar. 26, 2026, 91 FR 16147). Contracts must carry a clause banning “racially discriminatory DEI activities,” with flow-down and an express statement that compliance is material to payment under the False Claims Act, 31 U.S.C. § 3729(b)(4). FAR Council to amend the FAR and issue FAR 1.4 deviation/interim guidance.

4. Bind to the payment record

The payment record and the buying rulebook are adjacent machines. They are not the same machine.

The FAR sits on obligation. It is the rule for how the United States chooses a vendor and writes the contract that creates the obligation. Defend the Spend publishes a bounded set of payments. It does not publish which FAR part, which class deviation, or which protest clock governed the award.

A clean payment line can sit on a dirty procurement. Moving “how-to” out of the FAR into the Companion moves it off the protestable regulation and off the public rule text that a later payment record could cite.

If the chronicle ever joins a payment to its award terms, the join key is the solicitation/contract, the deviation number, and the FAR part then in force — not the Companion PDF.

6. Entity Theory read

The United States buys through a consolidated group. The FAR is the group’s purchasing manual — one book, three issuers, one OFPP editor. The overhaul is a function transfer inside the same legal person. The statutory FAR duty in § 1303 does not move. What moves is content: mandates leave 48 C.F.R. and land in a guide the Council says is not a regulation. That is how a consolidated group changes how a subsidiary must buy without amending the organic act.

Four clocks: statutory existence (Title 41 still there) · personnel (CO discretion grows where the mandate leaves) · function (“how-to” leaves the regulation; protest jurisdiction follows the regulation, not the guide) · obligation (the same appropriated dollar; the rule that shaped it may now be a class deviation). A press release that “the FAR is gone” is not a repeal.
