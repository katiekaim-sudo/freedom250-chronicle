The entity map that governs the sweep

The federal government cannot be researched as one flat list of websites.

Layer Controlling treatment
President and EOP A presidential instrument is distinct from department implementation and operating evidence.
Congress Committee action, chamber passage, enrollment, presentation, enactment, effectiveness, and implementation are separate clocks.
Judiciary Filing, docketing, hearing, order, judgment, mandate, and operating effect are separate objects.
Departments Department, bureau, office, mission, and OIG remain separate actors even when one parent site publishes the record.
Independent regulators Commission/board, chair, staff, ALJ, and inspector general carry different authority; quorum effects are action-specific.
Federal Reserve System Board, FOMC, Reserve Banks, payment services, and SOMA must not be collapsed.
Corporations, trusts, and GSEs Statutory corporation, trust instrumentality, chartered private nonprofit, GSE, and conservatorship require separate legal and accounting states.
Coordination overlays NSC, FSOC, CFIUS, FFIEC, and the Intelligence Community coordinate participants; they do not own every member.
Contractors and utilities An award, GOCO role, or operating agreement does not make the private legal person a federal agency.
International organizations The United States may be a member, shareholder, representative, or seat-holder; the organization remains a separate international legal person.

The campaign used one common object chain:

proposal → authority → delegation/determination → rule/order/award → legal effectiveness → obligation/commitment → implementation → draw/disbursement/delivery → acceptance/operation → measured result → audit/adjudication → correction or closeout

Amounts were kept in their own states. A request, ceiling, face value, obligation, commitment, draw, disbursement, expenditure, questioned cost, restitution order, and cash recovery are not interchangeable.

Priority corrections landed

Custodia is on a merits docket

Custodia filed its certiorari petition July 10. The Supreme Court docketed it July 14 as No. 26-62; a response is due August 13 absent waiver or extension. The older No. 25A1320 remains the extension application. Docketing is not a cert grant, merits decision, or master-account access.
Source: Supreme Court docket No. 26-62

Warsh and Powell occupy different offices

Kevin Warsh is Chairman of the Board of Governors and Chair of the FOMC. Jerome Powell’s chair term ended, but his separate term as a Governor continues through January 31, 2028. The official appointment, oath, FOMC selection, roster, and biographies control over an absence from the speeches index.
Sources: Warsh oath and FOMC selection; Board roster; Powell biography

The May ESF statement is genuine; the $9B Mexico line is not a July increase

The May statement records no May SDR sale, the closed Argentina swap, and a $9 billion Mexico Exchange Stabilization Agreement ceiling. The $9 billion ceiling dates to the November 30, 2018 amendment and is independently supported by CRS. Treasury’s overview remains stale at $3 billion. The June statement remained unposted at the July 25 cutoff, before its statutory 30-day clock expired. The original May publication timestamp is not publicly stated.
Sources: ESF reports; May statement PDF

NSCC has three separate clocks

The July 27 SR-NSCC-2026-009 SLD comment deadline remains intact. DTCC Important Notice A9799 separately moved CNS client testing from July 27 to August 24 because the PSE transition required more operational work. November 14 remains a stated production target, not an authorization or occurred go-live.
Source: DTCC Important Notice A9799

The GENIUS November premise failed

No public final implementing rule surfaced through July 25. The July 18 deadline passed, but that miss did not itself put the Act into force and no November ignition clock fired. The outside statutory backstop remains January 18, 2027, unless a later final rule starts an earlier 120-day clock. The former November watch was removed; the approximately September 20 hinge remains explicitly labeled derived arithmetic rather than a statutory date.

Section 122 and forced-labor Section 301 are not one tariff

Proclamation 11012 expired by its own terms at 12:01 a.m. EDT July 24. No extension or replacement Section 122 object surfaced through the cutoff; customs cessation was not independently observed. A separate forced-labor Section 301 architecture covering 60 investigations became customs-operative at the same clock, with economy/product-specific annex treatment and future separate TRQ notices.
Sources: Section 122 proclamation; USTR forced-labor action

The H.4.1 question is bounded

The central-bank liquidity-swap aggregate printed $250M / $170M / $135M / $378M on July 1/8/15/22. H.4.1 supplies no counterparty breakdown. The series remains a watch; a named counterparty cannot be inferred from it.

High-value operating-state changes

  • OCC: Corporate Decision 1381, dated July 21, denied Wise National Trust. That is not a master-account ruling or a permanent bar.
  • CFTC: Part 20 routine reporting sunset did not eliminate all books, special-call, and other data duties.
  • Defense: CMMC Phase II was suspended July 13 pending review; Phase I and underlying cyber duties remain.
  • Defense software: the Oracle vehicle carries a $3.311B base ceiling and $6.990B option-inclusive ceiling with zero obligated at award.
  • Autonomous flight: VENOM-AIR moved a fleet-standard F-16 into human-supervised AI-agent flight testing; it is not combat fielding or engagement authority.
  • DLA: Weapons Support is a multi-clock organizational transition; no immediate readiness or throughput gain is established.
  • Health: GLP-1 Bridge reached an operating-program state. Medicaid deferrals remain reversible documentation-pending actions, not permanent cuts or proven fraud.
  • Energy: AEP Texas reached financial close, not full draw or completed grid construction. Zero-power reactor criticality is not commercial operation or generation.
  • Commerce/space: the Space Commerce licensing object remains a nonbinding coordination proposal; FAA, FCC, and CRSRA authorities remain separate.
  • Congress: H.R. 9770 was House-passed only; H.R. 9725 remained committee-stage at cutoff.
  • AmeriCorps: the Corporation for National and Community Service remains an active statutory corporation; July 20 awards are operating evidence. “Dissolving” is not a valid unqualified state.
  • USAID/State: USAID persists statutorily while independent operations largely ended; State manages transferred functions and awards; residual closeout duties and USAID OIG jurisdiction continue. The approximately $45B figure is reported award face value, not cash spent, lost, or recovered.
  • EXIM/DFC: these are separate statutory corporations with separate boards, instruments, and accounts.
  • Sanctions: designation, list publication, legal effectiveness, blocking, license, transaction, enforcement, judgment, collection, and removal remain separate clocks.
  • Alien Terrorist Removal Court: FJC reports the first government application in July 2026, but no exact date, docket, applicant, hearing, ruling, or outcome is public. Activation is not an authorized removal.
  • CIT: Administrative Order 26-01 ended the automatic stay for newly filed IEEPA tariff cases; that is procedural docket control, not tariff merits or refund entitlement.
  • OGE: the July 23 interim final rule applies to specified Schedule Policy/Career employees, not the entire workforce; comments are due August 24.
  • CPSC: mandatory certificate eFiling became effective July 8; the July 22 Section 12 complaint is a complaint and requested relief, not a judgment.

Federal Group architecture seam

The live Federal Group tree correctly separates many bodies, but the sweep exposed a deeper schema problem: parent-child rendering is doing work that should belong to typed relations.

  • USAID’s simultaneous statutory, transferred-function, closeout, and OIG states cannot be represented honestly by dissolving under State.
  • The Intelligence Community is a coordination architecture, not a parent agency.
  • International organizations are legal persons connected by membership/shareholding/representation, not U.S. quasi-agencies.
  • USF is a mechanism; USAC is its private nonprofit administrator.
  • FCA, FCSIC, the cooperative Farm Credit System, and its Funding Corporation are regulator, insurer, system, and issuer—not an ownership chain.

No forced topology edit was made in this promotion. The safe next change is a typed-relation schema (member_of, administered_by, transferred_functions_to, shareholder_in, coordination_overlay) plus a durable chart-data injector. That is a structural build, not a factual wording correction.
