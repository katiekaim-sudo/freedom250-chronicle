2026 ingress–lunation seams

An ingress never opens onto a blank lunar page. Each of the four 2026 cardinal ingresses occurs inside a New Moon chapter planted while the preceding seasonal chart still governed. The ingress changes the seasonal authority at its exact clock. It does not reset the monthly chapter already in motion.

This distinction changes how the readings join. The first Full Moon after an ingress is not the beginning of the season's lunar story. It is the first revelation inside a chapter whose seed crossed the seasonal boundary with it.

The four clocks remain distinct:

  • the inherited New Moon governs the local month until the next New Moon;
  • the new cardinal ingress governs its seasonal or six-month frame;
  • the Full Moon simultaneously belongs to its longer Pessin Moon-family lineage;
  • direct planetary relationships retain their own conjunction-seed histories.

None receives extra votes merely because the clocks overlap.

The control prevents an inflated uniqueness claim: uncommon in this twelve-year span is not unique in history.

Pisces becomes action at the Aries threshold

The March 19 Pisces New Moon is only 1.557 days old when the Sun enters Aries. Its Jupiter–Moon dispositor circuit remains intact, and the ingress adds Mars. The seasonal handoff does not discard the seed's meaning-making and public feedback loop. It supplies an action carrier.

The sky's raw distribution barely changes: its occupied span moves from 160.22° to 160.23°. This is not a scattered field reorganizing into a new shape. It is nearly the same field acquiring a new command function.

The seed degree also reaches the incoming chart:

  • Uranus receives an exact-band sextile within 0.146°;
  • the ingress Sun remains within 1.549° of the seed;
  • Neptune remains within 3.322°.

The April 2 Libra Full Moon then changes the command picture completely. Its root is Venus alone; none of the ingress's Jupiter–Mars–Moon root survives as the final authority. That is why the Full Moon can reveal the Pisces seed while nevertheless speaking in a distinctly Venusian register of terms, balance and counterparty.

Gemini survives the Cancer threshold intact

The June 15 Gemini New Moon is 6.229 days old at the Cancer ingress. Its Mercury–Moon circuit passes through the threshold unchanged. This is the cleanest continuity case of the four: the sign of the seasonal Sun changes, but the chart's root-level conversation between record and public need does not.

The seed degree squares the ingress Moon within 0.774°. The inherited message therefore meets the incoming public body as an active test rather than a loose background echo.

At the June 29 Capricorn Full Moon, Mercury and Moon remain in the root while Mars and Saturn join them. The revelation does not replace the seed's conversation. It gives that conversation force and boundary.

This seam also clarifies the July configuration. The July 14 Cancer New Moon opens the next chapter and carries the Barbault Basket, the already-owned Jupiter–Uranus–Neptune–Pluto configuration, into a Moon-ruled national frame. The separate long-clock companion deepens its complete-graph geometry; it does not rename or duplicate the Basket. Virgo hardens at the Libra threshold

The September 11 Virgo New Moon is already 11.860 days old when Libra arrives. Its four-member Mercury–Venus–Mars–Moon root does not pass through unchanged:

  • Mars and Moon survive;
  • Mercury and Venus leave the root;
  • Saturn enters it.

The chapter begins with repair, record, value, force and public need entangled. At the ingress, record and value no longer hold root seats; force, public need and boundary do. At the September 26 Aries Full Moon the circuit contracts again to Mars–Moon. Saturn leaves the root while the contest and the public response remain.

This is a structural explanation for why the Aries Full Moon and the October 3 Mars–Pluto opposition feel sharper than a generic Libra-season reading would suggest. The Virgo chapter is not erased by the ingress; its repair question is being carried into a root that has already hardened around action and limit, and the Full Moon pares the command field down to the two bodies most capable of making that contest visible.

The restraint case matters: the Virgo seed degree makes no major contact to an ingress body or angle within the declared 3.5° band. Continuity here is earned by the active chapter and root handoff, not by inventing an exact-degree hit. Not every seam needs one.

Sagittarius meets itself across the Capricorn horizon

The December 9 Sagittarius New Moon is 12.832 days old at the Capricorn ingress. Its Sun–Jupiter mission circuit is not discarded. It is absorbed into the winter chart's five-body operating chain:

> Sun → Saturn → Mars → Mercury → Jupiter → Sun

The seasonal handoff adds obligation, execution and record to the seed's purpose and mandate. This is not a new mission appearing on December 21. It is the prior mission becoming an interdependent institutional procedure.

The exact degree relay makes the handoff unusually literal. The Sagittarius seed at 16°56′ lies on the incoming Capricorn ingress Descendant at 16°55′, within 0.026764°. Equivalently, it opposes the ingress Ascendant by the same orb.

The mission planted under Libra governance therefore appears on winter's counterparty horizon: the other party, opponent, agreement, hearing or contract through which the season must meet what was already set in motion. The Capricorn reading's outward-facing Mercury-in-Sagittarius emphasis is not just new winter symbolism. The horizon itself receives the inherited Sagittarius seed.

Two days later, the December 24 Cancer Full Moon retains all five ingress-root members and adds Moon as a separate final dispositor. The operating chain survives the revelation, but public need acquires an authority seat of its own.

The January 7, 2027 chapter-closing New Moon has an exact registered clock but no chart-reading bone at this cutoff. Its clock delimits the chapter; its dispositors, shape and interpretation remain withheld rather than invented.

What the four seams add to the method

The practical reading order is now:

  1. name the governing ingress that is taking authority;
  2. identify the New Moon chapter already active at the exact ingress;
  3. compare the seed root with the incoming ingress root;
  4. test the seed degree against the new chart's bodies and angles;
  5. follow the first Full Moon as revelation, not as the chapter's beginning;
  6. retain the Full Moon's separate Moon-family ancestry;
  7. keep every direct planetary relationship on its own conjunction-seed clock.

This prevents two opposite errors. We no longer narrate an ingress as though it clears the lunar memory. We also do not make an inherited lunation the ruler of the entire season. The authority changes; the story crosses the threshold.
