# INTERNATIONAL MONETARY INITIATION — RESEARCH GUIDE

**Status:** Current Workbench reading guide; not a Chronicle landing.  
**Original package cutoff:** 2026-07-20, with July 13 component comparisons. The maintained regional answer identifies targeted later saved corrections separately.  
**Question:** If the next monetary phase does not begin in the United States, what would it mean for Japan, South Korea, Indonesia/ASEAN, Europe or BRICS to “start it”?

## Read in this order

1. `INTERNATIONAL_MONETARY_INITIATION_MAP.md` — the cross-region answer and the separate initiation clocks.
2. `INTERNATIONAL_CRYPTO_LEGAL_STATUS_DELTA_2026-07-20.md` — Japan's enacted FIEA crypto perimeter versus Russia's pending currency-asset/property framework.
3. `INTERNATIONAL_CRYPTO_NEXT_STEPS_SCENARIO_MAP_2026-07-20.md` — the implementation ladders, staged clocks, branch scenarios and promotion tests for Japan and Russia.
4. `INTERNATIONAL_BANK_MONETARY_TRANSITION_COMPARATIVE_DEEP_DIVE.md` — the all-country puzzle: deposits/export FX, sovereign collateral, public settlement, private money and cross-border adapters.
5. `JAPAN_BANK_MONETARY_TRANSITION_DEEP_DIVE.md` — bank-by-bank Japan expansion: deposit franchises, JGB positioning, trust money, tokenized deposits and asset-side production.
6. `INTERNATIONAL_MONETARY_REGIONAL_ANNEXES.md` — entity-by-entity factual spine.

## Method

The package treats “start” as six different clocks:

| Clock | Test |
|---|---|
| **Stress** | Did a market, currency, collateral pool or balance sheet force adaptation? |
| **Law** | Did an enacted or effective rule change what may legally be issued, held or settled? |
| **Pilot** | Did a controlled experiment use real or simulated participants/value? |
| **Production** | Can eligible users move live value under operating service terms beyond a bounded experiment? A closed-account service can be production. |
| **Interoperability** | Does the rail cross systems, currencies or borders? |
| **Control** | Who can admit, freeze, sanction, reverse, collateralize or confer settlement finality? |

Every regional record separates:

- the legal entity from the political brand;
- the tokenized asset from the settlement asset;
- messaging/orchestration from final settlement;
- tokenized central-bank reserves, retail CBDC, tokenized commercial-bank deposits and stablecoins;
- `proposed`, `law passed`, `effective`, `PoC`, `pilot`, `controlled go-live`, `production`, `scheduled` and `unverified`.

## Scope boundary

This is a factual-first research layer for later synthesis. It does not predict a single replacement currency, treat BRICS as a supranational issuer, or infer production from a proof of concept. Market-stress interpretation is kept separate from the legal and operational record.
