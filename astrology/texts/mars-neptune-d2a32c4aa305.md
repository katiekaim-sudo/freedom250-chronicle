# Mars–Neptune

## The planets

**Mars** — *Ares, conflict, action, capacity to assert.* MUNDANE: the military; war and violent crime; epidemic disease; natural disasters. Also: the men of the nation, men's vote and issues. Mars angular = classic war warning. Mars shows where the nation's combative energy is directed.

**Neptune** — *Dissolution, the ideal, oceanic depths.* MUNDANE: collective movements, mass sentiment, propaganda; popular causes; unity (divine to mob). Almost always malefic unless completely unafflicted. Neptune dissolves whatever it touches — sometimes into transcendence, more often into confusion.

## The combination — mundane

Under helpful aspects (conjunction when well-placed, sextile, trine), Mars-Neptune channels combative energy into popular causes. Greer describes results "fortunate for interests of the people; popular causes make headway; long-standing problems can be overcome." The military serves the people's interests rather than elite ones. Mass movements gain the energy to break through long-standing obstacles. Public health improves because Mars-as-surgeon works in service of Neptune's collective welfare. The fog lifts enough for decisive action on behalf of the disadvantaged.

Under hostile aspects (square, opposition), Mars enters Neptune's fog and the result is confusion, scandal, and hidden menace. Greer warns that "interests of the people suffer; scandals attract public attention; much talk of secret crimes and conspiracies; unfortunate for public health; can indicate epidemic." This is the fog of war made literal — military operations are compromised by deception, intelligence failures, or covert action gone wrong. Scandals involving secret crimes dominate the news cycle. Conspiracy theories proliferate because the actual conspiracy (Mars acting in Neptune's shadows) is real enough to feed them. The epidemic warning is significant: Mars rules disease and Neptune rules mass contagion, making hostile Mars-Neptune aspects a traditional red flag for public health crises.

**As trigger:** Mars-Neptune aspects are fast-moving (days to a couple of weeks) and mark the specific moments when covert operations, public health scares, or scandal eruptions activate the broader outer-planet storylines.
