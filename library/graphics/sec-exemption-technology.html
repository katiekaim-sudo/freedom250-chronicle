Freedom 250 · Digital money · Rules · Tokenized-stock exemption · Technology

Which crypto technologies could fit the SEC's tokenized-stock exemption, and has anyone actually started?

Plenty could fit, but no one has started under the order. The library reads 20 core systems and 14 added ones against the ten seats an exempt venue needs: 4 are core technical candidates (Ethereum, Stellar, Solana, Algorand), 8 conditional candidates, 5 supporting layers and 3 outside the core design. The only public step so far is Imperium's filing as a prospective liquidity provider on September 28, 2026; it says it has not begun, and no venue notice or launch was found through October 5, 2026.
