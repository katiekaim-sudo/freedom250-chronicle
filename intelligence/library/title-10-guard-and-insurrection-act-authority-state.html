Title 10 Guard And Insurrection Act Authority State

The President federalized Guard under **10 U.S.C. § 12406**. He did **not** invoke the Insurrection Act, **10 U.S.C. §§ 251–255**. No § 254 proclamation to disperse was retrieved. *Trump v. Illinois*, No. 25A443 (Dec. 23, 2025), **denied** the Government’s stay. The unsigned order held that “regular forces” in § 12406(3) “likely refers to the regular forces of the United States military.” Because the President had not invoked a statute that excepts the Posse Comitatus Act, 18 U.S.C. § 1385, the Government had not carried its burden to show that § 12406(3) lets him federalize the Guard to protect federal personnel and property in Illinois. The Court did **not** decide the Insurrection Act. Justice Kavanaugh said so in footnote 2 of his concurrence. Vault notes that say “SCOTUS greenlit the Insurrection Act” name a different object. The order leaves that statute unused and the Illinois deployment barred at the stay stage.
