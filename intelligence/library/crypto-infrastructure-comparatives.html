Who's building the new money machine?

Official study title: Crypto Infrastructure Comparatives

How do Ripple and other crypto-network buildouts compare across technology, corporate control, legal access and token value capture?

Research through 2026-09-30

Home room: Crypto & Digital Money
The short version (draft, not yet reviewed by Katie)

Who's building the new money machine?

No single coin is winning. The field is becoming many specialized networks linked through a few companies and regulated middlemen, and the native tokens are mostly fuel, not the money institutions actually move.

What we found

  • Each network is going after a different job: Ripple owns the most connecting pieces, Stellar distributes regulated assets, Hedera runs hybrid public/private workflows, XDC does trade finance, Quant coordinates bank ledgers, Chainlink supplies data, and Canton hosts private institutional apps.
  • Tokens like XRP, XLM, HBAR, QNT and LINK pay fees or secure networks. What institutions actually move is stablecoins, bank deposits, Treasury and fund shares, securities and trade claims.
  • On the September 17 update, J.P. Morgan/Kinexys is the most complete overall operation; Circle, Coinbase, and Stripe/Bridge are the closest crypto-native and payments versions.
  • Securitize complements Ripple rather than competing: Ripple handles money, movement, custody and liquidity, while Securitize handles the legal securities record and shareholder servicing.
  • The big test is moving from hosting one token or one pilot to repeated, legally enforceable use with rules, recovery and stress-proof cash. Power will sit with whoever keeps the official records and the cash.

Why it matters

The new financial plumbing still ends at banks, DTC, custodians and courts for final say. Old institutions are absorbing crypto networks rather than being replaced by them.

What to watch

  • H2 2026 · DTCC's target for production readiness of DTC-held securities on the Canton network; not yet live.
  • H1 2027 · DTC's planned connection of its tokenization service to the Stellar network.
  • End-2026 · Algorand's planned post-quantum multisignature support and treasury migration steps.
  • 2026 · J.P. Morgan's deposit token (JPMD) phased rollout on Canton; announced, not yet confirmed complete.

Research summary

A comparison of Ripple and other network and company buildouts that separates technology, corporate control, legal access and token value capture. The September 17 corporate-stack extension finds J.P. Morgan/Kinexys the most complete overall organism, Circle the closest crypto-native money-network-payments analogue after Arc mainnet, Coinbase the closest custody-markets-network machine, and Stripe/Bridge/Privy with separately controlled Tempo the strongest payments-distribution federation. Securitize is a complementary organism rather than a second Ripple: Ripple is the money, movement, custody, liquidity, prime and enterprise-treasury plane, while Securitize is the security, title, shareholder-record, servicing and correction plane. Their BUIDL/VBILL-to-RLUSD interface joins two claims without merging corporate or record authority.
