Governing question
How are the largest banks and systemically important incumbent asset managers
rebuilding themselves for tokenized money, securities, custody, collateral and
settlement—and which parts are research, announced construction, pilot,
bounded operation, recurring production or demonstrated scale and recovery?
Controlling answer
The transition is not a single race toward “being on blockchain.” Incumbents
are choosing different constitutional positions:
bank-money orchestrators issue or mirror deposits and connect them to
existing account, payment and liquidity systems;
custody and record bridges preserve authoritative books while adding
wallets, token mirrors and multi-network servicing;
capital-markets manufacturers issue bonds, funds or receipts and automate
lifecycle events;
regulated public-chain issuers put fund shares or e-money claims directly
on public networks; and
research-and-access institutions prepare clients for digital assets
without yet showing a proprietary operating rail.
The strongest organizations are therefore not simply those with the most
products. They are those that can join an exact legal claim, authoritative
record, client route, funded cash leg, collateral treatment, correction power
and failure path. No reviewed institution publicly proves that whole chain at
recurring scale under stress.

Authority boundary
Institution-authored research establishes the institution's analysis and
strategy framing. A press release establishes the launch or transaction the
institution reports. Neither alone establishes every legal, customer, volume,
finality, accounting, failure or recovery claim.
The package keeps the bank parent, issuing bank, regulated subsidiary, asset
manager, custodian, network, CSD, transfer agent and settlement-money issuer
separate. It does not infer group control from a partnership or consortium,
does not treat a roadmap as operation, and does not convert gross notional into
economic value or native-token value capture.
