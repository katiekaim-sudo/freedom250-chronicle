2026-07-15 · FedNow Service · Report · Fedwire, FedNow & payment rails

FedNow's Third Birthday — 1,800 Banks, $271B a Quarter, and a Ceiling Raised Twentyfold

Fed360's anniversary item puts numbers on the public rail: 1,800+ live institutions, up from about 1,600 in January; seven of the ten largest U.S. banks aboard; Q1 2026 volume 2.7 million transactions worth $271.3 billion; reach claimed at over half of all American checking and savings accounts. The line worth keeping is the ceiling: launched at $500,000, lifted to $1 million in March 2025, lifted again to $10 million in November 2025. A retail instant-payments service does not need a ten-million-dollar transaction limit. That is a wholesale rail wearing a consumer coat.

2026-07-09 · FedNow Service · Announcement · Fedwire, FedNow & payment rails

FedNow at Three — the Fed Lays ISO 20022 Track Toward Cross-Border, and Sits Down With the Private Clearing House

Chief FedNow Executive Nick Stanescu's three-year Q&A names four things coming to the Fed's own instant rail: FedACH payee-name verification exposed to FedNow users by API; enhanced ISO 20022 message formats 'that will lay the groundwork for the FedNow Service to enable cross-border payments'; a request-for-payment pilot; and Federal Reserve Financial Services co-chairing a newly established X9 Fraud Forum jointly with The Clearing House. Read the last one twice. The Clearing House is the private rail owned by the biggest banks — RTP, the thing FedNow was built to compete with — and the Fed is now its co-chair on a shared standards body. The public rail and the incumbents' rail write the fraud rulebook together. Same absorption grammar as Cede & Co. surviving the DTC rewrite and Visa/Mastercard keeping their own book.

2026-02-10 · Treasury Department · Notice · The end of the paper check

Designation of the Social Security Administration's Numerical Identification System (Numident) Into Do Not Pay

Pursuant to the Payment Integrity Information Act of 2019 (PIIA) (31 U.S.C. 3351 et seq.), the U.S. Department of Treasury (Treasury) operates the Do Not Pay Working System--a centralized portal through which agencies can search multiple databases to verify payment or award eligibility. Treasury, under a delegation from the Director of the Office of Management and Budget (OMB), is authorized to designate new databases for inclusion in the Do Not Pay Working System. Treasury is issuing this Notice of Proposed Designation to provide the public an opportunity to comment on the proposed designation of the Social Security Administration's Numerical Identification System (Numident) to the Do Not Pay Working System.

2025-03-28 · Executive Office of the President · Presidential Document · The end of the paper check

Executive Order 14247 — Modernizing Payments To and From America's Bank Account

Directs the Treasury to cease issuing paper checks for all Federal disbursements and to move every payment into and out of the U.S. General Fund — “America's bank account” — onto electronic rails, to the extent permitted by law. Frames paper checks as a source of cost, delay, fraud, and theft. The founding order of the payment-rails rewiring.
