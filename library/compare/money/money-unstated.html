DTC Tokenization Service

Lets DTC participants (the brokers and banks that hold securities at DTC) turn selected securities into tokens on approved blockchains and back again. DTC's own system (LedgerScan) stays the official record of who holds the tokens, and Cede & Co. stays the registered holder on the issuer's books. Eligible: Russell 1000 stocks, ETFs tracking major indexes, and U.S. Treasury bills, notes and bonds. At first the tokens carry no collateral or settlement value inside DTC. Operator: The Depository Trust Company (DTC), a DTCC subsidiary; software from DTCC Digital (US)
