# Rebuilding the Federal Machine — October 2026 Payment Gate Pilot

## The story question

**Can a government screen money at scale and still name the person or office that may say no, repair a mistake and show what was actually saved or returned?** In this room, Libra's bargain is between shared detection and distributed legal responsibility. A bigger screening network is not a single new sovereign decision maker.

## Factual spine and clock

The [Treasury Do Not Pay structural owner](<Research-Workbench/Research Packages/Fraud and Payment Integrity/TREASURY_DO_NOT_PAY_GOVERNMENTWIDE_STRUCTURAL_MAP_2025-2026.md>), evidence through August 29, maps a shared matching system: source datasets, DNP queries or alerts, program verification, Treasury payment screening and later correction/recovery. A match is a signal. The paying agency or State program still applies its own eligibility law; Treasury may return a payment for review but does not adjudicate the benefit. Its reach across governments depends on legal purpose, agreements, datasets and actual implementation.

The [White House Fraud Task Force/CMS owner](<Research-Workbench/Research Packages/Fraud and Payment Integrity/WHITE_HOUSE_FRAUD_TASK_FORCE_UPDATE_2026-09-22.md>), evidence through September 22, records a distinct completed act: CMS says it cancelled about 315,000 unauthorized Marketplace enrollments covering more than 760,000 people after insurer and agency review. CMS expected about $2.2 billion in advance premium tax credits to return; that is **not** collected cash. Another roughly 419,000 people were pending verification. Officials described pattern recognition and AI/ML, but the reviewed record did not disclose model inputs, thresholds, error rates, appeal results or correction propagation, and did not establish a National Fraud Detection Center role in that CMS action.

The [October 10 federal money route map](<Research-Workbench/Research Packages/Fraud and Payment Integrity/FEDERAL_MONEY_FLOW_ROUTES_2026-10-10.md>) supplies the broader anatomy: authority, award, outlay, recipient receipt, lower-tier delivery and recovery can belong to different actors and records. It remains a structural map, not a new October payment or recovery receipt. The [Fraud package router](<Research-Workbench/Research Packages/Fraud and Payment Integrity/README.md>) keeps each study in its own lane.
