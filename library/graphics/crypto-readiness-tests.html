Freedom 250 · Digital money · Readiness

What must a tokenized system prove before it can be trusted with real money, and which systems pass each test?

No system is shown to pass yet. The library's 14-part readiness test, saved September 22, 2026, asks what a tokenized system must prove, from a legally defined claim to final money and the retirement of old systems, and rates 34 systems on fit only: in its words, "Functional fit is not legal qualification." The strongest candidates are DTC / DTCC tokenization (core on 7), Circle / USDC / CPN / Arc / Tazapay (core on 6) and Securitize (core on 6). No system is a core candidate for test 14, Economics and legacy retirement.

What's still uncertain

  • Functional fit is not legal qualification. Legal qualification is not operation. Operation is not recurring scale, failure proof or legacy retirement.
  • The ratings say which systems could do each job; whether a named company is authorized, has run the job with real money, at scale and through a failure, is the later steps, and the file does not record those.
  • Maturity grades (R1 to R4) are the source's own shorthand; it does not define them in this file.
