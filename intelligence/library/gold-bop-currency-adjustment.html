Research summary

Gold can materially change a country's goods and current accounts, external financing and reserve capacity, but it does not mechanically balance currencies. The exchange-rate effect depends on ownership, the financial counterpart, private conversion, central-bank absorption and intervention, reserve availability, valuation and the exact IMF statistical or program perimeter.
