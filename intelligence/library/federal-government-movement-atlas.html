The short version (draft, not yet reviewed by Katie)

One map to browse all saved federal research

This is a navigation hub, not a finding: it puts Katie's saved government research, a map of federal bodies, congressional committees, confirmed changes and known gaps in one place, while keeping each source's evidence separate.

What we found

  • The hub covers the Federal Group map of federal entities (642 nodes in the summary, 650 in the September 30 README), 401 governed links to research, 29 congressional bodies and eight confirmed before-and-after movements.
  • It shows seven separate views, including package cards, entity notes, budget and filing joins, committee records, typed relationships, confirmed movements and a gap board. Exact links and loose name matches are kept apart.
  • The September 25 refresh added administrative movements including OFAC penalty procedure, DOT DBE/ACDBE eligibility, the EXIM-Argentina framework, CMS's 37-state quality pledge and USPS NEPA procedure.
  • The September 22 Joint Warfighter Evaluation is logged as a Defense personnel-governance movement, but only as an announcement, not as carried out.
  • The gap board keeps six kinds of missing information separate, and a blank on the map is not treated as proof that nothing happened.

Why it matters

It is a tool for tracking the thesis rather than evidence for it: it lets agency-by-agency procedural changes be seen side by side without overstating them.
