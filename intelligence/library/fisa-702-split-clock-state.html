Fisa 702 Split Clock State

Two clocks. **Clock 1 — the statute.** Title VII of FISA, including section 702 (50 U.S.C. § 1881a), is repealed **effective June 12, 2026**, except as provided in section 404 of the FISA Amendments Act of 2008. The repeal sits in FAA § 403(b), last amended by **Public Law 119-87** (Apr. 30, 2026). House OLRC prelim, text in effect August 14, 2026, still prints that date. No later enacted reauthorization was retrieved at cutoff. **Clock 2 — the paper already issued.** FAA § 404(b)(1) (50 U.S.C. § 1801 note): any **order, authorization, or directive** issued under Title VII “shall continue in effect until the date of the expiration of such order, authorization, or directive.” Section 702 itself, while live, let the Attorney General and the DNI authorize targeting “for a period of **up to 1 year** from the effective date of the authorization.” **H.R. 9238** would have moved the repeal from June 12, 2026 to July 2, 2026. The House **rejected** it on June 11, 2026: **198–218** on a motion to suspend the rules. Tracker: Failed House. **FISC order not retrieved at cutoff.** The FISC public docket and ODNI/intel.gov releases do **not** contain a March 2026 (or nearby) section 702 certification opinion. No grandfather end date is written here. The charter to start **new** 702 work is gone as of June 12. Paper already signed, if any still has time on it, is a different object.
