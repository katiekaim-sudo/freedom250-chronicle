DTCC's tokens: a controlled layer, not a new market

DTC's first tokens move no cash and count for zero in its settlement checks; the old owner of record stays in place.

What we found

  • Limited production token workflows ran on July 15, 2026.
  • Cede & Co. stays the registered owner and DTC can mint, burn, move or correct tokens.
  • Collateral AppChain is an expected launch, and its claimed savings have no auditable model yet.
