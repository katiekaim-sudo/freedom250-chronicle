Research summary

Waves 1–5 organize the legitimacy-crisis lens across 15 contrastive function cases and 119 dated events and feed a generated seven-mode private thesis instrument. Dedicated Under the Hood and Fed Constellation rooms expose 19 technology objects, five System operating layers and the distinct contributions of all twelve Reserve Banks. The strongest recurring mechanism is that practical control can move faster than legal responsibility, authoritative records, supervision and remedy, especially as genuine technological capability advances and institutional functions migrate. Explicit public self-diagnosis is not required: the thesis is inferred from convergent observable authority conflicts, bypasses, reliance shifts, burdens, opacity, performance and correction. Successful incumbent absorption remains the strongest counter-reading, and no single function-level crisis state is treated as a proxy for the whole system.
