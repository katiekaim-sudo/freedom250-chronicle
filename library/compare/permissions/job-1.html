Federal securities or derivatives-market authority

Supervise and authorize

Legal basis: Federal securities laws and the Commodity Exchange Act, each within its statutory perimeter.

When it applies: A legally classified securities-market or derivatives-market activity calls for the applicable SEC or CFTC registration, designation, supervision, or rule route.

Named firms: U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission (public body); Commodity Futures Trading Commission (public body)

Who can use it: U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission; Commodity Futures Trading Commission; SEC or CFTC offices acting under delegated authority; Self-regulatory organizations only within their separate statutory rulemaking roles

ConditionsClassify the instrument, contract, and activity. Identify the exact entity and applicable SEC, CFTC, or SRO route. Recover current registration, designation, order, exemption, rulebook, and effective-date evidence.

The limitNeither tokenization nor a technical ledger decides securities/commodity classification or creates SEC/CFTC permission.

Federal bank chartering, supervision, and deposit-insurance route

Supervise and authorize

Legal basis: Applicable National Bank Act, Federal Reserve, and Federal Deposit Insurance Act authorities.

When it applies: A U.S. banking activity requires a charter, supervision, deposit-insurance analysis, or a related federal banking permission.

Named firms: Office of the Comptroller of the Currency (public body); Board of Governors of the Federal Reserve System (public body); Federal Deposit Insurance Corporation (public body)

Who can use it: Office of the Comptroller of the Currency; Board of Governors of the Federal Reserve System; Federal Deposit Insurance Corporation; Applicable state banking authority in its own statutory lane

ConditionsIdentify the exact legal entity and charter class. Identify the applicable primary federal and state supervisor and any insurer. Recover the current charter, approval, conditions, and product-specific authority.

The limitFederal supervision, a bank affiliation, preliminary charter approval, or deposit insurance does not establish a particular product authority, bank account, or affiliate permission.

Federal BSA/MSB registration plus bounded New York authorization route

Supervise and authorize

Legal basis: 31 U.S.C. section 5330 and the FinCEN MSB registration framework; New York's separate virtual-currency authorization framework.

When it applies: The proposed payment or value-transfer activity may fall within the federal MSB registration perimeter and/or covered New York virtual-currency activity.

Named firms: Financial Crimes Enforcement Network (public body); New York State Department of Financial Services (public body)

Who can use it: Financial Crimes Enforcement Network for federal MSB registration; New York State Department of Financial Services for its New York virtual-currency authorization framework

ConditionsClassify the activity and territorial nexus. Determine whether FinCEN MSB registration applies. For New York activity, identify the exact license/charter, approved scope, conditions, and current status.

The limitFederal MSB registration is not a nationwide operating license; NYDFS evidence is a bounded New York route and does not resolve other states' requirements or securities, commodities, banking, sanctions, or consumer-protection law.

Entity governing documents and formation-law route

Govern the organization or system

Legal basis: Applicable entity-formation law plus charter, bylaws/operating agreement, board or member action, and contract; Delaware corporate law is the bounded statutory example.

When it applies: The question is who may direct a legal entity, appoint officers, authorize contracts, or allocate internal fiduciary and voting powers.

Named firms: None named yet

Who can use it: Boards of directors of corporations; Managers of manager-managed LLCs; Members of member-managed LLCs; Other entity governing bodies only as provided by applicable formation law and governing documents

ConditionsIdentify the exact legal entity and formation jurisdiction. Recover current governing documents and the relevant authorization record. Separate entity governance from regulatory permission, network governance, and asset-holder rights.

The limitA corporate board, foundation, DAO vote, or product committee does not obtain public-law authority or control a separately issued asset absent the governing instrument and applicable law.

Sources (2)

  • Delaware LLC Act section 18-402: management · Statute ch

SRO rulebook governance subject to SEC process

Govern the organization or system

Legal basis: Exchange Act section 19 proposed-rule process plus the SRO's effective rulebook.

When it applies: A securities SRO's participant, membership, trading, clearing, or disciplinary rule is the claimed source of governance.

Named firms: The Depository Trust Company (registered or chartered); National Securities Clearing Corporation (registered or chartered); Fixed Income Clearing Corporation (registered or chartered)

Who can use it: National securities exchanges acting as SROs; Registered securities associations; Registered clearing agencies with applicable SRO rule obligations

ConditionsIdentify the SRO and exact rule version. Confirm the rule's Commission filing/approval or effectiveness status as applicable. Establish that the entity, participant, instrument, and event are within the rule's scope.

The limitAn SRO's private operating rulebook is not a general federal statute, and a corporate or protocol rulebook is not an SRO rulebook merely because market participants use it.

Title VIII designated-FMU public-authority and standards route

Supervise and authorize

Legal basis: Dodd-Frank Act Title VIII designation and supervisory framework.

When it applies: The operator is an FSOC-designated financial market utility and the relevant statutory supervisory-agency framework applies.

Named firms: Financial Stability Oversight Council (public body); Board of Governors of the Federal Reserve System (public body); U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission (public body); Commodity Futures Trading Commission (public body)

Who can use it: Financial Stability Oversight Council; Statutory supervisory agencies for designated financial market utilities; Board of Governors of the Federal Reserve System where the statute assigns the role; Securities and Exchange Commission where the statute assigns the role; Commodity Futures Trading Commission where the statute assigns the role

ConditionsRecover the specific FSOC designation and designated activity. Identify the statutory supervisory agency and applicable risk-management standards. Review the FMU's operative governance, recovery, and participant rules.

The limitPFMI terminology, technical settlement capability, or market importance does not itself produce an FSOC designation or Title VIII governance obligation.
