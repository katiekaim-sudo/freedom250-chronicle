Sun–Mars

The planets

Sun — Authority, illumination, "everything revolves around the Sun." MUNDANE: the head of government (the President); the executive branch; the focus of executive power. The Sun's condition shows how the government will fare. (Greer: "the Sun represents the actual head of government — whoever officially calls the shots in the executive branch.")

Mars — Action, aggression, the cutting edge. MUNDANE: the military; police and law enforcement; violent crime; industrial disputes; fires and accidents; surgeons. Mars energizes whatever it touches — constructively through decisive action, destructively through conflict and force. In a mundane chart, Mars shows where the nation's combative energy is directed.

The combination — mundane

When the Sun and Mars form helpful aspects (conjunction, sextile, trine), the result is a strengthened executive acting decisively and effectively. Greer describes "improvements in public health and the nation's position relative to foreign powers; strength and success to the government, especially fortunate in military affairs." The President looks commanding; military operations go well; the nation projects force credibly. Public health initiatives gain traction because Mars-as-surgeon works cooperatively with executive authority.

Under hostile aspects (square, opposition), the picture inverts sharply. Greer warns of "conflict and disagreement; heated public passions; partisan hatreds accentuated; violence against government officials possible." The executive picks fights or is targeted by them. Political rhetoric turns martial. Industrial disputes flare, crime rates spike, and the risk of actual violence — against officials or between political factions — rises. Military adventures undertaken during these aspects tend to go badly or provoke fierce domestic opposition.

As trigger: Sun-Mars aspects are fast-moving (days to a couple of weeks) and act as ignition points, lighting up the slower outer-planet themes wherever Mars's combative energy meets executive power.
