# Libra week as CMS cancels 315,000 enrollments

## The story (research side — facts only)

- On **September 22, 2026**, at a White House Fraud Task Force press conference, CMS announced it had cancelled approximately **315,000** Affordable Care Act Marketplace enrollments covering more than 760,000 people after CMS and insurers confirmed the enrollments were unauthorized. The agency fact sheet says the action is "expected to result in a return" of approximately **$2.2 billion** in advance premium tax credits. A further ~419,000 people were queued for additional verification. This is a completed cancellation and a forecast fiscal effect, not a collected-recovery receipt.
