## 2021

- **2021 — Anchorage Digital Bank, N.A. receives the first digital-asset national trust charter** (OCC · Occurred · Charters granted). The OCC charters the first national trust bank built for digital assets, under a 2021 operating agreement. Five years later it is still the only member of the cohort that has been a chartered, operating national bank the whole way through. A trust charter is not a commercial-bank charter: no insured deposits, no lending, no discount window, and no Federal Reserve master account.
