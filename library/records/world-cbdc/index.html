Japan: Possible retail CBDC

Japan money map · as of September 15, 2026

pilot; not authorized

What it would be
Tests a two-tier design in which the public instrument would be a direct liability of the BOJ distributed through intermediaries.
Where it stands
Would change the holder's debtor from a bank to the BOJ if authorized and issued; no public-value issuance exists at cutoff.
Limit
Technical readiness is not legislation, issuance, adoption or a settled failure constitution.
