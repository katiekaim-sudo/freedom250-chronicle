# Chart Reading Amendments — Ledger

Readings are sealed when they are written: their Studio Desk watches and comparison baselines are locked to the exact text. So a factual correction never goes *into* a reading. It goes here, dated and tied to the ruling that caused it, and Chart Readings shows it under the reading it corrects (DR-085). The reading above each amendment is the untouched original.

Only facts are amended here. A correction to how a chart was *heard* is a new reading or a comparison, never an amendment.
