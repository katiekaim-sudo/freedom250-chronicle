Government · Who counts as government

Which federal bodies are on the government's own books, and which sit outside?

Of the 352 bodies on the federal tree (committees counted separately), 271 are counted in the government's own audited statements. 26 are disclosed but kept off the books (Fannie Mae, Freddie Mac, Amtrak, The Federal Reserve System), 13 are related parties the U.S. influences but does not control (Federal Home Loan Banks, IMF, IBRD), and 33 sit outside (Farm Credit System, Brand USA, In-Q-Tel); 9 have no placement yet. The test is the federal accounting standard SFFAS 47. The 25 Reserve Bank branch offices are listed too and counted with their Reserve Bank; Buffalo is closed but stays listed.

What's still uncertain

  • The placements are the federal tree's reading of SFFAS 47 and the Financial Report of the U.S. Government. Most date from the July 2026 whole-federal sweep; a card shows a check date only where the status was rechecked since.
  • Being on or off the books is an accounting answer, not a control answer. Who appoints a body, who funds it and how much, and which steps of the work it holds are separate questions the federal tree does not record yet.
  • Federal advisory committees and councils, and most federally funded research centers, are not on the federal tree yet.
