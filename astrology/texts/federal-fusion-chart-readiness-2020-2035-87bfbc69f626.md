## Preserved material

## Governing clock and chart rules

1. Preserve the object's real clock role. Experiment, announcement, filing,
   publication, signature, enactment, effectiveness and deadline are separate
   events even when they concern the same instrument.
2. Preserve only the precision stated or deterministically recoverable from a
   primary object. Never manufacture midnight, noon, a timezone or a place as
   historical fact.
3. `Source place or locus` is factual metadata. An institutional headquarters,
   press-release dateline, project site and online publication origin are not
   interchangeable.
4. Under the live Freedom 250 national-mundane convention, a later wheel uses
   **Washington, D.C. (38.9072 N, 77.0369 W), Whole Sign**. An exact sourced
   instant is preserved and displayed in Washington local time. A known
   daytime event without a clock uses noon D.C.; a date-only record uses
   midnight D.C. Month/year/window records are not point-chartable.
5. A D.C. proxy is a declared chart convention, not an assertion that the
   event occurred there. Houses and angles from noon/midnight proxies are
   convention-derived and must not be presented as source-timed geometry.
6. No chart is implied by the mere presence of a calendar date. The overlay
   below states readiness explicitly.
