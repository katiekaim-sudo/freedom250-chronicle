Aries against Libra is initiative against balance. Here the initiative belongs to the public mood in the government house, and the balancing act falls to an executive with less leverage than usual.

The root is simple: Mars in his own sign at 10°05′ Scorpio in the 5th, the chart's sole final dispositor. The constructive pole is a public mood that legitimately takes the initiative when the executive is weakened. The shadow is a government house full of feeling and short of direction, with the real strength held by a dignified force in the arena.
