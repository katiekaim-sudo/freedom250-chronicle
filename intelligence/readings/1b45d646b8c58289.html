Project Finance Rail Feed-Through — 2025–2026
The four braided rails
A project does not travel down one financial rail. Four rails move together:
cash rail — commitment, draw, project account, vendor payment, revenue
receipt and debt service;
risk rail — guarantee, insurance, completion support, offtaker risk,
reserve and default waterfall;
title/collateral rail — equity pledge, liens, equipment/commodity title,
warehouse/custody, eligible collateral and releases; and
information/control rail — diligence, milestones, engineer certificates,
draw requests, account controls, covenants and reporting.
The project becomes financeable when these rails agree on who owes, what can be
drawn, what secures it, who controls the accounts and who bears failure.
Full project chain
G7/G20 priority or framework
-> national/MDB/DFI program authority
-> facility, appropriation or guarantee envelope
-> project selection and diligence
-> definitive loan/equity/offtake/guarantee
-> borrower or SPV and controlled accounts
-> conditions precedent and certified draw request
-> lender/treasury advance
-> project-account and vendor/EPC payments
-> collateral/title and construction monitoring
-> commissioning and operating revenue
-> waterfall, reserves and debt service
-> cure/default/enforcement/guarantee/loss
Each arrow needs its own receipt. No G7/G20 declaration reviewed here is the
legal authority for the project loan or the operator of the project account.

Worked case 1 — Thacker Pass
Lithium Nevada LLC is the DOE borrower. DOE advances and project cash are held
in restricted accounts administered by Citibank as collateral agent/depositary.
DOE advanced $435 million on October 20, 2025 and $432 million on February
24, 2026. The March 31, 2026 filing reports $1.2772 billion of cumulative
capitalized project cost. Advances are typically quarterly and project
withdrawals monthly; repayment begins in January 2029.
This proves:
named borrower;
operative federal loan;
two advances;
restricted account/collateral agent;
project-cost accounting; and
future repayment clock.
It does not publish the advance-to-vendor ledger, payment messages, settlement
system, exact cash-finality time or individual collateral releases. The project
has crossed from commitment to draw and controlled deployment; the lowest cash
rail remains only partially public. Source: Lithium Americas Q1 2026 Form
10-Q.
Worked case 2 — MP Materials
The July 2025 transaction constitution joins preferred equity, a warrant,
Samarium loan, price protection, offtake and a future 10X financing facility.
MP later reported $550 million cash consideration received: $400 million of
preferred equity and a $150 million Samarium loan. The price-protection agreement
began October 1, 2025; MP recognized $51.016 million of Q4 price-support
income and described a cash right against the government.
This is the strongest permanent-magnet example because it shows how different
rail objects work together:
Object
Function
preferred equity
immediate capital and governance/economic rights
loan
cash claim with interest, maturity and acceleration terms
offtake
prospective demand/revenue channel
price protection
contractual support to realized/projected revenue
future 10X financing
construction-funding path, still on its own clock
Accrued price-support income must not be treated as proof that every amount was
cash-settled. The public filings do not expose the bank route or settlement time.
Sources: transaction agreement,
Q3 2025 filing,
and 2025 results.
Worked case 3 — CoreWeave DDTL 4.0
CCAC VIII is the GPU-project borrower. The facility has an $8.5 billion
commitment and $1.260 billion drawn/outstanding at March 31, 2026, with MUFG
as administrative agent. It grants first-priority security over borrower equity
and substantially all borrower assets, imposes restricted-cash requirements and
routes assigned customer payments through controlled accounts.
The operating waterfall is especially useful as a comparator:
customer receipts
-> permitted operating expenses
-> interest and fees
-> principal
-> required reserves
-> cash trap / permitted distribution
The documents prove the cash-control constitution but not the exact wire messages
or settlement system. Sources: Q1 2026 Form 10-Q
and credit agreement.
Worked case 4 — BlueOval / Ford successor
DOE/Federal Financing Bank made $7.83554 billion of advances to BlueOval SK.
On May 20, 2026, Ford Energy Battery acquired the Kentucky plants subject to DOE
liens and assumed the $3.80504 billion note for the advanced Kentucky plant.
Ford's former 50 percent guarantee and contribution obligation ended.
This case proves that a draw does not freeze the project constitution. Borrower,
asset ownership, lien coverage, guarantees and future loss allocation can change
after cash has moved. A project tracker must therefore version:
obligor;
guarantor/support provider;
collateral package;
amount advanced/outstanding;
payment schedule; and
amendment/assumption effective time.
Source: Ford May 20, 2026 Form 8-K.
Worked case 5 — Rio Grande LNG Train 5
Rio Grande LNG Train 5 LLC is the project borrower. Public documents describe a
$3.59 billion senior-secured loan, $500 million of notes and $2.58 billion of
committed equity. Advances depend on independent-engineer certificates, EPC
payment requests, lien waivers and construction reports; operating cash travels
through controlled debt-service, reserve and distribution accounts.
This case has a detailed rulebook but an incomplete public cash history. It
proves transaction constitution, not the aggregate construction-loan draw.
Source: filed credit agreement.
Approval-stage cases
Project Vault
EXIM's February 2026 release proves board approval of a direct loan up to
$10 billion for an independently governed public-private partnership. It
does not identify a legal borrower/SPV, executed loan, first draw, inventory
bought, title, warehouse/custodian, OEM contract, repayment source or default
waterfall. It remains approval-stage. Source: EXIM.
USA Rare Earth
Commerce signed a final award up to $277 million and a loan agreement up to
$1.3 billion on June 3, 2026. That proves a definitive instrument and company
counterparty. First payment/draw, borrowing subsidiaries, milestones, collateral,
repayment and loss terms were not public in the reviewed release. Source:
NIST/Commerce.
How the G7 critical-minerals architecture feeds through
The G7 2025 Action Plan, standards roadmap and 2026 Alliance can affect projects
through five channels:
selection: common priority lists and dependency targets influence which
projects members examine;
market structure: traceability, qualification and standards affect which
output can enter allied supply chains;
revenue: offtake aggregation and reference prices can change forecast
demand and modeled cash flow;
risk: public loans, guarantees, insurance, equity and MDB de-risking can
change the lender's loss and capital calculation; and
coordination: co-finance, permitting and shared diagnostics can reduce
transaction friction.
None of those channels is itself the project draw. Canada's C$6.4 billion
portfolio mixes offtakes, investments, LOIs, MOUs and conditional approvals.
It must be decomposed object by object. The 2026 Alliance is non-binding and has
no common treasury or operating settlement account.
MDB feed-through
The April 2026 six-MDB statement commits the institutions, under their separate
mandates, to diagnostics, project preparation, co-finance, infrastructure and
de-risking. The World Bank/Japan $20 million RISE+ trust-fund facility is a
real facility. Neither proves a named project loan or draw.
The proper chain is:
MDB collaboration framework
-> country compact / project preparation
-> board or delegated approval
-> loan / equity / guarantee agreement
-> conditions satisfied
-> first disbursement
-> project spend and physical delivery
Sources: joint MDB statement
and World Bank/Japan RISE+.
Guarantee feed-through
A project-payment guarantee can backstop an SOE/offtaker obligation or a bank
letter of credit. It changes the timing and bearer of failure:
SOE/offtaker payment due
-> delayed or defaulted payment
-> letter-of-credit liquidity if structured
-> guarantor payment after contractual conditions
-> sovereign indemnity / recourse where applicable
It does not necessarily transfer cash at financial close and does not prevent
the underlying default. It makes a defined failure more financeable by moving
the loss to a stronger balance sheet, subject to conditions. See World Bank
Group project-based payment guarantees.
Mission 300
Mission 300 is a World Bank/African Development Bank operating platform later
welcomed by the G20, not a G20 financing vehicle. Aggregate connections,
commitments and co-finance are material program receipts. To connect those
figures to the rail, project-level files must identify:
country and implementing entity;
loan, grant, equity or guarantee instrument;
borrower/beneficiary and account;
disbursement and procurement;
grid/connection completion and operating service;
tariff/revenue/repayment path; and
default, subsidy or loss allocation.
Source: Mission 300 implementation update.
Digital-rail and continuous-underwriting boundary
No reviewed flagship financing document named stablecoin funding, tokenized
debt, tokenized collateral or DLT settlement. Swift's shared ledger remains a
separate interbank-orchestration project with external final settlement.
Production merchant-credit platforms do show payment-stream underwriting and
automatic repayment. The infrastructure-project equivalent would need to prove:
verified physical telemetry or contracted receivable
-> rule-based borrowing-base or covenant update
-> controlled advance or repayment
-> settled cash and auditable journal
-> correction/default path
No reviewed mineral, battery, LNG, grid or data-center project exposed that full
live chain. Treat it as a falsifiable hypothesis, not an operating trend.
Project research gates
For every project, return in this order:
definitive instrument and legal parties;
borrower/SPV and controlled accounts;
conditions precedent and first draw;
project/vendor expenditure;
collateral/title/custody;
construction and commissioning;
qualified output or operating service;
revenue/offtake cash receipt;
interest/principal or distribution waterfall; and
amendment, default, guarantee, enforcement or realized loss.
Public absence at layer 4 does not invalidate a confirmed layer 3. It simply
prevents the stronger claim that the advanced money reached the project object.
