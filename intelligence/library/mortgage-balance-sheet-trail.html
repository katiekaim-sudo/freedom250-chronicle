Research summary

The accounting walkthrough follows a mortgage through balanced debits and credits, trust consolidation, MBS issuance, repo, Fed reserves and deposits, and principal repayment. Cash can return to Fannie while the mortgage remains recognized and external trust debt supplies its funding. Legal entities, consolidated groups and selected economic aggregates have distinct elimination boundaries.
