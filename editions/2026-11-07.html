EU names the countries that can relocate asylum seekers

BREAKING:

The European Commission announced today which countries are under “migratory pressure” and will be able to send away some of their asylum seekers to other EU states under the “migrant relocation quotas” created by the EU’s new Migration Pact. The countries are:

🇪🇸 Spain 🇮🇹 Italy 🇬🇷 Greece 🇨🇾 Cyprus

These 4 countries will be able to put asylum seekers into a “Solidarity Pool” for 2027 from which migrants will then be relocated in other EU states.

The size of the Solidarity Pool was decided today but the information about it is being kept secret for now.

6 countries were classified as facing a “significant migratory situation” and will be allowed to request from exemptions of receiving quota migrants. Those 6 countries are:

🇵🇱 Poland 🇨🇿 Czechia 🇭🇷 Croatia 🇦🇹 Austria 🇧🇬 Bulgaria 🇪🇪 Estonia

To receive an exemption, the country’s request has to pass a QMV vote (Qualified Majority Voting) in the Council of the European Union.

Countries which are forced to take part in the Solidarity Pool will either have to take in the number of quota migrants assigned to them by the EU, pay EUR 21 000 for every quota migrant they refuse to receive or send significant resources to help the 4 countries under migratory pressure to handle the situation.

Two countries have refused to take part in the entire new migrant relocation system and its fines. Those two countries are:

🇭🇺 Hungary 🇸🇰 Slovakia
