How much Gulf oil still gets out, and through which routes, compared with before the war?

About 13 million barrels a day of Gulf oil got out in August 2026 (IEA estimate, through Hormuz and the bypass pipelines), against 20.9 million through the Strait of Hormuz in the first half of 2025 before the war; the bypass pipelines carry part of it, but no current throughput is published for them.
