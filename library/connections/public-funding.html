Syracuse housing uses grants, tax credits and nonprofit developers

Current saved study

Impoundment And Rescission Legal Map

Current saved study

The purse has two doors. The fraud machine asks whether a payment should leave. This map asks whether appropriated money may be **unspent**. The Impoundment Control Act of 1974 lets the President *propose* a rescission by special message and withhold the money for 45 calendar days of continuous session. The money "shall be made available for obligation unless, within the prescribed 45-day period, the Congress has completed action on a rescission bill." Only Congress can cancel the appropriation. That is 2 U.S.C. § 683(b). Public Law 119-28, signed 2025-07-24, is that bill for the June 3 2025 special message. It is an enacted rescission. The August 28 2025 special message is a later § 683 proposal — 15 items, $4.9 billion as the Federal Register notice itself states. It is not P.L. 119-28. Calling it a "pocket rescission" is the sender's theory. GAO B-330330 (2018) holds the ICA does not permit withholding through the date the money expires. EO 14215 § 5 is the quiet twin: OMB may adjust independent-agency apportionments, including a bar on spending particular activities, "so long as such restrictions are consistent with law." Apportionment is not a rescission.
