Research summary

Official record is not one universal legal status. Authority, the exact recorded object, the accountable keeper and the consequence determine officialness for each purpose. The September 24 CFTC staff FAQ permits covered entities to keep qualifying regulatory records natively on-chain without a separate off-chain duplicate solely because blockchain is used, but the responsible entity must still satisfy content, retention, production, outage and inspection duties. That permission does not make blockchain consensus the controlling ownership record, a GAAP conclusion, sufficient audit evidence or settlement finality.
