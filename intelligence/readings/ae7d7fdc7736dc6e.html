Case-specific firewalls
DTC real production trades ≠ DTC digital cash settlement. Launch-phase
transactions are not settled in digitized form; tokenized entitlements begin
with no DTC collateral or settlement value.
SEC staff no-action relief ≠ Commission approval. It is time-limited
enforcement posture under stated conditions.
Clearstream D7 totals ≠ D7 DLT totals. Broader digital issuance includes
non-DLT dematerialized securities.
SDX total issuance ≠ total wCBDC settlement. The wholesale-CBDC subset is
a continuing pilot with its own volumes and limits.
Euroclear D-FMI production ≠ Canton collateral-network production. One is
a digital issuance operation; the other was a pilot/design phase.
Swift ready for use ≠ completed live settled payment. Banks were preparing
pilots and retain final settlement authority outside the ledger.
Kinexys aggregate volume ≠ JPM Coin public-chain volume. Product, currency,
chain and legal-claim states must be sourced separately.
DSS Gate 2 ≠ a live transaction. It permits bounded activity but does not
prove an issuance or settlement occurred.
Pontes Q3 launch target ≠ live at cutoff. User testing and a scheduled date
are not an operative receipt.
Agorá's 80-second average ≠ production efficiency. Scripted windows,
manual approvals, external account funding and a small controlled sample limit
the performance inference.
mBridge MVP ≠ production global rail. BIS expressly exited and rejected
the mature-operating characterization.
Project finance adjacency ≠ project finance adoption. A DLT collateral or
deposit system does not become a project's funding rail without the named
instrument, borrower, draw, settlement and accounting chain.
Counterexamples to the “adapter-only” thesis
The transition cannot be summarized as “the chain is only a front end.”
SDX is a regulated DLT CSD with operative securities state.
D7 DLT supports DLT-native issuance inside a regulated CSD framework.
Helvetia places SNB wholesale central-bank money on the SDX environment for
same-ledger DvP in a limited pilot.
The Eurosystem intends Pontes to extend from T2 synchronization toward a
public DLT settlement platform with native finality and programmability.
Kinexys performs recurring bank-ledger transfers and programmable deposit-
money workflows, rather than merely displaying a legacy payment.
These cases prove genuine state migration. They do not prove universal
disintermediation or complete migration of title, liquidity, accounting and
failure authority.
Counterexamples to the “one ledger is inevitable” thesis
DTC is explicitly multi-chain and retains an institutional reconciliation
layer.
Swift's ledger is an orchestration layer above bank environments and multiple
settlement options.
LSEG designs for multiple chains and traditional infrastructure.
Appia keeps single-ledger and interoperable multi-ledger options open.
Central banks warn that a single ledger creates governance, concentration,
confidentiality, resilience and technology-lock-in problems.
Different objects require different issuers and legal regimes even when they
share technical rails.
What would strengthen the core synthesis
multiple major CSDs and banks use compatible rulebooks while retaining local
legal authority;
central-bank DLT money becomes routinely available to settle assets on or
across several ledgers;
collateral can move between venues without duplicated buffers or
detokenization;
shared events feed audited accounting/capital reports with governed
correction;
handled incidents show operators, courts and resolution authorities aligning
the technical and legal state; and
recurring volumes and measured user benefits grow while legacy infrastructure
remains as a governed bridge or fallback.

What would disprove a specific operating claim
Promote or demote per object, not by sector narrative.
A first transaction can be disproved by an operator correction, test-only
classification or absence of the claimed settlement leg.
“24/7” can be narrowed by conversion windows, maintenance, RTGS closure or
participant funding limits.
“Central-bank-money settlement” can be narrowed if the actual holder owns only
an operator claim backed by an omnibus account.
“Collateral use” can be narrowed if the transaction was simulated, pledged but
not financed, or required conversion to a conventional CSD position.
“Finality” can be narrowed if the technical state remains probabilistic or the
law/rulebook permits a conflicting insolvency outcome.
First-failure falsifier suite
The architecture is not proved until at least one case publicly answers:
Which record prevailed after a mismatch?
Who had power to pause, freeze, reverse or compensate?
Did one leg become final before the other failed?
What liquidity funded the gap?
How were the books corrected?
Did a participant/client bear loss, and under which rule?
Could the position be redeemed, ported or detokenized?
Did the system resume without double-spend, duplicate payment or orphaned
collateral?
Net rule
Treat every DLT claim as a vector. A strong receipt in one column does not fill
the others. The research standard is not skepticism for its own sake; it is
making the exact transition visible without erasing what really changed.
