Which payment rails are being rebuilt around Swift, or alongside it?

The world record holds 35 dated payment-plumbing moves in 10 regions, January 6, 2025 to July 7, 2026: China's CIPS signing foreign banks, BRICS Pay targeted for a pilot, Africa's PAPSS, Indonesia's QRIS reaching Japan, Korea and China, Project Nexus, and the ECB's Pontes and Appia links. Swift's own shared ledger has four readings: announced September 29, 2025; 'controlled initial use preparing' on the Japan map (September 15, 2026); 'MVP ready; pilots pending', grade M4, on the UK map (September 14, 2026); and, newest, 17 listed banks and 6 bank-published bilateral pilot cases (October 19, 2026).

Evidence and source scope
