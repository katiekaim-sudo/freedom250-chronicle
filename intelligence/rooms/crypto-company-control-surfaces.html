Crypto Company Control Surfaces — Liability × Ledger × Distribution

Twenty-two company families and protocol constitutions showing what they own, where they sit, and what they can stop

The stablecoin contest is not one coin against another. It is a race to control adjacent gates: legal liability, reserves, ledgers, keys, risk, conversion and customer distribution.

LiabilityWho owes redemption, owns reserves and administers the token?
LedgerWho orders transactions, upgrades software and recovers from failure?
DistributionWho controls the wallet, customer, merchant and conversion relationship?
RiskWho chooses collateral, oracles, curators, venues and emergency powers?
EconomicsWho receives reserve income, interest, fees, spreads and token rewards?
SurvivalWhich claim and control surface survives if the visible brand disappears?

CircleIssuer stackPublic filingUSDC live · U.S. entity migration open · IBM IP acquired

Circle is building outward from the regulated dollar

What does it own or control?USDC/EURC platform, CCTP, Gateway, CPN, wallet tooling, Arc development, USYC administration and a newly acquired IBM blockchain patent estate.
Where does it sit?Issuer liability, cross-chain transport, institutional settlement and purpose-built chain
What can it stop?Mint/redeem access, supported chains, token addresses and compliance actions; separate processors and chains keep their own gates.
open the company constitution
Exact entities

Circle Internet Group, Inc. is the public parent; Circle Internet Financial, LLC is the current non-EEA issuer; Circle Internet Financial Europe SAS is the EEA issuer. Proposed Circle Internet Trust Company is the intended New York issuer. Circle National Trust has final OCC approval as a separate non-issuing custody/trust box. Circle Technology Services operates CPN technology without becoming transaction principal.

Money objects

USDC and EURC payment coins; USYC fund-share token; Arc network asset and infrastructure are distinct objects.

Economic engine

Reserve income dominates: Circle reported Q1 2026 reserve income of $652.5 million versus $41.6 million of other revenue, with $405.4 million of distribution/transaction costs including major distributor payments. Platform, network, fund and future Arc economics are secondary engines.

Control surface

Issuer contracts, token-admin functions, Mint onboarding, CCTP/Gateway support, CPN participation and Arc roadmap.

Outside dependencies

BlackRock-managed Circle Reserve Fund, BNY custody, banking partners, supported chains, distributors including Coinbase/Binance and processors.

Stablecoin / DeFi role

USDC is the dominant regulated base asset beneath exchanges, DeFi collateral, vaults and merchant adapters.

Merchant path

USDC can be accepted directly through processors or used invisibly as the settlement/payout asset behind familiar checkout.

GENIUS read

High passport potential, but exact U.S. issuer handoff and product-specific approval remain separate legal clocks.

Live clock

Circle National Trust received final OCC approval 2026-07-10 but is not the issuer. IBM portfolio acquired 2026-07-27; exact patent schedule not publicly located. The proposed New York issuer and USDC novation remain unproved.

Open questions

Which IBM patents cover bank integration, permissioned settlement or enterprise controls? When do NY issuer terms and Arc/CPN production align?

Failure mode

Reserve-bank/custodian event, issuer-access outage, token-admin error, bridge/orchestration failure or legal handoff mismatch.

Do not overclaim

Circle did not acquire COBOL, IBM mainframes or bank customer systems merely by buying the blockchain patent portfolio.

Exact entities

Control surface

Outside dependencies

Circle for USDC liability, banks and card networks for fiat edges, Ethereum for Base settlement, external processors and merchants.

Stablecoin / DeFi role

Major USDC inventory and routing center; links stablecoins to trading, Base DeFi, institutional custody and agentic payments.

Merchant path

GENIUS read

Likely a critical digital-asset service provider/distributor even when it is not the issuer.

Live clock

Merchant acceptance and enterprise payouts announced live for eligible users; headline volume and Base metrics remain company reported.

Open questions

Exact allocation of compliance, conversion, custody and refund liability in each processor integration; status of proposed trust entity.

Failure mode

Platform outage can affect transfers, card transactions and onchain services simultaneously; Base sequencer or custody failures create correlated risk.

Do not overclaim

Base stablecoin volume is not Coinbase revenue, USDC circulation or merchant-payment volume. Open fault proofs do not erase Coinbase's sequencer and interface control.

Ethereum ecosystemNetwork ecosystemProtocol docslive · multi-client · socially governed

Ethereum hosts monetary systems without becoming their issuer

What does it own or control?No single body owns Ethereum. The Foundation, client teams, validators, node operators, standards authors, infrastructure firms and L2 operators hold different roles.
Where does it sit?Execution, settlement, smart-contract state and composability
What can it stop?Validators can censor or fail to include transactions; client/social coordination can choose software and recovery paths; application admins retain their own keys.
open the company constitution
Exact entities

Ethereum Foundation is a Swiss Stiftung and influential funder/convener, not the protocol, validator set or issuer. Consensys Software Inc. operates products such as MetaMask/Infura and contributes client software but does not own Ethereum.

Money objects

ETH pays gas and secures consensus. USDC, PYUSD, USDe, GHO and tokenized securities are liabilities or claims of other systems.

Economic engine

ETH fees/burn, validator rewards/MEV, L2 economics, RPC/wallet services and application fees.

Control surface

EIP discussion, AllCoreDevs coordination, independent client implementation, node/validator adoption, exceptional social recovery and application/L2 upgrade keys.

Outside dependencies

Client diversity, validator concentration, RPC access, bridges/L2s, oracles, application admins and stablecoin issuers.

Stablecoin / DeFi role

Primary composability substrate for issuer coins, lending, DEXs, synthetic dollars, vaults and tokenized assets.

Merchant path

Usually through L2s, wallets, processors or card adapters rather than direct L1 retail use.

GENIUS read

Network status does not decide payment-stablecoin classification; exact issuer and redemption promise do.

Live clock

Protocol upgrades require client implementation and validator/node adoption; no corporate switch controls every layer.

Open questions

Practical concentration across clients, staking providers, RPCs, L2 sequencers and stablecoin admins.

Failure mode

Client bug, validator concentration, finality failure, social split, L2/bridge failure or application-admin compromise.

Do not overclaim

Ethereum Foundation does not own Ethereum, Consensys does not control it, validators do not formally vote on every EIP, and stablecoin value does not become EF revenue or liability.

Exact entities

Hedera Hashgraph, LLC dba Hedera Council is the Delaware network-governance entity. Hedera Foundation and Hashgraph are independent ecosystem/software bodies. Hiero is the open-source project. All remain separate from token issuers.

Money objects

HBAR pays public-network fees and secures consensus. Stablecoins, tokenized collateral and state tokens are third-party claims.

Economic engine

HBAR-denominated network fees and ecosystem activity; issuer and application economics remain separate.

Control surface

Council decisions govern treasury, pricing, membership, releases and mainnet-node policy; only Council members currently operate mainnet consensus nodes. Issuer-configured HTS admin/supply/KYC/freeze/wipe/fee/pause keys govern each token.

Outside dependencies

Council continuity, nodes, external issuers, custodians, reserve data providers and oracles such as Chainlink.

Stablecoin / DeFi role

Merchant path

Issuer/payment-provider applications can use Hedera while merchant acceptance remains with wallets, processors and acquirers.

GENIUS read

Studio may help an issuer satisfy controls, but it cannot supply the legal issuer, reserve right or approval.

Live clock

Wyoming FRNT and commercial implementations are evidence of deployment; institutional pilots still require production verification.

Open questions

Which workloads recur at scale and how public Hedera relates to private HashSphere environments.

Failure mode

Council/node concentration, key compromise, issuer-admin action, oracle/custody fault or private/public synchronization failure.

Do not overclaim

Open-source Hiero code does not make mainnet admission permissionless, and Council governance does not make Hedera the issuer of every token created with its tools.
