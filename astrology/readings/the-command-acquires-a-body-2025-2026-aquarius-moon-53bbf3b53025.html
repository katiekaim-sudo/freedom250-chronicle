The astronomy adds a necessary correction

At the Full Moon, Sun and Jupiter are only 0.479° apart in the real sky. Moon and Pluto are separated by 3.157°. The Sun–Pluto separation is 175.159° and Moon–Jupiter 178.394°. The physical picture is therefore close to:

Sun–Jupiter — Earth — Moon–Pluto

but it is not perfect three-dimensional collinearity. Ecliptic longitude makes the Full Moon and the Sun–Jupiter/Moon–Pluto opposition exact or near-exact; orbital latitude preserves a wider true-sky separation.

Local altitude also refuses a simplistic “12th house means literally hidden” equation. At the exact Washington clock, eight traditional bodies—including the Sun and Jupiter—are above the true horizon. Only the Moon and Pluto are below it. The institutional authority is symbolically 12th-house yet physically above the local horizon; the 6th-house working body and concentrated power are the bodies literally below it.

That correction improves the reading. The hiddenness belongs to protected decision-making, motive and access—not optical invisibility. Conversely, the working body can be the public subject of the Full Moon while remaining below Washington's horizon at the exact daytime clock. House, horizon, visibility and factual disclosure remain different layers.
