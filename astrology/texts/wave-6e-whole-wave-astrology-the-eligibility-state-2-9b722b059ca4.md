## The whole institutional finding

### The charter map changes less than the permission map

The strongest thesis is not that the federal state was abolished and replaced in one quarter. FEMA, contracting agencies, universities, Treasury, the Federal Reserve System, FDA, DEA, CDC, CBP, land agencies and the courts retain distinct legal identities and statutory holders.

The material change is the permission map:

`existing institution → qualifying chair / clause / record / category → upstream reviewer → permission to proceed → later transaction or contest`

This explains why the wave can feel revolutionary even when organic statutes survive. A system can change radically if a new control plane decides:

- which career chair retains ordinary removal procedure;
- which contract clause controls responsibility and payment materiality;
- which official approves a contract form;
- which record supports citizenship, customer risk or importer status;
- which private provider may appear at a public portal;
- which institution may ask for a narrow settlement account;
- which evidence reaches an approval or vaccine recommendation;
- which foreign person becomes blocked;
- which executive land criterion still exists; and
- which model may enter a protected government security relationship.

The legal persons endure; their membrane becomes more selective.

## Thesis against the institutional-crumbling story

The evidence supports a more exact version of “crumbling”:

1. **Old vertical authority is not simply gone.** Statutes, appropriations, courts, Reserve Banks, state governments and private bodies remain active holders.
2. **Inherited boundaries are being loosened or bypassed.** Chapter 75 procedure, old land criteria, ordinary contract discretion and dispersed eligibility practice are narrowed or rerouted.
3. **A horizontal membrane is taking their place.** Shared techniques—classification, clause, list, screening, designation, approval and voluntary access agreement—sit across many old entities.
4. **The new regime is real but unevenly mature.** Some objects reached classification, award, designation and operation; many stopped at proposal, review or future deadline.
5. **Contest is constitutive, not accidental.** Correction, license, court, application, advisory and statutory boundaries decide whether the membrane becomes durable architecture or a temporary executive overlay.

So the institutional structure is not best pictured as rubble. It is being **hollowed, cross-braced and re-skinned**: old legal persons carry a new system for deciding who may enter and under what record.
