Research summary

Carbon nanotubes are a low-dose conductive and mechanical electrode network, not a new battery chemistry. Their strongest sovereignty value is enabling higher-silicon anodes, lower-inactive-material cathodes and thick or dry electrodes, but the United States does not yet have a publicly proven mass-scale chain from domestic battery-grade CNT synthesis through named serial cell production.
