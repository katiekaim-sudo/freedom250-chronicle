# Wave 4G — Whole-Wave Astrology

**Research window:** Exact 2025 Libra ingress through exact 2025 Capricorn ingress.  
**Evidence cutoff:** June 20, 2026, 11:59 p.m. EDT. Later facts remain in their actual operating waves.  
**Question:** What single institutional story emerges when the twenty Libra-period Executive Orders are joined to their later bodies, reversals and limits—and what do the annual governor, nested ingress, lunar chapters, true Moon families and outer-planet return chamber naturally endorse without becoming causal proof?  
**Finding:** Wave 4 is **the counterparty and enforceability state**. The summer's new interfaces now have to decide whom they recognize, under what terms and through which remedy. A corporation becomes a qualified U.S. joint venture; a foreign partner receives an executive security assurance; trade bargains become product and refund books; scientific ambitions acquire departments, dockets and application channels; workers, foster children and advisers enter new classification interfaces; terrorist branches become prohibited counterparties; and private market intermediaries become objects of fiduciary and antitrust law. The strongest descendants are not one uniform “new regime.” They are agreements, legal persons, lists, task forces, guidance, court filings, forms and agency procedures of sharply different maturity. The broad IEEPA tariff carrier later fails, proving the quarter's core question: **a relationship becomes real only when its legal container survives the enforcement test**.

The wave's factual grammar is:

`claim or relationship → named counterparty → terms → carrier → classification or decision right → operation → contest → surviving relationship`

## Maturity as of June 20, 2026

### Legal-person and relationship clock

TikTok is the cleanest completed relationship chain. The September order's qualified-divestiture constitution reached implementation agreements, a new U.S. legal person, allocated ownership and control rights, and live app operation. The public record does not reveal the full consideration, settlement or CFIUS terms.

Fostering the Future later acquired a State-to-Federal account bridge, but the account itself came from statute. Guidance allowed a child-welfare guardian to open it; an identified funded account did not exist publicly by the cutoff.

### Rule and classification clock

Agricultural and Brazil tariff modifications became executable product books with retroactive entry and refund rules. Muslim Brotherhood branches and one leader entered operative FTO/SDGT machinery. DOL's proxy guidance changed the legal characterization of actual voting control and qualifying paid advice. Pay schedules became effective tables.

These are not equivalent outcomes. A tariff line applies at entry; an SDGT record applies to a named person and ownership chain; fiduciary status depends on conduct and relationship; a pay table still needs a person and payroll cycle.

### Operating clock

The strongest operations include the TikTok JV, customs treatment, resumed but incomplete soybean shipments, Genesis mission intake, DOJ's AI and food-market litigation, the foster-care performance pilot, staffing-plan machinery and Treasury's named sanctions actions.

Most knowledge and market objects remain one or two steps earlier: mission, guidance, solicitation, task force or filing rather than validated result, price change, vote, cure, launch or transaction.

### Contest clock

The broadest reversal is external to the quarter but decisive for its thesis. In February 2026, the Supreme Court held that IEEPA does not authorize the challenged tariffs, and EO 14389 terminated the affected duties. The political bargains and product relationships did not evaporate, but their enforcement carrier did. Some pieces migrated to other statutes; refund and collection clocks remained separate.

The quarter therefore does not narrate smooth institutional conquest. It narrates **terms searching for a durable holder**.

## Both poles

### Constructive pole — relationship becomes inspectable and reciprocal

- counterparties receive defined ownership, security, tariff, data and review terms;
- surviving departments translate ambition into lawful agreements, lists and procedures;
- vulnerable people and retirement assets gain explicit guardianship or fiduciary routes;
- designations are branch- and conduct-specific rather than purely rhetorical;
- courts and administrative records test the government's theory; and
- failed authority can be severed without erasing every underlying relationship.

### Shadow pole — every relationship becomes an eligibility and coercion surface

- corporate settlement and sovereign assurance can hide private economics or unreviewable commitments;
- exception books and mission platforms can privilege favored products, firms and knowledge;
- hiring, foster matching and proxy voting can be centralized through opaque scoring or ideology tests;
- terrorist and WMD language can expand beyond precise conduct;
- “financial interest” and “competition” can become political selection devices; and
- complex procedure can manufacture the appearance of reciprocity while power remains asymmetric.

Both poles remain live. Contracts, list entries, entry records, awards, platform logs, State plans, account receipts, votes, cases and remedies—not the astrological rhyme—show which matured.
