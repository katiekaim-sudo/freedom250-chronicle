Failure and remedy: when it breaks, who can freeze, who pays, who decides?

For 18 of the 36 crypto dollars the library tracks, the issuer can freeze or block coins; for 15 the terms read do not say. When a firm collapsed, customers were being paid back at least partly in 3 of the 10 cases on the International Crypto Map, and courts decided whether customer crypto was the customer's property or the estate's. The 7 programmable-payment failures were corrected by forks, freezes or restitution, not one rule. 37 of 41 U.S. jurisdictions in the register have enacted UCC Article 12, the law on who owns a controllable electronic record.
