## 2026 sidereal-stage relays

Two late-2026 Full-Moon-to-New-Moon intervals are almost exactly **fourteen
sidereal days**. Earth therefore turns Washington back to nearly the same
horizon and meridian while the Moon changes from opposition to conjunction with
the Sun.

The result is unusually literal continuity:

> The stage returns. The phase changes. Three fast actors move. The command
> structure reorganizes.

This does not make the Full Moon the monthly governor. The earlier New Moon
still governs its chapter until the following New Moon opens another. The
return describes how one Full-phase disclosure is handed into the next seed.

## The astronomy

A mean sidereal day—one rotation of Earth relative to the stars—is
23h56m04.0905s. Fourteen sidereal days equal **13.961773924 mean solar days**.

| Full phase → next seed | Interval | Difference from 14 sidereal days | Sidereal residual |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Aug. 28 Pisces lunar eclipse → Sep. 11 Virgo New Moon | 13.964211551 days | +3.510 minutes | 0.879949° |
| Sep. 26 Aries Full Moon → Oct. 10 Libra New Moon | 13.959058509 days | −3.910 minutes | 0.980226° |

Because the elapsed time is almost an integer number of sidereal rotations,
the Washington axes return:

| Relay | ASC change | MC change |
|---|---:|---:|
| Aug. 28 → Sep. 11 | 0.909501° | 0.901701° |
| Sep. 26 → Oct. 10 | 0.816003° | 1.068474° |

The Moon can simultaneously change phase because phase is measured relative to
the Sun, while the local stage is measured relative to Earth's rotation and the
stellar frame. These are two different clocks.

## What this adds to normal analysis

For every important transition, ask four separate questions:

1. **Clock:** How many solar and sidereal days elapsed?
2. **Frame:** Did Washington's ASC and MC rotate to a new stage or return to an
   old one?
3. **Cast:** Which planets actually changed signs or houses?
4. **Command:** Did the final dispositor or bounded root circuit change?

A near-identical wheel can carry a different story because phase and root have
changed. A radically rotated wheel can carry the same root. Neither resemblance
nor difference should be asserted from appearance alone.
