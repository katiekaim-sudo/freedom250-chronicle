# AI Infrastructure Transaction and Risk Ledger — 2026-08-10

## Evidence-state vocabulary

- **MOU / framework:** intention and architecture; no facility or funded asset.
- **Definitive / committed:** operative agreement or binding commitment exists.
- **Drawn / acquired:** cash advance or asset acquisition is evidenced.
- **Deployed:** equipment or facility is delivered and available.
- **Operating:** recurring service, rent or power revenue is evidenced.

Do not promote a row merely because another clock in the same transaction is
further advanced.

## Risk must be assigned by type

| Risk | Party that can bear it | Documentary test |
|---|---|---|
| Customer credit | compute provider, landlord, project SPV, guarantor, lender | obligor, parent guarantee, termination rights, replacement-customer rights |
| Utilization | customer under take-or-pay; otherwise owner/operator | minimum payment, capacity reservation, resale/redeployment rights |
| GPU technology obsolescence | GPU owner, residual guarantor, lender after foreclosure | title schedule, useful life, refresh/swap terms, buyback or residual support |
| Data-center design obsolescence | property owner and property lender | power density, cooling standard, retrofit obligation, tenant improvement allocation |
| Power price / availability | customer, operator, utility, hedge counterparty | PPA, pass-through, hedge, curtailment and force-majeure terms |
| Construction / delivery | developer, EPC/OEM, sponsor, lender | completion guarantee, fixed-price contract, delay damages, acceptance test |
| Refinancing | borrower equity and maturing creditor | maturity, amortization, balloon, extension and cash-trap terms |
| Collateral recovery | secured lender and any guarantor | lien, perfection, serial schedule, remarketing mechanics and deficiency recourse |
