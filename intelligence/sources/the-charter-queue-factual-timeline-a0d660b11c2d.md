# The Charter Queue — Factual Timeline

**This note is the canonical source.** The Observatory view (`The Charter Queue.html`, sub-view of obs-om) is *derived* — edit here, then re-run `build_charter_seed.py` → `build_charter_astro.py` → `build_charter_timeline.py`. Never hand-edit the JSON or the HTML.

**What it tracks:** the **legal operating clock** of the private monetary stack — application → conditional approval → final approval → consummation → **account access**. Not the company build clock (founding → product → pilot → production). A press release moves the first; only a regulator moves the second.

**The seven tracks**

- **The Door** — the OCC charter queue. Who applied, who was conditionally approved, who consummated.
- **The Gate** — Federal Reserve account access. Master accounts, Reserve Bank stock, Custodia. *The door nobody has walked through.*
- **The Ledger** — clearing and depository permission. PSSC, DTC's tokenization service, the Collateral AppChain.
- **The Rulebook** — the incumbents absorbing the new rails while keeping the constitution. Swift, Visa, Mastercard, the capital-treatment guidance.
- **The Coin** — the issuers and their obligors. Who owes the holder money, and who can freeze it.
- **The Interior** — the permissioned bank rails. Fnality, Partior, Canton, Kinexys, Broadridge — where regulated balance sheets actually meet.
- **The Stack** — the acquisitions. Vertical integration is moving faster than any protocol merger.

**Status vocabulary:** `Occurred` · `Proposed` · `Scheduled` · `Public position` · `Research`. Month-precision dates get **no chart** — month is not a moment, and the vault does not fake one.

## The record

### 2021 — Anchorage Digital Bank, N.A. receives the first digital-asset national trust charter

**Status:** Occurred
**Entities:** OCC; Anchor Labs; Anchorage Digital Bank, N.A.
**Track:** The Door

The OCC charters the first national trust bank built for digital assets, under a 2021 operating agreement. Five years later it is still the only member of the cohort that has been a chartered, operating national bank the whole way through. A trust charter is not a commercial-bank charter: no insured deposits, no lending, no discount window, and no Federal Reserve master account.

**Source:** [OCC interpretations and decisions](https://www.occ.gov/topics/charters-and-licensing/interpretations-and-decisions/index-interpretations-and-decisions.html)

### 2019-05 — Fnality International is established

**Status:** Occurred
**Entities:** Fnality International Limited; shareholder banks
**Track:** The Interior

A private company limited by shares, England and Wales, owned by a consortium of banks and market utilities. Its purpose is the one thing no stablecoin can offer: a settlement asset that *is* central-bank money, held in a pooled omnibus account at the Bank of England.

**Source:** [Fnality](https://www.fnality.com/)

### 2021 — Partior is founded by DBS, J.P. Morgan and Temasek

**Status:** Occurred
**Entities:** Partior; DBS; J.P. Morgan; Temasek; Standard Chartered
**Track:** The Interior

Out of the Monetary Authority of Singapore's Project Ubin. The settled asset is tokenized **commercial-bank money** — a liability of the participating settlement bank, not of Partior, and not of any central bank. Whoever the money is owed by is the whole question.

**Source:** [Partior](https://www.partior.com/)

### 2022-08-31 — The sterling Fnality Payment System is recognized as a UK payment system

**Track:** The Door

The famous names are the visible part. The OCC's public digital-asset application list holds a far longer queue — a president's family venture, a British neobank, an exchange consortium, and a row of trust companies almost nobody has heard of. **A charter application is not an operating bank; a conditional approval is not a final one; and a trust charter is neither deposit insurance nor a Federal Reserve account.** The queue is the story.

### 2026-07-11 — Kraken's claimed Federal Reserve account is not in the public record

**Status:** Research
**Entities:** Payward; Kraken Bank (Wyoming SPDI); Federal Reserve
**Track:** The Gate

Kraken's group **states** that its Wyoming special-purpose depository holds a limited-purpose Federal Reserve account. The Fed's public access database does not corroborate it under any known Kraken or Payward name. Either Kraken is the first crypto-adjacent body through the door in American history, or the claim inflated in the telling. **Unconfirmed, and checkable** — which makes it a watch, not a finding.

**Source:** [Federal Reserve access-request database](https://www.federalreserve.gov/paymentsystems/master-account-and-services-database-access-requests.htm)

### 2026-07-11 — The Ripple master-account claim is a conflation

**Status:** Research
**Entities:** Ripple; Standard Custody & Trust Company; Federal Reserve; OCC
**Track:** The Gate

The OCC's conditional-approval letter says the *proposed* Ripple National Trust Bank will apply for **Federal Reserve Bank stock** — a membership step. That is not a master-account request, and no Ripple or Standard Custody request was found in the Fed's public database. Membership, stock, a reserve account and a confirmed master account are four different things, and the retail story treats them as one.

**Source:** [OCC conditional approval 1359](https://www.occ.treas.gov/topics/charters-and-licensing/interpretations-and-actions/2026/ca1359.pdf)
