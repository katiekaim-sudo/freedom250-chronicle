Research summary

A comparison of Ripple and other network buildouts that separates technology, corporate control, legal access and token value capture. The September 1 extension adds the regulated control plane above the chains: one authoritative record/control book can reconcile representations across specialized networks, while bridges remain transport rather than issuers, transfer agents or legal-finality authorities.
