Freedom 250 · Foreign relations and world money · World money institutions

How do the IMF, the World Bank bodies and the BIS fit together, and where does the Fed connect?

Treasury speaks for the U.S. at the IMF and the five World Bank Group bodies (IBRD, IDA, IFC, MIGA, ICSID); the Fed Board is the U.S. member of the BIS. The Fed touches them at 5 points: U.S. policy seat; depositary and fiscal agent; SDR certificate chain; IMF surveillance; BIS membership. None of these gives the IMF or the Bank a say in Fed policy, or the Fed a vote inside them. Treasury's October 14, 2026 statement is a U.S. position, not yet a rule change at any of the institutions.
