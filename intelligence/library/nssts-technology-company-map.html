Research summary

A twelve-sector forecast and structural map of which exact companies may shape the National Security Science and Technology Strategy buildout. It works from federal demand through site and transaction control, hidden delivery, independent evidence and government acceptance—not fame or generic adjacency—and keeps confirmed performers, serious contenders, pivotal third parties, referees, facilities, bottlenecks and disconfirmers in separate evidence states.
