# Wave 4E — Designation, Blocking and Security Classification

**Research window:** Exact 2025 Libra ingress through exact 2025 Capricorn ingress.  
**Evidence cutoff:** June 20, 2026, 11:59 p.m. EDT. Later facts are quarantined as handoffs.  
**Question:** When the executive named Muslim Brotherhood branches as possible terrorist organizations and illicit fentanyl as a weapon of mass destruction, what changed merely in rhetoric, what entered an existing statutory classification, and what became an enforceable exclusion?  
**Finding:** The two orders mature differently. EO 14362 initiated a report-and-designation process rather than designating a group itself. In January 2026, State and Treasury completed named actions: the Lebanese branch became an FTO and SDGT, its secretary general became an SDGT, and the Egyptian and Jordanian branches became SDGTs. Those classifications activate blocking, reporting, transaction, immigration and review machinery, although no blocked-dollar or rejected-payment ledger was located. EO 14367 immediately applied a presidential WMD label to illicit fentanyl and core precursors, then directed Justice, State, Treasury, War and Homeland Security to use existing criminal, financial, intelligence and support authorities. Treasury later expressly treated a 23-person/entity precursor-network sanctions action as “in furtherance” of the order—but the action's legal authorities were EO 14059 and EO 13224. The packet's central result is therefore **classification as a control interface: a name becomes operational only when a competent holder places a defined person, organization, substance or network inside an existing legal machine**.

The shared ladder is:

`threat narrative → candidate class → competent designator → named legal object → list / record → prohibitions or operating mission → transaction / prosecution / intelligence use → review / delisting / correction`

## 4E.1 — November 24: a candidate threat enters a legal hearing

[EO 14362](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2025/11/28/2025-21664/designation-of-certain-muslim-brotherhood-chapters-as-foreign-terrorist-organizations-and-specially) directs State and Treasury, after consultation with Justice and the intelligence community, to report within thirty days on specified Muslim Brotherhood chapters, including those in Lebanon, Jordan and Egypt. Within forty-five days after the report, the competent Secretary must take appropriate action under:

- section 219 of the Immigration and Nationality Act for Foreign Terrorist Organization designation; and
- IEEPA and EO 13224 for Specially Designated Global Terrorist treatment.

The structure matters. The President does not use the order to skip the designation record. State must assemble and judge the FTO administrative record; State or Treasury must make the SDGT determination; publication, list entry and statutory remedies follow on their own clocks.

### January 13–14: the named exclusions become effective

On January 13, 2026, [Treasury announced coordinated State and OFAC actions](https://home.treasury.gov/news/press-releases/sb0357):

- OFAC designated the **Egyptian Muslim Brotherhood** and **Jordanian Muslim Brotherhood** as SDGTs for material support to Hamas;
- State designated the **Lebanese Muslim Brotherhood**, also known as al-Jamaa al-Islamiyah, as both an FTO and an SDGT; and
- State designated the Lebanese branch's secretary general, **Muhammad Fawzi Taqqosh**, as an SDGT.

The FTO notice was published on January 14 and states that the designation became effective upon publication. That publication clock should not be collapsed into Treasury's January 13 announcement.

The resulting machinery is concrete:

- U.S.-held property and interests in property of blocked persons must be blocked and reported;
- entities owned fifty percent or more by blocked persons are also blocked;
- U.S.-person and U.S.-nexus transactions are generally prohibited absent exemption or license;
- contributions of funds, goods and services are restricted;
- foreign financial institutions can face correspondent-account consequences for qualifying significant transactions; and
- the FTO status activates distinct immigration, material-support and judicial-review consequences supplied by statute.

**Maturity.** Complete branch- and person-specific legal designation, publication/list entry and enforceable compliance perimeter.

**Counterevidence and limit.** No public record in this packet establishes a dollar amount blocked, a particular payment rejected, a prosecution, a license denial or a completed deprivation of operational capacity. The action does not designate every person associated with the Muslim Brotherhood, every national branch, Islamic civic organization or religious belief. FTO and SDGT are different legal classes with different holders and remedies; the Egyptian and Jordanian branches were not made FTOs by Treasury's SDGT action.

## 4E.2 — December 15: fentanyl moves from narcotics language into national-security language

[EO 14367](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2025/12/18/2025-23417/designating-fentanyl-as-a-weapon-of-mass-destruction) declares **illicit fentanyl and its core precursor chemicals** to be weapons of mass destruction. The definitions are narrower than the headline:

- illicit fentanyl means fentanyl manufactured, distributed, dispensed or possessed with intent in violation of 21 U.S.C. §§ 841 and 846; and
- core precursors include substances such as piperidone-based chemicals used to produce illicit fentanyl and analogues.

The order then distributes work across surviving institutions:

- Justice is to pursue investigations, prosecutions and appropriate sentencing arguments;
- State and Treasury are to act against relevant assets and financial institutions under applicable law;
- War and Justice are to determine whether support under 10 U.S.C. § 282 is warranted;
- War, in consultation with DHS, is to update Armed Forces chemical-incident directives; and
- DHS is to use WMD and nonproliferation intelligence to identify fentanyl-smuggling threat networks.

This is a mission and coordination reclassification. It does not itself amend the Controlled Substances Act, define a new criminal offense, supply an automatic sentencing enhancement, transfer civilian law enforcement to the military or authorize force against a named target.

### April 23: an old authority carries the new mission label into a named network action

On April 23, 2026, OFAC [sanctioned twenty-three individuals and entities](https://home.treasury.gov/news/press-releases/sb0468) across an India- and Latin America-linked synthetic-opioid procurement network supporting the Sinaloa Cartel. Treasury explicitly said the action was taken “in furtherance” of EO 14367 and targeted chemical suppliers, brokers and other parts of the precursor chain.

The legal authorities were not EO 14367. OFAC designated the targets under:

- EO 14059, which targets international illicit-drug proliferation; and
- EO 13224, which targets terrorists and their supporters.

This is the cleanest institutional reading of the WMD label: it changes mission integration and threat framing while preexisting counternarcotics and counterterrorism authorities continue to do the legal work. The associated Homeland Security Task Force investigation, DEA, FBI, HSI, CBP, foreign-law-enforcement and FinCEN participation show a joined operating network, but each entity retains its own powers.

Official War and ONDCP materials later used WMD language in describing counter-cartel operations and Homeland Security Task Forces. The public packet does not isolate a revised Armed Forces chemical-incident directive, a specific 10 U.S.C. § 282 resource transfer, a WMD-derived prosecution charge or a DHS intelligence product. Those remain next-proof watches rather than assumed results.

**Maturity.** Immediate presidential threat classification; later explicit incorporation into a named sanctions and interagency-investigation action.

**Counterevidence and limit.** The April sanctions action would have legal effect because of EO 14059 and EO 13224 even though Treasury connected it to the newer mission. Ordinary fentanyl prosecutions under Title 21 are not automatically EO descendants. Aggregate claims that drug flows declined cannot be attributed to one order without a defined measurement and counterfactual. Lawfully manufactured and administered medical fentanyl is outside the order's illicit-fentanyl definition.

## The classification-state theorem

These orders show three distinct acts that are often collapsed:

1. **political naming** — the executive identifies the threat and desired treatment;
2. **legal classification** — the authorized official applies a statute or delegated authority to a defined object; and
3. **operating enforcement** — a bank blocks property, a border or intelligence system identifies a network, or a prosecutor brings a charge.

EO 14362 traversed all three stages for named branches by the cutoff, though no transaction receipt was public. EO 14367 clearly completed the first stage and acquired a linked interagency sanctions operation, but its legal coercion still rode older authorities. This is not a semantic quibble. It reveals where the decision right actually lives.

The regime's change is less “the President can call anything a terrorist or WMD” than:

`presidential threat frame → department-specific finding → shared list / intelligence object → distributed compliance by banks, carriers, prosecutors and partners`

That is how a designation becomes an institutional body.

## Both poles

### Constructive pole — precise designation makes hidden harm governable

- branch-specific administrative records can distinguish an operative support network from a broad religious or political label;
- formal lists create notice, compliance duties, licensing and review routes;
- counter-fentanyl agencies can combine chemical, financial, border and terrorism intelligence without creating a new department;
- precursor suppliers and brokers can be targeted upstream of the finished drug; and
- judicial review, delisting and licensing preserve correction mechanisms.

### Shadow pole — the threat label colonizes ordinary law

- association can be overread as conduct and a specific branch action expanded into a civilizational category;
- banks and platforms can over-comply beyond the named person and suppress lawful activity;
- WMD language can militarize a health and narcotics problem without adding a clear legal test;
- existing sanctions and prosecutions can be rhetorically rebranded as proof of a new power; and
- secrecy around intelligence and administrative records can make error difficult to challenge.

Both poles remain live. Administrative records, list entries, blocked-property reports, licenses, indictments, court review, delisting petitions and public-health evidence—not the astrological rhyme—show how the classification operated.
