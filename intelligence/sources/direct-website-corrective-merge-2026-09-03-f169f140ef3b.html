Corrective ruling

The first weekly package did not prove the direct-site coverage the wave method required. Its 430-document Federal Register census was a valid legal- publication backbone, but it was allowed to dominate discovery. A corrective three-wave campaign then found 49 missed material objects or coherent clusters, plus two material EPA objects recovered separately. A dedicated military follow-up found 15 more in-window service-site objects or coherent operational clusters beyond the items already in Wave 2C. An entity-chart- guided pass then added 23 genuinely missed material objects or clock transitions after operator deduplication and direct comparison of the CAAF rules editions. These are not 89 identical-weight headlines; they are at least 89 units that required explicit admission, folding, enrichment, or return-gate treatment.

This file supersedes the first merge wherever the two conflict. The original merge remains useful for the legal-publication record and objects it sourced correctly.

Crypto and market-structure correction

The CFTC direct product database is the key addition. Seventeen Bitnomial perpetual futures appeared as certified on September 2: Bitcoin, Ether, Solana, XRP, Bitcoin Cash, Litecoin, Dogecoin, Avalanche, Chainlink, Polkadot, Stellar Lumens, Hedera, Cardano, Aave, Tezos, Tron, and Shiba Inu. Separately, two KalshiEx perpetual proposals—EUR/USD and U.S. 10-year Treasury—appeared as Approval Pending (45) on August 31. The combination is evidence that perpetual-style product design is entering regulated derivatives records across crypto, FX, and rates. It is not evidence that all products are approved, listed, liquid, cleared, or available to every customer.

CFTC also published a final CORRA/Overnight TIIE clearing rule, reporting relief for Electron Exchange, and leadership remarks describing programmable, composable, continuous markets and their liquidation, rehypothecation, procyclicality, oracle, interoperability, and cyber risks. The speech is official context but expressly personal views, not Commission policy.

The SEC added three staff beneficial-ownership interpretations and set a September 17 operational roundtable on 24-hour trading. These direct-site objects materially strengthen the existing thesis that market authorities are working through the operating requirements of continuous and tokenized market infrastructure. They do not prove official adoption of any cryptocurrency as money, settlement finality, or a replacement for existing rails.

Coverage and access limits

Official direct sites are fragmented. Several DHS, CBP, CISA, FEMA, State, FCC, clearinghouse, award-search, and public intelligence surfaces were blocked, dynamic, stale, or not reliably enumerable. Date-only publication pages were not assigned invented intraday times. The absence of a located item on those surfaces is not evidence of agency inactivity, and this package does not claim to census classified activity, every contract action, every field operation, every court docket, or every payment.
