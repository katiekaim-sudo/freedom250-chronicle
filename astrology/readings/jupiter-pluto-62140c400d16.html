Jupiter–Pluto

The planets

Jupiter — Zeus, expansion, faith, courage to hope. MUNDANE: hierarchical organizations — government, corporate, nonprofit; the corporate sector; courts, judges, religion, charitable organizations. (Greer: "the planet of hierarchy; he rules every organization set up in a hierarchical manner.")

Pluto — God of the Underworld, resurrection, transformation. MUNDANE: treat with healthy skepticism per Greer; collective power, plutocratic forces, the masses-as-force. Don't overweight. (Greer does not include Pluto in his system; the following draws on Barbault and general practice with appropriate caution.)

The combination — mundane

Jupiter-Pluto is the cycle of hierarchical and corporate power confronting transformative and plutocratic forces — the attainment of power of all kinds, physical, material, mental, spiritual. Barbault links it to international terrorism and the growth of plutocratic influence. When Jupiter amplifies Pluto, the hidden power structures that operate beneath the visible hierarchy expand: billionaire influence, intelligence agencies, organized crime, and the masses-as-force all grow stronger. The corporate sector and the plutocratic underworld become harder to distinguish.

Greer offers no specific delineation for Jupiter-Pluto — Pluto was outside his framework — but the mundane logic follows from his Jupiter principles. Under helpful aspects, hierarchical organizations undergo constructive transformation: corporate reform succeeds, courts confront entrenched power effectively, religious institutions engage genuine spiritual renewal. The masses-as-force work through institutional channels rather than against them. Under hostile aspects, the hierarchy serves plutocratic interests — corporate monopoly expands, courts protect the powerful, religious institutions become vehicles for control rather than transcendence. International terrorism and organized crime (Barbault's specific correlations) gain institutional sophistication. The Pluto caveat applies throughout: these delineations carry less empirical weight than the traditional-planet pairs.

Current: The 2020 Capricorn conjunction (alongside Saturn-Pluto) = pandemic power consolidation. Hierarchical organizations — governments, corporations, international bodies — concentrated unprecedented power under emergency conditions. The July 2026 opposition = the full moon of this cycle. Whatever power was concentrated in 2020 now faces its reckoning: the corporate sector, the courts, and the political hierarchy must account for what they did with the authority they seized.
