Research summary

The institutional DLT transition is a selective migration of representation, shared execution, authoritative record, money and collateral state into a federation of programmable legal ledgers and adapters. Regulated CSD issuance, bank-deposit ledgers and bounded real-value settlement are real; common cross-network title, routine central-bank-money settlement, liquidity integration, accounting close and failure/exit proof remain uneven. A fixed definition of done supplies fourteen non-negotiable joins and an R0-R6 maturity ladder; its technology qualification crosswalk now maps the Crypto Hub's governed systems to the exact functions, legal seats, external dependencies and proof receipts that each path still requires.
