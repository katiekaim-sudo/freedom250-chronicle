Astrology Files

This is the Chronicle-owned home for mundane astrology methods, chart experiments, persona work, and story-overlap analysis. It is part of Sky & Charts, not government Research.

The simple working method

  1. Follow and document the real-world story first. Government facts and primary sources live in the Research Workbench and Research Desk.
  2. Analyze the astrology separately here.
  3. Compare the two afterward and notice what naturally overlaps.

Astrology never creates, upgrades, corroborates, or substitutes for factual evidence. It does not prove causation. A symbolic fit may be interesting and useful to revisit, but it cannot change what the primary record says.

What owns what

  • ASTROLOGY FILES CATALOG.json owns the browsable Astrology Files collections and their stable astrology_id values.
  • ASTROLOGY FILES REGISTRY.json owns exact file custody, source history, states, hashes, and optional links back to related factual Research objects.
  • 99 - Templates/build_astrology_files.py builds the Astrology Files view and its generated data under the Chronicle.
  • Q is separate. Q Corpus Astrology and Q Plotline Atlas remain in their own Obsidian vault and do not belong here or in Research.

The filename Astrology Reference Room.html remains as a compatibility shell target. The human-facing room is Astrology Files.

Where future story overlaps go

When a future government story also receives an astrology pass, keep the two records separate:

  • Save the primary-source facts and mundane timeline in the normal Research package.
  • Save the chart work, symbolism, timing questions, and interpretive notes in an existing or new collection here in Astrology Files.
  • Put the later side-by-side comparison in Mundane Story Overlaps/. Money and payment-system comparisons may use Money x Sky/ instead.
  • Connect the astrology collection back to the factual package with related_research_ids. Do not copy the astrology file into the Research Registry or treat the link as evidence.

If an overlap is only a passing observation, add it to the closest existing Astrology Files collection instead of creating another room or research object.
