Freedom 250 · Digital money · Rules

Which crypto rules are final, which are only proposals, and what does each one actually allow?

Of the 8 crypto rules, orders and bills the library tracks outside the GENIUS Act, 2 are in effect, 3 are formal proposals, 1 is still at the advance-notice stage, 1 is non-binding staff guidance, and the one market-structure bill, the CLARITY Act, is blocked: the Senate fell short of the 60 votes needed to take it up on September 15, 2026. None of the proposals is final yet.
