# Uranus–Pluto

## The planets

**Uranus** — *Prometheus. Liberation, breakthroughs, sudden awakening.* MUNDANE: democracy, the individual, countercultures, alternative movements; machines, factories, computers, the internet. The force that storms past the establishment's walls. When dignified, constructive reform advances, new legislation benefits the people, the nation enjoys technological success. When debilitated, crises and conflict erupt, failed reforms destabilize the government, shocking crimes seize the national imagination. (Greer: Uranus rules technologies that run with little human input — automation, computing, the internet — vs. Mercury which rules tools requiring skilled human operators.)

**Pluto** — *God of the Underworld. Deep purgation, resurrection, transformation.* MUNDANE: collective power wielded from below or behind the scenes; the compulsion to purge the body politic of corruption; emerging nations and mass movements that overthrow existing power structures. (Greer caution: "treat Pluto with healthy skepticism — more than any other planet, Pluto invites projection." Keep him in the chart but don't overweight his effects relative to empirically stronger cycles.)

## The combination

The most explosive combination in mundane astrology. Barbault: radical restructuring of civilizations — "building on the ruins of the old." Not reform but revolution. When the democratic-technological impulse (Uranus) meets the mass-transformative underworld force (Pluto), entire national orders are overturned and rebuilt. The 1965-66 conjunction seeded the computer age, the counterculture, the civil rights revolution, and the generation now holding executive power across the Western nations. Technology and mass upheaval arrive together; the laboring classes and the countercultures gain tools that the establishment cannot control.

Greer does not give a specific Uranus-Pluto entry (Pluto skepticism), but his Uranus chapter clarifies the mundane stakes: Uranus represents "democracy, the individual, countercultures, machines/computers/internet." The helpful expression channels revolutionary energy into technological breakthroughs that benefit the people and restructure the national economy without violence. The hostile expression unleashes mass upheaval against the government — failed states, revolutionary movements, the destruction of the existing corporate and bureaucratic order.

**Current (trine from Gemini to Aquarius):** The harvest phase of the 1960s revolution, flowing freely. The democratic-technological fire (Uranus in Gemini = the information sector, AI, the press) pours into the civilizational transformation (Pluto in Aquarius = the paradigm shift in how the people organize politically). The corporations building AI, the legislature writing technology law, the executive branch restructuring federal data systems — all are riding this trine. The radical restructuring of how the nation thinks and communicates arrives without the violence of a conjunction, benefiting the party in power and the technology sector alike.
