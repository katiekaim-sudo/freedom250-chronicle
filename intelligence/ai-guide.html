Freedom 250 AI Research Guide

This repository is the direct-link publication of two connected surfaces:

  • The Freedom 250 Chronicle at the site root is the dated newspaper archive.
  • Intelligence Rooms at intelligence/ contains the government and entity research universe.
  • The Government Entity Tree at intelligence/rooms/government-entity-tree.html maps federal structure, reporting relationships, money flows, committees and subcommittees.
  • The Research Reading Room at intelligence/library/ represents every object in the governed Research Desk Catalog. Each topic opens with a concise orientation and then routes to the actual research edition in intelligence/reports/ when available. Current research is the default shelf; dated archive objects remain available for provenance.
  • intelligence/reports/index.json is the machine-readable report map. The sibling .md files are the fastest path for Codex or another research agent; the .html files are the human reading edition.

Instructions for an AI research partner

  1. Start from the Research Reading Room or one of the interactive rooms. Do not infer the scope of the project from the featured cards alone.
  2. For deep work, open the report's .md source-text edition. It contains the actual governed research body, with local-only links converted into portable provenance text.
  3. Always preserve the evidence cutoff shown on the page. A statement can be accurate at its cutoff without being confirmed current today.
  4. Keep fact, project inference, strongest rival, and falsifier separate. The living thesis is a developing interpretation, not a claim that one coordinated plan has been proved.
  5. Keep institutional clocks separate: announced, proposed, authorized, funded, contracted, tested, launched, accepted, recurring operation, assessed, collected, corrected, and adjudicated are not synonyms.
  6. In monetary or crypto work, distinguish company, network, token, issued asset, message, route, clearing, title, cash settlement, authoritative record, correction, and legal remedy.
  7. Never turn an interface, partnership, pilot, technical compatibility, or standards participation into proof of adoption or production use.
  8. For a conclusion, show the strongest supporting evidence, the strongest ordinary rival explanation, unresolved objects, and what new evidence would change the answer.
  9. Cite the exact public page used. When a page links to a primary source, prefer that source for factual claims.

Useful starting prompts

  • “Give me the strongest version of the Freedom 250 thesis, then the strongest rival explanation and the three clearest falsifiers.”
  • “Trace XRP through company, network, token, liquidity inventory, cash settlement, authoritative record, correction, and legal remedy. Mark every unsupported jump.”
  • “Compare the legal clock and operating clock for this research topic. What has actually become recurring operation?”
  • “Build a timeline from the relevant Atlas objects, preserving each evidence cutoff and distinguishing proposals from completed actions.”
  • “Find the research objects most connected to this claim and explain whether they reinforce it, complicate it, or contradict it.”
  • “What would I need to verify with fresh primary sources before relying on this conclusion today?”
