What does Securitize actually run, and which of its companies holds which license?

Securitize runs the official ownership record for tokenized funds and stocks, not the money. Through 6 separate companies it acts as an SEC-registered transfer agent, a broker-dealer with a trading system, an investment adviser, a fund administrator and an EU trading-and-settlement operator; each license belongs to that one subsidiary, not to the listed parent.

What's still uncertain
