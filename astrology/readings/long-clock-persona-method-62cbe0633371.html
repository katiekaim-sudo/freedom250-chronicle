Governing rule

Conjunction personas are kept as conditional, subordinate, zero-vote subsidiary charts. For each canonical Long Clocks family, derive one shared persona from the registry's display_anchor_pass_id: the first later geocentric tropical solar crossing of that pass's fixed conjunction longitude, cast for Washington, D.C., in Whole Sign houses with traditional rulers.

The conjunction family—not the exact pass and not the persona—remains the cycle seed. Every registered pass and the co-presence window remain visible. The persona does not govern time, create a phase chapter or seed echo, prove an event, or add a convergence vote. Its sole job is to clarify how the already-read seed may organize, enter, aim, or become operational.

One conjunction longitude produces one shared persona with two focal lenses. It never produces two charts or two votes.

Required hearing order

  1. Resolve governed identity. Record the cycle, family, display-anchor pass, every family pass, fixed longitude, seed and persona clocks, elapsed interval, solar residual, solver receipt, and independent-verification status.
  2. Read the seed first. Hear its authored cycle question, whole-chart gestalt, Ascendant and ruler, houses, actual MC and Whole Sign 10th, aspects, governance routes, root or loop, condition, available America contacts, and constructive, shadow, and continuity poles.
  3. Read the persona as one whole. Hear its Big Three, Ascendant and ruler, pattern, Persona-Sun house, actual MC and Whole Sign 10th, both focal planets, governance routes, root or loop, aspects, and condition. The persona owns its houses; the seed is an overlay only.
  4. Compare after both wholes exist. Classify every proposed finding as guaranteed, dependent, seed_restatement, correlated, potentially_additive, contradiction, or unsafe_or_unsupported. Retain the complete admitted relationship-delta ledger in machine-readable evidence. Mark the human-facing promoted subset as selective.
  5. Use two lenses, never two votes. Ask what each focal planetary function wants, supplies, or constrains inside the same chart, then name the shared operating arrangement.
  6. Close answer-first. Keep no more than one or two materially additive clarifications. Name constructive, shadow, and continuity. End with a question-only watch that may refine the parent Long Clock. Do not create a persona-owned State Transition Watch or Forecast Ledger call.

  • Mundane place: Washington, D.C., 38.9072 N, 77.0369 W, displayed in America/New_York local time.
  • Houses: Whole Sign; traditional rulers.
  • Comparison wheels: one explicit house owner. The persona owns a persona biwheel; seed angles remain labeled overlay markers.
  • 916 America: include only when the canonical cache covers the required instant. Otherwise state America unavailable; never extrapolate.

What the subsidiary chart can and cannot do

It can sharpen a seed's operating arena, interface, routing, or public aim when those features are derived rather than mechanically inherited. It can raise a disciplined question for the record.

It cannot:

  • replace the canonical conjunction family;
  • turn one pass into the whole cycle;
  • create a new Long Clock hand, phase, chapter, seed echo, ingress, lunation, or day governor;
  • make a slow-body near-return count as a discovery;
  • corroborate itself through two focal lenses or multiple family passes;
  • add an event vote, prove causation, or promote a literal prediction.

The acceptance test is simple: if removing the persona would change the parent clock's timing or evidence status, the persona has been given too much authority.
