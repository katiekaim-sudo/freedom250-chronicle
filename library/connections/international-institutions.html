What the IMF can and can't legally do

Current saved study

The IMF's strongest lever is access to its financing and the conditions attached. Amending its Articles needs Governors' approval, then acceptance by three-fifths of members holding 85% of total voting power. Three protected subjects require every member's acceptance. Entry into force normally follows three months after formal certification, unless a shorter period is specified.
