## 2026 conjunction-radix relays

A conjunction does not seed only a zodiacal degree. It also arrives through a
complete Washington chart: horizon, meridian, ruler, ruler house, pair house,
dispositor roots and internal geometry. This companion asks whether those
larger structures reappear in the twenty-nine registered 2026 ingress and
lunation charts.

The answer is **yes, but sparsely and in different ways**. One current chart may
inherit an old frame, another an old command root, and another a reciprocal
exchange between the seed degree and the seed chart's horizon. None of those
relations gives the conjunction chart a second vote. The current ingress or
lunation remains governor.

## What the computer compares

The audit loads only the canonical **radix** member of each of the ten Long
Clock conjunction dossiers. The later persona charts are not loaded and retain
zero independent vote.

For each conjunction radix the projection preserves:

- ASC and MC coordinates and signs;
- chart ruler and chart-ruler house;
- the conjunction pair's Whole Sign house;
- final dispositors and bounded-loop members; and
- the horizon and meridian as axes rather than four independent points.

Each 2026 chart remains a complete chart object. The smaller comparison tables
are reversible views joined by chart ID; they do not replace the whole chart.
Lunations contribute their light, horizon and meridian axes. Ingresses
contribute the ingress Sun, horizon and meridian. Exact axis strength remains
the standing **≤0.25°** band.

This is therefore a practical example of the right computer architecture for
the astrology corpus: preserve the whole chart, then generate typed projections
for specific questions.

## The 2020 Earth frame repeats three times

Three conjunction radices share the same large Washington frame:

| Conjunction | ASC / MC | Pair house | Root |
|---|---|---:|---|
| Saturn–Pluto · Jan. 12, 2020 | Taurus / Capricorn | 9H | Saturn |
| Jupiter–Pluto · Nov. 12, 2020 | Taurus / Capricorn | 9H | Mars–Saturn–Venus |
| Jupiter–Saturn · Dec. 21, 2020 | Taurus / Capricorn | 10H | Mars–Saturn |

This is the only three-member repeated sign frame among the ten canonical
radices. Saturn–Pluto and Jupiter–Saturn are nearly exact frame twins: their
horizons differ by **0.172010°** and their meridians by **0.106182°**.

The stable frame is material and institutional—Taurus rising with a Capricorn
meridian—while the command structure changes. The pair begins in the 9H field
of law, doctrine, international affairs and long trade; by Jupiter–Saturn it
has moved into the 10H public-authority field. Saturn begins as the sole root,
then the root system admits Mars and Venus, and finally settles into a
Mars–Saturn command.

That is a coherent archetypal chronology. It is not evidence that the three
conjunctions caused or secretly governed one historical program.

## The Aries ingress is a two-radix splice

Only one 2026 chart exactly repeats a conjunction radix's full
`ASC sign + chart ruler + ruler house` signature:

> The 2026 Aries ingress repeats the 2026 Saturn–Neptune conjunction radix's
> **Gemini rising · Mercury ruler · Mercury in the 10th house** frame.

But its command root comes from somewhere else. The ingress's exact
**Jupiter–Mars–Moon** root is the root of the 1965 Uranus–Pluto conjunction
radix.

The ingress is therefore not “the Saturn–Neptune chart again.” It is a
two-radix splice:

`Saturn–Neptune outer frame + Uranus–Pluto command root`

That is a more precise inheritance claim than either a degree hit or a broad
outer-planet analogy. The seasonal chart itself still governs the season.

After collapsing the near-identical Saturn–Pluto and Jupiter–Saturn frames,

A 1,439-offset whole-frame rotation control places twenty-two receipts at only

The useful information is relational:

- which old chart supplies a current frame;
- which supplies the command root;
- whether seed degree and seed frame form a reciprocal exchange; and
- whether several historically different frames concentrate in one current
  chart.

That is also why the pair-house scan is retained only as dependent context. For

several 2026 charts, but their continuing proximity and similar horizons make
those repetitions structurally dependent. They are not additional evidence
votes.

## What changes in the method

The conjunction chart now has four separately named ways to recur:

1. **Degree inheritance** — the conjunction coordinate contacts a current
   body, light or angle.
2. **Frame inheritance** — ASC sign, ruler and ruler house recur.
3. **Command inheritance** — the complete dispositor-root signature recurs.
4. **Axis inheritance** — a seed horizon or meridian contacts a current light,
   horizon or meridian.

A fifth, rarer label—**reciprocal seed/frame relay**—is admitted only when the
same cycle and current chart carry both a seed-degree hinge and a seed-axis
hinge.

These labels describe different structures and cannot be added as votes. They
let the computer preserve the whole-chart context while asking bounded
questions of it.

## Controls and limits

- The ten canonical conjunction **radices** are admitted; persona charts are
  excluded and have no independent vote.
- Saturn–Pluto and Jupiter–Saturn remain separately named charts but count as
  one axis corridor where their nearly identical frames would inflate totals.
- Every axis is counted once; its opposite endpoint remains inspectable.
- Pair-house recurrence is dependent context, never a receipt.
- Prospective registered charts after the September 25 cutoff are structure,
  not occurred events.
- No recurrence supplies factual evidence, corroboration, causal proof or an
  automatic Forecast Ledger call.
- Locked chart readings remain unchanged. This is a companion layer.
