Freedom 250 · Digital money · Banks

Which big banks have live tokenized deposits or deposit tokens?

4 of the 16 big institutions in the library have tokenized deposits or deposit tokens in use: J.P. Morgan, Citi, BNY and HSBC. The rest are piloting, have announced plans, or show nothing yet. 3 of the 16 are asset managers, not banks.
