Where the evidence stopsCustomers do not hold the internal Citi token or operate wallets, Coinbase conversion uses a separate stablecoin claim, and the planned consortium stablecoin is not yet issued.

Two readings: the bank comparison (cutoff 2026-10-03) says 'Recurring production'; the master says 'Live, limited'.

Earlier wording and status notes
  • Bank comparison's words (Recurring production): Citi Token Services moves interbranch deposit representations continuously across seven live markets and reports billions in processed value; Citi also joined the conditional shared-stablecoin-company commitment.
  • Why this status: Live, limited (Katie's rule, 2026-10-19): Tokenized Citi deposits move between Citi branches for Citi clients; 'Clients are not required to hold or manage any tokens' (Citi, 2024).

Two different datesOctober 10, 2024 is the commercial milestone announcement. Citi’s collateral report separately dates live use to April 2024. These describe different milestones; the announcement date is not treated as the first operating date.
