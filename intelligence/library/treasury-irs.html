Research summary

The legal tax core remains sovereign while the interface layer has become more border-facing, electronic and intermediary-heavy. Congress creates the tax; Treasury and IRS retain assessment and enforcement authority; private and cross-agency nodes increasingly carry reporting, filing, scanning, collection, payment and data-use functions.
