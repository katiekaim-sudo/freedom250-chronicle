Research summary

Japan spent August defending several systems at once: disaster recovery after the Kumamoto earthquake, the yen through officially acknowledged U.S.-coordinated purchasing and continued BOJ normalization pressure, household legitimacy through staged fiscal relief, industrial and energy resilience through signed financing and mixed-energy policy, and bank-based digital settlement through controlled real-value tests. Several instruments and pilot clocks moved, but no evidence establishes a production international money rail, completed industrial deployment, durable yen repair or sovereign funding failure.
