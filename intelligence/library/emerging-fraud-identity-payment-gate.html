Research summary

The hearing supplied a front-end identity and continuing-risk lens for an emerging federated person, provider, account and payment-risk architecture. It did not create a national digital ID, enact continuous monitoring, change payment law or prove vendor efficacy; the House payment-control bills remained House-passed and not enacted at the cutoff.
