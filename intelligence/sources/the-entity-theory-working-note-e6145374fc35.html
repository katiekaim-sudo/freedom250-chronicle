The Entity Theory — the government as a stack of legal entities

The claim (Katie's)

The government is not one thing. It is layers upon layers of legal entities — parents, subsidiaries, chartered corporations, funds, and off-books vehicles. And right now the administration is running mergers and acquisitions on the federal group: winding up old entities, folding them into survivors, and standing up brand-new vehicles to hold the assets that matter. Read it the way an accountant reads a consolidated group, and the re-founding shows up as a corporate reorg.

We take this all the way — eventually clickable entity charts, consolidation layers, the works. But facts first; the speculative "USA Inc." layer gets built on top of a solid, verifiable base, not instead of one.

Why this is Katie's angle, and new to the Chronicle

The vault is deep on the money plumbing (Money / State / World Machine, the Hearings) — how the dollar moves. It has never watched the owners of the pipes — the government as a balance sheet of legal entities being restructured. A grep of the event layer for entity moves (dissolved / absorbed / chartered) comes back almost empty except money events. This is open ground.

And it reads in Katie's native tongue: she's an accountant. To her a government is a consolidated group — and consolidation turns on control, not ownership, which is exactly the hinge the whole theory swings on.

The seven legal forms (seven consolidation treatments)

The federal group is not uniform. Each entity type is a different answer to "is this the government?"

  1. Executive departments — wholly-owned operating subsidiaries (State, Treasury, Defense…). Created by statute; hardest to kill.
  2. Independent agencies & regulators — special governance (fixed-term boards) that resists a single controlling hand. The commissions (SEC, CFTC, FTC…) are insulated; the single-administrator agencies (EPA, NASA…) far less so.
  3. Government corporations — literally chartered as corporations, with their own charter, board and balance sheet (USPS, Amtrak, TVA, FDIC, Ginnie Mae, PBGC…).
  4. Government-sponsored enterprises (GSEs) — privately owned, federally chartered (Fannie, Freddie, Federal Home Loan Banks, Farm Credit). Control and ownership point in different directions.
  5. The Federal Reserve System — public head (Board of Governors) bolted to twelve separately incorporated Reserve Banks. Member banks hold mandatory, nontransferable statutory subscription stock with callable capital, capped dividends, bounded director-election rights and no ordinary residual claim. That instrument is not ordinary equity ownership. The deepest ambiguity in the stack. See Federal Reserve Financial Constitution — Source Document.
  6. Federal funds / trust funds — off-balance-sheet accounting entities holding intragovernmental IOUs (Social Security, Medicare, Highway, Military Retirement, the Exchange Stabilization Fund).
  7. New & proposed vehicles (2025–26) — the fresh charters (Strategic Bitcoin Reserve, US Digital Asset Stockpile, the Sovereign Wealth Fund, the Administration for a Healthy America, DOGE).

What the 2025–26 reorg is actually doing (facts)

Dissolutions & absorptions — fast and concrete: - SAMHSA dissolved 27 Mar 2025 (~$8.1B, ~900 staff) → rolled into a new entity, the Administration for a Healthy America; parts of HRSA with it. - USAID cut ~95%; surviving functions folded into State. - Dept of Education marked for wind-down (department-level dissolution — the rarest kind). - CFPB funding cut off and gutted — starved, not formally abolished; the shell survives while the function is hollowed. - DHS internal offices shut (Civil Rights & Civil Liberties, two ombudsmen), Mar 2025; ~500 investigations frozen. - 7 small agencies marked for elimination (media, libraries, museums, homelessness); 200+ programs killed in the first nine months.

New vehicles — announced but legally half-built: - Strategic Bitcoin Reserve + US Digital Asset Stockpile — chartered by EO 6 Mar 2025, Treasury-held, seeded from forfeited assets. Still fighting to become statute (BITCOIN Act → ARMA, May 2026, 20-yr lockup). - US Sovereign Wealth Fund — directed by EO 3 Feb 2025; plan delivered; as of Jul 2026 no legal entity stood up — yet ad-hoc equity stakes (chipmakers, rare-earth miners, quantum) are already being taken.

The signature (the accountant's tell): the dissolutions complete; the new vehicles act before they legally exist. Mandate and money move through vehicles whose articles aren't signed. That always gets "papered" after the fact — and whoever writes the paper decides what the entity really is.

The instrument is itself an entity: DOGE is a re-flagged USDS, re-chartered to run the cuts. The reorg eats from inside the org chart.

The two seams for the speculative layer (later)

Consolidation turns on control, not ownership — so the entities where those diverge are where "the government is a corporation / trust" reading finds real ground: - The Federal Reserve Banks — privately owned corporations doing public work. - The conservatorship GSEs (Fannie/Freddie) — private shareholders, government control; 2025–26 talk of releasing them = a possible spin-OUT reversing a 17-year consolidation.

These are drawn in the diagram hanging off the sovereign on dashed (control-ambiguous) lines — deliberately not under the Executive, because they aren't. That's the honest picture and the hook the deeper reading pulls on.
