Louisiana Makes the Gold Debit Card Legal Tender

June 11, 2025 · Louisiana

Reconstructed (not an official filing)

Landry signs Act 304: gold and silver specie and gold-backed debit instruments are Louisiana legal tender, with authority to use another state's bullion depository — Texas's, by name. It passed 84-0 and 39-0. Nobody was watching the quietest second gold state in the nation.

A Gold Dealer Sues Texas Over Its Own Money

February 5, 2026 · Texas

Reconstructed (not an official filing)

Texas Precious Metals sues the Comptroller and the Bullion Depository administrator in Travis County over the Redback coin program. Not a constitutional case — a trade fight: the dealer says the state exceeded its authority, competes unlawfully with private business, and stole its trademarked Texas-outline mark. The money from below has its first casualty: the private gold market the state just walked into.

Gold Is Legal Tender in Florida

July 1, 2026 · Florida

Reconstructed (not an official filing)

The gold-and-silver legal tender law takes effect — the largest state ever to do it. Gold above 99.5 percent and silver above 99.9 percent recognized as money, sales-tax exempt, with a custodian regime for electronic specie payments. Signed in May 2025, effective the week of the 250th anniversary: while Washington tokenizes the dollar from above, the big states are re-monetizing metal from below.

New Hampshire Passes the First Bitcoin Reserve Law

May 6, 2025 · New Hampshire

Reconstructed (not an official filing)

Ayotte signs HB 302 — the first state law in the nation letting the treasurer put public money into bitcoin, up to five percent of funds, assets over $500 billion market cap only. The treasurer's answer the next day: no plans to buy. Fourteen months later she still hasn't. First to pass, last to act — the law was the point.
