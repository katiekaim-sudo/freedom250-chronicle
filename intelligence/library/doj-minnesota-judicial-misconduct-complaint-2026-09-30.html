Research summary

DOJ filed a judicial-conduct complaint against Chief Judge Patrick Schiltz, Judge John Tunheim and five unnamed District of Minnesota judges over public comments attributed to them in a New York Times article. The filing creates no misconduct finding, recusal, stay, vacatur or removal. Its narrow public-comment theory is materially better documented than DOJ's dismissed 2025 Reyes and Boasberg complaints, but its blanket DHS-recusal request collides with the case-specific Section 455 route; its reliance on White v. NFL omits that decision's no-recusal holding; and its merits-heavy and anonymous-respondent allegations face Rules 4 and 6 boundaries.
