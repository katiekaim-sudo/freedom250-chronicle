Uranus–Neptune

The planets

Uranus — Prometheus. Liberation, breakthroughs, sudden awakening. MUNDANE: democracy, the individual, countercultures, alternative movements; machines, factories, computers, the internet. The force that storms past the establishment's walls. When dignified, constructive reform advances, new legislation benefits the people, the nation enjoys technological success. When debilitated, crises and conflict erupt, failed reforms destabilize the government, shocking crimes seize the national imagination. (Greer: Uranus rules technologies that run with little human input — automation, computing, the internet — vs. Mercury which rules tools requiring skilled human operators.)

Neptune — The Ocean. Dissolution, ideals, transcendence. MUNDANE: collective movements, mass sentiment, propaganda, speculative bubbles; the force that dissolves existing national structures and replaces them with a new collective image. Almost always malefic in mundane charts — Neptune favors unity, but the political and economic spheres punish unity-seeking with mob behavior and lowest-common-denominator politics. The ONLY exception: when Neptune is unafflicted and in favorable aspects, he benefits the masses, favors democratic causes, and indicates peace. In any other condition: chaos, scandal, deception, the collapse of the government's legitimacy.

The combination

Barbault: the interaction between capital and labour, conservatism and liberalism, the individual and the mass — the fundamental polarity of national economic life. Greer's binary-pair framework places Uranus and Neptune as the outermost pair: the democratic-individual impulse vs. the collective-unity impulse. The 1993 Capricorn conjunction seeded the post-Cold War order — globalization, the internet economy, the "end of history" narrative in which the Western establishment declared permanent victory over the laboring classes' alternative.

Greer's delineation for this pair is among his "most fortunate" when helpful: "harmony between the political class and the people, constructive reform" — the legislature and the executive branch cooperate with the popular will, the national economy benefits, democratic causes advance. Hostile Uranus-Neptune aspects bring "turmoil, disruption, confusion; the government is shaken; attempted reforms bog down" — the political class loses touch with the masses, the bureaucracy's reform efforts fail, and the people lose confidence in democratic institutions.

Current (sextile from Gemini to Aries): The first cooperative flowering of whatever was seeded in 1993. The democratic-technological lightning bolt (Uranus in Gemini = the information sector, AI, the national conversation) cooperates with the new collective ideal (Neptune in Aries = sovereignty-first, primal national self-interest). The political class and the people find alignment; the legislature can pass constructive reform; the national economy benefits from the technology sector's expansion meeting the popular demand for national renewal. The sextile = active, willing cooperation between the individual and the mass, between the corporations building the future and the people demanding it serve their interests.
