China Domestic Economy → Foreign-Policy Transmission Map — 2026

Current saved study

A balance-sheet chain: property correction -> household wealth/confidence and developer cash flow fall -> high saving -> weaker land revenue and LGFV capacity -> low demand and low inflation with strong industrial supply -> real depreciation and excess output support exports -> surpluses and partner trade defenses -> Beijing weights export access, supply-chain security, self-reliance and managed trade conflict. A disciplined transmission hypothesis supported by official data and IMF surveillance; not a theory that domestic weakness produces military aggression or that every export is excess capacity.
