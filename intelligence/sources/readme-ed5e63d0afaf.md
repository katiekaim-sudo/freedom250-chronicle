## Evidence boundary

- A White House social post is an activation carrier, not an origin finding.
- A White House editorial page is executive framing, not a new intelligence
  product or scientific adjudication.
- A page's later assertion that an investigation opened is not a charge,
  indictment, conviction or adjudicated fact; locate the operative DOJ object.
- A congressional majority report and minority report are official
  institutional records with different findings; neither is a court judgment.
- A hearing witness's Fifth Amendment invocation is not an admission of the
  allegation that prompted a question.
- A chair's announced contempt vote is not a committee vote, Senate adoption,
  certification to a prosecutor, charge, conviction or sentence.
- A prospective research-funding policy is a control response, not proof of a
  historical origin chain.
- Release timing can establish temporal alignment. Coordination or motive
  requires an additional source.
