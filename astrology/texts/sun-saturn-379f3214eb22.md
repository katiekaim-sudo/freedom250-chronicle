# Sun–Saturn

## The planets

**Sun** — *Authority, illumination, "everything revolves around the Sun."* MUNDANE: the head of government (the President); the executive branch; the focus of executive power. The Sun's condition shows how the government will fare. (Greer: "the Sun represents the actual head of government — whoever officially calls the shots in the executive branch.")

**Saturn** — *Limitation, structure, time, discipline.* MUNDANE: the established order; the conservative opposition; the laboring classes and the elderly; agriculture and mining; infrastructure and bureaucracy; borders and boundaries. Saturn is the weight of reality — the structures that hold the state together and the constraints that hold it back. In a mundane chart, Saturn shows where the nation encounters hard limits.

## The combination — mundane

Under helpful aspects (sextile, trine), Sun-Saturn "gives the government strength rather than popularity," as Greer puts it. The executive gains the ability to "hold or extend power by skillful maneuvering." This is not a beloved government — it is a competent one. Legislation passes not because the public is enthusiastic but because the administration works the levers of institutional power effectively. Bureaucratic machinery runs smoothly. The nation's infrastructure holds. The President looks serious, disciplined, and in command of the structural realities of governance.

Under hostile aspects (conjunction, square, opposition), Saturn's weight falls on the executive. Greer describes "loss of power and popularity; obstacles and delays; economy stagnates." The government looks old, tired, or out of touch. Legislative agendas stall. The economy contracts or flatlines — Saturn squeezes rather than stimulates. The President ages visibly under the burden. The conservative opposition gains ground, the laboring classes feel the pinch, and the public mood turns grim and patient (Saturn's patience is not optimistic — it is the patience of people who have stopped expecting things to improve).

**As trigger:** Sun-Saturn aspects are fast-moving (days to about two weeks) and mark specific moments when structural limits, institutional power, and the weight of the established order activate whatever broader outer-planet storylines are in play.
