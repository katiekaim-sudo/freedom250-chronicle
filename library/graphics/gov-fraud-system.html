Which parts of the national fraud-detection system are proven, and which are only announced or missing?

Of the 33 parts of the national fraud-detection system the library mapped, 18 are proved by a public record, 7 are announced with no public text, 2 are proposed and 2 are the library's inference. 4 are missing: Technical annex / SLA, NFDC system records, Collected recovery and Correction, notice and remedy path. Since the map, Treasury reported pre-payment verification fully operating on September 30, 2026.
