2025 Winter — Storyline Comparison

> RETROSPECTIVE CALIBRATION — NOT PREDICTIVE PROOF

The readings compared here were written on September 25, 2026, long after the 2025 record existed, and frozen as hash-bound baselines before this comparison was drafted. They cannot demonstrate prediction, and no correspondence here may be promoted into a forecast hit. The exercise tests whether the readings make disciplined distinctions, survive contact with the dated record, keep both poles alive, and admit what does not fit. It also serves a second purpose that 2026 did not need: it reconstructs the build-up — how the stories the Chronicle follows in 2026 were set in motion a year earlier.

The comparison window is December 21, 2024 through March 19, 2025, governed by the 2024 Capricorn ingress (Scorpio rising, fixed; Aries resets the year on March 20). Three evidence lanes remain separate:

WINTER-2025-F01 — Reversal from the government house, and a contested money circuit

Disposition: Lines up
Lanes: Official; Attention secondary

What the chart said. The Capricorn ingress was steered by a retrograde Mars in the 10th — the executive's force moving backward through the house of government, aimed at what came before — opposed by Pluto newly arrived at the foundations. Everything drained into a mutual reception between Mercury in the treasury and Jupiter in the debt house, both in detriment: a pact over money conducted out of plain view. The Sun spoke through the press house square Neptune. Constructive pole: a clearing of what had calcified and an open renegotiation of the money circuit. Shadow: reversal as an end in itself, with the money circuit governing out of sight.

What the record later showed. The official lane is dominated by reversal verbs. EO 14148 (January 20) is titled Initial Rescissions of Harmful Executive Orders and Actions; EO 14236 (March 14) continues them; the attention lane records eighty prior orders revoked in a single stroke. Ninety-three numbered orders follow in eight weeks. The money circuit becomes the season's most litigated object: Alliance v. Bessent (filed February 3) and New York v. Trump (TRO February 8, PI February 21) both concern access to Treasury payment systems, while the OMB funding-freeze memo appears and is walked back within a day (January 28–29 in attention).

Calibration reasoning. This lines up at the level the chart claimed: form. A retrograde ruler in the 10th describes a government whose first acts are undoings, and the root describes a treasury–obligation circuit that becomes contested. The chart does not name DOGE, and it could not tell us that the money fight would be dragged into open court rather than kept hidden — the record shows the shadow pole (access to payment systems) being checked by the constructive one (litigation and disclosure).

Misses and unresolved questions

Miss

  • Greer-quiet did not mean quiet. Three of the season's six lunations (December 30, January 13, January 29) made no hard contact with the ingress, and the January lunar months contain the densest executive action of the entire year. The method already warns that a Greer-quiet month must be checked against the U.S. natal before it is called empty, and here the U.S. contacts were live (January 13: Ascendant opposite U.S. Moon, Mars opposite U.S. Pluto). The lesson stands: the ingress trigger rule is not a volume meter.

Unresolved

  • The December 30 lunar month (F02) is too thin to test.
  • Several official actions carry date precision without a verified release time; they remain dated states, not minute-exact chart events.
  • Because every reading was written after the window, none can estimate prospective accuracy.

Calibration lesson

The winter charts are strongest where they describe the form of executive action: undoing before building (retrograde Mars in the 10th), a money circuit that becomes the contested object (the treasury–debt root), force aimed at home defense (Mars in Cancer in the 1st), and dissolution in the working body ratified or resisted in court (the February 27 New Moon). They weaken when asked to own a specific audit (March 14) or a quiet month.

What this season set in motion. The Treasury payment circuit, the fraud-and-efficiency lineage (War on Waste, Shifting Gears), the Strategic Bitcoin Reserve, and the reversal of the prior administration's orders all begin here. The 2026 Aries root — exalted Jupiter guarding the 2nd house, the treasure — is the far end of a story whose first chart put the treasury in a debilitated pact with debt.
