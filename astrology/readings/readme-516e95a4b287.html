Institutional Regime Waves 2025–2026

This package is the curated research layer beneath the Observatory's institutional-wave view. The Executive Orders provide the dated skeleton; these packets recover the less visible memoranda, implementing guidance, named decision-makers, court clocks, transactions, reversals and statutory continuities that show what acquired a body and what remained only a direction.

It is a workbench package, not the live-vault authority. The factual government record remains here as Research Desk material. The separate astrology readings are Chronicle-owned Astrology Files under Sky & Charts; they may compare against this completed factual chain, but they do not supply evidence or force a connection back into it.

The reconciled cross-wave reading is The State Changes at the Threshold. It joins the seven seasonal states into one sequence—control plane, routing, interface, counterparty, carrier, eligibility and inheritance—while preserving the five evidence clocks and both archetypal poles.

Authority stack and scope

The reading rhythm

Each micro-wave does seven things:

  1. asks one narrow institutional question;
  2. reads the controlling research packet and primary objects in full;
  3. extracts the dates, actors, decision rights and hidden movers;
  4. separates legal direction, operating machinery, money, observed effect and contest;
  5. traces one institutional chain across the wave boundary;
  6. places the chain inside the governing ingress, nested seasonal frame and lunar chapters; and
  7. states the natural archetypal rhyme without treating astrology as causal proof or a day-level prediction.

The compact row grammar is:

date → mover → institutional change → maturity → counterevidence → plotline → astrological rhyme → handoff

Evidence boundary

  • A directive is not an exercised decision.
  • A plan is not a reorganization, RIF, award or payment.
  • Data access is not a legal transfer of the agency's charter or decision right.
  • A named ceiling, pledge or selection is not an obligation or outlay.
  • Litigation, statutory minimums, appropriations and restored operations are part of the entity story, not footnotes.
  • Later implementation may mature an earlier seed, but it must keep its own date and astrological home.
  • Both constructive and shadow expressions are required. Neither is a factual conclusion.
