Research summary

The vault-canonical map of firms and product lines that connect regulated money, tokenized assets, card credentials, merchant acceptance, identity, messaging, custody and settlement without becoming one unified ledger. The transparency extension shows that stablecoins can expose a public transfer record while reserves, beneficial ownership, direct redemption, complete cost, legal finality and remedy remain institutionally governed. The accounting extension separates signed business events, versioned rules, judgment objects and cash/GAAP/tax/regulatory views. A targeted September 3 refresh records OCC preliminary conditional approval for proposed World Liberty Trust Company, N.A.; the bank still cannot commence business before final approval, and its planned USD1 transfer from BitGo remains future-state.
