Research summary

Blockchains mostly threaten the middlemen whose job is copying messages, keeping duplicate records and reconciling breaks; they don't replace the institutions that supply money, credit, liquidity, legally enforceable netting, finality, custody or loss rules. Core public rails are already very cheap (FedACH works out to under a cent per item). The biggest hidden cost of instant settlement is losing netting: the clearinghouse NSCC turns about $2.2 trillion of daily trades into about $33.5 billion of actual payments, and one-for-one instant settlement would give that up.
