The federal restart found operating checks, joined bank orders and new legal limits

Treasury put a government-wide payment check into operation, regulators reached three American Express companies, GAO found two unlawful impoundments and the President opened a Federal Reserve removal inquiry.

What we found

  • Treasury said its payment-verification system screened 1.1 billion payment requests worth $3.7 trillion and returned 13,500 deceased-payee requests totaling $175 million. Those are screen/return measurements, not public transaction, fraud, recovery or audited-savings totals; agencies still own adjudication, notice, contest and reissue.
  • The Federal Reserve's order reaches American Express Company and American Express Travel Related Services Company; OCC separately ordered and penalized American Express National Bank. Group supervision and bank supervision remain different legal perimeters.
  • GAO found unlawful impoundment in the reviewed EPA and AHRQ cases, while each decision retained its own program, appropriation and factual record rather than creating one automatic government-wide rule.
  • The Lisa Cook committee of inquiry opened a procedure for investigating statutory cause. Paired 2026 Supreme Court decisions preserve the Fed Governor's exceptional cause protection while rejecting comparable protection for ordinary commissions; the committee can investigate and recommend but cannot itself remove.
  • The health-price transparency rule reached final-rule status, while SPR exchanges, the Vistra loan, Alaska energy action, sanctions and the GLM Components review retain later contract, payment, delivery, review and effect gates.

Why it matters

The direct agency sites show where policy became an operating check, entity-specific order or legal finding. They also show why an announcement, approval, inquiry or conditional commitment cannot be booked as completed delivery or effect.

What to watch

  • 2026-10-15 · Treasury and IRS determination and implementation guidance under EO 14435.
  • 2026-11-10 · Lisa Cook committee findings, cause determination or later removal/judicial step.
  • 2026-11-22 · Mexico's GLM Components review finding, remediation or customs result.
  • 2027-01-06 · American Express 90-day remediation-plan submission, acceptance and first operating result.
