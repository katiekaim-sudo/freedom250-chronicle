## 2017

- **2017-06-27 — Tenth Circuit splits three ways over Fourth Corner's account** (Kansas City · Occurred · Fed account access). The Tenth Circuit issued a fractured judgment in the cannabis credit union's master-account suit — one judge saw statutory entitlement, one would allow a lawful-purpose amendment, one rejected relief. No binding majority established an unconditional right to an account. The first crack of the gate question that Custodia later answered. (Judicial Watch JM-08; the Judicial Watch carries the courthouse-anchored chart.) Source: https://www.ca10.uscourts.gov/opinion/16-1016
