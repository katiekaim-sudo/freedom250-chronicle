# Inch by Inch: The Operational Refounding

America and the institutional order around it are undergoing an operational
refounding. The federal government is leading much of the change, but the
refounding reaches State and local governments, private companies, financial
networks, universities, standards bodies and international institutions.

The refounding is advancing inside existing institutions rather than through
one constitutional rupture or replacement system. Legal shells remain while
mission, staff, records, data, money, infrastructure, operating control, loss
and remedy are reassigned function by function. Public authority and private
capability are being recombined into a new operating order, inch by inch.

The change is driven by an institutional legitimacy crisis. Inherited authority
is no longer enough when institutions cannot carry out their purpose, explain
their decisions, correct mistakes or produce results that people will accept.
Institutions must absorb new technology and capacity while keeping power
answerable, records authoritative and remedies effective.

Freedom 250 believes this refounding is necessary and broadly for the best. The
opportunity is to build institutions that are more capable, transparent,
productive and correctable. Not every change will succeed or deserve support,
but the larger transition is the future being built around us through the
ordinary rules, systems, agreements and records that make power real.

Freedom 250 reads this period in mundane astrology as a necessary season of
restructuring: **as above, so below**.
