Research summary

Trump and Xi publicly established a leader-level framework in May for a constructive China-U.S. relationship of strategic stability. At the September 24 state dinner, Xi said that day's common understandings added substance and strategic guidance to the May framework; he did not announce a newly signed strategic-stability accord. No standalone bilateral instrument with defined obligations, signatures, entry into force, termination or implementation terms was located by cutoff. A separate Chinese meeting readout says the economic teams reached a new joint arrangement, but its controlling text, complete terms and U.S. confirmation were not public at cutoff.
