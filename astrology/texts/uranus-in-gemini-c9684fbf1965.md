## My interpretation

Prometheus enters the house of Information.

Every 84 years, Uranus returns to Gemini and *breaks open* the way humanity communicates, learns, and shares knowledge. The pattern is staggering:

- **1775–1782** — the American Revolution. Pamphlets (Common Sense), the Declaration of Independence, the idea that information can spark a revolution. The birth of a nation through the written word.
- **1858–1866** — the American Civil War. The transatlantic telegraph cable, mass-circulation newspapers, photography documenting war for the first time. Information crossing oceans instantaneously.
- **1941–1949** — World War II. Radio as mass propaganda and mass mobilization, code-breaking (Enigma/Turing), the atomic bomb (information = power = destruction), the founding of the United Nations. Information as the weapon that wins wars.
- **2025–2033** — now. AI, the information revolution, the doubling of information itself.

The Promethean Awakener in the Eternal Quest for Information. Breakthroughs and Liberation in Duality and Openness to the Unexpected. This is not a minor transit. This is civilization's communication system being torn apart and rebuilt every 84 years, and it's happening again.

The critical detail: Uranus enters Gemini in 2025 while forming a **trine to Pluto in Aquarius**. The last time Uranus and Pluto were in harmonious aspect from air signs was the 1960s sextile that produced the internet's conceptual foundations. Now it's a trine — the line of least resistance. The Promethean fire flows directly into Pluto's paradigm-shifting transformation. AI isn't knocking on the door. It's already inside the house.

And Gemini's Duality is the key. Every Uranus-in-Gemini era produces a *split* — two versions of reality running simultaneously. In 1776 it was loyalist vs. patriot. In 1862 it was Union vs. Confederacy. In 1942 it was Axis vs. Allies. In 2025 it's the Information War — legacy media vs. citizen media, official narrative vs. dissident narrative, curated AI vs. open-source AI. Gemini *is* duality. Uranus *is* the awakening. Together = the moment you realize there are two truths and you have to choose.
