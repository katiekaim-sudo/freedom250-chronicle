Japan is rewiring its money without replacing it

Who earns interest and holds the bonds is shifting, but the core institutions and final settlement stay put.

What we found

  • The BOJ's fiscal-2025 assets shrank by ¥66.742 trillion.
  • Interest on excess reserves more than doubled to ¥2.710 trillion.
  • A new bank joins the BOJ's current-account system on October 1.
