# Chart reading authoring template

Use this after the chart facts have been computed and verified. This is a
**voice and judgment template**, not a placement checklist. The chart must be
heard as one living whole before it is asked to illuminate a Freedom 250 state
transition.

## The order is binding

1. **Chart hearing first.** Begin with the structural whole: gestalt, governing
   climate, root, principal dialogue, and the question put to the sovereign
   core. Write one voice that can breathe. Do not lead with the method or a list
   of placements.
2. **Focused weather second.** For a lunation or eclipse, show how it carries,
   times, reveals, or interrupts the governing ingress. It does not invent an
   unrelated story.
3. **Astronomical context considered.** Check the physical arrangement,
   Earth distance/solar elongation when material, motion, declination and the
   geometric constraints behind chart shape. Narrate only what changes the
   hearing. The Orrery supplies the broad picture; the chart-grade engine owns
   exact zodiacal and local claims. For Mercury and Venus, distinguish signed
   solar side, elongation, illuminated fraction, local twilight altitude and
   modeled visibility; longitude separation alone does not establish that a
   planet can be seen. For an eclipse, keep exact syzygy, greatest-eclipse
   clock, global type/path, Washington-local type, magnitude/obscuration,
   true/apparent altitude, rise/set state and observable duration distinct.
   Also type every historical recurrence: **Saros** for the same 223-lunation
   shadow series, **Metonic** for the 235-lunation same-phase/date/degree echo,
   and **Moon Family** for the separate 273/546/819-day seed-phase line. Record
   when a Metonic phase return was not an eclipse. Never use “eclipse family”
   when more than one of these identities could be meant.
   Global totality is not a Washington visibility claim. Astronomy is context,
   not causal proof.
4. **Both poles and continuity.** Name the constructive use, the shadow use,
   and the possibility that the institutional state does not change.
5. **State Transition Watch last.** For a prospective reading, freeze a separate
   structured addendum. It is a lens for later comparison, not a revision of
   the chart prose and not a claim that the Freedom 250 thesis must be true.

## State Transition Watch — frozen addendum

The addendum asks **which legitimate decision right may be changing state**.
Answer each field from the chart hearing; never reverse-engineer the chart to
fit a desired political conclusion.

| Field | Required question |
|---|---|
| **Authority** | What legitimate decision right is in motion? |
| **Holder** | Who or what currently holds that right in law or operation? |
| **Claimant / constraint** | Who is trying to acquire, redirect, limit, expose, or bypass it? |
| **Candidate transition** | What is the precise **from → toward** change? |
| **Mechanism** | Through what named legal, fiscal, technological, coercive, informational, electoral, or institutional mechanism could it occur? |
| **Maturity** | Is the change merely **proposed**, or has it become **authorized, effective, operating, contested, reversed, failed, or unchanged**? Do not skip states. |
| **Poles** | What are the constructive pole, shadow pole, and continuity / unchanged case? |
| **Synchronization** | Which Freedom 250 arcs would have to move together for this to be a real system transition rather than one isolated headline? |
| **Evidence test** | What would count as **Lines up**, **Miss**, or **Unresolved** across Attention, Official, and Entity evidence? |

### Chart role changes the watch

- **Ingress / climate:** names the standing authority contest and the possible
  institutional direction for the full validity window.
- **Lunation / gate:** names the decision, disclosure, mobilization, or
  implementation gate through which the climate may become visible.
- **Quarter Moon / context chapter:** names implementation pressure at First
  Quarter or consequence/revision at Last Quarter. It is not a main-character
  chart unless a governing, root, angle, radix, long-clock or same-object story
  contact earns promotion.
- **Eclipse / discontinuity:** names the break, exposure, forced hand, or
  long-fuse reset that may reorder who can certify or act.
- **Entity clock:** tests whether an institution itself moved from founding
  authority into a new legal or operating phase. It does not borrow houses from
  an unrelated chart.

## Freezing and later comparison

- Existing frozen chart baselines are never edited to absorb this lens.
- A new watch is a timestamped, immutable, hash-pinned record bound to the
  baseline reading ID and SHA-256.
- Attention, Official, and Entity remain separate evidence lanes.
- A proposed action is not an authorized one; authorization is not an effective
  date; effectiveness is not operation.
- **Unchanged, failed, and reversed are real outcomes.** They are not defects in
  the reading.
- A rhyme is not a cause. Chart readings and watches receive no score. A literal
  event call belongs in the Forecast Ledger before its window.

The governing sequence is:

> **Chart → Authority → Candidate transition → Mechanism → Maturity → Counterpole → Evidence test.**
