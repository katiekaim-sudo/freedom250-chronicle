## Five factual object classes

### 1. Capability

A named model, agent or AI-enabled system must demonstrate a materially new
capability under a declared test. A qualifying receipt identifies the system
and version, the task, the comparison baseline, the dated result, and the
limits or failure conditions.

Developer adjectives, an undocumented demo, a generic release and a benchmark
claim without method do not establish this class.

### 2. Architecture and interoperability

Separately governed compute, data, model, identity, instrument or workflow
layers must become one documented callable architecture. The strongest receipt
is operational: an authorized external system submits a structured job, the
facility executes it, and a signed or otherwise authoritative result returns.

A partnership, reference architecture, shared cloud provider or planned
integration does not establish interoperability.

### 3. Institutional authority and adoption

A named institution must move a specific AI object from plan or pilot toward
authorized, deployed or accepted operation. Authority, accountable owner,
funding, obligation, authorization to operate, acceptance, monitoring, remedy
and termination remain separate.

An AI inventory row is not capability. A solicitation is not an award. An
award is not delivery. A pilot is not accepted operation.

### 4. Physical and network deployment

The enabling compute, power, data-center, laboratory or edge network must cross
a real project or operating gate. Land and power instruments, project-specific
authorization, construction, commissioning and operation are separate clocks,
and the capacity must be connected to the named AI workload.

Site selection, lease negotiation, FAST-41 coverage, an equipment order or a
capacity announcement does not establish operation.

### 5. Control, records and public consequence

The system must acquire an inspectable control, record, audit, remedy or
measured consequence. This lane can be constructive or difficult: public
service, accepted performance, an incident, suspension, audit, appeal,
termination or documented burden can all be real returns.

Assumed surveillance, unsourced user counts and vendor-reported benefits
treated as independent effects do not qualify.

## What would make the AI reading line up

Every condition below is required:

1. One named system or explicitly documented stack keeps stable identity across
   its receipts.
2. At least three object classes are established. One must be institutional
   authority/adoption, and one must be architecture/interoperability or
   physical/network deployment.
3. At least two genuine state transitions occur. Repeating the same
   announcement in new language is not a transition.
4. At least one qualifying receipt occurs on or after the September 26 Full
   Moon and before the December 9 evaluation close.
5. The November return supplies operation, interoperability, disclosure,
   control, failure or measured consequence—not publicity alone.
6. Every factual claim is established in the independent Research lane before
   it is compared with the astrology.

The result need not be benevolent. A captured switchboard, failed deployment,
termination, public disclosure or system breakdown may fit the chart's shadow
more honestly than a successful product launch. Negative evidence stays in.

## Superintelligence is a separate claim

The stronger thesis does not inherit a win from ordinary AI buildout.

For “superintelligence” to become more than an evocative name, the record would
need all of the following:

- a named system with stable version identity;
- broad cross-domain capability materially beyond the declared frontier
  comparison;
- method-bearing independent, governmental, operator or equivalent validation
  rather than developer rhetoric alone;
- demonstrated transfer or generalization outside one narrow task;
- a documented operating or institutional consequence; and
- enough published limitations and failure conditions to make the claim
  testable.

AGI language, one benchmark record, a larger training run, a compute purchase,
a capital raise, a valuation, a stock move, narrow automation or deployment
scale without a new capability threshold are automatic non-receipts.

The honest later sentence may therefore be:

> **The Basket lined up with the architecture and institutional deployment of
> AI, but the public record did not establish superintelligence.**

That is not a lesser finding. It is the boundary that keeps the exciting idea
from swallowing the evidence.

## How it can fail

The thesis misses or weakens materially if:

- AI activity remains abundant but fragments into unrelated objects;
- architecture and partnerships multiply without an interoperability or
  operating receipt;
- adoption remains at strategy, inventory, solicitation, award or pilot;
- physical capacity is announced but never authorized, commissioned or tied to
  a named workload;
- the November chapter yields no material network, disclosure, operating,
  control or failure return;
- a candidate reverses or terminates and the reading quietly drops it; or
- the AI-buildout test lines up but the stronger superintelligence threshold
  does not.

AI is a high-volume news category. Many headlines are the base rate, not a
signal. A crowd of weak objects cannot substitute for one traceable mature
chain.
