# Reading the charter — SWIFT

## Boundary first

SWIFT is a member-owned financial-messaging cooperative and critical service provider. Its public ledger material expressly says SWIFT is not the money issuer, custodian, settlement agent or financial market infrastructure. The shared ledger must therefore be read as an operator/orchestration/notary service around bank liabilities and external settlement—not as a SWIFT deposit or central-bank-money system.

## Primary-source shelf

### Belgian statute

[Law of 25 May 2025 on the supervision of providers of financial messaging services, no. 2025004515](https://refli.be/fr/lex/2025004515)  
Official ELI identifier: `2025/05/25/2025004515`.

Relevant article map:

- Article 3(4) — financial-messaging service perimeter and related operational/ancillary services;
- Articles 40–41 — outsourcing responsibility, cooperation and prior NBB authorisation for critical/important functions;
- Article 46 — legal clarity, enforceability and conflicts of law;
- Articles 47–48 — participant risk incentives, recovery and orderly wind-down;
- Article 71 — public access, refusal, suspension and orderly-exit criteria;
- Article 73 — public key rules/procedures, service descriptions, rights/obligations, documentation and fees.
