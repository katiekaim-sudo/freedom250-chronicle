`embodied test → exact informational and legal hinge → foundational consequence`

## Source-separated maturation: sandbox, law, licence and operation are different clocks

The factual object was selected independently from the chart. It remains in
the Workbench's International Crypto Map under a September 15, 2026 primary-
source cutoff. No event is given an exact time where the official record gives
only a date.

The stablecoin regime develops through four distinct states:

1. **Before the Nov. 1, 2024 seed — sandbox test:** HKMA had admitted three
   participants in July 2024. They could test intended business models and
   receive supervisory feedback. Admission was expressly not endorsement,
   qualification or a future licence.
2. **Dec. 6, 2024 to May 21, 2025 — legislation:** the Stablecoins Bill was
   gazetted, scrutinized and passed. A bill and then an enacted ordinance
   created the rule architecture; neither named a licensed issuer or launched
   a token.
3. **Aug. 1, 2025 — exact First-Quarter date:** the Stablecoins Ordinance and
   supervisory framework commenced. HKMA began accepting applications. Legal
   effectiveness was not an issuer licence.
4. **Apr. 10, 2026 — 21 days before Full:** HKMA granted FRS01 to Anchorpoint
   Financial Limited and FRS02 to The Hongkong and Shanghai Banking Corporation
   Limited. The release said preparations and business launch were to follow in
   the coming months. Licence status was not proof of a circulating coin.

The First Quarter is the factual hinge as clearly as it is the chart hinge. The
seed degree returns within 3.6625 arcminutes on the exact date the legal regime
becomes operative. But the appropriate claim is narrow: **the rules switch on**.
No issuer licence, token contract, circulating balance, reserve portfolio,
customer use or redemption follows automatically from commencement.

By Full phase, the legal foundation has changed again. Two named legal persons
now hold licences. That is a genuine maturation, not a proposal. Yet the
negative control remains decisive:

`sandbox ≠ licence ≠ product launch ≠ circulation ≠ successful redemption`

Later May 2026 SFC–HKMA rules created a regulated intermediary framework for an
authorised “Relevant Stablecoin.” They still did not prove that either issuer
had launched a public token, funded its reserves, achieved liquidity or

This is an unusually exact factual companion for the chart's Venus–Saturn
