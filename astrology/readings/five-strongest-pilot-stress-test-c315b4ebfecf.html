Five Strongest Pilot Stress Test

Disposition: proposal_for_review
Factual cutoff: 2026-08-30
Astrological evidence or forecast credit: 0
Purpose: test whether adding a conjunction persona changes a factual question after the relationship clock and governing chart have already earned the pair

Answer first

The five pilots do not all survive as equally strong persona cases. That is a useful result, not a failure.

  • The conjunction family and its exact-pass charts hold up across all five. They consistently add condition, house, governance, pass chronology, and aspect structure to the parent relationship clock.
  • Venus–Saturn 2025 is the clearest persona-level discriminator. It separates promise, record, held value, counterparty obligation, and enforceability in a way that can reject plausible but wrong monetary-story matches.
  • Mercury–Mars 2025–26 and Mercury–Neptune 2025 remain useful, but their apparent novelty is partly dependent on adjacent governing events. Their personas improve the questions only conditionally.
  • Venus–Mars 2024 supplies genuine focal separation, but later governing charts already state much of the distinction. It is better as a question-forming lens than as a demonstrated storyline detector.
  • Mars–Pluto 2026 has strong differentiated anatomy, but the persona-level storyline result is unresolved. The future-derived persona coordinate is anatomy only, not a 2027 event window.

The practical finding is therefore:

> Keep the conjunction chart as a normal relationship-clock layer. Admit a persona only when it survives an adjacency-dependency audit and changes the factual partition. Do not assume that the most visually differentiated persona is the most independently informative one.

Pilot 1 — Venus–Mars 2024

Seed-only question

Venus and Mars are fused in Aquarius/3rd with Pluto, opposed by the Moon and squared by Jupiter. Value, alliance, diplomacy, action, conflict, message, transport, and orders initially share one carrier under political and institutional pressure.

Persona delta

The persona separates exalted Venus in Pisces/11th from retrograde, fallen Mars in Cancer/3rd. Both receive tight derived-MC contacts. This is real focal differentiation: public compact, alliance, and value can be asked separately from revised orders, rhetoric, transport, or operational force.

Stress result

The distinction is not independently strong enough to grant plot-selection credit.

  • The 2025 Aries ingress follows the persona by only 53 days and repeats much of the same Venus/Mars condition sequence.
  • The 2025 Libra ingress supplies its own visible-Venus/hidden-Mars distinction.
  • On 2026-01-03, five days before the successor family, the governing Full Moon brings Venus and exalted Mars back together in Capricorn/2nd, applying to conjunction by 2.294°. The chart itself already says value and action share a resource decision.
  • Retrospective Iran records contain both military action and diplomacy, but that is the broad Venus–Mars archetype. Their coexistence does not prove that the persona found a unique mechanism.

Disposition: restates_seed_or_current_chart for storyline selection; moderate question-forming value. The persona separation is an available lens, not a permanent family law.

Falsifier: if any diplomacy-versus-force episode is counted without showing that the alliance/value carrier and the order/operations carrier are factually distinct, the persona adds no discrimination.

Pilot 2 — Mercury–Mars 2025–26

Seed-only question

The five-pass family asks whether a message, record, order, transport, or trade object becomes executable action, enforcement, deployment, or conflict. State transition—not rhetoric about action—is the factual test.

Persona delta

The persona places both planets in Sagittarius/3rd while Mercury stations retrograde and is in detriment and Mars remains direct. Mars receives exact derived-angle emphasis. The proposed refinement is: the record or order is revised while action continues.

Adjacency finding

The candidate occurs only 1.630 days before P2, a retrograde Mercury–Mars recontact. Much of the apparent revision/continuation contrast is therefore dependent on the approaching same-family pass. It is not an independent second revelation.

The pass chronology remains highly useful:

  • P3 is exact 12.19 hours before the 2026-01-18 Capricorn New Moon, with both planets direct and Mars exalted.
  • P4 occurs 3.719 days before the 2026-03-19 Pisces New Moon and cleanly restores Mercury-retrograde/Mars-direct divergence.
  • P5 follows the 2026-04-17 Aries New Moon by 3.412 days, conjunct Saturn and in the 7th.

This proves why the family must outrank the persona. Revision is a chapter inside the family, not its permanent rule.

Disposition: conditional_increment. The relationship clock and pass chronology are strong; the persona has medium incremental yield after dependency is removed.

Falsifier: rhetoric, leaks, headlines, or a revised statement without a changed executable state is not a hit. If “Mercury revises while Mars continues” is applied when the live pass has both planets direct, the persona has overwritten the family and failed.

Pilot 3 — Mercury–Neptune 2025

Seed-only question

Debilitated Mercury rules the Gemini-rising seed and is fused with Neptune in late Pisces/10th. The controlling record and collective image are joined. The factual task is to distinguish evidence, inspiration, confusion, propaganda, and verification without treating “misinformation” as a sufficient match.

Persona delta

The persona separates retrograde Mercury in Aries/4th from Neptune in Pisces/3rd and places Mercury within 0.291° of the derived MC relationship. It sharpens a useful question: can the source record be revised independently of the public or collective image?

Adjacency finding

The candidate occurs only 1.053 days before the 2025 Aries ingress, which contains nearly the same Mercury-retrograde/Sun–Neptune transit pack. Neptune also remains only 0.624° from the target. P2 then re-fuses retrograde Mercury and Neptune, and P3 re-fuses them direct.

The persona therefore describes a temporary differentiation and a verification discipline. It cannot be credited for information already present in the governing ingress or the next exact passes. The 2025 persona also stops at the 2026-04-17 successor; the 2026-09-11 opposition belongs to the new family.

Disposition: conditional_increment, low-to-medium. It improves the verification question more reliably than it selects a plot.

Falsifier: a broad narrative episode without a discrete claim, controlling document, correction, named user, repeated procedure, or measurable verification object is not a persona-level hit.

Pilot 4 — Venus–Saturn 2025

Seed-only question

Exalted Venus and Saturn are fused in Pisces/7th under Virgo rising. Mercury rules the chart and actual MC and sextiles both focals within 0.371°. The seed already describes a pact, alliance, obligation, or counterparty relationship whose terms and record are built into the agreement.

Persona delta

The persona separates retrograde, detrimented Venus in Aries/3rd from Saturn in Pisces/2nd. The useful distinction is not generic austerity. It is the difference among:

  • the promised value;
  • the stated or revised term;
  • the asset, liability, or obligation actually held;
  • the counterparty and institution able to enforce it.

This is a revision chapter: later P2 and P3 re-fuse Venus and Saturn. The separation cannot be imposed on the whole family.

Stress result

The 2025 Capricorn ingress is the strongest later test. Venus–Saturn is in a 0.502° separating waning square and is nameable through Saturn as ruler/root. The governing reading already asks whether an agreement about value is an auditable commitment or desire standing in for one; the persona further partitions promise, record, custody, and enforceable obligation.

The 2026-01-18 New Moon adds binding terms, pooled resources, debt, and liability. The 2026-03-03 eclipse approaches the successor conjunction and its retrospective comparison preserves exact states such as proposal, approval, limited account, and operation. Those are strong craft tests, but the comparison was authored after the facts and earns no prediction credit.

Disposition: adds_discrimination — the strongest of the five, still retrospective. It narrows monetary and Crypto material from “institutionalized value” to custody, liability, enforceability, and the record carrying the pact.

Falsifier: price movement, a proposal, an application, a wrapper, or a public promise without a separately identifiable held asset, binding term, counterparty duty, or operating rail does not satisfy the persona.

Pilot 5 — Mars–Pluto 2026

Seed-only question

Mars and Pluto are joined in Aquarius/7th inside a Sun–Mercury–Venus stellium. Force and concentrated power initially share an open-counterpart structure: opponent, alliance, treaty, bargain, or publicly contested relationship. The continuity case is intense language or inherited pressure without changed authorization, force disposition, custody, or power holder.

Persona delta

The derived persona separates retrograde Mars in Virgo/3rd from Pluto in Aquarius/8th; Mars rules the persona 10th and opposes the derived MC within 0.246°. After carryover is removed, the useful two-part mechanism is:

  1. a named order, record, logistics, communication, filing, or execution channel; and
  2. a changed custody, shared-resource, liability, forfeiture, seizure, or concentrated-power body.

The Persona Sun repeating the seed and Pluto remaining close to Aquarius are not new evidence. The 2027 persona timestamp is not an activation date.

Stress result

The negative record matters most. The live Winter comparison marked the 2026-02-01 Full Moon a Miss; the later Iran executive order remained only a loose thematic echo. The exact Mars–Pluto seed cannot rescue that miss. During Aries, Iran dominated Attention while verified Official evidence centered monetary implementation and the war itself was inherited before the ingress. Attention volume is not a Mars–Pluto state transition.

The persona is nevertheless structurally promising for distinguishing:

  • military action from an order or logistics change that transfers control of a strategic resource;
  • legal publicity from a filing or enforcement act that changes custody, forfeiture, or institutional power;
  • generic sanctions or price movement from an operating mechanism that changes control of held/shared assets.

Disposition: unresolved. Strong added anatomy; no demonstrated persona-level storyline success yet.

Falsifier: generic force without a custody/power transition, or a custody change without a named execution mechanism, is not a persona-level hit.

Prospective observation locks

These locks were written on 2026-08-30 before the named windows. They generate no forecast claim and name no expected event.

Lock A — Mercury–Mars at the 2026 Libra-ingress Full Moon

Parent geometry: 2026-09-26 Aries Full Moon under the Libra ingress; Mercury–Mars applying square, 4.363°, lawfully nameable through root and exact U.S.-natal contact.

Seed-only evidence question: does a named message, order, record, transport, trade, intelligence, or specification object become executable action, enforcement, deployment, integration, or conflict?

Persona removal test: does adding the persona separately reveal a revision to the controlling record while execution continues? If the window shows execution with no record revision, the relationship family may hold while the persona increment does not. If it shows revision with no executable change, neither is counted as a completed transition.

Reject: rhetoric, leak, headline, exploratory statement, or attention spike without a changed executable state.

Lock B — Mars–Pluto at the 2026 Libra-ingress opposition

Parent geometry: Mars–Pluto opposition across the 2026-09-23 Libra ingress and 2026-09-26 Aries Full Moon; 6.226° at ingress and 3.992° applying at the Full Moon, with root and exact U.S.-natal nameability at the lunation.

Seed-only evidence question: does force visibly confront or redistribute concentrated power inside a named counterpart relationship?

Persona removal test: does the record contain both a named execution channel and a changed custody, liability, seized/held resource, institutional controller, or coercive power body? One side without the other is a family-level or unrelated match, not persona discrimination.

Reject: attention-only drama, threat, strike headline, generic sanctions language, price movement, proposal, application, or test without a verified operating or legal-state change.

For both locks, the same fixed window and the same Attention, Official, and Entity evidence corpus must be used for seed-only and seed-plus-persona review. Another planetary pair wins when it asks the more precise question.

Method consequence

The five-pilot test adds one required restraint to the proposed method:

> A persona hearing must disclose its temporal distance from the next exact conjunction pass and from the next governing ingress or lunation. Close adjacency does not invalidate the persona, but it downgrades any claim that the persona supplied independent novelty.

That guard explains why Mercury–Mars and Mercury–Neptune looked stronger in anatomy than they proved under a fixed comparison. It also protects later passes and governing charts from being silently credited to the persona.
