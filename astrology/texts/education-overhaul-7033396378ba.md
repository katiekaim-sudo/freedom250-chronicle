# Schools, students and the education overhaul

*Who decides how schools and student aid work as federal services shift toward states, and do students benefit?*

## The braid so far

This story is larger than the [university and science-money braid](universities-science-money.md). The [Chronicle's Department of Education legal map](<../../../02%20-%20Research/Federal%20Group%20Control/Department%20of%20Education%20%E2%80%94%20Consummation%20Map.md>), evidence through August 15, says the Department still exists under 20 U.S.C. § 3411. Executive closure direction, staff reductions and interagency service agreements can change who does work without repealing that charter. The March 2026 ED–Treasury agreement bought collection services; it did not transfer title to the student-loan book. A court stay is not a merits holding that the Department was abolished.

The [State Learning Agenda Playbook study](<Research-Workbench/Research Packages/Education/Learning Agenda Playbook 2026-09-09/LEARNING_AGENDA_PLAYBOOK_INSTITUTIONAL_ANALYSIS_2026-09-09.md>), evidence through September 9, finds a voluntary method for State education agencies to tie questions and evidence to programme and budget decisions. Pilot cases show changed planning and coordination, **not** demonstrated student gains or a legal transfer of federal authority. The [Education router](<Research-Workbench/Research Packages/Education/README.md>) keeps school/State planning, higher-education funding, research security and student-loan administration on their own clocks.

The forward question is whether a State or local decision maker actually receives power, staff, records and money to act, uses credible evidence to change a school or aid programme, and shows a student result that survives the next leadership change. The constructive route is plural State capacity with visible reasons and remedy; the shadow is a hollow federal shell and many new plans while students, teachers and borrowers cannot tell who is accountable. Ordinary decentralization and uneven local improvement remain strong rivals to a single national overhaul.

This braid begins **without authored planetary strands**. The 2027 authority/action bridge, 2028 care/record split, 2029 price and 2030 working-bargain sky are forward questions in [Capacity or Spectacle](../Story%20Rooms/capacity-or-spectacle-2027-30-forecast-route.md), not an automatic education forecast. An exact school, aid or State-capacity receipt must first name its actor, instrument, student consequence and review gate; then the lawful chart may earn a bounded overlap. Social posts about campuses or classroom politics remain attention, not a measured education result.
