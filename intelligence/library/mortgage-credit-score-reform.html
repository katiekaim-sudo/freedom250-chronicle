Credit approval rules are shifting, but not all toward easier lending

Lenders now have more approved scoring models and more kinds of proof they can use, and some fixed checks are gone, but these changes move judgment and work around rather than plainly lowering the bar. Mortgages run on their own rules, separate from retail and small-business credit.

What we found

  • Fannie Mae dropped its 620 minimum credit score for loans run through its automated system (DU) for new files from November 16, 2025. Fannie says DU does its own risk check and expected almost no change in approval volume.
  • Under the July 2026 joint Fannie/Freddie playbook, participating lenders can use Classic FICO or VantageScore 4.0; FICO 10T is still future. A September 3-4 direction to open VantageScore to all lenders is reported, but official operating papers checked September 7 still had the older limited-rollout wording.
  • Fannie reports more than 21,000 applications got a better DU result from rent payments in bank statements between September 2021 and May 2026. That counts improved recommendations, not closed loans or new homeowners.
  • Fannie cut the standard W-2 ask from two years to the latest W-2 plus a current paystub (mandatory for applications from June 1, 2026) and removed the fixed 10% prefunding quality-check sample (from July 1, 2026), leaving lenders to design their own sampling.
  • FHA announced on April 22 that it would allow new score models, but on May 21 told lenders to keep current rules until implementation dates are set. The study's summary also reports lender-by-lender retail automation and SBA replacing its old SBSS screening score with explicit repayment analysis.

Why it matters

This is the procedural pattern in the thesis: credit standards are being rewritten through agency guides, model approvals and quality-control rules inside the existing system, not by repealing lending law. Who carries judgment and risk shifts, while legal ability-to-repay duties stay in place.

What to watch

  • pending · FHA's actual implementation dates and guidance for VantageScore 4.0 and FICO 10T.
  • pending · Updated Fannie/Freddie/FHFA operating papers that match the September all-lender VantageScore direction.
  • pending · Agency rules carrying out the March 13, 2026 executive order on mortgage credit, and lender-level outcome data.
