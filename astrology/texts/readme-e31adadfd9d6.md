This package rebuilds the persona experiment as one governed collection for
all ten canonical Long Clock conjunction families. Each family contributes one
shared persona derived from its registered `display_anchor_pass_id`. The two
conjunct planets are read as two focal lenses inside that single subsidiary
chart; they are never two charts or two pieces of corroboration.

The clickable collection is [charts/index.html](charts/index.html). It is
self-contained so Research Desk can stage it without chart sidecars. Select a
family to inspect its exact seed, persona wheel, seed–persona biwheel,
calculation receipt, pass family, dependency boundary, and hearing summary.
