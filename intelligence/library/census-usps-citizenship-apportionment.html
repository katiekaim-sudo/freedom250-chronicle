Research summary

The test establishes a scalable postal-enumerator channel inside a Census environment whose online questionnaire includes citizenship, but it does not establish one joined enforcement database or that postal workers ask the citizenship question. Census now confirms the Huntsville and Spartanburg field test concluded August 31; no response, cost, quality, productivity, USPS-comparison or 2030 production-decision result is yet published. The unpublished interagency agreement and shorter field instrument remain decisive missing objects.
