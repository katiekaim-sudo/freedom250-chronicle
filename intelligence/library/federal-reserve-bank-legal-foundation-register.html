Federal Reserve Bank Legal Foundation Register

Congress supplied the corporate form in the Federal Reserve Act; in 1914 five designated subscribing banks per district executed an organization certificate whose filing with the Comptroller made each Reserve Bank a body corporate; a separate Comptroller authorization let it commence business; bylaws are subordinate internal rules. Only the St. Louis certificate was recovered directly; the gap is custody, not non-formation.
