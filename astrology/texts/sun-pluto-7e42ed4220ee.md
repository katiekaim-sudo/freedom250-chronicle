# Sun–Pluto

## The planets

**Sun** — *Authority, illumination, "everything revolves around the Sun."* MUNDANE: the head of government (the President); the executive branch; the focus of executive power. The Sun's condition shows how the government will fare. (Greer: "the Sun represents the actual head of government — whoever officially calls the shots in the executive branch.")

**Pluto** — *Transformation, death and rebirth, the underworld, plutocratic power.* MUNDANE: organized crime; intelligence agencies; plutocratic elites; nuclear power and weapons; mass extinctions of institutions or norms; whatever is hidden, buried, or operating in the shadows. *Note: Greer is skeptical of Pluto's status as a major mundane significator and does not provide a specific delineation for Sun-Pluto. The interpretation below extends his framework cautiously.*

## The combination — mundane

Under helpful aspects (sextile, trine), the executive branch successfully confronts or harnesses transformative and plutocratic forces. The President gains power by exposing corruption, reforming intelligence agencies, or channeling the nation's deep structural energies constructively. Investigations into organized crime or financial fraud produce results. Nuclear diplomacy advances. The government appears to have command over forces that normally operate in the shadows — a rare and potent position.

Under hostile aspects (conjunction, square, opposition), the executive is drawn into a power struggle with forces that operate beneath the surface of ordinary politics — plutocratic elites, intelligence agencies, or organized criminal networks. Corruption scandals erupt that implicate the highest levels of government. The President appears either complicit in hidden power structures or overwhelmed by them. Nuclear confrontations or existential policy crises force the government into zero-sum thinking. The tone is not Neptunian confusion but Plutonian exposure — the ugly machinery beneath the state becomes visible, and the public is forced to reckon with it.

Because Greer does not specifically delineate this pair, treat Sun-Pluto readings as provisional and weight them less heavily than pairs Greer addresses directly.

**As trigger:** Sun-Pluto aspects are fast-moving (days to about two weeks) and mark the specific moments when plutocratic power, hidden forces, and transformative crises activate whatever broader outer-planet storylines are in play.
