Government · Executive orders

What has actually happened after an executive order was signed?

Only 4 of the 509 orders on the record have any follow-through written up in the library's confirmed list of changes: 7 records on EO 14178, EO 14215, EO 14395, EO 14431. 3 deadlines set by EO 14395 have passed with no public record of the deliverables. For the other orders the library has no dated record of what happened next, which is not the same as nothing happening.

Evidence and source scope

Saved graphic evidence: September 23, 2026. The October 22 calendar marker and elapsed order deadlines are not a new revocation or implementation check. The linked movement registry has its own October 12 cutoff and October 13 correction; those dates do not refresh every order.

What's still uncertain

  • The confirmed list of changes is small on purpose: a record joins only with a named actor, a dated official record, a stage and a limit on what it proves. Other orders may have follow-through the library has read about but not written up.
  • A public non-finding (for example EO 14395's deliverables) does not prove the agencies never sent them internally.
  • EO 14431 set a 30-day start gate for the Wage and Hour review; counted from signing (September 18, 2026) that falls on October 18, 2026, which has passed with no recorded start. The master records the gate as text, not as a date.
