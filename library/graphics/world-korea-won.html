Freedom 250 · Foreign relations and world money · Korea

Where do Korea's won stablecoin and Han River project stand?

The won stablecoin still has no law: the Korea country file says that as of October 3, 2026 South Korea had no enacted general public-won stablecoin regime, and that a future won stablecoin's issuer, reserves and redemption rules were unresolved. In the world money record, the Digital Asset Basic Act was introduced June 10, 2025, stalled December 30, 2025 over who may issue the token, the government proposed its own bill April 8, 2026, and on May 12, 2026 it was left off the National Policy Committee's last agenda before recess. The Bank of Korea paused Phase 2 of Project Han River (Hangang), its wholesale CBDC and tokenized-deposit trial with the banks, on June 26, 2025, and relaunched it March 18, 2026 with nine banks, retooled around tokenizing government disbursements; on July 1, 2026 the Bank floated growing it into foreign-bond investment infrastructure; and on July 15, 2026 its Phase II designation was expanded, with Kyongnam and iM Bank newly designated. The country file treats Hangang tokens as commercial-bank deposit claims in a controlled pilot; no public-won issuer or production wholesale CBDC was established.
