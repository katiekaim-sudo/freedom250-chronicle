Who issues nine dollar stablecoins, what backs them, and what the GENIUS Act requires

Nine dollar tokens, what each issuer promises today, and five legal paths in the statute

The GENIUS Act does not make a blockchain or wallet legal. It makes a specific issuer, with specific reserves and terms, eligible to issue in the U.S. Later, it lets U.S. platforms keep distributing that issuer's coin. Existing payment companies and integrations can keep operating through the transition. But the token they carry must meet the issuer, reserve, redemption and lawful-order requirements.

The two-stage statutory switch

18 Jul 2025GENIUS enacted. Present charters and licenses continue to be the current authority.
General effective dateEarlier of 18 Jan 2027 or 120 days after final implementing regulations.
At effectivenessU.S. issuance becomes a permitted-issuer activity; non-permitted coins lose specified institutional money treatment.
18 Jul 2028U.S. service providers may carry only permitted coins or coins satisfying the foreign exception.

USD₮ Foreign issuer Live, with independent attestations Tether Live worldwide at large scale · quarterly BDO attestation

Tether's main coin holds gold and bitcoin, which U.S. law does not allow as reserves

Who owes the dollar?Tether International, S.A. de C.V. issues USD₮ under authorization from El Salvador's National Commission of Digital Assets (CNAD). It is also a digital-asset service provider (DASP) and the party customers redeem with. Tether Operations S.A. de C.V. is a separate entity for the owner, brand and website roles.
What backs it?Mostly Treasuries, but broader than the GENIUS list. The Q1 2026 disclosure includes about $141 billion of direct and indirect Treasury-bill exposure, about $20 billion of physical gold and about $7 billion of Bitcoin, plus other assets.
GENIUS gateForeign-issuer path: a finding that the home regime is comparable, registration with the Office of the Comptroller of the Currency (OCC), liquidity reserves for U.S. customers and the ability to comply with lawful orders. The reserve mix is a further, substantive hurdle.
open the stablecoin constitution
Issuer obligation

Tether reports token-related liabilities and reserves that exceed them. Direct redemption runs through eligible Tether customer relationships.

Reserve fit

Gold and Bitcoin are not permitted reserve types under GENIUS. So the current portfolio cannot be assumed to meet the Act without restructuring, segregation or a different product.

Custody / segregation

The issuer's regulatory document says reserves sit with several third-party banks and licensed financial institutions, not one public custodian. It does not establish the U.S.-located liquidity reserve that the foreign exception requires.

Primary redemption

Only customers Tether approves can redeem directly. Most holders use exchanges, market makers and wallets.

Distribution surface

USD₮ has the world's largest stablecoin distribution network. That is its strength, and it is what the 2028 rule for U.S. intermediaries puts at risk.

Required structural move

Tether can qualify USD₮ through the foreign-issuer path with reserves that meet GENIUS. Or it can shift U.S. distribution to USA₮ while USD₮ stays global.

18 July 2028

U.S. wallets and exchanges cannot offer or sell the coin to U.S. persons after July 18, 2028 unless the foreign exception is met.

Treasury channel

USD₮ is already a major offshore source of demand for U.S. Treasuries. GENIUS makes continued U.S. distribution depend on placing the issuer and its reserves and terms under supervision that U.S. law recognizes.

Do not overclaim

Reserves larger than liabilities is a different test. GENIUS asks whether every reserve asset is on its permitted list.

USDC State-qualified issuer Live, with independent attestations USD Coin Live with current issuer · planned move to a New York issuer · federal trust bank for reserves

Circle plans to move USDC to a new New York issuer and its reserves to a new federal trust bank

Who owes the dollar?Circle Internet Financial, LLC is still the issuer outside the European Economic Area (EEA). Circle told the Office of the Comptroller of the Currency (OCC) it plans to move USDC issuance to a separate company. That company is Circle Internet Trust Company, LLC, a proposed New York limited-purpose trust. Circle Internet Financial Europe SAS remains the EEA issuer.
What backs it?Highly liquid dollar reserves, held separately for holders: bank deposits, short-dated Treasuries and overnight Treasury repo. Most are held through the Circle Reserve Fund, managed by BlackRock.
GENIUS gateCircle's stated plan is a real change of issuer, to Circle Internet Trust Company, LLC. That company would then seek approval as a State-qualified PPSI under a New York regime certified under GENIUS. Using today's issuer, CIF LLC, as a federal nonbank issuer is only a fallback if the plan changes.
open the stablecoin constitution
Issuer obligation

Eligible Circle Mint customers can create and redeem USDC 1:1. A holder gets a direct claim only by becoming an eligible Mint customer. Individuals generally get USDC through wallets, exchanges and neobanks.

Reserve fit

The asset types closely match the GENIUS Act's list. But no public determination exists that the reserves meet the rules for a permitted payment stablecoin issuer (PPSI). Circle's own disclosure is still the evidence.

Custody / segregation

BlackRock manages the Circle Reserve Fund, and BNY is the primary reserve custodian. Circle National Trust is designed to manage reserves, act as collateral trustee and hold digital assets in the future. It expressly does not issue USDC.

Primary redemption

Direct 1:1 redemption is for institutions, through Circle Mint. Everyday holders normally sell or redeem through an intermediary.

Distribution surface

Wallets, exchanges, banks, card networks and payment processors already distribute USDC widely. The companies that connect USDC to these systems are already established.

Required structural move

Circle Internet Trust needs a charter and stablecoin approval from the New York State Department of Financial Services (NYDFS). It also needs the required GENIUS state certification and issuer approval. Circle must then sign an assignment and assumption or new terms. Token administration, reserve-control agreements and attestations must move to the new issuer.

18 July 2028

If the issuer becomes permitted, U.S. digital-asset service providers can keep offering the coin. Existing integrations do not by themselves meet the issuer requirement.

Treasury channel

USDC already places the money behind circulating coins in deposits, Treasury bills and Treasury repo. GENIUS would standardize this flow from private dollar tokens into government debt.

Do not overclaim

Circle National Trust is not the planned issuer. Circle Internet Trust Company, LLC is a separate proposed New York company. We found no operative novation (legal transfer of the obligations) and no New York approval.

Issuer obligation

Paxos manages issuance and redemption. Paxos states that PYUSD is fully backed and redeemable 1:1 for U.S. dollars.

Reserve fit

The disclosed reserve types closely match the GENIUS list. Exact maturity, custody, diversification and operating requirements still await final rules and supervisory review.

Custody / segregation

Primary redemption

Treasury channel

PYUSD turns PayPal-scale wallet balances into demand for cash, Treasury instruments and Treasury-backed liquidity.

Do not overclaim

Paxos's current authority is its national trust-bank charter. That is not a completed GENIUS approval as a PPSI.

Issuer obligation

Paxos sells and redeems USDG 1:1. Reserves are held in segregated accounts.

Reserve fit

The reserve assets appear to match closely. But GENIUS also tests the foreign regulatory regime, a U.S. liquidity reserve and registration, not only the portfolio.

Custody / segregation

Distribution surface

Treasury channel

Do not overclaim

SoFi's bank issues SOFID itself, and the law requires a separate subsidiary

Who owes the dollar?SoFi Bank, N.A. says it issues SOFID directly. The bank is an insured depository institution (IDI) regulated by the Office of the Comptroller of the Currency (OCC).

open the stablecoin constitution
Issuer obligation

SoFi says SOFID is intended to be redeemable 1:1. It states plainly that SOFID is not a deposit, not FDIC-insured, not bank-guaranteed and may lose value.

Reserve fit

Cash is the simplest permitted reserve type. The harder question is which legal entity issues the coin.

Custody / segregation

SoFi's bank systems control issuance, app distribution and reserve operations. The public product record does not identify a separate approved issuer subsidiary.

Primary redemption

SoFi provides 1:1 conversion for eligible users and partners.

Distribution surface

The SoFi app, enterprise services and potential white-label integrations with banks and fintechs.

Required structural move

SoFi would move or novate (legally transfer) issuance, redemption and the reserve obligation into an approved SoFi Bank subsidiary. SoFi's terms already let it name a substitute issuer and assign obligations in response to new stablecoin rules.

18 July 2028

The SoFi app can stay the wallet. But the token in it must be issued by the permitted entity.

Treasury channel

A mostly cash reserve relies on Federal Reserve and bank liquidity rather than Treasury-bill yield. The final reserve mix can change.

Do not overclaim

This is an inference from the law's structure. It is not a finding that SoFi's current issuance is unlawful before GENIUS takes effect.

The product is expressly designed for the GENIUS era. But final approval as a permitted payment stablecoin issuer (PPSI) and legal reserve compliance are still future steps.
