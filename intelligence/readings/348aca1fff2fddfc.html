Inclusion rule
This is not a crypto-news chronology. It includes events that change at least one gate in the private monetary stack: charter, account, issuer, custody, title/record, network governance, interoperability, clearing, collateral, or production status.
Status words are literal:
completed/live — the dated action occurred;
temporary/limited — operative within stated limits;
pilot/test — controlled use, not general production;
conditional — permission subject to preopening or other conditions;
announced/targeted — future state, not yet achieved.
2025
2025-02-04 — Stripe completes Bridge acquisition
Status: completed
Track: payment orchestration / vertical integration
Stripe pulled stablecoin orchestration inside its merchant-payments group. Wallet infrastructure, proprietary-chain development, and a proposed trust-bank layer followed later. Stripe
2025-05-17 — Kinexys, Chainlink, and Ondo complete cross-chain DvP test
Status: test transaction with real-world value
Track: tokenized asset / bank deposit / orchestration
Ondo OUSG formed the asset leg, J.P. Morgan USD deposits formed the payment leg, and Chainlink coordinated the cross-network workflow. The event proved a transaction path; it did not establish a general production service. J.P. Morgan
2025-05-22 — R3 announces public/private integration path with Solana
Status: announced/development
Track: Corda / public-chain bridge
R3 selected Solana as a public Layer-1 substrate for a planned permissioned consensus and asset-distribution path. Existing Corda production networks did not thereby migrate. R3
2025-06-24 — Digital Asset raises $135 million strategic round
Status: completed
Track: institutional infrastructure ownership
The investor group joined dealers, market makers, banks, market utilities, and crypto firms around the company that builds Canton technology. Company equity and Canton governance remain separate structures. Digital Asset
2025-09-23 — Fnality closes $136 million Series C
Status: completed
Track: tokenized central-bank-backed wholesale cash
The round supported expansion beyond the operating sterling Fnality Payment System. Currency-by-currency authorization and central-bank account arrangements remain the scaling gate. See FINTECH_SWEEP_INSTITUTIONAL_RAILS.md.
2025-09-24 — Chainlink becomes a Canton Super Validator
Status: completed / integration rolling
Track: interoperability provider becomes governance participant
Chainlink added Canton governance/validation to its oracle and cross-chain role. That narrows the line between neutral connector and governed market participant. Canton and Chainlink
2025-09-25 — Deutsche Bank completes first euro cross-border payment on Partior
Status: live transaction
Track: multi-bank commercial-bank money
The event expanded Partior's production evidence beyond Singapore-dollar and dollar paths. Each participating settlement bank still issues its own liability. See FINTECH_SWEEP_INSTITUTIONAL_RAILS.md.
2025-12-12 — OCC conditionally approves five digital-asset trust-bank applications
Status: conditional at this date
Track: federal charter gate
The OCC conditionally approved proposed First National Digital Currency Bank/Circle National Trust and Ripple National Trust Bank, plus conversions for BitGo, Fidelity Digital Assets, and Paxos. Later consummation status diverged by entity. OCC
2025-12-17 — DTCC and Digital Asset announce DTC Treasury-token path on Canton
Status: announced/controlled-production target
Track: authoritative record / token representation
DTC planned token representations of participant entitlements to immobilized DTC-custodied Treasuries on Canton. The underlying securities remain registered to Cede & Co.; the later DTC FAQ moved the preliminary service expectation to H2 2026. DTCC
2026
2026-01-15 — Canton working group demonstrates multi-asset cross-border repo
Status: completed staged real-asset transactions
Track: collateral / commercial-bank money
The exercise used European government bonds, U.S. Treasuries, euro and dollar cash, including tokenized commercial-bank deposits supplied through LSEG Digital Settlement House. It remained a working-group program rather than an open market. See FINTECH_SWEEP_INSTITUTIONAL_RAILS.md.
2026-02-09 — OCC terminates Anchorage Digital Bank operating agreement
Status: completed supervisory change
Track: federal trust bank
Termination of the 2021 operating agreement did not terminate Anchorage's charter. Its separate Federal Reserve access request remained pending in the latest reviewed public database. OCC
2026-02-12 — Bridge National Trust Bank receives preliminary conditional approval
Status: conditional/preopening
Track: payment orchestrator moves into licensed custody/issuance layer
The proposed Bridge/Stripe trust must satisfy preopening conditions. The required Federal Reserve Bank stock application is not a confirmed master account. OCC
2026-04-02 — Coinbase National Trust receives preliminary conditional approval
Status: conditional/preopening
Track: exchange/custody federalization
The approval did not authorize the proposed trust to commence business. Coinbase's existing state and federal regulated entities continued to carry distinct functions. OCC
2026-04-21 — Broadridge invests in HQLAx and announces Canton migration path
Status: investment completed; migration planned
Track: repo/collateral consolidation
The event linked Broadridge's production DLR repo workflow more tightly to HQLAx and Canton, while actual custody, settlement, and migration milestones remained future work. Broadridge
2026-04-27 — WalletConnect goes live on Canton
Status: live connectivity
Track: wallet/custody connection
The integration opened a connection path for wallets and custodians. It did not prove production liquidity for every referenced asset. Canton

Status: production milestones
Track: bank deposit token / asset lifecycle
J.P. Morgan said its institutional public-chain deposit product on Base was available to eligible clients and that Fund Flow had launched. Canton-native issuance remained a planned phased path rather than verified production. See FINTECH_SWEEP_INSTITUTIONAL_RAILS.md.

Status: live pilot/regional rollout
Track: card-network settlement
Visa reported a $7 billion annualized stablecoin-settlement run rate and nine supported chains. The figure is a Visa program metric; chain support does not prove equal liquidity or volume on each network. Visa

SoFiUSD launched in the SoFi app on Ethereum and Solana. The token is issued by SoFi Bank, N.A., but is not itself an insured deposit. SoFi

2026-07-09 — Swift says blockchain ledger is ready for initial use

Track: bank-token orchestration
Swift's Besu-based shared ledger synchronizes bank-issued tokenized-deposit commitments. Swift says final settlement still completes through existing systems. The first completed live transaction and exact balance-sheet path remained open at the cutoff. Swift

SWIFT said the ledger extends infrastructure already trusted by its banking community and is designed to help the existing financial ecosystem evolve rather than replace it.
