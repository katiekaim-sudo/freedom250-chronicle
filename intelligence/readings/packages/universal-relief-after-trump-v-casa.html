# Universal Relief — Trump v. CASA Remedy Map

Updated: 2026-08-15  
Status: factual remedy map  
Rule: this note is **procedure**. The Citizenship Clause holding is Citizenship Operating Clock — Barbara and EO 14418. Do not mix them.

## Short answer

On June 27, 2025 the Supreme Court, per Justice Barrett, held that **universal injunctions likely exceed the equitable authority Congress gave the federal courts in the Judiciary Act of 1789.** The Court granted the government's applications for partial stays of three preliminary injunctions against EO 14160, "but only to the extent that the injunctions are broader than necessary to provide complete relief to each plaintiff with standing to sue."

The applications "do not raise — and thus the Court does not address — the question whether the Executive Order violates the Citizenship Clause or Nationality Act." That sentence is load-bearing. *CASA* did not decide birthright. *Barbara* did, a year later.

What remains open, because the Court said so: APA vacatur, nationwide class relief under Rule 23, and how wide "complete relief" runs when the plaintiff is a State. A single district judge no longer freezes the whole country for non-parties. That changes the operating clock of every later order on the EO spine.

## 1. Entity / control table

| Legal person | What it can freeze after *CASA* | What it cannot | Do not collapse |
|---|---|---|---|
| District court | Party-specific injunction; relief no broader than complete relief to plaintiffs with standing | A "universal" bar on enforcement against anyone | The opinion is not the judgment |
| Court of appeals | Stay or narrow the injunction on appeal | Create a new equitable power the Judiciary Act does not give | Same |
| Supreme Court | The stay geometry in *CASA* itself | The birthright merits (expressly not reached) | *CASA* ≠ *Barbara* |
| Individual / association plaintiff | Relief that makes *that* plaintiff whole | Relief that covers everyone similarly situated as a matter of course | A class is a different machine |
| State plaintiff | Possibly broader relief if the State itself cannot be made whole by a patchwork — left to the lower courts | An automatic nationwide freeze | State-as-plaintiff is not a class |
| United States / agencies | Bound only as far as the judgment reaches | A district opinion does not, by itself, bind the Executive as to non-parties | "The government lost" is not a holding on the policy |

## 2. What *CASA* held

**Cite:** *Trump v. CASA, Inc.*, Nos. 24A884 / 24A885 / 24A886 (June 27, 2025), reported 606 U.S. 831. Consolidated with *Trump v. Washington* and *Trump v. New Jersey*. Barrett, J., for the Court (Roberts, Thomas, Alito, Gorsuch, Kavanaugh). Thomas, Alito, and Kavanaugh each concurred. Sotomayor, J., dissented, joined by Kagan and Jackson. Jackson, J., also dissented separately.

**The question decided:** "whether, under the Judiciary Act of 1789, federal courts have equitable authority to issue universal injunctions."

**The holding, in the Court's words:**

> Universal injunctions likely exceed the equitable authority that Congress has given to federal courts.

> The Judiciary Act of 1789 endowed federal courts with jurisdiction over "all suits … in equity." … this statutory grant encompasses only those sorts of equitable remedies "traditionally accorded by courts of equity" at our country's inception.

> Neither the universal injunction nor any analogous form of relief was available in the High Court of Chancery in England at the time of the founding.

> "Complete relief" is not synonymous with "universal relief." It is a narrower concept … that allows courts to "administer complete relief *between the parties*."

> When a court concludes that the Executive Branch has acted unlawfully, the answer is not for the court to exceed its power, too.

**What the Court expressly did not decide:**

- Whether EO 14160 violates the Fourteenth Amendment or 8 U.S.C. § 1401.
- The legality of APA vacatur as a form of universal relief (the opinion notes this avenue was left open).
- Whether a certified Rule 23 class may obtain class-wide injunctive relief (the bill of peace, the Court says, lives on as the class action, not as the universal injunction).

**On the state plaintiffs:** the Massachusetts district court had said a universal injunction was needed to give the *States* complete relief, because children move and a patchwork would break federally funded benefits. The Supreme Court "declines to take up these arguments in the first instance" and leaves narrower-injunction design to the lower courts.

**On irreparable harm:** an overbroad injunction against the government is itself the injury for stay purposes. The applicant need not also win the underlying merits of the EO.

## 3. The leftover machines

| Remedy | What it binds | Status after *CASA* | Vault use |
|---|---|---|---|
| Party-specific injunction | The named plaintiffs | Alive. This is the default. | Every EO challenge now starts here |
| "Complete relief" that incidentally helps non-parties | Still a party decree; third-party benefit is incidental (nuisance example in the opinion) | Alive, narrow | Do not stretch it into a national freeze |
| State-as-plaintiff "complete relief" | Possibly statewide or system-wide if the State itself cannot be made whole | Open — remanded in *CASA* | Watch the Massachusetts remand |
| Rule 23 class injunction | The class, if certified | Alive; the Court says this is the historical analogue | The *Barbara* district court later certified a nationwide class — that is a different case |
| APA vacatur | The rule, as to everyone, if vacatur is lawful | **Not decided** | Do not treat *CASA* as killing vacatur |
| Universal / nationwide injunction as such | Anyone | **Likely unlawful** under the Judiciary Act | Do not write "the court blocked the EO nationwide" after June 27, 2025 without naming the leftover machine that actually did the work |

## 4. Operating-clock consequence for the EO spine

After June 27, 2025 an executive order can be **enjoined as to the plaintiffs** and **operative as to everyone else**, unless a leftover machine (class, APA vacatur, or a State complete-relief decree) is in force.

That is why EO 14160 could still be litigated on the merits in *Barbara* after *CASA*. Procedure first, Clause second. The two notes stay apart.

Do not use this geometry to invent outcomes for Education, USAID, or Schedule Policy/Career. Each needs its own judgment. The method is: name the plaintiffs, name the remedy type, name the date.

## 6. Entity Theory read

The judiciary is a control on the federal group. *CASA* shrinks that control to the parties. The Executive can now run a policy in jurisdictions (and as to persons) that have not sued, while the same policy is frozen as to those who have. That is a patchwork on purpose. The group is no longer stopped at one district courthouse. Anyone who wants a national freeze must now use a leftover machine the Court still allows — a class, perhaps a vacatur, perhaps a State that can prove it cannot be made whole any narrower way.
