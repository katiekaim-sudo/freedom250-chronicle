## Right to Development finding

The President of the General Assembly's as-delivered statement supplied the
clearest official thesis of the commemorative meeting. It treated development
as participatory and indivisible across rights, peace and the environment;
named debt, finance, technology and market access as structural barriers; and
made inclusive AI governance part of the right-to-development frame. It also
reported that only 36 per cent of 139 SDG targets are on track or making
moderate progress and that 2.6 billion people remained offline in 2024.

That speech is an official delivered record. It is not the meeting's negotiated
outcome. The 1986 Declaration remains adopted non-treaty text, the covenant
remains a draft, and no new Conference of States Parties, financing mechanism
or implementation authority was established at this cutoff.
