Freedom 250 · Foreign relations and world money · International bodies

What does the U.S. pay, and how does it vote, in each international body?

The U.S. share runs from 49.99% (Organization of American States, 2026 assessed quota) down to 9.09% (ITER, construction sharing baseline). At the UN it pays 22% of the regular budget and 26.1371% of peacekeeping, but still has one Assembly vote. Paying more buys more votes only where votes are weighted by money, at the IMF and World Bank: the U.S. IMF share can block decisions requiring 85% of total voting power; IBRD influence derives from shareholding.
