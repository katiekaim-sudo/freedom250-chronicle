Freedom 250 · Digital money · Around the world

Which licensed stablecoins outside the U.S. are actually live, and is their backing proven?

The library tracks 13 stablecoin products outside the U.S. 6 are live under a local license, and 4 of those publish regular attestations of their reserves (USDG · Singapore; XSGD · Singapore; XUSD · Singapore; Zand AED / AEDZ · United Arab Emirates). The rest are in beta, announced, used without a local license, or have no dated public proof of backing yet; the backing of 1 failed.
