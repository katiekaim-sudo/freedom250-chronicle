Freedom 250 · Digital money · Around the world

When a crypto firm collapsed, did its customers get their money back, and how?

Rarely in full. Of 10 cases the library tracks, customers are being paid back, at least partly, in 3 (Cryptopia liquidation, Mt. Gox civil rehabilitation, FTX Chapter 11 distribution), no payout is confirmed in the library's record for 5, and 1 recovered everything (Wuhan virtual-currency cash-out fraud). How much comes back depends on whether a court treats customer crypto as the customers' own property held in trust or as the failed firm's assets.
