Freedom 250 · Banks and the dollar · The Fed

How big is FedNow now, and how fast is it growing?

FedNow settled 4,997,811 payments worth $274.664 billion in the second quarter of 2026 (54,921 a day), up from 2.7 million payments worth $271.3 billion in the first quarter. There were more than 1,800 live institutions by mid-July 2026 (about 1,600 in January), and the largest payment allowed rose from $500,000 at the July 20, 2023 launch to $1 million in March 2025 and $10 million in November 2025. The library holds only these two quarters of volume, so it cannot show a longer growth trend yet.

What's still uncertain

  • The two quarters come from two different homes: the first-quarter count (2.7 million transactions, $271.3 billion) is from the July 15, 2026 Fed360 row in records/money.json; the second-quarter count (4,997,811 transfers, $274.664 billion) is from the Fed technology map. The count nearly doubles while the value barely moves; the library has not checked both against the same Fed statistics table, so the jump may partly reflect how each source counts.
  • Institutions: 'more than 1,800 live institutions by mid-July' (Fed technology map) and '1,800+ live institutions' (Fed360, July 15, 2026). Neither says how many can send as well as receive.
  • Participation and volume do not prove send enablement, universal adoption, mature cost recovery or stress resilience.
  • Still to come: Q3 volume, send/receive mix, use concentration, uptime/incidents, revenue and application RACI.
  • The June 24, 2026 hearing claim that about 80% of eligible institutions had not connected is a witness's statement; the master marks it needs_primary_source.
