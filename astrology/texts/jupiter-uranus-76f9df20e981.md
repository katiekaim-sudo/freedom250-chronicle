# Jupiter–Uranus

## The planets

**Jupiter** — *Zeus, expansion, faith, courage to hope.* MUNDANE: hierarchical organizations — government, corporate, nonprofit; the corporate sector; courts, judges, religion, charitable organizations. (Greer: "the planet of hierarchy; he rules every organization set up in a hierarchical manner.")

**Uranus** — *Prometheus, awakener, breakthroughs, liberation.* MUNDANE: democracy, countercultures, alternative movements; machines, factories, computers, the internet; radical change. Uranus disrupts whatever it touches — liberating what was trapped, destabilizing what was settled.

## The combination — mundane

Barbault calls Jupiter-Uranus "a most powerful agent for change and progress" — and also warns of dangerous war-mongering potential in crisis periods. It is the cycle of hierarchy meeting radical change, strongly capitalist-oriented: free-market buoyancy, growth of consciousness, Promethean rebellion amplified through institutional channels. When Jupiter's hierarchical organizations encounter Uranus's democratic and technological disruption, the result is either constructive modernization or institutional crisis.

Under helpful aspects (conjunction, sextile, trine), Greer describes results "favorable for constructive legislation; helpful reforms and new laws benefit society; strengthens party in power; peace, good government." The hierarchy reforms itself — legislation modernizes outdated structures, the courts embrace innovation, corporate organizations adopt new technologies. The party in power benefits because it is seen as the agent of progress. Democracy and the counterculture find their reforms codified into law. Machines, computers, and the internet expand under institutional support. It is the aspect of the reform that actually works.

Under hostile aspects (square, opposition), Greer warns that "new laws cause more problems than they solve; reforms prove unworkable; unfortunate for party in power; in wartime, defeat likely." The hierarchy attempts to manage radical change and fails — legislation backfires, reforms destabilize rather than improve, the corporate sector's modernization efforts create chaos. The party in power is weakened because it is blamed for disruption it cannot control. Counterculture and democratic movements challenge institutional authority, and the institutions respond clumsily. Barbault's war-mongering warning activates: in crisis periods, the hierarchy reaches for military force to control change it cannot absorb.

**Current:** Early in a new cycle after the April 2024 Taurus conjunction. The practical (Taurus) revolution is just germinating — hierarchical organizations are beginning to integrate the technological and financial disruptions (crypto, AI, manufacturing reshoring) that this conjunction seeded. The early aspects will show whether the hierarchy absorbs the revolution constructively or resists it.
