# Method and Doctrine Review

## The model

The useful unit is not “which planet owns this story?” It is:

> What enduring question does this planetary relationship carry, what developmental phase is it in, and is the pair speaking directly in this chart?

That produces three independent readings:

1. **Synodic phase:** the long developmental chapter from conjunction seed toward opposition fruit and back toward a new seed.
2. **Direct aspect:** the pair's current dialogue—conjunction, sextile, square, trine, or opposition—inside Katie's governed orb.
3. **Current motion:** applying, exact, or separating. This is not the same thing as waxing or waning. A retrograde loop can reverse the directed hand, make a pair separate and reapply, or return it across an aspect or phase boundary; it cannot rewind the chronological history already lived.

The ten governed Long Clocks retain their three authored tiers, seed families, readings, personas, routes, and app behavior. The Mars-, Venus-, and Mercury-led bands in this package are mechanical delivery bands for review. They are not new Long Clock tiers and do not receive equal plot-vote authority merely because they can be calculated.

## Motion is the invariant

Planets retain their archetypal motivations, but they do **not** receive fixed
plot assignments. A planet may carry several jobs at once. What becomes plot is
the current relationship among actors and the accumulated history of that
relationship.

The method therefore distinguishes four objects:

1. **Planetary function:** the actor's enduring motivation and available civic
   expressions.
2. **Relationship identity:** the stable pair whose enduring question persists
   across charts and seasons.
3. **Relationship state:** the pair's exact phase, direct aspect, orb, and
   immediate motion at one chart snapshot.
4. **Relationship history:** the ordered sequence of entries, perfections,
   separations, returns, phase crossings, and seed-family passes through time.

A chart is a frame; a relationship history is the film. The direct aspect is
the present scene, the synodic phase locates that scene within the longer relationship arc, and
immediate motion describes how that scene is being delivered. Retrogradation
can produce recurrence, revision, delay, reversal, or another exact encounter,
but a later pass is never the earlier pass reused. It has its own identity and
inherits everything that occurred between the two encounters.

Historical time therefore always moves forward even when the geocentric hand
temporarily moves backward. An Aries reset or another lawful ingress succession
changes the governing mundane frame; it does not reset a planetary relationship
or erase its prior chapters. A period reading must enter the relationship at
the state already in motion, not treat the ingress chart as the pair's origin.

This is also the anti-ownership rule. Crypto, Iran, AI, government, war, and
money may each be carried by several relationships, while one relationship may
operate across several stories. Pair-to-story use remains many-to-many and must
be earned in the actual chart and factual comparison.

## Shared phase grammar

| Phase | Developmental question |
|---|---|
| New, 0–45° | What has been seeded or fused? |
| Crescent, 45–90° | What is differentiating and learning how to act? |
| First Quarter, 90–135° | What implementation crisis forces form? |
| Gibbous, 135–180° | What is being refined under pressure before visibility? |
| Full, 180–225° | What fruit, contradiction, or confrontation is fully visible? |
| Disseminating, 225–270° | What result is being distributed, taught, exploited, or institutionalized? |
| Last Quarter, 270–315° | What structure must be revised after the verdict? |
| Balsamic, 315–360° | What is being salvaged, released, or prepared for the next seed? |

Constructive versus shadow expression is orthogonal to phase. Waxing does not mean good; waning does not mean failure. A conjunction is powerful and condition-dependent, not inherently helpful or hostile.

## Direct-aspect grammar

| Aspect | Relationship action |
|---|---|
| Conjunction | fusion, seed, concentration |
| Sextile | offered capacity requiring uptake |
| Square | friction that forces implementation |
| Trine | open flow, sometimes complacency |
| Opposition | confrontation, fruit, maximum awareness |

Direct aspects remain visible even when phase prose is not nameable. Phase prose is admitted only through the existing DR-020 fence: chart root, chart ruler, the outer-pair era exception, or an exact governed US-natal contact.

## Relationship-field overlap control

Direct-aspect visibility and phase nameability must be audited as independent
sets. For each governed chart, the relationship graph therefore partitions all
28 pairs into four non-overlapping states:

1. **Both:** the pair is in direct aspect and its phase is lawfully nameable.
2. **Direct only:** the pair speaks now, but phase prose is not admitted.
3. **Phase only:** the developmental chapter is nameable, but the pair is
   between direct aspect chapters.
4. **Neither:** computed background or lawful silence.

The graph also retains the direct, nameable and silent totals so the partition
can be checked. These are census measures, not intensity, importance,
convergence, evidence or forecast-confidence scores. The useful question is
how the overlap changes through time: whether immediate dialogue and lawful
developmental context are converging, diverging or exchanging emphasis.

A field with many direct-only relationships can be operationally crowded while
remaining comparatively thin in admitted phase context. A later chart can have
fewer direct aspects but much broader phase nameability. That is a change in
the field's reading structure, not proof that the later chart is stronger or
that any real-world transition occurred.

Aggregate equality is equally unsafe. Two charts can have the same counts in
all four partitions while different relationships occupy them. The graph
therefore preserves the cycle-ID membership of every bucket and audits
consecutive equal-count charts for identity turnover. Read the cast, not only
the volume.

This distinction also separates astronomy from chart jurisdiction. If the
direct-aspect cast remains unchanged while phase admissions change across an
ingress, the physical relationship field did not suddenly acquire different
direct speakers. The new root, ruler or exact natal gate changed which
continuing phases may be narrated in the new mundane frame.

## Inside-pair seed families

The 17 phaseable inside pairs require **55** conjunction families and **73** exact conjunction passes to bracket the governed chart window from the 2025 Aries ingress through the 2026 December lunation. Their median family cadence ranges from about **0.98 years** for Mercury–Neptune to about **2.22 years** for Mars–Jupiter.

A family is not necessarily one conjunction. Retrograde loops can produce multiple exact passes inside one seed episode:

- Mercury–Mars has a three-pass family active at the start of the window, a five-pass 2025–2026 family, and another five-pass family after the window.
- Mercury–Neptune, Venus–Saturn, and Venus–Neptune have three-pass families around 2025.
- Venus–Mars has a three-pass family after the governed window.

The first pass is only a provisional staging anchor. An authored display anchor remains a separate reviewed choice.

## Mercury–Venus exception

Mercury–Venus is a real, useful geocentric relationship, but it is not currently admissible to the same eight-phase contract:

- neither planet can reach a geocentric opposition to the other;
- the directed hand reverses rather than traversing a monotonic conjunction → opposition → conjunction arc;
- a computed 0–360 sector label would therefore look like a governed phase while describing geometry the pair cannot physically complete.

This package retains exact angle, relative motion, conjunction crossings, and direct-aspect state for Mercury–Venus, but sets its governed phase name and direction to `null`. Its phase prose can never become nameable until Katie adopts a separate doctrine for the pair.

## Relationship lexicon

The existing 18 Transit Weather pair notes are retained as the source lexicon. Their strongest contribution is their enduring mundane question and domain field. Their structural weaknesses are now fenced:

- generic “cycle length” wording is not treated as pair-specific cadence;
- a “days to two weeks” trigger window describes a direct aspect, not the whole synodic cycle;
- waxing/waning is not collapsed into applying/separating;
- helpful/hostile buckets do not determine the meaning of conjunctions or phases;
- illustrative Chronicle arcs do not create plot ownership;
- warnings remain watch conditions, not predictions.

## Reading discipline

For every plot test:

1. Freeze the factual state and the question before reading the chart.
2. Read the ingress as the governing climate.
3. Name only the phase relationships admitted by DR-020.
4. Read every active direct aspect, including background pairs.
5. Use lunations as chapters/triggers under their governing ingress; treat eclipses as amplified lunations with longer fuses.
6. Pre-register both transition evidence and the continuity case.
7. Compare the post-window factual state with those tests, including unchanged or contradictory results.
8. Keep pair-to-domain resonance many-to-many. No pair permanently owns Crypto, Iran, AI, or any other plot.

## Relationship braids

A storyline may require several planetary relationships at the same time. This
is a **relationship braid** only when the strands perform distinguishable jobs;
it is not permission to accumulate symbolic matches. Correctness is
role-specific, and every role remains answerable to the frozen factual state,
the registered checkpoints, continuity, counterevidence, and the stated cutoff.

For every proposed strand, record:

- `role`: `lead mechanism`, `co-mechanism`, `supporting context`,
  `outcompeted`, or `unresolved`;
- `presence_mode`: `inherited substrate`, `active dialogue`, or `seed echo`;
- the exact relationship state and governed chart checkpoint;
- what factual transition or continuity would make the strand win or lose.

The three presence modes answer different questions. Inherited substrate means
the synodic chapter continues even without a direct conference. Active dialogue
means the pair is within the governed five-Aspect relationship-clock geometry.
A seed echo is a separate chart-point contact to the conjunction family; it
does not manufacture direct geometry or phase narration.

Read a braid by delta: what continued, what entered or exited a direct Aspect,
what crossed a phase boundary, what separated or reapplied, and what the new
chart lawfully foregrounded. One useful hypothesis is **long relationship as
historical condition → faster relationship as present carrier → governed
lunation or eclipse as visible beat**. It must be tested rather than imposed;
the roles may differ, remain unresolved, or fail entirely.

Several correct strands do not add convergence or forecast confidence. They
increase descriptive resolution only when their jobs are non-duplicative and
the chronology distinguishes them. Conjunction and persona charts remain
optional seed dossiers and cannot substitute for the continuing relationship
history or rescue an outcompeted strand.

## Cross-season relationship history protocol

The complete 28-pair calculation is a technical census, not a prose assignment list. A seasonal history uses two layers:

1. **Complete matrix:** retain every pair's phase, direct aspect, motion, and nameability at the ingress and each governed lunation or eclipse.
2. **Change history:** narrate a pair only when it crosses a phase boundary, enters or exits a direct aspect, flips immediate motion, begins or repeats a seed family, changes lawful visibility, or materially distinguishes a factual transition.

Nameability gain or loss is a chart-visibility change, not a synodic-cycle change. An unchanged outer-era relationship is inherited substrate and should be stated once unless its aspect chapter or exact state changes.

Aries always resets the mundane year, regardless of the prior ingress. Cancer, Libra, and Capricorn take over only when the currently governing ingress has completed the term allowed by its rising sign:

- Mutable rising governs three months, so the next cardinal ingress takes over.
- Cardinal rising governs six months, so the next cardinal ingress does not take over; the following one can.
- Fixed rising governs the year, subject to Aries's absolute annual reset.

The rising sign therefore decides whether a non-Aries cardinal ingress becomes the new seasonal governor. Synodic relationships can cross every seam, but they do not decide ingress succession.

The lunation ladder can cross that seam without weakening the reset:

- the March 19 New Moon sits on the Winter chart shelf but governs the opening lunar month of the fresh Aries year;
- the June 15 New Moon sits on the Aries chart shelf and governs the opening lunar month when the Gemini-rising Aries term expires at Cancer.

The graph's singular `governing` field correctly preserves seasonal ingress authority for this window. Cross-season synodic continuity belongs to the planetary relationships themselves and does not override the modality-based ingress succession rule.

For each storyline:

1. freeze the opening Attention, Official, and Entity states;
2. name the candidate state transition and continuity case;
3. preselect two to four plausible relationships and state how each would win or lose;
4. trace the same evidence corpus through the governed checkpoints;
5. preserve exact legal and operating verbs;
6. close as `Lines up`, `Partial`, `Miss`, or `Unresolved` without a score.

Use `lead mechanism`, `co-mechanism`, `supporting context`, `outcompeted`, or `unresolved` for pair roles. Several symbolic fits do not add convergence or forecast confidence. A persona is not part of the 28-pair matrix and cannot rescue a miss; it may appear only as an optional family note after the seed and dependency hearings.

Two silences must remain distinct:

- **quiet against the governing ingress** — the lunation has few or no close parent-chart contacts;
- **quiet in the relationship field** — few or no planetary pairs are in direct aspect.

A chart may be quiet in one sense and dense in the other.
