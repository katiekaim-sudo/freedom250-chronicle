Reader contract

1. Private cyber power moved under a federal approval chain

The August 12 presidential memorandum directs the National Coordination Center to establish and manage a program in which vetted U.S. companies may conduct cyber-surveillance and cyber-effects operations against foreign cyber-enabled transnational criminal organizations. DOJ and DHS co-executive directors oversee it; participants contract with DOJ or DHS; each operation requires a written package, federal approval and federal direction; and the memorandum includes legal, targeting, deconfliction, incident, and bond or escrow controls.

Procedures are due within 60 days and the first status report within 180 days. The state reached is a command and approval architecture. No company, contract, target, operation or effect was established by cutoff.

Atomic record: 2026-08-12-1200 - President Creates a Federally Controlled Private Cyber Program.
Source: White House memorandum.

2. Shipbuilding and drones formed one industrial-mobilization stack

The August 13 Navy memorandum sets 60-, 90- and 120-day planning and review gates around CVN-81 systems, acquisition competition, trade-deal investment, repair capacity, NAVSEA reform and a possible fifth public Navy yard. Its conditional “Finland Model” and delegated waiver route do not themselves create a foreign-owned yard, waiver, contract or congressional clearance.

Atomic record: 2026-08-13-1200 - President Orders Navy and Shipbuilding Industrial Base Plans.
Source: White House memorandum.

The same day, a section 232 proclamation scheduled additional UAS duties for September 3, including nominal 100-percent and 25-percent bands subject to product, country, origin, certification and other exceptions. A separate 25-percent component band begins February 9, 2027. Commerce must build the administration/onshoring route and report on the market within 120 days.

Atomic record: 2026-08-13-1200 - UAS Import Duties and Onshoring Framework Are Proclaimed.
Source: White House proclamation.

BIS simultaneously removed the wind-gust criterion, raised a specified national-security endurance threshold from 30 minutes to three hours and removed associated controls for some software and technology, while preserving military end-use/end-user controls. Its text says effective August 13; the public-inspection filing at 8:45 a.m. ET was scheduled for August 14 publication.

Atomic record: 2026-08-13-0845 - Commerce Loosens Civil Drone Export Controls.
Source: Federal Register public inspection.

Together these objects widen domestic protection and planning while loosening a band of civil export control. They do not prove an import, export, approved onshoring plan, factory, shipment or domestic production result.

3. FinCEN narrowed the broad registry while tightening a local payment lens

FinCEN's Minnesota geographic targeting order is effective August 11 through February 6, 2027. Covered banks and money transmitters with an office in Hennepin or Ramsey County must report and retain specified information for covered outgoing international transfers of at least $3,000. The order asks about government-benefit and contract sources and carries a five-year retention duty. It does not declare every covered payment fraudulent or prove recovery.

Atomic record: 2026-08-11-1200 - FinCEN Activates a Minnesota Payment GTO.
Source: Federal Register.

On August 13 at 8:45 a.m. ET, FinCEN's BOI final text reached public inspection for scheduled August 14 publication and effectiveness. It makes the March 2025 narrowing permanent: domestic reporting companies and U.S. persons remain outside the operative reporting population; covered foreign companies still report non-U.S. beneficial owners. A contemplated, NARA-coordinated deletion of certain prior U.S. BOI has no completion date and was not complete at cutoff.

Atomic record: 2026-08-13-0845 - FinCEN BOI Final Rule Reaches Public Inspection.
Source: Federal Register public inspection.

The useful story is the contrast: a broad domestic-company registry remains narrowed while a geographically targeted transaction-reporting program becomes operative.

4. Health policy moved at both the recommendation and funding layers

Executive Order 14420, signed August 10, declares universal, high-risk and shared-decision categories for federal childhood-vaccine recommendations, recognizes separate MMR shots once such products are domestically available, and orders an HHS implementation/research plan within 90 days. It does not by itself make separate products available, change an operating schedule, move a grant or file a lawsuit.

Existing captured record: 2026-08-10-1906 - POTUS For far too long America has recommended more.
Source: White House order.

CMS's August 13 final rule bars federal Medicaid funding for the rule-defined procedures for people under 18 and corresponding CHIP funding for people under 19. It is effective October 13, and permits up to six months of continued federal matching for certain beneficiaries already receiving cross-sex hormone therapy. It does not prohibit state-only or private funding, exclude every provider, or establish that a claim was denied before effectiveness.

Existing captured record: 2026-08-11-2132 - Today at my direction Dr Mehmet Oz announced that.
Source: Federal Register.

5. DTCC disclosed a deadline around an earlier settlement failure

Separate August 13 DTC and NSCC notices say that July 13 interest payments followed a June 29 settlement rollover. A participant claiming direct losses materially above those payments must submit substantiating evidence by August 31; later submissions will not be considered.

Atomic records: 2026-08-13-1200 - DTC Sets August 31 Rollover Loss Deadline and 2026-08-13-1200 - NSCC Sets August 31 Rollover Loss Deadline.
Sources: DTC B24712-26 and NSCC A9807.

The notices establish the rollover, prior interest payments and hard proof deadline. They do not establish the number of claimants, a liability admission, an additional loss amount or a new August incident. DTCC's official all-notices RSS listed 25 notices dated August 10–13, correcting the earlier claim that the site had no post-July 15 material; the JS-paginated UI remains unverified.

6. The sleeper was a federal device-control reversal

OMB Memorandum M-26-17, dated August 10, rescinds M-23-13 after a July DOJ OLC opinion concluded TikTok was no longer a covered application under the statute. The operative memo says TikTok may be used on government devices. This is a government-wide control reversal, not proof that an agency installed or authorized the app or accepted a particular risk.

Atomic record: 2026-08-10-1200 - OMB Rescinds the Federal TikTok Device Ban Memo.
Source: OMB M-26-17.

Money and market state retained beside the headline items

  • SEC's 24X order is a conditional future fallback only. It can begin January 24, 2027 only if the Equity Data Plans miss December 6, 2026, and it ends at actual implementation or July 2, 2027. 24X had not begun its overnight session. Atomic record: 2026-08-13-0845 - SEC Creates a Conditional 24X Overnight Fallback.
  • FinCEN's August 7 Iran-linked crypto action reached public inspection August 13 for August 14 publication; that is delayed publication, not a new August 13 designation or proof of seizure.
  • Treasury completed its three August refunding auctions. The awards are not the August 17 cash settlement or a realized TGA effect.
  • FICC's August 11 publications fixed comment and review clocks without approving or funding the proposed Guaranty Fund or proving first use.
  • The SEC Treasury-clearing requests remain requests: comments close August 31, no relief was granted, and the cash/repo compliance dates did not move.

Three supporting items worth keeping on the board

  • Project IKE: NRC received Orano's application for a Tennessee centrifuge facility capable of enrichment up to 8 weight-percent U-235 and possible depleted-tails re-enrichment. A September 21 security filing remains due. No license, construction, enrichment or delivery exists.
  • Apple v. Epic: Justice Kagan's short administrative hold ended August 13 at 5:00 p.m. EDT and the stay application was denied. That is not the merits decision in No. 25-1311 or a final payment-rail remedy.
  • SPP: DOE's temporary section 202(c) authority expired August 10 without a posted successor on the checked live page. The public record does not yet establish dispatch, generation, emissions or reliability outcome.
