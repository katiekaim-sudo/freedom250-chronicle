What is complete

All ten standard Planets now have a full mundane candidate rather than a list of loose keywords. Each candidate states:

  • one irreducible collective motivation;
  • the civic function or actor that may embody it;
  • candidate carriers and institutional expressions;
  • intrinsic gift and shadow poles;
  • scale rules and scope conditions;
  • source divergences;
  • the decisions Katie must make before promotion.

No candidate is active doctrine. The runtime owner mold deliberately contains ten unreviewed records and no active revisions. The review sequence is:

complete candidate
→ deterministic validation
→ Katie: keep / revise / reject / hold
→ promotion receipt
→ immutable active revision + content hash

This lets us do a serious full first pass without confusing a complete answer with a ratified answer.

Governing owner question

> What irreducible collective motivation does this Planet carry, which civic > function or actor may embody it, and what remains unchanged before Sign > equipment, House arena, condition, relationships, governance and whole-chart > return are considered?

The Planet remains the sole actor. The Sign supplies equipment; the House supplies the civic arena; the Aspect owns the relationship; Condition and Dignity describe operation; the whole chart decides hierarchy, materiality and the Planet's actual job in that chart.

Cross-cutting rulings already preserved

  1. Mundane owners are independently authored. Archetypal continuity may be evidence, but natal Planet prose is not inherited.
  2. Both poles are intrinsic. The workbook's Gift (well-dignified) and Shadow (afflicted) headings are useful shorthand, not a causal equation. Dignity changes the quality of the Sign tools; it does not switch a Planet's gift or shadow on and off.
  3. Keep the four chart functions distinct. Planet supplies actor and motivation, Sign supplies equipment, House supplies arena and Aspect supplies relationship. No conventional analogy among them creates semantic identity.
  4. Traditional seven retain formal authority. Jupiter and Saturn remain rulers, dignity-bearing actors, dispositors and possible roots. Uranus, Neptune and Pluto may define an era but receive no formal rulership, dignity, dispositor route or root authority.
  5. Outer scale is not automatic salience. An outer field can be historically central without becoming a formal ruler; sign or cycle presence alone gives no Evidence, prominence, Judgment or Forecast credit.
  6. Special objects remain separate. Chiron, lunar nodes, planetary nodes and 916 America do not enlarge the ten-Planet foundation roster.

☉ Sun — the effective governing center

Candidate foundation. The Sun is the collective drive to establish and exercise a visible center of governing authority. Mundanely it signifies the effective executive: the person, office or concentrated command through which government acts.

Gift. Coherent, illuminating direction and the power to build and coordinate government.

Shadow. Discredited, floundering, captured or over-centralized rule that co-opts other civic functions.

Katie decisions. Is to build part of the essence or a gift of effective authority? Is national self-image a secondary Sun expression? Does a ceremonial crown remain a Sun correspondence while failing the actual-power test?

☽ Moon — voiced common life

Candidate foundation. The Moon is the collective drive to receive, nourish, reflect and give public voice to common life. The primary Greer lens is the political class and moving public mood. DR-009 separately sanctions the Campion sense, the people (Moon), and it must not be corrected away.

Gift. Responsive representation, nourishment, continuity and attunement to lived national conditions.

Shadow. Volatility, emotional contagion or estrangement between the voiced political layer and the population it claims to represent.

Katie decisions. Should the organism store one Moon owner with two typed lenses, or one primary lens plus a sanctioned alias? Retain the roughly 20% gloss? How do Moon/common-life women and Venus/organized women's interests divide? Are food and agriculture core correspondences or contextual carriers?

☿ Mercury — national information and exchange

Candidate foundation. Mercury is the collective drive to perceive, encode, exchange, negotiate and carry information, records and goods. Mundanely it is the nation's communicative and transactional circuitry: press, speech, public record, formal documents, transport and trade.

Gift. Lucid exchange, an intelligible record and functioning channels.

Shadow. Trickery, confusion, manipulation, capture, broken records or failed communication, transport and exchange.

Katie decisions. Is to trick intrinsic motivation or shadow? Is education and science core or context-dependent? Does Venus want agreement while Mercury negotiates and documents it? Ratify the human-skilled tool versus autonomous system distinction with Uranus.

♀ Venus — civic valuation and accord

Candidate foundation. Venus is the collective drive to attract, value, harmonize and bind through affinity. Mundanely it signifies civic valuation and accord: diplomacy and social peace, arts and culture, value and currency, and women's organized interests.

Gift. Concord, viable value, cultural flourishing and relationship grounded in genuine mutual recognition.

Shadow. Appeasement, indulgence, distorted valuation, scandal, commodification or neglect of what the nation claims to value.

Katie decisions. Ratify valuation-and-conciliation as the one function. Should currency be core or one contextual expression of value? Are women a primary actor or a major constituency correspondence? Do treaties belong to Venus, or does Venus want accord while Mercury records it?

♂ Mars — force and decisive action

Candidate foundation. Mars is the collective drive to separate, assert, compel, attack and defend. Mundanely it signifies the polity's force function: military power, coercive enforcement, conflict and violence.

Gift. Courageous defense, decisive enforcement, necessary severance and hard action capable of removing what has failed.

Shadow. War, repression, riot, crime, accident, bloodshed or force without a governed aim.

Katie decisions. Saturn may own rule/administration while Mars owns applied coercion—is that the police boundary? Keep men and the men's vote as actor, correspondence or historical Greer lens? Are reform and prosecution intrinsic gift language or possible results? Keep epidemics and disasters in configuration packets only?

♃ Jupiter — expansion and legitimation

Candidate foundation. Jupiter enlarges and legitimizes collective meaning, confidence, participation and reach. It animates institutions that bless, judge, finance, teach, ally or expand a social order.

Gift. Generous possibility, lawful legitimacy, synthesis, confidence and abundance that expands actual capacity.

Shadow. Gorging, inflation, hubris, corruption, protected privilege, overreach or hierarchy too large to perceive consequence.

Katie decisions. Is hierarchical organization the primary civic actor or one expression of expansion and legitimation? Keep patriarch, aristocracy and upper-class language? Are banks, publishers and charities universal or contextual carriers?

♄ Saturn — structure and consequence

Candidate foundation. Saturn binds collective life to material reality through form, limit, time, duty and consequence. It maintains, constrains, tests and sometimes retires the institutions that make order durable.

Gift. Continuity, accountability, realistic limits, patient construction and wise endurance.

Shadow. Inertia, deprivation, punitive control, decay or structure preserved after it has ceased to serve.

Katie decisions. Are land and metal in money universal expressions or Freedom-specific translations? Where does Saturn's administration of law meet Jupiter's judiciary? Ratify rule/administration versus Mars's applied force as the police boundary.

♅ Uranus — collective reconfiguration

Candidate foundation. Uranus makes discontinuity available to the collective: a new system, idea, technology or freedom breaks through inherited continuity and reorganizes what people can perceive or do.

Gift. Liberation, invention, discovery, wider autonomy and systems that release what the old form constrained.

Shadow. Rupture, instability, alienating systems, failed reform or change that destroys continuity without building a livable successor.

Katie decisions. Which of democracy, counterculture, decentralized systems, automation, nuclear technology and networks are universal versus historical manifestations? Ratify Mercury as skilled communication/tool use and Uranus as autonomous or system-level reconfiguration. Use era-defining field but never formal ruler rather than importing natal sidekick language?

♆ Neptune — collective image and dissolution

Candidate foundation. Neptune makes collective boundaries permeable, drawing institutions and populations toward an image, ideal, longing or union whose reality may be compassionate, visionary, glamorized, deceptive or dissolving.

Gift. Solidarity, imagination, reform, peace and meaningful connection beyond separateness.

Shadow. Lost distinction, propaganda, illusion, speculative bubbles, mass contagion or institutions unable to tell ideal from fact.

Katie decisions. Should malefic-leaning/high-vigilance be a foundation modifier, a pair rule, an admission/Judgment posture or provenance only? Are masses, sea/oil and money-as-signal universal expressions or Freedom adapters? Is there any reviewed mundane analogue to the natal Sun-plus-Saturn container test?

♇ Pluto — concentrated root power

Candidate foundation. Pluto concentrates collective intensity where power, survival, ownership, taboo and irreversible consequence meet. It describes what is buried, bound, leveraged, eliminated, exposed or regenerated under extreme pressure.

Gift. Radical truth, recovered power, necessary elimination and transformation deep enough to change the structure.

Shadow. Coercion, repression, totalization, criminal or secret power, and destruction that mistakes domination for renewal.

Katie decisions. What precise evidence and proportion gate operationalizes Greer's skepticism without deleting Pluto? Are debt, buried wealth, crime and masses-as-force universal or context-dependent expressions? Should mundane doctrine affirm a stronger collective definition than the natal hierarchy while still saying era-defining field, never formal ruler?

Recommended review order

Review functional pairs, not ten isolated vocabulary lists:

  1. Sun / Moon — governing center and voiced public response.
  2. Mercury — the connector and carrier between actors.
  3. Venus / Mars — accord and valuation versus force and severance.
  4. Jupiter / Saturn — expansion and legitimation versus structure and consequence.
  5. Uranus / Neptune / Pluto — three different collective-scale fields with no formal rulership.

For each Planet, Katie can keep, revise, reject or hold. A kept card is then made into an immutable revision, validated, and promoted with a receipt. Only after that do Planet–Sign, Planet–House, Aspect, Condition and whole-chart objects gain permission to reference it.
