## Libra to Capricorn 2026

The omitted First- and Last-Quarter Moon-family chapters do add useful context
to the late-2026 sequence, but they do not all deserve equal narrative weight.
The audit produces one major cross-cycle collision, four materially useful
supporting chapters, one narrow value-and-power chapter, and one restraint case.

New or Full Moon. They are not additional event votes. Their job is to show
where an older seed reaches an action test or a revision/release chapter while
the main charts continue to govern.
