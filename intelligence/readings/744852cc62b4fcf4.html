NVIDIA
NASDAQ: NVDA
Colusa AI and Energy Infrastructure Campus, Cachil Dehe Band land, northern California. Named technology-ecosystem partner for AI compute and proof-of-concept work.
Announced partnership / proof-of-concept deployment claimed. Larger campus remains pre-construction.
Colusa names NVIDIA among certified technology partners and says proof-of-concept deployments are underway through the existing tribal microgrid. No NVIDIA contract value, equipment order, investment, revenue recognition, or independently confirmed live workload is public.

WEC Energy Group
NYSE: WEC
Data Holdings, Forest County Potawatomi trust land, Milwaukee. WEC subsidiary We Energies supplies the facility's grid power.
Operating: facility opened in 2013 and currently provides colocation/private-cloud services.
DOE states that Data Holdings purchases power from We Energies. This is a real operating utility relationship, but Data Holdings is small relative to WEC and no facility-specific revenue is disclosed.
Blue Owl Capital
NYSE: OWL
Indirect exposure through Blue Owl's oversight of STACK Americas. STACK is reported as a prospective beneficiary/offtaker near the contested Goldendale pumped-storage and data-center development affecting a Yakama sacred landscape.
Watch only / connection unconfirmed. Energy project contested; nearby data-center/offtake arrangement not finalized publicly.
Blue Owl confirms that its Digital Infrastructure team oversees STACK Americas. Reporting links STACK to prospective Goldendale development, but STACK did not confirm the reported arrangement. This is not yet a verified tribal-land data-center project or contract.
Stage key
Operating exposure — WEC: the tribal facility exists, is energized, serves customers, and has an identified public utility supplier.
Named supplier exposure — NVDA, SMCI, AMD, INTC: the companies are named in an announced tribal project ecosystem; proof-of-concept work is claimed by the sponsors, but commercial commitments are undisclosed.
Adjacent capital exposure — OWL: the public company is connected to a private data-center operator appearing in an Indigenous opposition trail; the specific project relationship remains unconfirmed.

What is not publicly investable yet
The core project and land entities remain private or tribally owned: Colusa Indian Energy, Strata Expanse, Data Holdings/Potawatomi Ventures, UnityCloud, STACK Infrastructure, and DDN. No publicly traded hyperscaler has yet been verified as the tenant or offtaker for a specific contemporary tribal-land hyperscale campus.
Bottom line
The cleanest current public-market connection is hardware exposure to Colusa through NVDA, SMCI, AMD, and INTC. WEC is the only identified public company tied to an already operating tribal trust-land data center. OWL is an early watch rather than a confirmed tribal-project exposure. The major future tell is a binding disclosure naming a public hyperscaler, equipment order, utility contract, financing commitment, or revenue-bearing tenant at a specific tribal site.
