## Governing rule

Conjunction personas are kept as **conditional, subordinate, zero-vote
subsidiary charts**. For each canonical Long Clocks family, derive one shared
persona from the registry's `display_anchor_pass_id`: the first later
geocentric tropical solar crossing of that pass's fixed conjunction longitude,
cast for Washington, D.C., in Whole Sign houses with traditional rulers.

The conjunction **family**—not the exact pass and not the persona—remains the
cycle seed. Every registered pass and the co-presence window remain visible.
The persona does not govern time, create a phase chapter or seed echo, prove an
event, or add a convergence vote. Its sole job is to clarify how the
already-read seed may organize, enter, aim, or become operational.

One conjunction longitude produces **one shared persona with two focal
lenses**. It never produces two charts or two votes.

## Required hearing order

1. **Resolve governed identity.** Record the cycle, family, display-anchor
   pass, every family pass, fixed longitude, seed and persona clocks, elapsed
   interval, solar residual, solver receipt, and independent-verification
   status.
2. **Read the seed first.** Hear its authored cycle question, whole-chart
   gestalt, Ascendant and ruler, houses, actual MC and Whole Sign 10th,
   aspects, governance routes, root or loop, condition, available America
   contacts, and constructive, shadow, and continuity poles.
3. **Read the persona as one whole.** Hear its Big Three, Ascendant and ruler,
   pattern, Persona-Sun house, actual MC and Whole Sign 10th, both focal
   planets, governance routes, root or loop, aspects, and condition. The
   persona owns its houses; the seed is an overlay only.
4. **Compare after both wholes exist.** Classify every proposed finding as
   `guaranteed`, `dependent`, `seed_restatement`, `correlated`,
   `potentially_additive`, `contradiction`, or `unsafe_or_unsupported`.
   Retain the complete admitted relationship-delta ledger in machine-readable
   evidence. Mark the human-facing promoted subset as selective.
5. **Use two lenses, never two votes.** Ask what each focal planetary function
   wants, supplies, or constrains inside the same chart, then name the shared
   operating arrangement.
6. **Close answer-first.** Keep no more than one or two materially additive
   clarifications. Name constructive, shadow, and continuity. End with a
   question-only watch that may refine the parent Long Clock. Do not create a
   persona-owned State Transition Watch or Forecast Ledger call.

## Slow-body dependency and novelty gate

> A slow or outer planet being near its seed position is not news. Within 31
> days it is automatically dependent carryover. At any longer interval,
> proximity alone still does not qualify as additive. Promotion requires a
> verified station or direction change, sign change, gained, lost, or
> operator-changed relationship or formal lane entry or exit, or a genuinely
> new derived-angle relationship. The novelty belongs to the changed
> condition, relationship, or derived angle—not to the slow body's near-return.

Implementation boundaries:

- `outer_planets = Uranus, Neptune, Pluto`
- `tracked_slow_bodies = Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus, Neptune, Pluto`
- Mercury may enter the interval station scan, but is never relabeled slow or
  outer.
- Scan the whole interval, including ingress, re-entry, and D→R→D sequences.
- Mere tightening or widening is carryover unless a governed aspect lane is
  actually entered or exited. Record the operator change when it occurs.
- Mark relationship changes principally driven by a fast partner as such.
- A new persona angle contacting a slow body may qualify; that angle contacting
  the radix near-return of the same body is a correlated echo, not a second
  finding.
- The Persona Sun's seed degree and its seed-target contacts are guaranteed by
  construction.
- Focal planets remaining near the Persona Sun are dependent carryover.
- Multi-pass family geometry is seed-family context, never corroboration.
- Exactness alone never promotes a contact.

## Evidence and calculation boundaries

- Zodiac: geocentric tropical.
- Mundane place: Washington, D.C., `38.9072 N, 77.0369 W`, displayed in
  `America/New_York` local time.
- Houses: Whole Sign; traditional rulers.
- Comparison wheels: one explicit house owner. The persona owns a persona
  biwheel; seed angles remain labeled overlay markers.
- 916 America: include only when the canonical cache covers the required
  instant. Otherwise state `America unavailable`; never extrapolate.
- Runtime receipt: record the ephemeris flag actually returned. Requesting
  Swiss Ephemeris while the runtime falls back to Moshier is not independent
  Swiss-file verification.
- Astro Gold: pending or not recorded remains visible until the external check
  actually occurs.

## What the subsidiary chart can and cannot do

It can sharpen a seed's **operating arena, interface, routing, or public aim**
when those features are derived rather than mechanically inherited. It can
raise a disciplined question for the record.

It cannot:

- replace the canonical conjunction family;
- turn one pass into the whole cycle;
- create a new Long Clock hand, phase, chapter, seed echo, ingress, lunation,
  or day governor;
- make a slow-body near-return count as a discovery;
- corroborate itself through two focal lenses or multiple family passes;
- add an event vote, prove causation, or promote a literal prediction.

The acceptance test is simple: if removing the persona would change the
parent clock's timing or evidence status, the persona has been given too much
authority.
