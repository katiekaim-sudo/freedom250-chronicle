Five sets of COVID records, kept apart on purpose

This is an index holding five separate families of COVID records: the 2020 emergency-powers baseline, June 2026 intelligence releases, a July 2026 HHS research policy, the White House COVID.gov relaunch and Sen. Rand Paul's reading room. It keeps their claims from being merged.

What we found

  • ODNI released biolab slides on June 12, 2026 and a 67-item, 391-page COVID-origin release on June 18.
  • Sen. Rand Paul's COVID Origins Reading Room (July 29, 2026) is saved as a 14-PDF, 3,887-page set, including Proximal Origin Slack messages, a Baric transcribed interview, Fauci award records and 'Tony's Diary' packages.
  • HHS's high-risk life-sciences research policy is dated July 20 and was released July 28, 2026; it reaches federally funded and international research, and the study flags a gap for privately funded work.
  • The White House reactivated COVID.gov on July 29, 2026, the same day as a Fauci hearing; the package also notes an open contempt procedure.
  • The 2020 baseline is a 20-item set of primary sources on emergency powers, guidance changes, funding records and vaccine authorizations.

Why it matters

The government is reopening its own pandemic record from inside existing offices, through official releases, a new research policy and a relaunched website. Keeping allegation, funding and finding apart is what lets that record be tested rather than just repeated.

What to watch

  • pending · Implementation dates for the HHS high-risk research policy and whether it reaches privately funded research.
  • pending · The outcome of the open contempt procedure tied to the July 29 Fauci hearing.
