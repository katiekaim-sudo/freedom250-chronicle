October 2024 Libra eclipse seed → April 2026 Libra Full phase

The locked April 2, 2026 reading says the Full Moon reveals the cost of maintaining equilibrium: an exalted Aries Sun in the sixth faces a Libra Moon in the twelfth, while Venus in Taurus governs the entire chart from the seventh house and applies to Pluto. The Moon-family sequence shows that this was not a sudden Full-Moon arrangement. The missing First Quarter had already installed almost the same structural container nine months earlier.

The family begins with an annular solar eclipse in Libra in the tenth house. Sun, Moon and Mercury are together there: executive purpose, public condition and the record occupy the visible government room. At First Quarter, the Moon moves to the twelfth and remains there through Full phase. Venus moves from the eleventh to the seventh, becomes the sole final dispositor and remains there. Pluto moves from the first to the fourth and remains there. Saturn and Neptune move from the third to the sixth and remain there. The quarter is not the main character, but it quietly assigns nearly every room in which the later confrontation will occur.

The working judgment is:

> A public balancing act is seeded under eclipse, but the eclipse shadow > leaves long before harvest. First Quarter converts the public question into > a durable arrangement: the public need goes behind the wall, agreement and > counterpart terms acquire final authority, concentrated power settles into > the foundations, and institutional ambiguity enters the labor and service > system. Full phase does not invent that container. It illuminates the cost > of continuing to govern through it. The shadow departs; the terms remain.

The source-separated factual comparison is unusually clean. The East and Gulf Coast port strike began October 1, 2024. On October 3, the parties reached a provisional wage agreement, stopped the job actions and extended their prior contract while bargaining continued. A complete six-year agreement was announced in January 2025, ratified in February and signed in March—before the July First Quarter. By the April 2026 Full Moon, the contract was already an operating settlement. The Full Moon did not create it. What the factual record offers is a durable set of terms continuing to govern labor, technology and counterparty relations after the crisis window closed.

The Moon moves steadily away from the nodal axis. That distinguishes this family from Virgo and Pisces, where the later family chapter approaches or enters eclipse shadow. Libra's seed begins under shadow and then releases it. The later Full Moon is not a second eclipse and should not inherit eclipse status by narrative momentum.

The seed-to-Full degree gap is 2.289440°. First Quarter to Full is 1.189414°, or 71.3649 arcminutes. This is a clear same-sign family relay, but not an exact degree return. The more precise inheritance lies in the chart's container, terminal authority and slow-body pressure.

There is also an important astronomy correction. The October 2 event was globally annular: greatest eclipse occurred over the southeast Pacific, with annularity crossing southern Chile and Argentina. Washington did not see even a partial eclipse. At exact syzygy the Sun was 39.915° above the true horizon, but the local eclipse state was none because the Moon's shadow path missed the capital.

That distinction matters in mundane work. The Washington chart belongs to a real global eclipse, but a person standing in Washington saw an ordinary afternoon Sun. “Eclipse over the government” is therefore symbolic chart language, not a claim that the capital was physically darkened.

Full phase: Venus remains final, but final authority is under pressure

But Venus is not untouched. It applies to square Pluto in the fourth within 2.249°. A binding agreement can govern and still be tested by concentrated power in the foundations. Stability is not the same as freedom from pressure.

The Aries Sun in the sixth faces the Libra Moon in the twelfth. The visible executive/labor demand and the hidden public/institutional cost cannot be collapsed into one side. Mercury in Pisces trines Jupiter within 1.202°. The record can reach the legal or doctrinal field, but Mercury is in detriment and fall. Connection is possible; clean definition is not guaranteed.

Declination makes the institutional contradiction nearly exact

At Full phase, Saturn and Neptune are separated by 3.438450° in ecliptic longitude. They are an applying conjunction in Aries in the sixth house. Their declinations, however, sit on opposite sides of the celestial equator:

  • Saturn: +0.302279°
  • Neptune: −0.307442°
  • contra-parallel orb: 0.005163°, or about 18.59 arcseconds

This is not a longitude opposition. It is a longitude conjunction plus an extraordinarily tight contra-parallel. Rule and ideal occupy the same zodiacal room while moving on opposite north–south sides of the sky. Institutional limit and institutional ambiguity are joined in subject but polarized in orientation.

Two other declination relationships deepen the picture:

  • Jupiter contra-parallels Pluto within 0.078015°: legal, foreign or doctrinal scale faces concentrated foundational power; and
  • the Moon parallels Mercury within 0.132592°: hidden public need and the record move together even though they occupy different whole-sign houses.

The First Quarter already foreshadows the power axis. Its Sun contra-parallels Pluto within 0.106° and Jupiter contra-parallels Pluto within 0.133°. The later Jupiter–Pluto relationship is therefore not a one-night invention.

Source-separated political maturation: the settlement is already governing

The factual object was selected independently from the astrology. The date match is day-level only; no exact strike, bargaining or signing time is claimed where the primary sources do not supply one.

The previous ILA–USMX master contract expired September 30, 2024. A coastwide strike began October 1. On October 3, the parties announced that they had reached a tentative wage agreement, would cease current job actions and would resume covered work. They extended the prior master contract to January 15, 2025 so bargaining could continue on the remaining issues. The Labor Department welcomed the wage package and explicitly described the new contract as unfinished.

The later gates matter:

  1. October 3, 2024: provisional wage agreement and work resumption;
  2. January 8, 2025: tentative agreement on all items for a new six-year contract, still subject to approvals;
  3. February 25, 2025: ILA rank-and-file ratification, reported by the union at nearly 99 percent; and
  4. March 11, 2025: formal signature of the 2024–2030 master contract.

The executed term runs retroactively from October 1, 2024 through September 30, 2030. By the July 2 First Quarter, the contract had already been signed for nearly four months. By the April 2026 Full phase, it was still operating. No reviewed source identifies either lunar date as a new contractual gate.

The factual sequence is therefore not:

eclipse seed → quarter bargaining → Full-Moon agreement

It is:

strike / provisional wage settlement → complete tentative agreement → ratification → signature → continued governance under settled terms

That negative control prevents a seductive but false claim that the Full Moon “delivered” the agreement. It did not. The more bounded comparison is about what persists:

The public record also preserves the government's role boundary. Labor issued statements and USMX describes federal engagement during the dispute. That supports governmental attention and involvement. It does not prove that the government dictated the private agreement, compelled ratification or became a contracting party.

What this adds to the locked reading

The locked reading already saw Venus in Taurus as the final authority and the sixth/twelfth polarity as visible duty facing hidden need. The Moon family adds seven pieces:

  1. the story begins with a tenth-house Libra solar eclipse joining Sun, Moon and Mercury in the public government room;
  2. the eclipse shadow releases steadily rather than arriving at harvest;
  3. First Quarter moves the Moon into the twelfth and leaves it there through Full phase;
  4. First Quarter installs Venus as sole final dispositor in the seventh, the same authority retained at Full phase;
  5. quarter and Full share the Moon → Pluto empty-arc boundary and differ by less than one degree in raw distribution;
  6. quarter Pluto squares later Venus within 0.118°, preparing the pressure on agreement before the Full Moon; and
  7. Saturn–Neptune becomes a conjunction in longitude and an 18.59-arcsecond contra-parallel in declination.

The constructive pole is a settlement durable enough to move beyond the crisis that produced it while retaining a place for work, technology and counterparty obligation. The shadow pole is a balance maintained by moving public cost behind the wall, allowing binding terms to conceal who absorbs the pressure.

The chart does not decide which pole history chooses. The factual comparison does something more useful: it proves that the agreement was already made and therefore stops the Full Moon from claiming authorship of a result that preceded it.
