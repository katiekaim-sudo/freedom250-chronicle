What Crypto Companies Control: Who Owes Holders, Who Runs the Ledger, Who Reaches Customers

Twenty-two company groups and protocols: what each owns, where it fits and what it can block

The stablecoin competition is not one coin against another. Companies are racing to control the steps around the coin: legal liability to holders, reserves, ledgers, keys, risk, conversion to regular money and reaching customers.

LiabilityWho owes redemption, owns reserves and administers the token?
LedgerWho orders transactions, upgrades software and recovers from failure?
DistributionWho controls the wallet, customer, merchant and conversion relationship?
RiskWho chooses collateral, oracles, curators, venues and emergency powers?
EconomicsWho receives reserve income, interest, fees, spreads and token rewards?
SurvivalWhich claim and control surface survives if the visible brand disappears?

CircleIssuer groupsPublic filingUSDC live · move to a U.S. issuer not finished · IBM patents acquired

Circle starts from its regulated USDC coin and is expanding into payments, a blockchain and patents

What does it own or control?USDC and EURC, CCTP (Cross-Chain Transfer Protocol), Gateway, CPN (Circle Payments Network), wallet tools, Arc development, USYC administration and a newly acquired IBM blockchain patent portfolio.
Where does it sit?Issuer liability to holders, moving coins between blockchains, institutional settlement and its own purpose-built blockchain
What can it stop?Who can mint or redeem, supported blockchains, token addresses and compliance actions. Separate processors and blockchains keep their own approvals.
open the company constitution
Exact entities

Circle Internet Group, Inc. is the public parent. Circle Internet Financial, LLC is the current issuer outside the European Economic Area (EEA); Circle Internet Financial Europe SAS is the EEA issuer. The proposed Circle Internet Trust Company is the intended New York issuer. Circle National Trust has final approval from the Office of the Comptroller of the Currency (OCC) as a separate custody and trust company that does not issue coins. Circle Technology Services runs CPN technology without becoming the principal party to transactions.

Money objects

USDC and EURC are payment coins. USYC is a token for fund shares. The Arc network's asset and its infrastructure are separate things.

Economic engine

Reserve income makes up most revenue. Circle reported Q1 2026 reserve income of $652.5 million versus $41.6 million of other revenue. It also reported $405.4 million of distribution and transaction costs, including large payments to distributors. Platform, network, fund and future Arc income are secondary.

Control surface

Issuer contracts, token-admin functions, Circle Mint onboarding, CCTP and Gateway support, CPN participation and the Arc roadmap.

Outside dependencies

Circle relies on the BlackRock-managed Circle Reserve Fund, BNY as custodian, partner banks, supported blockchains, distributors such as Coinbase and Binance, and payment processors.

Stablecoin / DeFi role

USDC is the leading regulated base coin under exchanges, DeFi (decentralized finance) collateral, vaults and merchant payment services.

Merchant path

Merchants can accept USDC directly through processors. USDC can also settle or pay out behind a familiar checkout without the shopper seeing it.

GENIUS read

Strong chance of approval under the GENIUS Act. But the exact move to a U.S. issuer and product-specific approval are separate legal steps.

Live clock

Circle National Trust received final OCC approval on 2026-07-10, but it is not the issuer. Circle acquired the IBM patent portfolio on 2026-07-27; we have not found a public list of the patents. The proposed New York issuer and the transfer of USDC contracts to it (novation) are not yet proven.

Open questions

Which IBM patents cover bank integration, settlement among approved parties only, or enterprise controls? When will New York issuer terms line up with Arc and CPN production?

Failure mode

A reserve-bank or custodian problem, an outage in issuer access, a token-admin error, a bridge or routing failure, or a mismatch when legal duties move to a new issuer.

Do not overclaim

Buying IBM's blockchain patent portfolio did not give Circle COBOL, IBM mainframes or bank customer systems.

Distribution → full stack
CoinbaseCustomer distributorsCompany disclosurepayments, custody and Base live · figures are self-reported

Coinbase earns from distributing USDC and runs Base, but Circle issues the coin

What does it own or control?Exchange and custody platforms, Coinbase Developer Platform, payment APIs, wallets, x402 and its relationship with the Base network.
Where does it sit?Reaching customers, custody, conversion to regular money, merchant APIs and Base as the preferred place to run transactions
What can it stop?Listings, customer access, custody support, payment methods, API access, and access to Base's sequencer (which orders transactions) and interface. Circle keeps USDC issuer powers.
open the company constitution
Exact entities

Coinbase Global is the parent of regulated exchange, brokerage and custody subsidiaries. Base and each product role are separate; do not treat them as one legal person.

Money objects

Circle issues USDC. Coinbase distributes it and earns money under a commercial agreement. Other supported and custom stablecoins keep their own issuers.

Economic engine

Trading and custody fees, subscriptions and services, USDC-related income, payment infrastructure and activity tied to Base.

Control surface

Customer identity checks (KYC), supported assets, custody keys, merchant APIs and Base transaction ordering. Anyone can submit Base fault proofs, but upgrades need 9 of 12 Security Council members.

Outside dependencies

Coinbase relies on Circle to owe USDC holders, banks and card networks to move regular money in and out, Ethereum to settle Base, and outside processors and merchants.

Stablecoin / DeFi role

A major holder and router of USDC. Links stablecoins to trading, DeFi (decentralized finance) on Base, institutional custody and payments by AI agents.

Merchant path

Coinbase Payments handles acceptance. Processors such as Checkout.com or PPRO keep the merchant relationship and the contract to settle in regular money.

GENIUS read

Likely a critical digital-asset service provider and distributor, even when it is not the issuer.

Live clock

Coinbase announced merchant acceptance and enterprise payouts as live for eligible users. Headline volume and Base figures are company-reported.

Open questions

In each processor integration, who exactly is responsible for compliance, conversion, custody and refunds? What is the status of the proposed trust company?

Failure mode

One platform outage can hit transfers, card transactions and onchain services at the same time. A failure in Base's sequencer or in custody would hit many services at once.

Do not overclaim

Stablecoin volume on Base is not Coinbase revenue, USDC in circulation or merchant-payment volume. Open fault proofs do not remove Coinbase's control of the sequencer and interface.

Ripple familyIssuer groupsCurrent legal termsRLUSD live · set of acquired businesses growing

Ripple owns the widest range of institutional services around its RLUSD stablecoin

What does it own or control?Payments software, a central place in the XRP Ledger (XRPL) ecosystem, Standard Custody, Ripple Custody, Ripple Prime, Ripple Treasury and acquired wallet and payment systems.
Where does it sit?Issuing a stablecoin, custody, prime brokerage, connecting corporate treasuries and routing institutional payments
What can it stop?Standard Custody controls RLUSD issuance and redemption. It can freeze, blacklist, burn or replace tokens and suspend redemptions. Product subsidiaries control access to custody, prime and treasury services. XRPL validators keep their network roles.
open the company constitution
Exact entities

Ripple Labs Inc. is separate from Standard Custody & Trust Company, which issues RLUSD today. Ripple National Trust has only preliminary, conditional approval. Hidden Road Partners CIV US LLC—not Ripple Labs—holds the relevant memberships in FICC (Fixed Income Clearing Corporation) and DTC (Depository Trust Company). The Metaco/Palisade, Rail and GTreasury entities keep the specific roles they had when acquired.

Money objects

RLUSD (owed by its issuer), XRP (the ledger's native asset), third-party assets issued on XRPL, and customer securities and collateral are legally separate.

Economic engine

Payments, custody and software fees; prime brokerage and financing; treasury software; XRP holdings and markets; and RLUSD-related business.

Control surface

RLUSD token administration, customer onboarding, custody policies, prime-brokerage collateral, software integration, and market memberships held by specific acquired subsidiaries.

Outside dependencies

BNY for reserve custody and banking, other banks and exchanges, the specific subsidiaries that are DTC and FICC members, XRPL validators and outside blockchains such as Ethereum.

Stablecoin / DeFi role

RLUSD is a regulated base coin for tokenized funds, DeFi (decentralized finance) and Ripple Payments. USDC also exists natively on XRPL.

Merchant path

Ripple Payments and the acquired Rail systems can convert between stablecoins and regular money. Evidence of direct use at consumer checkout terminals is more limited.

GENIUS read

RLUSD likely seeks approval as a state-qualified issuer through Standard Custody. Ripple National Trust is on a separate timeline.

Live clock

Standard Custody issuance, Ripple Prime, Ripple Treasury and Ripple Custody are live. Ripple National Trust has only preliminary, conditional approval. The deciding proof is repeated transactions that use several acquired businesses together.

Open questions

Which acquired connections become Ripple's own production systems? Does post-trade activity actually move to XRPL?

Failure mode

Too many services concentrated in one corporate group, a custodian or issuer failure, losses at the prime broker, a token-admin action, or a ledger or bridge incident.

Do not overclaim

A subsidiary's connection to DTC, FICC, Swift, Canton or a bank does not prove that institutions have adopted XRP, XRPL or RippleNet.

TetherIssuer groupsCurrent legal termsUSD₮ global · USA₮ a separate coin issued by Anchorage

Tether keeps global trading in USD₮ while Anchorage issues its separate U.S. coin, USA₮

What does it own or control?The Tether brand, offshore token relationships and a broad investment and technology group. It licenses a separate U.S. product that Anchorage issues.
Where does it sit?Liquidity on global exchanges, reserve management and token administration
What can it stop?Tether controls direct customer issuance and redemption, supported networks and token freezes. Exchanges and Anchorage separately control access to their products.
open the company constitution
Exact entities

Tether International, S.A. de C.V. is the legal entity behind USD₮ after a documented 2025 assignment (legal transfer). Tether Operations owns and licenses the brand and operating intellectual property but does not issue USA₮. Anchorage Digital Bank issues USA₮ under a separate reserve trust.

Money objects

Offshore USD₮ and U.S. USA₮ share a brand but not an issuer, reserve or legal promise.

Economic engine

Reserve income, returns on its investment portfolio, issuance and redemption relationships, and group investments.

Control surface

Token freeze and burn, primary minting and redemption only after know-your-customer (KYC) checks, choice of blockchains, self-custody wallet distribution and reserve allocation.

Outside dependencies

Banks, custodians, primary dealers, exchanges, blockchains and—only for USA₮—Anchorage and its reserve trust.

Stablecoin / DeFi role

USD₮ is a leading base coin for trading and DeFi (decentralized finance). What its reserves hold differs materially from the assets the GENIUS Act permits.

Merchant path

It usually reaches merchants through conversion at an exchange, wallet or processor, not through direct retail redemption.

GENIUS read

Offshore USD₮ needs a foreign-issuer approval path or limits on its distribution. USA₮ is a separate regulated U.S. option.

Live clock

Watch whether U.S. access moves to USA₮ while global trading stays in USD₮.

Open questions

For each product: its exact status under GENIUS, its U.S. distribution policy and its reserve and custody details.

Failure mode

Losses at reserves or custodians, exchange delisting, a redemption backlog, a mismatch between legal entities, or token controls on a specific blockchain.

Do not overclaim

USA₮'s Anchorage and Cantor structure does not apply to USD₮.

White-label issuer
Paxos / PayPalIssuer groupsCurrent legal termsPYUSD, USDP and USDG live, issued by different Paxos entities

Paxos issues the coins and owes holders while PayPal and other brands reach customers

What does it own or control?Paxos runs regulated systems for issuing coins and tokenizing assets. PayPal controls the consumer wallet and the merchant channels that distribute the coin.
Where does it sit?Issuing coins under other brands (white-label), running reserves and redemptions, and branded consumer access
What can it stop?Paxos controls minting, redemption and token administration. PayPal Digital controls customer support, the custody product customers see, and whether the product is available.
open the company constitution
Exact entities

Paxos Trust Company, N.A. issues PYUSD and USDP under current terms. Paxos Digital Singapore and Paxos Issuance Europe manage and issue USDG. PayPal Digital holds the customer crypto contract and the rewards relationship but does not issue PYUSD. Paxos Securities Settlement Company is a separate clearing entity.

Money objects

PYUSD, USDP, USDG and legacy BUSD each have their own issuer and terms, even though they share systems.

Economic engine

Reserve income, fees for issuance, tokenization and settlement, and deals that share earnings with distributors. PayPal Digital separately runs a PYUSD rewards program with a variable rate, paid in PYUSD.

Control surface

Issuer terms, reserve accounts, token administration, who qualifies for a direct account, and distribution contracts.

Outside dependencies

Banks and custodians holding reserves, PayPal and other distributors, supported blockchains and payment networks.

Stablecoin / DeFi role

Paxos coins can move into wallets, exchanges and DeFi (decentralized finance), while Paxos remains the place to redeem.

Merchant path

Shoppers can pay with PYUSD at PayPal checkout. Other crypto may be converted to regular money at checkout, so the merchant is settled as usual.

GENIUS read

Paxos is well prepared to be an issuer. Product-specific approvals and the treatment of distributor payments remain open.

Live clock

Current terms dated 2026-06-30 name the issuing entities. Moving PayPal customer contracts did not change the issuer.

Open questions

How the limits on issuers paying yield treat Paxos's shared earnings and PayPal Digital's balance-based rewards. Which Paxos entity serves each jurisdiction.

Failure mode

Losses at the issuer or custodian, a distributor dropping the product, a blockchain admin action, or confusing the brand with the company that owes holders.

Do not overclaim

PayPal does not owe PYUSD redemption just because the token carries its brand.

Stripe can make spending from a DeFi-funded balance look like an ordinary payment

What does it own or control?Merchant processing, Bridge (stablecoin payment routing and Open Issuance), Privy wallets and a strategic relationship with the Tempo payments blockchain.
Where does it sit?Merchant acceptance, conversion to regular money, in-app wallets, branded coin issuance, cards and platform accounts
What can it stop?Which merchants and payments qualify, wallet setup, supported assets, conversion paths, the card program and regional access.
open the company constitution
Exact entities

The Stripe parent and its regional payment companies are separate from Bridge Building Inc. (U.S.), Bridge Building Sp. z o.o. (European Economic Area), Bridge Building Limited (other regions) and Horkos, LLC/Privy. Each product's sponsor bank, custodian and issuer must be identified separately.

Money objects

USDC, EURC, and Bridge-issued or custom stablecoins. A Morpho position is a separate DeFi (decentralized finance) claim. A card transaction remains a card-network obligation.

Economic engine

Payment processing, currency exchange and payment routing, issuing, financial accounts, platform services and acquired systems.

Control surface

Dashboard and API access, identity checks (KYC), who holds wallet keys, which issuer is used, supported payment paths and card approvals.

Outside dependencies

Banks, card networks, blockchains, Bridge's bank-payment connections, licensed custodians and Morpho vault liquidity.

Stablecoin / DeFi role

Links balances to branded coins, in-app wallets and Morpho yield picked by curators.

Merchant path

A stablecoin balance or DeFi-funded wallet can pay through a regular card or Stripe checkout, while the merchant receives ordinary settlement.

GENIUS read

The issuer depends on the product. Stripe or Bridge may be distributor, payment router, issuer or program manager in different flows.

Live clock

Bridge, Privy and some stablecoin products are live. Tempo had a public test network. Stripe's Sessions 2026 announcements list generally available (GA), preview and future-quarter items separately.

Open questions

Who exactly issues and holds custody for each custom coin? How do liquidation and credit work when someone spends from a DeFi position?

Failure mode

One outage hitting payments, wallets and routing at once, a sponsor-bank failure, a smart-contract loss, or unclear treatment of who owns customer property.

Do not overclaim

A roadmap item is not a live product. Bridge routing payments does not automatically mean Bridge issued the coin.

Protocol substrate
Ethereum ecosystemBlockchain networksProtocol documentationlive · several independent software clients · community-governed

Ethereum hosts stablecoins and other financial tokens but is not their issuer

What does it own or control?No single body owns Ethereum. The Ethereum Foundation, software client teams, validators, node operators, standards authors, infrastructure firms and layer-2 (L2) network operators have different roles.
Where does it sit?Running and settling transactions, smart-contract records and apps that plug into each other
What can it stop?Validators can censor transactions or leave them out. Client teams and the community can choose software and recovery plans. Application admins keep their own keys.
open the company constitution
Exact entities

The Ethereum Foundation is a Swiss Stiftung (foundation) and an influential funder and organizer. It is not the protocol, the validators or an issuer. Consensys Software Inc. runs products such as MetaMask and Infura and contributes client software, but it does not own Ethereum.

Money objects

ETH pays transaction fees (gas) and secures consensus. USDC, PYUSD, USDe, GHO and tokenized securities are liabilities or claims of other systems.

Economic engine

ETH fees and burn, validator rewards and MEV (profit from ordering transactions), L2 income, RPC and wallet services, and application fees.

Control surface

EIP (Ethereum Improvement Proposal) debate, AllCoreDevs coordination, independent client builds, node and validator adoption, rare community-led recovery, and app and L2 upgrade keys.

Outside dependencies

Client diversity, validator concentration, RPC access, bridges and L2s, oracles (outside data feeds), app admins and stablecoin issuers.

Stablecoin / DeFi role

The main network where issuer coins, lending, decentralized exchanges (DEXs), synthetic dollars, vaults and tokenized assets work together.

Merchant path

Usually through L2s, wallets, processors or crypto-linked cards, not direct retail use of the base layer-1 (L1) chain.

GENIUS read

The network does not decide whether a coin is a payment stablecoin. The exact issuer and its redemption promise do.

Live clock

Protocol upgrades need client teams to build them and validators and nodes to adopt them. No single company controls every layer.

Open questions

In practice, how concentrated are clients, staking providers, RPC providers, L2 sequencers and stablecoin admins?

Failure mode

Client bug, validator concentration, finality failure, community split, L2 or bridge failure, or a compromised app admin.

Do not overclaim

The Ethereum Foundation (EF) does not own Ethereum, and Consensys does not control it. Validators do not formally vote on every EIP. Stablecoin value does not become EF revenue or liability.

Distribution network
Stellar / SDFBlockchain networksCompany disclosurecash conversion and stablecoin payments live · DTC connection not yet live

Stellar treats reaching users, not the token, as the main constraint

What does it own or control?The Stellar Development Foundation (SDF) supports protocol development and ecosystem programs. Third-party issuers, anchors (cash-conversion companies), validators, wallets and MoneyGram keep separate roles.
Where does it sit?Moving assets cheaply, standards for anchors, converting cash, and distributing regulated assets
What can it stop?Each issuer can set authorization, freeze or clawback-style controls on its asset. Anchors can refuse conversion. Validators choose which validators they trust (quorum sets) and vote on protocol versions and settings.
open the company constitution
Exact entities

The Stellar Development Foundation is a nonprofit support body. It is not the issuer of USDC, PYUSD, MGUSD or tokenized funds.

Money objects

XLM pays fees and minimum balances (reserves). Third-party stablecoins and securities keep their own legal issuers.

Economic engine

Users need XLM for fees, rent and minimum balances. But fees go into a fee pool no one can access, and validators receive no protocol rewards. Anchors, wallets and applications earn separately.

Control surface

Protocol releases, validator quorum choices, SDF ecosystem funding, issuer settings on each asset, anchors and wallet integrations.

Outside dependencies

Issuers, the MoneyGram/Crossmint cash network, anchors, validators, wallets, card networks and a future DTC (Depository Trust Company) connection.

Stablecoin / DeFi role

USDC, PYUSD and locally distributed stablecoins can connect cash, remittances, aid, wallets and tokenized assets.

Merchant path

MoneyGram and wallet apps convert cash to and from stablecoins. Visa/Wirex settlement and spending through apps offer other paths.

GENIUS read

SDF is normally infrastructure, not a payment-stablecoin issuer. Each asset on Stellar needs its own legal test.

Live clock

Cash conversion and payments are live. The DTC connection targets H1 2027 (first half). The Protocol 26/CAP-77 emergency ledger-key quarantine must be confirmed on mainnet before we call it live.

Open questions

How wider distribution turns into lasting income for the network and token, and how issuer controls work with Soroban smart contracts.

Failure mode

Anchor insolvency, issuer freeze, validator quorum fault, wallet failure or trading split across too many places.

Do not overclaim

SDF is not a bank or stablecoin issuer. XLM is not reserve backing. A DTC connection does not replace DTC's own books. Validators are not paid like miners or stakers.

Governed network
HederaBlockchain networksProtocol documentationnetwork and tools live · range of real production use still uneven

Hedera offers businesses built-in tools that let issuers control their own tokens

What does it own or control?A network whose rules and ownership the Hedera Council governs, plus ecosystem tools. Stablecoin Studio is an open-source kit that third-party issuers use to issue coins.
Where does it sit?Council-run consensus, built-in token tools, predictable fees and business workflows mixing public and private systems
What can it stop?The Council governs network software, treasury and pricing. Token issuers set their own keys for identity checks (KYC), freezing, pausing, wiping balances and supply.
open the company constitution
Exact entities

Hedera Hashgraph, LLC dba (doing business as) Hedera Council is the Delaware entity that governs the network. Hedera Foundation and Hashgraph are independent ecosystem and software organizations. Hiero is the open-source project. All are separate from token issuers.

Money objects

HBAR pays public-network fees and secures consensus. Stablecoins, tokenized collateral and state-issued tokens are claims on third parties.

Economic engine

Network fees paid in HBAR and ecosystem activity. Issuers and applications earn separately.

Control surface

Council decisions govern treasury, pricing, membership, releases and mainnet-node policy. Only Council members currently run mainnet consensus nodes. Each token is controlled by keys its issuer sets in the Hedera Token Service (HTS): admin, supply, KYC, freeze, wipe, fee and pause.

Outside dependencies

Council continuity, nodes, outside issuers, custodians, reserve-data providers and oracles (outside data feeds) such as Chainlink.

Stablecoin / DeFi role

Stablecoin Studio provides connection points for compliance and proof of reserves. Hedera Token Service provides built-in controls over a token's whole life.

Merchant path

Issuer and payment-provider apps can use Hedera. Merchant acceptance still runs through wallets, processors and acquirers (merchants' payment banks).

GENIUS read

Stablecoin Studio may help an issuer meet control rules. It cannot supply the legal issuer, rights to reserves or approval.

Live clock

Wyoming FRNT and commercial projects show real deployment. Institutional pilots still need confirmation that they are in production.

Open questions

Which uses repeat at scale, and how public Hedera connects with private HashSphere networks.

Failure mode

Council or node concentration, stolen keys, an issuer-admin action, an oracle or custody fault, or private and public networks falling out of sync.

Do not overclaim

Open-source Hiero code does not let anyone join mainnet without approval. Council governance does not make Hedera the issuer of every token made with its tools.

Data providers, node operators, sending and receiving blockchains, protocol admins and institutional counterparties.

Merchant path

Indirect. It supports reliable conversion, risk checks and cross-chain settlement that wallets and processors use.

GENIUS read

Likely an intermediary or service provider, not an issuer. Legal duties depend on the exact service and its control over transfers.

Live clock

It is used in production. But public terms on liability, corrections and recovery for institutional workflows remain limited.

Open questions

Which services staking secures, who bears losses from bad data, and how corrections for institutions are decided.

Failure mode

Wrong or outdated data, nodes colluding, a cross-chain hack, a misconfigured route or unclear liability.

Do not overclaim

An oracle integration is not issuance or custody. LINK does not back stablecoins moved between chains. Staking does not currently secure every service.
