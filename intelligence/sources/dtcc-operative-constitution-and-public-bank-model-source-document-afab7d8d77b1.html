DTCC Operative Constitution and Public-Bank Model — Source Document

[!abstract] Controlling ruling The near-term federal-securities model is one fungible national market with an optional post-trade token-form instruction. DTC—not the chain alone—decides eligibility, maintains the official token-entitlement record and can correct or reverse token state. Public exchange rules already describe the instruction, but the DTC supplemental Participant and wallet terms that allocate commercial loss were not located in the public rulebook, procedures or fee guide. Collateral AppChain remains a credible expected product architecture without a public operator contract, settlement cash, finality rule or waterfall. The public-bank case supports a sensitivity model, not a generic capital-release claim.

3. Public exchange rule / private Participant constitution seam

Nasdaq has a Commission approval order. NYSE, NYSE Texas, NYSE Arca, NYSE National, NYSE American and 24X filed substantially similar immediately effective rules. None can begin tokenized trading until DTC infrastructure/post-trade service exists and the venue gives its required advance notice. O/F

ordinary order + optional tokenization preference
  -> existing exchange match/routing/execution
  -> flag transmitted to DTC after execution
  -> DTC validates Participant + security + chain + wallet + controls
     -> executable: token-form processing
     -> not executable: traditional DTC settlement
  -> T+1 remains

Traditional and tokenized shares must be fungible and share the class, rights, identifier, symbol, order book, price/time priority, routing and fees. Public market data does not distinguish the form; CAT can receive tokenization data as required. This is one price-discovery pool with multiple post-trade representations, not an on-chain exchange or atomic settlement cycle. O/F/I

The DTC FAQ says a Participant must acknowledge supplemental Participant terms, register wallets and identify primary users. The current public DTC Rules, fee guide, Settlement, Distributions, Reorganizations and Operational Arrangements reviewed do not contain tokenization terms. The supplement likely carries the legally decisive instruction, indemnity, correction, liability and claims rules, but it was not located publicly. F/NR

SEC staff knowingly permitted the Preliminary Base Version to proceed without ordinary Rule 19b-4 filings on the represented facts. Staff did not approve DTC's legal conclusions and can modify or revoke relief. This is a procedural and transparency design fact—not evidence of wrongdoing. S

The public gap is not administrator capability; it is the decision and loss constitution:

  • root-key approval and emergency-control architecture;
  • proof of theft, error or malfeasance;
  • chain/LedgerScan last-good state and replay order;
  • notice and appeal before/after forced action;
  • market, tax and financing loss after reversal;
  • failed-Participant wallet freeze, porting and customer treatment; and
  • treatment of prior on-chain pledges and secured-party priority. NR

5. Public-bank financial model

The representative filings cover JPMorgan, BNY, State Street, Bank of America, Citi and Goldman Sachs. June 30 reported SLRs ranged from 4.3% at Goldman Sachs to 6.3% at BNY; standardized CET1 ratios ranged from 10.8% at State Street to 14.1% at JPMorgan. A common release coefficient cannot represent firms subject to different legal-entity, leverage, RWA, LCR, stress and internal constraints. F

Gross repo, reverse repo, securities borrowing/lending, collateralized financing, custody AUC/A and client securities-on-loan figures are not AppChain-eligible exposure. They mix principal and agency activity, products, terms, counterparties, netting sets, legal entities and reporting clocks. F/I

eligible daily exposure × realized timing reduction × all-in spread difference

+ separately recognized binding-constraint reduction + demonstrated opex reduction

− platform, custody, integration, control, dual-run, contingency, capital, tax and stress costs

“Freed resource” must be named as cash, HQLA, regulatory capital, internal buffer, collateral capacity or balance-sheet exposure. Freed capital is not revenue. D/I

Published 2025 Federal Reserve daylight-overdraft fees totaled $12,404; peak overdrafts were 95–100% collateralized and averages were 91–98% collateralized. Direct Fed fee avoidance cannot support the promoted million-dollar case. Private credit, spread, collateral opportunity cost and internal buffer can still matter, but they are separate objects. F/D

6. Sunday-night tokenized-Treasury correction

[!important] Mandatory subsidiary separation NSCC's Sunday 8:00 p.m. immediate guarantee is an equities-clearing fact. It does not attach to a free DTC token transfer or a Treasury repo. A Sunday Treasury repo remains bilateral until actual FICC/GSD comparison and novation.

DTCC lists FICC GSD/MBSD data-transmission/connectivity support from Monday 12:00 a.m. The GSD Schedule's 8:00 p.m. final-input deadline belongs to its Business Day cycle; it is not a Sunday guarantee trigger. F

Current GSD Rule 12 requires eligible Treasury settlement through clearing banks against simultaneous federal-funds payment. No public GSD rule identifies a DTC token or LedgerScan position as settlement delivery or Clearing Fund collateral. O/F/NR

9. AppChain constitutional gates

Before AppChain can be promoted from product architecture into a legally final settlement or loss-mutualization system, the public record needs:

  • contracting operator and regulatory perimeter;
  • participant agreement, governing law and forum;
  • validator admission and protocol-change authority;
  • source-record precedence and root/fork/rollback rules;
  • collateral form, control, perfection and priority;
  • cash issuer/account/redemption/finality/discharge;
  • valuation/oracle, stale-price and appeal rules;
  • eligibility, haircut, concentration and wrong-way-risk rules;
  • reuse/substitution and cross-CCP reservation priority;
  • default, liquidation, recovery and loss waterfall;
  • outage, error, liability, indemnity and tariff; and
  • explicit DTC/NSCC/FICC rule recognition for settlement, margin or default value. NR

Final rulings

  1. The exchange layer is legally ahead of DTC's public commercial layer.
  2. The token is a post-trade representation inside one fungible market, not a new exchange.
  3. DTC preserves Cede, Article 8, LedgerScan and administrator authority.
  4. Staff non-enforcement is not Commission approval or a public Participant loss contract.
  5. DTC's control power is visible; proof, appeal and compensation rules are not.
  6. AppChain's operator, cash, finality, perfection, default and fee constitution remains public-NR.
  7. The $225m case implies 12%, not a demonstrated funding-spread saving.
  8. Gross bank collateral/custody scale is not releasable capital.
  9. NSCC Sunday guarantees are equities-specific; FICC Treasury novation has its own clock.
  10. A movable token is not automatically CCP collateral, final payment, legal discharge or accounting close.
