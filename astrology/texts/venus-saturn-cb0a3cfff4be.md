# Venus–Saturn Cycle Review

**Enduring question:** What value, alliance, or peace can endure obligation, scarcity, and institutional judgment?  
**Disposition:** 2025 is a primary pilot; 2026 is mostly an arena translation.

## Family record

- 2025: three-pass Pisces family; proposed P1 candidate +46.368 days, before two later exact recontacts.
- 2026: single Aries family; candidate +14.702 days.
- 2027: first-after bracket.

## What the charts add

The 2025 seed puts exalted Venus with Saturn in Pisces/7th under Virgo rising. Mercury sextiles both within 0.371°, so terms and record are built into the pact. At the candidate, Venus is retrograde and in detriment in Aries/3rd, while Saturn remains Pisces but moves to the 2nd. The seed's Grand Trine/Kite/rectangle structure becomes a single stellium circuit. Agreement, value, obligation, and the record carrying them become different jobs.

The 2026 seed places detrimented Venus with fallen Saturn in Aries/12th. Fourteen days later both remain together in Aries/4th; the new frame locates the issue but adds little focal differentiation.

## Narrowing use

For Crypto, banking, alliances, or peace terms, ask what obligation is enforceable, what value is actually held, and what record or counterparty carries the pact. This is stronger than a generic austerity or hardship label.
