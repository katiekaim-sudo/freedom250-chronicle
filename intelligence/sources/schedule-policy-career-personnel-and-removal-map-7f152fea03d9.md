# Schedule Policy/Career — Personnel and Removal Map

Updated: 2026-08-15  
Status: factual personnel-control map  
Rule: count the appendix, not the slogan. Conversion of a career job is not removal of a principal officer. Those are different creatures. Officer leash: [Independent-Agency Officer Removal — Slaughter and Cook](../sources/independent-agency-officer-removal-slaughter-and-cook-434d0b12f5a3.html).

## Short answer

Schedule Policy/Career is the excepted-service box for **career** jobs the President has determined to be "confidential, policy-determining, policy-making, or policy-advocating." EO 14171 (Jan. 20, 2025) reinstated EO 13957 (the 2020 Schedule F order) and renamed the schedule. EO 14410 (June 3, 2026; 91 FR 34893) **places the positions in the Appendix into that schedule** and tells each agency head to notify the encumbering employees within seven days.

The control move is 5 U.S.C. § 7511(b)(2). Chapter 75 adverse-action procedures "do not apply" to an employee "whose position has been determined to be of a confidential, policy-determining, policy-making or policy-advocating character" by the President (for a position the President has excepted from the competitive service). That is the statutory hook the orders use. It is a personnel-status change. It is not a rewrite of an agency's organic act. It is not *Humphrey's Executor*.

The vault file for EO 14410 ends at the general provisions. The Appendix lives beside it: EO-14410 — Appendix — Schedule Policy-Career positions (captured 2026-08-15; 4,861 PD numbers + 1 NIH class). Count that sibling, not a press figure.

## 2. The three excepted schedules that matter

The orders themselves distinguish them.

| Schedule | Source | What it is for | Removal |
|---|---|---|---|
| **C** | Existing excepted service | Noncareer jobs of a confidential or policy-determining character. Political. | Already outside ordinary career tenure |
| **Policy/Career** | EO 13957 as amended by 14171; implemented by 14410 | **Career** jobs of a confidential, policy-determining, policy-making, or policy-advocating character, "not normally subject to change as a result of a Presidential transition." Filled on merit, not political affiliation (EO 14410 § 1). | Chapter 75 procedures do not apply once the position is so determined (§ 7511(b)(2)) |
| **G** | EO 14317 (July 17, 2025) | A further excepted schedule created beside Policy/Career. EO 14410 treats Schedule G with Schedule C as the noncareer side for several Civil Service Rule edits (trial periods; qualifying appointment for severance). | Do not treat G as Policy/Career. Read 14317 for that box. |

EO 14410 § 1: "Schedule Policy/Career positions are policy-influencing career positions that will be filled based on merit and not political affiliation. At the same time, Schedule Policy/Career positions are exempted from the adverse action procedures…"

That is the whole design: career hire, easier fire.

## 3. Legal stack

**5 U.S.C. § 7511(b)(2)** — chapter 75 (removal, long suspension, reduction in grade or pay, long furlough) does not apply to an employee whose position has been determined to be of a confidential, policy-determining, policy-making or policy-advocating character by:

- (A) the President, for a position the President has excepted from the competitive service;
- (B) OPM, for a position OPM has excepted;
- (C) the President or an agency head, for a position excepted by statute.

EO 14410 § 5(a)–(b) is the (A) determination plus the exception: the Appendix positions "are hereby placed in Schedule Policy/Career of the excepted service." Subsequent renumbering or realignment of a listed position "do not alter its placement."

**What conversion does**

- Moves the *position* out of the competitive service (or from Schedule A/B/D) into Schedule Policy/Career.
- Turns off chapter 75 for that position under § 7511(b)(2).
- Requires 7-day notice to the person sitting in the chair (EO 14410 § 5(c)).

**What conversion does not do**

- It does not repeal an agency organic act.
- It does not, by itself, fire anyone. Removal is a later act.
- It does not strip veterans preference from the *hiring* rule: EO 14410 § 2(c)(i)(B) keeps "the principle of veteran preference as far as administratively feasible."
- It does not, in the text of these orders, repeal 5 U.S.C. § 2302 (prohibited personnel practices) or the whistleblower statutes. Residual overlays stay until a later instrument removes them. Do not invent a repeal.
- Competitive status: EO 14410 § 2(a) amends Civil Service Rule I so an employee who *had* competitive status when the position is first listed "shall be in the excepted service but shall retain his competitive status." Status retained is not chapter-75 coverage retained.

EO 14410 § 2(f) adds a severance-pay wrinkle: a Policy/Career employee whose agency identifies unacceptable performance or misconduct in a written notice may fall into a new § 550.704(b)(6) category. That is a pay-on-the-way-out rule, not a hearing.

## 4. The clocks

| Date | Object | What it legally did | What it did not do |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2020-10-21 | EO 13957 | Created Schedule F | Did not survive the next administration as operative policy |
| 2021 (Biden revocation) | Later order revoking 13957 | Took Schedule F off the table | Not re-read here; 14171 is the reinstatement |
| 2025-01-20 | EO 14171 | Reinstates 13957 "with full force and effect," subject to named amendments; replaces the letter "F" with the words "Policy/Career" | Does not itself list the jobs |
| 2025-07-17 | EO 14317 | Creates Schedule G | Not the Policy/Career census |
| 2026-02-06 | OPM final rule, FR 2026-02375 | OPM rulemaking the January 2025 order directed. **Full text not extracted into this note at cutoff.** | A rule is not the Appendix listing |
| 2026-06-03 / pub. 2026-06-10 | EO 14410, 91 FR 34893–35124 | Determines the Appendix positions; places them in Schedule Policy/Career; 7-day notice clock; amends Civil Service Rules | Does not enact a statute. Does not fire. Appendix sibling captured 2026-08-15 |
| 2026-06-10 + 7 days | Agency notice under § 5(c) | Records and occupants are to be conformed | Notice is not a removal |

Two clocks, always: **rule / order effective** vs **this position listed**. A person is not in Schedule Policy/Career because the schedule exists. They are in it when their position is on the Appendix or on a later listing OPM publishes.

## 5. Who actually moved

EO 14410 § 5 points at "the positions set forth in the Appendix." The Federal Register document is 232 pages. That appendix is the census.

Counted 2026-08-15 from the Appendix itself: **4,861** position-description numbers plus **one** NIH class listing (branch chief / division director over a ≥$20 million grant portfolio). Sibling: EO-14410 — Appendix — Schedule Policy-Career positions. Do not use "50,000" — that figure is not in the Appendix.

A later agency petition under amended EO 13957 § 5(a) can add more positions. Those additions are a second wave. They are not the June 3 list.

## 8. Entity Theory read

The operating subsidiaries still have the same charters. What changed is the tenure of the people who turn the charter's wheels. Conversion is a control change without a charter amendment — the same grammar as starving a bureau's balances without repealing its organic act. The Appendix is now in the vault beside EO 14410. The entity chart can mark the schedule and the listed chairs. No new chart nodes were added.
