# Venus–Mars

## The planets

**Venus** — *Magnetize, value, harmony, beauty of form.* MUNDANE: the women of the nation; women's vote and issues; the arts, culture, tourism, entertainment; diplomacy and social harmony. Venus shows where the nation seeks peace, pleasure, and economic ease.

**Mars** — *Ares, conflict, action, capacity to assert.* MUNDANE: the military; war and violent crime; epidemic disease; natural disasters. Also: the men of the nation, men's vote and issues. Mars angular = classic war warning. Mars shows where the nation's combative energy is directed.

## The combination — mundane

Venus-Mars is the oldest polarity in mundane work: diplomacy versus conflict, trade versus war, the drawing room versus the battlefield. Under helpful aspects (conjunction when well-placed, sextile, trine), Greer describes results "favorable for economic matters; improvement of domestic and foreign trade; increase in birthrate; fortunate for entertainment." The economy benefits because Mars's energy is channeled into productive enterprise rather than destruction. Entertainment and the arts flourish — the nation is in a mood to enjoy itself. The birthrate note is distinctive: when the masculine and feminine principles cooperate, the nation literally grows.

Under hostile aspects (square, opposition, poorly-placed conjunction), Venus-Mars brings what Greer calls conditions "unfortunate for economic conditions; excessive government expenditure; scandals; birthrate declines." The economy suffers because martial energy disrupts commercial harmony. Government spending surges — often on military or enforcement — at the expense of the arts, diplomacy, and social programs. Scandals erupt, frequently involving the intersection of sex and power. The birthrate decline signals a nation where the masculine and feminine principles are at war rather than in partnership.

**As trigger:** Venus-Mars aspects are fast-moving (days to a couple of weeks) and act as ignition points, lighting up the slower outer-planet themes wherever trade-versus-conflict or diplomacy-versus-force tensions are already simmering.
