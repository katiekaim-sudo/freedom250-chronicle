Tokenization and the new market plumbing

What actually moves when stocks, deposits and payments go onto ledgers, and who still runs the pipes?

The braid so far

What moves onto a ledger is still a second copy of an old instrument, and tonight the copy was used as collateral. BNY and Citadel Securities ran a 2-year Treasury through BNY's private digital ledger, settled it within minutes, and posted it in an overnight triparty repo. DTCC's Fund/SERV, which handles more than 85 percent of U.S. mutual-fund transactions, added Ondo Finance as its first tokenization member, held by Ondo's broker-dealer Oasis Pro Markets. Securitize said Ripple Prime plans to clear and finance Securitize Stocks inside a book that already spans traditional and digital markets. Citi said institutional clients will be able to pay in stablecoins at checkout, with Coinbase converting them to fiat so the merchant never holds the coin. Wells Fargo's chief executive said the bank is preparing for stablecoin opportunities. A repo pledge, a fund-servicing membership, a clearing intention, and two bank statements: none of those is yet a public cash-leg receipt or a failed-transfer correction.
